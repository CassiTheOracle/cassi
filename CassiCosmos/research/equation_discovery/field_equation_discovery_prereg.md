The target is an effective equation in the registered reduced coordinates. It is not a claim that the selected equation replaces the engine's microscopic meshless N-body law.

## Registered observation and derivative

For each trajectory arm, position and velocity are loaded from the existing recorder. Acceleration is the centered difference

\[
\mathbf a_k = \frac{\mathbf v_{k+1}-\mathbf v_{k-1}}{t_{k+1}-t_{k-1}}.
\]

The center is the receipt's declared `window_center`. Live finite tracers with radius greater than `0.25` are eligible. To bound work without data-dependent sampling, retain tracer indices congruent to zero modulo 16. Each arm contributes equally to the selection statistic regardless of its surviving row count.

## Frozen candidate language

The field sees opaque content-addressed candidate identities. Human names and comparisons are added only after selection.

A radial primitive with exponent `p` is

\[
B_p(\mathbf r)=\mathbf r\,|\mathbf r|^{p-1},
\]

whose vector magnitude scales as `|r|^p`. The exponent grid is exactly `-3.0, -2.75, ..., 1.0`. Candidates contain either one primitive or two distinct primitives. Coefficients are the ordinary least-squares projection fitted once on development-fit evidence. No intercept, velocity term, arm identity, seed identity, future statistic, or learned sidecar is allowed.

Candidate validation score is

\[
S = \overline{\mathrm{NRMSE}}_{validation} + 0.005\,N_{terms},
\]

where each arm's NRMSE is `rms(a-prediction)/rms(a)` and the overline is the unweighted mean over arms. The field obligation priority is `1-S`; `semantic.autonomous-agenda` makes the selection. Holdout values are unavailable until the selection record exists.

## Human comparison and novelty classes

The preregistered human reduced baselines are:

- point-mass inverse square: the single primitive `p = -2`;
- uniform-density harmonic core: the single primitive `p = 1`.

Post-selection classification is mechanical:

1. `HUMAN_EQUIVALENT`: selected support is exactly one registered human baseline support.
2. `ALTERNATIVE_EFFECTIVE_LAW`: support differs, holdout mean NRMSE improves by at least 10% relative to both baselines, and no qualifying holdout arm is worse than the better human baseline by more than 5% relative.
3. `DIFFERENT_DESCRIPTION_ONLY`: support differs but predicted holdout vectors have cosine at least 0.999 with a human baseline or fail the improvement criterion.
4. `UNSUPPORTED_DIFFERENCE`: support differs and holdout mean NRMSE is at least 0.75 or any required holdout arm is unavailable.

An alternative effective law describes this simulated collective regime only. It does not contradict the microscopic N-body equation unless a later experiment exposes and compares the complete microscopic force state.

## Controls and stopping rule

- Harmonic synthetic control must select `p = 1` with holdout NRMSE at most `1e-10`.
- Inverse-square synthetic control must select `p = -2` with holdout NRMSE at most `1e-10`.
- Conservation control must select source work alone for the semidiscrete energy derivative and reproduce the recorded equality to relative error at most `1e-10` on every nonzero row; the all-zero frozen arm is reported separately and cannot establish the identity.
- Source mutation, selected-equation mutation, coefficient mutation, split mutation, and field-record removal must each make the independent verifier fail.

The runner is invoked once after implementation and calibration tests pass. It writes `CassiFI/_diag/equation-discovery/receipt.json`. The verdict is determined from the tree above without changing the grammar, split, coefficient fit, threshold, or holdout set after invocation.
