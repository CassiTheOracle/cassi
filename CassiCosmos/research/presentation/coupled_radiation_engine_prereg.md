# Coupled material and moving-multigroup engine: first implementation gate

## Status: FROZEN BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION OR SCIENTIFIC/GPU RUNS—September 2026

## Scope

This schedule qualifies the first default-off `CassiRadiationEngine` implementation over one deliberately bounded supplied-material regime. The engine owns extensive material mass, internal energy and volume plus group-integrated radiation energy. Its selected material is a homogeneous ideal monatomic gas with a supplied total heat capacity and a supplied, versioned physical unit map. Its radiation source is groupwise LTE absorption/emission with coefficients held over one source substep. Its moving-frequency operator is the nonrelativistic isotropic-affine reduction of `CassiTheory/turbulence/moving-multigroup-radiation-closure.md`, using shared group-edge fluxes, piecewise-constant upwinding and an open outer-frequency ledger.

The implementation records only on accepted physics steps. Rendering, camera motion, observation quality and wall-clock cadence cannot advance it. With the feature disabled, it creates no shader, pipeline, uniform set, buffer or dispatch and cannot alter existing solver bytes.

This scope does not qualify a native Cassi-to-baryonic material map, variable-density spatial hydrodynamics, conservative native-geometry remapping, spatial radiation transport, a general velocity-gradient/aberration operator, non-LTE populations, atomic data, physical unresolved emitters or Observatory coupled-source readiness. The supplied verification unit map and material are control inputs, not a native identification.

## Frozen state and numerical contract

For each canonical control element the state is:

- material mass $M$ in kg, internal energy $U_m$ in J, physical volume $V$ in m³ and EOS-derived temperature $T$ in K;
- one supplied isotropic expansion rate $H$ in s⁻¹;
- extensive radiation group energies $U_g=VE_g$ in J over strictly increasing material-frame frequency edges;
- cumulative low/high outer-frequency energy ledgers in J;
- accepted-step, state-epoch and model/unit/group identities.

The ideal-gas affine work update uses $\dot V=3HV$ and $\dot U_m=-3H(\gamma-1)U_m$. The P4c frequency update uses

$$
\widehat\Phi^E_{g+1/2}=-H\nu_{g+1/2}E^{\rm up}_{\nu,g+1/2},
$$

$$
U_g^{n+1}=U_g^n-\Delta t\,HU_g^n-\Delta t\,V
(\widehat\Phi^E_{g+1/2}-\widehat\Phi^E_{g-1/2}),
$$

with one stored edge value shared by its two adjacent groups. Expansion samples the higher-frequency side; compression samples the lower-frequency side. Inflow at either outer edge is zero; outgoing energy is accumulated in the matching ledger. The operator subcycles until

$$
\Delta t\max_g\frac{|H|\nu_{g+1/2}}{\Delta\nu_g}\leq0.4.
$$

For the thermal source, $\lambda_g=c_\gamma\alpha_g\Delta t$ and

$$
U_g^{n+1}=\frac{U_g^n+\lambda_gVE_g^{\rm LTE}(T^{n+1})}{1+\lambda_g},
\qquad
U_m^{n+1}+\sum_gU_g^{n+1}=U_m^n+\sum_gU_g^n.
$$

A monotone bisection solve is used inside the declared temperature table domain. No clipping, extrapolation or silent zero source is accepted. Any invalid state sets a persistent failure status and prevents a new accepted publication.

## Independent reference fixture

A data-only fixture is generated by `research/presentation/coupled_radiation_reference.py` using float64 arithmetic and no import from production GDScript or GLSL. It contains:

- the complete supplied model, unit map, spectral grid, LTE table and immutable hashes;
- null, expansion, compression and thermal-exchange initial states;
- exact or refined float64 reference endpoints;
- expected outer-boundary ledgers, group sums, temperatures and conservation residuals.

The fixture is written once to `research/presentation/reference/coupled_radiation_reference.json`. Godot loads only this data and vetted local kernels.

## Frozen controls

### CR-G0 — fail-closed model and allocation boundary

Reject missing or mismatched whole-file SHA-256, unknown schema major, absent identities, nonpositive/inconsistent units, unordered/nonpositive frequency edges, malformed LTE tables, negative opacity or initial energy, unsupported closure/reconstruction/tail policy, and an initialization temperature outside the declared table range. Disabled mode reports inactive and allocates/dispatches nothing.

### CR-G1 — extensive initialization and publication identity

The first publication reproduces every supplied extensive scalar within FP32 roundoff, derives the supplied temperature within relative error $2\times10^{-6}$, carries model/unit/group hashes, reports accepted step zero and contains no renderer epoch. Every state component is finite and nonnegative.

### CR-G2 — exact null path

With $H=0$ and all absorption coefficients zero, eight accepted steps leave material, radiation, volume and outer-ledger bytes unchanged. Only accepted-step/publication counters may change.

### CR-G3 — affine material work

Pure expansion and compression controls match the float64 ideal-gas affine reference after the registered interval with maximum normalized material-energy, volume and temperature error $\leq3\times10^{-5}$. Mass is byte-identical and every state remains positive.

### CR-G4 — moving-frequency conservation and direction

Expansion moves a planted spectral feature to lower groups; compression moves it to higher groups. GPU group energies and low/high outer ledgers match the independently subcycled piecewise-constant reference with maximum normalized error $\leq4\times10^{-5}$. The internal shared-edge telescoping residual is $\leq3\times10^{-6}$ of the initial radiation energy, the group-plus-ledger balance matches the registered pressure-work transaction to the same tolerance, and no group becomes negative. $H=0$ is a byte-exact frequency null.

### CR-G5 — implicit LTE matter/radiation exchange

Hot-matter/cold-radiation and cold-matter/hot-radiation controls move energy in the expected directions. The GPU endpoint matches the float64 backward-Euler bisection reference with normalized group-energy and temperature error $\leq5\times10^{-5}$. Material plus radiation energy changes by $\leq3\times10^{-6}$ of the transferred energy, with a scale-aware absolute floor of $10^{-7}$ J. Zero opacity and exact LTE are null controls. All accepted temperatures stay inside the provider domain.

### CR-G6 — accepted-step and batching identity

Four accepted steps recorded in one command list and the same four steps recorded one at a time produce byte-identical physical state and identical accepted-step/publication identities. A render-only call, camera change or elapsed wall time produces no physical change.

### CR-G7 — complete checkpoint lifecycle

A checkpoint contains all extensive material/radiation arrays, expansion rates, outer ledgers, failure state, accepted-step/state epochs and immutable model/unit/group/numerical identities. Restoring into the identical engine reproduces state bytes exactly and the next accepted step remains byte-identical to an uninterrupted control. Missing arrays, wrong lengths, wrong hashes or incompatible numerical identities are rejected without modifying the live state.

### CR-G8 — engine integration and default-off compatibility

A focused local-RD `CassiPhysicsEngine` control proves that enabling the supplied bundle advances the physical publication exactly with accepted engine steps. The corresponding disabled control retains an inactive publication and no radiation resources. After integration, the full configured windowed battery must pass without changing any existing gate threshold or fixture.

## Stopping and verdict rules

Each focused run stops at the first malformed state, GPU/runtime error, nonfinite value, negative state, identity mismatch or failed gate. Thresholds and fixtures are not changed after observing candidate results. A shader/runtime failure is `FAIL`, not missing evidence. A missing required unit/material bundle is `INCONCLUSIVE` for a requested physical configuration and must fail closed without affecting the default solver.

A complete CR-G0–CR-G8 pass supports only the bounded supplied-material capability stated above. It does not promote native P2 acceptance, spatial P4b transport, the general P4c angular/gradient regime, or P5 material-provider claims.
