# Inflation from Cascade Steps 20–60: The Qi-Gate Epoch

## Status: Derivation (mechanism Hypothesized, C4; r exponent Mapped—ledger)—August 2026

## Abstract

Cosmic inflation—the early-universe period of quasi-exponential expansion—
is assigned, as a Hypothesized inflationary identification, to cascade steps
$n \approx 20$–$60$ in the Cassi framework. In the
framework triad, Yang and Yin are the canonical real density components
$E_Y$ and $E_I$, with $\rho = E_Y + E_I$, $\varepsilon = E_Y -
\varphi E_I$, and $q = \rho^2/(\rho^2 + \varphi^{-2} + \varepsilon^2)$.
The positive-root amplitude lift $\Psi = (\sqrt{E_Y},\sqrt{E_I})$ has
$\theta_\Psi = \operatorname{atan2}(\sqrt{E_I},\sqrt{E_Y})$ and supports the
local spatial diagnostic $J_\Psi = \rho\nabla\theta_\Psi$; the density
angle $\theta_d = \operatorname{atan2}(E_I,E_Y)$ instead gives
$J_d = (E_Y^2 + E_I^2)\nabla\theta_d
= 2\sqrt{E_YE_I}\,J_\Psi$ for that positive-root lift. These diagnostics
have different units and neither is canonical inter-rung transport without
an explicit constitutive law. Compact phase, SO(2) turns, anti-phase waves,
and directional out/in channels are optional Hypothesized extensions, not
intrinsic properties of the canonical two-density state.
The inflationary dynamics are driven by the **Qi gate** as the ratio $r(t) =
E_Y/E_I$ evolves through these rungs. Along the intended trajectory
normalization, the low-$r$ regime is taken to have an open gate
($1-q \approx 1$), driving rapid conversion and expansion; the gate's
progressive closing terminates the Hypothesized inflationary mechanism when
$r$ crosses the declared threshold $r = \varphi^{-1}$ (step $\sim 60$), before
the canonical fixed point at $r = \varphi$.
The primordial power spectrum follows from the Hypothesized wake-wave mechanism
with cascade spacing: $n_s = 1 - 2\varphi^{-1}/N_e$, with $N_e = 40$ as the
Mapped cascade-e-fold window; interpreting one step as one e-fold is
conditional. The tensor-to-scalar ratio is $r = 12/N_e^2 \approx
0.0075$ at the Mapped e-fold window $N_e = 40$; a target
$r\approx0.003$ requires $N_e=63.2$, outside the ledgered window, while the
$\varphi^{-12}\approx0.003$ expression is not generated by the displayed
$N_e$-formula. The candidate expressions tabulated in §4 evaluate to $0.0557$,
$2.0\times10^{-7}$, $0.142$, and $0.0568$, respectively, and do not reproduce
the Mapped target. The exponent is Mapped per the Fit-Status
Ledger (`parameter-inventory.md` §10), selected against the observed bound. The
closed-form values compare favorably with the cited CMB data, but the gate
slow-roll trajectory does not reproduce them simultaneously: it gives
$(n_s,r)=(0.813,0.188)$ or $(0.914,0.060)$ under the two conventions, so the
catalog values do not coexist on that trajectory.

## 2. The Qi gate as the inflaton

The canonical Qi gate is
$$1-q = \frac{\varphi^{-2} + \varepsilon^2}
{\rho^2 + \varphi^{-2} + \varepsilon^2}, \qquad
\rho = E_Y + E_I,\quad \varepsilon = E_Y - \varphi E_I,$$
and it modulates the conversion rate $\lambda g(q)(E_Y - \varphi E_I)$.
Because $q$ depends on both the total amplitude $\rho$ and imbalance
$\varepsilon$, the ratio $r = E_Y/E_I$ alone does not determine gate
openness; an amplitude normalization or reference state is required.
During the inflationary construction, the intended trajectory is in the
low-$r$ regime with a chosen normalization for which $(1-q)$ is approximately
one. Conversion then drives $r(t)$ upward, while $H \propto \lambda(1-q)$
is nearly constant as a slow-roll input.

The threshold $r = \varphi^{-1}$ (step $\sim 60$) is a declared
inflation-ending pinch, distinct from the canonical density-conversion fixed
point $r = \varphi$. At that threshold the gate begins to close, but its
transparency remains the amplitude-dependent expression above; no universal
numerical value follows from the ratio alone.

In this Hypothesized construction, the closing gate reduces $H$ and supplies
the graceful-exit mechanism. No separate inflaton field is introduced, but
the gate-as-inflaton interpretation remains conditional on the stated
trajectory and normalization.

## 3. Primordial power spectrum: $n_s = 1 - 2\varphi^{-1}/N_e$

The wake-wave mechanism (`consciousness/consciousness-from-phi.md` $\S2.3$) imprints
$\varphi$-scaled perturbations on the density field as the ratio evolves.
During inflation, these perturbations are stretched to super-horizon scales
and frozen in.

The scalar spectral index follows from the cascade spacing of wake waves,
incorporating the Qi gate's partial transparency at the end of inflation:

$$n_s = 1 - \frac{2}{N_e^{\text{eff}}}$$

The effective number of e-folds is larger than the geometric $N_e = 40$
because the Qi gate does not close instantaneously at step 60. Its residual
transparency at closure is the amplitude-dependent expression given in §2,
not a universal value fixed by the ratio $r$ alone. In the Hypothesized
gate-correction construction, the final e-folds contribute MORE to the
spectral index—the expansion rate $H \propto (1-q)$ drops more slowly,
and modes freeze in later, experiencing more e-folds of near-constant $H$.

The integrated effect over the full 40 e-folds gives:

$$N_e^{\text{eff}} = N_e \cdot (1 + \varphi^{-1}) = N_e \cdot \varphi$$

since $1 + \varphi^{-1} = \varphi$. Therefore:

$$\boxed{n_s = 1 - \frac{2\varphi^{-1}}{N_e} = 1 - \frac{2}{N_e\varphi}}$$

With $N_e = 40$:

$$n_s = 1 - \frac{2 \times 0.618034}{40} = 1 - 0.03090 = 0.9691$$

The correction can be expressed in pure $\varphi$-powers:

$$\delta n_s = \frac{2}{N_e} - \frac{2\varphi^{-1}}{N_e} = \frac{2\varphi^{-2}}{N_e}
\approx 0.0191$$

This is consistent with Planck 2018 $n_s = 0.9649 \pm 0.0042$ at $1.0\sigma$.
The gate correction is a closed $\varphi$-form once the window $N_e = 40$ is
fixed (Fit-Status Ledger: Mapped—`parameter-inventory.md` §10); it introduces no
additional fit beyond that mapped input.
Computation: `computations/ns_gate_correction.py` (July 2026).

**Trajectory test (2026-08-06, `computations/slow_roll_trajectory.py`):** the
gate slow-roll trajectory does not reproduce this value at the CMB-exit
anchor. Under the doc's own step count (1 step = 1 e-fold) the trajectory
gives $(n_s, r) = (0.813, 0.188)$; with $N_e = 40$ read literally (1 step =
$\ln\varphi$ physical e-folds) it gives $(0.914, 0.060)$—$n_s$ is 12–36σ
from Planck and $r$ is excluded by the BK18 bound. $N_e = 40$ is a threshold
choice of the start, not a derived count, and the closed form and trajectory
values do not coexist on the trajectory. The closed form stands as arithmetic;
its realization in the trajectory is not demonstrated.

## 4. Tensor-to-scalar ratio: $r = 12/N_e^2$ at the Mapped window

Gravitational waves (tensor modes) are generated by the Qi-gravity coupling
at the inflation scale. The catalog value is the $N_e$-formula at the Mapped
window (ledger row 495):

$$r = \frac{12}{N_e^2} = \frac{12}{40^2} = 0.0075 \qquad (N_e = 40, \text{ Mapped window})$$

An $r \approx 0.003$ target requires $N_e = \sqrt{12/0.003} \approx 63.2$
(outside the ledgered window), while the $\varphi$-power expression
$\varphi^{-12} \approx 0.0031$ is not generated by the displayed $N_e$-formula.
The candidate expressions in this document evaluate as follows:

| Formula | Evaluation | Mapped value | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| $r = \varphi^{-6}$ | $0.0557$ | $0.0075$ | ✗ 7.4× too large |
| $r = \frac{16}{\pi} \cdot \frac{(\varphi^{6}-1)\, q(n=40)}{\varphi^{N_e}}$ with $q = 0.5$, $N_e = 40$ | $1.9\times10^{-7}$ | $0.0075$ | ✗ $4\times10^{4}$× too small |
| $r = \varphi^{-6} \cdot \frac{16}{\pi} \cdot 0.5$ | $0.1419$ | $0.0075$ | ✗ 19× too large |
| $r = \varphi^{-6} \cdot \frac{16}{5\pi}$ (§8) | $0.0568$ | $0.0075$ | ✗ 7.6× too large |

None of the derivations produce the catalog value, so $r$ remains a Mapped
quantity at the Mapped window (ledger row 495), not a derived consequence of
the gate or coupling formulas.

The current bound from Planck/BICEP is $r < 0.036$ (95% CL; BK18: $r < 0.032$).
The Cassi value $r = 0.0075$ survives the bound and is well within reach of
next-generation CMB experiments (Simons Observatory, CMB-S4: $\sigma_r = 0.001$,
a $7.5\sigma$ test; LiteBIRD).

**Trajectory test (2026-08-06, `computations/slow_roll_trajectory.py`):**
the trajectory does not realize the catalog value: at the CMB-exit anchor it
gives $r = 0.188$ (1 step = 1 e-fold) or $0.060$ ($N_e = 40$ literal)—both
excluded by the BK18 bound ($r < 0.032$); the two claimed numbers ($n_s$,
$r$) do not coexist on the trajectory. The closed form stands as arithmetic;
its realization in the trajectory is not demonstrated.

## 5. Running of the spectral index

The running assigned to the closed form is evaluated conditionally under the
same Hypothesized gate-correction ansatz:

Differentiating $n_s=1-2\varphi^{-1}/N_e$ with respect to the e-fold variable gives

$$\alpha_s=\frac{d n_s}{d\ln k}\approx-\frac{2\varphi^{-1}}{N_e^2}=-\frac{2(0.618034)}{1600}\approx-7.7\times10^{-4}.$$

This is consistent with Planck's $\alpha_s=-0.0045\pm0.0067$ at about
$0.6\sigma$; the ansatz value is too small to be detected at current
sensitivity.
