# Temporal Rank Recovery in an Active Rank-Deficient Control: Fixed Supplement

## Status: Pre-registered—September 2026

## Abstract

This supplement extends the published control in
`turbulence/navier-stokes-rank-deficient-stretching.md` without modifying its
seven-check bundle. The control has positive initial vortex-stretching
production while the instantaneous vorticity-gradient determinant vanishes.
The supplement asks whether the seeded covariance can nevertheless recover the
missing direction through its time-dependent parabolic evolution.

The frozen calculation differentiates the exact covariance equation at the
initial time, evaluates the first two covariance jets at
$(x,y)=(\pi/2,0)$, and checks the leading determinant coefficient of the Taylor
polynomial. It does not simulate Brownian paths, integrate a generic
Navier–Stokes trajectory, or assert a uniform lower bound over initial data.

## 1. Bound source and covariance equation

The bound control is the explicit periodic field
\[
u_0(x,y,z)=\bigl(-\sin y,\ 0,\ \sin x+\cos x\sin y\bigr)
\]
from the published note. Its full initial-control verification and invariant
2.5D evolution are bound by that note's source hash in the receipt generated by
this supplement.

For the vorticity-seeded covariance $R$, use the exact equation
\[
\mathcal L_uR
=LR+RL^{\mathsf T}+2\nu Q_\omega,
\qquad
R(0)=0,
\qquad
Q_\omega=(\nabla\omega)(\nabla\omega)^{\mathsf T},
\tag{TRS1}
\]
where
\[
\mathcal L_u=\partial_t+u\cdot\nabla-\nu\Delta,
\qquad L=\nabla u.
\tag{TRS2}
\]
The control remains in the 2.5D class, so $\det Q_\omega(t)=0$ at every
smooth time. The supplement evaluates the covariance jet generated by (TRS1),
not the instantaneous determinant functional.

## 2. Frozen initial-time Taylor convention

Let $Q_0=Q_\omega(0)$ and let $Q_t(0)$ be obtained from the full vorticity
transport equation
\[
\omega_t(0)=-(u_0\cdot\nabla)\omega_0+L_0\omega_0+\nu\Delta\omega_0.
\tag{TRS3}
\]
Differentiating (TRS1) at $t=0$ gives
\[
R_1:=R_t(0)=2\nu Q_0,
\tag{TRS4}
\]
\[
R_2:=R_{tt}(0)
=-u_0\cdot\nabla R_1+\nu\Delta R_1
+L_0R_1+R_1L_0^{\mathsf T}+2\nu Q_t(0).
\tag{TRS5}
\]
The frozen Taylor polynomial is
\[
R_{\mathrm{jet}}(t)=tR_1+\frac{t^2}{2}R_2.
\tag{TRS6}
\]
All matrix entries are simplified exactly before determinant extraction.

## 3. Active point and determinant coefficient

At
\[
(x,y)=(\pi/2,0),
\]
the source and the first covariance jet are
\[
Q_0=
\begin{pmatrix}1&-1&0\\-1&2&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
R_1=
\begin{pmatrix}2\nu&-2\nu&0\\-2\nu&4\nu&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{TRS7}
\]
The second jet obtained from (TRS5) is
\[
R_2=
\begin{pmatrix}
8\nu(1-2\nu)&2\nu(6\nu-5)&0\\
2\nu(6\nu-5)&4\nu(1-6\nu)&0\\
0&0&4\nu^2
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{TRS8}
\]
The frozen determinant check is
\[
\det R_{\mathrm{jet}}(t)=8\nu^4t^4+O(t^5).
\tag{TRS9}
\]
For every $\nu>0$, the leading coefficient is positive. Since the exact
covariance is positive semidefinite and the leading top-left block has rank
two while the third direction appears at order $t^2$, the local time-jet
result implies $R(x,y,t)\succ0$ at the active point and on a spatial
neighborhood for sufficiently small positive $t$. The remainder implication
uses smooth covariance evolution and is analytical; the verifier checks only
the frozen coefficient.

## 5. Decision tree and scope

1. If any exact check fails, classify the supplement `FAIL` and propagate no
   temporal-recovery result.
2. If all five checks pass, classify the active control as `SUPPORTS temporal
   covariance rank recovery at the frozen point`.
3. Keep the instantaneous determinant functional on the 2.5D class equal to
   zero, as established by the bound control.
4. Keep a quantitative production-relative recovery estimate, a uniform
   initial-data-ball bound, and arbitrary-data global regularity
   `UNRESOLVED`.
5. The supplement does not evaluate the stochastic-flow Gramian directly. The
   covariance-PDE jet is a local exact consequence of (TRS1); it does not supply
   a data-uniform lower bound or a continuation theorem.

## 6. Evidence boundary

The supplement verifies a temporal rank-recovery mechanism at one active point
of one globally smooth invariant-class control. It separates instantaneous
source rank from source-time accumulation. The coefficient $8\nu^4$ does not
classify the full accumulated determinant-root envelope, the production-
relative inequality, or the three-dimensional regularity problem.
