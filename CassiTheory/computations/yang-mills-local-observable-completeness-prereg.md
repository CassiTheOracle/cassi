# Pure Yang–Mills local observable completeness

## Status: Pre-registered analytic verification—September 2026

## Abstract

The RG endpoint criterion in `foundations/loop-to-bubble-projection-theorem.md` §9.31 needs two separate inputs: a positive spectral rate and completeness of the retained observable family. The finite transfer diagnostic in §9.32 shows that exact retained correlations can omit a lower transfer channel. This protocol isolates the completeness part by specifying the full local gauge-invariant algebra rather than a selected finite list of Wilson loops.

For a finite connected graph $\Gamma$, let

$$
\mathcal A_\Gamma=C(SU(2)^E)^{SU(2)^V}
$$

be the continuous gauge-invariant cylinder algebra. Let $\mathcal W_\Gamma$ be the unital *-algebra generated by fundamental Wilson traces $\operatorname{tr}(U_\ell)$ over all closed oriented graph paths $\ell$, with inverses allowed. The analytic target is

$$
\overline{\mathcal W_\Gamma}^{\|\cdot\|_\infty}=\mathcal A_\Gamma,
$$

and therefore $\mathcal W_\Gamma$ is dense in the finite-graph gauge-invariant $L^2$ space for every regular gauge-invariant state. On an infinite lattice, the union over finite supports is the local gauge-invariant *-algebra. Its GNS vectors are dense by construction; subtracting the state expectation makes the centered local vectors dense in the vacuum-orthogonal subspace.

The executable schedule checks the finite identities behind this statement: tree-gauge reduction, normalized $SU(2)$ word traces, character recursion, the $2\times2$ trace contraction identity, simultaneous-conjugation invariance, and the orientation witness for three loop variables. Mutation controls show that dropping gauge invariance, the first genuinely orientation-sensitive word, centering, or the infinite word family creates a false completeness claim.

This is a completeness construction, not a Yang–Mills mass-gap proof. It makes the remaining bridge explicit: an RG map must preserve the full local gauge-invariant algebra, or prove that its chosen retained family has the same GNS closure, and the resulting centered correlations must satisfy a volume- and regulator-uniform positive lower spectral bound.

## 1. Finite local algebra

Let $\Gamma=(V,E)$ be a finite connected oriented graph. A link configuration is $U=(U_e)_{e\in E}\in SU(2)^E$, and a gauge transformation $h=(h_v)_{v\in V}$ acts by

$$
(h\cdot U)_e=h_{s(e)}U_eh_{t(e)}^{-1}.
$$

The normalized Haar product measure descends to the invariant Hilbert space

$$
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{gi}}(\Gamma)=L^2(SU(2)^E)^{SU(2)^V}.
$$

Choose a root $o$ and a spanning tree $T$. Tree transport sends every tree link to the identity and leaves $r=|E|-|V|+1$ chord holonomies $h_1,\ldots,h_r$. The residual action is simultaneous conjugation:

$$
(h_1,\ldots,h_r)\mapsto(kh_1k^{-1},\ldots,kh_rk^{-1}).
$$

For a word $w=(i_1^{\sigma_1},\ldots,i_m^{\sigma_m})$ with $\sigma_j\in\{+1,-1\}$, define

$$
\tau_w(h):=\operatorname{tr}(h_{i_1}^{\sigma_1}\cdots h_{i_m}^{\sigma_m}).
$$

Every $\tau_w$ is gauge invariant and real for $SU(2)$. The fundamental character of one loop obeys

$$
\chi_0(U)=1,\qquad \chi_1(U)=\operatorname{tr}(U),\qquad
\chi_{n+1}(U)=\operatorname{tr}(U)\chi_n(U)-\chi_{n-1}(U).
$$

Thus every irreducible character is a polynomial in the fundamental Wilson trace. For a general finite graph, Peter–Weyl matrix coefficients are dense in $C(SU(2)^E)$. Gauge averaging leaves the invariant matrix-coefficient span dense in $\mathcal A_\Gamma$. For $SU(2)$, every finite-dimensional representation is generated by the fundamental doublet; invariant tensor contractions are built from the invariant tensors $\delta$ and $\epsilon$. The identity

$$
\epsilon U\epsilon^{-1}=\overline U,
$$

and the $2\times2$ contraction identity

$$
\operatorname{tr}(A)\operatorname{tr}(B)
=\operatorname{tr}(AB)+\operatorname{tr}(AB^{-1})
$$

reduce every closed fundamental contraction to a finite linear combination of products of closed word traces. Consequently the Wilson-word algebra contains the invariant Peter–Weyl span and is uniformly dense in $\mathcal A_\Gamma$.

The executable checks are finite controls for these identities. They do not replace the Peter–Weyl and invariant-tensor argument.

## 2. Infinite-volume GNS consequence

Let $\Lambda$ be the infinite cubic lattice and

$$
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{loc}}^G
:=\bigcup_{K\Subset\Lambda}\mathcal A_K^G
$$

be the unital local gauge-invariant *-algebra. For any positive state $\omega$ on its completion, the GNS construction gives

$$
\overline{\pi_\omega(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{loc}}^G)\Omega_\omega}
=\mathcal H_\omega.
$$

Since

$$
\pi(A)\Omega
=\bigl(\pi(A)-\omega(A)I\bigr)\Omega+\omega(A)\Omega,
$$

the centered local vectors are dense in the orthogonal complement of the vacuum. Therefore a completeness premise in a transfer-correlation theorem is automatic when the retained family is the full centered local gauge-invariant algebra. It is not automatic for a finite Wilson list, a fixed block ansatz, or an RG family whose closure has not been proved equal to $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{loc}}^G$.

For a positive transfer operator $T=e^{-H}$, a uniform lower bound on the centered local quadratic form,

$$
\langle v,Hv\rangle\geq\Delta\|v\|^2
\quad
(v\text{ centered local}),
$$

extends by closure to the full vacuum-orthogonal form domain. This is the precise spectral target that would turn local correlation control into a physical gap. The protocol does not supply such a bound.

## 4. Mutation firing schedule

The four mutations must all fire:

1. `accept_raw_link_matrix_entries`: add a non-gauge-invariant fundamental matrix entry to the retained family; a vertex gauge transformation changes it while the Wilson traces remain fixed.
2. `drop_orientation_words`: retain only words of length at most two on `bouquet3`; the $C_+$ and $C_-$ triples collide, so completeness is falsely accepted without the witness.
3. `omit_centering`: claim that the local family is dense in the vacuum-orthogonal space without subtracting $\omega(A)I$; the constant function is a firing witness.
4. `finite_word_cutoff_is_complete`: retain only $\chi_0,\ldots,\chi_3$ on one loop and test $\chi_8$; the evaluation residual is nonzero, so a finite cutoff cannot be declared the full local algebra.

A mutation fires when it either accepts the displayed false claim or makes one required witness disappear. The primary verdict is `FAIL` if any mutation lacks a firing witness.
