# Forced Critical Concentration: Fixed Analytical Verification

## Status: Post-run qualification—September 2026

## Abstract

This schedule checks the source terms in the ordinary incompressible Navier–Stokes budgets, the forcing extension of the spectral-spread estimate, and the scaling of a smooth force near a proposed singular point. The work remains within the original equation. Fixed instantaneous Fourier controls and a kinematic Euclidean profile verify algebra and normalization. No Navier–Stokes trajectory, singularity search, formal-proof build or audit of the announced blow-up construction is performed.

This specification governs the post-run qualification of the fixed cases, using 215 checks with explicit source-work signs, scaled-profile maximum speed and the stated endpoint/$F_+$ assumptions. The separately accepted preregistered run is `runs/navier_stokes_forced_concentration/verification.json`, bound to its own protocol and verifier snapshots.

## 1. Equation and conventions

The analytical statements concern smooth solutions with finite displayed norms on $\mathbb R^3$, or real mean-zero velocity and mean-zero forcing on the $2\pi$ torus with volume-normalized integrals. Write the external force as $f_{\rm ext}$, its Leray projection as $g=\mathbb P f_{\rm ext}$, and
$$
u_t=B-\nu\Lambda^2u+g,\quad B=-\mathbb P[(u\cdot\nabla)u],\quad\Lambda=(-\Delta)^{1/2}.
$$
Use the existing definitions
$$
K=\tfrac12\|u\|_2^2,\ E=\tfrac12\|\Lambda u\|_2^2,\ G=\tfrac12\|\Lambda^2u\|_2^2,
\quad\mathcal C=\|\Lambda^{1/2}u\|_2^2,\ Y=\|\Lambda^{3/2}u\|_2^2,
$$
$$
A=\langle\Lambda^2u,B\rangle,\ F=\langle\Lambda u,B\rangle,
\quad\mathcal V=KE-\mathcal C^2/4.
$$
The amplification functional is $f=A-3\nu G$; its symbol is distinct from $f_{\rm ext}$. The strain quantities $S,M,\mathcal R,\delta$ follow `computations/navier-stokes-critical-recurrence-prereg.md` §1. Put $T=\nabla_{\rm sym}g$ and $I_j=\langle\Lambda^j u,g\rangle$ for $j=0,1,2$.

## 2. Fixed analytical targets

1. Verify all source terms:
   $$
   K'=-2\nu E+I_0,\qquad E'=A-2\nu G+I_2,
   $$
   $$
   \mathcal C'=-2\nu Y+2F+2I_1,
   $$
   $$
   \mathcal V'+\nu(2KG+2E^2-\mathcal C Y)
   =KA-\mathcal C F+EI_0+KI_2-\mathcal C I_1.
   $$
2. Verify the forced strain derivative
   $$f'=-\tfrac32\delta-6\langle M,T\rangle.$$
   Compute the left side by differentiating the velocity functional along the forced equation and the right side through the complete projected strain remainder. This identifies why the unforced monotonicity and departure deadlines require their original forcing assumption.
3. With $H=\|g\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}$, check the duality bound $I_1^2\le YH^2$. The continuum proof uses Cauchy–Schwarz. For $d=\nu(1-\theta)>0$, Young's inequality and the hypothesis $c_{\rm S}\sqrt{\eta\mathcal C}\le\theta\nu$, $\eta=\mathcal V/(KE)$, give
   $$\mathcal C'+dY\le H^2/d.$$
   Set $\eta=0$ at the zero field. Check the scalar completion of squares and the coefficient of the integrated estimate. The continuation argument additionally assumes $g\in L^2_t\dot H^{-1/2}_x\cap L^2_tL^2_x$ and a force smooth through the finite endpoint in the strong-solution class. It uses $4E^2\le\mathcal C Y$ and $L^4_tL^6_x$ continuation. No preservation of the spectral hypothesis is assumed to follow from the equation.
4. The unconditional forced budget also gives
   $$\mathcal C(t)+\nu\int_{t_0}^tY\le\mathcal C(t_0)+2\int_{t_0}^tF_++\nu^{-1}\int_{t_0}^tH^2.$$
   Here $F_+=\max(F,0)$; the pointwise bound $F\le F_+$ precedes integration. Thus $\int_{t_0}^{T_*}F_+\,dt<\infty$ is sufficient for continuation under the same regular force assumptions. This is a conditional implication, with no bound on that transfer supplied.
5. For $u_\lambda(y,s)=\lambda u(x_*+\lambda y,T_*+\lambda^2s)$ and $(f_{\rm ext})_\lambda=\lambda^3 f_{\rm ext}(x_*+\lambda y,T_*+\lambda^2s)$, verify the equation exponents, the force derivative factor $\lambda^{3+|\alpha|+2j}$, and
   $$\int_{-L}^0\|g_\lambda(s)\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}^2ds
   =\int_{T_*-L\lambda^2}^{T_*}\|g(t)\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}^2dt.$$
   This tends to zero for integrable forcing. Passing the velocity nonlinearity to a nontrivial limit requires separate compactness and nondegeneracy hypotheses. The Euclidean energy scales as $K[u_\lambda]=\lambda^{-1}K[u]$.

## 3. Fixed periodic controls

Use the cyclic and three-coordinate velocities from `computations/verify_navier_stokes_critical_recurrence.py` at amplitude one. Use viscosities $\nu=1/100,1$. For each velocity use exactly five solenoidal forces: $g=0$, $g=u$, $g=-u$, $g=\Lambda^2u$, and
$$g=(\sin 2z,\sin 2x,\sin 2y).$$
These give 20 exact rows. For every row add $\nabla\cos(x+y+z)$ to the unprojected force and check that projection removes it. Retain all Fourier modes generated by the nonlinear convolution. Record $K,E,G,\mathcal C,Y,A,F,f,\delta,I_0,I_1,I_2,H^2,\mathcal V$, their relevant first derivatives, $\langle M,T\rangle$, and the duality slack. Check the five budget identities and both source signs for $g=\pm u$.

Add one exact zero-velocity control driven by the off-support trigonometric force. Its first kinetic and critical derivatives vanish, while $K''=\|g\|_2^2$ and $\mathcal C''=2\|\Lambda^{1/2}g\|_2^2$ are positive. This addresses forcing from rest without dividing by $K$ or $E$.

Independently reconstruct all 20 rows on each of the grids $N=24,32$. Build the velocities and forces from explicit trigonometric formulas, compute gradients and Leray projection by NumPy FFTs, and compute the full strain remainder using its advection, strain-square and vorticity-square terms. This path must not call exact tensor/convection functions. Compare every recorded scalar with normalized discrepancy $|x-y|/\max(1,|x|,|y|)<10^{-10}$. All comparisons must be nonempty and finite.

## 4. Fixed Euclidean scaling control

Use the divergence-free Schwartz field
$$U(x,y,z)=(-2y,2x,0)e^{-(x^2+y^2+z^2)},\qquad U_\ell(x)=\ell^{-1}U(x/\ell).$$
For the unitary Fourier transform, $|\widehat U(k)|^2=(k_x^2+k_y^2)e^{-|k|^2/2}/8$. Verify the zeroth, first, second and third radial moments by exact symbolic integration and independent high-precision quadrature. Check
$$K[U_\ell]=\ell K[U],\quad\mathcal C[U_\ell]=\mathcal C[U],\quad\|U_\ell\|_\infty=\ell^{-1}\sqrt{2/e}.$$
This is a kinematic family, with no Navier–Stokes trajectory claim. It checks that bounded energy and diverging maximum velocity alone do not determine divergence of the critical norm.

For the announced construction's quoted core scales, check only the exponent arithmetic with symbolic $0<h<1/100$: volume $\tau^{3/2-h}$ and characteristic speed $\tau^{-1/2-h}$ give core energy exponent $1/2-3h$ and cubic-speed integral exponent $-4h$. Applying these scales as a lower bound to the complete constructed flow requires a source-specific justification; the arithmetic alone does not verify that construction.

## 5. Qualification and stopping rule

- Every exact identity must pass; every numerical comparison must be finite and below $10^{-10}$. Duplicate check names, empty comparison sets or an altered frozen input fail qualification.
- Qualified source identities and scaling calculations receive **PASS** at their displayed algebraic scope. The analytic inequalities require the separate continuum derivation and independent review.
- The Gaussian profile receives **CONTRADICTS** for the purely kinematic implication from bounded energy and diverging maximum velocity to divergent critical norm. This classification addresses no solution of the Navier–Stokes equation.
- Arbitrary-data regularity, an unforced finite-energy blow-up construction, novelty, the announced theorem's proof correctness and a nontrivial blow-up limit remain **UNRESOLVED** or **NOT_AUDITED**, as applicable.
- The post-run qualification re-executes the fixed controls under the endpoint and $F_+$ assumptions stated here. No new fixtures, parameter searches, trajectories or proof builds are added. Freeze the qualification inputs before execution; any input change requires a fresh receipt and matching manifest and snapshots. Stop after the controls and analytical reconciliation.
