# Pure Yang–Mills Radial Feshbach Transfer and Weak-Coupling Cutoff: Finite-Dimension Amendment

## Status: Pre-registered—September 2026

## Abstract

This investigation tests the Feshbach transfer generated by an electric-character cutoff for the continuous-$SU(2)$ isolated open square. The radial Hamiltonian is an infinite Jacobi operator whose discarded tail contributes a rank-one, energy-dependent self-energy. The frozen analytical and numerical controls separate low-energy spectral convergence from unweighted operator-norm control and test how the character cutoff must grow at weak bare coupling. This versioned protocol amends only the finite-section lower bound needed by the declared three-Ritz-value inventory. The preserved v1 schedule has $N_{\rm grow}=N_{1/2}=1$ at $x=1/4$, so those two sections have dimension $N+1=2$ and cannot supply three Ritz values. Version 2 replaces every nonfixed scheduled cutoff by $\max\{2,\lceil\cdot\rceil\}$; it is a new campaign, not an implementation repair or reuse of the failed v1 receipt. The square remains a finite regulated control. Neighboring plaquettes, boundary representation sectors, volume-uniform estimates, the thermodynamic limit, the continuum Yang–Mills mass gap and Cassi microscopic identification remain separate obligations.

## 1. Regulated Hamiltonian and normalization

Use the isolated-square normalization of
`foundations/loop-to-bubble-projection-theorem.md` §9.8. A single link in representation $j=n/2$ has Casimir $j(j+1)=n(n+2)/4$. The gauge-invariant square character carries that representation on four links, so the total electric operator has eigenvalue $n(n+2)$.

On

$$
\mathcal H_\square
=\ell^2(\mathbb N_0)
=\overline{\operatorname{span}}
\{|n\rangle=\chi_{n/2}:n\ge0\},
$$

define

$$
K|n\rangle=k_n|n\rangle,
\qquad k_n=n(n+2),
$$

and

$$
T|0\rangle=|1\rangle,
\qquad
T|n\rangle=|n-1\rangle+|n+1\rangle\quad(n\ge1).
$$

The bounded self-adjoint operator $T$ has $\|T\|=2$. The nonnegative
self-adjoint operator $K$ has compact resolvent, so $h_x$ is self-adjoint
on $D(K)$, nonnegative and has compact resolvent. Every tail operator
$D_N$ is self-adjoint on $Q_ND(K)$ and has discrete spectrum.

For $g,a>0$, set

$$
x=\frac{2}{g^4},
\qquad
h_x=K+x(2I-T),
\qquad
H_\square=\frac{g^2}{2a}h_x.
\tag{RF1}
$$

This gives

$$
(H_\square)_{nm}
=\left[\frac{g^2}{2a}n(n+2)+\frac{2}{ag^2}\right]\delta_{nm}
-\frac{1}{ag^2}(\delta_{n,m+1}+\delta_{n,m-1}).
\tag{RF2}
$$

With

$$
d\mu(\theta)=\frac{2}{\pi}\sin^2\theta\,d\theta,
\qquad
\chi_{n/2}(\theta)=\frac{\sin((n+1)\theta)}{\sin\theta},
$$

the unitary map

$$
u(\theta)=\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\sin\theta\,\psi(\theta)
$$

sends $h_x$ to

$$
\widetilde h_x
=-\frac{d^2}{d\theta^2}-1+2x(1-\cos\theta),
\qquad
D(\widetilde h_x)=H^2(0,\pi)\cap H_0^1(0,\pi).
\tag{RF3}
$$

All statements below concern this continuous-group physical class-function sector. Finite-group quadrature may be used only as a numerical control and cannot qualify an analytical claim.

## 2. Exact Feshbach pencil

For an integer $N\ge0$, let

$$
P_N=\sum_{n=0}^{N}|n\rangle\langle n|,
\qquad Q_N=I-P_N,
$$

$$
A_N=P_Nh_xP_N,
\qquad D_N=Q_Nh_xQ_N,
\qquad V_N=P_Nh_xQ_N=-x|N\rangle\langle N+1|.
$$

For $z\in\rho(D_N)$ define

$$
m_{N+1}(z)
=\langle N+1|(D_N-z)^{-1}|N+1\rangle
$$

and the Feshbach pencil

$$
\boxed{
F_N(z)
=P_N(h_x-z)P_N
-x^2m_{N+1}(z)|N\rangle\langle N|.
}
\tag{RF4}
$$

Derive on the operator domain that, for $z\in\rho(D_N)$,

$$
z\in\sigma(h_x)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
0\in\sigma(F_N(z)),
\tag{RF5}
$$

with multiplicities, and reconstruct the discarded component from

$$
Q_N\psi=-(D_N-z)^{-1}V_N^*P_N\psi.
\tag{RF6}
$$

Writing $d_n=k_n+2x$, the tail Weyl function must satisfy the convergent Jacobi continued fraction

$$
m_n(z)
=\frac{1}{d_n-z-x^2m_{n+1}(z)}.
\tag{RF7}
$$

For a finite terminal index $M$, freeze

$$
\Delta_{n,M}(z)
=(d_n-z)\Delta_{n+1,M}(z)-x^2\Delta_{n+2,M}(z),
$$

$$
\Delta_{M+1,M}=1,
\qquad
\Delta_{M+2,M}=0,
\qquad
m_n^{(M)}(z)=\frac{\Delta_{n+1,M}(z)}{\Delta_{n,M}(z)}.
\tag{RF8}
$$

The finite determinant control is

$$
\frac{\det(h_x^{(M)}-z)}{\det(D_N^{(M)}-z)}
=\det F_N^{(M)}(z).
\tag{RF9}
$$

For real $E<\inf\sigma(D_N)$, verify $m_{N+1}(E)>0$ and

$$
\frac{d}{dE}m_{N+1}(E)
=\langle N+1|(D_N-E)^{-2}|N+1\rangle>0.
\tag{RF10}
$$

## 3. Tail and resolvent bounds

Let $r=N+1$. The positive multiplication form $x(2I-T)$ in (RF1) gives

$$
D_N\ge k_r Q_N.
\tag{RF11}
$$

For real $E<k_r$, define $\delta=k_r-E>0$ and

$$
\Sigma_N(E)=x^2m_r(E),
$$

$$
s(\delta,x)
=\frac{\delta+2x-\sqrt{\delta(\delta+4x)}}{2}
=\frac{2x^2}{\delta+2x+\sqrt{\delta(\delta+4x)}}.
$$

Prove

$$
\boxed{
\frac{x^2}{\delta+2x}
\le\Sigma_N(E)
\le s(\delta,x)
\le\min\left\{x,\frac{x^2}{\delta}\right\}.
}
\tag{RF12}
$$

The same bounds give the operator bracketing

$$
A_N-E-s(\delta,x)|N\rangle\langle N|
\le F_N(E)
\le A_N-E-\frac{x^2}{\delta+2x}|N\rangle\langle N|.
\tag{RF12a}
$$

At a root $E<\inf\sigma(D_N)$, choose
$p=P_N\psi$ with $\|p\|=1$ and
$p_N=\langle N|p\rangle$. Equations (RF6) and (RF10) give

$$
Q_N\psi
=xp_N(D_N-E)^{-1}|N+1\rangle,
$$

$$
\|Q_N\psi\|^2
=x^2|p_N|^2m_{N+1}'(E),
\qquad
-F_N'(E)
=I+x^2m_{N+1}'(E)|N\rangle\langle N|.
\tag{RF12b}
$$

These identities provide a direct tail-weight and eigenvector-normalization
certificate without an extrapolated decay exponent.

The lower bound follows from
$\langle r|(D_N-E)|r\rangle=\delta+2x$ and the inverse-form inequality. The upper bound compares the tail with $\delta I+xL_+$, where $L_+$ is the Dirichlet half-line discrete Laplacian, and evaluates its boundary Green function exactly.

Consequently, if $\delta=o(x)$ then $\Sigma_N(E)=\Theta(x)$. If $\delta/x\to\infty$, the two bounds are asymptotic to $x^2/\delta$. The first statement excludes a weak-coupling treatment of the exact self-energy as a small unweighted correction for every schedule with $k_{N+1}-E=o(x)$.

For $\eta>0$, derive the block-resolvent bounds

$$
\left\|
P_N(h_x+\eta)^{-1}P_N-(A_N+\eta)^{-1}
\right\|
\le
\frac{x^2}{\eta^2(k_r+\eta)},
\tag{RF13}
$$

$$
\|Q_N(h_x+\eta)^{-1}P_N\|
\le\frac{x}{\eta(k_r+\eta)}.
\tag{RF14}
$$

At the natural weak-coupling scale $\eta=c\sqrt{x}$, (RF13) tends to zero under the sufficient unweighted schedule $N/\sqrt{x}\to\infty$. Making its right-hand side negligible relative to the natural resolvent scale $x^{-1/2}$ requires the stronger sufficient schedule $N/x^{3/4}\to\infty$. These are conservative operator-norm statements. They do not set the minimal cutoff for low-energy spectral convergence.

## 4. Weak-coupling spectral scale

Under $y=x^{1/4}\theta$, (RF3) has the fixed-level expansion

$$
\lambda_j(h_x)
=(4j+3)\sqrt{x}+c_j+O(x^{-1/2}),
\qquad j=0,1,2,\ldots,
\tag{RF15}
$$

where

$$
c_j
=-1-\frac{1}{12}\left(6j^2+9j+\frac{15}{4}\right).
\tag{RF16}
$$

Thus

$$
\lambda_j(h_x)-\lambda_0(h_x)
=4j\sqrt{x}
-\left(\frac{j^2}{2}+\frac{3j}{4}\right)
+O(x^{-1/2}),
\tag{RF17}
$$

and, since $\sqrt{x}=\sqrt2/g^2$,

$$
\Delta_{\square,1}
=\frac{g^2}{2a}[\lambda_1(h_x)-\lambda_0(h_x)]
=\frac{2\sqrt2}{a}-\frac{5g^2}{8a}+O(g^4/a).
\tag{RF18}
$$

The verifier must encode the symbol as `Delta_square_1`; the displayed subscript in (RF18) denotes the isolated square.

The finite section has the exact quadratic form

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle f,A_Nf\rangle
={}&\sum_{n=0}^{N}k_n|f_n|^2\\
&+x\left(
|f_0|^2+|f_N|^2
+\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}|f_{n+1}-f_n|^2
\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{RF19}
$$

The smallest eigenvalue of its discrete-Laplacian part is
$4\sin^2[\pi/(2(N+2))]$, so

$$
\boxed{
\lambda_0(A_N)
\ge4x\sin^2\frac{\pi}{2(N+2)}.
}
\tag{RF20}
$$

Equation (RF20) proves that fixed $N$, and every $N=o(x^{1/4})$,
misses the $O(\sqrt{x})$ low-energy scale when the tail is omitted from
the bare compression $A_N$.

The discarded block has the exact quadratic form

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle f,D_Nf\rangle
={}&\sum_{n=N+1}^{\infty}k_n|f_n|^2\\
&+x\left(
|f_{N+1}|^2
+\sum_{n=N+1}^{\infty}|f_{n+1}-f_n|^2
\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{RF20a}
$$

For $N/x^{1/4}\to C\in(0,\infty)$, derive the two
finite-difference form limits

$$
x^{-1/2}A_N
\longrightarrow
-\frac{d^2}{dy^2}+y^2
\quad\hbox{on }(0,C),
\tag{RF21}
$$

$$
x^{-1/2}D_N
\longrightarrow
-\frac{d^2}{dy^2}+y^2
\quad\hbox{on }(C,\infty),
\tag{RF21a}
$$

both with Dirichlet data at the finite endpoint. Denote their ordered
eigenvalues by $\mu_j(C)$ and $\nu_j(C)$, respectively. Then
$\mu_j(C)>4j+3$, while

$$
\frac{\inf\sigma(D_N)}{\sqrt{x}}
\longrightarrow\nu_0(C)\ge C^2.
\tag{RF21b}
$$

Consequently a sufficiently large fixed $C$ can isolate a fixed finite
low-energy window for the exact Feshbach pencil while retaining its full
self-energy. Freeze $J=2$, $\delta_{\rm iso}=1$ and $C_J=4$, for which
$C_J^2>4J+3+2\delta_{\rm iso}$. With
$N_{\rm iso}=\lceil C_Jx^{1/4}\rceil$, (RF15) and (RF21b) give, for
sufficiently large $x$,

$$
\lambda_J(h_x)
<k_{N_{\rm iso}+1}-\delta_{\rm iso}\sqrt{x}
\le\inf\sigma(D_{N_{\rm iso}})-\delta_{\rm iso}\sqrt{x}.
\tag{RF21c}
$$

Exact Feshbach isolation and bare-compression convergence therefore have
distinct cutoff requirements. Recovery of the half-line values by $A_N$,
and vanishing discarded mass for each fixed low eigenstate, require

$$
\boxed{
N/x^{1/4}\longrightarrow\infty,
\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad gN\longrightarrow\infty.
}
\tag{RF22}
$$

These conditions concern electric-character truncation of the isolated
square. They do not provide a representation-count theorem for an
interacting lattice block.

## 6. Decision tree and stopping rule

- Missing or duplicate rows, nonfinite values, source mutation, failed reference convergence, or a discrepancy at or above its tolerance gives **INCONCLUSIVE** for the affected control domain.
- Qualified continuous-$SU(2)$ normalization, Schur complement, continued fraction, determinant, resolvent and form identities receive **SUPPORTS** for the stated regulated construction, subject to source-independent reconstruction and analytical review.
- Complete proofs of (RF4)–(RF14) permit **ADOPT** for the exact isolated-square Feshbach transfer and bounds.
- Complete form convergence for (RF19)–(RF22) permits **ADOPT** for the isolated-square character-cutoff condition. A failed derivation gives **INCONCLUSIVE** rather than a fitted replacement.
- The fixed or $o(x^{1/4})$ character cutoff receives **CONTRADICTS** as a weak-coupling low-energy truncation only if (RF20), the exact radial scaling and independent review all pass.
- Treating the self-energy as an $o(x)$ unweighted correction for $k_{N+1}-E=o(x)$ receives **CONTRADICTS** only if (RF12) and its asymptotic consequence pass independently.
- A gauge-compatible interacting fibre on a genuine refined block, boundary-representation transport, neighboring-plaquette correlations, volume-uniform resolvent control, the thermodynamic and continuum limits, a continuum mass gap and Cassi microscopic identification remain **UNRESOLVED**.

Run the complete fixed schedule once after source review, implementation review and independent analytical review. The v1 protocol, manifest, source snapshots and failed receipt remain unchanged. Version 2 uses distinct schemas, source identities and output paths; its independent verifier must reject every v1 receipt. Stop after one v1-rejection control, one qualified primary receipt, one source-independent reconstruction, final analytical reconciliation and registry integration. Do not scan couplings, optimize cutoff coefficients, fit exponents, truncate the schedule, or infer a many-plaquette result.
