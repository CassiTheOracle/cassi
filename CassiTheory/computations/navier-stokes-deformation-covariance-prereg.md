# Deformation-Covariance Metric for Navier–Stokes: Fixed Analytical Verification

## Status: Audit-rechecked protocol—September 2026

## Abstract

This schedule tests a covariance-inverse metric generated by the Constantin–Iyer stochastic deformation. The inverse metric is designed to cancel vortex stretching while its nonlinear spatial term completes the viscous contribution into a nonnegative covariant square. A Hilbert-space projection identity gives the same weighted enstrophy law as an exact stochastic regression residual. The controls distinguish this identity from uniform control of Euclidean enstrophy. The execution is symbolic and finite-dimensional; arbitrary-data regularity requires a separate bound on active metric distortion.

## 1. Equation, domain, and conventions

Work with a smooth mean-zero solution of the unforced incompressible Navier–Stokes equation on the normalized $2\pi$ torus:

$$
\partial_tu+(u\cdot\nabla)u=-\nabla p+\nu\Delta u,
\qquad
\nabla\cdot u=0,
\qquad
\nu>0.
\tag{DC1}
$$

Put

$$
\omega=\nabla\times u,
\qquad
L=\nabla u,
\qquad
S=\frac12(L+L^{\mathsf T}),
\qquad
\mathcal L_u=\partial_t+u\cdot\nabla-\nu\Delta.
\tag{DC2}
$$
The gradient convention is $L_{ij}:=\partial_j u_i$, so
$L\omega=(\omega\cdot\nabla)u$.

Then

$$
\mathcal L_u\omega=L\omega,
\qquad
\frac12\frac d{dt}\|\omega\|_2^2
+\nu\|\nabla\omega\|_2^2
=\int\omega\cdot S\omega\,dx.
\tag{DC3}
$$

All spatial integrals use normalized volume. Matrix inequalities use the Euclidean order on symmetric matrices.

## 2. Stochastic deformation and its second moment

Let $X_t(a)$ be the Constantin–Iyer stochastic flow and $\mathscr A_t=X_t^{-1}$ its spatial inverse:

All stochastic identities are restricted to compact intervals $[0,\tau]\subset[0,T_*)$ on which the periodic solution and initial datum are smooth. These hypotheses supply a smooth volume-preserving stochastic flow, finite deformation moments, differentiation under expectation, Fubini, and mean-zero Itô integrals.

$$
dX_t(a)=u(X_t(a),t)\,dt+\sqrt{2\nu}\,dB_t,
\qquad X_0(a)=a.
\tag{DC4}
$$

Define the Eulerian forward deformation and sampled initial vorticity

$$
F_t(x)=\bigl(\nabla_aX_t\bigr)(\mathscr A_t(x)),
\qquad
V_t(x)=\omega_0(\mathscr A_t(x)).
\tag{DC5}
$$

Every realization preserves volume, $\det F_t=1$, and the stochastic Cauchy formula is

$$
\omega(x,t)=\mathbb E[F_t(x)V_t(x)].
\tag{DC6}
$$

The Eulerian deformation equation has the Stratonovich form

$$
dF+\bigl(u\cdot\nabla F-LF\bigr)dt
+\sqrt{2\nu}\sum_k\partial_kF\circ dB_t^k=0,
\tag{DC6a}
$$

or, equivalently, the Itô form

$$
dF+\bigl(u\cdot\nabla F-LF\bigr)dt
-\nu\Delta F\,dt
+\sqrt{2\nu}\sum_k\partial_kF\,dB_t^k=0.
\tag{DC6b}
$$

The covariance closure below uses (DC6b), including the quadratic variation generated by the common noise.

The fixed second moment and its inverse are

$$
C=\mathbb E[FF^{\mathsf T}],
\qquad
G_C=C^{-1}.
\tag{DC7}
$$

The common translational noise closes the second-moment equation:

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal L_u C=LC+CL^{\mathsf T},
\qquad C(x,0)=I.}
\tag{DC8}
$$

The stochastic representation makes $C$ and $G_C$ symmetric positive definite throughout every smooth interval. Differentiating $CG_C=I$ gives

$$
\partial_kG_C=-G_C(\partial_kC)G_C,
\tag{DC9}
$$

and the inverse equation

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal L_uG_C
+G_CL+L^{\mathsf T}G_C
+2\nu\sum_k
(\partial_kG_C)C(\partial_kG_C)=0.}
\tag{DC10}
$$

The sign and factor in the final term are part of the frozen verification target.

## 3. Exact covariant weighted-enstrophy law

Define

$$
\nabla_k^C\omega
:=\partial_k\omega+C(\partial_kG_C)\omega
=\partial_k\omega-(\partial_kC)G_C\omega,
\tag{DC11}
$$

and

$$
z_C=\omega^{\mathsf T}G_C\omega,
\qquad
\mathcal D_C=
\sum_k\int
(\nabla_k^C\omega)^{\mathsf T}
G_C(\nabla_k^C\omega)\,dx.
\tag{DC12}
$$

The reaction terms in $\mathcal L_u z_C$ cancel. The diffusion product rule and (DC10) give the pointwise identity

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal L_u z_C
=-2\nu\sum_k
(\nabla_k^C\omega)^{\mathsf T}
G_C(\nabla_k^C\omega)\le0.}
\tag{DC13}
$$

The square in (DC13) expands as

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\nabla_k^C\omega)^{\mathsf T}G_C(\nabla_k^C\omega)
={}&(\partial_k\omega)^{\mathsf T}G_C(\partial_k\omega)
+2(\partial_k\omega)^{\mathsf T}(\partial_kG_C)\omega\\
&+\omega^{\mathsf T}(\partial_kG_C)C(\partial_kG_C)\omega.
\end{aligned}
\tag{DC14}
$$

Periodic integration yields

$$
\boxed{
Z_C(t)+2\nu\int_0^t\mathcal D_C(s)\,ds
=W(0),
\qquad
Z_C(t)=\int z_C(x,t)\,dx,
\quad W=\|\omega\|_2^2.}
\tag{DC15}
$$

This identity uses no scalar work projection. Its useful strength still depends on the size of $G_C$ in the current vorticity distribution.

## 4. Stochastic projection and regression residual

At fixed $(x,t)$ let

$$
\mathscr H=L^2(\Xi;\mathbb R^3),
\qquad
T_xq=\mathbb E[Fq],
\qquad
T_x^*y=F^{\mathsf T}y.
\tag{DC16}
$$

Then

$$
T_xT_x^*=C,
\qquad
\omega=T_xV,
\qquad
P_x:=T_x^*G_CT_x
\tag{DC17}
$$

and $P_x$ is the orthogonal projection onto $\operatorname{ran}T_x^*$. The pointwise Pythagorean identity is

$$
\boxed{
\mathbb E|V|^2-\omega^{\mathsf T}G_C\omega
=\mathbb E\left|V-F^{\mathsf T}G_C\omega\right|^2\ge0.}
\tag{DC18}
$$

Volume preservation gives

$$
\int\mathbb E|V_t(x)|^2dx
=\int|\omega_0(a)|^2da=W(0).
\tag{DC19}
$$

Combining (DC15), (DC18), and (DC19) yields the exact bridge

$$
\boxed{
\int\mathbb E\left|V-F^{\mathsf T}G_C\omega\right|^2dx
=2\nu\int_0^t\mathcal D_C(s)\,ds.}
\tag{DC20}
$$

Thus the accumulated covariant viscous loss equals the unresolved stochastic regression energy at the endpoint.

Writing

$$
\mathcal R_C(t):=W(0)-Z_C(t),
\tag{DC20a}
$$

the same identity gives, at smooth times with $Z_C(t)>0$,

$$
\mathcal R_C'=2\nu\mathcal D_C,
\qquad
\frac{\mathcal R_C'}{W(0)-\mathcal R_C}
=2\nu\frac{\mathcal D_C}{Z_C}
=\frac d{dt}\bigl[-\log Z_C\bigr].
\tag{DC20b}
$$

This is an interior smooth-time identity and asserts no finite limiting rate at $T_*$.

## 5. Determinant and inverse-Jensen comparison

Equation (DC8) and incompressibility imply

$$
\mathcal L_u\log\det C
=\nu\sum_k
\operatorname{tr}\left[(G_C\partial_kC)^2\right]
\ge0.
\tag{DC21}
$$

Since $C(0)=I$, the minimum principle gives

$$
\det C\ge1,
\qquad
\det G_C\le1.
\tag{DC22}
$$

Let

$$
X=FF^{\mathsf T},
\qquad
H=\mathbb E[X^{-1}]
=\mathbb E[F^{-\mathsf T}F^{-1}].
\tag{DC23}
$$

The inverse-deformation second moment $H$ obeys the forward cancellation equation used in `turbulence/navier-stokes-adaptive-metric.md`. Here $G_C=(\mathbb E X)^{-1}$ is the inverse arithmetic mean of $X$, equivalently the matrix harmonic mean of the random inverse metric $X^{-1}$, and

$$
\boxed{
H-G_C
=\mathbb E\left[
(X^{-1}-G_C)X(X^{-1}-G_C)
\right]\succeq0.}
\tag{DC24}
$$

Equality holds when $X$ is deterministic. The gap measures stochastic deformation spread. Positivity and determinant information alone provide no upper bound on $C$ and therefore no lower bound on $G_C$.

## 6. Active distortion and continuation obligation

For $W(t)>0$, define

$$
\mathfrak A_C(t)=\frac{W(t)}{Z_C(t)},
\tag{DC25}
$$
with $\mathfrak A_C=1$ for the zero solution.

The projection in (DC17) retains exactly the part of the sampled initial
vorticity seen by the deformation operator:

$$
P_xV=F^{\mathsf T}G_C\omega,
\qquad
T_xP_xV=\omega,
\qquad
z_C=\|P_xV\|_{\mathscr H}^2.
\tag{DC25a}
$$

Consequently,

$$
\boxed{
\mathfrak A_C(t)=
\frac{\int\|T_xP_xV\|_{\mathbb R^3}^2dx}
{\int\|P_xV\|_{\mathscr H}^2dx}.}
\tag{DC25b}
$$

This is a Rayleigh quotient of the stochastic deformation operator on the
data-conditioned projected component. A uniform bound on the full operator
norm is sufficient and stronger than the active estimate.


Equation (DC15) gives
$$
W(t)\le\mathfrak A_C(t)W(0).
\tag{DC26}
$$

On every interval on which $W$ and $Z_C$ are positive, these quantities and $\mathfrak A_C$ are locally continuously differentiable. Equations (DC3) and (DC15) then give the exact
signed growth rate

$$
\frac d{dt}\log\mathfrak A_C
=
\frac{2P}{W}
+2\nu\left(
\frac{\mathcal D_C}{Z_C}-\frac D W
\right)
=:\Gamma_C.
\tag{DC26a}
$$

One sufficient closing target is

$$
\Gamma_C(t)
\le b_C(t)\left(1+\log_+\mathfrak A_C(t)\right),
\qquad b_C(t)\ge0\ \text{a.e.},
\qquad
\int_0^{\min(T,T_*)}b_C(t)\,dt
\le B_C(\nu,T,R)<\infty.
\tag{DC26b}
$$

Set $Y_C=1+\log_+\mathfrak A_C$. Its active branch satisfies $Y_C'=\Gamma_C\le b_CY_C$, while its inactive branch satisfies $Y_C'=0\le b_CY_C$. The integrating-factor estimate is

$$
\mathfrak A_C(t)
\le\exp\left[
Y_C(0)\exp\left(\int_0^t b_C(s)\,ds\right)-1
\right].
\tag{DC26c}
$$

Since $\mathfrak A_C(0)=Y_C(0)=1$, (DC26c) gives a finite bound in (DC27). Constructing $b_C$ from independently controlled quantities is part of the unresolved estimate.

The all-data target is the following uniform estimate: for every $\nu>0$, finite $T>0$, and $R\ge0$,

$$
\boxed{
\sup_{0\le t<\min(T,T_*)}\mathfrak A_C(t)
\le C_C(\nu,T,R)<\infty}
\tag{DC27}
$$

for all $u_0\in C^\infty(\mathbb T^3;\mathbb R^3)$ satisfying $\int_{\mathbb T^3}u_0\,dx=0$, $\nabla\cdot u_0=0$, and $\|u_0\|_{H^3}\le R$. Equation (DC27), together with (DC26) and the periodic $H^1$ restart criterion, implies continuation through $T$. Finite-time loss of regularity would force $\mathfrak A_C$ to diverge along an endpoint sequence.

Under the stated smooth-flow hypotheses, the paper derives the stochastic-calculus, covariance, projection, balance, comparison, and control identities through (DC26c). The fixed schedule checks their declared symbolic components, four-member ensemble, scalar prototypes, and exact controls within executable scope. It leaves (DC27) **UNRESOLVED**.

## 7. Fixed controls

### 7.1 Homogeneous extension

Use

$$
L_a=\operatorname{diag}(a,-a,0),
\qquad a>0,
\tag{DC28}
$$

with a state initially parallel to $e_1$. The fixed matrices are

$$
F_a=\operatorname{diag}(e^{at},e^{-at},1),
\quad
C_a=\operatorname{diag}(e^{2at},e^{-2at},1),
\quad
G_a=C_a^{-1}.
\tag{DC29}
$$

For $\omega_a=e^{at}e_1$,

$$
\omega_a^{\mathsf T}G_a\omega_a=1,
\qquad
\mathfrak A_C^{\mathrm{loc}}=e^{2at},
\qquad
\det C_a=1.
\tag{DC30}
$$

This finite-dimensional control excludes coercivity inferred from positivity, determinant, the exact weighted law, or unrestricted matrix algebra. It is curl-free, nonperiodic, and outside the finite-energy Navier–Stokes data class, so it does not classify a bound derived from the coupled periodic equation.

### 7.2 Periodic shear heat flow

Use the exact periodic Navier–Stokes solution

$$
u_s(y,t)=b e^{-\lambda t}\sin(ny)e_1,
\qquad
\lambda=\nu n^2,
\qquad
n\in\mathbb N,
\tag{DC31}
$$

and abbreviate

$$
a_s(y,t)=bn e^{-\lambda t}\cos(ny).
\tag{DC32}
$$

The fixed covariance solution is

$$
C_s=
\begin{pmatrix}
1+q_0(t)+q_2(t)\cos(2ny)&t a_s&0\\
t a_s&1&0\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix},
\tag{DC33}
$$

where

$$
q_0(t)=\frac{b^2n^2}{4\lambda^2}
\left[1-(1+2\lambda t)e^{-2\lambda t}\right],
\tag{DC34}
$$

$$
q_2(t)=\frac{b^2n^2}{4\lambda^2}e^{-4\lambda t}
\left[e^{2\lambda t}(2\lambda t-1)+1\right].
\tag{DC35}
$$

Equation (DC33) must satisfy (DC8) exactly. Its third direction remains unchanged:

$$
(G_s)_{33}=1,
\qquad
\omega_s=-a_s e_3,
\qquad
Z_C=W,
\qquad
\mathfrak A_C=1.
\tag{DC36}
$$

This control checks that deformation in an inactive plane need not weaken the vorticity-weighted metric.

## 9. Decision rules

The classifications are fixed before execution.

1. **Covariance-inverse weighted law:** `SUPPORTS` only if all 19 stochastic-conversion, covariance, inverse, and covariant-law checks pass.
2. **Finite-ensemble projection and inverse-Jensen comparison:** `SUPPORTS` only if projection checks 1–6 and 9–10 and all three inverse-Jensen-comparison checks pass. The synthetic scalar prototypes in projection checks 7–8 are excluded from this gate. This classification covers the fixed finite ensemble and inverse-Jensen algebra; (DC20) and (DC20b) remain analytic consequences of the projection theorem, realization-wise volume preservation, and the covariant weighted law rather than independently executed stochastic-flow checks.
3. **Uniform coercivity from positivity, determinant, and exact cancellation:** `CONTRADICTS` if extensional checks 1–5 preserve the weighted law and determinant while $\mathfrak A_C^{\mathrm{loc}}$ grows exponentially. This classification concerns unrestricted deformation algebra, not the benign periodic shear or arbitrary-data Navier–Stokes regularity. A missing extensional check gives `INCONCLUSIVE`.
4. **Arbitrary-data Navier–Stokes regularity:** `UNRESOLVED` unless (DC27) is proved from the original coupled equation with constants depending only on $\nu$, $T$, and $R$. The fixed schedule cannot upgrade this classification.

Any failed exact check gives overall `FAIL`. The script must exit nonzero on `FAIL` or an inventory mismatch. Implementation failure gives `ERROR`; repairs preserve the equations, controls, and decision boundaries declared here and write a fresh evidence path.
