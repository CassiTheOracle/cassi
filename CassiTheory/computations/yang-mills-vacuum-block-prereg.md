# Pure Yang–Mills Vacuum Geometry and Exact Block Reduction

## Status: Pre-registered—September 2026

## Abstract

This investigation reformulates the regulated Yang–Mills excitation gap through its exact positive vacuum and checks the information required by a block reduction. It derives the interactions generated by a one-plaquette exponential trial vacuum and tests the associated group identities. Gaussian controls resolve the relation between conditional block gaps, global variance, configuration marginals and quantum reduced states. They are controls for the block mathematics. The interacting four-dimensional continuum theory and its mass gap remain separate proof obligations.

## 1. Target and conventions

Use the source-free $SU(2)$ Hamiltonian and normalized link Haar measure of `foundations/loop-to-bubble-projection-theorem.md` §§9.4–9.12:

$$
H=\frac{g^2}{2a}h,\qquad
h=K+2xN_p-xS,\qquad
K=-\sum_{e,A}(X_e^A)^2,\quad
S=\sum_p V_p,\quad V_p=\operatorname{Tr}U_p,\quad x=2/g^4.
$$

Here $X_e^A$ generates left multiplication by $\exp(it\sigma_A/2)$, $g,a>0$, and each elementary unoriented plaquette appears once. Full link holonomies and Gauss invariance are retained. The ordinary periodic cubic family has even side at least four. The smaller seven-link graph below is a declared operator control.

For each finite lattice let $\Omega>0$ be the normalized exact vacuum and
$d\mu=\Omega^2dU$. Derive the unitary ground-state transform on its closed
$H^1$ form domain and the exact equality

$$
\Delta_{\mathrm{phys}}=\frac{g^2}{2a}
\inf_{\substack{f\in H^1(\mu)\ \mathrm{gauge\ invariant}\\
\mu(f)=0}}
\frac{\sum_{e,A}\int|X_e^Af|^2\,d\mu}{\int|f|^2\,d\mu}.
$$

For blocks $B$ and fixed nonnegative weights $w_B$, all conditional measures
and variances below are those of the exact vacuum measure $\mu$. Suppose an
approximate tensorization bound holds for every gauge-invariant $f$ in the
form domain,

$$
\operatorname{Var}_\mu f\le A_{\mathrm{AT}}\sum_Bw_B\,
\mathbb E_\mu[\operatorname{Var}_\mu(f\mid U_{B^c})],
\qquad
\sup_e\sum_{B\ni e}w_B\le\rho,
$$

and every conditional fibre has Poincaré rate at least
$\lambda_{\mathrm{loc}}>0$ for the restricted functions that arise this way.
It suffices to require invariance under gauge transformations supported
strictly inside $B$; an all-function fibre inequality is a stronger variant.
Derive the sufficient gap bound
$g^2\lambda_{\mathrm{loc}}/(2aA_{\mathrm{AT}}\rho)$. Its continuum target is
$\lambda_{\mathrm{loc}}/(A_{\mathrm{AT}}\rho)\ge2am_*/g^2$ for a fixed
positive physical $m_*$. This target has content only along the declared
bare-coupling trajectory $g=g(a)$ and is sufficient, not necessary. A
positive gap for an isolated block with its own ground state does not imply
the required conditional inequality for $\mu$: the fibre measure, boundary
uniformity and tensorization constant are separate obligations.

## 2. Full-holonomy vacuum identities

For real $\kappa$, use the positive gauge-invariant trial function $\Phi_\kappa\propto\exp(\kappa S)$. Derive its exact dimensionless local energy

$$
\frac{h\Phi_\kappa}{\Phi_\kappa}
=2xN_p+(3\kappa-x)S-\kappa^2\sum_{e,A}(X_e^AS)^2.
$$

At a shared link orient the two $SU(2)$ plaquettes so both start with that link; their characters are invariant under orientation reversal. Denote the resulting based holonomies by $A_p,A_q$. Check

$$
\sum_A(X_e^AV_p)(X_e^AV_q)
=\tfrac14V_pV_q-\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}(A_pA_q),
\qquad
\sum_{e\in p,A}(X_e^AV_p)^2=4-V_p^2.
$$

Test the proposed full-lattice obstruction by Haar projection of the local energy onto $\chi_1(U_p)=V_p^2-1$. The proposed coefficient is $\kappa^2$. The proof must account for every neighboring-plaquette cross term and for edges outside $p$. At $\kappa=0$, the fundamental-character coefficient is $-x$. Acceptance requires a proof on the ordinary lattice, rather than extrapolation from the small graph. The conclusion concerns this trial family only.

The weak-field quadratic expansion uses $U_e=\exp(iA_e^A\sigma_A/2)$ and plaquette curl $C$. On the transverse nonzero-mode subspace,

$$
H^{(2)}=\frac1{2a}\left[g^2p^Tp+\frac1{2g^2}A^TC^TCA\right].
$$

Check its positive vacuum kernel $\sqrt{C^TC}/(\sqrt2g^2)$ and mode frequency $\sqrt\lambda/(\sqrt2a)$. A single isolated square has $\lambda=4$ and the radial singlet excitation spacing $2\sqrt2/a$, agreeing with the separate square control. State the excluded zero-mode and nonlinear-Gauss limitations. The one-plaquette exponential has a quadratic kernel proportional to $C^TC$; one scalar $\kappa$ cannot reproduce the square-root kernel on distinct positive eigenvalues.

## 4. Fixed Gaussian block controls

Use ordinary real Gaussian states as exact controls for the block identities. They supply no interacting Yang–Mills vacuum. For $N\in\{4,8,16,32,64\}$ and $m\in\{0,1/2\}$, define the positive Dirichlet matrix

$$
D_N(m)=\operatorname{tridiag}(-1,2+m^2,-1),\qquad Q=\sqrt{D_N(m)},\qquad
\psi(q)\propto e^{-q^TQq/2}.
$$

There are exactly **10 connected Gaussian rows**. Compute the primary square root by symmetric diagonalization and an independent reconstruction from the explicit discrete-sine eigenvectors and eigenvalues $m^2+4\sin^2[j\pi/(2(N+1))]$. The normalized matrix discrepancy threshold is $10^{-10}$.

For $d=\operatorname{diag}Q$, check the Gaussian global Poincaré rate
$2\lambda_{\min}(Q)$, single-coordinate conditional rates $2d_i$ and the
proposed optimal all-function conditional-variance tensorization constant

$$
A_{\mathrm{AT}}=
[\lambda_{\min}(\operatorname{diag}(d)^{-1/2}
Q\operatorname{diag}(d)^{-1/2})]^{-1}.
$$

Its all-function assertion requires a separate proof. Use the orthogonal
conditional expectations $P_i f=\mathbb E(f\mid q_{-i})$, Gaussian Wiener
chaoses and linear-function saturation; do not substitute the distinct
gradient Poincaré inequality for the conditional-variance statement. At
$m=0$, verify $1\le d_i\le\sqrt2$,
$\lambda_{\min}(Q)=2\sin[\pi/(2(N+1))]$ and
$A_{\mathrm{AT}}\ge1/\lambda_{\min}(Q)$. A claim of volume-uniform global
control from the conditional gaps alone is rejected by this explicit
sequence.

Retain the even zero-based coordinates $R=\{0,2,\ldots,N-2\}$ and eliminate
$E=R^c$. Record all blocks and the exact configuration marginal precision

$$
Q_{\mathrm{eff}}=Q_{RR}-Q_{RE}Q_{EE}^{-1}Q_{ER}.
$$

Check both the block-inverse identity
$Q_{\mathrm{eff}}^{-1}=(Q^{-1})_{RR}$ and the nontrivial discarded term
$Q_{RR}-Q_{\mathrm{eff}}=Q_{RE}Q_{EE}^{-1}Q_{ER}\succeq0$. The true reduced
quantum covariances are $C_q=(Q^{-1})_{RR}/2$ and $C_p=Q_{RR}/2$. The pure
state formed by taking the square root of the configuration marginal would
instead give $C_p^{\mathrm{pure}}=Q_{\mathrm{eff}}/2$. Record their positive
difference and the symplectic eigenvalues
$\nu_k=\tfrac12\sqrt{\lambda_k(Q_{\mathrm{eff}}^{-1}Q_{RR})}$. A connected
row must have at least one $\nu_k>1/2+10^{-6}$; all $\nu_k>1/2$ requires the
stronger condition that $Q_{RE}$ have full row rank. A separate uncoupled
control $Q=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,3,4)$ must have zero momentum difference
and all retained symplectic eigenvalues $1/2$. Matrix identities and
positivity allow normalized tolerance $10^{-10}$.

The marginal calculation is a state-reduction identity. Any claim of exact
quantum Hamiltonian blocking must additionally retain the mixed reduced
state or an energy-dependent Feshbach–Schur operator
$PHP-PHQ(QHQ-z)^{-1}QHP$. Record that the Hilbert-space projection $P$ must
respect Gauss invariance, $z$ must lie in the resolvent set of $QHQ$, and the
coupling to the discarded sector must satisfy the required form-domain
bounds. No energy-independent static marginal is assumed.
