# Volume-Adapted Local-Plaquette Feshbach Family

## Status: Pre-registered—September 2026

## Abstract

This screen tests a gauge-compatible retained family whose dimension grows
with the spatial graph. The retained space is generated by the vacuum and the
fundamental plaquette actions on the exact cutoff-one source basis. The test
uses the seven-link two-plaquette graph and the open $3\times2\times2$ graph at
four fixed couplings. It measures whether adding local plaquette sectors
removes the finite-volume growth of the coupling block seen for the constant
retained state. The schedule remains finite and supplies no volume-uniform,
lattice-spacing-uniform or continuum mass-gap theorem.

## 1. Frozen source spaces and retained family

Use the exact source and recovered large-volume Hamiltonian fixed by
`computations/verify_yang_mills_exact_block_spectrum.py` and
`computations/verify_yang_mills_su2_larger_volume_hamiltonian.py`. The outer
space is the complete gauge-invariant doubled-spin cutoff-one basis:

$$
\dim L_1=4\quad\text{on the seven-link graph},
\qquad
\dim L_1=868\quad\text{on the open }3\times2\times2\text{ graph}.
$$

Let $S$ be the diagonal Haar overlap and let $e_0$ be the normalized
coordinate of the all-trivial vacuum state. For every plaquette $p$, let
$W_p$ be the exact plaquette matrix on the outer source space and define

$$
 u_p=S^{-1/2}W_pS^{-1/2}e_0.
$$

The retained source family is the fixed span

$$
 L_R=\operatorname{span}\{e_0,u_p:p\text{ is a plaquette of the graph}\}.
 \tag{VAF1}
$$

No vector is selected after inspecting a spectrum. The source vectors are
fundamental Wilson-loop actions on the vacuum, so the family is gauge
invariant and local before the ground-state projection. The retained rank is
the numerical rank of the frozen columns in (VAF1), using singular-value
threshold $10^{-10}$ relative to the largest singular value. The rank and all
singular values are recorded for both graphs.

The schedule is

$$
 x\in\left\{\tfrac1{64},\tfrac1{16},\tfrac14,1\right\}.
$$

## 2. Projected Feshbach blocks

For each graph and coupling, form the normalized Hamiltonian
$\widehat H_{G,x}$, its lowest eigenpair $(E_0,\Omega)$, and the measured
outer gap $\Delta_{G,x}$. Project the retained family into
$\mathcal K=\Omega^\perp$ and orthonormalize it to obtain $V_P$. Define
$V_Q$ as the orthonormal complement of $\{\Omega\}\cup\operatorname{ran}V_P$.
The blocks are

$$
 A=V_P^*(\widehat H_{G,x}-E_0)V_P,
 \quad
 B=V_P^*(\widehat H_{G,x}-E_0)V_Q,
 \quad
 D=V_Q^*(\widehat H_{G,x}-E_0)V_Q.
$$

Record

$$
 \alpha=\lambda_{\min}(A),
 \qquad
 \delta_Q=\lambda_{\min}(D),
 \qquad
 \beta=\|B\|_2.
$$

At
$\lambda_{\rm test}=\frac12\min(\Delta_{G,x},\delta_Q)$, evaluate the
resolvent norm, the self-energy matrix

$$
 \Sigma(\lambda)=B(D-\lambda I)^{-1}B^*,
$$

and the bounds

$$
 \|(D-\lambda I)^{-1}\|_2\le\frac1{\delta_Q-\lambda},
 \qquad
 \|\Sigma(\lambda)\|_2\le\frac{\beta^2}{\delta_Q-\lambda}.
 \tag{VAF2}
$$

The direct Schur matrix
$A-\lambda I-\Sigma(\lambda)$ must be positive at the test energy. Use
the scalar lower form

$$
 \Phi(\lambda)=\alpha-\lambda-\frac{\beta^2}{\delta_Q-\lambda}
 \tag{VAF3}
$$

and, when $\Phi(0)>0$, record

$$
 \gamma_{\rm Fesh}=
 \frac{\alpha+\delta_Q-
 \sqrt{(\alpha-\delta_Q)^2+4\beta^2}}2.
 \tag{VAF4}
$$

The measured gap must be at least (VAF4) within $10^{-10}$ absolute
tolerance. The tail-floor domain is invalid when the smallest discarded
 eigenvalue is itself the floor; this condition is recorded rather than
inverted.

## 3. Statistic and decision tree

The primary statistic is the eight-row inventory containing, by graph and
coupling:

- retained source rank and singular values;
- post-projection ranks of $\mathcal P$ and $\mathcal Q$;
- $\alpha$, $\delta_Q$, $\beta$, the direct resolvent and self-energy norms;
- the positive-root count, $\gamma_{\rm Fesh}/\Delta_{G,x}$ and
  $\beta^2/(\alpha\delta_Q)$;
- the large-to-small ratios of $\alpha$, $\delta_Q$ and $\beta$ at each coupling.

Any source, rank, finite-matrix, residual, inequality, Schur, root, domain or
summary failure is `INCONCLUSIVE`. If every control passes and all eight rows
have a positive (VAF4) root, classify
`SUPPORTS_FINITE_VOLUME_VOLUME_ADAPTED_FAMILY`. If controls pass but one or
more roots are absent, classify `NO_POSITIVE_VOLUME_ADAPTED_FAMILY`.

The screen stops after the fixed eight-row schedule. No graph, coupling,
plaquette, vector, rank threshold or tolerance is added after inspection.
The independent checker reconstructs all scalar bounds, root formulas, ranks,
row schedule, grouped ratios and source bindings from the primary receipt
without assembling either graph matrix.

A positive result establishes a finite volume-adapted bridge only. It does not
establish a volume-uniform bound, a lattice-spacing limit, a continuum
construction or a Yang–Mills mass gap.
