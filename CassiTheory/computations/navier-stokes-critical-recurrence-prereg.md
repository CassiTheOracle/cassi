# Critical Remainder and Recurrence: Fixed Analytical Verification

## Status: Pre-registered—September 2026

## Abstract

This schedule tests the connection between strain departure and critical concentration in the original unforced Navier–Stokes equation. It checks a centered remainder estimate, exact spectral-spread dynamics, full-equation instantaneous controls and a scalar-budget obstruction. No Navier–Stokes trajectory, parameter fit or singularity search is run. The existing departure and depletion protocols remain unchanged.

## 1. Equation and conventions

The analytical statements use sufficiently regular smooth solutions on $\mathbb R^3$ with finite displayed norms, for example divergence-free Schwartz initial data, or smooth real mean-zero data on the $2\pi$ torus where explicitly stated. The fixed velocity controls use the torus and volume-normalized Parseval sums. Take
$$
B=-\mathbb P[(u\cdot\nabla)u],\quad u_t=B+\nu\Delta u,
\quad S=\nabla_{\mathrm{sym}}u,
$$
$$
K=\tfrac12\|u\|_2^2,\quad E=\|S\|_2^2,\quad G=\|\nabla S\|_2^2,
\quad \mathcal C=\|u\|_{\dot H^{1/2}}^2,\quad Y=\|u\|_{\dot H^{3/2}}^2.
$$
The full strain remainder is
$$
\mathcal R=P_{\mathrm{st}}\left((u\cdot\nabla)S+S^2/3+\omega\otimes\omega/4\right),
$$
$$
M=-\nu\Delta S+2P_{\mathrm{st}}(S^2)/3,\quad
\delta=\|\mathcal R\|_2^2-4\|M+\mathcal R/2\|_2^2,
\quad f=-3\nu G-4\langle\det S\rangle.
$$
The multiplier $P_{\mathrm{st}}$ projects onto strains of solenoidal velocities and is zero at mode zero. At $k\ne0$, for symmetric $A$,
$$
P_{\mathrm{st}}(k)A=
\frac{k\otimes(\mathbb P(k)Ak)+(\mathbb P(k)Ak)\otimes k}{|k|^2}.
$$
Every mode generated by convection or differentiation is retained. No convolution is restricted to the initial support.

## 2. Fixed algebraic targets

Define
$$
\mathcal V=KE-\mathcal C^2/4,\quad
W=\langle\Lambda^{-1/2}S,\Lambda^{-1/2}\mathcal R\rangle,
\quad A=\langle\Lambda^2u,B\rangle,\quad F=\langle\Lambda u,B\rangle.
$$
Check the following identities and inequalities, with positive $E$:

1. Optimal scalar centering gives $\|(\Lambda^{-1}-a)S\|_2^2=K-a\mathcal C+a^2E$, minimized at $a=\mathcal C/(2E)$; $\langle S,\mathcal R\rangle=0$ therefore implies $W^2\le(\mathcal V/E)\|\mathcal R\|_2^2$.
2. $\mathcal V\ge0$ and
   $$
   \mathcal V'+\nu(2KG+2E^2-\mathcal C Y)=KA-\mathcal C F.
   $$
   The heat dissipation is a nonnegative double spectral sum. Verify its factors using moment symbols and two independent frequency variables.
3. $E'=f+\nu G$, $K'=-2\nu E$, $\mathcal C'=2F-2\nu Y$, and $f'=-3\delta/2$ with the complete projected remainder.
4. For $J=\langle\Lambda^{-1}S,P_{\mathrm{st}}(S^2)\rangle$, verify $2F=-8J/3-4W$. Both pieces remain in the critical budget.
5. Under Euclidean Navier–Stokes scaling, $(K,E,G,\mathcal C,Y,\mathcal V,W,\|\mathcal R\|_2^2)$ have exponents $(-1,1,3,0,2,0,2,5)$. The centered estimate has matching exponent four after squaring $W$.

The infinite-dimensional implications require the accompanying analytical proof and independent review; finite symbolic controls do not establish a new general regularity theorem.

## 3. Fixed original-equation controls

Use three real finite-Fourier fields:

- The cyclic field $v=(\sin y,\sin z,\sin x)$.
- The three-coordinate fixture from `computations/verify_navier_stokes_transfer.py`, with positive coefficients $v_{(1,0,0)}=i(0,1,1)$, $v_{(0,2,0)}=i(1,0,0)$, $v_{(1,2,0)}=i(-2,1,0)$ and $v_{(0,0,3)}=i(1,0,0)$.
- The planar fixture from that source, with $v_{(1,0,0)}=i(0,1,0)$ and $v_{(0,2,0)}=i(1,0,0)$.

Negative modes are the conjugates. For each fixture compute exact formulas at $u=av$ and retain the amplitude/viscosity polynomials. The fixed numerical rows are $a\in\{-4,-1,1,4\}$ and $\nu\in\{1/100,1\}$, giving 24 rows.

For every row record $K,E,G,\mathcal C,Y,A,F,f,f',\delta,\|\mathcal R\|_2^2,W,\mathcal V,\mathcal V',\mathcal C'$ and the centered-estimate slack. Compute $f'$ independently from the moment derivatives along $u_t=B+\nu\Delta u$ and from the squared remainder identity. Record whether $\delta>0$ coexists with $\mathcal C'>0$ and whether $f<0$ coexists with $\mathcal C'>0$. These are instantaneous signs on actual admissible initial velocity fields; no subsequent trajectory is inferred beyond smooth local continuity for strict signs.

For the cyclic field, calculate $\mathcal V(0)$, $\mathcal V'(0)$ and $\mathcal V''(0)$ exactly along the full equation. Differentiate with $u_{tt}=B(u_t,u)+B(u,u_t)+\nu\Delta u_t$. Test whether $\mathcal V''(0)>0$ at every fixed nonzero amplitude and both viscosities. The analogous strain-remainder $W'(0)$ may be recorded from the same fixed directional derivative; it receives no universal-sign claim.

## 5. Fixed scalar-budget control

This control is a collection of scalar functions and spectral moments. It is **not** a Navier–Stokes velocity trajectory. On $0\le t<1$, put $\tau=1-t$ and $\nu=1$:
$$
K=8\tau^{1/4},\quad E=\tau^{-3/4},\quad G=\tfrac32\tau^{-7/4},
\quad f=-\tfrac34\tau^{-7/4},\quad\delta=\tfrac78\tau^{-11/4}.
$$
Check $K'=-2E$, $E'=f+G$, $f'=-3\delta/2$, $E^2\le KG$, $f<0$, $\delta>0$, $KE\to\infty$ and $\int_0^1E\,dt<\infty$.

To keep the critical moments mutually compatible, use a positive two-atom spectral measure of total mass $2K$, weights $44/45,1/45$, and frequency radii $r_1=\tau^{-1/2}/4$, $r_2=\sqrt{46}\,\tau^{-1/2}/4$. Verify its zeroth, second and fourth moments equal $2K,2E,2G$. Set $\mathcal C$ and $Y$ equal to its first and third moments, and $F=(\mathcal C'+2Y)/2$. Check $\mathcal C\to\infty$, the critical budget and the interpolation inequalities. Finally evaluate whether $\int_0^1(G-E^2/K)^{2/3}\,dt$ is finite or infinite. Atomic spectral moments do not construct an $L^2(\mathbb R^3)$ velocity or meet the original nonlinear PDE compatibility condition.

No free exponent, time profile or parameter is fitted after execution. These checks address closure from the listed scalar identities only.

## 6. Qualification and stopping rule

- An exact failure, nonfinite value or discrepancy at or above $10^{-10}$ makes the affected scientific classification **INCONCLUSIVE** and prevents adoption.
- A qualified positive cyclic $\mathcal V''$ receives **CONTRADICTS** for universal preservation of initially zero spectral spread. It does not refute a conditional spectral-concentration regularity theorem.
- A qualified row with $\delta>0$ and $\mathcal C'>0$ receives **CONTRADICTS** for the universal instantaneous implication that departure makes the critical norm nonincreasing. No qualifying row is **INCONCLUSIVE** about that universal implication.
- A qualified scalar profile with divergent critical moment receives **CONTRADICTS** for closure from only the listed scalar budgets and positive departure; it is not a fluid singularity.
- Data-controlled critical production, recurrence control and arbitrary-data regularity remain **UNRESOLVED** unless a separately checked analytical proof establishes them.

Execute this fixed schedule once after source freeze. An implementation defect may be repaired with every failed receipt retained, the protocol unchanged and a fresh output path. No extra fixtures, phase search, amplitudes, viscosities, resolutions, time evolution or fitted criterion may be added. Stop after the fixed controls and independent analytical reconciliation.
