## Abstract

This directory collects the principles that govern how $\varphi$ enters the
framework across every sector. `de-resonance-principle.md` records the Derived
Hurwitz/continued-fraction identity, a Derived topological winding boundary,
and a conditional counterflow theorem that transfers the declared density

remains unexplained, the residual between the raw measured
$v_0/M_{\rm Pl}$ exponent and the nearest integer $\varphi^{-80}$, while
keeping the separate gap-adjusted cascade placement explicit. The documents
are ordered alphabetically, which is also the reading path: the general
principle first, then its hardest application.

### `de-resonance-principle.md`—The De-Resonance Principle in Cassi

The principle's arithmetic basis is the Derived worst-approximability of
$\varphi$ under its continued fraction $[1;1,1,1,\ldots]$. Given the declared
density target and explicit compact-current closure, the conditional
counterflow theorem selects $\theta_I'/\theta_Y'\to\varphi$; the frozen PC1–PC7
receipt verifies its algebra and controls. A nonzero scalar loop amplitude
conserves integer winding exactly, so compact-sector tracking requires phase
slips or altered topology. The compact phases, current closure, phase-slip
dynamics, and physical resonance suppression remain Hypothesized. The

$\sin^2\theta_W$ compares the $\varphi^{-3}$ baseline with the measured

### `v0-hierarchy-problem.md`—$v_0/M_{\rm Pl}$: The Electroweak Hierarchy Gap

An open-problem document facing the claim $v_0/M_{\rm Pl}\approx\varphi^{-80}$
directly: the empirical match remains an underived comparison, and the
framework does not compute the residual. It shows the Cassi potential's
$\varphi$-attractor term leaves the equilibrium $v_0^2=-\mu^2/g$ independent
of $\lambda$, so the VEV is set by Standard Model parameters that $\varphi$
does not determine. Four candidate sources for the correction are
examined—RGE running of $v_0$ (sign analysis shows the SM running moves the
wrong way), threshold corrections at the matching scale (plausible,
uncomputed), the nearest-integer-power approximation (raw exponent
$N_{\rm raw}\approx79.89$, while the gap-adjusted convention gives
$N_g\approx79.7$), and the Bohm quantum potential (negligible at the listed
scale, $\sim10^{-34}$). None is computed as a correction in the Cassi
framework. The document leaves the residual open and records the candidate
mechanisms as conditional, not as a completed correction.
