## 1. The two ingredients

### 1.1 Organized annihilation (Hypothesized conditional mechanism)

The coherence-budget product supplies conditional algebra for the organized

Only the residual Yang excess—particles without antimatter partners—survived.

### 1.2 The Yang-Yin imbalance at freeze-out (Derived conditional on declared gap input and annihilation map)

The Wu Xing five-element structure sets the initial Yang-Yin gap:

$$g = \frac{|E_{Y,0} - E_{I,0}|}{\rho_0} = 1 - \varphi^{-5} \approx 0.9098$$

This gives the initial ratio $r_0 = E_Y/E_I \approx 0.047$—Yin-dominated by

progressively transferred energy from Yin to Yang. At the GUT seed
$n_{\text{GUT}}\approx13.3$, the ratio had reached
$r_{\text{GUT}} \approx 0.3$–$0.5$.

Under the conditional organized-annihilation map, the Yang excess at
freeze-out is the fraction of Yang that was **unpaired** with Yin-equivalent
antimatter. Since the fields were created with the Wu Xing gap, the paired
fraction $(E_Y + E_I)$ is subject to organized annihilation, but the
**difference** $E_Y - E_I$ survives:

$$\eta_{\text{GUT}} = \frac{E_{Y,\text{GUT}} - E_{I,\text{GUT}}}{E_{Y,\text{GUT}} + E_{I,\text{GUT}}} = \frac{r_{\text{GUT}} - 1}{r_{\text{GUT}} + 1}$$

At $r_{\text{GUT}} \approx 0.4$: $\eta_{\text{GUT}} \approx -0.43$—
negative because Yin dominates, but the absolute Yang excess $|E_Y - E_I|$
is 43% of the total density at GUT. Under the conditional map, the paired
matter-antimatter component in the $(E_Y + E_I)$ channel is removed, leaving:

$$\eta_{\text{unpaired}} \approx \frac{1}{2} \cdot |\eta_{\text{GUT}}| \approx 0.21$$

This is the fraction of energy in the **unpaired** Yang-Yin difference. Under
the same conditional branch assignment, only one sign (Yang) produces stable
matter after cascade freeze-out; the Yin excess condenses into the Qi field as
dark energy. The matter yield is:

$$\eta_{\text{matter}} \approx \frac{1}{2} \cdot |\eta_{\text{GUT}}| \cdot f_{\text{Yang}}$$

where $f_{\text{Yang}} \approx \varphi^{-3} \approx 0.236$ is an independent
phenomenological factor in this GUT-scale estimate. At the

$E_Y/(E_Y+E_I)=\varphi/(1+\varphi)=\varphi^{-1}\approx0.618$. For the

$\varphi^{-3}$ factor in the estimate is a declared phenomenological input,

$$\eta_{\text{matter}} \approx 0.21 \times 0.236 \approx 0.05 \approx \varphi^{-6.2}$$

### 4.2 Separate unit-density normalized gate ansatz

This closure attempt defines a dimensionless ratio-space gate

$$
\bar q(r)=\frac{1}{1+\varphi^{-2}+\bar\varepsilon^2},
\qquad
\bar\varepsilon=\frac{|r-\varphi|}{1+r}.
$$

It follows by setting the canonical fields to
$E_Y=r/(1+r)$ and $E_I=1/(1+r)$, fixing $\rho=E_Y+E_I=1$, and normalizing the
imbalance by $\rho$. It is therefore a separate Hypothesized ansatz, not an
identity for the canonical
$q=(E_Y^2+E_I^2)/(E_Y^2+E_I^2+\varepsilon_{\rm raw}^2)$ at arbitrary density.
In particular, its attractor floor is
$1-\bar q(\varphi)=1/(1+\varphi^2)\approx0.276$; the canonical gate has no
universal floor because its value also depends on $\rho$.

The signal map declares uniform per-rung dilution efficient while
$(1-\bar q)\geq\varphi^{-1}$. Solving that chosen threshold gives

$$\frac{\varphi^{-2}+\bar\varepsilon^2}
        {1+\varphi^{-2}+\bar\varepsilon^2}=\varphi^{-1},$$

$$\bar\varepsilon^2(1-\varphi^{-1})
  =\varphi^{-1}+\varphi^{-3}-\varphi^{-2},$$

$$\bar\varepsilon^2
  =\varphi^2(\varphi^{-1}+\varphi^{-3}-\varphi^{-2})
  =\varphi+\varphi^{-1}-1
  \approx1.236,$$

so $\bar\varepsilon\approx1.112$ and

$$
r=\frac{\varphi-\bar\varepsilon}{1+\bar\varepsilon}
  \approx0.240.
$$

The optional Wu Xing phase assignment places $r\approx0.240$ at the
Earth-to-Metal boundary, approximately step 40. That step assignment is not
derived from the normalized gate or from the canonical PDE. The active-channel

$\lambda=0.1$ is a solver normalization/timescale convention, while
$\lambda=1/(2w)$ with $w=5$ remains a Hypothesized Wu Xing linkage requiring
independent cycle-time and dynamical closure. The solver value cancels from
$\Gamma=H$, leaving

$$
\frac{\varphi^{-2}+\bar\varepsilon^2}
     {1+\varphi^{-2}+\bar\varepsilon^2}
=\frac13\left[\varphi^{-2}+\frac{(\varphi-r)(1+r)}{r}\right],
\qquad
\bar\varepsilon=\frac{\varphi-r}{1+r}.
$$

The equation has a unique root $r_f = 1.3494886$ (a quartic over
$\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$; $\log_\varphi r_f = 0.6229$—not a
$\varphi$-power), with $\Gamma/H = 1$ by construction. The route does not
close, for a reason distinct from §4.5(a)–(e): **the crossing is a thaw,
not a freeze.** $\Gamma/H$ rises monotonically along the ratio trajectory,
$0.062$ at $r_0 = 0.047$ → $0.29$–$0.39$ at the GUT seed
($r_{\text{GUT}} \approx 0.3$–$0.5$) → $1.000$ at $r_f$ → $2.17$ at the
attractor. The conversion rate is *sub-critical relative to expansion*
throughout the seeded epoch and super-critical thereafter, so no
$\Gamma = H$ freeze-out exists for the dilution of the seeded asymmetry.
Reading the crossing as the dilution endpoint anyway, the homogeneous rung
span from the seed is $N = 2.48$–$3.20$. Its unit-normalized dilution factor
$\xi_{\text{dilution}}=\varphi^{-N}\approx0.2$–$0.3$, roughly
$5\times10^{8}$× the observed value when read as an asymmetry with unit
normalization, is 15–18 rungs short of the required 44.09–44.13, consistent
with the known homogeneous-depth deficit ($N_{\text{total}} \approx 9$, §6
item 2).
The radiation-era cross-check compares
$\Gamma=\lambda(1-\bar q)$ with
$H_{\text{rad}}(n)=1.66\sqrt{g_*}\,E(n)^2/M_{\text{Pl}}$, using
$E(n)=M_{\text{Pl}}\varphi^{-n}$ and the solver normalization
$c=\lambda\ell_{\text{Pl}}/\tau_{\text{PDE}}$ (with $\tau_{\text{PDE}}$ the calibrated PDE-to-physical-time conversion). It places the crossing at
$n\approx3.3$–$6.7$, before the GUT seed ($n=13.33$), with
$\Gamma/H\approx9\times10^2$ at the seed and
$\approx2\times10^{21}$ at the proposed endpoint
($n\approx57.46$). Within this normalization family, changing $\lambda$ over
the tested range does not move the crossing past the seed.

**Result.** The rate-based condition selects no post-seed dilution endpoint
within the declared homogeneous ansatz; the 44-rung span remains the ledgered
fit (`parameter-inventory.md` §10 row 481). Its unique
$\Gamma/H=1$ crossing at $r_f=1.3495$ is a thaw, and the radiation-era
crossing is pre-seed. This negative result does not establish the behavior of
the canonical PDE at arbitrary density because $\bar q(r)$ fixed the
unit-density normalization. A canonical rate test would need evolved
$E_Y$, $E_I$, and $\varepsilon_{\rm raw}$ together with a derived
ratio-to-step map.

### Open—requires the full 3D PDE (structure formation / wake-wave dynamics)

The following gaps prevent promotion of the conditional mechanism to a fully
Derived physical account:

1. **Dimensional cascade depth.** The freeze-out coordinate 52 and the
   optional pinch assignment at step 60 are positions within the 292-step
   dimensional cascade ($N = \log_\varphi [R_H / \ell_{\text{Pl}}]$). The
   GUT seed (n ≈ 13.3) is pinned by the dimensional ratio; assigning the
   threshold $r=\varphi^{-1}$ to step 60 uses the separate Hypothesized
   coordinate map. The physical freeze-out step and span remain unclosed.

2. **Homogeneous ODE insufficient.** The homogeneous two-fluid ODE (no spatial
   structure) gives only $N\approx9$ $\varphi$-steps for the full ratio
   evolution from $r_0$ to $\varphi$
   (`computations/cascade_depth_integral.py`). A spatial wake-wave extension
   has been proposed to slow the ratio evolution, but no derived calculation
   shows that it supplies the missing depth. A full spatial PDE probe would
   need a pre-registered ratio-to-rung observable and null before testing
   whether additional depth emerges. The closure sweep (§4.5) isolates the
   current blocker: the conditional
   $(1-\bar q)=\varphi^{-1}$ crossing at $r\approx0.240$ has no derived
   $r\to$ step map, so it pins no freeze-out rung.

3. **Sphaleron freeze-out temperature.** The Standard Model sphaleron
   freeze-out at $T \sim 100$ GeV (cascade step 80) is distinct from the
   dilution freeze-out. The Cassi framework's hierarchical gauge coupling
   running may shift the sphaleron rate, but the relationship between the two
   freeze-out processes has not been derived from the PDE.

4. **Exact photon-production cascade profile.** Determining which specific
   rungs between the GUT seed and the freeze-out contribute maximally to dilution
   (versus which are dark in photon production) requires the full $w(a)$
   evolution through the radiation era with the Qi gate profile integrated over
   the ratio trajectory. The current 44-rung even-dilution model
   ($\varphi^{-1}$ per rung) is a simplifying approximation: the per-rung
   dilution likely varies across the Wu Xing phases as each optional channel
   opens and closes.

5. **Dilution-span endpoints.** The span's endpoints are not fixed by any
   mechanism (§4.5, §4.7). The seed is pinned at $n\approx13.3$, but the
   freeze-out rung is not. The normalized-gate crossing at $r\approx0.240$
   has no derived $r\to$ step map, and its floor
   $1/(1+\varphi^2)=0.276$ prevents a
   $(1-\bar q)\to0$ freeze-out inside that ansatz. The endpoint that would
   close the fit, $n\approx57.43$–$57.46$ with
   $E\approx1.3\times10^7$ GeV, is an empty desert scale. The homogeneous
   $\Gamma=H$ calculation adds no endpoint: its unique crossing at
   $r_f=1.3495$ is a thaw, and the radiation-era crossing is pre-seed
   (§4.7). The closest mechanism-anchored span,
   $60-13.3=46.7$, misses the exact log 44.126 by 5.8%; the
   unit-normalized dilution factor is 3.4 times low when read as an asymmetry.
