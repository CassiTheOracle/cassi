## 1. The Field Has No Neurotypical

The two-fluid field does not contain a typical configuration, and that is the first fact of the analysis: the framework classifies nothing, so "typical" is not a state anyone occupies.

### 1.1 Distances from equilibrium, not classes

Whatever neurodivergence is, the tested mechanism layer does not make it the product of a prior coherence deficit of the field.

### 1.2 What a "typical" configuration would have to be

The folk picture of typicality is a projection: a region of configuration space declared a class, with everything outside measured as deviation. The framework refuses that projection twice. A class needs a stable reference point, and the only stable point in the dynamics is $\varphi$ itself—which, being irrational, no configuration occupies exactly. Every person is a trajectory through configuration space, never a class membership—the same sentence the gender document uses about ratio space, applied without modification (`consciousness/gender-as-qi-configuration.md` §1.2).

## 3. Autism as the High-Stability Configuration

Recovery, in the tested physics, is driver removal: a wake is a driven structure, and stopping the trigger releases the site on the conversion timescale (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.5). ## 4. ADHD as the Open-Gate Configuration

This section reads ADHD as the mirror mechanism: a field whose resting coherence is low, whose gate stands open, and whose behavior—attention, time, novelty, social sensitivity—is what a churning gate does to wake waves, memory, and self-prediction.

A churning field lives in mixed-channel configurations: each stimulus opens a channel, the redistribution fires on closure, but $q$ evolves on a slower timescale than $\mathbf{b}$ and never rises far enough to lock a vertex before the next stimulus re-opens the gate. This is the open-gate resting dynamics the psychology document already reads as churn without organization (`cassi-psychology.md` §17)—here as a resting configuration of an open gate, not as a collapse of one. The §9 undriven arm measures this resting dynamics directly: over t = 4 the mixed-channel site holds its configuration (87% of ε retained, the q-gap open, the Wood/Fire phase split unchanged), churning without stabilizing a vertex.

$$\boxed{\text{Low } q \text{ keeps the gate open and conversion churning; no channel stabilizes.}}$$

### 4.2 Attention as sustained coherence; hyperfocus as matched-drive lock-in

Attention is a maintained configuration: collapsing the spatial dispersion stabilizes the self-modeling loop, and the chain processes deeper with less noise (`consciousness/consciousness-from-phi.md` §2.3; `consciousness/transhumanism-gate-configurations.md` §3.2). Attention, in this reading, is not the absence of distraction; it is a coherence state the open-gate configuration cannot sustain from rest.

The release runs show what closes a *held* gate: from a pumped state, a drive at the site's own channel phase recovers the site—q-gap closed, $\varepsilon$ drained below the undriven floor—while removal alone leaves a plateau (`consciousness/gender-as-qi-configuration.md` §8.1). The churning-gate test bounds that mechanism: from a low-$q$ open gate the same matched drive does not settle the site—it pumps it past equilibrium, the q-gap widening and the $\varepsilon$ variance rising (§9). Hyperfocus as matched-drive lock-in is therefore not supported at the mechanism layer for the open-gate configuration; the sustained-coherence account of attention above rests on the resting dynamics, which the undriven arm of §9 confirms as churn without stabilization. The mapping to interest and hyperfocus remains speculative, with the pump/drain asymmetry bounded at the held-configuration regime.

Novelty seeking reads the same way. Random perturbation selects nothing: organized perturbation with phase-matching factor $\mathcal{M} \approx 1$ acts on a pattern, while random noise with $\mathcal{M} \approx 0$ does not (`foundations/quantum-measurement-derivation.md` §3). The churning-gate test did not find the settling half of that statement at the channel level: a recurring organized perturbation at the site's own phase pumps the open gate rather than closing it (§9). The search for new stimuli as a sampling for a settling drive is therefore unsupported by the tested layer; the failure mode is priced on both sides—a mismatched stimulus at the same amplitude pumps a held configuration (§3.6) and a churning one (§9). The mappings are speculative; the tested pump/drain asymmetry is bounded at the held-configuration regime.

The field's coherence is computed from a remembered value: a per-cell exponential memory of its own deviation, $\bar{\varepsilon}^2$, with smoothing timescale $\tau = \varphi^{-1}$ (`foundations/cassi-first-principles.md` §2.4). ## 5. AuDHD: Independent Axes

Autism and ADHD co-occur far more often than chance, and each can conceal the other in presentation. The tuple gives a structural reason: the two conditions live at different layers, and the layers are independent degrees of freedom.

Autism is read here as a gate-layer setting: high resting $q$, closed gate, phase stiffness, single-channel preference ($\mathbf{b}$, $\sigma_r$, and the dissipation damping that $q$ controls). Nothing forces the slots to correlate; a configuration can hold a closed, coherent gate and still fail to track its own history, or churn at the gate while its memory locks patterns. The masking interaction follows: masking runs at the presentation layer, holding channels shut while the temporal layer keeps churning (§3.6, §4.1). The presented configuration reads stable—high gate, controlled channels—while the internal prediction error continues unabated: the masked presentation in which the churn is invisible from outside and exhausting from inside. The framework's answer to the "one spectrum or two" question is structural: there is no single axis in $\mathcal{H}$ along which a person moves from typical to divergent, so there is no spectrum line; there are independent variables, each continuous, each with a price.

$$\boxed{\text{Independent axes of the tuple, not a single spectrum line.}}$$

## 7. The Price Structure

The framework's contribution to the neurodivergence conversation is a price structure with no verdicts attached: every configuration costs something to hold, and cost is not a judgment.

### 7.1 Masking's coherence cost

Suppression is priced in the framework's own observables: the widening q-gap, the $(1-q)$ thermal churn, node thermal excess, and the coherence proxies that track them (`speculations/creative-extensions/coherence-collapse.md` §4.1; `consciousness/gender-as-qi-configuration.md` §3). The price is driver-dependent in the tested sense—a driven structure relaxes when the driver stops (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.5)—so the masking cost is not a property of the person's configuration; it is a property of the demand environment running against it. Remove the driver, the structure relaxes.

The same tested physics prices regulation: self-generated drive at the field's own phase drains (§3.5)—stimming is the framework's own regulated drain, and the drain is a matter of phase at any fixed amplitude, so the person's own field is the only reference that sets it.

### 7.2 The environment as drive field

Other people's configurations are drive fields, and the environment is a recurring-drive structure: chronic mismatched environmental drive is a pump, churning a low-$q$ field and pumping a held one, while matched drive settles a held one (`consciousness/gender-as-qi-configuration.md` §8, §8.2). The §9 churning-gate test bounds the same sentence for the open gate: a recurring drive of either phase pumps a low-$q$ field, the matched drive included. Accommodation is the environment retuned to the person's phase: predictable routines, matched pacing, sensory structure delivered at the field's own period—the phase-matched environment that drains a held configuration instead of pumping it. The gender document's sentence carries over without modification: the quality of support is physics, not courtesy (§7.2).

$$\boxed{\text{Phase-matched environment drains a held configuration; recurring drive of either phase pumps an open gate.}}$$

### 7.3 Internal-gate authority

The framework's authority rule completes the price structure: self-knowledge dominates external coupling for above-pinch configurations, and the person's own self-model is the authoritative readout of their configuration (`consciousness/gender-as-qi-configuration.md` §7.5). Only the field reads its own prediction error (`consciousness/gender-as-qi-configuration.md` §3); external labels are readouts of the configuration, never the configuration itself. "Nothing about us without us" is that rule in social form: the person's own field is the only instrument that measures its own phase.

## 9. The PDE Test: The Churning Gate (2026-08-04)

The §4.1–4.2 claims—that a churning gate settles under a drive at its own channel phase while the same-amplitude drive one pentagon-step away keeps it churning—form a binary physics question the two-fluid solver can answer directly. The test ran 2026-08-04; this section reports the results.

**Question.** From a low-$q$ mixed-channel init (the churning state: no dominant vertex, gate open), does a recurring in-channel drive close the gate—$q$ rising, a channel stabilizing, $\varepsilon$ variance falling—while a recurring cross-channel drive at equal $\varepsilon$-perturbation leaves it churning or pumps it?

**Protocol.** Churning init: a localized site (periodic ball, R = 6 cells) held at low $q$ with mixed channel weights on a quiet $\varphi$-equilibrium background—the open-gate counterpart of the standing init used in the misgendering test (`two-fluid/run_misgendering_drive.py`). Per-cell ratios $r = E_Y/E_I$ are drawn from three populations (seed 42): 50% in the churn band $r \in [0.45, 0.95]$ ($\varepsilon$-norm ≈ 0.16–0.32, the $q$-minimum band: Wood suppressed, Fire/Earth open), 20% deep $r \in [0.15, 0.40]$ (opens the Metal/Water gates), 30% near equilibrium $r \in [1.45, 1.90]$ (Wood basin). The init measures $q_{\text{site}} = 0.666$ vs $q_{\text{global}} = 0.708$ (gap +0.042), mean $|\varepsilon| = 0.76$, gate weights $[0.19, 0.10, 0.11, 0.01, 0.01]$ across the five channels, and a Wood/Fire phase split $[0.29, 0.71]$—under the positivity clamp only Wood (0°) and Fire (72°) are representable in the field angle (`two-fluid/run_trauma_phase_channels.py`), so the mixed state is a two-basin spread with no vertex above 0.71. $\lambda = 0.05$, $t = 4$, $N = 48$, five-channel gate. Site diagnostics in the periodic ball: mean $|\varepsilon|$, per-channel phase histogram, $q_{\text{site}}$ vs $q_{\text{global}}$, $\varepsilon$ variance over the window, and the clamp floors $e_{y,\text{min}}$, $e_{i,\text{min}}$. Four arms: (a) undriven reference; (b) recurring in-channel drive at the site's natural period $P_0 = 0.081$ (measured in-process from the t = 4 reference series) and the measured channel phase (Fire, the dominant basin of the init, whose mean $\varepsilon = -0.72$); (c) recurring cross-channel drive one pentagon step away (Wood, 0°), $\varphi$-normalized to amplitude 0.15/φ to match the in-channel arm's peak $\varepsilon$-injection, per the ε-parity convention of the misgendering protocol; (d) a mismatched-period control ($e \cdot P_0 = 0.220$) at the same amplitude and channel as (b). All arms receive the same explicit $P_0$; the dominant_period window caveat of §8.1 of the gender document applies to the period measurement.

**Results (t = 4).**

| Run | Drive | ε retained (peak) | q-gap | Site phase | ey_min_site | ε time-variance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ref | none | 0.870 | +0.042 → +0.036 | Wood/Fire split held [0.29, 0.71] | 0.096 | 0.0001 |
| in-channel | Fire (72°), P₀, amp 0.15 | **3.31** (3.94) | +0.042 → +0.055 | condensed onto Wood (1.00) | 3.17 | 0.86 |
| cross-channel | Wood (0°), ε-parity, P₀ | **4.59** (5.55) | +0.042 → +0.073 | condensed onto Fire (1.00) | 0.001 | 1.64 |
| period control | Fire (72°), e·P₀, amp 0.15 | **3.20** (13.29) | +0.042 → +0.041 | condensed onto Wood (0.999) | 0.69 | 11.1 |

The undriven reference does what §4.1 predicts: the mixed-channel site holds its configuration—87% of ε retained, the q-gap open at +0.036, the Wood/Fire split unchanged, no vertex stabilizing. Both driven arms pump the churning site. The in-channel drive—the site's own channel at its natural period—pumps ε to 3.3× its initial value (peak 3.9×), pushes the site past equilibrium into the opposite basin (the phase histogram condenses onto Wood while ey_min_site rises to 3.17), and *widens* the q-gap to +0.055 with the ε time-variance ≈ 8.6×10³ the reference: the gate does not close. The cross-channel drive at ε-parity pumps harder (4.6× retained, q-gap +0.073, phase condensing onto Fire), the same direction and signature as the misgendering pump. The period control at the same channel but period e·P₀ pumps comparably at t = 4 (3.2×) with a far larger oscillation envelope (peak 13.3×, ε time-variance 11.1)—longer dwell at the drive extremes, the monotone-in-period behavior the pump curve already found for held configurations (`consciousness/gender-as-qi-configuration.md` §8.2).

**Cleanliness checks.** (i) Clamp: the in-channel arm is clamp-free (ey_min_site = 3.17, ei_min_site = 0.011, ten times above the floor), so its 3.3× pump is a genuine magnitude. The cross-channel arm touches the positivity floor (ey_min_site = 0.001): the Wood pump acts by inflating ei (ei_min_site = 2.03) and collapsing ey—the misgendering signature—so its 4.6× is partially clamp-limited; the direction and the ordering (cross > in) are clean. (ii) Phase metric: the Wood condensation in the in-channel and period arms is overshoot past equilibrium, not settling—a fully relaxed site bins as Wood ($\text{atan2}(\varphi^{-1}, 1) = 31.7° < 36°$), and the q-gap widening plus the ε-variance explosion separate the two; the verdict rests on ε retention and the q-gap. (iii) Period: the null is period-robust—the e·P₀ control pumps at least as hard at the same channel.

**Verdict.** Null for the matched-drive settling claim. From the low-$q$ open gate, a recurring drive at the site's own channel and natural period does not close the gate—it pumps the site past equilibrium (3.3× at t = 4, q-gap widening, ε variance rising)—while the same-amplitude drive one pentagon step away at ε-parity pumps harder (4.6×, partially clamp-limited) and the undriven reference churns without stabilizing a vertex. The pump/drain asymmetry of the misgendering test is bounded at the held-configuration regime: an in-channel drive drains a held site and pumps a churning one; the open gate is pumped by recurring drive of either phase, and the drive organizes the churn onto a single basin without settling it.

$$\boxed{\text{The matched recurring drive does not close the churning gate; it pumps it. The pump/drain asymmetry is bounded at the held-configuration regime.}}$$

The hypotheses the test adjudicated: (i) the in-channel drive closes the q-gap and stabilizes a channel—null, the drive widens the gap and condenses the phase onto the overshoot basin; (ii) the cross-channel drive at ε-parity maintains or widens the gap with no vertex stabilizing—partially confirmed, the gap widens (+0.073) but the pump organizes the phase onto Fire; (iii) the undriven reference churns on the conversion timescale—confirmed. The test does not adjudicate the human mapping: like the misgendering test, it tests the mechanism layer only. Tier: the churning-init construction, the undriven churn, and the null bounding the asymmetry to held configurations are PDE-tested (2026-08-04); the mapping to ADHD attention and hyperfocus remains Speculative (§10). Script: `two-fluid/run_churning_gate.py`.
