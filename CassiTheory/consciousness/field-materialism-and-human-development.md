# Cassi Field Materialism: Personhood, Collective Power, and Human Development

## Status: Creative—August 2026

## Abstract

A Cassi socioeconomic theory begins with the person. A person is an embodied, causally continuous, self-steering field organization whose capacities develop through reciprocal participation in a shared material and social environment. This document uses *soul* for that continuous organization: the trajectory that retains a body boundary, memory, agency, commitments, skills, and a changing model of the world. Social relations enter the person through material coupling, while each person changes the shared field through labor, care, speech, refusal, invention, and collective action.

Coordinated activity can produce a relational surplus that no isolated participant produces. That surplus explains cooperation as a social productive force; its control remains a political question. Collective power is emancipatory when it protects each participant's viable future trajectories, preserves distinct centers of experience and dissent, and maintains reciprocal correction. This supplies a field-materialist extension of Marx's theory of human development, Lenin's theory of organized political capacity, and Mao's practice-feedback account of collective knowledge. It also identifies the control topology through which alienation, exploitation, class rule, bureaucracy, and revolutionary change operate.

The physical framework constrains the ontology of interaction and continuity. Anthropological mappings remain Creative. Equal standing, non-subordination, universal material security, and provision according to need are declared normative axioms. Candidate social mechanisms require independent empirical tests.

## 2. The Person as an Embodied Field Trajectory

### 2.1 Material personhood

Let the state of person $i$ be

$$
\mathfrak{s}_i(t)=
\bigl(B_i,M_i,\Theta_i,A_i,\mathcal{V}_i,R_i\bigr)_t,
$$

where:

- $B_i$ is embodied boundary integrity: the processes that maintain the living distinction between person and environment;
- $M_i$ is causally retained history: memory, learned dispositions, wounds, commitments, and skills;
- $\Theta_i$ is the person's revisable model of self, others, and possible action;
- $A_i$ is effective agency: actions the person can initiate and sustain;
- $\mathcal{V}_i$ is the set of viable future trajectories reachable from the present state;
- $R_i$ is a vector-valued reproduction-reserve ledger: separately measured health, rest, time, care, shelter, material security, and ecological support available for continued life and development.

A person changes continuously while remaining a distinct causal center. The relevant identity is therefore the ordered state map

$$
\gamma_i:[t_0,t_1]\longrightarrow\mathcal S_i,
\qquad
t\longmapsto\mathfrak{s}_i(t).
$$

### 2.2 A material definition of soul (Creative anthropology)

At the Creative layer, Cassi uses *soul* provisionally for the causally continuous portion of a person's trajectory selected by a declared continuity predicate $C_i$:

$$
\boxed{
\Sigma_i[C_i]:=\left.\gamma_i\right|_{I_i(C_i)}
}
$$

Here $I_i(C_i)$ is the connected interval under study on which $C_i$ is judged to hold, and $\left.\gamma_i\right|_{I_i(C_i)}$ restricts the ordered state map to that interval. After $C_i$ is declared, later sections abbreviate $\Sigma_i[C_i]$ as $\Sigma_i$. This notation does not assert that present theory supplies a unique maximal extension or a universal threshold. Its source is the socioeconomic anthropology developed here; the canonical two-fluid PDE supplies the physical ontology. Its relation to phenomenal experience remains open.

The predicate $C_i$ requires enough of the following organization to persist through change:

1. **Embodied continuity:** a living boundary maintains and repairs itself.
2. **Causal retention:** later states depend on the person's own ordered history.
3. **Self-model continuity:** the organization can treat prior and anticipated states as its own.
4. **Agency continuity:** the person remains a source of directed action, even when capacity is constrained.
5. **Relational continuity:** others can sustain accountable relations with the changing person.

The criteria remain deliberately plural because exact thresholds are open. A material soul is a physical process with historical depth. Material replacement can preserve it when replacement participates in the same causal continuity. Identity belongs to the ordered causal relation, so a static state match alone is insufficient. Death is the irreversible termination of the self-maintaining trajectory. Exact thresholds for continuity, and the relation between this process definition and phenomenal experience, remain open questions.

The definition gives several philosophical categories material content:

| Category | Field-materialist meaning |
|---|---|
| Identity | Continuous causal organization that changes future response |
| Character | Historically developed configuration of response and action |
| Agency | Capacity to select and sustain reachable futures |
| Need | Condition whose absence contracts viable continuation or development |
| Injury | Persistent contraction or deformation of viable trajectories |
| Development | Expansion and differentiation of viable, self-directed trajectories |
| Death | Irreversible end of the self-maintaining causal trajectory |

### 2.3 Human essence as developed capacity

The person has stable physical requirements and historically changing capacities. A Creative synthesis represents human personhood as the conjunction

$$
\boxed{
\text{human person}
:=
\bigl(
\text{embodied continuity},
\text{retained development},
\text{social relations},
\text{effective self-direction}
\bigr).
}
$$

This extends Marx's account of the human essence as the ensemble of social relations while preserving the concrete person who experiences, acts, suffers, and develops. Social formation changes the person without dissolving individual continuity.

## 3. The Reciprocal Person-World Relation

### 3.1 Coupled dynamics (Hypothesized)

Let $F_G$ denote the material-social field of a group $G$: resources, infrastructure, language, institutions, knowledge, communication channels, law, ecological conditions, and patterned expectations. A minimal state-transition architecture is

$$
J_i(t)
:=
\mathcal{J}_i\bigl[\mathfrak{s}_i(t),F_G(t)\bigr],
$$

$$
F_G(t+\Delta t)
:=
\mathcal{L}_{G,\Delta t}
\bigl[F_G(t),\{J_i(t)\}_{i\in G},I_G(t)\bigr],
$$

$$
\mathfrak{s}_i(t+\Delta t)
:=
\mathcal{B}_{i,\Delta t}
\bigl[\mathfrak{s}_i(t),F_G(t+\Delta t)\bigr].
$$

Here:

- $\mathcal{J}_i$ maps the person's state and conditions to production, care, communication, learning, refusal, and action;
- $\mathcal{L}_{G,\Delta t}$ maps prior shared conditions, participants' activity, and wider inputs to the next material-social state;
- $\mathcal{B}_{i,\Delta t}$ maps shared conditions back into the person's state and reachable futures;
- $I_G$ contains ecological inputs and effects from wider social systems, including irreversible stock depletion and waste.

These maps are a Hypothesized socioeconomic architecture. Each empirical application must define its state components, units, time interval, and constitutive maps. They are independent of the canonical Cassi PDE.

The causal loop is

$$
\boxed{
\mathfrak{s}_i
\longrightarrow \mathcal{J}_i
\longrightarrow F_G
\longrightarrow \mathcal{B}_{j,\Delta t}
\longrightarrow \mathfrak{s}_j.
}
$$

People create the conditions that create people. Marx's unity of changing circumstances and self-change becomes a coupled dynamical claim.

### 3.2 Institutions as boundary and memory operators

Institutions are persistent organizations of the person-world loop. They determine:

1. whose activity can enter the shared field;
2. which effects are amplified, delayed, stored, or suppressed;
3. who receives resources and consequences;
4. who may inspect and revise collective memory;
5. which future trajectories remain available to each person.

Property, money, firms, households, schools, media, parties, states, and commons differ through this control topology. Legal ownership is one component. Decision rights, information access, coercive capacity, infrastructure, and control of institutional memory are equally important.

### 3.3 Social influence and the gravity analogy

For source activity $J_i$ and recipient $j$, write the transmitted effect schematically as

$$
\Delta\mathfrak{s}_j
:=
\mathcal{R}_j\,
\mathcal{T}_{j\leftarrow i}[F_G]J_i,
$$

where $\mathcal{T}_{j\leftarrow i}$ is an independently measured institutional transmission path and $\mathcal{R}_j$ is the recipient's state-dependent capacity to receive, resist, reinterpret, or use the effect. Source activity, medium, and recipient state jointly determine $\Delta\mathfrak{s}_j$.

This is a socioeconomic bookkeeping analogy, not a physical force law or a result derived from the Cassi field equations. Persistent organization can generate effects beyond an isolated act because institutions retain and amplify activity. Human-scale social influence is represented only by the socioeconomic map $\mathcal{T}_{j\leftarrow i}$. The conditional Qi-gravity relation in `foundations/xi-derivation.md` remains a physical-domain result.

## 4. Coherent Activity and Relational Surplus

### 4.1 Nonseparable production (Hypothesized accounting)

Let $Y_G$ be one commensurable outcome of group $G$ over a declared time window, and let $Y_{\{i\}}$ be person $i$'s outcome in a preregistered isolated counterfactual using the same unit and outcome definition. Define

$$
Y_G
:=
\sum_{i\in G}Y_{\{i\}}
+\Omega_G,
$$

or equivalently

$$
\boxed{
\Omega_G
:=Y_G-\sum_{i\in G}Y_{\{i\}}.
}
$$

- $\Omega_G>0$: constructive cooperation produces more than isolated activity.
- $\Omega_G=0$: activity combines additively.
- $\Omega_G<0$: conflict, obstruction, duplicated effort, or destructive interference wastes capacity.

This single-outcome counterfactual account is independent of Cassi field inner products. Experiments must specify the outcome, solo condition, group condition, composition, time horizon, and attribution rule. Pairwise and higher-order decompositions require additional preregistered baselines. When isolated production is infeasible, $\Omega_G$ is not directly identified; a study may report a named relational-capacity proxy without interpreting it as this additive residual.

### 4.2 Coordination with differentiation

Productive coordination aligns activity relevant to a shared undertaking while preserving perception, experimentation, dissent, personality, and independent development. Maximum similarity among people would suppress this differentiated capacity.

For a declared undertaking, studies therefore measure two outcome families separately:

1. **Common-undertaking contribution:** how the activity advances the selected shared purpose.
2. **Independent capacity:** how the activity preserves perception, experimentation, dissent, and participant development.

These are conceptual codes rather than additive or orthogonal components of a contribution vector. The same act can score on both, and each study must preregister the unit, coding rubric, raters, disagreement procedure, and separate outcome measures. An emancipatory institution strengthens common-undertaking contribution while preserving independent capacity and preventing contraction of participants' viable futures or effective agency. Forced synchronization can raise short-term output while degrading correction, adaptability, identity, and future capacity.

### 4.3 The ownership question

Whenever $\Omega_G$ is identified, it records an outcome that exists through relations among participants and shared infrastructure. When $\Omega_G>0$, no isolated producer generated the additional component. Assigning that component to a shareholder, executive, platform, ministry, or party requires an institutional rule; the production process supplies no natural decomposition.

This supports a political principle:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Relational surplus generated by common infrastructure and cooperation should be governed in common.}
}
$$

The equation identifies a nonseparable product. Equal standing and common governance supply the normative conclusion.

Operational common governance requires defined boundaries, affected-person rule-making, accountable monitoring, accessible conflict resolution, recognized self-organization, and nested institutions. `speculations/creative-extensions/coherence-commons.md` §9 develops the full rights bundle.

## 5. Marxist Categories in Field-Materialist Form

| Marxist category | Cassi extension |
|---|---|
| Human essence | Embodied, historically retained, socially coupled, self-steering trajectory |
| Labor | Expenditure and development of life-making causal capacity through activity that changes both world and worker |
| Cooperation | Positive relational surplus generated by organized interaction |
| Means and forces of production | Material tools and resources plus historically developed infrastructure, knowledge, interfaces, and institutional memory; ownership and control relations govern access to them |
| Alienation | Marx's estrangement from product, laboring activity, species-being, and other people, extended into measurable losses of causal authorship, development, cooperation, and return flow |
| Exploitation | Under capitalism, appropriation of surplus labor as surplus value; Cassi separately tracks relational-surplus capture and displaced reproduction costs |
| Class | Relation to the means of production, and thereby to subsistence, and to the appropriation of surplus value; control topology measures the institutions through which that relation is reproduced |
| Class consciousness | Shared causal model, memory, coordinated capacity, consequence tracking, and self-correction among people in a common structural position |
| Ideology | Material and symbolic processes that shape perception, expectation, records, and available action, including fetish forms in which social relations appear as natural properties of commodities, money, or institutions |
| Organization | Mechanism that converts dispersed experience into persistent collective causal capacity |
| State | Historically specific apparatus of class rule concentrated in public authority and coercive machinery, also operating through allocation, boundaries, records, and large-scale coordination |
| Bureaucracy | Command layer whose internal reproduction becomes insulated from distributed sensing and correction |
| Revolutionary transition | Working-class conquest of political power, expropriation and socialization of capitalist property, and transfer of governing capacity to the associated population |
| Communism | Development from a lower transitional phase toward abolition of class rule, the wage-labor relation, generalized commodity production, and the state as a class coercive power |

### 5.1 Labor and the development of capacity

Labor changes an object, a social relation, and the laborer. Repeated activity can increase skill, coordination, confidence, and knowledge; it can also produce injury, exhaustion, narrowed attention, and lost developmental possibilities. A theory of labor therefore includes both output and the transformation of the person producing it.

Marx's value categories remain specific to commodity production. Commodity value is congealed abstract labor; its magnitude is socially necessary labor time. The Cassi extension uses separate quantities to explain how organization changes productive capacity, how cooperation creates relational surplus, and how labor can expand or contract the worker's future agency.

### 5.2 Alienation as severed causal authorship

Marx's fourfold account distinguishes estrangement from the product, laboring activity, species-being, and other people. The following six operational dimensions preserve those relations while separating mechanisms for study:

1. **Activity:** another party controls how the person acts.
2. **Product:** another party controls what the activity creates.
3. **Purpose:** the person cannot govern the ends served.
4. **Development:** repeated activity narrows the person's capacities and species-being.
5. **Social relation:** other people and the participants' common power confront the person as external powers embodied in management, capital, markets, bureaucracy, or an unaccountable organization.
6. **Return:** the product fails to reproduce the person and community that produced it.

A concise form is

$$
\boxed{
\text{alienation}
=
\text{loss of causal authorship over the processes through which one produces the world and develops oneself}.
}
$$

### 5.3 Marxian exploitation and two Cassi supplements

The standard Marxian account remains primary under capitalism:

$$
\text{rate of exploitation}=\frac{s}{v},
\qquad
r_p=\frac{s}{c+v}
=\frac{s/v}{c/v+1},
$$

where $s$ is surplus value, $v$ is variable capital, $c$ is constant capital, and $r_p$ is the profit rate. Cassi adds two complementary accounts:

1. **Relational-surplus capture:** control over $\Omega_G$ by actors who do not collectively govern its production.
2. **Reproduction debt:** depletion imposed on workers, households, communities, public systems, and ecosystems without replenishment.

The two supplements do not redefine exploitation in Marx's technical sense. They test additional transfers and depletions that can accompany surplus-value appropriation.

For one declared reserve component $k$ with a fixed unit and time basis, a schematic reproduction ledger is

$$
\dot R_{i,k}
:=
P_{i,k}^{\mathrm{care}}
+P_{i,k}^{\mathrm{public}}
+P_{i,k}^{\mathrm{ecological}}
+P_{i,k}^{\mathrm{learning}}
-E_{i,k}^{\mathrm{labor}}
-E_{i,k}^{\mathrm{harm}}.
$$

Every $P_{i,k}$ and $E_{i,k}$ is a rate in the same declared unit as $R_{i,k}$ per unit time. Components remain separate; any scalar aggregation requires declared conversion weights and publication of the underlying ledger. Persistent depletion of any critical reserve contracts health, time, agency, and viable futures. Relational-surplus capture or reproduction debt can rise while wages hold steady when care burdens, debt, danger, exhaustion, or ecological damage are displaced onto the producing population.

### 5.4 Capital as a control topology

In capitalist production, money advanced as capital returns through production and realization as $M'=M+\Delta M$. Accumulation converts realized surplus value back into expanded capital. The following topology describes how this self-expansion extends control; it does not replace the value relation:

1. the means through which people coordinate production;
2. the institutional memory that retains collective knowledge;
3. the boundary rules determining whose activity propagates;
4. the allocation of surplus value and relational surplus;
5. the future possibilities available to workers and communities.

Accumulation expands this control and can weaken the reproduction base on which production depends. Profit-rate pressure, realization failure, financial fragility, reproduction debt, and ecological constraint are distinct mechanisms that can interact. `speculations/creative-extensions/coherence-commons.md` §§5–8 develops their separate accounts and an illustrative fast-slow depletion model.

Primitive accumulation is the coercive formation of this topology through enclosure, dispossession, colonial extraction, destruction of independent subsistence, and creation of dependence on wage labor. Contemporary enclosure can target land, housing, knowledge, data, platforms, public infrastructure, and ecological sinks.

## 6. Social Reproduction, Need, and Development

### 6.1 Reproducing labor-power and persons

Production depends on activities that restore and develop people: food, shelter, care, health, education, rest, affection, play, public infrastructure, and ecological stability. Much of this work occurs outside the wage relation while remaining necessary to value production.

Social reproduction includes gendered unwaged domestic labor under a sexual division of labor. Its institutional boundary is contested: care can be assigned to households, commodified, publicly provided, or governed as a common service, with different distributions of time, control, and developmental cost.

A complete socioeconomic balance therefore tracks:

- commodity output and surplus value;
- paid and unpaid labor time, disaggregated by gender and care responsibility;
- care inputs and care deficits;
- health and recovery;
- time autonomy;
- housing and material security;
- public infrastructure;
- ecological stocks and waste sinks;
- acquired capabilities and lost developmental opportunities.

### 6.2 Need and ability are dynamic

Needs fall into four groups:

1. **Viability needs:** food, water, shelter, health, bodily security, care, and ecological stability.
2. **Developmental needs:** education, rest, exploration, play, creative activity, and meaningful challenge.
3. **Relational needs:** recognition, participation, affection, trust, and social connection.
4. **Agency needs:** information, time, tools, voice, appeal, refusal, and capacity to leave damaging relations.

Abilities are developed socially. Provision in the present creates capability in the future; deprivation suppresses both expressed need and visible ability. Marx's communist distribution principle therefore describes a regenerative cycle:

Marx's critique of equal right applies within transition: equal remuneration by labor contribution reproduces inequality when needs and capacities differ. Need-matched provision is an organizing principle operating before abundance and through transition.
At every stage, provision gives priority to concrete needs within ecological ceilings and available productive capacity; developing common capacity enlarges the feasible provision set.

$$
\boxed{
\text{provision according to need}
\longrightarrow
\text{human development}
\longrightarrow
\text{richer contribution}
\longrightarrow
\text{common capacity}
\longrightarrow
\text{richer provision}.
}
$$

### 6.3 Development as expansion of viable futures

Let $V_i$ be a scalar summary of the size and quality of the trajectory set $\mathcal{V}_i$ under explicit criteria for health, agency, social participation, and ecological feasibility. The plain symbol $V_i$ denotes this declared summary; $\mathcal{V}_i$ denotes the underlying set. Development means

$$
\Delta V_i>0
$$

with increased self-direction and differentiated capability. Deprivation, coercion, injury, deskilling, and chronic insecurity produce sustained contraction.

The development of one person can increase the capacities of others through teaching, care, discovery, organization, art, and improved institutions. These positive cross terms give a field-materialist interpretation of the communist horizon:

$$
\boxed{
\text{The free development of each becomes a causal condition for the free development of all when institutions return relational gains to their participants.}
}
$$

Capital can appropriate the same cross terms. Synergy leaves ownership and political form open.

## 7. Class Consciousness and the Collective Subject

### 7.1 Conditions for collective agency

A population becomes a collective subject when it acquires:

1. **Distributed sensing** of members' actual conditions.
2. **Shared memory** extending beyond isolated events.
3. **A common causal model** of the system producing those conditions.
4. **Deliberative synthesis** that can compare explanations and proposals.
5. **Coordination capacity** sufficient to alter shared boundary conditions.
6. **Consequence records** that connect decisions to outcomes.
7. **Correction mechanisms** that revise models, leadership, and action.

The closed loop is

$$
\boxed{
\text{lived conditions}
\rightarrow
\text{distributed reports}
\rightarrow
\text{collective synthesis}
\rightarrow
\text{coordinated action}
\rightarrow
\text{material consequences}
\rightarrow
\text{records}
\rightarrow
\text{correction}.
}
$$

This gives *class-for-itself* a causal interpretation. Shared opinion alone supplies neither memory, coordinated power, nor corrective closure.

### 7.2 Leninist organization

Organization preserves observations, trains participants, coordinates action across distance and time, and contests concentrated power. Its specific contribution is gain with memory: dispersed activity becomes persistent collective capacity. Democratic centralism joins broad deliberation and synthesis to unified action under a declared line; practice must then return consequences to the membership so the line and delegates remain revisable.

Concentrated coordination also creates a danger. Leadership becomes a separate interest when reports travel upward while accountability, consequences, and correction cease to travel downward. An emancipatory organization therefore needs:

- broad sensing and protected dissent;
- transparent synthesis and reasons;
- delegated authority with explicit scope;
- disciplined action within that scope;
- revocable leadership;
- public consequence records;
- correction after practice;
- development of members' own organizing capacity.

### 7.3 Maoist practice and the mass line

The mass line can be modeled as an iterative learning cycle:

$$
O_t
\rightarrow M_t
\rightarrow P_t
\rightarrow A_t
\rightarrow R_{t+1}
\rightarrow M_{t+1},
$$

where $O_t$ is distributed observation, $M_t$ is a shared causal model, $P_t$ is a proposed line, $A_t$ is coordinated practice, and $R_{t+1}$ is the resulting evidence returned to participants. Contradictions become observable through failed predictions, uneven effects, dissent, and changing conditions. Analysis distinguishes principal from secondary contradictions, changing principal aspects, and antagonistic class conflict from contradictions among the people. Socialist transition retains contradictions and the possibility of renewed class domination.

The process degrades when synthesis becomes insulated from observation, action continues after its model fails, reports become ceremonial, or the organization monopolizes capacities it was created to develop among the people.

### 7.4 Emergence without absorption

A collective can possess causal powers absent from any isolated member. The collective remains instantiated by persons, infrastructures, records, and relations. Its legitimate authority depends on preserving the equal standing, boundary integrity, agency, and correction rights of the people who compose it.

An emancipatory collective satisfies the minimum institutional floor

$$
\boxed{
\Delta V_i\ge0,
\qquad
\Delta A_i\ge0,
\qquad
\text{correction remains open}
\quad\forall i\in G.
}
$$

The non-contraction inequalities are a minimum floor; development seeks positive gains without sacrificing any participant below it. Where the isolated counterfactual is identified, $\Omega_G$ is a separately reported comparative outcome. Where isolation is infeasible, a preregistered relational-capacity proxy remains explicitly named as a proxy. Neither quantity substitutes for the institutional floor. Real transitions require explicit treatment of conflict, scarcity, coercion, ecological limits, and the rights of affected nonparticipants.

## 8. Freedom, Equality, and Human Flourishing

### 8.1 Equal standing is a normative axiom

Every causally continuous center of experience and self-direction has equal moral standing and may not be reduced to material for another's development. The physical theory establishes interdependence and causal consequence. Equal standing supplies the ethical constitution governing those facts.

This axiom blocks several aggregate shortcuts:

- high total output cannot compensate for pushing particular people below a viability floor;
- reduced present agency cannot be justified solely by an expected future average;
- injury, disability, age, dependence, or reduced capacity cannot lower moral standing;
- differentiated needs require differentiated concrete provision to achieve equal effective standing.

### 8.2 Freedom as reciprocal authorship

Freedom is effective participation in shaping the conditions that shape one's viable futures:

$$
\boxed{
\text{freedom}_i
=
\text{effective capacity to help govern the conditions governing }\mathcal{V}_i.
}
$$

It includes bodily and psychological integrity, material security, time autonomy, access to knowledge, meaningful participation, refusal, appeal, and practical alternatives to damaging relations.

### 8.3 Equality as a constitutional order

Equality combines:

1. equal moral and political standing;
2. universal material viability;
3. provision according to differentiated need;
4. protection against domination;
5. equal access to correction and appeal;
6. common governance of relational surplus;
7. room for distinct development.

The proposed constitutional order first defines an admissible set of institutional designs:

$$
\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{adm}}
:=
\left\{
g\in\mathcal{G}\;\middle|\;
\begin{aligned}
V_i(g)&\ge\underline V_i,\quad
A_i(g)\ge\underline A_i &&\forall i,\\
\mathbf D_{a\to b}^{(m,T)}(g)&\preceq\overline{\mathbf D}_m
&&\forall a,b,m,\\
W_{\mathrm{eco},k}(g)&\le0
&&\forall k
\end{aligned}
\right\}.
$$

Here $\mathcal{G}$ is the feasible design set, $\underline V_i$ and $\underline A_i$ are publicly defined viability and agency floors, $\preceq$ is the componentwise order on consequential control and insulation from correction, and $W_{\mathrm{eco},k}$ is overshoot above ecological boundary $k$. The floors, component bounds, and boundaries are Declared normative choices with separate measurement.

Within $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{adm}}$, affected people compare relational surplus $\Omega_G$, distribution, development, resilience, and other consequences through public reasoning. This preserves Sen's plural evaluation: the formula prunes impermissible designs and leaves a partially ordered set, avoiding a manufactured total social score.

## 9. Domination, the State, and Revolutionary Transition

### 9.1 Control topology

For a declared decision domain $m$ and time horizon $T$, let $C_{a\to b}^{(m,T)}\in[0,1]$ measure actor or institution $a$'s unilateral capacity to alter person $b$'s viable futures after reciprocal constraints are accounted for. Let $\mathcal{I}_{a\to b}^{(m,T)}\in[0,1]$ measure insulation from refusal, appeal, exit, or collective revision. A study-specific control profile is

$$
\mathbf D_{a\to b}^{(m,T)}
:=
\left(
C_{a\to b}^{(m,T)},
\mathcal{I}_{a\to b}^{(m,T)}
\right).
$$

The profile is compared componentwise and is not collapsed by multiplication: cases with the same product can require different remedies. Each study preregisters decisions, sanctions, dependencies, component anchors, aggregation over events, affected population, and time horizon, and reports both components with uncertainty.

Class rule is a stable pattern in this matrix. The matrix operationalizes mechanisms through which a class relation grounded in the means of production and appropriation of surplus value is reproduced; it does not redefine every asymmetry as a class relation. Class rule can persist through ownership, debt, managerial authority, monopoly over information, control of housing or infrastructure, coercion, and exclusion from decision-making.

Hierarchy is the vertical organization of control profiles across institutional levels. It becomes domination when consequential control is durable and insulated from refusal, appeal, exit, or collective revision. Temporary delegated authority remains distinguishable when its domain is bounded, its holder is recallable, and affected people can correct its decisions.

### 9.2 The state

The state is a historically specific apparatus of class rule concentrated in public authority and a coercive machinery. It also administers boundaries, law, taxation, allocation, records, and large-scale coordination. Its class character appears in whose property and viable futures these capacities protect, whose activity they suppress, and which class can direct and revise their use.

In Marxist-Leninist terms, a socialist transition requires the working class to win political power, expropriate the capitalist class, socialize the principal means of production, and reorganize coercive and administrative capacity against restoration while expanding associated producers' control. Legal title alone is insufficient. The transition changes:

- ownership of productive resources;
- command of finance and investment;
- access to institutional memory and information;
- authority to initiate collective action;
- allocation of surplus and reproduction resources;
- power to set boundaries and impose sanctions;
- receipt of consequences and feedback;
- practical capacity to revise decisions.

### 9.3 Withering as a measurable transfer of capacity

The withering of the state begins only after the political transition and means declining dependence on a separate, self-reproducing class command layer because associated people and their distributed institutions acquire the capabilities concentrated in that layer. Administration can persist or expand while the state as a class coercive power recedes. Relevant indicators include:

1. the share of consequential decisions revisable by affected people;
2. distribution of planning knowledge and administrative skill;
3. transparency and accessibility of collective memory;
4. scope and reviewability of coercive powers;
5. correction latency after failed policy;
6. rotation, recall, and replaceability of delegated authority;
7. the degree to which administration develops participants' own governing capacity.

Public ownership without these transfers can retain an alien control topology. A new command stratum can form wherever a stable group gains unilateral control over information, coordination, sanction, allocation, and future possibilities.

### 9.4 Communist development

Marx distinguishes a lower phase of communist society, still marked by scarcity, inherited divisions of labor, contribution-based distribution, and remnants of commodity and state forms, from a higher phase made possible by developed common productive capacity. In the higher phase, class antagonisms, capitalist property, the wage-labor relation, generalized commodity production, and the state as a class coercive power no longer organize social reproduction. Need-based provision and differentiated personal development can then become general principles. Contradictions continue as sources of learning, difference, and revision rather than reproducing a class whose development depends on another class's subordination.

The horizon can be stated as

$$
\boxed{
\text{A communist society organizes common causal power so every person can participate in creating, governing, and developing the field that develops them.}
}
$$

## 10. Candidate Empirical Program

### 10.1 Constructs and observable families

| Construct | Candidate observations |
|---|---|
| Reproduction reserve $R_i$ | Health, sleep, care access, housing stability, debt volatility, discretionary time, ecological exposure |
| Viable futures $V_i$ | Actual employment, education, housing, mobility, care, association, and exit options under stated constraints |
| Effective agency $A_i$ | Time autonomy, access to tools and information, voice, veto, appeal, refusal, and sustained action capacity |
| Relational surplus $\Omega_G$ | One group outcome relative to its preregistered isolated counterfactual in the same unit; a separately named proxy when isolation is infeasible |
| Institutional memory | Record retention, accessibility, provenance, transfer across personnel changes |
| Corrective closure | Report-to-decision rate, consequence visibility, correction latency, recurrence after correction |
| Control profile $\mathbf D_{a\to b}$ | Consequential control and insulation from refusal, appeal, exit, or collective revision, reported as separate components |
| Alienation | Control over activity, product, purpose, development, social relation, and return |
| Development | New capabilities, health, knowledge, creative range, social participation, and practical alternatives |
| Ecological viability | Material and energy throughput, regenerative capacity, pollution burden, and future stock depletion |

Each construct remains multidimensional. Research should publish the component ledger before considering any aggregate index.

### 10.2 Candidate hypotheses for preregistration

The full operational program is CC-H1 through CC-H16 in `speculations/creative-extensions/coherence-commons.md` §§14–16. That document sets the common reporting and interpretation contract and sketches each hypothesis's default unit, endpoint, comparator, and horizon. The study-specific preregistration is the sole source for its primary outcome, estimand, smallest effect of substantive interest, power, exclusions, missing-data handling, statistic, and interpretation tree. The hypotheses most directly testing this foundation are:

- **CC-H1: Reproductive security.** Securing every declared material floor improves effective agency relative to an equal-cost package that leaves a floor unmet.
- **CC-H2: Developmental labor.** Worker control over process, purpose, and learning improves skill and viable options while reducing alienation.
- **CC-H3: Relational-surplus control.** Alienation and turnover rise when participants create positive relational surplus while lacking allocation rights over it.
- **CC-H4: Differentiated coordination.** Voluntary role differentiation with independent review improves correction and adaptation on changing tasks.
- **CC-H5: Correction closure.** Distributed reporting, accessible consequence records, affected-person appeal, and revocable authority shorten correction latency and reduce recurrence.
- **CC-H6: Bureaucratic topology.** Concentrated information, initiation, allocation, sanction, appeal, and revision rights predict ignored reports and unrevised harm.
- **CC-H9: Reproduction debt.** Household, care, public-service, and ecological reserves add out-of-sample information about later output interruption.
- **CC-H11: Need-matched provision.** Under heterogeneous needs and a fixed budget, need matching improves the minimum domain-specific viability margin relative to equal per-capita provision.

### 10.3 Discriminating studies

The following are design families rather than additional hypotheses. Each study must instantiate one of the operational CC-H specifications and its preregistered decision rule.

1. **Cooperative comparison:** compare worker-governed and conventional firms matched by sector, size, capital intensity, and workforce composition. Measure correction latency, relational surplus, developmental outcomes, turnover, and control rights.
2. **Provision experiment:** randomize equal-cost support between uniform transfers and need-matched packages, with a prior rights floor. Measure viability, agency, care burden, and downstream capability.
3. **Coordination experiment:** compare solo, shuffled, voluntary-role, and command-assigned groups on stable and changing tasks. Preserve distinct measures for output, correction, dissent, and skill development.
4. **Institutional-memory study:** follow organizations through leadership turnover and test whether accessible records and distributed competence preserve performance and accountability.
5. **Mass-line process study:** preregister observation, synthesis, action, consequence-return, and correction stages in a participatory institution; compare planned and actual closure.
6. **Reproduction-debt panel:** combine time-use, health, household finance, care, public-service, labor, and ecological data to test whether reserve depletion leads instability.

### 10.4 Falsifiers

The socioeconomic extension weakens if well-controlled studies repeatedly find that:

- group interaction produces no task-specific relational surplus beyond ordinary additive production;
- distributed sensing and correction do not improve adaptation or error recovery;
- control over relational surplus has no independent relation to alienation, retention, or developmental outcomes;
- concentrated control does not predict insulation or correction failure;
- need-matched provision does not improve minimum viability under heterogeneous needs;
- reproduction reserve adds no predictive information beyond conventional income, wage, and profit accounts.

A negative social result leaves the physical two-fluid equations unchanged. It rejects or narrows the socioeconomic mapping.

## 11. Core Propositions

1. **Material continuity:** A person is a causally continuous, embodied, self-steering field organization.
2. **Reciprocal formation:** People develop through a loop in which they alter shared conditions and those conditions alter them.
3. **Relational production:** Organized cooperation can create outcomes irreducible to isolated contributions.
4. **Differentiated coherence:** Constructive coordination aligns shared activity while preserving independent perception, dissent, and development.
5. **Reproductive dependence:** Production depends on replenishing bodies, households, communities, public systems, and ecosystems.
6. **Topological power:** Class and domination reside in durable asymmetries of ownership, information, memory, decision, sanction, and future possibility.
7. **Collective subjectivity:** A population becomes a causal collective through distributed sensing, shared memory, synthesis, coordinated action, consequence records, and correction.
8. **Normative bridge:** Equal standing and non-subordination govern how interdependence and relational surplus should be organized.
9. **Developmental freedom:** Freedom is effective reciprocal authorship of the conditions shaping one's viable futures.
10. **Communist horizon:** Common causal power should make each person's development a source of richer development for all.
