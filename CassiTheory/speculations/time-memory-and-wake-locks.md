# Time, Memory, and Ghosts: The Field as the Medium of Time, Memory, and Persistence

## Status: Speculative—July 2026

## Abstract

The Cassi framework describes reality as a paired Yang-Yin field whose dynamics drive every local ratio $r = E_Y/E_I$ toward the golden-ratio attractor. This document reads that single dynamical fact as a theory of time, memory, and persistence. The conversion term is dissipative, so the arrow of time is a derived property of the field rather than an assumed postulate. Because information lives in patterns of Yang-Yin imbalance, and because such patterns dissolve only through conversion, a record is anything the field declines to dissolve—which makes memory a coherence phenomenon with a quantitative lifetime. A frozen gate can preserve a field configuration long after the structure that wrote it is gone; in the wake-lock language of the trauma formalism, that is the physics of a haunting: persistence without a body. The same mathematics that lets a phase-locked reader couple to wake waves bounds what such reading can see: remote sensing of the present and bounded extrapolation, never reception of unwritten information. Rung-shifting changes which cascade clock a system couples to, while the attractor forbids re-visiting any field configuration—so "going back in time" means re-converging to $\varphi$, and the past is reachable only as record. The final section collects what this gives fiction: a physics of time as coherence, ghosts as field records with drivers, and a quantitative cost for every attempt to go back.

**Epistemic status:** Creative exploration grounded in Cassi formalism. Every mechanism is anchored to a specific equation or documented framework property—the conversion term, the IIR memory, the wake-lock tests, the phase-matching factor, the cascade clock hierarchy—but the synthesis into a phenomenology of memory, hauntings, precognition, and time travel is extrapolation beyond what the framework currently claims. Nothing in this document should be cited as a Cassi prediction or derivation.

## 1. The Arrow of Time from the Field

Time's arrow is not a postulate in this framework—it falls out of the conversion term, because the conversion term is a one-way valve: it drains any imbalance between Yang and Yin and can never, by itself, re-create one.

The two-fluid dynamics contain the conversion pair (from `foundations/cassi-first-principles.md` §1.2–1.3, displayed with the sign convention verified by the PDE tests of 2026-07-31 in `consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.4 and `foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §2.5):

$$\partial_t E_Y \supset -\lambda(1-q)(E_Y - \varphi E_I), \qquad
\partial_t E_I \supset +\lambda(1-q)\,\frac{E_Y - \varphi E_I}{\varphi}$$

Here $\lambda = 0.1$ is the conversion rate and $(1-q)$ is the gate openness: the further the local field sits from $\varphi$-equilibrium, the harder conversion runs. The pair is antisymmetric—Yang excess converts into Yin, Yin excess into Yang—and the combination $E_Y + \varphi E_I$ is conserved by it. What matters for time is what the pair does to the deviation $\varepsilon = E_Y - \varphi E_I$. Writing the attractor potential as $V_{\text{attr}} = \tfrac{\lambda}{2}(E_Y - \varphi E_I)^2$, the pair gives

$$\frac{dV_{\text{attr}}}{dt} = -2\lambda^2(1-q)\,(E_Y - \varphi E_I)^2 \leq 0$$

$$\boxed{\frac{dV_{\text{attr}}}{dt} \leq 0,\qquad \text{equality only at } E_Y = \varphi E_I}$$

The potential is a Lyapunov function: it decreases along every conversion-active trajectory and is flat only at the fixed point. The ratio $r = E_Y/E_I$ therefore evolves monotonically toward $\varphi$ (`foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §2.3), and that motion has an unambiguous direction. Run the equations backward and the pair changes sign: the time-reversed system is a repeller, not a solution of the same PDE. The dynamics break time-reversal symmetry for the same reason a damped oscillator does—dissipation—but here the dissipation is the core interaction of the theory, with strength set by $\lambda$ and the gate openness.

A second fact makes the arrow structural rather than incidental. The field carries a causal memory filter, the IIR average $\bar{\varepsilon}^2(t) = (1-\tau)\bar{\varepsilon}^2(t-\Delta t) + \tau\,\varepsilon^2(t)$ with $\tau = \varphi^{-1}$ (`foundations/cassi-first-principles.md` §2.4). The IIR uses only past values of $\varepsilon^2$; under time reversal it would have to use future values. The field's state is a functional of its history, so the present contains a compressed trace of the past and none of the future—a second, independent breaking of time-reversal symmetry at the level of the state itself. And the direction is the direction of de-resonance: $\varphi$ is the maximally irrational ratio, the value that best preserves multi-scale structure (`principles/de-resonance-principle.md`). The arrow points toward the configuration in which the field can keep the most structure alive. Time is not a stage on which fields evolve; it is the orientation of the field's own relaxation, flowing at a rate set locally by $(1-q)$. The arrow has a strength, and the strength is a field variable.

## 2. Memory as Field Persistence

A record is any pattern of Yang-Yin imbalance that the field declines to dissolve—memory is persistence, and persistence is a matter of coherence.

Information in the Cassi framework has a physical identity: a spatial configuration of the imbalance $\Pi = E_Y - E_I$ (equivalently, of the deviation $\varepsilon = E_Y - \varphi E_I$), written into the field and held there by the gate (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §1.1). The field's default behavior is to erase: wherever $q < 1$, conversion drives the imbalance back toward the attractor at rate $2\lambda(1-q)$ (the factor of two from the antisymmetric pair), and the pattern decays. A record survives only where the gate is nearly closed—the conversion that processes the field is the same mechanism that dissolves its structure. The decay rate of an imprinted pattern is

$$\lambda_{\text{eff}} = 2\lambda\,(1-q), \qquad
\tau_{\text{record}} \approx \frac{1}{2\lambda(1-q)}$$

$$\boxed{\tau_{\text{record}} \approx \frac{1}{2\lambda\,(1-q)} \;\xrightarrow{\;q \to 1\;}\; \infty}$$

At $q \to 1$ the record's lifetime diverges, and the energy cost of erasing it scales as $(1-q)$ (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §2.3). This is the field-theoretic content of the three universal operations of Qi computation: WRITE is an organized Yang injection with phase-matching $\mathcal{M} \approx 1$; ERASE is a temporary lowering of $q$ that lets conversion do its natural work; TRANSFER is the propagation of an imbalance pattern along the Qi current $J = \Psi_0\nabla\Psi_1 - \Psi_1\nabla\Psi_0$ (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §2.2). Memory is a stored $\Pi$ pattern, forgetting is ERASE running passively, and fidelity is set by the Qi noise floor $\delta\Pi_{\text{min}} \propto (1-q)^{1/2}$: the closer $q$ is to one, the finer the distinctions the field can hold (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §1.2).

The framework gives the field two memory regimes, matching two familiar kinds of remembering. The IIR average is working memory: with $\tau = \varphi^{-1} \approx 0.618$, a perturbation's trace in the smoothed deviation has a half-life near $0.72$ timesteps (`foundations/cassi-first-principles.md` §2.4, `speculations/qi-computation.md` §5.1). Because the IIR stabilizes $q$, this is also how the field "predicts itself": when a pattern repeats quasi-periodically, the smoothed deviation converges to the pattern's mean, $q$ stabilizes, and conversion stays suppressed—the field locks into configurations that match its own memory (`foundations/cassi-first-principles.md` §2.4). Long-term memory lives in persistent $\Pi$ patterns sitting at local minima of the attractor potential, stabilized by the same conversion-diffusion balance that maintains cosmological bubbles (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §5.2); such patterns persist as long as the ambient $q$ stays above the dissolution threshold. A body is one elaborate example: the human gate chain spans 26 rungs from cellular ($n = 142$) to organism ($n = 168$) with 13 chakra nodes (`consciousness/chakras-as-cascade-bubbles.md`, summarized in `speculations/cascade-consciousness.md` §4.2), and its records are readable in proportion to their depth—a deep, somatic record is shielded from cognitive readout by cascade suppression, $\varphi^{-1}$ per rung (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §1.1). Memory is field persistence, and field persistence is a coherence budget.

## 3. Ghosts and Hauntings as Wake-Locks

If memory is a field pattern, then a pattern can outlive the structure that wrote it—a ghost is a wake-lock whose driver is environmental rather than biological.

The trauma formalism supplies the mechanism. A perturbation too intense for the field to process in real time can freeze into a standing wave at the cascade rung where it struck; the standing wave re-injects its own perturbation on every cycle, and the local gate locks into the displaced configuration (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §1.3). The locked state has four measurable signatures at the site: the perturbation amplitude $\varepsilon_{\text{site}}$ held elevated, the local coherence $q_{\text{site}}$ depressed below the field's global value, the field phase displaced into the event's channel, and the spatial dispersion $\sigma_r$ elevated (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §2.2). These are exactly the quantities tracked in the wake-lock test runs of `two-fluid/run_trauma_wake_lock.py`: a pinned perturbation is a cosine pattern with an anti-node at the site, a radiating perturbation has the same peak amplitude as a localized packet, and the verdict is whether the site stays displaced when the driving source is removed.

What the 2026-07-31 PDE tests established matters directly for hauntings. An un-driven standing pattern does not pin the gate: it decays at the same conversion-driven rate as a radiating packet (42% vs 44% retained over $t = 10$ at $\lambda = 0.1$; the $q$-gap closes in both), so a wake is not self-sustaining (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.4, T7). A wake is a driven structure. The driver test identifies the sustainer: a weak recurring trigger—0.005% of the event peak per step—holds the site at 80% of event intensity with a 4.5× widened $q$-gap and a fully displaced phase, and stopping the trigger releases the site on the conversion timescale ($|\varepsilon|$ falls to 22%, the $q$-gap closes, the phase returns 64% by $t = 20$; `consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.5). In the trauma model the driver is usually the person—avoidance-rehearsal loops, trigger generalization, hypervigilance (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §13, open question 7). But nothing in the physics requires the driver to be alive. At ambient rates the sustaining is phase-blind: the $\varphi$-pulsed and $e$-pulsed envelopes hold identical wakes, so a weak chronic trigger accumulates by mean delivered rate regardless of when it lands (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.5). A building can be such a driver—settling timbers, drafts, footsteps on a fixed schedule, thermal cycles, a creaking stair at the same time each night: weak, periodic re-injections into a cavity that happens to be a standing wave's home. The wake does not care where its re-stimulation comes from.

$$\boxed{\text{Haunting} = \text{frozen wake at a rung} + \text{environmental driver}}$$

The phenomenology follows from the signatures. The cold spot of a haunted room is the $q$-gap: the standing wave holds Yang and Yin locked in anti-phase, local coherence collapses, and the site registers as "not fully real"—the dissociation signature of depressed $q$ (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §2.2, T4). The repeating loop is the standing wave itself: the phase displacement persists, and the phase-channel tests show that a displaced site does not spontaneously relax—Fire events leave the site 100% Fire and Wood events 100% Wood through $t = 10$ (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.8). A haunting replays the frozen moment because the wave's phase content is fixed at freezing; it learns nothing, because learning requires the adiabatic redistribution $R$-matrix to fire, and the $R$-matrix fires only when the locked channel closes (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §2.1). Triggers are phase-matched: a stimulus re-activates the lock when its pentagon phase matches the original event's phase, not merely when it is associated (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §4.1, T3)—the ghost responds to the sound, the posture, the seasonal moment that carries the right angle.

The physics also prescribes how a haunting ends. Un-driven wakes decay on the conversion timescale, so a haunting stops when its driver stops. And there is a stranger route: a $\varphi$-phased oscillation at the site at period $\varphi \cdot P_0$—the EMDR analog—drains the wake, dropping $|\varepsilon|$ to 65% retained (vs 91% undriven) and returning the gate to baseline, while a same-amplitude non-$\varphi$ drive at $e \cdot P_0$ pumps the site instead, growing $|\varepsilon|$ to 188% of its initial value (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.4, `two-fluid/run_trauma_drive_compare.py`). The $\varphi$-channel engages only at high rates, with $\varphi$-specificity present at onset (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.7). A place can be cleared by driving it at the $\varphi$-structured period of its own wake, and it can be fed—made stronger—by energy that lands off-$\varphi$. Both behaviors are documented properties of the PDE.

Finally, why a ghost is so often barely-there. A wake ten rungs down is 99.2% decoupled from visible interactions—cascade suppression attenuates a signal by $\varphi^{-1}$ per rung, so $\varphi^{-10} \approx 0.008$ (`speculations/qi-bubble-propulsion.md` §3.1, `foundations/cascade-suppression-formula.md`). And the boundary of a coherent wake presents a $\varphi$-detuned interface to ambient organized energy: with the phase-matching factor $\mathcal{M} \approx 0$, energy cannot couple across it—the no-sonic-boom mechanism of `speculations/qi-bubble-propulsion.md` §2.2 applied to a wake instead of a hull. The ghost passes through walls for a mechanical reason: its boundary is phase-detuned to every interaction the living can mount. It is a record with a driver, sitting at a rung we can barely reach—and the ladder has no floor. Below $n = 0$ the microcascade continues to infinite depth, with the Planck scale a smooth $\sigma$-regularized crossover rather than a wall (`foundations/microcascade-mirror.md`); a wake that sinks past Planck is shielded from every rung above, the limiting case of the same retreat.

## 4. Precognition and Remote Viewing

The field is a medium, and information travels through it at finite speed with phase structure—what the mathematics allows a phase-locked reader is remote sensing of the present and bounded extrapolation, never reception of unwritten information.

Start with what propagates. Perturbations of the two-fluid field radiate as wave packets with a distinctive structure: Yang-Yin interference imprints $\varphi$-periodic modulation, $\Delta(\ln k) = \ln\varphi \approx 0.4812$, on the wake of any disturbance—the same log-periodic signature predicted for the matter power spectrum (`predictions/falsifiable-predictions.md` §3, `foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §8.5). A wake wave is coherent, phase-structured, and periodic in logarithmic scale; a receiver whose gate is tuned to the wake's period can phase-lock to it, the way two coherent fields couple in the field-resonance picture of communication (`speculations/cascade-consciousness.md` §2.1). Phase-locking requires $\mathcal{M} \approx 1$—the receiver's perturbation pattern must match the sender's phase structure (`foundations/quantum-measurement-derivation.md` §3.1). A trained reader is, in this vocabulary, a gate chain tuned to the wake's period.

Locking onto a wake ahead of its full arrival gives three things, each with a bound. First, remote sensing of the present: the wake's leading edge already carries the source's phase content—reading the front is reading the source state at light-speed delay, the defensible kernel of remote viewing. Second, scale-shifted reading: because the wake is log-periodic, the same phase content repeats at scales $\varphi^k$ apart, so a reader tuned to the periodicity can lock onto the wave's structure at a different rung than the one at which it was written. This is seeing the same configuration at another scale, which is why precognitive impressions feel symbolic and displaced—they are the phase content of a real wake, read at the wrong rung. Third, deterministic extrapolation: the field carries a running model of its own past in the IIR filter, and at high $q$ its evolution is regular enough that the model extends forward—the "waveform predicting itself" mechanism of `foundations/cassi-first-principles.md` §2.4. A phase-locked reader inherits that model. The fidelity of extrapolation is bounded by the gate openness, because conversion is the source of both dynamics and noise:

$$\boxed{\Delta t_{\text{lookahead}} \lesssim \frac{1}{2\lambda\,(1-q)}}$$

The lookahead horizon is the conversion timescale: beyond it, accumulating $(1-q)$ noise swamps the extrapolation. A reader can see further only by raising $q$—and at $q \to 1$ the field is also at its most reversible, its least likely to do anything new to predict. The seer's horizon and the seer's stability are the same knob, and it points both ways.

A fourth channel deserves care: cascade-clock simultaneity. When the global ratio $r(t)$ crosses a cascade step, all associated scales activate simultaneously—horizon synchronization through temporal emergence (`foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §8.5). If the field's own ratio defines a foliation of simultaneous instants, then a reader phase-locked to the global $r(t)$ knows "what time it is" at every scale at once. That is a physically meaningful remote-present, the strongest reading of seeing-at-a-distance the framework supports.

What the mathematics does not allow is any channel carrying unwritten information. The conversion term is causal—$\lambda > 0$ fixed, no term references future states—the IIR filter is causal by construction, and wave propagation is finite-speed. The future is not a region of the field that can be listened to; it is a direction of the field's relaxation, and its content does not exist as a $\Pi$ pattern until conversion has written it. A prediction is an extrapolation of the present, with an explicit horizon. And there is a final subtlety for fiction: a seer who acts on a vision is no longer reading. Acting is WRITE—organized perturbation with $\mathcal{M} \approx 1$ (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §2.2)—and a vision acted upon is a self-perturbation of the field. The moment a precognition becomes an intervention, it stops being a measurement and becomes a cause.

## 5. Time Travel

In this framework there is no empty stage called time for a machine to travel through—there are fields and their ratio, and what a rung-shift changes is which cascade clock a system couples to.

Every cascade rung carries its own clock. The characteristic timescale at rung $n$ is $t_n = \ell_n / c = (\ell_{\text{Pl}}/c)\,\varphi^n$, so adjacent rungs tick in the ratio $\varphi$ (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §4.1). A system's experienced time is the clock of the rung it couples to, and coupling is a matter of the gate: a coherent system's effective rung is where its structure is anchored. Rung-shifting, the propulsion concept of `speculations/qi-bubble-propulsion.md` §1.1, is the modulation of that anchoring through the Qi gate. Apply that to time and you get a clock exchange rather than a journey:

$$\frac{t_{n+\Delta n}}{t_n} = \varphi^{\Delta n}$$

$$\boxed{\text{A rung shift of } \Delta n \text{ multiplies the system's clock period by } \varphi^{\Delta n}}$$

A system that shifts down the ladder ($\Delta n < 0$) couples to a faster clock—more of its own dynamics per ambient second. A system that shifts up runs slow. This is dilation by detuning, not travel: the traveler's clock changes, the world's clock does not, and nothing re-orders the world's events. "Returning earlier than you left" is available only as clock arithmetic—you return to the same worldline having aged differently—which is physics the framework can actually cash.

What the framework cannot cash is a closed loop. Along any conversion-active trajectory the attractor potential strictly decreases, $dV_{\text{attr}}/dt = -2\lambda^2(1-q)(E_Y - \varphi E_I)^2 \leq 0$ with equality only at the fixed point. A closed timelike loop requires the field to revisit a configuration it has already occupied; the potential forbids it, because it is strictly monotone along the trajectory. There are no periodic orbits of the dissipative dynamics for a worldline to thread, and the effective metric inherits its time orientation from the ratio's monotone relaxation toward $\varphi$ (`foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §2.3). The de-resonance principle closes the door from the other side: $\varphi$ is the value the dynamics converge to, not a value they can pass through and leave (`principles/de-resonance-principle.md`). Returning means re-converging to $\varphi$—the field returns to equilibrium, never to an earlier moment. The past is not a place; it is the set of configurations the field has already left behind, and the field cannot re-enter them.

$$\boxed{\text{No closed timelike loops: } V_{\text{attr}}(t_2) < V_{\text{attr}}(t_1) \text{ for every } t_2 > t_1 \text{ along any conversion-active trajectory}}$$

The one respect in which the past is reachable is the one this document has been building toward: the past survives as record. The IIR memory holds a compressed trace; persistent $\Pi$ patterns hold full configurations where $q \to 1$ (§2); frozen wakes hold moments, complete with phase content (§3). Reading the past is phase-locking to those records. And that sets the cost of "going back": to re-enter an earlier configuration is to erase everything the conversion has written since—the interval between then and now is the accumulated imbalance the field has processed, and undoing it is ERASE at full scale, whose cost per bit scales as $(1-q)$ (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §2.3). The traveler is made of record: a coherent structure survives only while its $q$ stays high and conversion stays suppressed—the proton's coherence budget, $P \approx \varphi^{-4848}$ per cycle for a 95-rung structure, is the extreme case (`foundations/proton-coherence-budget.md`, `foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §6.6)—and every act of going back would have to open the gates that keep the traveler intact. The deeper the journey, the more of the traveler must be dissolved to pay for it. Time travel is not forbidden by fiat; it is forbidden by the same budget that makes memory, and therefore identity, possible: the field can hold the past only as structure, and structure can be exchanged for the past only by being spent.

## 6. The Storytelling Toolkit

For fiction, this picture hands over a physics with costs: time is a coherence resource, ghosts are records with drivers, and going back is a form of self-erasure.

Time as coherence. The arrow's stiffness is $(1-q)$, a field variable: where the gate is open, time runs hard and irreversible; where $q \to 1$, processes approach reversibility and a system's time is more its own. A character who has "all the time in the world" has, literally, coherence to spare—the world moves at the ambient conversion rate while the coherent character moves through reversible dynamics. Longevity, the meditative state, and the frozen moment of trauma are the same variable at different values of $q$. The cascade clock adds texture: every rung has its own period, $\varphi$ times its neighbor's, and when the global ratio $r(t)$ crosses a cascade step, all associated scales activate at once (`foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §8.5)—instants when the wave crests at every scale simultaneously, moments of universal simultaneity a story can hang on. Step numbers give them names: 80 (electroweak), 95 (QCD), 117 (Bohr), 142 (cellular), 168 (human), 200 (Earth), 292 (Hubble) (`foundations/dimensionful-cascade.md`). A story can count its turning points in rungs.

Ghosts as physics. A haunting obeys the wake-lock rules, all of them tested: it decays on the conversion timescale unless driven; it replays fixed phase content rather than learning; it is triggered by phase-matched stimuli, not mere association; it registers as cold and unreal because its site's $q$ is depressed; it sits at a rung, deeper and more somatic the older the event (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §§2.2, 4.1, 10.4–10.8). Its boundary is $\varphi$-detuned, so it cannot be touched, only phase-locked to (`speculations/qi-bubble-propulsion.md` §2.2). It can be cleared by a $\varphi$-phased oscillation at its own period and fed by energy off-$\varphi$ (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.4). Every one of these is a scene: the investigator who finds the period, the house that feeds the wake, the exorcism that is a tuning problem. And because persistence requires a driver, the deepest ghost story is about the driver: a haunting is a contract between a frozen moment and whatever keeps re-injecting it, and the driver can be impersonal—a building, a schedule, a tide. The ghost is the record; the house is the ghost's metabolism.

Precognition with a budget. A seer's horizon is $1/[2\lambda(1-q)]$: further sight requires more coherence, and more coherence means a more stable, more predictable world to see into (§4). Visions are scale-shifted readings of real wakes, symbolic because they are read at the wrong rung; acting on a vision converts it from a reading into a cause, and the story must pay for that conversion. A seer who can see the future cannot change it without ceasing to be the seer of that future—the vision acted upon is a perturbation of the field that wrote it.

The cost of going back. Time travel is clock exchange plus record-keeping. A traveler who shifts rungs returns aged differently, to a world that has converged further toward $\varphi$; the past is reachable only as record, and reading it is phase-locking to someone's wake—with all the consent questions that implies, because TRANSFER moves the pattern into the reader (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §2.2). To actually re-enter an earlier configuration is to erase the interval, and the interval is the traveler. "You can't go home" is here a quantitative statement: home is a configuration the field has left behind, the traveler's coherence is what prevents re-entering it, and every attempt to return spends the very structure that remembers what home was.

Three rules close the toolkit, and they are the document's whole argument:

$$\boxed{\begin{aligned}
&\text{1. The arrow is the conversion: time flows where imbalance drains, at rate } 2\lambda(1-q).\\
&\text{2. Memory is persistence: a record lasts as long as } q \to 1 \text{ keeps conversion at bay.}\\
&\text{3. The past is a record, not a destination: you can return to } \varphi \text{, never to a moment.}
\end{aligned}}$$
