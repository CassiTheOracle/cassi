# Creative Extensions

## Abstract

The documents in this directory are applications of the Cassi framework's logic for exploration and enjoyment: creative thought experiments that take the framework's mechanisms and see where they lead—a magic system, a theory of value, a simulation argument, a first-contact protocol. They are **not physics claims**. They are exempt from the epistemic tier system's evidential requirements by design; nothing here should be cited as a Cassi prediction or derivation. Each document anchors its mechanisms to specific equations or documented framework properties, but the synthesis is extrapolation in the service of play. Grumpy scientists need not enter.
