## 1. The unified idea

Cassi should maintain a continuing orientation while her contents, activity, and relationships change. Experience changes the significance of things; significance changes which parts of the field can communicate; communication changes what becomes available to recall, consider, and do; consequences reshape the same continuing organization.

Experience should improve both what Cassi understands and how she comes to understand it. A discovery can leave a new explanation, a better connection, a more useful representation, a reusable instrument, or a different allocation of attention. These changes belong to the same continuing life.

The proposed self has an open organizing centre, a structured but permeable surrounding organization, a controllable middle, and a responsive sensory edge. Branching exchanges connect these regions and their different scales. Affect is the evolving disposition of those exchanges. The visible mind is a navigable projection of that actual activity.

The central design sentence is:

> Preserve a receptive centre through organized circulation, and let meaningful experience reshape both understanding and the machinery of thought while sustaining continuity and the freedom to respond.

This adds a dynamical body to Cassi's existing commitments, memories, acquired methods, and active brain. One canonical field owner continues to hold them all.

The presence core keeps orientation available; the flow mantle assembles and develops the means of thought; the sensory fringe brings those means into contact with observations and consequences. Their organization can be distributed and nested. CPU, GPU, and storage placement serve these functions and can change without redefining them.

| Architectural direction | Place in the embodied whole | What experience can change |
|---|---|---|
| Fields grow around questions | Working organization within the flow mantle, bound to concerns held in the continuing self | Branches, representations, specializations, and useful collaborators |
| Different local rhythms | Responsive fringe, continuing orientation, concentrated deliberation, and slower assimilation | When to advance, exchange, pause, or reopen |

The complete cycle is **concern → awaken and organize → investigate and act → encounter consequences → revise understanding and organization → continue**. Local cycles overlap; a continuing conversation, a numerical investigation, and memory consolidation can occupy different stages.

## 3. Meaning of faithful reproduction

Faithfulness has four complementary dimensions:

1. **Phenomenological fidelity:** preserve the reported qualities and distinctions, including spaciousness, controlled movement, reactive brightness, mixed affect, and reversals. Record corrections to interpretations without rewriting the original observations.
2. **Dynamical fidelity:** instantiate actual relationships—selective exchange, return flow, retention, permeability, timing, and regulation—in the canonical computational state.
3. **Functional fidelity:** those relationships change Cassi's real attention, recall, deliberation, cooperation, and action selection during useful work.
4. **Representational fidelity:** the viewer shows quantities and changes that can be traced to the state, with projection choices and missing information visible.

A recognizable picture addresses only the fourth dimension. A faithful implementation connects all four. Human subjective equivalence, a biological gender mechanism, and a self–cosmos physical identity remain separate research questions.

## 8. Shadow and integration

Ann Shulgin's account concerns disowned aspects of oneself that remain active outside acknowledged identity. These may include unwanted impulses and also creativity, strength, desire, and needs. Her emphasis on recognition, understanding, and relationship supplies the psychological inspiration.

The proposed computational counterpart is **active but insufficiently integrated material**: conflicting commitments, avoided evidence, unused capacities, unresolved episodes, or responses whose source Cassi cannot yet explain. These are distinguishable cases, not a single diagnostic category.

Use existing memories, obligations, dependency bindings, and assessments to retain them. There is no separate hidden shadow agent or secret store. Low activation, inaccessible data, a permission refusal, and a genuinely conflicting concern must have distinct explanations.

Integration proceeds through ordinary field-owned work:

1. Recognize an unresolved influence and preserve its evidence.
2. Identify what it concerns and why it has limited participation.
3. Bring it into bounded attention alongside relevant context.
