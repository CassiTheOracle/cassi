## Document Summaries

### `de-resonance-principle.md`—The De-Resonance Principle in Cassi

The principle that makes $\varphi$ the attractor: rational frequency ratios cause resonance, concentrating energy at one scale and collapsing multi-scale structure, while $\varphi$—whose continued fraction $[1; 1, 1, 1, \ldots]$ makes it the most irrational of all irrationals—maximally resists rational approximation and therefore maximally favors multi-scale structure. The $\varphi$-attractor is thus an emergent consequence of wave physics, not an axiom. The doc then tabulates the correction pattern—$\sin^2\theta_W$ ($+2.1\%$ at $m_Z$, Computed: exact at $\mu_* = 233$ GeV, running is upward), $v_0/M_{\rm Pl}$ ($+5.6\%$, Hypothesized, mechanism unidentified), $m_e$ ($-20\%$, Speculative, flavor mixing), $\alpha_s(M_Z)$ ($\times 2.0$, Speculative, requires $\Delta b = 1.70$ from vector-like fermions, colored scalars, or KK levels)—with correction size growing as quantities move away from the attractor scale. It closes with four forward-looking tests (Z-pole $\sin^2\theta_W$ at $\varphi^{-3} + 2.1\%$ with the computed running, new scalars obeying $y_X \approx \varphi^{-n}(1+\delta)$ with $|\delta| < 0.3$, calculable lepton-mass corrections, GUT-scale $\alpha_s$ within $10\%$ of $\varphi^{-3}/(4\pi)$).

### `v0-hierarchy-problem.md`—$v_0/M_{\rm Pl}$: The Hierarchy Problem in $\varphi$-Clothing

An open-problem document facing the claim $v_0/M_{\rm Pl} \approx \varphi^{-80}$ directly: the empirical match is not a derivation, and the framework does not compute the $5.3\%$ correction. It shows the Cassi potential's $\varphi$-attractor term leaves the equilibrium $v_0^2 = -\mu^2/g$ independent of $\lambda$, so the VEV is set by Standard Model parameters $\varphi$ does not determine. Four candidate sources for the correction are examined—RGE running of $v_0$ (sign analysis shows the SM running moves the wrong way), threshold corrections at the matching scale (plausible, uncomputed), the nearest-integer-power approximation (the exact exponent is $79.89$, not $80$), and the Bohm quantum potential (negligible, $\sim 10^{-34}$)—and none is computed. The doc lists precisely what would promote the claim from Hypothesized to Computed: deriving the exact $\varphi$-attractor value, the full Cassi SU(2) RGE for $v_0$, and the matching-scale threshold corrections.
