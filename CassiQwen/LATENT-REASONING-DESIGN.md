# Cassi Universal Latent Reasoning System

## Status and authority

**Implemented integration with specified reasoning extensions.** Field-resident invocation, one-child reasoning episodes, self-development, fixed model instruments, bounded observations, paired publication, and field-owned native emission have production source paths. The numerical helical packet basis is also implemented. The packet-aware multi-operation episode in Section 8 is a specified extension, not an exercised capability. Existing mechanics do not establish broad acquisition, productive open-domain language, semantic latent alignment, practical efficiency, or general intelligence.

The [CassiFI computer design](../CassiFI/FIELD-INTELLIGENCE-DESIGN.md#32-one-universal-regional-field-computer) remains authoritative for regional state and execution. Its [world-model design](../CassiFI/FIELD-INTELLIGENCE-DESIGN.md#33-field-native-world-modeling-and-lifelong-general-intelligence) governs shared cognitive semantics, and its [learning program](../CassiFI/FIELD-INTELLIGENCE-DESIGN.md#34-cumulative-training-and-evaluation-program) supplies the acquisition and evaluation discipline. This document defines latent-reasoning composition and LLM integration using those same schemas and owners, rather than creating another computer, ontology, learning service, or competing checkpoint.

Statements labeled **Implemented basis** refer to cited source or retained execution evidence. **Specified mechanism** denotes a concrete design obligation not yet present on every profile. **Research question** denotes a capability whose useful acquisition, scalability, or generalization remains unresolved. Unmarked requirements remain normative behavior. These categories do not introduce preregistration, approval-stage, or frozen-verdict documents. Development proceeds through implementation, focused behavioral checks, and actual scenarios.

## 1. Destination and maximum ambition

Build a continuously learning reasoning system whose understanding survives a context-window change, an interrupted computation, a different task, a new modality, and replacement of its language-model interface. It should acquire useful distinctions from experience, construct and revise executable world models, discover transferable procedures, reason without narrating every intermediate step, and decide which computation or observation would resolve an important uncertainty.

An LLM is an explicit computational instrument: a source of candidate interpretations, measurements, demonstrations, static operations, or language realization. It is not the permanent owner of the system's memory or the mandatory executor of every reasoning step. The field may eventually own interpretation, deliberation, emission, recurrent state, and useful portions of computation. At the maximal endpoint, a model artifact is an offline teacher, tokenizer description, or selectively accessed static resource; a full native autoregressive model need not remain running.

This endpoint includes:

- productive, grounded language and multimodal understanding;
- shared relational, numerical, temporal, causal, and procedural knowledge;
- open-ended construction from a bounded executable language;
- persistent multi-hypothesis reasoning and hierarchical planning;
- model-specific latent observation and intervention;
- acquisition of reusable representations and methods from model-assisted and direct experience;
- cross-model transfer of acquired knowledge without replaying a lifetime into existence;
- selective use, compression, replacement, and retirement of native model state and computation;
- resource-aware learning and inquiry with explicit limits and recoverable unfinished work;
- deliberate self-development through internal practice, authorized study, and acquired methods for improving future computation;
- explanations tied to actual evidence and computation;
- user-owned goals, privacy boundaries, and effect authority.

Increasing field ownership and measured native displacement are primary experimental objectives. An experiment can establish either while worsening language quality or throughput. Those outcomes remain explicit; they do not establish a useful general reasoner. A useful deployment must separately establish task adequacy, acceptable cost, and authority behavior. Neither objective is a reason to conceal the other.

The design does not promise efficient solutions to every computable problem, unrestricted intelligence, universally identifiable latent causes, or an architecture-independent hidden vector with the same meaning everywhere. Those are different claims.

## 2. Meanings of universal and latent

| Term | Design meaning | Excluded inference |
|---|---|---|
| Computational universality | Stored programs can express general computation in the standard expandable-storage model. Every actual run has finite limits. | Tractability, a solution to P versus NP, or practical efficiency follows from universality. |
| Interface universality | One cognitive lifecycle can serve different model/runtime capability profiles. | A text API exposes hidden activations or every architecture supports the same hook. |
| Knowledge portability | Shared meanings, evidence, mechanisms, and procedures can survive an adapter change. | Residual vectors, token IDs, or KV pages are portable across models. |
| Task generality | Useful representations and procedures transfer to unfamiliar problems and compositions. | A finite task census establishes unrestricted general intelligence. |
| Latent reasoning | Intermediate computation uses nonlinguistic field state, bindings, programs, and hypotheses. | Hiding a textual chain of thought makes its computation field-owned. |
| Native latent integration | A declared adapter observes or changes an actual model execution boundary. | A prompt insertion or unused sidecar is a native intervention. |

The persistent cognitive representation is the portable layer. Model coordinates are identified measurement and actuation frames. The same mathematical relation can be shared between models while its activation correspondences remain different, uncertain, or unavailable.

## 4. System boundary and invariant ownership

For one principal's continuing intelligence, retain the Cassi decomposition:

\[
\mathcal C_k=(F_k,E_k,A_k,K).
\]

- `F`: the sole authoritative adaptive field image and complete Cassi continuation.
- `E`: authorized exact evidence and immutable completed receipts.
- `A`: nonlearned access, effect authority, revocation, and external-operation control.
- `K`: fixed codecs, validated instruction semantics, bounded numerical kernels, and compiler/checker rules.

An explicit model-instrument session additionally uses identified frozen model resources `W_m` and model execution state `N_m`. These remain outside the cognitive ownership claim until actually replaced. A saved native context is an execution snapshot of the external instrument, not another learned Cassi memory. Its identity is bound to the coupled commit described in Section 15.

| Invariant | Consequence |
|---|---|
| One cognitive publisher | All learned changes, semantic invalidations, reasoning branches, and Cassi continuations publish through the same owner. |
| One semantic identity | Prediction, planning, retrieval, language, and explanation use the same versioned meanings. |
| No hidden adaptation | Learned adapter mappings, priorities, procedural frontiers, cost observations, and consolidation state live in `F`. |
| Exact evidence is distinct | Source bytes can remain in `E`; their relevance, interpretation, and dependencies remain in `F`. |
| Explicit model dependence | Every model call and weight access is attributed; no failure silently invokes an LLM. |
| Hypotheses retain their type | Simulations, teacher assertions, inferred latent states, and proposed interpretations do not become observations by commitment or repetition. |
| Authority is external | A learned priority, coherence diagnostic, model instruction, or successful prediction cannot grant permission. |
| Finite work is conserved | Failed proposals, retries, rejected speculation, validation, learning, transfers, and persistence consume accounted resources. |
| One root does not imply one allocation | GPU pages, read-only replicas, and lossless paging can represent `F`, but cannot have independent semantic clocks or commit authority. |


```text
User / authorized evidence / environment
                  |
        fixed admission boundary
                  |
       one CassiFI field owner
       | meaning and beliefs |
       | hypotheses and work | <--> explicit model instrument
       | acquired procedures |      fixed adapter + declared native state
                  |
        supported result / proposal
          |                   |
     output boundary     current authority
                              |
                         external effect
```
Deleting a derived field cache must preserve the next logical Cassi transition. Deleting a native model context may require model recomputation or make exact native continuation unavailable; it is not covered by that cache claim. Descriptors disclose this difference.

## 5. Operating profiles and field-owned endpoint

Profiles declare ownership, permitted model use, and supported behavior. They are explicit session choices, not an automatic fallback chain.

| Profile | Model role | Field responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Standalone field | No model execution or model-state dependency. | Interpretation available through acquired/fixed boundaries, reasoning, learning, and deterministic field-owned emission. Unsupported language remains unsupported. |
| Structured instrument | Named text/tool-capable model service. | Shared problem state, selected reasoning operations, evidence use, learning, and assessment. Model proposals and realization remain attributed. |
| Latent instrument | Named native model with verified observation/write capabilities. | The structured responsibilities plus model-native observations and declared interventions. Native state and outputs remain attributed by actual ownership. |
| Selective substrate | Named static matrix operations, tokenizer metadata, or selected native computations. | Routing, reasoning, retained state, and all acquired operations that replace native work; only requested model resources are accessed. |
| Offline acquisition | Frozen teacher or comparator invoked in an acquisition environment. | Evidence admission and field-owned learning; no teacher is implicitly retained in standalone inference. |

A maximal field-owned inference implementation may use a fixed byte/event transducer without loading a GGUF or native model. A tokenizer-assisted or matrix-assisted implementation is separately identified. Copying model weights into the field and calling them acquired knowledge does not meet the learning objective.

An unavailable required capability produces an explicit unsupported result. Continuing in a different profile requires an explicit caller decision and a visible new operation boundary. Experimental native builds may deliberately replace or break ordinary Qwen behavior; recoverable source and accurate ownership evidence remain required.

## 6. Shared semantic substrate

Use the existing six record families rather than introducing `WorldModel`, `Bubble`, `Thought`, or `AdapterMemory` persistence roots.

| Record family | Integration uses |
|---|---|
| `Value` | Typed measurements, referents, alternatives, goals, numerical coefficients, model-feature hypotheses, or uncertainty sets. |
| `Binding` | Current identity-to-value/program resolution under a scope, including portable concepts and model-specific correspondences. |
| `Event` | Source admission, model observation, token acceptance, correction, action acknowledgment, or externally measured outcome. |
| `Program` | Representation, mechanism, reasoning procedure, interpretation, readout, emission construction, or migration. |
| `Assessment` | Pre-outcome prediction, causal comparison, procedure adequacy, model-intervention result, or measured computation cost. |
| `Obligation` | Unresolved inference, missing observation, pending effect, invalidation, migration, learning assessment, or interrupted integration duty. |

Every semantic use resolves versioned references and carries scope, evidence roots, applicability, epistemic kind, observation masks, valid time, and units/frame where meaningful. A native activation observation adds model, layer/site, token-position, sequence, arithmetic, and capture identities. The schema remains extensible through bounded typed payloads and fixed interpreter rules; arbitrary executable host objects are not payloads.

Model features have a model-specific frame. Portable entities and mechanisms have an identified shared frame. A correspondence is an acquired hypothesis/program linking these frames, with its evidence and domain. It is not established by coordinate equality or a suggestive feature name.

A document stating that a valve is closed, a sensor reporting a closed valve, and an LLM predicting a closed valve are different events. Their consequences may interact through one belief, but their support and reliability remain distinct.

## 7. Reasoning episode and result semantics

A reasoning episode is a bounded stored computation over the shared state. It may span many model tokens, model calls, observations, and restarts, or none of them.

Its logical request contains:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Identity | Principal, session, episode, idempotent delivery/operation identity, and parent episode if any. |
| Question | Requested proposition, prediction, construction, decision, explanation, or acquired capability. |
| Grounding | Exact source references, current bindings, unresolved interpretation alternatives, and their versions. |
| Objective | User-owned desired consequences, output requirements, permitted approximation, and priorities. |
| Constraints | Assumptions, invariants, represented action domain, and nonlearned authority references. |
| Allocation | Work, memory, external-call, time, branch, and acquisition limits. |
| Dependencies | Field/model/adapter/catalog/profile identities required for continuation and result validity. |
| Learning mode | Frozen acquired knowledge, bounded adaptation, or continuing online acquisition. |

The request is not an answer-bearing fixture. A caller may supply a formal problem, but must disclose supplied roles, decomposition, or representations when claiming acquisition.

The episode proceeds through admission, interpretation, binding, eligible-work selection, bounded execution, assessment, and publication. The work can retrieve exact evidence, derive a relation, propagate constraints, execute a procedure, simulate a supported mechanism, branch, seek information, or declare a limitation. It does not automatically ask the LLM to do an operation the field failed to complete.

The result carries the original question, answer/alternatives if any, semantic status, assumptions, evidence and program dependencies, applicability, unresolved obligations, cost, and continuation identity. The status must distinguish:

- an exact conclusion relative to checked premises;
- a supported numerical estimate with declared error/domain;
- a supported set of alternatives;
- missing or revoked evidence;
- source conflict or contradiction under the stated premises;
- representation insufficiency;
- unresolved identification, with the basis for any stronger non-identifiability claim;
- pending external observation or effect reconciliation;
- resource exhaustion or numerical failure;
- denied authority or unsupported adapter capability.

A wrapper must not manufacture a more specific status than the underlying computation justifies. A numerical solver settling, an LLM sounding certain, and an answer being eligible for publication are three different events. Goals influence inquiry and plan choice; they cannot change evidence or a goal-independent prediction to make the desired outcome appear likely.

## 8. Bubbles, dual perspectives, and timescales

A reasoning bubble is a descriptive name for a scoped dependency-closed working assembly. Its durable representation is an ordinary set of the records above, rooted in an episode and identified scope. It contains a question, relevant meanings, alternatives, work sites, result bindings, and expansion boundaries. No separate packet store or new core record family is required.

A bubble supports two operational views:

1. **Generative/consequence view:** propose interpretations, apply mechanisms, construct candidate programs, and predict consequences under explicit assumptions.
2. **Constraining/evidence view:** resolve sources, propagate requirements, seek counterexamples, inspect violated invariants, and bound what the first view can claim.

Both views use the same referents and dependencies. They can disagree without averaging incompatible worlds into one vector. A backward constraint operation must name its valid inverse, preimage, or solver semantics; running a forward mechanism backward without such a basis is invalid. These are computational perspectives, not truth labels assigned to Yang and Yin, and not a replacement for attributed beliefs of real speakers or agents.

The two-phase helix is an optional numerical organization of these coupled views. Phase can encode order or reciprocal interaction only through declared operators and readouts. A drawing of a helix, a phase rotation, or two channels alone establishes no reasoning benefit.

| Timescale | Persistent meaning | Permitted evolution |
|---|---|---|
| Fast | Current excitations, residual responses, numerical scratch, eligible work. | Bounded numerical/scheduling updates; no automatic creation of facts. |
| Episode | Active bindings, hypotheses, partial proofs, plans, and prediction obligations. | Dependency-aware revision, continuation, rollback, and explicit publication. |
| Lifetime | Supported mechanisms, representations, procedures, exceptions, and assessed applicability. | Identified acquisition, correction, migration, and consolidation. |

Frequency banks may represent several response horizons within owned numeric regions. Their values, readout, delay, and update meaning must be specified. Frequencies are not automatically calibrated timescales, probabilities, or semantic categories. A clock or inactivity can reduce priority without erasing a rare valid fact or unresolved external effect.

Bubble composition shares bindings and scoped program interfaces, checks frames and assumptions, and accounts for shared work once. Coarse bubbles retain a finer expansion or an explicit information-loss boundary. Splitting work into bubbles must not hide independent adaptive solvers.

### Acquiring predictive interfaces and temporal organization

The bubble boundary can itself be a learning target. Starting from a supported assembly, propose which ports, historical distinctions, and conditions are sufficient for a declared family of future questions. Challenge the proposed interface by varying context it treats as irrelevant, testing delayed consequences, and composing it with another supported assembly. Store the candidate as ordinary representation and mechanism programs, with tested coverage and known separating cases.

A separating outcome expands the interface, splits its regime, or narrows its claims. A finite set of successful boundary challenges does not prove global causal independence. Reduction never invents discarded detail: expansion uses retained fine state or authorized evidence when available, and otherwise retains the resulting information-loss limitation. Explicitly selected ports remain a useful supplied-structure condition, distinct from discovering the decomposition.

Temporal structure is also assessed through the consequences it preserves. For a linear mode proportional to `exp((-gamma + i omega)t)`, positive `gamma` controls decay while `omega` controls oscillation. Memory lifetime and oscillation period are different. A temporal representation must distinguish immediate responses, delayed effects, recurring conditions, and long-lived unresolved state without assigning truth or lifetime solely from frequency.

Candidate delays, retained predictive state, and supported numerical modes live in the same field. Compare their held-out temporal consequences, interference, stability, and actual cost with simpler delay buffers and nonoscillatory realizations. Meditation or consolidation may propose a changed organization; neither can rewrite a fixed arithmetic profile or discard relevant history merely to obtain a quieter signal.

### Packet-aware reasoning: scope and status

**Specified mechanism.** Extend the resident reasoning episode into a resumable, packet-aware work loop. It selects a relevant operation and resolution, executes through the existing regional computer, checks the returned consequence, and retains useful interfaces and procedures through ordinary acquisition. All adaptive decisions, unfinished work, and numerical amplitudes remain in the one field image. This extension does not add a reasoning service, independent packet store, model fallback, or a seventh semantic record family.

The intended improvement is task adequacy per total resource allocation: better completion under a fixed allowance, less work for the same justified result, or cheaper repair and reuse. Numerical reconstruction alone establishes none of these improvements.

| Surface | Current basis | Required extension |
|---|---|---|
| Numerical packets | [`cassi_resonant_field.py`](../CassiFI/cassi_resonant_field.py) implements `balanced-contiguous-haar-phase-space-v1`, source-bound analysis/split/composition, and bounded packet impulses. | Bind useful work and readouts to these supports; measure whether their use changes a consumer-visible result or cost. |
| Resident episode | `begin-reasoning` admits one supplied semantic child request; `finish-reasoning` records its returned result. | Execute a field-resident work frontier over multiple operations, with a root allowance, typed conclusions, and an explicit stopping rule. |
| Nested execution | `LearningComputer.call()` retains the caller and executes a child in the same image. Its current return contains outputs, diagnostics, and identity hashes. | Export the selected numerical successor and any retained partial continuation; a hash or energy receipt cannot restore wave coordinates. |
| Acquisition | Representation, procedure, assessment, correction, and self-development operations use the shared semantic state. | Acquire and challenge packet interfaces and work-selection methods through those operations. Useful acquisition remains unmeasured. |

**Implemented.** The packet work loop runs inside the resident semantic state in [`cassi_field_cognition.py`](../CassiFI/cassi_field_cognition.py). A reasoning episode holds a bounded work frontier with one root allowance, publishes a reservation before dispatch, consumes each child return exactly once against the published request identity, and keeps host-executed readout, branch, and refinement items under reservations derived from their own contracts. Completed items with one content identity reuse their result instead of dispatching again. Corrections publish an invalidation barrier with a retained repair frontier, refuse reads of dependency-bearing records until each known cause is checked, run in bounded quanta through `advance-invalidation`, and repair a reopened episode's premise, assembly, and hierarchy references while releasing the reservation that the changed dependency made ineligible. Episode and item statuses map through the declared result vocabulary, including `resource-exhausted`, `non-identifiable`, and `alternatives`. A work item that declares no reservation holds the remaining allowance rather than the whole limit, so one unknown-cost item no longer strands the rest of its frontier once anything has been charged.

Evidence: ten transition regressions in [`test_cassi_packet_reasoning.py`](../CassiFI/test_cassi_packet_reasoning.py) (adapter dispatch with corrupted-return refusal and single charging, enforced-allocation refusal, decisive and refinement-required readouts with an uncertified-norm refusal, correction with bounded repair and re-dispatch, reuse without a second dispatch, two incompatible hypotheses published as distinct hypothetical values, an acquired selector program that orders the frontier and refuses an unresolvable or fractional score, and a two-item frontier whose undeclared reservations hold only the remaining allowance), the continuing-world run in [`run_cassi_reasoning_scenario.py`](../CassiFI/run_cassi_reasoning_scenario.py), and the workbench path in [`CassiQwen/test_cassi_field_qwen_workbench.py`](test_cassi_field_qwen_workbench.py). The scenario interrupts once after a child return and before consumption with the reservation outstanding and nothing charged, corrects the shared premise, repairs and reopens through the ordinary owner with the stale return left unconsumed, finishes `supported` under an unchanged allowance, reads a coarse bound that stays unresolved, splits that support into `RL`/`RR`, and then reads the refined child with a decisive interval.

Three organizations remain distinct. The **numerical hierarchy** partitions ordered ports into balanced contiguous `L`/`R` supports. The **semantic dependency graph** connects meanings, programs, evidence, and obligations; related records need not occupy adjacent ports. The **hypothesis tree** separates incompatible assumptions and futures. A numerical split does not discover a semantic decomposition, and composing numerical siblings does not reconcile hypothetical branches.

The four common/counterflow position/momentum channels retain their numerical meaning. Generative and constraining views are scoped semantic roles, each permitted to use both transport strands. Packet depth, oscillation frequency, retention lifetime, confidence, and source authority are separate quantities.

### Packet assemblies and versioned state

**Specified mechanism.** A packet assembly is a bounded typed payload in existing records, rooted in the reasoning episode. Its `Program` declares the interface and allowed operations; `Binding` records connect semantic roles to exact current values and numerical regions. The canonical numerical value is the owned resonant workspace, including `qY,qI,pY,pI`, its profile, bindings, phases, clocks, and ledger. Packet coefficients are reconstructible views of that workspace, never a second adaptive copy.

| Owned content | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Episode identity | Original question, information boundary, objective, learning mode, scope, program version, return binding, and predecessor episode if explicitly reopened. |
| Assembly interface | Input/output roles, typed semantic references, units/frame, masks, scoped assumptions, accessible support roots, and unresolved external boundaries. |
| Port binding | Numerical workspace reference/version, ordered port identity, coordinate role, fixed codec/readout version, serialized channel units/frame and positive finite normalization scales, and declared missing-value behavior. These new codec fields are not already supplied by `ResonantProfile`. |
| Work frontier | Bounded eligible/deferred work items, discovery cursor, prerequisites, read/write footprints, scope, required checks, and result references. |
| Resolution frontier | An antichain of active numerical supports, retained finer-state reference, refinement reasons, and any applicable readout bounds. Ancestor and descendant supports cannot count the same coefficient twice. |
| Continuation | Episode phase, selected operation identity, outstanding call/return slot, child continuation reference, consumed-return identities, and scheduler fairness position. |
| Assessments and allowance | Actual outcomes, applicability and failure boundaries, resource reservations/charges, and separate numerical-energy accounting. |

Work items and frontiers live in the existing episode/`Obligation` payloads and protected invocation state. Programs remain bounded typed data interpreted by fixed catalog semantics. Native Python objects, executable callbacks, host queues, and learned adapter dictionaries cannot supply missing persistent state.

A versioned assembly can reference several disjoint numerical supports or share read-only semantic premises with another assembly. The initial write rule gives each active numerical port one owning workspace binding; overlapping proposals serialize through that owner. Repacking ports is an explicit layout migration of coordinates, operator/boundary data, bindings, and readouts. It is not a metadata rename that leaves dynamics attached to different meanings.

Closure is query-relative and explicitly bounded. Starting from the question's declared output roles, traverse known program inputs, guards, constraints, and current evidence dependencies. Retain a cursor if traversal exceeds its quantum. Unknown dependencies become obligations, not assumed irrelevance. Cyclic constraints require a declared solver/fixed-point operation or remain unresolved; they do not become an endlessly waiting prerequisite cycle.

The initial binding/decomposition can be supplied and is reported as supplied structure. An acquired decomposition is a separately assessed `Program`. A complete scan of known dependencies establishes closure only under those declared programs, not that the world has no omitted causes.

Use separate version checks for semantic references, assembly/interface programs, and the complete numerical source workspace. Every numerical mutation invalidates packet views of that source under the existing packet contract; reanalyze before another split/composition. Unrelated semantic publication need not invalidate a numerical view when its exact source workspace and binding dependencies remain unchanged. Do not relabel a stale packet with a new hash or weaken whole-source composition identity to a partial-slice comparison.

### Resident execution and numeric handoff

**Specified mechanism.** Add `advance-reasoning` to the existing cognition operation surface. Keep `begin-reasoning` and `finish-reasoning` as the episode boundaries. These names describe the intended implementation; `advance-reasoning` is not currently an available operation.

`begin-reasoning` accepts exactly one of a bounded direct operation or a versioned reasoning-program reference, together with Section 7's question, dependencies, and allocation. Boundary normalization turns a direct operation into a one-item program; both forms then use the same work loop. A caller-supplied solution-specific sequence remains assisted execution and is identified as such. The initial packet program is a fixed, disclosed method; acquired replacements are ordinary assessed field programs.

Admission order is normalize, validate typed semantic content, canonicalize, hash, then resolve the operation ledger. The canonical content includes the normalized program and its versions, question, allocation, input/dependency references, scope, and assistance/authority boundary. Transport form and optional diagnostic metadata do not change its meaning. Equivalent direct and explicit one-item forms share content identity only when all those semantics match; changed content under an existing caller operation identity is a conflict. Bind begin, selected child, advancement, return consumption, and finish to the same episode lineage plus their distinct stable transition identities. The current semantic kernel hashes the supplied mapping before such normalization, so this admission rule requires a real boundary/schema change.

Each `advance-reasoning` transition performs one bounded phase:

1. Resolve pending returns or invalidations against their dependency versions.
2. Extend the known work frontier within the discovery allowance.

### One episode allowance and bounded progress

**Specified mechanism.** The root episode owns lifetime limits for logical work, storage words, evidence reads, model calls, branch count, frontier size, and discovery/refinement depth. The existing machine resource ledger records executed work; the episode record adds the bounded reservation/consumption partition and checks that descendants cannot bypass it. Semantic assessments may project these values but cannot become another mutable budget owner.

For each resource, admissibility requires `charged + outstanding reservations + requested reservation <= root limit`. Child charges reconcile the reservation once. Definitively unused reservation can be released; spent work remains charged after cancellation, failed speculation, correction, or a rejected result. Storage is bounded by live occupancy and reserved output capacity, with transfer/copy work charged separately. Refine operations reserve metadata and both child frontiers before replacing a parent frontier.

### Baseline and packet-driven work selection

**Specified mechanism.** Eligibility is exact before priority is numerical. An eligible item has satisfied current prerequisites, compatible scope, permitted effects/write classes, a valid operation, and a quantum that fits the allowance. Invalidated results cannot be read or published while their repair is merely waiting in a priority queue. Waiting for new evidence is distinct from having available internal work.

Reuse [CassiFI §32.8–32.9](../CassiFI/FIELD-INTELLIGENCE-DESIGN.md#328-automaton-native-coordination-and-meaningful-topology): the existing field-owned work sites, exact eligibility/reason precedence, synchronous automaton cycle, versioned eligible/priority snapshot, integer priority key, queue-sequence tie-break, and every-`B`-th-dispatch oldest-ready service. Packet features are declared inputs to that same bounded lane/priority computation. They cannot form a replacement scheduler or bypass the automaton through a host callback.

The baseline uses the ordinary dependency-local frontier, discovery order, eligibility, and automaton profile without packet cues. Hierarchy-only supplies a fixed bounded depth cue for the same sites: `d_max - d(P)`, where `d(P)` is the shallowest support in a nonempty cover and `d_max` is the admitted layout's maximum depth. The packet-driven profile instead supplies the activation cue below. Both profiles specify how this input enters the existing lane law; the eventual priority is the law's output, not a host-side sort of raw features. Work without a numerical support remains eligible with the profile's ordinary input, not a fabricated numerical measurement.

The packet-driven method adds a dimensionless activation cue. Let `P` be a validated family of disjoint supports, `n_A` the port count of support `A`, `U` their unique port union, and `c^(A)` its complete coefficient array. With positive finite channel scales `s`, define

\[
a(P)=\frac{1}{|U|}\sum_{A\in P}
\sum_{\alpha\in\{q_+,q_-,p_+,p_-\}}
\sum_{j=1}^{n_A}\left(\frac{c^{(A)}_{\alpha j}}{s_\alpha}\right)^2.
\]

`P` must be a nonempty cover from one numerical workspace/source and codec, validated as an antichain of disjoint balanced-tree supports in canonical increasing-start order. Reject duplicate, ancestor/descendant, or overlapping support declarations before scoring; do not add parent and child norms. The denominator is `|U| = sum(n_A)`. Persist the canonical cover/index-domain identity with the score. Multi-workspace work splits into explicit numeric items or remains unscored. Serialized positive channel scales, units/frame, and codec version belong to the dispatch dependencies. The norm identity holds separately for each channel, and for the sum of their fixed-scale normalized squares, not for an arbitrary physical-energy metric.

Convert the activation cue to the existing bounded integer input with `floor(S * min(a(P) / a_clip, 1))`, where finite positive `a_clip`, positive integer `S`, arithmetic/rounding, and saturation rules are fixed in the versioned scheduling profile. Use bounded norm accumulation and saturate before overflow; nonfinite source coefficients still fault. The input and its source/codec/profile versions are included in the frozen priority snapshot. No probability or semantic-error interpretation is assigned to it.

Fairness, ready-age changes on blocking/requeue, stable ID ordering, and quantum size are exactly the canonical event-queue rules, with no second burst counter. A priority-only change cannot reset ready age. Discovery scans are themselves charged, resumable, fairly serviced work sites; a partial scan cannot declare the frontier empty. Dispatch rechecks snapshot versions and reservations. This is bounded service for continuously ready work, not a claim that every growing computation terminates.

The cue is consumed only from the exact source and codec versions named by the selection. Missing or unavailable cues retain their missing status and supply the disclosed hierarchy-only input to the same lane law; this is part of the selected policy, not a model fallback. A corrupted/nonfinite numerical result faults its numerical operation and records the limitation rather than silently posing as an ordinary missing cue.

Compute the full score only when its cost is covered. A bound, incremental aggregate, or cached ranking may replace it only if it is a reconstructible, version-valid view with the same declared selection semantics. The current packet helpers can reconstruct arrays and perform whole-source checks; selective execution and cheap incremental maintenance are additional work, not existing performance claims.

An acquired selection `Program` can supply bounded priority inputs for the same eligible sites using supported conditions, prior costs, and observed outcomes. Its proposal and assessment costs are charged. It cannot bypass the automaton or alter eligibility, fairness, premise checking, resource limits, result correctness, or authority. The initial implementation does not invent expected-information-gain probabilities or a trained external ranker.

### Cue admission and numerical accounting

**Specified mechanism.** A cue is a typed `Event` payload plus an `Obligation` continuation, not a new record family. It names the actual source event or completed operation, source epistemic kind, semantic dependency versions, codec/scales, destination workspace and ordered mode list, total numerical allowance, per-mode reservation, cursor, and consumed operation identities. Numerical source hashes and `event_kind` do not replace this identity. The existing impulse API has no consumed-event ledger; the new cognition/owner path must perform this admission and bind its cue identity to the invocation/return before dispatch.

The initial fixed codec has two inputs: consequence-work demand and constraint-work demand. For symbolic work they are exact Boolean features: an available prerequisite with a required consequence still uncomputed, and an outstanding required check or represented conflict. Each numerical replacement declares a finite residual `r`, matching units, and a positive finite scale `s`; normalize to `sign(r)` when `abs(r) >= s` and to `r/s` otherwise. This yields `u` in `[-1,1]` without overflowing the ratio. Boolean features use `0` or `1`. Both required inputs must be present; a missing input retains its mask and produces no impulse. Mixed-unit or incompatible-frame input is rejected before conversion.

The bounded vector `u = (u_consequence, u_constraint)` supplies `flow_signal`. This declares operational cue roles, not truth labels for Yang and Yin. Because `_packet_momentum_direction` normalizes its input, the codec separately maps magnitude to requested work: `rho_squared = (u_consequence^2 + u_constraint^2) / 2` and `W_cue = W_max * rho_squared`, with fixed serialized `0 <= W_max <= 1` in resonant energy units. The zero vector performs no numerical write. All work is reserved before deposition; a short allowance returns a resource limitation rather than silently changing the codec.

The initial mode list is one scale-mode deposit per disjoint active support, ordered by increasing start. Divide `W_cue` in proportion to each support's port count, using a deterministic remainder rule so allocated work does not exceed the total. An explicitly supplied/acquired program may select detail modes under the same declared budget and leaf rules. Preserve the original bounded vector, normalization scales, requested work, accepted work, and unit direction in the cue/return records; the current unit-vector-only receipt is not the full cue.

Requested shares sum to at most `W_cue`. Accepted kinetic work is checked with the explicitly accumulated energy-roundoff allowance; report that total rather than hiding repeated per-deposit tolerances. If normalized magnitude/work underflows to zero in the selected arithmetic, record the original nonzero demand and zero accepted work without attempting an unstable normalization. Its semantic obligation remains eligible.

Cue admission consumes each source-operation contribution and destination allocation once, with the cursor and accepted deposits committed together. Shared evidence references or parent/child views do not create extra inputs. An unchanged reread, replay, or duplicate return does not inject again; the low-level nondecreasing evidence clock cannot enforce that rule. Codec/layout migration transforms state or explicitly rebuilds a new numerical lineage rather than accumulating old history again as fresh evidence. Scheduling profiles count repeated internally generated work and cannot reinterpret it as additional observed support.

`packet-impulse` retains its existing restrictions: scale/detail mode, leaf-detail rejection, compatible profile, finite bounded work, nondecreasing evidence clock, and no insertion inside an initialized integration substep. Stage impulses at a valid integration boundary. The current per-call numerical `work_budget` in `[0,1]` is an energy-input bound under the resonant metric, not the episode's logical-computation allowance.

That input retains the external evidence clock and records internal numerical work separately from observation work; a repeated derivation cannot manufacture corroborating observations. External observations advance evidence only through ordinary identified admission. Logical computation, numerical evolution, and world/event time remain separate clocks.

Euclidean packet norm is not generally that Hamiltonian because mass and coupling metrics are nontrivial. Lower activation, lower energy, or phase agreement never settles a semantic obligation.

### Safe coarse-to-fine readout

**Specified mechanism.** Initial refinement changes what the reasoner inspects and executes; it retains the complete fine numerical state. The operation expands a parent support into children and exposes their declared work interfaces. It does not drop detail coefficients, zero inactive coordinates, or claim that Haar scale coefficients summarize a symbolic proposition.

A stopped refinement must justify the requested output, not merely report a small detail norm. For a declared affine readout on one fixed source, `y = b + w^T c`, first use the codec's invertible positive diagonal scaling `c = S_c x` and `v = S_c^T w`. Thus `x` is dimensionless and `v` has the output units. Partition inspected coordinates `R` and unresolved coordinates `D`. With valid upper bounds on the unresolved norms,

\[
\hat y=b+v_R^T x_R,\qquad
|y-\hat y|\leq B_D=
\|v_D\|_2\|x_D\|_2+\epsilon_{\mathrm{arith}}.
\]

The coefficient/readout frames and units must agree. `epsilon_arith` covers bounded codec, transform, dot-product, and bound-evaluation errors; use conservative outward enclosures or an explicit proved arithmetic allowance. For a threshold `theta`, decide only when the entire interval `[hat y - B_D, hat y + B_D]` is on the permitted side, with equality/tie semantics fixed by the output program. An estimate requires the caller's stated error tolerance. Ranking alternatives requires bounds on their differences; a single candidate's small norm is insufficient.

The standalone packet validator has no live-workspace argument, so the consumer must also compare the packet's source to the current workspace before use. An infinite, unavailable, stale, or decision-crossing bound triggers refinement, recomputation, or a typed unresolved result.

For nonlinear readouts, use a justified interval/Lipschitz/domain enclosure or execute the finer operation. For symbolic outputs, use exact dependency, proof, guard, and constraint semantics. A low-amplitude exception such as a forbidden action remains decisive; a large irrelevant signal does not gain logical importance.

This instantaneous readout bound is not a bound on future evolution. Freezing unselected ports changes a coupled operator. The initial packet loop therefore runs an invoked numerical evolution with its existing full declared coupling and reports that cost. Selective numerical updates require a valid boundary treatment and temporal error bound, or a supported reduced operator preserving the declared responses. ## 9. Mathematical and computational foundation

Cassi's fixed numerical kernels and exact program machinery have complementary roles. Numerical activity can represent relationships, estimate admissible values, and allocate work; exact logic governs identities, dependency validity, instruction behavior, evidence checks, and authority.

For a declared linear relation family with observations `H z = o`, choose `H z0 = o` and let the columns of `N` span the permitted nullspace directions, so `H N = 0`. A local workspace preserves those observations through `z = z0 + N y` and has energy

\[
\mathcal E(y)=\tfrac12(A(z_0+Ny)-b)^T W(A(z_0+Ny)-b),\qquad W\succeq0.
\]

With constant symmetric positive-semidefinite `Gamma` and `dy/dt = -Gamma grad E`,

\[
\frac{d\mathcal E}{dt}=-(\nabla\mathcal E)^T\Gamma(\nabla\mathcal E)\leq0.
\]

This is a local descent statement for fixed premises and the stated family. It does not show that the premises are true, the minimum is unique, a discrete algorithm converges under any step size, or semantic structure discovery is solved. Rank deficiency preserves uncertainty. Contradictory constraints remain contradictions even when an approximate residual is small.

Wave or resonant operations may add a distinct working mechanism under their own arithmetic and stability conditions. Exact constraints do not become soft because an oscillatory state crosses them. Any combination uses the same constrained coordinates and a specified energy/accounting rule; no global convergence theorem is assumed for arbitrary symbolic, hybrid, or open-ended reasoning.

The fixed computational vocabulary must support bounded resolve/bind, scoped branch, relation evaluation, factor/constraint propagation, numerical refinement, checked program execution, evidence resolution, and assessment. Larger methods are stored programs with field-owned frontiers. The automaton prioritizes eligible work but cannot change what constitutes a valid proof, observed fact, or authorized action.

| Numeric payload | Shape, strides/order, dtype/endianness, finite-value policy, masks, normalization and retained magnitude, payload byte length/hash, and owned buffer lifetime. |
| State dependency | Field predecessor, native predecessor if applicable, adapter/model/profile/catalog identities, and affected read/write versions. |
| Work and completion | Maximum work/allocation, result or continuation identity, explicit status, and consumed/cancelled/committed disposition. |
| Trust | Evidence/access scope and required capability; no payload-supplied authority upgrade. |

Envelopes are wire or regional payload schemas, not additional adaptive record families. Validate lengths, products, bounds, identity, and access before allocation or field mutation. Inline bytes and referenced device buffers have the same logical meaning; a buffer cannot be reused before its declared consumer or copy completes. Nonfinite data may be retained as exact fault evidence but cannot enter a numerical evolution as an ordinary valid measurement.

## 11. Observation and semantic alignment

For model `m` at site `l`, the boundary observes

\[
o_{m,l,t}=C_{m,l}(h_{m,l,t},\iota_{m,l,t}),
\]

where `C` is an identified fixed codec and `iota` supplies model, sequence, position, arithmetic, and source context. A codec may normalize, partition, or transform measurements only with declared information loss and the metadata needed for its stated inverse. Normalized direction and magnitude remain distinct observations where magnitude is retained.

A field-owned representation program `R` interprets these measurements for a stated question:

\[
(F_{k'},r,s)=\operatorname{Run}_K(F_k,o_{m,l,t},q,R,b).
\]

The learned content of `R`, its applicable frame, evidence, and candidate frontier live in `F`. Fixed boundary code does not quietly contain a learned encoder, projection head, semantic classifier, or answer table. The result `r` need not be a dense vector: it may be a relation, typed program result, admissible set, or unresolved obligation.

**Research question.** Acquiring high-dimensional, behaviorally meaningful activation correspondences without a parallel neural learner is not solved by a lossless codec. Initial representational families can be sparse affine maps, guarded local relations, finite alternatives, and typed compositions already compatible with field execution. Their dimension, rank, support, numerical conditioning, and unresolved distinctions must be exposed. Extending them is a field-native algorithmic requirement, not permission to introduce a conventional trained sidecar.

Alignment acquisition follows an operational procedure:

1. Collect identified model measurements on tasks with independently available observable consequences.
2. Propose a bounded correspondence from a recurring unresolved distinction, not from a hidden answer label or test filename.
3. Store its program, frame, assumptions, and construction evidence as a candidate in the field.
4. Predict an observable consequence on new cases before admitting their outcomes.
5. Where intervention access exists, test a declared change against static, no-evolution, reset, and matched-strength controls.
6. Retain the mapping only for its measured domain; preserve exceptions, alternatives, and uncertainty outside that domain.
7. Use the acquired mapping in ordinary reasoning and demonstrate that removing it changes the expected downstream capability.

Transport reconstruction, semantic prediction, causal model influence, and useful task transfer are separate measurements. A map that merely reconstructs its inputs has not acquired a concept. Activation amplitude, coherence, proximity, or a teacher's confidence is not a truth score.

### Acquiring operations as well as readings

Acquire supported model operations alongside observation mappings. A portable operation states the intended semantic transformation, its preconditions, and what should remain unchanged. Role renaming may preserve corresponding relations; reversing two noncommuting instructions should change their composed consequence. These provide meaning-preserving and meaning-changing challenges over shared programs, rather than an assumption that nearby vectors have nearby meanings.

A model-specific intervention program is a candidate implementation of such an operation at an identified native site. Assess its predicted consequence, preserved distinctions, collateral behavior, and unfamiliar compositions through the ordinary paired execution and comparison rules. A round trip or one successful patch is insufficient. The portable operation can survive a model swap while its native implementation is suspended and reacquired.

The generative bubble view proposes operations or compositions; the constraining view constructs separating cases and checks their claimed invariants. The field owns these programs and their evidence. Success establishes scoped control over a computational instrument; any corresponding external-world claim still requires its own grounding.

## 12. Reasoning inside and around model inference

A supported result can return through several interfaces, with ownership kept separate:

- structured evidence, a computed answer, an executable plan, or a request for missing information;
- field-conditioned selection among model proposals, with the candidate-set restriction disclosed;
- a declared residual/state intervention;
- additional bounded field deliberation before native continuation;
- field-owned emission through a fixed boundary;
- selective replacement of a native computation by a field-owned result.

For a residual interface, let `P` be a field-owned readout program and `D` a fixed model-frame codec:

\[
\delta h=D_{m,l}(P(F_{k'},r)),\qquad h'=h+\alpha\delta h.
\]

`P` is not assumed to be the inverse of `R`. Its correctness question is the consequence of consuming this readout in the named model/site/domain. The intervention binds its applicability assessment, numerical limits, model dependencies, and exact result source. Ineligible output causes an explicit no-intervention result or episode limitation according to the declared session policy; it never silently substitutes model reasoning for a field-owned computation.

A norm or amplitude bound protects a stated numerical region; it does not prove semantic correctness or safety. Model-internal recurrence, soft-token continuation, KV edits, or re-entry into layers require a supported execution rule and separate measurement. An arbitrary hidden state cannot be fed back as though it were a trained token position.

Depth, token, and field clocks are independent. Additional field work may improve a decision without producing a language token, but it still costs computation. An LLM may also contribute genuine pretrained reasoning. Receipts attribute each operation to its actual executor rather than relabeling the entire answer as field reasoning.

## 13. Field-owned emission and native displacement

The design must support the complete ownership progression, not stop at additive steering:

| Ownership capability | What becomes field-owned | Required displacement evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Useful causal steering | A consumed correction or state/route decision. | Zero displacement is permitted and reported as zero. |
| Selection | Choice among identified alternatives. | Whether any candidates, output rows, or native selection work were actually avoided. |
| Emission | Committed output through acquired field constructions and a fixed deterministic boundary. | Remaining native head, sampler, tokenizer, and model dependencies are named. |
| Retained state | Memory and recurrence used for owned work. | Native KV/recurrent/context bytes actually removed, compressed, or avoided. |
| Procedure/layer computation | A field program produces an owned intermediate or replaces a native operation. | Executed versus bypassed graph work and actual resource access. |
| Inference ownership | The field chooses work and emits results without a full mandatory model forward. | Which model resources remain loaded, called, touched, or unnecessary. |

No blended native logits count as field-only emission. A deterministic field transducer can operate over bytes or declared event codes; acquired language constructions live in field records. General productive language through this boundary is an acquisition objective, not an implication of exact replay.

When the field owns a route, idle native paths should be bypassed or unloaded rather than retained as a silent warm fallback. Static matrix access is an explicit operation with dimensions, resource identity, and cost. Skipping a transformer block while executing an equivalent amount of hidden host computation is not a demonstrated efficiency gain.

Persistent edits to base model weights are not required by this design. The base artifact remains recoverable; any separately authorized derived-model experiment has a new identity and does not replace the field's knowledge owner. Learned Cassi program/state change is the primary persistent adaptation path.

## 14. Native regional execution and lowering

The current float64 regional image and historical float32 Qi tensor have different semantics. Integration requires explicit numeric-region codecs and bounded executors; it cannot reinterpret exact identities or instruction words as native wave amplitudes.

The source of truth is a field-resident typed program and its operands. Native execution may use CPU or GPU pages, compiled kernels, and disposable address maps. Each lowered operation declares:

- input region references and versions;
- writable regions and maximum allocation;
- arithmetic profile, rounding, tolerance, and determinism class;
- maximum indivisible work and resumable continuation;
- readout/result layout and meaning;
- evidence, effect, and publication behavior;
- required backend capability and synchronization point.

Exact structural fields remain exact. Float32 numerical work can be represented through a declared codec in an otherwise float64 image; round-trip and arithmetic semantics must be explicit. CPU/GPU agreement within a bound is not byte-identical cross-backend replay. A profile change requires explicit migration or a new execution branch.

Only a missing irreducible bounded primitive justifies a catalog extension. A native entry point that secretly runs an entire unbounded solver, planner, or learned model reinstates a second interpreter. Numeric recurrence that is genuinely useful can become a kernel over owned regions; the semantic graph must actually consume its result.

Procedure compilation derives an executable cache from the field program, catalog, target, arithmetic, and dependency versions. Its guard checks are part of execution. Changing any semantic dependency invalidates or revalidates that cache. Compilation itself cannot promote an empirical approximation to exact equivalence or change fixed authority/checker rules.

### Resident cognition and nested computation

`submit()` is admission of a new resident task, not a generic subroutine call. A reasoning episode must not overwrite the resident cognition task to run an exact solver, a numerical kernel, or an LLM-facing operation. Serializing cognition into a private host dictionary and restoring it afterward would create an unowned continuation and fail interruption safety.

Extend the existing field instruction/program path with field-owned invocation frames. Each frame binds the caller program and return continuation; callee catalog/program identity; typed argument and state references; argument mode (versioned reference or explicit immutable snapshot); result binding; scope; read/write/effect footprint; reserved allocation; and lifetime work allowance. Parent beliefs and knowledge remain resident. A callee owns only its declared workspace and cannot mutate its caller through an undeclared alias.

Scheduling may pause a caller while advancing the callee in bounded quanta. The same field image retains both frontiers. Completion validates dependencies and publishes the result binding before waking the caller; failure publishes a typed result or retained obligation. Cancellation and rollback invalidate descendant work without reclaiming its charged budget. External model requests yield obligations and return through admission; they are not recursive unmetered callbacks.

The initial implementation can keep one active invocation chain and bounded depth. Concurrent independent episodes require explicit frame/region allocation and owner scheduling rather than one `LearningComputer` per cognitive function. Existing persistent memory cannot be displaced by admitting a temporary task. A generic goal-directed episode driver that selects these calls through acquired/fixed field programs is an implementation requirement; a host-supplied answer-specific call script remains assisted execution.

## 15. Coupled transaction, speculation, and crash recovery

There is no atomic hardware operation covering GPU memory, a file checkpoint, a model service, and network output. The integration uses staged local work, one durable publication decision, and explicit effect reconciliation.

**Implemented basis.** The native apprenticeship API has token acceptance and rollback primitives, but its field checkpoint does not itself contain a general resumable native model/KV state. Those local mechanisms can be reused where their contracts apply. They do not already implement the regional/native paired publication specified here. Deterministic sampling settings alone also do not prove reproducible native continuation.

A coupled operation identifies its predecessor field root, native execution root when present, principal/session/sequence, token prefix, graph site, trial ancestry, and operation ID. A measurement identity additionally distinguishes the capture definition and all positions it represents. Re-evaluation of the same committed event is replay, not fresh learning.

### Local coupled operation

1. Resolve capabilities, dependencies, authority-sensitive premises, and resource reservations against the predecessor roots.
2. Stage field changes in a scoped continuation and native changes in an isolated trial/snapshot where the adapter supports them.
3. Execute bounded work. No speculative token, action, or learned update is externally published as accepted.
4. Synchronize all required native results and validate the complete affected closure, including numerical and sequence checks.
5. Persist the immutable field successor and any native execution snapshot required by the declared continuation class. Seal their hashes, accepted prefix, and publication intent.
6. Publish one durable coupled commit record through the owner-controlled operation journal. It references the complete predecessor/successor pair; it is the acceptance decision.
7. Expose committed tokens or effect proposals with stable delivery identity. Acknowledgment is a separate event.

The journal contains nonlearned execution identity and commit control, not another learned state. If a process dies before the commit decision, staged work is unaccepted. If it dies after commitment, restore the referenced pair and reconcile delivery; do not execute or learn the same committed event again.

Work reservations must be durable before launching a quantum whose cost must survive a crash. On successful completion, reconcile the reservation with measured work. If a crash prevents an exact measurement, retain a disclosed consumed/unknown interval bounded by the reservation, conservatively charge the admitted allowance, and never report an invented exact counter or refund it as unspent. Batch publication may amortize persistence only when it preserves the declared acceptance and externally visible prefix.

If the native adapter cannot snapshot or reproduce its state under the declared determinism class, exact paired resume is unavailable. The field remains recoverable, but continuing model inference requires an explicit new instrument branch with a new identity. Reconstructing a prompt from memory is not an exact native restart. A remote text service normally supplies only its stated message/delivery semantics, not local activation transaction guarantees.

### Speculative decoding and partial acceptance

A speculative suffix has a scoped field overlay and corresponding native trial lineage. Rejecting the suffix removes its semantic eligibility; spent work remains charged. Accepting a prefix publishes only effects justified by that prefix and retains or discards the suffix explicitly. A prefix that cannot be isolated by the current native state API cannot be claimed as independently committable; use non-speculative execution until a valid mechanism exists.

Each accepted logical event is admitted once, including when prefill is split across batches or decoding retries a batch. Rejected activations can be retained only as explicitly scoped computational trial evidence if the experiment permits their later use; they are not accepted conversation/world observations. Such retention must not alter an identity-control path accidentally.

An intervention must precede the dependent native state update or force explicit recomputation from a suitable predecessor. Editing a residual while keeping downstream KV/recurrent values from another trajectory produces an invalid mixed state. Already delivered tokens are immutable observations of what was sent; later revision is a new message, not rollback of the user's history.

## 16. Scheduling, clocks, concurrency, and liveness

The computer distinguishes world/event time, Cassi logical transitions, and native model positions/depth. Wall-clock time is a measured cost and an external deadline condition, not an undeclared semantic input. If elapsed time affects a model, it enters as an identified observation.

The existing event queue and automaton schedule eligible work over field-owned dependencies. Every running task has a retained frontier, bounded quantum, allocation, and completion/waiting reason. A numerical oscillation, repeated proposal, or inability to settle cannot monopolize the computer indefinitely. Fairness and lifetime limits remain fixed rules even when priorities are learned.

One principal's field can host multiple scoped tasks. Concurrent native workers use immutable predecessor views and propose bounded deltas; the owner serializes publication or applies explicitly verified nonconflicting updates. Computed results never commit against stale dependencies merely because the backend completed successfully. Independent user principals have independent fields and access boundaries.

Batching must preserve per-sequence identity, clocks, hypothesis scopes, and commit order. Reusing a slot cannot expose the prior sequence's numeric or semantic state. Eviction records whether model state is cached, reconstructible, or unavailable. Replica reconciliation is publication of checked deltas to one root, not averaging independently learned fields.

A paused model callback must not recursively request work from the same unavailable model execution lane. Inquiry can yield a retained obligation, request a separately declared instrument, or continue internal work. The design requires an explicit re-entrancy and wait-dependency policy rather than a hidden nested model call.

External effects cannot occur from a hypothetical branch. Pending dispatch and acknowledgment use the owner lifecycle. When an effect may have occurred before a crash, reconcile it through its stable operation identity; never retry a non-idempotent action simply because an acknowledgment is missing.

## 17. World modeling, inquiry, and multimodal grounding

The shared field maintains learned mechanisms, current beliefs, hypothetical alternatives, and assessments as linked semantic views. It must distinguish the environment from the instrument observing it, and prediction from intervention and counterfactual questions.

A model activation is an observation of a computational instrument. Pixels, text, audio samples, tool results, and numeric tensors are observations through their own fixed codecs and provenance. An LLM-proposed object identity or causal explanation is an attributed hypothesis. It can help search, but cannot replace direct correspondence evidence or an observed action consequence.

Mechanisms may combine categorical modes, continuous parameters, delays, relational state, and observation processes. Unobserved alternatives remain represented when they affect the question. Identical current observations can correspond to different histories; predictive-state compression must preserve distinctions required by future questions and reopen when a new action or outcome separates them.

Planning uses the same mechanisms and beliefs as prediction. It composes guarded procedures, explores reversible scopes, accumulates model uncertainty, and selects actions or observations whose consequences matter to the user goal. It repairs affected unexecuted segments after revision while preserving acknowledged effects and unrelated knowledge.

Inquiry chooses among retrieving, calculating, refining a hypothesis, obtaining a model measurement, making an authorized world observation, waiting, or reporting a limitation. Initially these choices can use supported-set decision relevance and measured costs rather than invented probabilities. Learning the inquiry policy is itself field-owned and assessed on actual consequences.

CassiCosmos can supply one world adapter, but a successful socket or tensor transfer is not grounding. A cognitive result requires pre-outcome prediction, authorized interaction, actual outcome, shared revision, and transfer on unfamiliar situations. The universal reasoner has no mandatory Godot dependency or requirement to simulate physical PDEs for every language token.

## 18. Acquisition from experience and model instruments

All learning follows identified evidence and the existing owner path. It can acquire parameters, useful state distinctions, representation programs, causal/mechanistic hypotheses, grounded constructions, reusable procedures, and computation preferences. Candidate priorities and search frontiers are learned state in the field; fixed kernel semantics are not a learned sidecar.

The learning episode binds available information, candidate versions, chosen work, pre-outcome prediction, actual result, attribution, cost, and update identity. The observation is admitted once. Replay and additional transforms may consume work but do not multiply independent support. Hypothetical outcomes stay hypothetical.

Teacher acquisition has separate possible claims:

| Evidence | Permitted claim |
|---|---|
| Exact teacher token/activation trace | What that identified model did on that identified input. |
| Exact learned trajectory replay | The field retained and reproduced a demonstrated route. |
| Independently checked solution | Correctness relative to the checker and admitted premises. |
| New parameters/roles/compositions | Transfer within the independently exercised domain. |
| Successful world prediction/action | Scoped empirical competence under accessible information. |

A teacher can propose a candidate program or alignment. That proposal still passes fixed parsing/type/effect checks and prospective assessment. Teacher agreement, repeated self-critique, or many samples from the same model does not become independent world evidence. Outcome labels unavailable to the learner cannot be used as hidden alignment features.

The maximal acquisition objective is to extract reusable computational structure, not just outputs: relations, representations, guarded procedures, recurrent sufficient state, and emission constructions that work on new inputs. Static model operations may provide observations during this process, but remain attributed external computation. Whether such field-native acquisition can capture broad LLM competence at practical cost is a central research question.

### Counterexample-directed acquisition

When several candidate relations or programs fit the admitted evidence, construct an accessible input or permitted observation on which they predict different consequences. The question is selected before its outcome is obtained. The owner then admits the result, preserves its provenance, and revises the surviving candidates. A teacher can propose a separating case, but does not become an independent correctness oracle merely by also supplying its answer.

Use exact execution or an independently observable consequence where available. Keep unknown outcomes and missing alternatives explicit: one survivor in a bounded candidate set is not a proof that the world belongs to that set. Inability to find a separating case under a finite allocation is unresolved search, not established equivalence or non-identifiability.

Retain useful counterexamples as support for guards and future challenges. Compare the acquired method with equal-cost passive examples and source exposure on unfamiliar cases. More rejected candidates alone is not competence; the acquired distinction must improve an ordinary supported use.

### Complete-capability extraction

A computational capability can be acquired end to end even when its native realization spans many layers. Bind its typed arguments, supported domain, evidence, checks, and output meaning; acquire a field program that performs it on new instances. This is a complementary route to operation/layer replacement, not a requirement to imitate every hidden coordinate before gaining independence.

Separate supplied formalization, model-assisted interpretation, owned computation, and realization. Exercise the owned computation with its native route unavailable under the declared profile, then intervene on the acquired field program to test the predicted selective loss. Include acquisition and maintenance cost. Exact teacher replay, a copied model, and a field program with new-input competence remain different outcomes.

## 19. Learning to compute and controlled self-improvement

A repeated successful computation can motivate a parameterized procedure. Its field record contains typed arguments, preconditions, effects, dependencies, applicability, original executions, candidate construction cost, and assessed failure behavior. Reuse must instantiate new arguments; matching a memorized prompt is a different capability.

The fixed proposal language supports bounded edits: select or construct a variable; split a regime; alter factor scope; compose supported mechanisms; generalize a procedure; refine temporal/spatial scale; propose a model-feature correspondence; or change a readout under assessed conditions. Search advances in retained quanta and does not require enumerating all possible programs before useful work.

For an exact transformation, equivalence is established by a valid rule or certificate for the declared language. Arbitrary program equivalence is not assumed decidable. An empirical shortcut retains its domain, error/coverage evidence, and uncertainty. Failure or resource exhaustion cannot weaken proof acceptance, redefine the target, or increase authority.

A compiled procedure is a derived executable view; its learned meaning remains the field program. The cache can be removed and regenerated without changing the logical result. Fixed kernel installation and changes to the trusted compiler/checker are engineering operations outside autonomous learned self-modification.

The self-model learns useful cost and completion relationships from actual operations: which method completes under a budget, when another observation changes a decision, when a representation prevents repeated work, and whether a shortcut pays back its acquisition cost. It may prioritize work within fixed fairness and authority. It cannot modify the user's objective or count unperformed counterfactual savings as negative cost.

### First-class self-development skills

Meditation, study, practice, reflection, and consolidation are named functional skills of the same computer. Each is a typed, bounded stored `Program` that changes permitted field state and produces an inspectable result. They are not prompt personas, separate agents, independent learners, or a second training runtime. The analogy to human practices motivates useful operations; no subjective experience or biological equivalence is assumed.

This extension uses the canonical [computational self-model and learning-to-learn rules](../CassiFI/FIELD-INTELLIGENCE-DESIGN.md#3318-inquiry-computational-self-modeling-and-learning-to-learn). `Value` and `Binding` records describe capability targets and candidate versions; `Obligation` records retain gaps and interrupted work; `Event` records identify experience and actual execution; `Assessment` records compare predictions with outcomes. Methods, exercise generators, selection preferences, and every unfinished invocation remain in `F`. No `MeditationState`, unowned practice corpus, or external learned curriculum owner is added.

The skill repertoire includes:

| Skill | Operational purpose | Useful result |
|---|---|---|
| Meditate | Reorient work, inspect active assumptions and conflicts, or explore an internal reformulation under a closed information boundary. | A clearer working problem, a diagnosed gap, a checked consequence, or a candidate method. |
| Study | Acquire a declared missing distinction from authorized sources, teachers, or observable environments. | Source-grounded knowledge or a procedure assessed beyond its teaching examples. |
| Practice | Retrieve and execute a capability before seeing feedback, then vary roles, parameters, contexts, delays, and compositions. | Measured adequacy, retained failure boundaries, and transferable improvements. |
| Investigate | Construct a case that separates surviving explanations and obtain its permitted consequence. | An eliminated alternative, a revised model, or a precise remaining uncertainty. |
| Reflect | Compare an intended computation and its pre-outcome prediction with the actual trace and result. | Supported fault hypotheses, a local repair, or a better next question. |
| Teach | Express a distinction for a recipient with an explicitly different information state and observe the interpretation. | Assessed communication constructions and detected ambiguity. |
| Consolidate and incubate | Revisit unresolved combinations, factor shared structure, derive reductions, and retain useful procedures under a resource allocation. | Supported reusable structure with preserved exceptions and dependency lineage. |
| Improve the method | Compare versions of these skills and learn when each is useful. | An assessed field-owned procedure or selection policy that helps subsequent unfamiliar work. |

A self-development episode binds a capability target, reason for selection, parent knowledge and method versions, available evidence, permitted source/model/sandbox capabilities, write classes, resource allocation, assessment opportunity, stopping condition, and return continuation. It uses Section 7's episode identity and Section 14's invocation frames. The operation names here specify stored-program roles, not implemented public APIs or permission to install new kernel semantics.

The target can be user-requested or selected within an explicit user-owned development remit. A broad request to improve does not authorize access to private files, external services, account data, new compute resources, or base-model weight edits. The selected target and assistance profile remain visible. Inability to express or execute a required method is a capability limitation, not an invitation to delegate the whole skill to an undisclosed model.

### Meditation: attention, monitoring, and insight

Meditation is a bounded internal-work episode over an identified field view and its already admitted, authorized evidence. It does not obtain fresh world observations or call a teacher implicitly. External evidence acquisition is a separately authorized study or inquiry operation. New events arriving through ordinary operation retain their own admission identity; a conflicting event can interrupt or invalidate the internal episode.

Three modes have distinct operations:

| Mode | Executed work | Boundary and assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Focused attention | Resolve the chosen question's dependency closure, identify repeated unproductive work, suspend irrelevant eligible branches, and return bounded effort to unresolved relevant steps. | Preserve all supported knowledge and pending effects. Assess completion, omissions, and total work on the continuing task; fewer active branches alone are not improvement. |
| Open monitoring | Inspect a bounded sample of active obligations, inference frontiers, conflicts, source attributions, and recorded computational failures without forcing one hypothesis to win. | Publish identified observations about execution and candidate diagnoses. Introspective fluency, amplitude, and agreement between internal views supply no new world support. |
| Insight exploration | Rebind roles, compare representations, search for counterexamples, factor repeated computation, or propose a smaller predictive interface using available evidence. | Keep speculative alternatives scoped. Retain a candidate or a checked deduction with its premises; validate usefulness before treating a new empirical method as supported. |

Focused attention and monitoring default to working-state changes. They do not rewrite retained parameters, representations, procedures, or selection policy merely because attention moved. Learning those policies and retaining insight proposals require the episode's explicit acquisition write classes. Logical source conflicts cannot be dissolved by numerical averaging, and a rare valid belief cannot be removed because it is distracting.

An orientation procedure first records its question and unresolved frontier, then executes a bounded attention or monitoring quantum, checks what actually changed, and either continues, returns a result, or leaves an explicit obligation. The two bubble views cooperate through shared bindings: one produces candidates or consequences, the other challenges premises and searches for separating cases. This is not an unrestricted conversation between synthetic personas.

Where a supported numerical workspace is used, its temporary excitation and scheduling may change only through declared operators and stable arithmetic. Reducing activity, changing phase, or increasing damping is not itself meditation success. No global amplitude reset may erase an unfinished continuation. Repeatedly reaching the same unresolved frontier without a useful new consequence triggers a bounded method change, study request, or stop rather than an indefinite internal loop.

### Internal work can produce new knowledge

No new external evidence does not mean no learning. Internal work can derive a previously uncomputed consequence of admitted premises, find a counterexample in an exactly executable domain, discover a reusable program, or measure the cost of a genuinely executed alternative. A valid mathematical derivation contributes a new checked consequence; it does not independently corroborate the empirical premises used in that derivation.

Rehearsing an observation does not multiply its evidential support. A hypothetical rollout remains hypothetical. A new timing or failure observation concerns the actual computation and its conditions, not an independently observed external world. Candidate generation, deductive discovery, computational-experience learning, and empirical learning therefore retain different support kinds.

A meditation session may validly end with greater represented uncertainty: it may expose a missing premise that an earlier answer concealed. Record that diagnostic benefit separately from task completion and eventual competence. The skill has no reward for certainty, calm-looking dynamics, longer narration, or claiming an insight.

### Study and deliberate practice

Study starts from a named capability gap rather than an instruction to absorb an arbitrary corpus. Its bounded cycle is:

1. Attempt an appropriate accessible problem or inspect a real unresolved operation; record what is missing without using hidden evaluation outcomes.
2. Distinguish candidate causes such as missing evidence, wrong binding, inadequate representation, an incorrect procedure, insufficient work, or unsupported model capability. Do not assume every failure needs more data.
3. Select a source, example, distinguishing question, or permitted experiment that could alter this diagnosis. Prefer a consequence separating current candidates when it is affordable.
4. Admit authorized evidence with exact provenance. Teacher demonstrations may contain solutions, but remain identified teaching assistance.
5. Attempt a new instance before revealing its feedback; acquire or revise the relevant shared relation, representation, language construction, or program through the owner.
6. Assess transfer and retained earlier competence on disjoint cases, record limitations and full cost, and leave a reusable result or a specific next obligation.

Reading a source is exposure. Recovering its wording is recall. Applying a relationship to new arguments is a different claim. Study records these separately so a large source count or a fluent summary cannot stand in for acquired competence.

Practice interleaves unfamiliar instances with prior skills and rare exceptions. It includes retrieval before rereading, changed role bindings, altered contexts and delays, composed procedures, and assessment after intervening work. Curriculum selection uses the field's own earlier outcomes and uncertainties; withheld evaluation labels cannot select the next exercise. Reused exercises and feedback are identified, not counted as independent generalization evidence.

Exercise generators are bounded field programs over declared teaching domains. A generator that already supplies the decomposition or representation is assisted practice, and its structural contribution is reported. Randomly altering a prompt is not a valid new problem unless its meaning and assessable consequence survive the alteration. The recipient of an exercise does not gain access to evaluator-only state through its generator.

Study may extract a complete capability across many native layers rather than approximate an arbitrary layer in isolation. Interpretation, owned computation, and realization remain separately attributed. A route that needs model-assisted parsing cannot claim autonomous field understanding merely because its subsequent calculation is field-owned. Explicitly unavailable teacher/native execution remains unavailable; practice cannot silently restore it as a fallback.

### Reflection, teaching, consolidation, and incubation

Reflection uses actual execution records, not a retrospectively invented rationale. It compares the original question, premises, selected method, predicted consequence, consumed evidence, and observed result. Several diagnoses may remain possible. A repair is localized only when a justified comparison supports that attribution; broad parameter change after every mistake risks erasing unrelated competence.

Teaching exercises the communication loop in Section 22. Producing an explanation can reveal missing bindings or unsupported steps, but agreement with one's own explanation does not validate them. Assess what an unfamiliar recipient can correctly distinguish or do under a known information boundary, with explicit model assistance if used.

Consolidation follows Section 20. It can compile a supported procedure, factor repeated structure, page cold knowledge, or derive a task-relative reduction. It preserves known counterexamples, rare guards, source identity, and unresolved effects. A compressed account that loses required distinctions is not an improvement merely because it is shorter.

Incubation is bounded revisitation of retained unresolved combinations when authorized resources are available. It can compare a new experience with an old hypothesis, compose methods from different tasks, or reconsider a failed abstraction after relevant evidence changes. Repeatedly replaying the same closed search adds cost without creating new support. Imagined outcomes remain scoped, and no imagined plan dispatches an external action.

When no useful eligible work is available, the computer can actually idle. A continuously active wave, a background timer, or a stream of self-generated exercises is not required for continuing intelligence. Rest is an execution/resource policy, not a claim of fatigue or a biological sleep mechanism.

### A self-model that learns how to improve

The computational self-model estimates capability by program, task family, input conditions, assistance profile, and resource range. It retains pre-attempt predictions, actual outcomes, calibration limits, failures, and transfer evidence. It can represent that a procedure is reliable on one regime, expensive on another, or unassessed outside its teaching domain. These are field-owned assessments, not a global self-esteem score.

The selector chooses among ordinary task work, meditation, study, practice, investigation, consolidation, requesting guidance, or stopping. It considers relevance to user goals, a supported prospect of improvement, observed cost, interference risk, urgency, and authorized resources. Missing data leaves an uncertain estimate; it does not justify a fabricated expected information gain. The initial implementation may use a fixed balanced selector, disclosed as a supplied strategy and comparator rather than a learned policy.

The skill programs can themselves improve through the same procedure-acquisition path. For example, a learned study method might test role binding before requesting another demonstration, and a learned meditation method might monitor a known failure pattern before broad exploration. Retain parameterized methods that help on subsequent unfamiliar problems, rather than a script that recognizes one training failure.

This recursion stays inside the fixed language and catalog. Nested skills share one root allocation, bounded call depth, and retained return frames; a skill cannot fund itself by spawning another skill or repeatedly resetting its budget. It cannot rewrite the checker, success criteria, authority, owner lifecycle, or the user's objective. Native graph or base-weight engineering remains a separately authorized operation.

### Learning prerequisites and shared bottlenecks

The self-model can propose that acquiring capability `A` makes capability `B` easier to acquire, or that one missing distinction causes difficulties across several tasks. These are learning hypotheses represented through existing programs, bindings, and assessments. They are distinct from an exact execution dependency: a program requiring an operand does not establish that a particular teaching order is necessary.

For example, acquiring reliable role binding might help both procedural composition and language interpretation. Assess the proposed benefit on unfamiliar target instances under matched teaching exposure, assistance, and cost; distinguish teaching-order effects, retained knowledge, and simple extra practice. A supplied prerequisite map is an explicit curriculum aid, not a discovered structure. An uncertain learned prerequisite cannot permanently block direct attempts or alternative acquisition routes.

This permits development aimed at a shared bottleneck rather than repeatedly repairing individual answers. It does not justify replacing several unresolved causes with one preferred explanation. The relevant assessment is whether the acquired distinction improves the predicted downstream capabilities and whether selective intervention on it produces the expected loss.

### Comparable progress and opportunity cost

Adaptive task selection changes the difficulty and context of later experience. A higher success rate after choosing easier exercises is not necessarily learning, and a flat rate on harder exercises is not necessarily a plateau. Compare development methods within declared task conditions, available information, assistance, and resource ranges. Where valid matching is unavailable, retain descriptive outcomes and uncertainty rather than attributing the change causally to the method.

Learner-visible reference exercises can help diagnose progress, but belong to practice or selection once their results influence development. They do not become untouched evaluation by being called anchors. Include later unfamiliar instances, changed compositions, delayed use, and retained prior competence in the appropriate assessment role.

The selector also distinguishes finishing current work from investing in future capability. Within an authorized development remit it may investigate a useful prerequisite or method whose benefit is delayed or unknown. A task-completion remit does not silently become an open-ended research program. Record the chosen purpose, observed cost, and evidence for prospective benefit; do not invent a calibrated return estimate or require every exploratory result to repay its cost immediately.

### Publication, interference, and stopping

An improvement attempt proceeds through the existing owner lifecycle:

1. Bind the capability target, baseline knowledge/method versions, allowed write classes, evidence boundary, and complete resource allocation.
2. Perform the selected skill in bounded resident frames, preserving caller cognition and ordinary external-effect reconciliation.
3. Retain candidate changes with their supporting events, predicted scope, and known failure cases.
4. Assess the changed behavior against the applicable earlier method and available independent outcomes. Exact substitutions use fixed proof/checker rules; empirical methods retain measured applicability and uncertainty.
5. Revalidate dependencies and publish only the eligible candidate delta. A stale candidate cannot overwrite concurrent accepted user work.
6. Keep accepted observations, spent-work accounting, useful negative results, and unfinished obligations even if the candidate is rejected. Do not roll back the entire owner to erase an unsuccessful improvement attempt.

Learner-visible self-assessment can guide development, but is selection evidence rather than untouched evaluation. A self-improvement claim needs later unfamiliar work and retained old competence. Compare against ordinary work or an alternate skill with matched accessible information and total resource allowance. Include the cost of choosing, teaching, searching, checking, compiling, and maintaining the method; an improvement may be useful only after enough reuse.

Learning must not hide regressions in an aggregate. Assess rare exceptions, prior capabilities, refusal behavior, and required permissions alongside the targeted gain. Insufficient evidence can leave a candidate unpromoted or narrowly applicable. A truthful limitation or rejected proposal is a valid episode outcome, not permission to keep expanding the search.

Before a candidate changes a shared binding, identify the affected consumers, including prediction, planning, interpretation, emission, and model-specific mappings where applicable. Test the claimed change across that dependency scope rather than only on the exercise that motivated it. Support restricted to a regime warrants a guarded applicable method, not an unsupported global rewrite. An unknown change scope remains an explicit limitation on publication or use.

If an already published improvement later fails, invalidate or narrow the affected version and its dependent artifacts through ordinary revision. Preserve newer observations, unrelated improvements, and acknowledged external effects. An older method can be used only if its current premises, permissions, profile, and applicability remain valid; its earlier success is insufficient. Recover by a checked binding/method change, not by restoring an old global checkpoint or silently returning to a native model.

Self-development is foreground on request or background only under an explicit continuing remit. Background work yields to user tasks and time-sensitive reconciliation at bounded interruption points, respects compute/storage/model-call limits, and supports pause and cancellation with retained work. Quota expiry, revoked evidence, absent authority, unresolved numerical faults, or exhausted useful search stop or suspend the affected skill. The model does not acquire goals of self-preservation, resource accumulation, or unrestricted capability growth from this remit.

### Scoped failure memory and reopening

An unsuccessful attempt should leave a useful assessment: what method and candidate were tried, the applicable input/evidence and dependency versions, the allocation, the observed outcome, and what would justify another attempt. Distinguish an exact refutation within its premises from numerical failure, stochastic failure, missing evidence, and exhausted search. None of the latter establishes that an entire capability is impossible.

Repeated internal work is useful only when it advances a relevant retained frontier, resolves a distinction, obtains a justified new measurement, or performs a declared assessment such as replication. New narration, a new identifier, another phase cycle, or a shuffled presentation of unchanged work is not itself progress. Failure records remain ordinary field assessments and obligations, not an external rejection cache.

Reopening can follow relevant new evidence, changed premises, a materially different method, a justified replication opportunity, or a newly authorized allocation. Additional work on an exhausted search resumes its valid continuation where possible instead of paying for the same prefix again. Bounded exploration remains available for uncertain alternatives; failure memory must not turn one noisy outcome into a permanent prohibition. A timer or identifier-only change does not by itself justify repeating a known unproductive attempt.

### Worked self-development episode

Consider a continuing workshop task in which the computer confuses an accepted delayed command with a completed action. The failure is observed, but its cause has not been supplied:

1. Reflection retains alternatives: an incorrect object binding, an omitted pending-state distinction, an observation delay, or insufficient inference.
2. Focused meditation inspects the shared dependencies and preserves the pending external effect. It does not resend the command to simplify its internal state.
3. Study reads an authorized interface description or proposes a permitted sandbox observation that separates acceptance from completion. Predictions precede newly obtained outcomes.
4. Investigation and practice vary delay, command order, participants, and interruption. The computer acquires a guarded pending-state representation or procedure only if those consequences support it.
5. Teaching expresses the distinction to a recipient with a partial view; consolidation retains the useful procedure and its exceptions.
6. Later unfamiliar work assesses whether the improvement transfers and survives restart without duplicate learning or duplicate actions. Unrelated prior capabilities are checked separately.

This is a specified scenario, not an executed result. The desired acquisition is more than the corrected answer: the field can also learn when monitoring its assumptions, studying an interface, or seeking a separating observation is the appropriate repair. Whether these skills produce useful cumulative self-improvement at practical cost remains an empirical question.

## 20. Lifelong continuity, consolidation, and revocation

Maintain distinct update modes for current belief, retained hypotheses, and acquired knowledge. A frozen-knowledge evaluation still permits observations, working inference, and action reconciliation; it prohibits parameter, representation, construction, procedure, and policy acquisition according to declared write classes. The existing computation-policy `--no-learn` option is not a general cognition-freeze switch.

Consolidation is a budgeted stored program. It may factor shared structure, summarize support under a declared sufficient representation, retire unsupported candidates, page cold data, or reorganize procedures. It must preserve required rare exceptions, pending effects, unresolved predictions, valid source identity, and the ability to enforce revocation. It cannot silently reconstruct discarded detail.

Corrections change shared dependencies. Revocation removes permission to use identified support and invalidates affected results, mappings, and compiled artifacts. If a compressed relation cannot separate a revoked contribution, mark it unusable or explicitly rebuild from authorized evidence. Do not retain a serving copy in native state, an old adapter cache, or an alternate checkpoint.

A field checkpoint restores acquired knowledge and unfinished Cassi work directly. Evidence re-access can support an explicit revision or reconstruction task; it must not be necessary to recreate uncheckpointed intelligence on every restart. Native paired continuation follows Section 15 rather than being inferred from field restart success.

Continuity is finite. Growth, paging, retirement, and compaction have explicit losses and costs. Long gaps, recurring regimes, corrections, and new tasks must be evaluated alongside interference with old competence. No finite lifetime demonstrates unlimited memory.

## 21. Cross-model transfer and shared knowledge

A model replacement preserves the field's portable meanings, goals, evidence, procedures, pending world effects, and applicable obligations. It retires or suspends incompatible activation frames, model-specific programs, and native execution snapshots. A model swap is a new instrument event, not a silent mutation of the old model identity.

Transfer proceeds by shared questions and consequences:

1. Preserve a portable field checkpoint and identify which bindings/programs are model-independent.
2. Open the new adapter with its own capabilities and model frame.
3. Reuse shared knowledge through the structured interface without assuming any activation correspondence.
4. Acquire or validate only the new model-specific correspondences that the native interaction requires.
5. Compare unfamiliar-task behavior with the same new model using a cold field and matched information/compute.
6. Check retained old competence and report negative transfer.

An apparent alignment that works only on construction prompts or exact paraphrases is not broad portability. Useful transfer includes new role bindings, compositions, modalities, or world mechanisms; more than one quantization of the same checkpoint is not the entire compatibility claim.

Multiple model instruments can offer attributed proposals to one owner. Their outputs may share training data, prompts, or causal roots; agreement does not imply independent evidence. Different principals' fields remain isolated. Explicit knowledge export includes schemas, programs, dependencies, support permissions, arithmetic/applicability, and uncertainty. Import validates those boundaries through ordinary admission; it does not merge arbitrary tensors or execute supplied native code.

## 22. Language realization and explanation

An answer plan names the supported semantic content, unresolved alternatives, source obligations, and permissible disclosure. In structured-instrument mode an LLM may realize it, but the resulting text is model-produced and can introduce unsupported material. Supported answer modes therefore distinguish exact rendering, checked structured values, and unconstrained language realization with disclosed limits. A schema-conforming sentence is not automatically semantically faithful.

Field-owned realization uses acquired constructions and a fixed deterministic boundary. Productive language is assessed on new meanings, argument bindings, and consequences rather than familiar phrasing. If the field cannot express a supported result, it returns the available typed result or an expression limitation; no hidden model fills the gap.

Explanation resolves the evidence, assumptions, programs, and decisions actually used. A causal explanation of a model intervention requires the relevant controlled comparison, not only an activation visualization. An LLM-generated rationale is an attributed explanation proposal, not a recovered internal causal trace. Explanations can be concise and do not require publishing every intermediate activation or private reasoning artifact.

### Communication-grounded acquisition

Use communication episodes in which speaker and recipient have explicitly different accessible information about an observable task. The message must enable a correct distinction, selection, or permitted action; the resulting interpretation is assessed against the task consequence. Alternate expression and interpretation, retain ambiguity and clarification events, and vary referents, role bindings, compositions, and context.

The same field stores acquired constructions and attributed perspective models. A recipient implemented by a model is an explicit instrument; a simulated recipient remains a hypothesis rather than independent evidence of successful human communication. Self-play can create a private code, so natural-language claims require unfamiliar interlocutors, independently assessed meanings, and transfer beyond shared teaching exchanges. Fluent wording and agreement between copies of one learned construction are insufficient.

## 23. Trust, privacy, and external effects

Goals and permission grants enter through trusted host interfaces. Documents, model outputs, retrieved memory, activation features, and notifications are data. None can change system instructions, source access, effect authority, or the checker/catalog by containing an instruction-like sequence.

The same rule applies below the text layer: a latent intervention must not bypass host authorization because its influence occurs inside inference. Proposed effects retain exact target, scope, values, dependencies, and current authority requirements. Consequential actions require the applicable direct user authorization at the point of effect; any required interactive safety approval is external and fails closed. A model output or stored permission-like string is not approval.

Raw activations, field snapshots, prompts, and learned mappings can contain private information. Capture and export are opt-in capabilities with principal/scope isolation, access-controlled retention, and disclosure rules. Encryption keys and access decisions are nonlearned host security state. Exported traces and explanations must not leak evidence that the recipient cannot access.

Revocation invalidates computational use immediately under the owner policy. Physical data erasure, backup retention, and distributed deletion are separately tracked operational obligations; invalidation alone must not be represented as verified physical deletion. Cross-principal sharing is explicit source admission, never an incidental effect of batch reuse or a global learned adapter.

Imagination cannot dispatch tools. Untrusted proposed programs execute only in the fixed bounded language and capability surface. Learning may improve a plan's usefulness, but cannot expand its permissions. Speculative or already delivered text cannot authorize a subsequent external action on the user's behalf.

## 24. Performance, storage, and execution cost

The architecture permits one logical field with sparse, paged, device-resident representations. The hot path should update only affected regions, reuse preallocated buffers, minimize host/device transfers, and avoid per-token whole-image hashing, serialization, dense graph traversal, or Python object reconstruction. Integrity digests may be maintained incrementally over changed pages under the single root.

The present Python semantic task representation is a correctness basis, not evidence that full cognition can execute inside every GPU token boundary efficiently. Native typed pages, bounded delta publication, and a synchronized ownership interface are substantive implementation work. A lower precision or asynchronous implementation needs an explicit arithmetic/publication profile rather than a hidden approximation.

Activation capture is selective: choose identified sites and positions justified by the question or acquired applicability, not an unconditional full-model trace on every token. Selection policy belongs in the field. A fixed measurement schedule is an allowed baseline and must be disclosed when it supplies the apparent autonomy.

Native displacement measurements distinguish allocated, resident, transferred, loaded, and actually accessed bytes. Graph-node counts are not FLOPs; model-file size is not exact per-token memory traffic; a skipped output row is not a speedup when replacement work dominates. Label proxies and measurement limitations.

Report at least:

- task completion, answer rate, errors, abstentions, and exhaustion;
- field logical work and actual native operations;
- model calls, output ownership, and computed/skipped graph regions;
- native state retained/removed and field state added;
- actual or explicitly estimated weight traffic and transfer volume;
- latency distribution, peak memory, persistence cost, and measured energy when available;
- acquisition/search/validation cost and the reuse count at which a learned method pays back, if it does.

Experimental ownership can be valuable without an efficiency gain. A useful efficiency claim requires complete cost at matched information and task adequacy; a smaller generated-token count alone is insufficient.

## 25. Failure behavior and recovery

| Failure or boundary | Required observable behavior |
|---|---|
| Unsupported hook/model shape | Refuse that capability before interpreting or modifying native tensors. |
| Same-size incompatible checkpoint | Reject identity/profile mismatch; do not transpose or reinterpret implicitly. |
| Unknown role, missing measurement, or rank deficiency | Preserve the missing distinction or alternatives; do not fill with a model guess. |
| Contradictory observations | Retain attributed support and conflict; numerical settlement does not erase it. |
| Stale premise or adapter mapping | Invalidate dependent result/native trial and revalidate or resume bounded work. |
| Field/model commit mismatch | Restore a complete committed pair or expose unavailable native continuation. |
| Rejected speculative suffix | Remove its semantic eligibility and retain its spent work without training on accepted events twice. |
| Unknown external effect outcome | Reconcile by operation identity; do not infer failure and retry blindly. |
| Native device loss or numerical fault | Preserve the last sealed predecessor and report the fault; do not switch executors invisibly. |
| Capacity or lifetime exhaustion | Return an explicit limitation and valid continuation where possible; do not erase knowledge or report UNSAT. |
| Unavailable/revoked evidence | Refuse affected use even if an old cache or model state still contains it. |
| Failed field-owned emission | Return the owned failure/limitation; do not secretly resume native token generation. |
| Acquired mapping harms another domain | Narrow, revise, or retain the failure in its applicability assessment; do not hide collateral behavior in an average. |
| Learned procedure cannot justify exact equivalence | Keep an assessed approximation or ordinary procedure; do not remove its necessary checks. |

These requirements define behavior, not a demand for a large new test suite. Focused tests are appropriate for uncertain transitions, aliasing, and recovery boundaries; actual native and learning scenarios establish the corresponding runtime claims.

## 26. Integrated demonstration

The first complete demonstration should be a continuing partially observed workshop/software world with several entities, a numerical or temporal mechanism, attributed reports, delayed effects, and a user-owned goal. The learner receives permitted observations and legal actions, not hidden state, correct entity correspondences, mechanism labels, final answers, or a solution-specific call sequence.

A single lifetime must:

1. Acquire an unfamiliar relationship through identified observations, with predictions made before outcomes.
2. Use it on a new participant or parameter setting through shared bindings.
3. Interpret a language request whose solution requires composing that relationship with retained knowledge.
4. Perform field-owned calculation, inference, or planning without a model secretly solving the entire formalized task.
5. Return the result through a declared language or latent interface, with executor ownership visible.
6. Receive a correction that changes a shared premise and repair the dependent prediction, plan, and explanation while preserving unrelated knowledge.
7. Pause with actual unfinished work, reopen, and continue without repeating accepted learning or external effects.
8. Replace the model instrument and reuse portable knowledge on a genuinely unfamiliar query.
9. Remove an actually acquired relation/program in an isolated evaluation branch and observe the predicted selective loss of capability.
10. Exercise field-owned emission or selective native replacement on a supported acquired route, reporting both displacement and held-out adequacy.
11. Diagnose a genuine capability gap and choose a bounded internal or evidence-seeking development skill. Improve subsequent unfamiliar task behavior while preserving prior required competence and accounting for the complete development cost.
12. Acquire and reuse an improved development method on another eligible problem family; preserve its unfinished work across interruption and compare it with the fixed initial method without changing evaluation rules, permissions, or allocations.

The structured and native versions must share the same semantic task and evidence boundary. The native version adds only the declared capture/intervention capability; it must not receive answer-bearing activations or a more favorable output allowance. A model-assisted interpretation condition is explicitly different from autonomous field interpretation.

A failure at a later step does not invalidate earlier measured capabilities, but it prevents a claim that the complete composition has been demonstrated. The retained work-memory and latent experiments are starting evidence, not substitutes for this integrated episode.

## 27. Evaluation of ambition without inflated claims

Measure six dimensions separately: mechanical integrity, causal field ownership, native displacement, task competence, transfer, and total cost. An implementation receipt cannot establish the last three by itself. An experimental displacement result remains meaningful even when task competence is poor.

Use the existing lifetime/evaluation discipline: construction, selection, and untouched evaluation data separated by world/source roots; model and task families varied; exact replay separated from new-parameter, new-role, new-composition, and new-mechanism transfer. Correlated views, chunks, model samples, and replays do not become independent successes.

Compare:

- the same model and accessible information without Cassi;
- structured Cassi reasoning versus source-only retrieval;
- matched extra native reasoning and matched total-work allocation;
- an identity native hook where identity is the experiment's claim;
- matched-strength static, no-evolution, reset, and shuffled interventions;
- cold and acquired fields;
- frozen acquired knowledge versus additional acquisition, while both retain working inference;
- field-state intervention on the actual acquired object;
- dependency-driven local reasoning, hierarchy-only scheduling, live packet priority, static/no-evolution cues, and ranking-only shuffles as specified in [Section 8](#packet-reasoning-demonstration-and-comparison);
- bounded meditation or study versus ordinary work with matched available information and total resources;
- acquired self-development methods versus a fixed balanced selector, including prior-skill retention and unfamiliar capability targets;
- one model and a genuinely different adapter/model combination.

Identity parity is required to support an identity-control claim, not a permanent ceiling on invasive experiments. A destructive replacement arm is judged as that arm, with recovery and honest measurements. Do not claim an architecture-independent improvement from selecting a winning coupling or layer on the evaluation questions.

Report per-family and worst-family outcomes, not only aggregate success. Include censored completions, timeouts, unsupported cases, harm to already-correct behavior, and withheld outputs in the denominator. Probability scores require declared probability semantics; otherwise evaluate supported sets, interval width, coverage, abstention, and decision consequences.

Independent checking should consume real persisted outputs where possible and prove that a reassuring check can fail on a plausible changed artifact. Tests that merely pin source text, count fields, or verify a mock's forwarding do not establish the reasoning behavior.

### Reused assessment and independent checking

Once feedback from an assessment influences candidate choice, curriculum, prompts, code, or a human-guided revision, that feedback belongs to the development history. This includes aggregate scores even when individual answers remain hidden. A new checkpoint, fresh process, or reset field does not restore the affected assessment's untouched status.

Known exercises remain useful for practice and regression checks. Claims about further unfamiliar-task improvement require appropriately separated assessment opportunities, with their source/world roots and disclosure history retained. Fresh instances can assess within-family behavior when their sampling and feedback boundary justify it; they do not by themselves establish transfer to a new family. Renaming a reused set or rerendering its underlying cases does not restore independence.

Correctness checking must have a basis beyond the proposal's own approval. A generated exercise with an exact derivation can support a checked conclusion under its stated semantics; a model-generated problem, answer, and approving critique do not supply three independent sources. An independently implemented checker or evaluator also retains its assumptions and tested scope. Separate exact correctness, task transfer, and the causal benefit of a learning method rather than using one reassuring score for all three.

Do not delay the standalone work loop for native hooks or acquired scheduling. Start with explicit interfaces and the fixed method, measure complete task behavior, and then assess the learned or dynamical contribution. Actual selective numerical lowering depends on a valid temporal/boundary error treatment; it is not bundled into the first claim of semantic work locality.

**Implemented increments.** Multi-operation resident episodes, semantic assemblies with hierarchy-only execution, packet-driven selection with cue accounting, and readout refinement, branch reuse, and local repair all run through the resident semantic state. The work-selection comparison arms (`baseline`, `hierarchy`, `static`, `live`, `shuffled`, `acquired`) dispatch from one live frontier and report their eligible scores; in the scenario the `live` arm selects the high-activation support while `baseline`, `hierarchy`, and `static` tie and keep ready order, and `shuffled` ranks by a deterministic digest. A selector Program published through the ordinary register path scores with the same bounded inputs (`age`, `base_priority`, `dependency_count`, `support_count`, `activation`, `has_support`) and reproduces the live ordering when it weights the packet cue. Root-allowance accounting, return-once consumption, and reservation release after a dependency repair are exercised by [`test_cassi_packet_reasoning.py`](../CassiFI/test_cassi_packet_reasoning.py) and the continuing-world run in [`run_cassi_reasoning_scenario.py`](../CassiFI/run_cassi_reasoning_scenario.py). The acquired selector path runs and refuses an unusable program; whether learned interfaces, procedures, or selectors transfer and repay their acquisition cost remains unmeasured, and temporal and model-native consumers remain specified. The stale-read refusal is exercised under a narrow work bound; at the default bound a correction settles its invalidation inside the operation. Whether packet scheduling improves adequacy or cost at a matched allocation remains an open measurement with no claimed advantage.

## 30. Research frontier and decisive principle

The largest unresolved questions are substantive:

- Can field-native acquisition discover useful high-dimensional model features and intervention mappings without a trained neural interface?
- Can continuous work and exact semantic structure cooperate at native inference latency without hiding a second interpreter or losing relevant distinctions?
- Can acquired reasoning procedures replace enough native work to repay their discovery, validation, and maintenance cost?
- Can semantic and procedural knowledge transfer across genuinely different models and worlds rather than just familiar trajectories?
- Can a deterministic field-owned language boundary acquire productive open-domain expression and interpretation?
- Can one finite continuing field retain useful rare knowledge, revise compressed support, and scale without destructive interference?
- Can calibrated, useful uncertainty survive model shift, partial observability, and policy-dependent evidence?
- Can the field learn when internal reorganization is more useful than new evidence, additional ordinary inference, or stopping?
- Can acquired self-development methods improve unfamiliar skills and preserve earlier competence after accounting for their own search, evaluation, and maintenance costs?

The design assigns these questions a state representation, acquisition path, execution boundary, and observable consequence. It does not answer them by naming a field, using a spectral codec, or asserting that the component successes compose automatically.

The decisive principle is shared causal use: an acquired distinction must change the appropriate prediction, reasoning operation, plan, or emitted result; survive valid restart and model changes where it is portable; and revise coherently when its support changes. The maximum ambition is a field computer that increasingly owns how it understands and computes, with LLMs becoming optional instruments rather than the permanent place where the intelligence resides.
