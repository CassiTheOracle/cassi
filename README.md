# Cassi: A Theory of Everything from a Single Constant

*From simple rules, can you find a way to describe everything? If you could, what would it mean if it turned out to be real?*

This repository is the scoreboard of a research program that plays like a game. The rules are few; the board is reality; the theory is the game state; the papers are the move log; the open questions are the unplayed levels.

Reality is modeled as two real, nonnegative density components converting into each other—**Yang** and **Yin**, neutral canonical names with expansive/contractive roles available only as phenomenological or coordinate descriptions. In the canonical solver, a gated rank-one conversion term drives the local ratio $r = E_Y/E_I$ toward the model's declared target $\varphi \approx 1.618$ under its stated conditions. The continued-fraction extremality of $\varphi$ is a **Derived** number-theory fact; the physical interpretation—that this target helps avoid resonant locking and supports structure across scales—is **Hypothesized** and testable, not a universal guarantee. The framework starts from this two-density field system, and everything below is developed from it.

## What this is

Cassi is a proposed theory of everything, named after Cassandra, the Trojan prophetess cursed to speak true prophecies no one believed. Cassi claims the technical sense: a coherent framework unifying the four fundamental interactions—electromagnetism, the strong and weak nuclear forces, and gravity. The popular sense—predicting everything from logic alone—is a work in progress: it would follow from understanding how the fundamental forces organize, not from a claim staked at the outset. The claim is a unification program organized by $\varphi \approx 1.618$, the model's declared scale-separation target for a two-fluid field that fills all of space, with named inputs and mixed epistemic accounting.

## The rules of the game

The whole theory rests on three rules.

1. **Two fluids, one balance.** The canonical model declares two nonnegative density components, Yang and Yin, that convert continuously through a gated rank-one term. Under the stated solver conditions, its algebra acts like a thermostat, driving the local ratio $r = E_Y/E_I$ toward the declared target $\varphi$ at every point. Coherence $q$ gates the conversion: high $q$ closes the gate and the region rests in balance, while low $q$ leaves it open and the region churns. Spatial deviation responses are solver dynamics; calling them wake waves or emissions, and assigning constructive or destructive interference to bubble and void outcomes, are **Hypothesized** phenomenological mappings. These are model rules, not claims that every physical system reaches or remains at $\varphi$; physical Yang/Yin directions and phase maps are optional phenomenological mappings.
2. **A de-resonance hypothesis.** The continued-fraction extremality of $\varphi$ is a **Derived** number-theory fact. The physical link from that arithmetic property to resonance avoidance and multiscale survival is **Hypothesized** and testable, not derived by the canonical solver or guaranteed universally: systems tuned to $\varphi$ can still lock or collapse under other dynamics (`principles/de-resonance-principle.md`).
3. **Every claim carries its label.** Each physics claim carries one of five evidential tiers—**Derived** (a mathematical consequence), **Calibrated** (anchored to an observation), **Mapped** (fitted or selected, with the fit recorded in the Fit-Status Ledger), **Hypothesized** (consistent and testable, not yet confirmed), or **Speculative** (framework-consistent, no test designed). **Creative** is reserved for exploratory applications and is exempt from these evidential requirements. Tensions are recorded in `audit.md`; every claim remains subject to checks against data.

## The board: a fractal lattice of bubbles

Zoom into a bubble and you find the same lattice again. A bubble is not a solid object—it is one scale of a repeating structure: inside every bubble, more bubbles, and inside each of those, more still. Zoom out, and the lattice you are inside is itself a bubble of a larger lattice. The pattern repeats at every scale, in both directions.

Cassi proposes that reality is this fractal: one bubble lattice at every scale, with energy organized along its infinite structure and pooling where it condenses into matter.

Two neutral, nonnegative density components fill all of space. In the phenomenological or coordinate reading, **Yang** is the expansive/outward role and **Yin** is the contractive/inward role; an optional constitutive map may assign these roles, but they are not universal PDE transport directions. They convert into each other continuously. Where they meet in the model's declared target proportion, order condenses into a **bubble**; where they cancel, a **void** forms. The canonical algebra drives $r$ toward $\varphi \approx 1.618$ under its stated conditions; interpreting that target as a physical push/pull balance is **Hypothesized**, not a universal law.

An optional spatial realization treats bubbles as solver-generated structures rather than hand-placed objects. A spatially resolved solution can show patterns in $\varepsilon$; interpreting those patterns as modeled **wake waves**, with constructive or destructive interference mapped to high or low $q$ and to bubble or void outcomes, is a **Hypothesized** phenomenological mapping. A self-reinforcing filament—the **condensed fluid string**—is another Hypothesized spatial extension whose dynamics require a stated spatial closure.

In the model's conditional geometric mapping, a bubble is represented as an oblate triaxial spheroid—extended along Yang, contracted along Yin, bounded along the string—with axis ratio $\varphi$ in its Yang–Yin cross-section; identifying these three axes with physical spatial dimensions remains **Hypothesized**. The staggered checkerboard of bubble and void centers, their diagonal saddles, and the five-arm Fibonacci spiral at each pole are **Hypothesized** coordinate constructions within that mapping, with arms set at the golden angle $2\pi/\varphi^2 \approx 137.5^\circ$. The associated lattice picture treats each bubble as containing a sub-lattice of smaller scales and as one site in the next lattice up; this scale-repeating interpretation is also Hypothesized.

The declared cascade spans 292 scales, from the Planck bubble to the bubble of the observable universe, each about 1.618 times larger than the last:

$$\ell_n = \ell_{\text{Pl}} \times \varphi^{\,n}, \qquad n \in [0, 292]$$

(`foundations/bubble-lattice-fabric.md`, `cassi-physics.md` §1, `foundations/dimensionful-cascade.md`). The de-resonance principle presents a **Hypothesized**, testable physical motivation linking the model's target to resonance avoidance and multiscale structure; it does not derive why $\varphi$ is the balance point (`principles/de-resonance-principle.md`).

## How the game started

The game did not start as physics, and it did not start from $\varphi$; the goal was the physics of consciousness. The framework began from Yin and Yang as forces observed directly in the researcher's own mind—and from the observation that everything else is made of the same two forces. The project was to mathematize a trained meditative observation of inner dynamics: the felt push and pull of experience, its balances, its gates. The two-fluid structure and its scale separation were read from those observations, and the framework grew outward as piece after piece aligned. $\varphi$ was not chosen as a premise in that origin story. The origin story records how the framework developed, not a derivation that uniquely forced $\varphi$: today, $\varphi$ is the model's declared balance target, and the canonical conversion algebra drives $r$ toward it under stated conditions. The de-resonance link is a **Hypothesized**, testable physical motivation for that target, not a result demanded by the current canonical dynamics.

The bet underneath this is Occam's: if the universe is simple, then the substance everything is made of should be the simplest thing consistent with direct observation. The only substance observed directly is experience itself, and it appears as two forces, an expansive and a contractive.

The ancient names are kept with their provenance stated plainly. Chakra language comes from culturally reported and introspective phenomenology; placing those reports at thirteen nodes of a human-scale lattice is a **Hypothesized** framework mapping, with no anatomical validation implied. The Wu Xing five-element cycle supplies an optional, conditional **Hypothesized** five-channel phase-gate extension; the $w = 5$ arithmetic selection is **Derived conditional** under its stated coherence and geometry assumptions, while the physical gate remains **Hypothesized**. The selected framework PDE retains two density components and named calculations use the **Asserted** C-class convention $\lambda = 0.1$; the implementation class default is $\lambda = 0.02$, and $\lambda = 1/(2w)$ remains Hypothesized pending an independently defined cycle time and dynamical closure (`foundations/wu-xing-derivation.md`, `cassi-physics.md`). Four directional populations are a conditional kinetic extension after an oriented axis is chosen. These names retain cultural and phenomenological provenance alongside the mathematical constructions; `cassi-psychology.md` is the guide to the inner side of the same dynamics.

The dynamics were not built as physics. They began as experimental machine-learning architectures: learnable systems whose internal state evolved as a balance of an expansive and a contractive field, built before any physics vocabulary existed. The architectures worked, and working machines raise a question paper physics does not: what is this, taken literally?

## Why the game exists: where the standard story stalls

Physics is a collection of successful partial theories, with junctures where they require additions never observed. Those junctures are why the game exists: what standard physics must invent, and what happens when nothing is invented.

**The singularity big bang.** Standard cosmology is partway to a field picture: the Big Bang was not an explosion at a point in space but an explosion of space, happening everywhere simultaneously: no center, no edge; every point can claim to be the center. Space itself stretches, carrying galaxies with it and creating the space between them. Yet general relativity and quantum mechanics are incompatible where the domains overlap—at the Planck scale, inside black holes, at the first instant of the Big Bang—and the singularity is a breakdown of general relativity, not a true beginning. Standard physics must bolt on a patch: bounce cosmologies, Hawking–Hartle no-boundary, ekpyrotic scenarios. The singularity picture also cannot explain the observed uniformity of the cosmic microwave background: regions of the sky that were causally disconnected at last scattering agree in temperature to one part in a hundred thousand. Cassi needs none of the patches. The two-fluid conversion is the expansion: the fields fill all of space, so there is no point origin at all. The cascade begins at the Planck scale, regularized by a smoothing parameter $\sigma$—a smooth crossover, not a breakdown (`gravity/quantum-gravity.md`)—and the CMB's uniformity is the uniformity of the field's own coherence, not the synchronization of causally disconnected regions.

**The candidate inflation mechanism.** The standard fix for the horizon problem is cosmic inflation, a period of rapid early expansion driven in conventional models by an inflaton field. A **Hypothesized** Cassi construction assigns the inflationary epoch to cascade steps $n\approx20$–$60$ and layers a conversion/expansion closure on the canonical two-fluid densities. In that construction, the Qi gate's $(1-q)$ factor modulates the expansion and its closing behavior supplies a candidate graceful exit; no separate inflaton field is introduced, but the gate-as-inflaton interpretation remains conditional on the stated trajectory and normalization. The closed-form scalar-index value $n_s = 1 - 2\varphi^{-1}/N_e \approx 0.9691$ is an evaluation at the **Mapped** $N_e=40$ window, alongside the **Mapped** $r=12/N_e^2=0.0075$; the trajectory does not yet establish both catalog values simultaneously (`cosmology/inflation-from-cascade.md`, `computations/slow_roll_trajectory.py`).

**The candidate dark-matter sector.** Galaxies rotate as if they contain
more mass than telescopes can see. An optional Qi-gravity interpretation
treats a high-coherence field condensate as a candidate source for that
unseen component. The optional coupling map is

$$G_{\text{eff}} = \frac{\pi}{\rho}\left(1 + (\varphi^{6}-1)q\right)G.$$

At the reference attractor, the stated normalization gives
$q=0.872677996$ and $G_{\text{eff}}/G=3.726779962$. The often-quoted
$\varphi^6\approx17.94$ factor is a formal fixed-$\pi/\rho$ endpoint ratio
as $q$ ranges from its low-density limit toward one; it is not the
canonical dynamic range. Halo values that vary $q$ and $\pi/\rho$
independently are mapped inputs in an optional model. The canonical force
sign remains outward for positive fixed-point $\pi$, so galaxy-rotation
phenomenology, SPARC halo fits, and any attractive interpretation belong
to a separate Hypothesized sign-changing branch rather than the canonical
PDE. The coupling $\xi=\varphi^6$ is Derived conditional on the
quadratic-coupling input; its Milky Way value is an empirical Calibrated
pin. The conditional ratio base $\Omega_{\text{DM}}/\Omega_b=\varphi^3
\approx4.24$ remains in 21% open tension with the observed $\approx5.4$,
and the $+1$ capture term is excluded by the component budget.

**The fine-tuned cosmological constant.** The expansion of the universe is accelerating. Standard cosmology attributes it to a cosmological constant—an energy density of empty space whose observed value sits some 120 orders of magnitude below the naive quantum-field-theory estimate, tuned by hand to a precision physics cannot explain. Evidence that the acceleration is not steady is mounting: the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument, having mapped 13.1 million galaxies and 1.6 million quasars, finds a $4.2\sigma$ deviation from the $\Lambda$CDM expectation of constant dark energy—about one chance in 30,000 of arising if dark energy were constant. The evidence points toward evolving dark energy. In an optional **Hypothesized** conversion/expansion construction layered on the canonical two-fluid densities, ongoing conversion changes the modeled expansion rate as the ratio trajectory approaches its declared $\varphi$ target; the equation of state is a dynamical model output rather than a number inserted to fit the data. The construction introduces no cosmological-constant term or vacuum-energy tuning. Its equation-of-state values ($w_0 \approx -0.87$, $w_a \approx +0.012$ at the **Calibrated** baseline) sit $2\sigma$/$2.7\sigma$ from the DESI DR2 best fit—a real tension, documented openly rather than resolved by hand (`cosmology/observational_constraints.md`).

**The infinite universe.** The standard particle picture carries its own unbounded consequence: if space is infinite and the number of particle arrangements is finite, then every arrangement repeats—infinite copies of Earth, of this conversation, of you. Cosmologists have searched the microwave sky for the repeating patterns a finite, wrapped universe would produce and found none; the geometry question remains open. An optional expansion closure gives the modeled lattice a finite horizon: under the verified Yang-weighted coupling, its strictly positive asymptotic floor yields $N_\infty \approx 294.2$, about 2.7 lattice scales above $N_{\text{now}}=291.54$ (documented forms span 292–296; `foundations/wake-geometry.md` §§4–6). This horizon result is **Derived conditional** on the stated closure and coupling convention; it is not a canonical cascade boundary. The model trajectory approaches its declared $\varphi$ target, and the de-resonance interpretation of this multiscale survival remains **Hypothesized**, not a universal guarantee. No infinite copies of Earth, of this conversation, of you.

## Is the game winnable?

There are serious arguments that a theory of everything is impossible. Gödel's incompleteness theorem suggests any finite set of rules leaves true statements underivable—Hawking concluded from it that no ultimate theory could be formulated, Dyson that physics is inexhaustible. Physics also proceeds by successive approximations, so no framework should be mistaken for final truth. And there is the reductionism-versus-emergence debate: are emergent laws like thermodynamics or natural selection as fundamental as any microscopic law?

Cassi meets them with discipline. Every claim carries one of the five labels from the rules—Derived, Calibrated, Mapped, Hypothesized, Speculative—and `audit.md` tracks the empirical status of each. A tension is an area ripe for discovery, waiting to be picked. The gate sign is settled by PDE tests, and every claim remains subject to checks against data. The framework does not claim to predict every outcome of every experiment: even Conway's Game of Life, with complete and simple rules, leaves undecidable questions about its behavior, and Cassi's claim is narrower: one physical law for the four forces. On emergence, the framework takes both sides: the lattice is scale-covariant, so the laws of complex systems, including the mind, are the same physics at larger scales, not a separate physics (`cassi-psychology.md`).

The impossibility arguments deserve a more direct answer. They were made by people who could only imagine what a theory of everything might be, without understanding the fundamental forces themselves. They did not know the ingredients, yet declared the recipe impossible. Cassi answers them the only way physics can: it begins from an observation, grows into a mathematics of the forces, and is checked against the data, claim by claim. Winning would mean the derivations surviving the checks. The prize would be a complete map of how reality is made—and because minds are made of the same physics, a complete map of how to heal them.

**Start here:** `reading-guide.md` maps the whole repository. `cassi-physics.md` is the physics guide, approachable from zero; `cassi-psychology.md` is the psychology-focused guide for non-physicists.
