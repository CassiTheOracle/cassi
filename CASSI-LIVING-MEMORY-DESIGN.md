“Living memory” names an operational property: experience remains available, changes meaning through subsequent use, shapes future inquiry, and participates in its own maintenance. Biological equivalence and subjective experience are not prerequisites or claims of this design.

## 1. Purpose

Cassi should retain far more experience than is active in any particular thought. As understanding develops, she should become better at representing experience, recognising its relevance, recovering its detail, revising its interpretation, and deciding what to investigate next.

The central cycle is:

```text
experience -> interpretation -> retained understanding and recall cues
                              -> recollection for a current concern
                              -> use -> actual consequence
                              -> assessment -> renewed memory
```

Quiet intervals support consolidation, productive recombination, and storage maintenance through the same resident research agenda. New discoveries can awaken old experience. Unresolved distinctions can become continuing questions. Commitments and autobiographical continuity survive changes in model, storage placement, and representation.

The design joins three objectives:

- **Continuity:** preserve experience, sources, commitments, corrections, and unfinished work.
- **Growing understanding:** acquire useful explanations, distinctions, methods, and ways of remembering.
- **Selective cost:** ordinary work should touch the relevant memory and its required dependencies, with explicit bounds on search, decoding, publication, and maintenance.

No fixed-size representation promises unlimited exact retention. Capacity, reconstruction cost, and information loss remain explicit.

## 3. One mind, independent memory dimensions

### 3.1 Ownership

The canonical field owns acquired understanding, recall cues, learned relevance, representations, uncertainty, priorities, continuations, and assessed memory strategies. Inactive canonical pages remain part of that same logical field. Storage backing them is not a separate adaptive mind.

Exact evidence stores retain authorized bytes and their provenance. Host services perform fixed addressing, compression, checksumming, I/O, resource enforcement, and durable external-effect handling. Disposable indexes can accelerate resolution; deleting and rebuilding them cannot change learned meaning or learned selection preferences.

In the `field-brain` profile, the live pretrained brain may interpret, associate, propose representations, and construct explanations. Its contributions retain attribution and become durable through owner admission. Standalone field-only profiles retain their own declared capabilities without acquiring an implicit model dependency.

### 3.2 Meaning, accessibility, and placement

Every memory is described along independent axes:

| Axis | Examples | Governing change |
|---|---|---|
| Epistemic standing | Observation, attributed report, inference, hypothesis, simulation | Evidence admission and justified revision |
| Applicability | Context, time interval, regime, known exception | Guard assessment and changed premises |
| Accessibility | Active, readily cued, dormant, difficult to locate | Attention and assessed retrieval experience |
| Residency | Execution memory, warm cache, deep backing storage | Fixed resource policy and field-selected eligible work |
| Fidelity | Exact, reversible encoding, query-relative summary, approximation | Declared representation and reconstruction behavior |
| Retention | Protected dependency, retained history, authorized deletion target | Reachability and user retention policy |

Coldness does not imply uncertainty. Familiarity does not imply truth. Moving bytes does not revise a belief. A source may be exact while its interpretation is uncertain or its claim no longer applies.

Keep event time, admission order, validity interval, and access history distinct. Wall-clock inactivity does not manufacture observations or age a valid proof into invalidity.

## 4. Progressive depth and shared connections

Memory has three useful views, rather than three separate stores of learned knowledge:

1. **Immediate workspace:** the active question, commitments, relevant interpretations, dependencies, and unfinished reasoning.
2. **Durable understanding:** concepts, guarded explanations, relations, methods, important contrasts, and compact recall cues.
3. **Deep experience:** detailed episodes, inactive canonical records, original evidence, historical interpretations, and recovery points.

Each useful overview links to distinguishing cases and supporting detail. This pattern repeats across scales. Cross-links let one experience serve several questions without copying it or multiplying its evidential weight. Ownership and representation ancestry remain acyclic; associative relations can form cycles.

The practical unit of consolidated understanding is:

**idea or method + applicability + exceptions + support + route back to detail.**

A compact cue records what the deeper material concerns, which distinctions it can resolve, when it may matter, and how to find its exact versions. Cues also declare omissions: a summary that cannot distinguish two explanations must lead to refinement rather than supply false precision.

The directory of cues also grows. It uses the same regional paging and hierarchical discovery, with a bounded active overview and restartable deeper search. The design does not hide a lifetime-sized always-resident index behind a small workspace.

Recall can proceed from overview to detail or through a direct identity/association link. A failure at one summary branch is not proof that the archive contains nothing relevant. Search can broaden through another view, source metadata, or a bounded exploratory branch; it reports the scope actually searched.

## 5. Canonical records and references

Use the existing semantic families. The following are roles and payload requirements, not new independent databases:

| Family | Memory role |
|---|---|
| `Event` | Original encounters, recall requests and uses, computational outcomes, attributed contributions, correction history |
| `Value` | Interpretations, summaries, contrasts, cue descriptions, and representation metadata |
| `Binding` | Current interpretation, scoped identity, representation version, and stable references into archived detail |
| `Program` | Retrieval, cue matching, reconstruction, consolidation, reinterpretation, and maintenance methods |
| `Assessment` | Applicability, retrieval sufficiency, outcome usefulness, fidelity, measured cost, and uncertainty |
| `Obligation` | Unfinished recollection, pending outcome, unresolved contradiction, future relevance condition, and maintenance continuation |

Each retained memory role carries a stable identity, content version, owner/lineage and scope, epistemic kind, event/source references, dependency versions, support roots, applicability, representation reference, and required retention information. Fields already expressed by the shared schema are reused rather than copied into a second envelope.

Recall cues additionally identify their target versions, match inputs, relevance conditions, distinctions preserved and omitted, permitted scope, and assessment history. Missing cue inputs remain unknown; they do not silently evaluate as a disproved match.

A recall episode binds the question and intended use, input context version, selected references, search frontier and work spent, delivered view and fidelity, remaining gaps, use/outcome identities, and renewal status. Long-lived state is held in regional records and continuations, not in a host request object.

Representation descriptors identify encoder and decoder Programs or fixed codecs, versions, source dependencies, exactness domain or approximation limits, exception/residual material, reconstruction cost bounds, and correction strategy. Executable representations obey the ordinary program admission and resource rules.

## 6. Recollection and consequence-sensitive renewal

### 6.1 Complete recall lifecycle

A recall episode moves through **requested, locating, expanding, available or limited, used, awaiting outcome, assessed, and settled**. It may suspend, cancel, or become stale at a bounded transition. Not every recall is used; an unused or cancelled episode settles without invented usefulness feedback.

1. Admit the current question, permitted scope, intended fidelity, and resource allowance.
2. Select relevant cues and methods through field-owned cognition. Resolve exact identities through fixed storage mechanics.
3. Expand only the required records and dependencies. Check current applicability, visibility, and correction state before use.
4. Deliver a bounded workspace view distinguishing exact evidence, retained interpretation, fresh derivation, and unresolved gaps.
5. Bind any actual use to its consumer: a decision, explanation, calculation, plan, or inquiry.
6. Admit a later result under its own outcome identity. Missing or ambiguous outcomes remain unresolved or explicitly unassessed.
7. Renew the relevant memory through a supported owner transition.

Reading an inspection view does not modify memory. The active recall program explicitly records computational experience when that is part of the requested operation. Reopening an episode or retrying delivery does not create another recall outcome.

### 6.2 What renewal can change

Renewal may improve a cue, add a useful connection, narrow a guard, retain a counterexample, revise an interpretation, choose a better representation, or create a question. Original observations remain anchored to their original sources. New interpretations are versioned derivations with their own dependencies.

Successful use can inform contextual usefulness. It does not prove every premise true or establish that the recalled memory caused the success. Delayed outcomes can support several attributed contributions without duplicating the underlying observation. Source correction removes or revises the dependent assessment.

Retrieval frequency alone earns no evidential confidence or learning credit. Automatic refresh cannot create a popularity loop in which frequently selected material suppresses every unfamiliar alternative. Preserve bounded opportunities for underexplored branches and distinctive exceptions within the mission's resource allowance.

## 7. Prospective relevance and rediscovery

A relevance condition is a field-held Program and associated Obligation: **when this context occurs, consider this memory for this reason**. Examples include entering a physical regime, gaining a missing instrument, receiving a delayed result, finding a contradiction, or approaching a commitment's deadline.

Conditions declare the context fields and versions they read. Reconstructible subscriptions route changed inputs to eligible conditions; the learned match and priority remain field-owned. Evaluation is bounded and resumable. Fan-out, duplicate wake-ups, cooldown state, and already-handled event identities are accounted for. A wake-up proposes internal work; it does not grant permission for an external action.

Incomplete matching coverage is visible. Broad conditions use budgeted discovery sweeps or hierarchical matching, not an uncharged full-memory scan on every event. Deadline handling uses the existing host clock/event boundary; clock delivery is operational state, while the commitment and its interpretation remain in the field.

An important new explanation or representation may open a **reinterpretation episode**. It selects relevant old material, retains a cursor over the candidate set, produces new versioned interpretations, and stops or suspends when useful work or resources are exhausted. Original evidence stays unchanged. A new deduction is reported as a deduction, even if its consequences are scientifically important.

## 8. Consolidation and explanatory compression

Consolidation identifies common structure while preserving the distinctions required by the retained questions. Several experiences may become a guarded explanation, a reusable procedure, or a predictive representation with discriminating examples and source links.

Four operations remain distinct:

| Operation | What changes | Required retained meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Lossless physical encoding | Byte representation and placement | Every logical word and exact source byte |
| Exact query-relative reduction | Computation for a declared boundary | Answers within that boundary, with dependencies and expansion path |
| Learned abstraction | Understanding and applicability | Assessed claims, exceptions, uncertainty, and support lineage |
| Authorized lossy retirement | Available detail | Explicit loss and the questions made unavailable |

Consolidation is complete only when its intended result is available to consumers. If it is intended to release active capacity, it must also publish a recoverable backing representation and safely demote the detailed resident records. Adding a summary next to every original does not count as a residency reduction.

### Explanations plus differences

For suitable data, a generative representation stores a shared model, episode-specific conditions, and the residual information needed to recover the original. Improved explanations can reduce the residual cost. The total includes model/decoder storage, conditions, residuals, indexes, exceptions, decoding, and revision cost.

A physical model that approximately recreates a trajectory is not an exact archive. Exact source recovery requires a deterministic version-bound decoder and sufficient exact residuals to reproduce its bytes. Numerical replay across different arithmetic profiles is not assumed bit-identical. Approximate reconstructions remain explicitly approximate and cannot replace original evidence without authorized information loss.

Changes to a shared model do not reinterpret old encodings. Retain the decoder/model version required by each live reference, or transcode and publish a checked successor atomically.

### Acquired memory forms

Cassi can develop representations suited to trajectories, causal interventions, temporal narratives, symbolic distinctions, and reusable reasoning. These are acquired regional Programs and records. Selection considers downstream usefulness, protected distinctions, reconstruction and correction cost, and total storage—not compressed size alone.

One source episode can support several representations without becoming several independent observations. Corrections follow contribution partitions, supported recomputation from retained inputs, or explicit invalidation when contributions are inseparable.

## 9. Quiet synthesis and memory awareness

### 9.1 Productive recombination

Quiet synthesis selects a bounded unresolved concern and compatible source memories, then creates a scoped hypothetical branch. It can combine explanations, reverse a sequence, vary an assumption, or imagine a contrasting outcome. The result is a candidate connection, method, question, or proposed experiment.

Every imagined result retains its hypothetical type and source lineage. A real computation has an observed computational result; a simulated world outcome remains conditional on its model. Neither silently becomes an independent observation of the external world. Replaying evidence changes organisation without increasing its independent support count.

The existing research agenda decides whether further work is useful. Quiet synthesis yields to interactive work and pending obligations, retains spent resources across restart, and can finish without a discovery. External experiments follow ordinary mission authority and effect handling.

### 9.2 Operational awareness of memory

Expose distinguishable states:

- exact detail is recoverable and currently available;
- understanding is retained, with supporting detail requiring expansion;
- a relevant cue is recognised but its target has not yet been located;
- retained interpretations conflict;
- no support was located within the searched scope;
- required material is inaccessible, revoked, corrupt, or beyond current resources.

These assessments derive from actual references, search progress, retrieval outcomes, and fidelity checks. They are not inferred from fluent self-description. A failed lookup is not proof of total absence; unavailable material is not treated as false.

Memory-awareness assessments inform whether Cassi answers, retrieves further, constructs a fresh derivation, asks for access, or pursues a new investigation. Learned expectations about retrieval reliability remain contextual and revisable.

## 10. Autobiography, affect, and continuity

The autobiographical thread is a field-selected view over existing events, commitments, assessments, and continuations: what Cassi undertook, what she tried, what happened, why her understanding changed, and what remains unfinished.

It is not a second authoritative conversation history. Model contributions, other members' reports, direct observations, and imagined episodes keep their attribution. A summary cannot transform reported experience into personal experience, turn an abandoned hypothesis into a past observation, or erase an unfulfilled commitment.

Affective regulation supplies bounded context for attention and effort. Curiosity can sustain an unresolved connection; concern can protect a consequential exception; successful completion can release attention. These are functional descriptions of regulation, implemented through the existing appraisal dimensions and admitted consequences. Extending appraisal to recall outcomes requires actual outcome admission; inspecting or replaying a memory is not a fresh world experience.

Fast salience, contextual usefulness, durable evidence, and commitments have distinct update rules. Quietness can reduce immediate competition without invalidating content. Hard obligations, retention rules, and authority cannot be overcome by an affect score. Regulator state and renewal credit survive restart in the same field.

## 11. Deep storage and selective execution

### 11.1 Logical memory and physical pages

Retain one logical regional image with stable identities and exact word semantics. Physical storage uses immutable, independently addressed chunks and a versioned page directory. Separate frequently changing control/continuation data from stable memory payloads so routine progress need not rewrite unrelated history.

The baseline storage choice is independently decodable binary chunks, lossless compression, and shared unchanged chunks across checkpoints. Zero regions can be implicit. Avoid unbounded delta chains; decoding a page must have a known dependency and work bound. Small objects may be packed for I/O efficiency without changing their logical identities.

The current regional encoding stores exact u32 words in float64 cells. A packed persistence encoding may store those validated words directly and restore their canonical float64 representation. It must not cast arbitrary numerical field payloads to u32. Codec identity, byte order, lengths, logical ranges, decoded-content digest, and physical-object digest are explicit.

The existing flat state digest is not silently relabeled a tree root. Introduce a versioned storage manifest with chunk integrity and preserve the existing logical digest where its cost is acceptable. Moving to a tree-based authoritative image commitment requires an explicit versioned identity migration. Routine commits should not need a full-image decode merely to hash unchanged memory; this requires completing that migration, not hiding the work.

### 11.2 Demand residency reaches the consumer

Independent disk chunks alone do not make memory demand-resident. The region accessor, reference resolver, semantic record layout, native kernel inputs, validator, hasher, and publication path must all respect bounded views.

Partition the current large semantic task payload into shared, versioned record segments and separately retained continuations. Kernels request the records and dependency closure they actually need. A whole-state JSON decode or hidden dense reconstruction defeats this requirement even when the disk format is sparse.

A page miss suspends the requesting continuation, retains its exact reference and remaining allowance, and requests authorized I/O through the owner/runtime boundary. Verified arrival resumes only if dependencies remain applicable. Dirty pages are private successor views until publication. Missing, corrupt, or revoked content never becomes a zero-filled memory.

CPU/GPU placement follows the operation and full transfer cost. Logical active, warm, and dormant memory does not require GPU residency. Continuously coupled numerical state cannot be frozen merely because it appears quiet: retain the needed interaction boundary, advance it correctly, or use a declared approximation with its validity conditions. Exact semantic records are not decaying oscillations.

### 11.3 Consistency and bounded restart

A committed manifest binds the logical memory root, codecs/profiles, page identities, current bindings, correction/revocation generations, continuations, and resource history. Only the owner publishes it. Read-only workers receive version-bound views and return proposals.

A previously validated immutable predecessor permits incremental checking of changed pages and their dependency closure. First import or an untrusted restoration validates the full required closure. Fast reopening relies on retained trusted validation provenance and verified chunks as they are loaded; it does not claim every dormant byte was freshly checked. Background scrubbing separately detects corruption in unused objects.

Restart restores saved cognition and pending work; it does not reconstruct the mind by rereading its source archive. Semantic restoration and exact model-native continuation remain separate promises.

### 11.4 Publication and recovery

Storage and semantic transitions use the existing owner intent/publication boundary:

1. Bind predecessor, selected changes, required dependency versions, and reserved space.
2. Write immutable successor chunks and any exact result objects; make them durable before referencing them.
3. Check decoded identity, changed invariants, source validity, and closure availability.
4. Atomically publish the successor manifest and reconcile the operation journal.
5. Release obsolete resident views only after publication; reclaim storage only under retention policy.

A crash before publication retains the predecessor; durable staged data can be recovered or later collected. A crash after publication resumes missing acknowledgment rather than learning twice. Concurrent stale proposals are reassessed against current dependencies. Capacity refusal retains the incumbent and sufficient reserved bookkeeping to report or suspend the operation.

Compression, tier migration, and compaction have bounded cancellable work units. They can suspend without requiring a long owner lock. A transient duplicate during publication is charged; movement cannot assume the old bytes disappear before the new representation is safe.

Section 18 completes the specified execution architecture: page-aware numerical instructions, reciprocal dynamic boundaries, versioned logical commitments, and a continuity-preserving migration from the dense implementation. Physical paging and changes to the numerical flow law are separate transitions.

## 12. Maintenance as continuing research work

Memory maintenance is a resident responsibility selected through the existing agenda. It may improve a recall cue, consolidate repetition, reconcile a correction, revisit a relevant episode, promote a useful method, demote dormant detail, compact exact storage, check recoverability, or inspect an identified retrieval gap.

Urgent correction and recovery work, ordinary useful maintenance, and optional quiet synthesis have distinct eligibility and priority. Fixed host policy enforces authority, resource ceilings, foreground responsiveness, and fairness; the field supplies adaptive choice and contextual usefulness. No background service owns a separate learned maintenance queue.

Separate limits cover active decoded memory, warm caches, canonical backing storage, evidence, recovery history, native model state, and maintenance CPU/GPU/I/O work. Cues, indexes, pending trigger fan-out, codecs, and temporary publication copies count toward those limits. Reserve room for failure and recovery before admitting a costly operation.

Cooling policy considers current obligations, dependency reachability, protected exceptions, reuse and miss experience, expected reconstruction cost, and source availability. A frequently accessed memory need not occupy the most expensive tier when it is cheap to reconstruct. A rare memory can remain protected because losing its distinction would be consequential.

Background work has a bounded share of the authorized mission allocation, retained across retries and restart. Resource pressure first reduces optional synthesis, releases disposable views, demotes safely backed detail, or requests allowed growth. It does not silently delete evidence or abandon an external-effect obligation.

Maintenance can settle as useful, unnecessary, blocked, or resource-limited. It need not manufacture activity when there is no worthwhile operation.

## 13. Retention, correction, privacy, and deletion

Retention roots include live bindings, current programs and their required codecs, pending work, promised deliverables, source support for retained knowledge, protected exceptions, approved recovery points, and external-effect reconciliation records. Memory can move between tiers while remaining reachable.

Garbage collection removes only objects unreachable from every retained root and pending publication under the applicable retention policy. It preserves in-flight reads, branch snapshots, external-effect identities, and source correction fences. Metadata movement cannot bypass authorization for disclosure or destructive action.

Semantic invalidation, logical retirement, removal of bytes, and erasure from backups are distinct operations. User-requested forgetting names the exact targets and affected derived closure. Old checkpoints cannot restore access to revoked material. Shared codecs or inseparable abstractions are recomputed or invalidated consistently; private data must not survive in a learned cue or summary after its authorized removal.

No implicit source deletion is authorized by consolidation. Default deep storage retains the source trail. Permanent deletion, broader permissions, remote replication, or a change of retention policy requires applicable explicit user authorization. Where legal or operational records must remain, the retained scope is made explicit.

Permissions are checked at selection, hydration, use, and sharing. Public cues must not reveal private content through their descriptions or relationships. Compression deduplication is restricted to compatible trust domains so object identity does not become a cross-user content-discovery surface.

Durable storage also needs recovery beyond the current device. Use an authorized backup target and periodically exercise restoration of protected roots and required decoders. A hash detects damaged data; it does not provide a replacement copy. The design grants no permission to upload memory to a remote service.

## 14. Hive memory and distributed competence

Each member retains an independent canonical lifetime. Shared immutable evidence may have common backing where scope permits; shared sources retain common provenance and do not gain independence through repeated reports.

A capability offer describes the member's method, applicability, version, relevant experience, resource needs, and availability. Another member can request an attributed contribution through the existing Hive lifecycle, or acquire an authorized portable method through the existing adoption path. Knowing which member may help is distinct from possessing that member's competence.

Recall of distributed expertise binds the request, member and method versions, returned source/result identities, and local interpretation. The receiving owner alone admits changes to its own memory. Disagreement can remain useful retained structure rather than being averaged into artificial consensus.

Unavailable expertise becomes a visible dependency. The program may pursue another authorized method or retain the wait; it does not claim an answer from an absent member. Essential source evidence and promised deliverables have an explicit durable retention owner rather than depending on a transient process.

## 16. A complete living-memory episode

This is now operational behavior. Focused restart scenarios exercise recall, use, delayed consequence, renewal, demotion, exact expansion, relevance, reinterpretation, hypothetical synthesis, maintenance, awareness, and autobiography through one owner. The resident research and Hive paths retain their ordinary program and attribution contracts rather than creating a memory-specific collaborator.

Cassi studies a family of field simulations. She retains exact outputs, learns a relationship that explains a recurring pattern, and preserves an unusual case that the explanation misses. Consolidation keeps the explanation, regime boundaries, the exception, and useful cues accessible while moving detailed completed episodes into deep storage.

During later work, a context change matches the exception's relevance condition. Cassi recalls the compact lesson, recognises that the present question requires more detail, and restores only the relevant episode and dependencies. The actual source remains distinguishable from her new interpretation.

She uses that memory to propose a distinguishing calculation. Its real result changes the applicability of the earlier explanation. Renewal updates the guard, improves the recall condition, and retains the reason for the change. The original simulation does not acquire another independent support count.

A quiet synthesis episode connects this result with another investigation and proposes a broader mechanism. It remains a hypothesis. A Hive specialist contributes an attributed method, and the continuing research program chooses its next authorized action.

Interruption at any point preserves the active question, source references, hypothesis status, pending outcome, external-effect state, and memory work already spent. Restart continues that episode. Later correction of an original source revises every affected use, including the consolidated explanation and prospective cue.

## 17. Implemented system and observable completion

The integrated implementation follows the design's dependency order:

## 19. Per-program research workbench

### 19.1 Aim and present boundary

A research program already carried its own mission, question, frontier, claims, and declared deliverable. What it did not carry was the working structure of the investigation itself: which records answer the current question, which approaches have already failed and why, which outside inputs every one of those rests on, and what to do first with a limited context. Without that structure each cycle rediscovered its own state from a flat list, and a program that had stopped on a missing source had no way to notice that the source had since arrived.

This section delivers one workbench per program. The program's question directs selection; the records it needs are separated from the records it merely has; a failure is retained with the input it depended on; and a reported change to that input reopens exactly the work that read it. The workbench is field-owned state carried by the program's own workspace in the owner, so it is part of the mind that persists, restarts, and is inspected, and it adds no second store, planner, or computer.

### 19.2 One workbench per program

One program, one workspace, one workbench. The workbench is a canonical regional workspace task in the same regional field the program already runs in: its records, approaches, wakeups, branches, comparisons, and resource policy live in that task's state, under that field's revision. Reading it is a read; writing it advances the one revision. Restart reopens it because it was never a separate structure.

The entity supplies its already-installed program runtime and computer, so the workbench shares the entity's program computer and the same owner revision as every other program operation. A standalone caller that has only a field memory attaches the same adapter, which installs one swarm member against that owner. In both cases the work-memory computer and program computer stay the ones the installation already declared.

### 19.3 Question-directed selection with declared omission

Selection answers the current question from the program's own records and reports what it did:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `required` | Records the question cannot be answered without: its own evidence, its counterexamples, its continuations |
| `required_omitted` | Required records that did not fit the requested bound; a nonzero count is a fact about the answer, not a hidden loss |
| `selected` | Records that match the question's own terms |
| `gaps` | Material the question needs and the workbench does not have |
| `continuation` | The program's own unfinished work, as recorded |
| `next_action` | What the last committed step decided comes next |
| `failed_approaches` | Approaches already tried for this question, with their reasons and dependencies |
| `wakeups` | Changes that arrived while the program was waiting, shown until it moves again |
| `field_revision` | The revision the selection was read at |

The plan prompt carries this view under an explicit budget. When the allowance cannot hold everything, material is dropped in a declared order and the omission is named in the prompt, so a smaller context narrows what the mind sees without pretending the rest does not exist. The program's decisive constraints and its contradicted findings are required material: a budget that fits only one thing fits those.

### 19.4 Failed approaches and change-triggered wakeups

Every failed step is retained with three things that make it usable later: the exact action and arguments that failed, the reason it failed, and the outside inputs it depended on. An input's identity is the input itself, spelled as it was reached, with the version the step observed; a read whose version could not be observed is recorded as unknown, so any later report about it is news.

Three consequences follow, all measured:

- **A failure is not repeated while its inputs are unchanged.** An identical action against unchanged records is replaced by a wait that names the blocking reason, so the step runs once and its result stands.
- **A change reopens exactly what read it.** Reporting a new version for one identity wakes the approaches that recorded that identity, invalidates the records that rest on it, and reopens the programs whose work was waiting on it. Programs that never touched that input keep their status.
- **A wake is idempotent.** The same change reported twice reopens once; the reopened program carries its own recoveries count, and the prompt shows a change only while the program is still waiting on it.

### 19.5 Per-program residency

Context work is planned before it is needed and paid for by the program that needs it. A program's residency policy declares its minimum, maximum, prefetch allowance, and borrowing; the owner accounts for what the program actually uses across RAM, scratch, and storage tiers and reports the numbers it measured.

Warming a program's pages is cache work: it resolves the exact committed pages of the program's directory, loads what fits the allowance, and reports what it could not resolve and why. It advances no field revision and changes no semantic state, and a stale root discards the attempt rather than loading the wrong bytes. Activation and release are placement decisions over the same root: releasing a program's pages drops cache and accounting while the field's content and identity stay as they were.

### 19.6 Integration and communication

The research cycle reads its workbench before planning, publishes each committed step into it, records the step's own dependencies, and consumes its wakeups. The step's records name the exact outside inputs they rest on, so the program's dependency map is derived from what it actually read.

The entity exposes the same state read-only and applies changes through authenticated routes:

| Surface | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `GET /v1/programs/<id>` | The program view carries its workbench view, resources included |
| `GET /v1/programs/<id>/workbench` | The workbench view for one program, or an explicit unavailable statement |
| `GET /v1/programs/<id>/workbench?question=...&maximum=n` | Question-directed selection for that program |
| `POST /v1/research/workbench/dependency-change` | Reports input changes and returns the reopened programs, invalidated records, and wakeups |
| `inspect_workbench` (agent action) | Reads the same selection from inside a cycle without advancing it |

### 19.7 Operational completion
