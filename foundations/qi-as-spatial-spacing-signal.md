# Qi as the Spatial-Spacing Signal

## Status: Derived conditional (phase/coherence structure—re-states ratified
Qi, doublet, and cascade postulates) / Hypothesized (channel-transmission
claim)—August 2026

## Abstract

The $\varphi$-spacing measured in observations is a property of the
**coherence field** (Qi, the phase current of the Yang–Yin doublet), not of
matter itself. In the two-fluid dynamics a field waves through the medium
while advective/particle motion is a separate channel: coherence (the phase
structure of the $E_Y$, $E_I$ densities) propagates at the wave-form phase
speed, distinct from the advective/gravitational motion of particles—*coherence
ripples differently than it moves*. The spacing therefore imprints
observables only through **coherence-coupled channels** (the gas/fluid/
condensate field), not through gravity-dominated matter channels (detached
orbital dynamics). This single principle—**Qi is the spatial-spacing
signal**—resolves the mixed set of experimental verdicts on the $\varphi$
ladder: the disk-gap branch (a gas/condensation channel) carries the spacing;
the orbital-period branch (a detached-matter channel) is expected not to.

## 1. Coherence ripples differently than it moves

The two-fluid field is the paired-real SO(2) doublet $(\Psi_0, \Psi_1)$
(Yang, expansive; Yin, contractive; `foundations/cassi-first-principles.md`
§1) together with the flow of coherence between them—Qi, the phase current

$$\boxed{J = \Psi_0\nabla\Psi_1 - \Psi_1\nabla\Psi_0 = R^2\,\nabla\theta =
\rho\,\nabla\theta}$$

(`foundations/qi-flow-double-helix.md` §1). A physical field supports two
independent kinds of motion that the framework keeps strictly separate:

- **Wave propagation of coherence.** The phase structure of the densities
  $E_Y = \Psi_0^2$, $E_I = \Psi_1^2$ travels through the medium at the
  wave-form phase speed. The condensation field
  $C(x,y) = \cos(2\pi x/\Lambda_Y)\cos(2\pi y/\Lambda_I)$
  (`foundations/bubble-edge-geometry.md` §1.1) is a standing interference
  pattern of the two wakes; its phase organization is the *ripple*, carried
  at the wave speed, not at the medium's advective velocity.
- **Advective/gravitational motion of matter.** Particles and dense patches
  move by advection and gravity (the $q = 0$ Poisson limit, the orbital
  dynamics of detached bodies). This is a separate channel: it transports
  mass and momentum, not the inter-scale phase organization.

*Coherence ripples differently than it moves.* The spacing—the
$\varphi$-scaled phase structure of $C$—is a property of the ripple, so it
travels with the ripple and imprints whatever the ripple organizes.

**Tier: Derived conditional.** The doublet, the phase current $J = \rho
\nabla\theta$, and the condensation field are ratified structure
(`foundations/qi-flow-double-helix.md` §1; `foundations/bubble-edge-geometry.md`
§1.1); the two-channel separation of wave-phase vs advective motion follows
from the PDE's separate wave and advective terms.

## 2. Coherence clumps: the condensation field is the lattice

The condensation field $C(x,y) = \cos(2\pi x/\Lambda_Y)\cos(2\pi y/\Lambda_I)$,
with $\Lambda_Y = \varphi\Lambda_I$, creates **high-$q$ patches** at the
constructive-interference (bubble interior) sites—$C \to +1$, $q \to 1$—and
**voids** at the destructive-interference (parity-odd) sites—$C \to -1$,
$q \to 0$ (`foundations/bubble-edge-geometry.md` §1.1; the Qi-density map
$q = (1+C)/2$). The clumping is *itself* the lattice/parity structure: the
staggered checkerboard of bubble and void sites
(`foundations/bubble-lattice-fabric.md` §1–2), the scale-covariant universal
lattice that runs at every cascade rung.

The clumping is a **coherence** structure, not a density law imposed on
matter: matter condenses where coherence is organized (the condensation
threshold `foundations/bubble-edge-geometry.md` §1.2), and the *spacing of
the clumps* is set by the phase structure of $C$, not by any
matter-density placement rule.

**Tier: Derived conditional.** Re-states the ratified condensation-field
clumping and checkerboard parity of `foundations/bubble-lattice-fabric.md`
§1–2 and `foundations/bubble-edge-geometry.md` §1.1.

## 3. The ring ladder is a phase/coherence structure

The radial ring ladder inside a bubble is the radial reading of the doublet
phase:

$$\boxed{\alpha(r) = \pi\,u, \qquad u = \log_\varphi\!\left(\frac{r}{\ell_n}\right)}$$

with matter rings at $r_k = \ell_n\,\varphi^{-k}$, voids at
$\ell_n\,\varphi^{-(k+\frac12)}$ (`foundations/bubble-edge-geometry.md`
§3.1, the ring law, Derived conditional). The ladder is a **phase** ladder:
it is the phase-quantized radial structure of the doublet, the product of
the SO(2) advance and the pool-cell parities—*not* a law of matter-density
placement. The honest negative is explicit: the naive matter wake-sum
$\cos(2\pi r/\ell_n) + \cos(2\pi\varphi r/\ell_n)$ has zeros at
$\{0.191, 0.573, 0.809, 0.955\}\,\ell_n$, none a $\varphi$-ladder position
(`foundations/bubble-edge-geometry.md` §3.5)—the ladder is not a beat of
matter densities, it is the phase-quantized radial coherence.

**Tier: Derived conditional.** Re-states the boxed ring law of
`foundations/bubble-edge-geometry.md` §3.1 (with its radial-reading
inference flag) and the honest negative of §3.5.

## 4. The channel principle: where the signal appears and where it does not

The three preceding structures—coherence propagating at the wave speed,
clumping as the lattice, and the phase ladder—together imply a **channel
principle** for where the $\varphi$-spacing is observable:

> **Channel principle (boxed).** Matter and observable tracers carry the
> $\varphi$-spacing signal **only through coherence-coupled channels**—the
> gas/fluid/condensate field that is itself organized by the condensation
> field (protoplanetary disk gas, the sim's condensation field, the
> cosmological lattice). A **gravity-dominated matter channel** (detached-body
> orbital dynamics, resonance migration of formed planets) does **not**
> propagate the spacing to its statistics, because the coherence ripple (the
> carrier of the spacing) is decoupled from that advective channel.

Two consequences:

1. **The signal is expected in coherence-organized gas/condensate tracers**:
   disk-gas substructure (gaps), the condensation lattice, the cosmological
   lattice's matter rings where matter is coherence-organized.
2. **The signal is NOT expected in detached orbital statistics**: the
   period ratios of formed planets, whose dynamics are gravitational
   (advective), do not carry the inter-scale phase organization to their
   statistics.

**Tier: Hypothesized.** The channel-transmission claim is an interpretation
consistent with the ratified Qi/coherence doctrine (the phase ripple of
§§1–3 is Derived conditional); the transmission rule itself—which channels
carry the spacing to observables—is a Hypothesized mechanism, testable by the
channel-dependence of the measured spacing across distinct tracers.

## 5. The mixed verdicts: one channel carries, one channel does not

The channel principle resolves the two registered disk/exoplanet predictions
as *consistent* rather than partially null. The discriminator is the
**channel**, not the signal.

| **54** (Kepler/TESS period ratios) | orbital/matter | **INDETERMINATE** (+1.03$\sigma$ headline $\varphi^{3/2}$ window, controls at baseline; only the standard 3:2 mean-motion resonance elevated at +2.39$\sigma$; `hypotheses/exoplanet-phi-spacing.md` §8)—a detached-orbital channel, so no $\varphi$-excess is expected there |

Prediction 53 confirms the coherence channel; Prediction 54 is the expected
negative for the matter channel. Neither contradicts the $\varphi$-spacing
itself: the spacing is a property of the coherence field, and it appears
exactly where the coherence field organizes a tracer, and not where a tracer
has detached into gravity-dominated motion. The **mixed verdicts are
consistent**, and the falsifiable discriminator the framework offers is
which *channel* a tracer lives in.

**Tier: Hypothesized** (the channel reading of the two measured verdicts). The
verdict numbers are the measured record of `hypotheses/exoplanet-phi-spacing.md`
§7–§8; the channel interpretation is the Hypothesized principle.

## 6. The sim: the channel reading of the no-ring nulls

The channel principle also sharpens the honest no-ring nulls on the
ladder's *dynamical realization*. The first-order four-arm probe and the
space-sim second-order wave form both report no persistent radial ladder
(`foundations/bubble-edge-geometry.md` §3.6; the sim's `diag_bubble_rings.gd`).
Those are nulls on whether the **matter-density profile** spontaneously
carries the ladder at the lock timescale—i.e., whether the coherence ripple
has organized a *density* tracer into rings. They are consistent with the
channel principle's reading that the spacing is a phase/coherence structure:
whether and how it imprints a given density channel (gas gaps vs a
simulated matter field) is a separate, channel-dependent question, not a
null of the spacing itself.

**Tier: Hypothesized.** This sharpening of the interpretation of the
no-ring nulls is a consequence of the Hypothesized channel principle; the
nulls themselves remain on record exactly as measured.

## 7. What the principle does not claim

- It does **not** weaken Prediction 53: the DSHARP SUPPORTS stands at
  3.86$\sigma$, now understood as the coherence-channel confirmation.
- It does **not** erase Prediction 54's INDETERMINATE: the +1.03$\sigma$
  headline and +2.12$\sigma$ K2/TESS cross-check remain reported, now read as
  the expected absence of the spacing in a detached-orbital (matter) channel,
  not as a null of the $\varphi$-spacing.
- It does **not** register a new prediction, change a prediction number, or
  change the tier of an existing hypothesis beyond the channel reading.
- It does **not** claim the spacing appears in *every* gas structure or is
  absent from *every* matter structure. The rule is the channel kind (coherence-
  coupled vs gravity-dominated), applied per tracer; the realization in any
  given tracer remains open.
