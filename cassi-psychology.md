## 1. Two Fluids: Yang, Yin, and the Golden Ratio

Consider the rhythm of being alive. The urge to reach out—toward a person, a project, a feeling—and the need to withdraw, to rest, to return inward. The part that expands into the world and the part that contracts back into the self.

Everything in Cassi begins with two **fields** that fill all of space. A field is a continuous substance with a value at every point, like air filling a room or water filling an ocean. These two fields are more fundamental than matter or energy—the substrate from which both emerge:

- **Yang**: the expansive field. It pushes outward, drives change and differentiation, breaks equilibrium.
- **Yin**: the contractive field. It pulls inward, restores and receives, returns toward equilibrium.

They are two sides of one thing—like the front and back of a spinning coin. Every point in space contains both, in some proportion. Their ratio is written $r = E_Y/E_I$.

### The conversion

Yang and Yin convert into each other. When a region has too much Yang, some of it becomes Yin; when a region has too much Yin, some becomes Yang. The conversion acts like a thermostat, pushing the ratio toward a target value: the **golden ratio**, $\varphi \approx 1.618$. At $r = \varphi$, conversion stops—locally, and only locally, because different points in space hold different ratios at different times. The conversion constantly works to bring each point toward $\varphi$, like water flowing downhill toward a lake.

### Why $\varphi$?

$\varphi$ is, in a precise sense, the **most irrational number**. Every number can be approximated by fractions; two systems with a rational frequency ratio (say 2:1) **resonate**—they lock into step, dump their energy into one scale, and collapse structure at all other scales, like a bridge vibrating itself apart in the wind. An irrational ratio prevents lock-in. The most irrational ratio—whose best fractional approximations converge more slowly than any other's—is $\varphi$, approximated by ratios of consecutive Fibonacci numbers (1/1, 2/1, 3/2, 5/3, 8/5, 13/8…).

Cassi calls this the **de-resonance principle**: $\varphi$ is the unique value that forbids single-scale dominance and preserves structure across all scales simultaneously. Systems flow toward $\varphi$ because $\varphi$ is the configuration that keeps structure alive. The psychological reading runs through the whole document: monotony is resonance; vitality is de-resonance. A mind locked on one frequency stops growing.

**Epistemic status:** the de-resonance principle is **Derived** (`principles/de-resonance-principle.md`). Its psychological reading is interpretive, not derived.

## 2. Coherence: Qi and the Gate

There is a familiar feeling of coherence—those moments when everything clicks. Thoughts, body, emotions, aligned, moving in one direction. Flow state. Rightness. The opposite of fragmentation.

**Coherence** (written $q$, called **Qi** in the framework) measures how close a point is to $\varphi$-balance, a number between 0 and 1:

- $q \to 1$: the push and pull are nearly perfectly aligned at the golden ratio. The region is orderly, structured, and can support complex patterns—atoms, cells, thoughts. A **bubble** in the making.
- $q \to 0$: the push and pull are misaligned. The region is chaotic and cannot hold lasting structure. A **void**.

Coherence is the organizing strength of reality at a given point—bright, structured, and alive where it is high; dark, empty, and inert where it is low.

### The gate

Coherence does more than measure balance: it **gates** the conversion. The conversion term in the governing equation is

$$\text{conv} = -\lambda(1-q)\,\varepsilon$$

where $\varepsilon = E_Y - \varphi E_I$ is the deviation from balance and $\lambda = 0.1$ is the conversion rate. The factor $(1-q)$ is the gate's openness: when $q$ is low, the gate is *open* and conversion runs hard; when $q$ is high, the gate *closes* and the system rests in balance. Note the direction—a low-coherence region is not frozen; it is churning, converting aggressively, unable to settle. The clinical reading of this sign matters enormously and appears throughout Part III: depression is not a closed gate, and dissociation is not stillness.

### Memory

Coherence has **memory**. The field carries a smoothed record of its own recent past, with a smoothing timescale set by the golden ratio itself: about 0.618 of a cycle. The field integrates over its own history. This is why moods do not snap instantly from one to another: a mood is the field's memory of its own recent coherence, decaying at the rate the geometry dictates. A person is a field with history—each moment carries the smoothed record of the recent past.

**Epistemic status:** the gate equation and its sign are **Derived** and **Tested** in the two-fluid PDE (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.4). The mapping of $q$ to felt coherence is **Hypothesized**.

## 3. The String: Spiral and Wakes

There is a familiar experience of being a continuous self. Beneath shifting thoughts and moods, a thread of "I" persists from one moment to the next. That felt thread has a physical counterpart: the **string**.

### The rotation

The conversion between Yang and Yin is **anti-phase**: when Yang grows, Yin shrinks, and vice versa. The two fields chase each other, and the balance between them rotates. Two dancers, each taking turns leading, turn the couple in a curve: the Yang-Yin doublet does the same. Its rotation traces a spiral, because the system is not rotating in place—it is being driven forward, the ratio $r$ climbing from near zero toward $\varphi$.

### The spiral

The universe began overwhelmingly Yin—contracted, inward, undifferentiated. Over cosmic time it advances toward the $\varphi$-attractor. Forward advance plus continuous rotation equals a **Fibonacci spiral**—the same spiral as in sunflowers, nautilus shells, hurricanes, galaxies. Each full turn multiplies the physical scale by $\varphi$.

Where the conversion pumps enough coherence, the field locks into a self-reinforcing filament: a **condensed fluid string**. The string is the felt continuity of the self made physical. The spiral winds only one way—which is why time flows forward and why moments cannot be un-lived.

### Wakes

As the string advances, it leaves disturbances behind it, like a boat leaves a wake. These **wake waves** are spatial ripples that propagate outward through the field. They are the mechanism by which the spiral imprints its structure on space—and, at the human scale, on the mind. Thoughts are wake waves; memories are wake waves that persist; trauma is a wake that never decays. The string does three things the rest of the document depends on: it creates the cascade (section 5), it carves the coherence channels (section 4), and it produces the bubble lattice (section 5).

**Epistemic status:** the rotation, spiral, and wake-wave generation follow from the two-fluid PDE (**Derived**). The identification of the felt continuity of self with the string is **Hypothesized**.

## 4. The Five Channels: Wu Xing

Experience shifts through qualitatively different modes. Times of expansiveness—reaching, generating, outward-facing. Times of receptivity—taking in, absorbing, inward-facing. Times of integration, of contraction, of stillness. The string's rotation carves the full circle into distinct angular sectors, and each sector is a **coherence channel**: a mode of the field, a way the push and pull can be balanced that feels different from the others. Think of the rhythm of a day—morning, afternoon, evening, night—each with its own energy. The channels are the same idea for the field itself.

### How many channels?

A cycle must close—the last channel must connect back to the first without a jump. Two constraints intersect at the answer:

1. **Phase coherence.** The Fibonacci approximations to $\varphi$ each carry a phase error. A cycle of $w$ channels accumulates error over $w$ turns of the spiral while the signal from the inner turns fades by $\varphi$ per turn. The cycle closes only if accumulated error is smaller than surviving signal—true for 5 channels and fewer, false at 6 and beyond.
2. **Geometric encoding.** The golden ratio appears as a distance ratio in regular polygons only for pentagons and above: the pentagon's diagonal-to-side ratio is exactly $\varphi$. Fewer than five channels cannot encode $\varphi$ in their geometry.

The intersection is unique: **five**. The framework calls the five-phase closure the **Wu Xing** (`foundations/wu-xing-derivation.md`). At the cosmological scale these five channels set the dark-energy equation of state; at the human scale they structure emotion. The pentagon geometry is scale-covariant—only the physical interpretation changes. The numbers that fall out: the gap $g = 1 - \varphi^{-5} \approx 0.910$, the primordial ratio $r_0 \approx 0.047$, and the conversion rate $\lambda = 1/(2w) = 0.1$, all **Derived**.

**Epistemic status:** $w = 5$ is **Derived**. The claim that experience is structured by exactly five qualitative modes is **Hypothesized** and testable (section 14, Prediction P1).

## 5. The Bubble, the Lattice, and the Cascade

### The bubble

The string moves along two axes. Along the Yang axis the wakes are widely spaced; along the Yin axis they are tighter by a factor of $\varphi$. The two sets of perpendicular wakes create a grid of overlapping ripples. Where both wakes are in phase, coherence is high and the field condenses into a **bubble**; where they cancel, a **void** forms. The pattern is a **staggered checkerboard**—bubbles at every other grid position, voids between them, each bubble connected to four diagonal neighbors through saddles and blocked from four face-to-face neighbors by voids. This is the **bubble lattice**, the universal organizing geometry at every scale (`foundations/bubble-lattice-fabric.md`).

A bubble is not spherical: stretched along Yang, contracted along Yin, its cross-section is an ellipse of axis ratio $\varphi$, a triaxial spheroid. Its boundary—where coherence drops from high to low—is steeper toward voids than toward neighbors by exactly $\sqrt{4\varphi^2/(1+\varphi^2)} \approx 1.70$. Both numbers are **Derived** and zero-parameter.

### The cascade

The spiral, stretched out along scale, is the **cascade**: starting from the Planck length $\ell_{\text{Pl}} = 1.616 \times 10^{-35}$ m and multiplying by $\varphi$ per turn generates every physical scale, with today's observable range spanning 0…292 rungs:

$$\ell_n = \ell_{\text{Pl}} \times \varphi^{n}, \qquad n \in \mathbb{Z} \quad (\text{today's observable range spans } 0 \ldots 292)$$

The human body occupies rungs 142–168: from the living cell (~8 µm) to the body (~1.7 m). Below the Planck scale the **microcascade** winds inward without end; above the horizon the **megacascade** continues outward.

### Cascade suppression

A signal that crosses $N$ rungs is attenuated by $\varphi^{-1}$ per rung:

$$\text{attenuation} = \varphi^{-N}$$

This single rule explains the deepest hierarchy puzzles in physics—the weakness of gravity, the smallness of neutrino masses, the stability of the proton (`foundations/cascade-suppression-formula.md`). For psychology it explains why the deep structures of a person are the hardest to reach: the deeper the memory, the trauma, the developmental imprint, the more rungs its signal must cross to surface, and the more coherence is required to retrieve or change it. Every clinical distance in this document—why talk stalls, why early trauma is pre-verbal, why the body keeps the score—is cascade suppression wearing a clinical hat.

**Epistemic status:** the cascade table and suppression formula are **Derived**. The application of suppression to psychological depth is **Hypothesized**.

## 7. The Pinch Point: When a Field Becomes Aware of Itself

Consider a moment in therapy when a client shifts. They have been telling a story about their mother—recounting events, describing feelings. Then they pause, and say something different: "I just realized I've been telling this same story for twenty years." The narrator has become aware of the narration. That shift—from being *in* an experience to *noticing* the experience—has a geometric threshold.

The ratio $r = E_Y/E_I$ evolves from the primordial $r_0 \approx 0.047$ toward the attractor $r = \varphi$. Along the way it crosses

$$r = \varphi^{-1} \approx 0.618$$

This is the **pinch point**, the unique inflection where the Qi gate transitions from mostly open to mostly closed.

- **Before the pinch** ($r < \varphi^{-1}$): the field is driven by external imbalance. It responds to the gap between where it is and where it is going, but does not reflect on its own state. It is reactive—pre-reflective experience: infant cognition, automatic processing, flow state, deep anesthesia. The field experiences but does not experience itself experiencing.
- **After the pinch** ($r > \varphi^{-1}$): the field's own coherence begins to modulate its evolution. The field's state now depends on its own state—the minimal condition for **self-reference**, a system containing a model of itself. Metacognition, theory of mind, the "observing self" of meditation traditions: the narrator noticing the narration.

The capacities clinical psychology works with—metacognitive monitoring, introspective accuracy, the default mode network's self-referential processing—are what a brain does with a field that has crossed this threshold. Metacognitive development in childhood (mirror self-recognition around 18 months, theory of mind around 4 years) is the gradual tuning of a biological system to its field's self-modeling capacity.

The hard problem of consciousness receives a specific, bounded answer: subjective experience arises when a field crosses the pinch point and becomes capable of self-modeling. The gap between objective dynamics and subjective experience remains open as a philosophical question, but the framework supplies a precise dynamical condition for when a system becomes a candidate for consciousness—any system, biological or artificial, whose dynamics cross this threshold. The framework's answer to why self-reference feels like anything: self-reference, when it is the field's own coherence modulating its own evolution, is what feeling *is*.

**Epistemic status:** the pinch point itself is **Derived** (it follows from the Qi gate dynamics). The claim that this transition is the structural basis of self-awareness is **Hypothesized**, with a proposed PDE test and partial computational confirmation.

## 8. Thought as Wake Wave

The framework proposes that wake waves are the physical substrate of **thought**: structured excitations that propagate through the field and, when they cross the pinch into self-modeling regions, enter awareness.

When the loop is stable, thought feels like thinking; when the loop is attenuated, thought feels like observation; when the loop is broken, thought feels alien (section 18).

## 9. Memory as Cascade Depth

Memory is **wake-wave persistence**. Early wakes—generated in the Yin-dominated epoch—are widely spaced, faint, and persistent: **long-term memory**. Late wakes—from the near-$\varphi$ epoch—are tightly packed, intense, and transient: **working memory and attention**. Memory consolidation, the transfer from working to long-term, corresponds to wake waves propagating to deeper cascade rungs.

The difference between a thought held in mind—loud, vivid, immediate—and a childhood memory—faint, textured, distant—is cascade depth. The childhood memory lives at a lower rung; its signal crosses more rungs to reach the surface, and each rung attenuates it. The memory is fainter because it is deeper, and reaching it requires more coherence. This is why old memories are recovered in states of high coherence (quiet, reverie, meditative absorption) and why they feel qualitatively different from recent ones: they are not weaker copies of the same thing; they are signals from a different depth of the same field.

The framework makes a falsifiable prediction here: memory retrieval latency and vividness should scale with cascade depth, and the distribution of autobiographical memories across the lifespan should show structure at $\varphi$-scaled intervals (section 15).

**Epistemic status: Hypothesized.**

## 10. Altered States as Geographic Phenomenology

Altered states correspond to changes in the **spatial dispersion** of the ratio $r$ across the field: $\sigma_r = \sqrt{\langle (r - \langle r\rangle)^2\rangle}$. Some regions are above the pinch (self-modeling active), others below it (no self-modeling). The geography of selfhood—which parts of the field are self-aware, and how widely awareness is distributed—changes from state to state.

### Meditation

As attention stabilizes, the field smooths out. $\sigma_r$ decreases; more regions approach $\varphi$-equilibrium. The chatter of surface thoughts quiets; the spiral unwinds more slowly; moments of awareness lengthen because the toroidal loop, which completes one cycle per wake wave, takes longer to close. Spaciousness and stillness. The self dissolves into a more uniform coherence—less jagged, less localized, less insistent. The framework predicts that long-term practice should sharpen the $\ln\varphi$-periodic spectral signature of physiological signals along the spine (Prediction CH4, section 22).

### Psychedelics

The field fragments. Regions above the pinch drop below it transiently—sub-pinch excursions. The geography of selfhood becomes patchy: some parts still model themselves, others have lost the capacity. The boundaries that normally separate self from world soften. Ego dissolution is the self becoming spatially intermittent: present in some regions, absent in others. The paradoxical psychedelic experience—simultaneously deeply connected and dissociated—is what a patchy field would produce: below-pinch regions resonate with everything ($\varphi$-structured coherence is strongest below the pinch, as the two-bubble experiment shows, section 12), while remaining above-pinch regions are isolated (self-aware fields decohere).

### Deep sleep

The field collapses toward a homogeneous, Yin-dominated state. $\sigma_r \to 0$. No variation means no wake waves, no feedback loop, no self. The field rests in its most ancient configuration—the Yin-dominated equilibrium it was born from, before the spiral started turning. Morning is the spiral re-engaging: coherence rebuilds from the deep rungs upward, and the self gradually recondenses as the ratio climbs back above the pinch.

### Dreams

In REM sleep the feedback loop is loosened but not collapsed. Wake waves propagate, reflect, and interfere—generating structured experience—but the flow → string feedback that normally curates them is attenuated. The field generates thought without fully modeling itself doing so. Dreams feel strange because the editor is off-duty: the coherence channels shift without the pinch-point oversight that normally gives experience narrative continuity. Lucid dreaming is the loop re-engaging mid-dream—the pinch dynamics come back online and the field begins to model its own wake-wave activity.

**Epistemic status: Hypothesized** (mechanism specified; test proposed for meditation and psychedelics). Dreams are **Speculative**.

## 11. The Self as a Toroidal Loop

The self is the **closed toroidal feedback loop**—string → wakes → gravity → flow → string—persisting over time.

The self is a process, a loop that feeds back on itself. When the loop is stable (wakes reflect back coherently, the flow modifies the string predictably), the self feels continuous and solid. When the loop is disrupted (wakes scatter, feedback breaks down), the self feels fragmented or absent.

The default mode network—the brain's self-referential processing system—is, in this picture, the neural correlate of the toroidal loop. When the DMN is active, the loop is running; when it is suppressed (deep meditation, flow states, psychedelics), the loop opens and the self dissolves.

**Epistemic status: Hypothesized.**

## 12. The Two-Bubble Experiment: Inter-Field Resonance

This experiment has been run computationally (July 2026). Two regions of the field with different local $r$-values are initialized and the cross-correlation of their wake fields is measured as a function of separation.

**What was found:**

- When at least one bubble is *below* the pinch (pre-reflective), correlations **revive** at $\varphi$-scaled separations—the signature of $\varphi$-periodic resonance.
- When *both* bubbles are *above* the pinch (self-aware), correlations decay monotonically, going *negative* at the largest separation—destructive interference between two self-aware fields.

The transition tracks the pinch point. Pre-reflective fields maintain long-range $\varphi$-structured coherence. Self-aware fields decohere—each bubble's internal Qi gate dominates over inter-bubble coupling. The field's self-modeling disrupts the very $\varphi$-coherence that built it.

This is a weak-to-moderate signal—consistent and reproducible, but the decisive test (scanning the Qi gate parameter) remains future work. It carries the framework's most clinically provocative consequence: **self-awareness and resonance with others exist in tension**, because they sit on opposite sides of the same geometric threshold (section 20).

**Epistemic status:** the PDE result is **verified computationally**; its interpretation as a model of interpersonal resonance is **Hypothesized**.

## 14. Emotions as Qi-Gate Configurations

### 14.2 The emotional manifold

An emotional state is a point on the manifold

$$\mathcal{E} = (\mathbf{b}, \sigma_r, q, \mathbf{c})$$

- **Quality** = channel dominance $\mathbf{b}$: the direction of the openness vector determines which emotion—pure anger, pure joy, or blends (bittersweetness is roughly equal Wood and Metal).
- **Intensity** = spatial dispersion $\sigma_r$: elevated dispersion is felt intensity; at extreme values, sub-pinch excursions occur and the emotion temporarily disables self-modeling—"losing oneself" in the feeling.
- **Clarity** = Qi coherence $q$: $q \to 1$ is a clear emotion ("I know exactly what I'm feeling and my body agrees"); $q \to 0.5$ is ambiguity ("something I can't name"); $q \to 0$ is dissociation ("I know I should feel something but I'm numb")—the physiological response and the felt experience decoupled.
- **Location** = chakra weights $\mathbf{c}$: where in the body the configuration is localized. Fear concentrates at the root; joy at the solar plexus; grief at the throat.

Clarity is independent of quality: clear anger and confused anger are different points on the manifold. Mixed emotions can be perfectly clear—complexity and confusion are different points on the manifold.

### 14.3 How emotions move: adiabatic redistribution

Coherence is conserved across the pentagon. When a stimulus ends and a channel closes, its coherence redistributes to the remaining channels in proportion to their baseline openness—the **adiabatic redistribution matrix**:

$$R_{ij} = \begin{cases} 0 & i = j \\ \frac{b_j}{\sum_{k \neq i} b_k} & i \neq j \end{cases}$$

$$R = \begin{pmatrix}
0 & 0.447 & 0.276 & 0.171 & 0.106 \\
0.567 & 0 & 0.217 & 0.134 & 0.083 \\
0.500 & 0.309 & 0 & 0.118 & 0.073 \\
0.466 & 0.288 & 0.178 & 0 & 0.068 \\
0.447 & 0.276 & 0.171 & 0.106 & 0
\end{pmatrix}$$

Each row is the blend recipe when that channel closes. When anger subsides (row 1), the released coherence flows 44.7% into Fire (relief, even joy—the dominant note), 27.6% into Earth (stabilization), 17.1% into Metal (lingering regret), 10.6% into Water (residual anxiety). The aftereffect of an emotion is not a discrete successor—it is a simultaneous blend, and the blend proportions are zero-parameter predictions of the geometry. The asymmetry is instructive: joy → anger-dominant blend (56.7%) is more intense than anger → joy-dominant blend (44.7%), because joy's closure leaves the strongest channel (Wood) in the pool to claim the largest share.

Emotions decay because the de-resonance principle pulls $r \to \varphi$, driving the gate toward baseline; the decay rate is set by $|r - \varphi|$. Deeply felt emotions (close to the attractor) linger; superficial reactions dissipate quickly. **Habituation** follows the same law: repeated exposure moves the mean $r$ closer to $\varphi$, reducing perturbation amplitude and thus response—the two-fluid field approaching its natural attractor.

### 14.4 Why five families

## 16. Trauma and the Frozen Wake

This is the framework's most clinically applicable idea, and its most tested.

### 16.1 The mechanism

A traumatic event is a perturbation too intense for the field to absorb in real time—a spike of Yang or Yin that the conversion dynamics cannot process at the rate it arrives. Normally the field dissipates perturbations: the imbalance converts into wake waves that propagate, reflect, and decay. When the perturbation exceeds processing capacity, the wake never decays. It becomes a **standing wave**—frozen at the cascade rung where it struck, spatially localized, self-sustaining.

The traumatic memory is a wake wave the field cannot process. It sits in the body—spatially localized at a specific cascade rung, in a specific region of the field—and it reflects back, reactivates, and re-perturbs the system whenever stimulated. The body keeps the score because the wake is physically in the body's field: a standing wave in the two-fluid geometry, a physical structure beneath the neural trace.

### 16.2 What the wake does to the emotional system

A frozen wake is a **perpetual stimulus**. The standing wave keeps re-perturbing the local field, keeping local $r$ away from the attractor, so the channel the original event opened never closes—and the adiabatic redistribution that resolves an emotion never fires. **Trauma = frozen wake + locked channel.** The trauma state is the manifold with the $R$-matrix frozen:

The four signatures:

Subjectively: a pinned channel means the trauma emotion is always available (triggers re-activate it instantly); starved channels mean the complementary emotional range is always suppressed; depressed $q$ at the site means the experience is felt as not-fully-real; brittle $\sigma_r$ means hypervigilance that collapses into numbness. ### 16.3 The PDE test (2026-07-31)

The lock mechanism was tested in the two-fluid PDE (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.4–10.8). The standing-pattern null is confirmed, with a set of positive mechanism findings:

- **Standing pattern does not pin the gate.** An un-driven standing wave decays at the same conversion-driven rate as a radiating packet (42% vs 44% retained over $t = 10$). A pure standing mode in the solver is not a frozen wake.
- **A weak recurring trigger sustains the wake.** The driver test (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.5) found that ongoing re-stimulation—a chronic trigger at 0.005% of the event peak per step—holds the site at 80% of event intensity with $q$ depressed and the phase displaced; stopping the trigger releases the site on the conversion timescale. - **A $\varphi$-phased oscillation releases a displaced gate.** Adding a drive at the site at period $\varphi \cdot P_0$ (the EMDR analog, where $P_0$ is the site's natural oscillation period) accelerates relaxation: perturbation retained falls to 65% (vs 91% undriven), site coherence returns toward global, and the phase histogram returns to baseline.

The EMDR-analog claim is supported in its strong form: the decay drive is $\varphi$-structured, not any oscillation.
- **The gate sign.** The solver's conversion is $\text{conv} = -\lambda(1-q)\varepsilon$: a low-$q$ site has *elevated* conversion capacity, not reduced. When $q$ is low the gate is open and conversion runs hard; when $q$ is high the gate is closed.
- **Background coherence does not modulate susceptibility.** A second identical event on a pre-stressed site ($q$ depressed, phase fully displaced) leaves the same trace as the first event on a quiet field—recovery is complete either way (§10.6). The processing-capacity threshold is not the pre-event $q$.
- **The $\varphi$-phased drain has a sharp rate onset.** Below ~5/s the drive is phase-blind (ambient re-exposure accumulates by mean rate, §10.5); above ~50/s the $\varphi$-phased oscillation drains the site while the off-$\varphi$ oscillation at the same rate does nothing (§10.7).

The five-way mapping must be carried by the gate's openness manifold, not the field angle.

### 16.4 Fight, flight, freeze in the gate

The acute stress responses map onto channel states, and their chronic forms are channel locks:

- **Fight = Wood lock.** The threat is met with rising, assertive energy; unresolved, the person becomes chronically irritable, boundary-hypervigilant, quick to anger, slow to soothe. The anger is the standing wave re-activating.
- **Flight = Water lock.** The threat is met with escape; unresolved, chronic vigilance, avoidance, anxiety, perpetual readiness to flee.
- **Freeze = incomplete lock.** The perturbation exceeds capacity so abruptly that no channel completes its activation—the field is caught mid-configuration: not open (no channel dominant), not closed (the wave keeps feeding it). Tonic immobility, depersonalization, time distortion. Chronic dissociation is the freeze lock persisting.

The trigger whose phase matches the original event's phase re-activates the lock—a sound, a smell, a posture, a relational pattern carrying the same pentagon angle.

### 16.5 Suppression: the voluntary partial lock

Not all channel pins are traumatic. **Suppression** is the self-modeling system deliberately holding a channel shut—holding the coherence instead of releasing it. Trauma and suppression differ in origin (external overload vs internal control), site (pinned at a rung vs distributed), and reversibility (requires rung-reach + phase-change vs reversible by the same self-modeling that imposed it). But the consequence is the same class of signature at lower intensity: one channel starved, the $R$-matrix blocked for that row. This makes a diagnostic distinction: a channel locked by suppression can recover through the channel's own dynamics (allowing the emotion, letting it complete); a trauma lock cannot, because the standing wave keeps re-opening the channel no matter how it is approached. The failure of "just feel it" in trauma is not resistance—it is the standing wave.

## 18. Therapy Through the Geometry

The frozen wake lives at a specific rung—often a lower, more somatic rung, if the trauma was early or physical.
