referenced by the implementation, runners, or retained receipts. Runtime
behavior is established by source and executable evidence rather than by these
documents alone.

- `00-foundations.md` through `13-requirements-registry.md` and
  `CASSI-QI-FLOW-INTELLIGENCE-IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md`: profile and flow
  specifications.
- `CASSI-FIELD-INTELLIGENCE-DESIGN.md`, `GROUNDED-LANGUAGE-PLAN.md`, and
  `MULTI-TURN-DISCOURSE-PREREG.md`: field and grounded-language records.
- `BILATERAL-COUNTERFLOW-UPGRADE-PLAN.md`,
  `GENERATIVE-ABSTRACTION-DESIGN.md`, and
  `UNIVERSAL-DATA-FIELD-DESIGN.md`: relational and abstraction records.
- L29–L49, `BLIND-LABYRINTH-PREREG.md`, and
  `HARMONIC-RECONSTRUCTION-AUDIT-PREREG.md`: fixed inputs consumed by retained
  runners and verifiers.
- `qi-native-field-dependence-prereg.md`: model-interface counterfactual
  measurement input; it is not a runtime dependency.
