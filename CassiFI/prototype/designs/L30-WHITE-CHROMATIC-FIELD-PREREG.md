## Frozen coordinates

For complex Yang/Yin position and velocity:

\[
D=Y-\phi I,\qquad C=\phi Y+I,
\]

\[
Y=\frac{D+\phi C}{1+\phi^2},\qquad
I=\frac{C-\phi D}{1+\phi^2},
\]

with \(\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\). The identical transform applies to velocities.

Across the seven color channels define the normalized white vector

\[
w=\frac{1}{\sqrt7}(1,1,1,1,1,1,1).
\]

The white carrier coordinates are

\[
c_w=w^\dagger C,\qquad v_w=w^\dagger V_C.
\]

All remaining common components, all differential components, and their velocities form the memory/chromatic complement. White is a derived global coordinate, not an eighth channel and not the violet/crown channel.

## Frozen energy budget and heartbeat

Mean active dynamic energy is

\[
E=\frac{1}{7(1+\phi^2)}\operatorname{mean}_m\sum_{s=0}^{6}
\left(|C|^2+|D|^2+|V_C|^2+|V_D|^2\right).
\]

White-carrier energy is

\[
E_w=\frac{1}{7(1+\phi^2)}\operatorname{mean}_m
\left(|c_w|^2+|v_w|^2\right).
\]

Every logical tick begins with one heartbeat. Frozen targets are:

- white-carrier energy `heartbeat_carrier_energy = 0.5`;
- total field energy budget `field_energy_budget = 1.0`.

The heartbeat performs one orthogonal budget operation:

1. Project `C` and `V_C` into the white carrier and its orthogonal complement.
2. If complement energy exceeds `0.5`, radially scale the complete complement to `0.5`; otherwise leave it unchanged.
3. Radially set white-carrier energy to `0.5`. If it is numerically zero, seed `c_w` uniformly across active modes with real unit phase and `v_w = 0` before scaling.
4. Recompose the seven channels. Do not change `epsilon2_ema`.

The operation may dissipate excess complement or carrier energy. Its positive total-energy change is reported as injected energy and its negative total-energy change magnitude as dissipated energy. Input events never supply energy.

For a zero state, the first heartbeat produces equal per-channel dynamic energy, zero differential coordinates, and total energy `0.5`.

## Frozen chromatic refraction

The fixed channel phases are

\[
h_s=e^{2\pi i s/7},\qquad s=0,\ldots,6.
\]

For codebook phase \(u_{xm}\) of symbol \(x\), define the unit chromatic direction

\[
q_{sxm}=\frac{h_su_{xm}}{\sqrt7}.
\]

For trust \(g\in[0,1]\), \(\alpha=\pi g/2\), and

\[
d_q=q_x^\dagger D,\qquad v_q=q_x^\dagger V_D,
\]

apply the two independent unitary rotations

\[
d'_q=\cos\alpha\,d_q+\sin\alpha\,c_w,
\qquad
c'_w=\cos\alpha\,c_w-\sin\alpha\,d_q,
\]

\[
v'_q=\cos\alpha\,v_q+\sin\alpha\,v_w,
\qquad
v'_w=\cos\alpha\,v_w-\sin\alpha\,v_q.
\]

Only those projected components change; all orthogonal components remain unchanged. This preserves total active dynamic magnitude. Applying input to an all-zero state leaves it exactly zero.

## Frozen coherent readout

Project every channel differential field into the immutable codebook:

\[
a_{sx}=\frac1W\sum_m \overline{u_{xm}}D_{sm}.
\]

Apply only the fixed channel-phase compensation

\[
\tilde a_{sx}=\overline{h_s}a_{sx}.
\]

Do not normalize banks independently and do not perform candidate-specific phase alignment. The global coefficient and score are

\[
A_x=\frac1{\sqrt7}\sum_s\tilde a_{sx},\qquad
S_x=|A_x|^2.
\]

Per-channel scores are \(|a_{sx}|^2\). A channel is active when its differential RMS is at least `1e-8`; readout is available when total differential energy is at least `1e-8` and at least two channels are active. It is not gated by channel 6.

For the winning symbol:

\[
\chi=\frac{|\sum_s\tilde a_s|^2}
{N_{\rm active}\sum_s|\tilde a_s|^2+10^{-12}}.
\]

Exact score ties resolve to the smaller symbol ID.

## Frozen read-only psychedelic projection

Projection adds no adaptive state and must leave the field byte-identical. Canonical side is `32`, using all `1024` active modes. For a smaller smoke state, use the largest integer side not exceeding both the request and \(\lfloor\sqrt W\rfloor\), and select `side**2` modes at deterministic evenly spaced indices `floor(j*W/side**2)`.

Reshape selected modal coefficients into a square spectrum and apply an orthonormal two-dimensional inverse FFT. Let `P_w` be the white-common wave and `P_s` each differential channel wave. Frozen intensities are

\[
I_w=|P_w|^2,\qquad I_s=(\operatorname{Re}P_s)^2.
\]

The fixed channel RGB vectors are:

1. red `(1.00, 0.00, 0.00)`;
2. orange `(1.00, 0.35, 0.00)`;
3. yellow `(1.00, 1.00, 0.00)`;
4. green `(0.00, 1.00, 0.20)`;
5. blue `(0.00, 0.25, 1.00)`;
6. indigo `(0.25, 0.00, 0.75)`;
7. violet `(0.65, 0.00, 1.00)`.

Raw RGB is `I_w + sum_s(rgb_s * I_s)`. The bounded display value is `raw / (1 + raw)`. The projection returns tensors and the canonical runner writes a PNG only as a read-only receipt.

## Frozen acceptance gates

### Mechanical `FAIL`

Return `FAIL` before functional classification on any of:

- missing, non-sibling, noncanonical, nonfinite, schema, identity, preregistration-hash, source-hash, configuration, shape, or independently recomputed metric mismatch;
- input changes an all-zero state;
- first-heartbeat carrier-energy relative error above `1e-5`;
- first-heartbeat total-energy absolute error above `1e-5` from `0.5`;
- first-heartbeat per-channel energy relative spread above `1e-5`;
- first-heartbeat maximum `abs(D)` above `1e-6`;
- maximum instantaneous input dynamic-energy drift above `5e-5`;
- any nonfinite state, inactive-mode change, component clamp, or aggregate safety rescale;
- maximum total mean dynamic energy above `1.05` after any heartbeat on the blank, task, or stress paths;
- readout or projection changes the field;
- repeated projection differs bitwise;
- projection is nonfinite, outside `[0, 1]`, or has RGB standard deviation below `1e-4`;
- target and counterfactual projection RMS difference is at most `1e-4`.

### Functional decision

With mechanics green, return `ADOPT` only if every condition holds:

1. Exact pre-evolution target readout accuracy is `1.0`.
2. Tick-0 post-evolution target accuracy is at least `0.875`.
3. Mean target reciprocal rank over pre-distractor ticks `(1, 2, 4)` is at least `0.75`.
4. Tick-8 distractor accuracy is at least `0.75`.
5. Mean distractor reciprocal rank over ticks `(16, 32, 64)` is at least `0.25`.
6. Mean original-target reciprocal rank over ticks `(16, 32, 64)` is at least `0.05`.
7. Mean winning white coherence at tick 0 is at least `0.90`.
8. The 128-tick blank path remains differentially blank at `max abs(D) <= 1e-6`.
9. The 128-tick cyclic-input stress path remains finite, clamp-free, and within the heartbeat energy gate.

Return `REJECT` when mechanics are green and any functional condition fails. Return `INCOMPLETE` only for an interrupted or unavailable canonical device before a complete board exists.
