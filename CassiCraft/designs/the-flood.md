## 1. The Flood, stated once

> **The Flood is the q-excess event — the field's one abundance-extreme.** A
> region where coherence pools so high the field oversaturates: the harvest-tide
> q-accumulation — the same accrual that normally fills veins and makes machines
> near-lossless — pushed past a limit where it stops *enriching* and starts
> *engulfing*. It is a hazard that is not loss but oversaturation; **the harvest
> that drowns**; and it is **the cold's opposite and equal danger** — the long-thin
> and the long-thick, two seasons the event-mechanics structurally miss by the
> same argument.

The corpus's danger layer designs *events* — each with a front, a readable onset,
and a verdict. The storm's `ε²` front arrives and passes; the desert's collapse
creeps; the Coda precipitates toward a trail it can resolve; the BH grows
monotonically. Each is a *happening*. The **cold** ([`the-cold.md`](./the-cold.md))
was the corpus's first answer to the class the event frame misses: the **long
exposure** — the sustained thin that is not an event but a *climate*. It has no
front and no verdict; its only "event" is that it keeps being.

The flood is the **mirror** the corpus has been missing. Every hazard the corpus
designed is a *deficiency* — the thin, the drain, the desert, the cold, the
BH's accretion, the Coda feeding on scarcity. The flood is the **too-much**: the
same long-exposure discipline aimed at the abundance end. Where the cold is the
window whose tide-times the thin-tide decade into a lived season, the flood is
the window whose tide-times the **harvest-tide into a lived season** — a
sustained period where regional `q` *holds above the enriching band*, every read
saturates, the vent backs up in reverse (nothing to shed, everywhere to drown),
and what a settlement does *across the whole surge* is the real difficulty: not
surviving one high-q peak but living through a season where the field cannot
carry your op.

**Two load-bearing anchors ground this. First, the tide's own abundance.**
`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2 names the harvest as the field's organized state —
`q` high, `ε²` low, near the attractor (`q ≈ 0.947`), machines near-lossless,
ore precipitating where q accumulates (§3.2), the biosphere's basins at full
strength. That is *bounty*. The flood's premise is that the same accumulation,
**pushed past its enriching band**, does not keep enriching — it starts
engulfing. **[design]** — the *surfeit* is this doc's reading of the harvest
state overshot, probe-calibrated against the tide's measured accumulation.

**Second, the fate's strain phase has an abundance twin.** `fate-of-a-window.md`
names decay as the arc where the bath drains and the field grows poor. A window
can also drift into the field-rich surplus — over-provisioned, over-held, the
bath grown past its need. The flood is that arc's climate at the other pole: not
the moment the window tips field-rich, but the *long season of oversaturation* in
which every ordinary op is no longer usable because the field is too organized to
carry it.

**The honest scope of the claim:** the **q-accumulation and the `(1−q)` waste law
are engine-real / [design]-landed** (exactly where their source docs draw the
line); **the flood as a season — the surfeit threshold, the over-bloom, the
shedding, the anti-bloom husbandry — is this doc's [design] over them.** That is
the line §5 draws and never blurs.

## 3. The honest resolution — the cap stress-tested from the other side

The corpus's single hard rule (`energy-harnessing.md` §6, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`) is
designed, in every prior hazard, to hold against **deficiency-farming**: the thin,
the drained, the hazardous — places a player might be tempted to *mine* because
the field is wasteful there. The flood is the **other side of the same gate**, and
it is the sharpest test the cap faces:

> **A q-surfeit must NOT yield free energy, or the "harvest is not free" guard
> breaks.** If a region so rich in organization could be *tapped* for net gain,
> the entire constraint economy — the charge/scar asymmetry, the ambient-pump cap,
> the no-mint discipline — would be a fraud: the field's own bounty would be a
> generator, and "harvest is not free" would be a lie the surfeit exposed.

**The Flood resolves this by making the surfeit waste upward.** The design does
not fight the surfeit's tempting yield; it *encodes the honest consequence*:

> **The excess organization sheds through the `(1−q)` waste faster than it can be
> captured, so a flood is a self-limiting bloom, never a power source.** The
> `(1−q)` waste law (`energy-harnessing.md` §2) is the machine-loss floor — but at
> the surfeit, the *surplus organization itself* runs the same law: the field
> sheds its over-organization as waste, and the shedding outpaces any capture. A
> machine built to harvest the surfeit reads — through the same §2 law — a
> throughput that *wastes upward* into the glow it cannot bank.

Concretely, three guards, all inherited from `energy-harnessing.md` §6 and
`field-hazards.md` §5.3, now pointed *up* instead of *down*:

1. **You cannot farm a flood.** The ambient cascade pump (§1.7) that every
   organized region rides is present at the surfeit and *stronger* — but it is
   forbidden to charge a capacitor (§6 rule 2), and the write-back amplitude cap
   `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` (rule 1) caps any harvest the way it caps every harvest:
   the surfeit's throughput is bounded by what the machine itself inputs, so at
   surplus the only "yield" is the `(1−q)`-rounded waste. **A flood is dangerous
   abundance, and the cap closes it: the richer the surfeit, the more it wastes,
   never the more it gives.**
2. **The auroral/collector and tide-farming gates close the flood the same way.**
   The auroral collector harvests only the `(1−q)` fraction that would be wasted,
   is amplitude-capped, and is *diminished as the player heals the field*
   (`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` §3.4; `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5d). At a
   surfeit the `(1−q)` fraction is *small* (`q → 1`, `(1−q) → 0`) — so the most
   tempting-abundant hazard yields the *least* per-site, and a player holding a
   region in surfeit to farm it reads a diminishing, costed tap, never a grinder.
3. **A player who tries to farm a flood reads the waste, not a harvest.** The
   honest consequence is legible and honest: the instruments show the surfeit's
   `(1−q)` glow *bright and wasteful* even though `q` is high — a designed
   inversion of the harvest's faint-glow signature — and every attempt to
   withdraw at the peak returns the shed waste, not a store. **The attempt is not
   hidden or penalized; it simply reads as what it is: the field's surplus cannot
   be banked, because banking it would mint.**

The through-line is the corpus's own both-sides honesty: the cold shows the cap
holding against the temptation of scarcity (**nothing wasteful can be minted**);
the flood shows the cap holding against the temptation of abundance (**nothing
overflowing can be minted**). Together they make the no-free-energy rule the most
tested law in the corpus: **it holds against both the empty field and the full
one.** The flood is the corpus's first abundance-extreme and its sharpest statement
of the cap's both-sides honesty.

**The wrong-band's aftereffect (reverse pointer, two-way).** This §3's self-limiting
surfeit is *only* self-limiting while nothing feeds the wrong-band — and what
happens when the surfeit's over-bloom outlives the season is the danger layer's
first *ecological* hazard: [`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md) §2.1 is the flood's
wrong-band as a *blight's habitat*, the over-bloom made *corrupt* (a sustained
`q`-floor keeps the kept organisms from shedding, and they turn into a living
drain — §2.1 there grounds the wrong-band on this §2's surfeit, and §3 there on
this §3's shedding the blight's own feeding must honesty obey). Where the flood
*sheds* and recedes, the blight *persists* if fed; the two docs now cite each other
as read-and-answered (the flood is the wrong-band's season, the blight its
aftereffect).

**The flood's medium (reverse pointer, two-way).** This §2/§3 surfeit-and-shedding
is the abundance-extreme of the corpus's water doc: [`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md)
§2c reads **the flood as the wave made water** — "the sea is the flood's medium;
the flood is the sea's overflow," every flood-feature (over-precipitation-then-
re-dissolve, the deafening resonance, the shedding) being the liquid regime pushed
past its band, with **no new overflow** invented by the sea doc (`the-sea.md` §2c —
it inherits this §2/§3 and this §6b's gates for its overflow unchanged). The flood
is the sea's own abundance-extreme; the two docs now cite each other as
read-and-answered.

## 4. The flood's life and what a settlement does

### 4.1 The season's arc

The flood is not a binary on/off; it is the tide's accumulation *read long enough
to become a season*. Its arc has three named phases, each a designed reading over
the real q-accumulation:

| Season phase | What the field does | The player reads |
|---|---|---|
| **The rise — the harvest becomes the flood when it overshoots.** The tide's q-accumulation (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.2) climbs through the enriching band into the surfeit. This is the farmer's warning ([`farm-that-feeds.md`] — *a crop precipitates in a band; a flood overshoots the band*): at the moment the band's top edge is crossed, the harvest-tide stops being "harvest the harvest" (§3.2) and becomes "the harvest is about to drown." **The abundance does not announce itself as a hazard; it announces itself as an unusually generous season.** | the reader's q-trend climbing past the band's top; the crop's glow shifting from the healthy faint (a well-held band) to the alarming wrong-bright (the band overshot); the sky's calm coherent bands *widening unnaturally*. |
| **The bloom's peak — the deafening.** The surfeit holds. Ore over-precipitates and re-dissolves; machines hum past useful; every read saturates; the shallow ecology over-blooms. **This is the peak's trouble: it is not a catastrophe that arrives, but a fullness that is already wrong.** | nothing left to direct — the reader pins, the glass saturates, the vent has nothing to shed, the world is *too organized to use*. |
| **The shedding — the self-limit.** The surfeit cannot hold; the excess organization sheds through the `(1−q)` waste (energy §2) faster than it can be captured, and the field recedes toward the enriching band. **A flood is self-limiting** — it does not end in a crash (the field does not empty), it recedes like a tide going out, the over-precipitate re-dissolving into the band the field can actually hold. | the wrong-bright glow decaying, the reader's pin loosening, the bloom thinning, the ordinary economy of the enriching band returning. |

The arc is the cold's mirrored in sign: the cold is *harvest that never returns*;
the flood is *harvest that will not leave* — and both are survived by the same
discipline.

### 4.2 The settlement's acts — husbanded, not harvested

The flood's difficulty, like the cold's, is **decision-shaped**: it is not
survived by winning one confrontation but by a season of bounded, legible
stewardship. But where the cold's acts *stretch scarce stores*, the flood's acts
**trim the surfeit back to the band**. The same stewardship the cold demands,
pointed at too-much instead of too-little:

| The husbandry act | What it is, in the flood | Grounded in | The honest cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anti-bloom** | the anti-corruption act inverted: where the cold *suppresses ε²* to hold `q` up (`energy-harnessing.md` §5.4), the flood *bleeds organized surplus* to hold `q` *down* — spending fed decoherence (the reverse of anti-corruption) to pull the regional `q` back under the band's top | `energy-harnessing.md` §5.4 (anti-corruption, the sign-inverted twin); `material-regimes.md` §3 (the band) | a **net-negative spend** — you pay coherence-like effort (a bounded Q4 write, amplitude-capped §6) to *stop* the field from overshooting, exactly as anti-corruption pays to stop it from undershooting. Never free; the Board books it faithfully as a contribution. |
| **Thinning the bloom** | the ecology's over-bloom is *pruned* — withdrawing the crowd's stored coherence (a deep-rung reaper's act, `energy-harnessing.md` §2.5) to make room for the deep-rung basins the field would otherwise return to | `field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.2 (the over-bloom crowds the basins); §5.2 (hunting/farming = interacting with coherence structures) | the thinning is a **withdrawal that scars** (the reaper's by-product, §2.5) — a flood settlement trades *crowd* for *scar*, a real bound on how hard it can thin before it wounds the band it is trying to save. |
| **Holding the sink open** | a flood surfeit has no natural recede while the accumulation keeps coming; the settlement keeps a *deliberate drain* (a Q4 write that bleeds excess q out of the region) so the band never overshoots | `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.1 (husbandry with a clock — invest before the worst); `energy-harnessing.md` §6 (the drain is bounded, net-negative) | the drain is a **standing, costed draw** — the settlement pays to keep the surfeit from forming, continuously, exactly as a cold settlement pays to keep the desert from taking. |
| **Timing the work off the peak** | the flood's deafening means *no productive deep work* — so a settlement plans its harvest, mining, authoring, and channeling **around the band**, doing the deep work at the enriching high, *before* the surfeit, and resting (or voyaging) through it | `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.2 (harvest-the-harvest; but a flood has *passed* harvest into unusable) — the timing axis inverts: the work is timed *off* the flood, not *into* it | the *foregone* work is the cost — the settlement chooses not to exploit the apparent bounty, a real and legible restraint. |
| **Voyaging out** | the surfeit, like the cold, is a legitimate reason to leave — a window stuck in a season the field cannot use is a window that leaves, endures (husbands), or decays (lets the surfeit swamp it) | `world-seams.md` §2.3 (the voyage is a coherence-budget problem; timing to the home window's state); `the-cold.md` §3 (the departure as a real reason) | the voyage is a **withdrawal** (spends stored coherence to leave), a real decision between enduring the surfeit and abandoning the window's over-full field. |

**The through-line: the flood is the arc's abundance-texture.** Every act above is
a **bounded Q4 write** or a **read-only consumer** of the publish (the

a hidden slider. And because the field is deterministic (one PDE), **a window's

§3): the same settlement, facing the same surfeit's demands, makes the same
anti-bloom choices and reaps the same result — husbandry teachable and honest,
never seeded-RNG luck.
