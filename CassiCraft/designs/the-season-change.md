## 3. The least-legible moment — the load-bearing design

### 3.1 The turn is when the field is least legible

This is the Season-Change's load-bearing design claim, and it is grounded in the
sources' own honest reads:

> **The turn is when the field is least legible.** As a window's tide crosses, its
> regional operating coherence `q` spends an interval in the **q-between** — neither
> the harvest's high organized `q ≈ 0.947` band nor the thin's low but *stable* noise-
> floor `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1` reading, but the *moving* value of a field in transition. In
> that interval the instruments' reads **noise up**: the pulse's channels — the bath's
> `(1−q)` trend, the lines' mutual drift — are at their *most ambiguous* during the
> crossing (`the-window-pulse.md` §2.1 — the pulse reads the *pattern over many
> tides*, never one event, and a crossing's per-instant samples are the raggedy,
> hard-to-read middle of that pattern); the weatherglass's read during the crossing
> shows a leaning, changing `(1−q)` rather than a settled phase (`field-instruments.md`
> §1.2's four forms are a *state* idiom; a crossing is the least-stateful moment).

The honest grounding: the tide's own §1.1 honesty — the *direction* the field moves
is engine-real, but the *period, amplitude, and phase-locking* are what the probe
must measure. During a crossing, the field is *changing*, so the reads are reading a
derivative, not a state. **The turn is the corpus's honest uncertainty: the moment a
settlement can least trust a single glance of any instrument, because every
instrument is reading a field in motion.**

### 3.2 The honest consequence — a settlement must act on the shift

The turn being least-legible is not a decoration; it is the risk's shape. The two
acts the corpus already designs at the crossing are *urgent and must happen despite*
the muddy read:

- **Husband the bath before it thins.** The Qi bath is a maintained high-`q` core, a
  draw, "brittle if it goes down" (`house-that-steers.md` §2.2; `energy-harnessing.md`
  §4.4). Through the turn into thin, the bath's standing draw bites — it must spend
  more to hold its core `q` up against a field that is de-organizing (the tide's exact
  words, `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2, energy row; the cold's husbandry, `the-cold.md`
  §3). **A settlement that reads the crossing correctly husbands the bath *before*
  it thins — the anti-corruption held when the field still pays for it, the margin
  kept before the trough's creep** (`house-that-steers.md` §3.4; `tide-of-the-attractor.md`
  §3.1 — invest before the trough).
- **Harvest before the band dies.** The farm harvests at tide-high (`farm-that-feeds.md`
  §5 — the crop's stored coherence peaks at high `q`, the band cheapest to hold). At
  the turn down into thin, the crop's band is about to be **under threat** — the farm
  must take the harvest *at* the crossing, on the cusp, before the band fails and the
  thin's `(1−q)` toll rises (`farm-that-feeds.md` §4/§5 — the harvest taken at the
  peak; `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.2 — harvest the harvest).

**The honest consequence, stated once.** Every urgent act at the turn — husband the
bath, harvest the band — must happen **on a field the settlement cannot read as
cleanly as it reads the settled phases.** That is the designed risk: the field's
honest uncertainty is *when the decisions that matter most must be made*. The
settlement that reads the least-legible moment well is the one that acted *through*
the mud, not the one that waited for a clean read that never came before the trough.

### 3.3 Never a hidden difficulty meter

The least-legible moment is **the corpus's honest uncertainty, never a hidden
difficulty meter.** Four guards keep it honest, each inherited from its source doc:

1. **It is a real, deterministic consequence of the field's motion, not a tuned
