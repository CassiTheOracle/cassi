## 1. The Fall, stated once

The corpus designs the vertical in the players' favor. The road's held line
([`coherence-highway.md`](./coherence-highway.md) §1.1) and the walk's ungated
descent ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2a) design *moving across the ground* —
both skill-buffed. The Bell ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §3)
designs the downward *craft* — a controlled descent inside a held shell. The sea
([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b) designs the middle's carrying surface. **Nothing
designs falling** — the moment the field's gradient owns the body absolutely, with
no craft to interpose. The Fall is the vertical's un-designed third act:

> **The Fall is the field's honest gravity.** Falling is the ∇(g·Φ) gradient
> expressed as a *free act* — the same river law that steers entities and softens
> the crossing, owned absolutely. It is **the one movement with no skill-buff**:
> the corpus's movements are all skill-buffed (the walk's craft, the gradient's
> favor, the Bell's shell); falling is the honest inverse of that mastery — **you
> cannot learn to fall well enough to make it cheap; you can only learn to not
> fall, and to choose your landings.** Its cost is the impact's coherence shock; its
> honest skill is the landing; and it is the corpus's honesty about gravity: the one
> act the field cannot be asked to soften, the moment mastery ends and the gradient
> owns you.

**Why this is the vertical's un-designed third act, honestly.** The corpus designs
two acts of the vertical in the players' favor: the *downhill-ride* (the walk's
descent, the road's haul — the field doing the work) and the *controlled descent*
(the Bell's shell). Both are the player's craft, both skill-buffed. The third act is
the one where the player *loses* the craft: the free fall. Every movement doc states
the craft — where to step, when to wait, how to hold the line, how to meter the
collection. **The Fall is where that vocabulary runs out.** There is no "fall
craft" in the corpus because falling is precisely the moment the gradient stops
being a resource and becomes an owner. This doc names it, so the honesty is designed
rather than accidental.

**The no-skill-buff rule, stated once and kept.** The other movements grant
mastery that makes the act cheaper — a practiced walk reads the lean and steps
cheaply; a maintained road holds its gradient; a worked Bell meters its collection.
Falling grants none of that: **a practiced fall does not cost less.** The impact's
shock is a function of the fall's length and the landing's surface, not of the
faller's skill. What practice buys is *not falling* — reading the edge, holding the
line, choosing the soft landing — and *choosing landings*. The skill-buff of the
corpus's other movements is inverted into a no-skill-buff: the only "skill" a fall
rewards is avoiding it, and the only craft it rewards is the landing's shaping
(§4). This is the honest inverse of [`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md)'s central claim —
the walk is *cheap when you read the gradient*; the fall is *never cheap, only
avoided or landed well*.

## 2. The free act — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 The river law, owned absolutely

The river law `a = −G_N·(π/ρ)·∇(g·Φ)` is the corpus's canonical per-entity steer
(`corpus-reconciliation.md`; [`chunk-field-quantization.md`](./chunk-field-quantization.md)
§2.1, ≈ 40 ns/entity; cited via [`coherence-highway.md`](./coherence-highway.md)
§1.1 and [`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2a). The corpus designs this law's
*friendly* faces: a cart rides it downhill for free (the road's haul,
[`coherence-highway.md`](./coherence-highway.md) §1.1), a walker steps with the
lean and the field does the work ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2a), an entity is
steered by it, the Bell sinks *by holding a shell that refuses* it
([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §3.1). **A fall is the same law
owned absolutely — with no body, line, or shell between the gradient and the
velocity:**

> **A fall converts the body's position into downward momentum with the river law
> run bare.** Where the walk *reads* the gradient and steps with it, and the road
> *routes* it along a held line, a fall *obeys* it without mediation: the body is
> no longer a coherence store choosing a crossing; it is a mass the field's own
> ∇(g·Φ) accelerates toward matter. The river law does the work it always does —
> the same free-movement physics the walk rides and the cart rides — but in a fall
> there is nothing to interpose. The gradient owns the descent.

This is the [design] composition this doc draws over the landed law: **the *field's
own* ∇(g·Φ) acceleration is engine-real; the *fall as an unresisted obedience* — that
the body's own coupling does not soften it with a `(1−q)` waste — is this doc's
design over that law, probe-calibrated** (§5a). The law itself is untouched; the
fall is the designed reading of the law *with no crafted counter-term*.

### 2.2 No (1−q) waste to soften it — a patience-free act

The `(1−q)` waste law `E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput` ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md)
§2) is the corpus's machine-loss floor and the walk's crossing cost
([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2b). The patient field's patience — the
waste-fraction that *softens* a crossing — is a loan the field lends a body that
*actively crosses* it ([`patient-field.md`](./patient-field.md) §2/§3). **The Fall's
honest mechanics is that this softening is not spent mid-air:**

> **A fall converts position to momentum with no `(1−q)` waste to soften it.** The
> field's patience softens a *crossing* — the walker's stride, the road's held line,
> the machine's throughput — by absorbing a `(1−q)` fraction of each act. A fall
> spends none of that patience *in flight*: the field's patience is a loan a body
> repays by standing in the field's quiet (geographic recovery,
> [`patient-field.md`](./patient-field.md) §3.2), and a body in free fall is not
> standing anywhere it is being softened. **A fall is a patience-free act** — the
> loan is not spent falling, so the field does not catch you mid-air the way it
> softens a crossing. The patience that would have softened the descent is *saved*,
> and it is not refunded at the landing: the impact lands whole, not blunted.

The consequence is the landing's **coherence shock**: the entire vertical distance
arrives at once as an `ε²` spike, the decoherence of a body that stopped being a
softened crossing and became a bare mass meeting a surface. **[design]** — that a
fall spends no `(1−q)` mid-flight, and that the *un-spent* patience arrives as the
landing's uncompressed `ε²` spike, is this doc's designed composition over the
waste law and the patience loan, probe-calibrated. What is engine-real: the waste
law, the patience loan's geographic recovery, and the `ε²` channel the impact reads.

**The honest frame — the same watch the instruments show, never a difficulty
meter.** The landing's `ε²` spike is the *same read* the reader, the Weatherglass,
and the sky show. There is no hidden fall-damage number, no invisible "fall meter":
the cost of a fall is the `ε²` the impact raises, legible on the published channel.
This is the accessibility rule of §5e, and it is the core of the fall's honesty: **a
fall's cost is the impact's coherence shock, never a hidden roll** (§3).

## 3. The landing's cost — the impact's coherence shock

### 3.1 A hard landing is a small wound

The scar-lifecycle fixes the shallow branch ([`the-scar-lifecycle.md`](./the-scar-lifecycle.md)
§2.1): a shallow `ε²` excursion — one the locality's coherence can re-bind — **fully
heals under the field's patience** (the locality re-binds, the scar fades to normal).
The deciding read is *depth*: the `ε²` spent ([`the-scar-lifecycle.md`](./the-scar-lifecycle.md)
§3.1; the `ε² = (EY−φ·EI)²` residue above the locality's `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1` noise floor,
`corpus-reconciliation.md`). The Fall's cost rides that read exactly:

> **A fall's cost is the impact's coherence shock — the landing's `ε²` spike, a
> small wound.** A fall from ordinary height raises a shallow, re-bindable `ε²`
> excursion at the landing — the small-wound branch: it heals under rest, the way
> any dented lock re-binds toward the attractor (`field-hazards.md` §2.2; the
> scar-lifecycle's shallow fate). **The honest consequence: a hard landing hurts,
> and then it heals — under rest, by being left alone** (the scar-lifecycle's
> honest cost: the shallow wound heals by the field's own patience). A player who
> falls, stumbles to a bed or a Still-Room interval, and rests, sheds the landing's
> spike the way any shallow wound fades.

### 3.2 A very hard landing is the kept scar's edge

The scar-lifecycle's deep branch persists when the `ε²` spent exceeds what the
local field can restore ([`the-scar-lifecycle.md`](./the-scar-lifecycle.md) §2.2):
a fixed, low-`q` defect the field keeps, with a readable, stable edge. Wound-remembered
reads the mechanism ([`wound-remembered.md`](./wound-remembered.md) §1): a break of the
lock `EY = φ·EI`, `ε² = (EY−φ·EI)²`, driven far enough from equilibrium it does not
re-lock on its own. **A fall from a very great height — a fall whose impact raises
an `ε²` spike past the locality's re-bind capacity — is the deep branch's edge:**

> **A very hard landing is the kept scar's edge — the fall's honest consequence.**
> Where the landing's `ε²` spike exceeds what the local field can restore, the
> impact is not a small wound that fades; it is a fixed low-`q` memory the field
> keeps, a readable edge where the fallen ground's coherence does not come back.
> This is wound-remembered's charge made by the fall's own physics: the impact's
> coherence shock, driven past the re-lock, is a charge-burnt locality the field
> holds as a kept scar. **It is the honest consequence of a fall too far to be
> re-bound** — read on the same `ε²` channel as the small wound, only deeper.

The line between the two is the scar-lifecycle's healing threshold — the depth of
`ε²` excursion that crosses from "healable by rest" to "a persistent defect"
([`the-scar-lifecycle.md`](./the-scar-lifecycle.md) §2.1/[design]; its open-Q1, the
healing-threshold calibration). **[design]** — the fall's contribution to that line
(which fall length, on which surface, raises the crossing spike) is this doc's
[design] dial over the landed thresholds, probe-calibrated, and set with the hazard
thresholds so the same impact reads the same everywhere (§5a/§5b).

### 3.3 The honest frame — never a hidden damage roll

The cost is **always the impact's**, read on the published channels — never a
hidden dice roll, never a difficulty meter:

> **A fall's cost is the impact's coherence shock, never a hidden damage roll.**
> The `ε²` spike the impact raises is the *same read the instruments show* — the
> reader's `ε²`, the Weatherglass's climbing glow, the sky's restless tell for the
> deep landing. There is no invisible "fall damage" quantity; the cost is the

## 4. The honest skill — the landing, not the falling

### 4.1 The corpus's movements are skill-buffed; falling is not

Every movement doc grants the player a buff-able craft: the walk's *where to step,
when to wait, how much to carry* ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §3 — all learned); the
road's *held line* and the station's re-raise ([`coherence-highway.md`](./coherence-highway.md)
§1.1/§3); the Bell's *metered collection* ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md)
§3.2). Practice makes each cheaper. **The Fall is the one movement with no
skill-buff:**

> **You cannot learn to fall well enough to make it cheap; you can only learn to
> not fall, and to choose your landings.** A practiced walker steps cheaply; a
> practiced faller does not land cheaply. The impact's shock is a function of the
> fall's length and the landing's surface — not of the faller's practice. What
> practice buys is the *avoidance* (reading the edge, holding the line, staying on
> the walk's gradient-favored path) and the *choice of landing* (§4.2). The
> no-skill-buff rule is the honest inverse of the walk's buff: the gradient's favor
> is a resource the walk *uses*; in a fall the same favor is an owner the player
> can only dodge or land around. **This is the corpus's honesty about gravity** —
> the one act the field cannot be asked to soften.

### 4.2 The fall's craft is the landing's shaping

Because the falling cannot be made cheap, the only craft a fall rewards is *the
landing*. The field's soft surfaces are the honest skill-buff — the designed
counter-move to the no-skill-buff rule:

> **The fall's craft is the landing's shaping — a deliberate fall into a held
> basin, a soft margin, a sea.** The honest skill is not resisting the gradient
> (there is no counter-term a player owns in free fall) but *choosing where the
> gradient ends*. A deliberate fall aimed at a **held basin** lands in a
> `q`-raised pocket whose coherence absorbs the impact — the bath's regional
> multiplier, [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §4.4, softening the
> landing as it softens any machine's throughput. A fall into a **soft margin** — a
> thin, decohered edge that costs `(1−q)` to cross but *cushions* the arrival (the
> field's waste, spent at the landing instead of in flight) — lands on the field's
> patience after all, at the hazard of the margin's own read
> ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2b). And a fall into the **sea** is the honest
> soft surface: the flat coherence plain that carries, the swim's beginning
> ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b/§4 — the fall into the sea *is* the swim's
> beginning, the water's carrying surface shaped as a landing).

**The field's soft surfaces are the honest skill-buff.** Where the walk's craft is
*learned movement* and the Bell's is *learned hold*, the fall's craft is *learned
placement*: the practiced faller does not land more softly by falling better; they
land more softly by *falling into the right surface*. The sea's flat plain catches
([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b); the held basin's `q` absorbs; the soft margin's
waste cushions. **[design], probe-calibrated** — *which* surfaces read as soft
landings (the sea's catch, the basin's absorption, the margin's cushion), and how
much each blunts the impact's `ε²` spike, is this doc's designed surface over the
sea's landed catch, the bath's regional multiplier, and the walk's `(1−q)` margin,
calibrated by the Phase-1 probe (§5b). None invents a surface; all compose landed
pieces.

### 4.3 The honest contrast — the Bell is the controlled fall; the free fall is the Bell with the craft removed

The Bell descends by *holding a refused shell* — the φ-detuned boundary with `M ≈
0` converts incoming disorder to heat at the boundary, so the Bell sinks through
the field's indifference ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §3.1/§2.1).
It is the craft-scale *controlled* descent: the operator meters the collection, the
shell holds the blast, the hold is a net-negative, spent coherence
([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §2.2). **The Fall is the exact
inverse — the Bell with the craft removed:**

| | The Bell (controlled descent) | The Fall (free act) |

| The device | a held shell pointed down (`deep-field-diving.md` §3.1) | a bare body, no shell, no hold |
| What it does | **holds** a φ-detuned shell to sink *through* the gradient (`M ≈ 0` refusal) | **obeys** ∇(g·Φ) bare, owns the gradient with no refusal |
| The descent | controlled by a coherence budget (the operator's `B`) | free — the patient field's un-spent loan, the no-craft | 
| The cost | the shell's standing `(1−q)` draw at depth | the landing's `ε²` spike (no mid-air softening) |
| The blast-risk | contained at the shell | the very-hard-landing's kept scar, held by the field |
| Player skill | metered collection — a learned craft, skill-buffed | **no skill-buff** — you cannot learn to fall cheaply |

The honest contrast, stated once: **the Bell is the descent *with* the craft — a
controlled, held, net-negative, skill-buffed fall; a free fall is the Bell with the
craft removed — the same ∇(g·Φ), owned bare, no shell, no hold, no metering, and
hence the one movement the corpus's skill-buffs do not reach.**
