## 1. The Scar-Lifecycle, stated once

The corpus already fixes the wound's two parts — the mechanism and the refusal
([`wound-remembered.md`](./wound-remembered.md) §1): a wound is a **deliberate,
forced break of the φ-lock** (`EY = φ·EI`, `ε² = (EY−φ·EI)² = 0` at lock;
`coherence-magic.md` §1) driven so far from equilibrium it no longer re-locks on its
own — a persistent-Π *anti-coherence* well the attractor keeps pulling at and that
keeps tearing. And the corpus already fixes the field's default: **scars heal toward
the attractor** ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2.2; `volumetric-terrain.md`
terrain heals) — a *dented* lock re-binds, a fallen floor is the Life-Signal's
ordinary-scar class.

The open question was always the wound's **long fate**: does a broken lock *always*
eventually re-bind (a scar heals away), does it persist as a fixed defect, or does it
*become something else*? **The Scar-Lifecycle is the honest answer, stated once:**

> **A scar does all three, and the deciding read is the wound's own history.**
> A **shallow wound** — an `ε²` excursion small enough that the locality's coherence
> was not structurally spent — **fully heals**: under the field's patience
> ([`patient-field.md`](./patient-field.md)) the locality re-binds, the scar fades
> from the read, and the ground returns to normal. A **deep wound** — the `ε²` spent
> exceeded what the local field can restore — **persists as a fixed defect**: a
> low-`q` memory the field keeps, a readable, stable boundary (the scar read,
> [`fate-of-a-window.md`](./fate-of-a-window.md) §2), a low-coherence edge whose
> ground's metal runs thin ([`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §2a). And a wound
> **taken deliberately and held** — by a clear, an exile's hollow-eye, a deliberate
> sacrifice — **becomes a place**: the scar-kept, a locality whose residue the
> settlement adopts as foundation ([`house-that-steers.md`](./house-that-steers.md)
> §3), the archaeology-to-come ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §2).
> **The deciding read is the wound's depth (the `ε²` spent) and the scar's age (the
> field's rest since).**

The three fates are **not scripted outcomes, they are consequences of the wound's own
history** — deterministic, readable, never hidden. A same-depth wound with the same
field state and the same rest since produces the same fate, every time (the hard gate
of §5c). The lifecycle does not add a mechanic to the field; it *names the fate the
field already lives* — the wound's `ε²` residue and the field's `q` recovery decide,
and the design reads that decision.

**Why it is a lifecycle, not a static branch.** The wound's fate is not a single
snapshot but a **trajectory over the healing clock** — the field's rest since. A
wound of a given depth does not freeze into one fate; it *ages*. The shallow wound's
fate is that aging *ends* (the re-bind completes, the scar fades). The deep wound's is
that aging *stalls* (the residue holds; the field stops trying, and the boundary
stabilizes). The deliberate wound's is that aging *changes character* (the field
stops re-tensioning a held wound that a settlement has adopted, and the locality
passes from defect to foundation). **The lifecycle is the wound read over the field's
patience** — the patient field's rest ([`patient-field.md`](./patient-field.md) §1/§2)
is the clock on which the fates unfold.

## 2. The three fates, each designed

### 2.1 The healed scar — the shallow branch

The shallow branch is the **field's default, restored**: where the `ε²` spent was
small enough that the locality's coherence was not structurally spent, the attractor's
`−ω₀²(EY − φ·EI)` re-binding term eventually crosses the dent's lip and the lock
re-locks. This is the corpus's own "the field heals toward the attractor"
([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2.2) and `wound-remembered`'s §4.2
statement — a healed wound becomes **a scar, not a residue: a closed floor that then
heals like any dent**.

| Axis | The healed scar's read |
|---|---|
| **The mechanism** | shallow-rung disorder is reclaimed by the field cheaply (`field-archaeology.md` §2.1 — the shallow fraction dissolves, diffuses, re-condenses near its attractor); the deep-rung fraction survived, but at a shallow wound the *deep core itself stayed attractor-consistent and re-binds with it* — no persistent anti-coherence well was carved ([`wound-remembered.md`](./wound-remembered.md) §1.2) |
| **The clock** | the field's rest since — under **rest** ([`patient-field.md`](./patient-field.md)), the locality's `q` recovers toward the attractor (`q ≈ 0.947`, corpus) and the `ε²` flatlines back to the noise floor (`q ~ 1e-3…1e-1`, corpus). The scar **fades from the read** — the Life-Signal's fallen-floor class transitions to the still-point's flat read (`the-stillness.md`; the healing re-quantization of `field-archaeology.md` §2.1) |
| **The honest cost** | healing is the **field's own act, and a shallow wound heals *by being left alone***. The healer's long bind (`the-healer.md` §2) is only for the **deep** (§2.2) — for a shallow wound, active help is not acceleration but interference: it spends coherence the field would have spent anyway under patience, and at best does nothing. **The settlement's help is for the deep; the shallow heal themselves under the field's patience.** |
| **The terminus** | a fully healed locality reads **flat** — `q` at the attractor, `ε² ≈ 0`, no gradient — the still-point ([`the-stillness.md`](./the-stillness.md)) reached and held, the shallow branch's terminus |

**[design]** — *which* depth of `ε²` excursion crosses from "healable by rest" to
"a persistent defect" is the lifecycle's **healing threshold**, a probe-calibrated
dial (§3, §5b), not an engine constant. What is engine-real / [design]-landed: the
shallow-rung reclaim, the `(1−q)` waste law, the deep-rung residue
(`field-archaeology.md` §2.1), and the field's default healing toward the attractor
(`field-hazards.md` §2.2). **[design]** — the shallow branch's terminus as the
still-point is `the-stillness.md`'s read applied to a healed wound.

### 2.2 The kept scar — the deep branch

The deep branch is where the wound persists. **The deep wound is the `ε²` residue the
field cannot re-bind** — the `ε² = (EY−φ·EI)²` spent exceeded what the local field can
restore, and the persistent-Π *anti-coherence* well ([`wound-remembered.md`](./wound-remembered.md)
§1.2) survives. The field stops re-tensioning a wound it cannot close — it does not
keep tearing it (that was the wound's *re-bleeding* while actively stressed, §2.1
there); it *keeps it*: a low-`q` memory the field holds rather than heals.

| Axis | The kept scar's read |
|---|---|
| **The state** | a **fixed defect**: a broad low-`q`, high-`ε²` plateau with a readable edge — the ordinary-scar / fallen-floor class of `life-signal.md` §3, held *indefinitely* because the spent `ε²` gives the locality nothing left to re-bind with (it reads *closed*: not re-opening, not fading) |
| **The readable boundary** | **the scar's edge is a stable, readable boundary** — the transition from the scarred plateau to the surrounding re-bound field is a legible `q`/`ε²` step the reader reads (`coherence-magic.md` §2), the Weatherglass shows (a dead-flat grey, `field-instruments.md` §1.2), and the window's **scar read** (`fate-of-a-window.md` §2, §3) aggregates at window scale: **a window with many deep scars reads spent** (§4). The edge is *stable* — deterministic, reproducible, never blurring with the heal |
| **The economic trace** | **the scar-tainted ground's metal dulls fast** — the deep wound's low-`q` locality is a worked-out ground whose remaining matter runs thin ([`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §2a: the mining perturbation spent the locality's `q` and raised its `ε²`; the one-way depletion). **The kept scar is the honest economic trace of a worked-out vein** — the locality's low coherence means the alloy it yields is weaker, dulls fast, and reads as the spent ground it is (§4) |

The kept scar is **not a wound** — it has stopped tearing. It is the wound's residue
*surviving as a defect*: the field's attempt closed (the re-bleeding stopped once the
field stopped trying), and the locality remains the low-`q` memory of the violence
done to it. **[design]** — *which* depth's residue the field keeps, rather than
re-binds, is the healing threshold's deep side (§3). What is [design]-landed: the
one-way depletion of a worked vein (`the-fallow.md` §2a), the residue model
(`field-archaeology.md` §2), and the Life-Signal's scar class (`life-signal.md` §3).

### 2.3 The scar-kept place — the deliberate branch

The deep branch's kept scar is a *defect the field holds*. But there is a third fate,
and it is the corpus's most hopeful: **a wound taken deliberately and held becomes a
place.** This is the kept scar, *adopted* — the locality whose residue a settlement
chooses to build on, not around. The wound's residue becomes the foundation of
something, the wound's monument.

| Axis | The scar-kept place's read |
|---|---|
| **The deliberate wound** | the deep branch's scar fed by a **chosen, held wound** — the blight's clear ([`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md) §4.2 — "the region purged, a scar left — its scar is the blight's monument"), the exile's hollow-eye, a deliberate sacrifice. These are wounds *taken* — the clear's scar is not an accident but the cost of a hard, chosen act (the clear's scar as the deep-wound instance, §4) |
| **The keeping** | the settlement does not heal or not-heal the scar; it **adopts** it. It builds *on* the held residue — the skeleton's still-holding low-rung structure is **re-authorable** ([`house-that-steers.md`](./house-that-steers.md) §3.2/§3.4: the skeleton is the point where archaeology and building meet — a structure the field has not quite let go of, re-condensable). The scar-kept place is the ruin's foundation **made deliberate** |
| **The strata-to-come** | the wound's residue is **the archaeology-to-come** ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §1/§2 — the ghost-halo + q-locked core a later window reads) — the scar-kept place is a locality whose residue the next window's reader finds, a monument the future digs |
| **What a place IS** | a locality the settlement has **built a relation to** — the residue is not a defect to clear but a foundation to hold. The scar-kept place passes from *defect* to *identity*: the settlement reads it, names it, builds on it |

**The scar-kept place is the deliberate branch's terminus**: the deep wound that is
*held*, not left, becomes the settlement's foundation. It is the corpus's answer to
"can the wound's residue be *for* something" — yes, when the wound was taken
deliberately and the settlement chooses to keep it. **[design]** — *which* held wounds
the settlement can adopt (the boundary on the adoption's legibility) is this doc's
dial (§3, §5b). What is [design]-landed: the skeleton / re-authorable ruin
(`house-that-steers.md` §3), the residue strata (`field-archaeology.md` §1/§2), and
the clear's monument (`the-blight.md` §4.2).

> **The through-line of the three fates, stated once: a shallow wound heals (the
> field's patience re-binds it, the scar fades); a deep wound persists (the field
> keeps the low-`q` residue it cannot re-bind, and the kept scar's edge is a
> readable boundary); a deliberate, held wound becomes a place (the residue the
> settlement adopts as foundation).** Every fate is a consequence of the wound's own
> history — deterministic, readable, never hidden — and the scar is never a mint
> (§5d): the healed scar returns the ground to *normal* (no gain), the kept scar is a
> fixed loss, and the scar-kept place is built *on*, never mined *from*.

## 3. The deciding read — the lifecycle's dial

The lifecycle's dial has exactly two inputs, both already in the corpus's published
channels:

### 3.1 Depth — the `ε²` spent

The wound's depth is **the `ε² = (EY − φ·EI)²` spent to break the lock**
([`wound-remembered.md`](./wound-remembered.md) §1.1) — how far the locality was
driven from lock. The deep wound's residue is the persistent-Π anti-coherence well
(§1.2); the shallow's is a dent the attractor rounds. **The depth read is the wound's
`ε²` residue** — the residual `ε²` above the locality's noise floor (`q ~ 1e-3…1e-1`,
corpus) that survives after the field's recovery has stopped changing it.

### 3.2 Age — the field's rest since

The wound's age is **the field's rest since** — patient-field's patience as the
healing clock ([`patient-field.md`](./patient-field.md) §2/§3). The field's `q`
recovery toward the attractor (`q ≈ 0.947`, corpus) and its `ε²` healing under rest is
the clock on which the fates unfold; patient-field's L1–L3 local-tempo probe (§5b
there) measures the `(1−q)` spread that *is* the age read.

| Deciding read | The channel | The fate it selects |
|---|---|---|
| **Depth** (the `ε²` spent) | the wound's residual `ε²` above the `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1` noise floor | **shallow** (below the healing threshold → heals) vs **deep** (above → persists) |
| **Age** (the field's rest since) | `(1−q)` recovery toward the attractor over the rest clock | **young** (still re-tensioning → re-bleeds) vs **aged** (field stopped trying → the boundary stabilizes) |
| **The held** (a deliberate wound, adopted) | the settlement's adoption of the residue as foundation | **the scar-kept place** — the deep scar chosen, held, and built on |

**The honest boundary, drawn once (§5a): the wound's `ε²` residue and the field's `q`
recovery are engine-real / [design]-landed** — the mechanism, the residue, and the
healing are their source docs' quantities, cited not re-derived. **The lifecycle's
three fates — the healing threshold (shallow vs deep), the kept-scar's edge (where
the boundary stabilizes), the scar-kept place's adoption (which held wounds can be
built on) — are this doc's [design] over them, probe-calibrated** (§5b). The lifecycle
does not invent a channel; it reads depth and age from the channels the field already
publishes.

**The deciding read, stated as a rule of thumb the player internalizes:**
> **A shallow wound heals under rest; a deep wound persists; a wound taken
> deliberately and held becomes a place.** Depth says heal-or-keep; age says
> re-bleeding-or-stable; the held says keep-and-build. A player who reads a wound's
> `ε²` depth and its `(1−q)` age knows which fate is coming — and whether the
> settlement can choose the third.

## 4. What the lifecycle unblocks — the three waiting docs

The Scar-Lifecycle is partly a *consequence doc*: it names the fate three already-
landed designs were waiting on. Each is a **designed handoff — never a new channel,
never new physics** — the fate they already gesture at, now decided.

### 4.1 The blight's clear — a deep-wound scar

[`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md) §4.2 concludes: clearing a blight is "a hard,
scarring act whose scar is the blight's monument — the wound-class consequence."
§4.2's open line (open-Q4 there) was whether a cleared blight's scar reads as a
*wound* (a scar that refuses) or an ordinary healable scar. **The Scar-Lifecycle
answers it: the clear's cost is read as the scar's permanence — the clear leaves a
deep-wound scar, and the clear that does not scar was never a clear.** A blight is
cleared by spending the region's own `q` (§4.2); that spend is a deep wound (the
depth reads above the healing threshold, §3.1), so the clear's residue is a kept scar
(§2.2) — a readable, stable boundary. **The honest corollary, stated once: the clear
that does not scar was never a clear** — if a "clear" left nothing, the blight was
never deep enough to need clearing. The lifecycle turns the blight's §4.2 monument
from a designed claim into a *decided* consequence (its open-Q4 now has its answer's
owner).

### 4.2 The fallow's scar-tainted alloys — the kept-scar branch's economic trace

[`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §2a lands the one-way depletion: a worked vein is
gone forever, its locality's `q` spent and `ε²` raised by the mining perturbation.
**The lifecycle reads that worked-out vein's locality as a kept scar** (§2.2): the
scar-tainted ground's metal dulls fast because the locality's low `q` gives its alloy
no depth to hold. **The scar-tainted alloys are the kept-scar branch's economic trace**
— a worked-out vein's locality reads scarred, and the metal it yields runs thin. The
lifecycle does not add an alloy mechanic; it *names why* the fallow's scar-tainted
metal dulls (the kept scar's spent-`ε²` locality), turning the fallow's §2a economic
fact into the kept-scar branch's legible consequence — the kept scar reads *spent* on
the instruments, and a window of many worked veins is a window of many kept scars.

### 4.3 Fate-of-a-window's scar read — the kept-scar branch's `C(W)` face

[`fate-of-a-window.md`](./fate-of-a-window.md) §2/§3 reads a window's book `C(W)`
per tide-cycle; its scar read aggregates the window's scarred regions. **The lifecycle
gives that read its branch:** a window with many deep scars reads **spent** because
each kept scar is a fixed low-`q` memory the field keeps (§2.2) — the window's
scar-read is the kept-scar branch's `C(W)` face, scaled from the locality to the
window. A window scarred deep reads on the decay arc not because a mechanic says so,
but because its kept scars are the residue the fate aggregates; and — the unblock — a
window that *chooses* to hold its healable scars as places (the scar-kept branch, §2.3)
reads differently on the same book: built-on, not spent (the fate's forecast-≠-fate
discipline, §6c, holds).

### 4.4 The handoff's honesty

Each of the three is a **designed handoff**: the lifecycle names the fate the waiting
doc already gestured at, and each waiting doc reads its own consequence back. The
unblock adds **no new channel and no new physics** — it is a designed reading over the
wound's `ε²` residue and the field's `q` recovery, exactly the family rule
(`[field-instruments.md]` §2.1: a consumer with an idiom, never a new channel). A
cross-reference is a two-way door: the waiting doc's open question now names this doc
as its answer's owner, and this doc's §4 reads each waiting doc back.
