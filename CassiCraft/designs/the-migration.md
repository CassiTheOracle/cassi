## 2. The many-scales act — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 A moving high-`q` knot of many bodies — the herd-as-chord at people-scale

The migration is a *moving coherence structure of many bodies*, and the field
already names its mechanics. The ecology's flocking/schooling behavior
([`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md) §5.3/§6c) steers the
assembled crowd by the river law (`a = −G_N·(π/ρ)·∇(g·Φ)`,
[`chunk-field-quantization.md`](./chunk-field-quantization.md) §2.1, the ≈ 40 ns/
entity steer, `corpus-reconciliation.md`) — the herds, schools, and flocks that
form when many organisms ride the same gradient toward a shared high-`q` band.
The field-music doc names the sonic side of the same fact: each organism hums at
its rung-depth, and **a herd is a chord** — creatures near each other stack
φ-intervals, the biosphere's hum being the field's living organization made
audible ([`field-music.md`](./field-music.md) §2.4).

The Migration scales that chord to a people:

> **A migrating people is the herd-as-chord at people-scale** — a moving high-
> `q` knot of many bodies, each a coherence store at its rung, all riding the
> same river-law gradient as one assembled band. The collective shares a phase-
> lock: the many move together, at one pace, as a **working** in the field-npc-ai
> sense — a phase-locked group IS a working, `M ≈ 1`
> ([`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §3.2, cited via the corpus). That
> shared phase-lock is the migration's coherence — the thing that makes the many
> *one* moving structure rather than a crowd of independent walkers.

**[design]** The *migration's form* — how wide a band the moving many hold, how
tightly the phase-lock binds them as one high-`q` knot, at what pace (relative to
the stride-cost of [`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2, scaled to the band) — is
this doc's dial, probe-calibrated against the ecology's flocking (§5.3/§6c) and
the ≈ 2,000-entity cap that bounds any crowd ([`chunk-field-quantization.md`](./chunk-field-quantization.md)
§4, `corpus-reconciliation.md`). The *direction* — that a moving collectivity
forms a phase-locked band riding the river gradient — is the flocking/schooling
field-mechanics, engine-real/[design]-landed.

## 3. The long thin order-band — the load-bearing design

The migration's load-bearing design is its **form**: a migrating people is not a
sprawling crowd but a **long thin order-band** — a high-`q` filament of many
bodies strung along the direction of travel, the way the road's held line is a
high-`q` filament along a route ([`coherence-highway.md`](./coherence-highway.md)
§1). And that long thin band, in motion, does two honest things to the field it
crosses — both grounded in the corpus's own depletion-and-return vocabulary.

### 3.1 The strip — the tracks a migration leaves (a fallow behind it)

A migration strips the strip it crosses. [`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §2
designs the one-way run-out — a worked deposit is gone forever, spent seed-orders
are un-re-precipitable, the pulled deep is exhausted — and its through-line is
the honest bound of husbandry: *you can steward a scarcity, you cannot un-mine an
ore* ([`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §1). **The migration's passage is that
one-way run-out at the band's scale:**

> **A migration leaves a fallow behind it.** The long thin band's passage
> depletes the strip it crosses: the moving many draw the strip's `q` as they
> pass (each body a coherence store pulling local order), shed `ε²` into the
> track (each body's wake, [`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2c at band scale), and
> pass on — leaving the crossed strip's `q` spent and its `ε²` raised, a **one-
> way depletion the field does not instantly re-organize**. The strip's fallow is
> the ecological truth of the exodus: what the many passed through is, for a
> season, spent.

**[design]** The *strip's* depletion — how wide the band's spending reach, how
deep the `q` draw per body, how long the raised `ε²` lingers before the field
heals — is this doc's [design] over the fallow's real one-way depletions
([`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §2) and the walk's wake
([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2c), probe-calibrated. What is *landed* is that
a worked strip depletes one-way (the fallow's hardest law) and that a body's
passage sheds `ε²` (the walk's wake); that a *many's* passage aggregates that
depletion into a fallow-band is the Migration's composition.

### 3.2 The re-turn — re-fertilized the way a compost does

A fallow-strip is not the end of the story. [`the-compost.md`](./the-compost.md)
§4 designs the deliberate re-turn of spent order — a designed heap where a
settlement turns its spent matter back into the field as a slow-release feed for
a depleted plot, *at a loss* (`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`,
[`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §6 — giving back, never a mint).
**The migration's stripped strip re-fertilizes the way a compost does:**

> **The spent strip's matter is turned back at a loss over the long horizon.**
> The fallow-band a migration leaves is not a permanent death — the field's own
> conservation ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §3, the field re-
> precipitates) and the spent matter's residue are re-turned into the strip, at a
> loss, the way a compost re-turns a depleted plot. The crossed strip recovers
> toward what the field can re-hold — never the migration's spent state restored
> to what it was (the fallow's one-wayness holds), but the residue deliberately
> turned back, the way the compost re-turns the spent.

**[design]** The *re-turn's* form — that the fallow-band's residue re-fertilizes
the strip over the long horizon — is this doc's [design] over the compost's
landed re-turn mechanics ([`the-compost.md`](./the-compost.md) §4) and the
fallow's one-way depletions ([`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §2), probe-
calibrated. The one-wayness of the *worked* strip (the fallow's hardest rule) is
never violated; the re-turn is the residue's loss, never the spent state's
recovery.

### 3.3 The honest frame — the field's own order, in motion

The through-line that makes the long thin order-band the Migration's load-bearing
