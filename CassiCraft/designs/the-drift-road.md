## 3. The re-anchored stack: the drift-branch's honest re-read

This §3 is the heart of the drift branch — how each time doc re-anchors under the monotone-unfolding attractor. Each re-anchor reads the source doc's own quantity (the states, the observances, the arcs) under the drift verdict, never changing it. **[design]** — every re-anchor is this doc's designed reading; the source quantities are unchanged.

### 3.1 The window-year's beat becomes the one-way healing's pace

Under a real season, the Window's Year's beat is a cycle: "work at harvest / rest at thin" repeats (`the-window-year.md` §2). Under drift, the beat does **not** repeat — it is the **pace of the one-way healing**:

> **The beat is the healing's cadence, not a rhythm.** The window's regional `q` rises monotonically toward the attractor; the 'work' is done along that single climb (ore precipitating where `q` accumulates, the bath forming, the basins strengthening — `fate-of-a-window.md` §1.1's settle read), and the 'rest' is the passing of the climb's slower stretches. There is no 'next harvest' to work toward again — the work-window is the organizing arc itself, and the act is **done once, at the fullest point, then the field is organized.**

The calendar's four observances re-anchor (each **[design]** over the source's own act):
- **The festival's day** becomes a **once celebration** — the single bright act datable only at the organizing's fullest point (§3.4).
- **The thin-year observances** become the **pre-organized field's approach** — the dates the field spends *most* of its organizing arc de-organized, read as the honest approach, not a recurring winter (§3.2's cold re-read).
- **The anniversaries** — already the departed's quiet dates (`the-window-year.md` §3.3) — re-anchor *stronger* under drift: a one-way world has a **single pass of the departed's days**, not a repeating memorial cycle; the re-memory is once, and carries more.
- **The steward's cycle** re-anchors to the **single organizing arc's close** — the tide-account close happens once, at the still-point, not each tide (§3.5).

### 3.2 The cold's decade is not a season but the pre-organized field's approach

The Cold is the long-thin as a lived season (`the-cold.md` §1). Under drift, the long-thin is **not** a winter that recurs — it is the pre-organized field itself:

> **A drift-window's 'cold' is the pre-organized field.** The field, before it organizes, spends a long stretch de-organized (`q` toward the floor, `ε²` elevated, the vents backed up, the harvest meager — `the-cold.md` §2). That stretch is not a season the field falls into and rises out of; it is the **starting condition the monotone healing rises out of, once.** The husbandry a settlement does in that stretch — hold the bath, stretch the pantry, fall the un-feedable farm, decide whether to leave (`the-cold.md` §3) — is the **husbandry of the single approach**, spent once, toward a single end: the organized field.

This changes the husbandry's meaning: the cold's decisions are not "endure the winter, wait for spring" (a cycle); they are **"endure the approach, husband the field through its one organizing"** (a process). A settlement's survivors are not the ones who outlast a cycle; they are the ones who **steward the field through its single climb** — the honest, costed husbandry of a one-way world. **[design]** — the cold's decade is the source's [design]-landed aggregation (`the-cold.md` §4); this doc re-reads its *meaning* under drift, not its mechanics.

### 3.3 The fate's arcs read under the monotone-unfolding attractor — the still-point

The Fate's four arcs (`fate-of-a-window.md` §1) are read forward per tide-cycle. Under drift, there is **no tide-cycle to read the arc per** — the arcs re-anchor to the single organizing trajectory:

- **Settle** is the *one* organizing phase — the field's regional `q` climbing toward the attractor, the bath forming, the coherence accumulating (`fate-of-a-window.md` §1.1 — the settle read under drift is the whole climb, not a recurring beginning).
- **Thrive** is the organized hold — `q` at the attractor, `ε²` suppressed, machines near-lossless. Under drift this is **not** a maintained state the settlement keeps feeding around a cycle; it is the **organized plateau at the top of the climb** (`fate-of-a-window.md` §1.2, held at the drift's end).
- **Decay** is the risk that the organizing never fully binds — the field-poor stretch that never rises, or is drained during the climb (`fate-of-a-window.md` §1.3). Under drift it is the **failed (or abandoned) single climb**, not a recurring decline.
- **Hunt** — unchanged in kind, gated on the Coda's formation (`fate-of-a-window.md` §6e), read under drift as a threat on the single arc.

> **The fate's end under drift is the still-point.** The monotone-unfolding attractor's terminus — the field once fully organized and then at rest: `q` pinned at the attractor, `ε² ≈ 0`, `∇(g·Φ) ≈ 0`, no net flux. A drift-window's fate, if it survives and holds the organizing, **ends at the still** — the fate-of-a-window §1.1 settle arc's end-state that [`the-stillness.md`](./the-stillness.md) §5.1 names: "a fully settled window is a window *done* — the attractor's victory, not a holding action." The still-point is the fate's reachable end precisely *because* the drift is monotone: there is a single direction, and its done is a real, reachable place — not decay (the desert), but **completion**.

This is the load-bearing re-read: **under a season, the fate loops (thrive returns, decay recurs); under drift, the fate's arcs all terminate at the still-point.** The still is the corpus's already-designed terminus (`the-stillness.md` §1/§5); the Drift-Road's drift-branch is the *one branch that makes the still a reachable endpoint*, because a one-way world reaches the field's resolved end. **[design]** — the fate's own quantities are the source docs'; the *still as the drift's end* is the [design] reading over `the-stillness.md` §5.1's end-state and the drift verdict.

### 3.4 The festival becomes the *once* celebration

The Festival is the corpus's only joy, timed to the tide-high feast day (`the-festival.md` §3). Under drift, there is **no repeating harvest apex** — the `q` organizes once:

> **A drift-window's festival is a *once* celebration.** The settlement's one bright act, datable only at the single fullest point of the organizing arc — the moment the field's `q` reaches what will become the still's attractor hold, the bed at its one maximal harvested state, the names at their one re-binding. The once-celebration is the settlement's **one bright act before the field resolves** (`the-stillness.md` §5.2's 'live in the resolved' contains this: the celebration happens *at* the resolution's approach, then the still holds).

The honest cost holds unchanged (`the-festival.md` §2.3, §6d): the once-celebration is **spent, never free** — the members' collective ε² vent is the price, bounded by the no-free-energy cap. **[design]** — the festival's mechanics (`the-festival.md` §2) are unchanged; *that it is once, and datable at the single organizing apex* is this doc's drift-reading over the festival's calendar and the tide probe's drift verdict.

### 3.5 The farmer's husbandry re-anchored to monotone

The farm harvests at tide-high (`farm-that-feeds.md` §5). Under drift, there is **no 'next harvest'** to husband toward:

> **The monotone husbandry.** A drift-window's bed organizes once toward its fullest stored coherence — the crop's run deepens as `q` climbs (`farm-that-feeds.md` §2), the band is held through the single organizing arc, and the **one maximal harvest** is taken at the arc's fullest point. There is no re-seed-and-reap cycle; the fallow is not waiting for the next tide but **letting the bed rest toward the still** — the husbandry is **once, not a calendar**. The farmer's role re-anchors from *calendar-keeper* (harvest at the high, fallow at the trough) to *single-arc steward*: hold the bed through the one climb, take the one rich yield, then tend the still (the no-op "work" of `the-stillness.md` §3.2 — nothing to tend because nothing drifts).

The yield's honest cap holds (a farm cannot mint, `farm-that-feeds.md` §4/§7d; `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`): the single maximal harvest is bounded by the bed's `q`, spent honestly, never free. **[design]** — the farm's mechanics are unchanged; *that the harvest is once, not seasonal* is this doc's drift-reading over `farm-that-feeds.md` §5 and the drift verdict.
