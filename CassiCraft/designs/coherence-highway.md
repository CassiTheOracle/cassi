## 1. A road is a steered coherence route — stated once

The core claim, stated once and kept:

> **A road is a steered coherence route, not a paved strip.** It is the conduit
> idiom of [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §4.1 raised to
> infrastructure scale: a manufactured **high-`q` filament** that makes a path
> the field's *preferred channel* for routing coherence. The player does not lay
> a strip of blocks; they **route coherence along a line** — hold a line's `q`
> up (like any maintained lock, §4) so the field flows down it instead of
> spreading, and a cart on that line rides the field's own `∇(g·Φ)`** gradient
> downhill *for free* (the field does the hauling). A road is the connective
> tissue of a settlement made legible as *sustained coherence*: where it lives,
> the field holds; where it dies, the field lets go.

### 1.1 What a road IS to the field

The grounding, unblurred. A **conduit** (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.1) is *"a
manufactured high-`q` filament that raises `ξ·q` along a path, making that path
the field's preferred channel for routing coherence."* That conduit idiom,
raised to infrastructure scale, is the road: a maintained high-`q` line that a
cart rides along `∇(g·Φ)`.

**The conduit at nature's scale (reverse pointer, two-way).** The corpus's water
doc reads the same conduit idiom grown by the field itself: [`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md)
§2a designs **a river as a moving-conduit band** — a high-`q` filament carrying
*flow* the way a manufactured conduit carries *coherence*, but held by the liquid
regime rather than by a player (the river "is a corridor the field itself
maintains, the highway's §1.1 conduit where the field is the roadman"; `the-sea.md`
§2a cites this §1.1's conduit as its idiom). A river rides `∇(g·Φ)` at nature's
scale the way this road does at infrastructure scale; the two docs now cite each
other as read-and-answered. The chord coupling
`ξ = φ⁶ ≈ 17.94` amplifies toward matter; raising `q` along a line steers
`∇q` so coherence flows *down* the path instead of spreading. A **road is that
conduit at infrastructure scale** — a long, low, kept-high-`q` filament across
the land, exactly as the conduit is a wire-like line, but sized and
route-placed to carry *traffic* (carts, harvests, travellers) rather than only
withdrawn coherence.

### 1.2 The free haul is real, the route is designed

The key honesty, stated once:

> **The `∇(g·Φ)` haul a cart rides is engine-real — field-hydro, the primary
> reservoir of `energy-harnessing.md` §1.1.** A mass condenses, the field around
> it carries a potential gradient, and *letting something move down that
> gradient is the field doing work on it* — the exact work the river law does
> on particles. **What the road adds is *route*: it raises `q` along a *chosen

> road's located, maintained, gradient-facing line is the design.

This is `house-that-steers.md`'s core at line scale: a house is a shape that
steers the local field; a road is a *long* shape that steers a channel across

gradient to make a line the field carries things down. Both are **[design]
steering surfaces over engine-real channels** (§6a), both calibrated by probe,
and neither mints (`house-that-steers.md` §5d, §6d).

## 2. The road network as the window's vein-map

### 2.1 A settlement's connective tissue IS a map of its sustained coherence

A settlement is a **gradient-stealing structure** (`house-that-steers.md` §2):
a high-`q` core (the Qi bath, `energy-harnessing.md` §4.4), productive edges,
deliberate drains. A road is that structure *extended to the land* —

> **A road network is the living vein-map of the window.** The routes that
> survive are the ones the field prefers; the ones that die are the ones it
> stopped holding. A settlement's connective tissue — its road to the farm, its
> lane to the frame-dine, its path to the seam-anchor — is **a map of its
> sustained coherence**: read where the roads are strong and you read where the
> settlement holds the field up. The network is the window's *circulation*,
> made visible as kept-high-`q` lines.

This is the house doc's §2 built-form union (a house is at once a bath-node, a
Board contribution, a fate's driver, a Life-Signal subject) at the *route*
scale: **a maintained road is a bath-extension** (its high-`q` line feeds the
cluster's regional multiplier, `energy-harnessing.md` §4.4), **a Board
contribution** (its upkeep books as a contribution, `shared-ledger.md` §2 —
§5 this doc), **a Fate driver** (a settlement whose roads decay drives its fate
toward decay, `house-that-steers.md` §4 / `fate-of-a-window.md`), and a
**Life-Signal subject** (a maintained road is a maintained lock; a dead road is
a fallen floor — §4 this doc, `life-signal.md` §3).

### 2.2 Routes follow the field's own organization

The site map is the route planner's blueprint (`chunk-field-quantization.md`
§5):

> **Build where the field is organized.** The moving-Voronoi sites are "where
> the field is most organized" — 2·16³ = 8,192 sites, Lloyd-relaxed weighted on
> coherence `q`, so sites **cluster where the field is coherent**, and a chunk is
> *active* where a site's cell overlaps it. A road laid **along** the site
> map's organized ridges is nearly free to hold (it rides `q` the field already
> keeps up); a road laid **across** the grain fights the field (every meter a
> drain, a line the field keeps shedding).

The build-order honesty is the house doc's §2.1 applied to routes: **a road
network is either the field's articulator or its opponent.** Laid with the
site map, a line carries traffic on field-kept `q`; laid against it, the same
line is a standing maintenance cost that fights the gradient it was meant to
ride. The site map is the "where the field does something" read that tells the
player where a road helps and where it drains. (Same tension as
`energy-harnessing.md` §6's machine-in-a-low-`q`-region bleeding the `(1−q)`
waste, applied to a whole route.)

**The same ridges, ungated (reverse pointer, two-way).** This §2.2's site-map
ridges are the blueprint the corpus's pedestrian doc walks *ungated*:
[`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2a/§3 rides the same `∇(g·Φ)` and the same
site-map ridges at foot-pace, with **no filament and no maintenance** — "a step
with the lean is cheap (§2a), a step against it labors," where this doc's road
would hold a line (`the-walk.md` §2a — it cites this §2's gradient physics as the
haul it rides ungated). The road and the walk are the same field's two crossings —
held-and-boosted vs free-and-freedom — and the two docs now cite each other as
read-and-answered.

### 2.3 The network as the settlement's extension

Where a settlement's houses hold a `q`-core (the enclosed rooms of
`house-that-steers.md` §1.1), its **roads hold the *links*** — the lines that
connect the core to its productive edges and deliberate drains. The managed
gradient of the house doc's §2.2 (bath at the center, productive edges,
deliberate drains) *is* the shape of the road network: the roads stretch toward
the farm (a productive edge, `farm-that-feeds.md`), toward the seam-anchor (a
world-boundary edge, `world-seams.md`), and — for a well-laid settlement — its
waste is channeled *down* roads toward the deliberate drains rather than pooled
in the core. **A road is the settlement's gradient-stealing layout, drawn across
the land.**

### 3.2 The no-free-energy cap — a road cannot mint

The corpus's single hard rule (`energy-harnessing.md` §6: **no conversion yields
more than it sinks**, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`, coded as write-back amplitude caps)
applies to roads unchanged, and this doc states it as a hard design bound:

> **A road cannot mint. It routes what the field provides.** There is no
> free energy in a road: the free haul a cart rides *is* the field's own
> `∇(g·Φ)` (the primary reservoir, already capped by the economy's constraint
> stance), and **holding the line costs** — the road is a conduit, and a conduit
> "is itself a storage draw (it must hold `q` up to be a channel), so it leaks
> while idle and needs a trickle charge to stay live" (`energy-harnessing.md`
> §4.1). The station's re-raise is that trickle charge, fed; the `(1−q)` waste
> law is the road's machine-loss floor (`energy-harnessing.md` §2) — a road in a
> low-`q` region (a thin tide, a scar-adjacent stretch) bleeds its throughput to
> glow just like any machine.

Concretely, three caps a road obeys (§6d):

1. **A rail's maintained gradient routes the field's existing `∇(g·Φ)`** — it
   does not steepen it out of nothing; a rail placed where there is no gradient
   (flat, unordered land) has nothing to ride. The haul is bounded by the field.
2. **A road cannot store a harvest as wealth** — no "banked" line coherence fed
   by the ambient attractor drift (`energy-harnessing.md` §1.7's background pump
   is **forbidden to charge a capacitor**, and a road is a live draw, not a
   store).
3. **Holding the line is a cost, not a free win** — the station's trickle, the
   (1−q) waste, the maintenance writes. A road that "generates" travel is a lie
   the cap forbids; a road that *routes* the field's own haul is the honest act.

## 4. The road's life

### 4.1 Seasonal — a thin tide drains a line's `q`

A road is a **maintained high-`q` lock** (§1). Stop holding it, or let the field
thin around it, and the lock fails:

> **A thin tide drains a line's `q`.** At the tide's trough (`tide-of-the-attractor.md`
> §2 — `q` low, `ε²` high, wasteful), the road's maintenance cost rises exactly
> as the field's ability to hold it falls: the `(1−q)` waste law makes the same
> road bleed *more* to glow (`energy-harnessing.md` §2), the conduit's idle leak
> is less cheaply replaced (§4.1), and the whole route runs bright-and-wasteful
> against the `(1−q)` floor. **Roads die without maintenance** — a settlement
> that stops feeding its lines through a thin trough loses them, exactly as a
> Qi bath is "brittle if it goes down" (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4) and a farm
> starves unless stewarded through the thin (`farm-that-feeds.md` §5). The
> thin-tide trough is the railway's season to *protect*, not to *ride*.

The seasonal life is a **planning clock, not a wipe** (`tide-of-the-attractor.md`
§5c — a predictable trough is a planning aid, not a removal): a settlement
*protects* its key lines through the thin (a station's Still-Room re-raise,
§5's upkeep writes) the way a farmer husband's a bed's bath. The thin floor is
designed to sit above the desert's dissolution threshold (§6d) so a neglected
road reads as *wasteful*, not *deserted* — but a road that crosses into a
genuine desert-margin (a regional `q` collapse, `field-hazards.md` §3) sheds
its line and *is* gone.

### 4.2 The Coda eats infrastructure too

The signature-predator (`signature-predator.md`) preys on *signature* — the
clean, phase-stable organized-perturbation trail channeling leaves — in the
`ε²` margins. A road is a **long, maintained, high-`q` line**; through the desert
margins a road's maintenance writes are *exactly* the clean channeling the Coda
attracts (§2.2 of that doc — sustained clean writes maximize trail quality ×
duration × phase-stability):

> **A Coda can drain a line.** A road routed through or near an `ε²` margin —
> a thin-tide stretch, a scar-adjacent length, a desert-margin crossing — is a
> *standing clean trail*: the settlement that keeps feeding it through the
> margin keeps writing the organized-perturbation signature the Coda resonates
> with. The predator's accumulation `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability`
> (`signature-predator.md` §2.2) grows with the road's own sustained upkeep, so
> **a heavily-maintained line through an `ε²` margin becomes a drain *on* the
> settlement — the field's margin responds to its own kept traffic.** The
> station's patient re-raise and the line's high-`q` core are the Coda's
> *refuge* (a bath or high-`q` room is ground it cannot enter, §4.4 of that
> doc) — but only where the road actually holds `q` up; a thin-tide road that
> slips below the coherent floor loses that protection and the margin's
> resonance accumulates toward it.

The honest consequence: **a road does not merely die of neglect; it can be
*preyed on*.** Precisely the line a settlement pours maintenance into through a
thin/marginal stretch is the line most visible to the field's margin. This is
the `signature-predator.md`-cross-hazard claim at infrastructure scale — the
predator eats infrastructure too, and the counterplay is the same four a
traveller uses (route away from the margins, don't over-sustain a marginal line,
let a broken line's resonance decay rather than re-fed, hold a high-`q` refuge
at the line's stops) — none a spawn-kill, all costed, all honest.

### 4.3 A dead road re-seeds as a scar

When a road stops being maintained — neglect, a thin trough, a Coda drain — it
behaves like any un-maintained high-`q` structure (`house-that-steers.md` §3):
the `ε²` drain outruns the φ-lock, the shallow rungs dissolve, and the deep-rung
fraction is left as residue. **A road is a maintained lock; a dead road is a
fallen floor** (`life-signal.md` §3) —

> **A dead road re-seeds as a scar.** The kept line's `q` collapses toward the
> attractor, the `(1−q)` waste glows as its last death-signal (`field-archaeology.md`
> §2.1), and what is left is a **long, low, decohered footprint** — broad
> `ε²`, no high-`q` core, the ordinary-scar class of `life-signal.md` §3. It is
> not just a gone path; it is a **de-ordered line of land the settlement once
> held** — and because residue is stable only where `q` stays above its
> dissolution threshold (`field-archaeology.md` §6b), a dead road in a
> settlement that then declines can *keep* the ground de-ordered. A route that
> dies is a wound, not an erasure; re-laying a road across its scar is the
> house-doc's re-authoring act (re-condense the order, §3.4 there) applied to a
> line.

**The Life-Signal reads it:** a maintained road is the maintained-live class
(steady held `q`, `ε² ≤` floor); a Coda-drained stretch is the Coda's own
residue class (`signature-predator.md` §5); a fully-dead road is the
ordinary-scar class — a fallen floor the field has already let go of. Reading a
settlement's vein-map is reading its roads' Life-Signal states.

## 6. Honest gates

### (b) Gated on the Qi bath / conduit (Phase-1.5) — with a Phase-1 ride-downhill slice

The **full network** (roads as a settlement's vein-map, rail gradients, station

  road network feeds and rides; gated Phase-1.5.

  a road *is*; the routing/loss calibration (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.3) is a

  instrument-family pattern (a consumer of the publish with an idiom) is

- the **site map** (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §5) — the routing blueprint;
  the active-chunk scheduler is already Phase-1, but road *routing* off it is
  Phase-1.5.

So the **full architecture is MEDIUM** (§7). But there is a clean **Phase-1
slice**, and this design is deliberately built so the slice is the *honest core*:

> **Phase-1: a single route you ride downhill along `∇(g·Φ)`.** Take a path
> the field *already* prefers — a route from a coherent ridge (a settlement's
> site, `chunk-field-quantization.md` §5) down toward an organized sink or

> (the KSP-vehicle-as-rigid-body pattern, `ksp-kernel.md` §2.1, re-hosted on

The ride-downhill slice de-risks the entire vocabulary: if a cart measurably

**[design]** addition (a cart on a field-preference line) tested directly on
the publish, no new physics.

### (c) Determinism

The field is deterministic (one PDE; `life-signal.md` §6d). The road inherits it:

> **Same layout, same field state → same ride.** A cart on a route is a
> deterministic function of the route's published `∇(g·Φ)`/`π/ρ` and the
> vehicle's state — no hidden randomness, no player-relative variance. A player
> who re-rides the same route at the same field state gets the same descent, and
> a road's held-line effect is a deterministic function of the layout and the
> local channels. This is what makes the route *learnable* and the site map a
> *blueprint* rather than a guess (`house-that-steers.md` §5c).

### (d) The no-free-energy cap — never a mint

§3.2. A road **cannot mint** (`energy-harnessing.md` §6: **no conversion yields
more than it sinks**, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` amplitude caps): the free haul is the
field's own `∇(g·Φ)`, holding the line costs (maintenance, the (1−q) waste, the
station's trickle), and it cannot store a tide as wealth. **A road routes what
the field provides; it never generates it.** The cap is the reason the
thin-tide/Coda life (§4) is honest — a road that paid for itself forever would
be a mint, and the living field forbids it.

### (e) Accessibility — the route's health is readable

The route's health must be readable from the existing field-reading surfaces,
per `field-music.md` §6 (an enhancement, never the only channel) and the
instrument family rule (`field-instruments.md` §2.1):

- the **reader / Sense** (`coherence-magic.md` §2) renders a line's `q` and a
  dead road's scar — the route's held line is *visible in the field read*.
- the **Weatherglass / Still Room** (`field-instruments.md` §1.2/§2.2) shows a
  road *holding* (steady `q`, flat-low `ε²`) vs a draining/Coda-fed one
  (climbing `ε²`, the tell's "less dead" desert).
- the **Life-Signal** (`life-signal.md` §3/§4) reads a road as maintained-lock
  vs static-residue vs fallen-floor — a road *breathes* (its upkeep pulses);
  a dead one does not.

A player who never uses a "coherence-road" overlay loses nothing — the route's
health is the *same channels the reader/glass already show*, read at the line's
scale. The [design] road vocabulary is an *idiom over real channels*, never a
hidden mechanic.

## Open questions

1. **The ride-downhill calibration.** Does a cart *measurably* ride a real
   `∇(g·Φ)` descent on the Phase-1 living-terrain demo — strong enough to feel
   as a free downhill ride without the bath's regional multiplier, and
   distinguishable from flat terrain? The Phase-1 slice (§6b) is the
   pre-registered probe that answers it; a negative answer (the gradient too
   weak at landscape scale to carry a cart) would push the mechanic to pure
   site-map placement (build on the descent, don't steer one) without the held-
   line dials. **[probe]**
2. **Routing: with the site map vs against it.** The house-doc's build-order
   honesty at route scale: does a road laid along a coherent ridge hold `q`
   measurably better (and cost measurably less to maintain) than one laid
   across the grain? The routing calibration is a probe over the active-chunk /
   site map sampling (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §5). **[design]/[probe]**,
   Phase-1.5.
3. **The thin-trough floor vs the desert line.** A thin-tide road's floor must
   sit above the desert's dissolution threshold (read *wasteful*, never
   *deserted*) — the same line `tide-of-the-attractor.md` open-Q3 /
   `field-hazards.md` §6.2 set. Does a road crossing into a regional `q`
   collapse shed as a joint hazard (the road dies with the desert) or does its
   held line *delay* the desert along it? Inherently shared with the hazard
   doc's threshold. **[design]**, Phase-1.5.
4. **The Coda-drain line's attribution.** §4.2 makes a maintained marginal road
   a standing clean trail. Does the Coda's drain over a road read as *the
   settlement's* overdraw on the Board, or (honestly) as the margin's response
   to the settlement's own upkeep? The guard is the same live-vs-residue
   classifier (`field-archaeology.md` §7 open-Q3 / `life-signal.md` §3) the
   shared-ledger already uses for misattribution (§3.3 there) — the road must
   not book as a self-inflicted drain unless the upkeep truly over-fed the
   margin. **[design]**, inherits the Coda's gates (`signature-predator.md` §7).
5. **The road as a travel-mint risk.** A well-placed road on a steep field-
   organized descent could feel like free travel forever (ride down, path
   hoppers up). Does the (1−q) waste + the maintained-line cost of the return
   (or of holding the line at all) keep a road a *route*, never a *mint* — the
   §6d cap stated mechanically, not by tuning hope? The same mechanical guard
   `energy-harnessing.md` §6 demands. **[design]**
6. **Rail vs road — does the gradient-wear form earn its cost?** A rail's
   "Qi-line with a maintained gradient" (§1.1) sharpens the haul at a real
   holding cost. Is a rail ever worth more than a well-placed road on the
   field's own descent, or is the maintenance cost of keeping a *steepened*
   gradient a net loss the honest economy avoids? If rails never beat the field's
   own gradient, defer the rail to Phase-2 and ship the road first.
   **[design]**, Phase-1.5.
