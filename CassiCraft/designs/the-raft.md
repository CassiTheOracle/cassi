# The Raft: The Flat Freight — the River's Bulk-Carrier, the Water-Borne Barge That Rides the Current

**Question under design:** the corpus designs the *crossing* of a water too deep to
ford (the ferry — banked as a future doc), the *body in the water* (the swim,
[`the-swim.md`](./the-swim.md) — §2a the stroke, the `(1−q)` cost with/against the
current), the *road's freight* (the cart, [`the-cart.md`](./the-cart.md) — §2 the
wain's ride down `∇(g·Φ)`, §3 the load-and-gradient trade), and the *living line*
(the river, [`the-river.md`](./the-river.md) — §2 the current that carries a raft the
way the highway's gradient carries a cart). But **nothing carries bulk order down a
current** — no flat water-borne carrier that rides the river's flow to move a
granary's harvest or a quarry's cut downstream. The cart moves goods *along a road*;
the voyage-ship (world-seams §2) crosses the *between*; **nothing moves the
settlement's bulk *down the river*** — the granary's harvest and the quarry's cut,
loaded once and ridden on the field's own carry.

This document is that design: **the Raft is the flat freight — the river's
bulk-carrier, the water-borne barge that rides the current.** It is a flat,
un-powered water-borne carrier that lets the river's own `∇(g·Φ)` carry do the
hauling — the granary's harvest and the quarry's cut ride the current the way the
cart's freight rides the highway's gradient (`the-river.md` §2 — a raft rides the
flow the way a cart rides the road; `the-cart.md` §2 — the `∇(g·Φ)` descent a
wheel-carriage compounds, made the water's ride). It is the cart's **water-form**,
the current's **freight-lane** — honest to the same law as every vehicle: the ride
is the field's own descent, the load is real, and the raft **converts nothing**: the
current's carry is the field's own `∇(g·Φ)` (energy-harnessing §1.1), the freight is
the granary's and the quarry's real order — **never a current-mint, never a hidden
freight-lane**.

## 2. The current's freight — the honest mechanics

The Raft rides the river's carrying current: **the load rides the `∇(g·Φ)` descent
the cart compounds, at the water** (the-river §2 — the current that carries a raft;
the-cart §2 — the descent a wheel-carriage compounds, made fluid). Each half is
grounded in its source doc's real quantity; **the raft's form — its deck's bound, its
load's draft — is this doc's [design]** over the river's carry and the cart's
ride-and-trade, probe-calibrated.

### (a) The ride — a laden raft rides the current's own `∇(g·Φ)` descent

**The landed pieces.** [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §1.1 fixes
the physics every vehicle rides: a mass condenses, the field around it carries a
potential gradient, and *letting something move down that gradient is the field doing
work on it* — the exact work the river law does on particles, `a = −G_N·(π/ρ)·∇(g·Φ)`
(the per-entity steer ≈ 40 ns/entity, corpus-reconciliation). [`the-river.md`](./the-river.md)
§2 names that descent, at the water, as the **current that carries**: a softer, wetter
relative of the highway's gradient — **a raft rides the flow the way a cart rides the
road**. [`the-cart.md`](./the-cart.md) §2a makes that haul *compoundable*: a
wheel-carriage on a road compounds the descent's work into a carried load — the
gradient does, at the wheel, the work the walk spends its stride on.

**The [design] over them — the raft is that descent made the water's ride:**

> **A laden raft rides the current's own `∇(g·Φ)` descent.** The river's carrying
> current is the field's own descent toward whatever organizes the line's matter
> ([`the-river.md`](./the-river.md) §2, the moving-conduit band of the-sea §2a); a
> flat carrier set with the current lets the field do the moving — the same
> `∇(g·Φ)` haul the cart's wheel-carriage compounds (the-cart §2a), at the water,
> carrying the granary's harvest and the quarry's cut *downstream* with it. The raft
> **converts nothing** — it is un-powered; the current is the field's own spent; the
> ride is the free movement that is never free energy ([`the-swim.md`](./the-swim.md)
> §5d; energy-harnessing §6).

**[design]** — the *ride mapping*. The source docs land that `∇(g·Φ)` is a real,
free, engine-real haul and that a cart compounds it into a carried load on a road.
What is this doc's [design] is **that a flat water-borne carrier compounds that
descent into a carried bulk at the water** — that the current does the downstream
haul the way the road's gradient does the cart's, at the raft's draft rather than a
wheel. This is probe-calibrated, exactly as the cart's ride is (the-cart §5b, the
highway's ride-downhill probe coherence-highway §6b; the-river §2's current-carry
dead-ends on the same measurement — *does the `∇(g·Φ)` descent carry a body at a felt
pace?*): *how much* of the current a flat hull converts into carried downstream motion,
and how a laden raft's ride reads against an empty one's, are measured on the
current-carry probe, the river's linear-regime read's sibling. The descent is real;
the raft's compounding of it is [design], calibrated, never asserted as
engine-verbatim.

### (b) The lane — only worthwhile on the current's steered line

The raft is not a free mover; it is the cart's **load-and-gradient trade at the
water's register** (the-cart §3 held hard):

> **A raft is only worthwhile on the current's steered lane.** Where the cart is only
> worthwhile on a road ([`the-cart.md`](./the-cart.md) §3 — off the maintained line
> it is worse than a walk), the raft is only worthwhile on the river's carrying
> current: **on a live, coherent flowing band (the-river §2's moving-conduit line) a
> laden raft rides cheaply downstream; off it — on a still reach, a counter-eddy, a
> bend where the coherent filament has smeared to the sea's flat plain or the marsh's
> patchy texture — it is no better than poling, dragging a deck and a burden the
> swimmer could carry free.** The raft's cheap haul is a *relation to the current*,
> never a free route: the current's carry is the field's own, and the raft is only
> worthwhile where that carry is actually flowing.

**[design]** — the *lane's mapping*. The source docs land that the river holds a
flowing coherent line ([`the-river.md`](./the-river.md) §2) and that a cart is only
worthwhile on a road ([`the-cart.md`](./the-cart.md) §3). What is this doc's [design]
is **that the raft's worthwhile ride is bound to that line** — that a raft off the
current's coherent band reads as the cart off-road: the gradient's full tax with none
of the current's cheapening. This is probe-calibrated with the ride: *whether* a
coherent flowing band measurably carries a raft, and *whether* a still reach reads
as worse-than-poling, are the same current-carry probe that answers the river's
linear-regime read (the-river §6, open-Q2's band-width/current-strength dial). A
smeared current pushes the raft to a *presentation-only* lane (the ride honest at
the glide, never a mechanical free-ride) — the river's own honest fallback.

### (c) The return — the raft rides down on the current, and the return is not the current's

The cart's §2b honesty — **a loaded cart needs the road's maintained line to climb
back** — has a water-form:

> **The raft rides down on the current; it does not ride back.** The current's carry
> is directional — downstream, along the `∇(g·Φ)` descent (energy-harnessing §1.1,
> the descent points toward matter). **The raft's return upstream is work the field
> does not do** — it is poled, towed, or left, the way the cart's return rides the
> road's maintained line (the-cart §2b) rather than the gradient. The raft is a
> **downstream freight-lane**, deliberately: its value is that the current hauls the
> settlement's bulk once, and the cost of the return is real, never hidden in the
> cheap downstream ride.

**[design]** — the *one-way form*. The source docs land that the `∇(g·Φ)` haul is
directional (energy-harnessing §1.1 — the descent points toward matter; the river's
current flows downstream, the-river §2). What is this doc's [design] is **that the
raft's freight-route is the downstream one-way lane** — the settlement ships a

paid for (poled, towed, or simply not shipped — the bulk goes down once). This is the
bound that keeps the raft from reading as a free round-trip freight-mint: the cheap
carry is one-way, and the return is real.
