**Question under design:** the corpus designs the vertical's three outer faces — the
**zenith** ([`the-zenith.md`](./the-zenith.md) — the vertical's ceiling, where the sky ends),
the **bedrock** ([`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md) — the vertical's absolute floor, where
everything precipitates onto), and the **cave** ([`the-cave.md`](./the-cave.md) — the

([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) — the liquid regime, the designed middle) and the
**sea-floor** as the plain's edge ([`the-sea-floor.md`](./the-sea-floor.md) — the first

and the **ecology** ([`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md) — the life

the dense, low-lit band beneath a canopy — is undesigned**: the shade-band where a tall
structure's own shadow is a real field state, where the roost hides, where the shade's

canopy's light, above the ground's floor — a low-lit, `q`-thinned, `ε²`-quiet zone whose
shade is its own regime: **the vertical's occupied shadow-zone**.

## 1. The Understory, stated once

The corpus designs the vertical's faces as *places with edges*. The zenith is the ceiling
([`the-zenith.md`](./the-zenith.md) §1 — the sky's edge, the top you read and cannot leave
through); the bedrock is the floor ([`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md) §1 — the base
everything precipitates onto, no op can move); the cave is the hollow
([`the-cave.md`](./the-cave.md) §1 — the deep's openness, a stratum holding nothing); the
sea is the liquid middle ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) — the plain you cross); the
sea-floor is the plain's edge ([`the-sea-floor.md`](./the-sea-floor.md) — the first
interface). **Each is a designed *state* of the vertical — a top, a bottom, a void, a
plain, a seam.** None designs the band a tall standing thing *occupies below its own
height*: the dense, low-lit layer beneath a canopy — the shade-band between the light
above and the ground below, where a tall growth's own shadow is a real field state.

The honest gap: wherever the vertical has *height*, tall growth — a tree, a standing
structure, a canopy the zenith's light falls on — casts a shadow, and that shadow is not
*absence* (the cave's void) and not *thickness* (the bedrock's density): it is a **thin
band the light did not reach** — the `q`-carry spent by the canopy's take above, the
low-lit regime that lives on the light the canopy did not take. The corpus has the light
(the zenith), the floor (the bedrock), the hollow (the cave), and the home (the roost) —
**but nothing designs the shade the height itself makes, the occupied middle where the
wild hides and the shade's own ecology lives.** The Understory is that band filled,
stated once and kept:

> **The Understory is the canopy floor — the vertical's forest middle, the shade-band
> between the zenith's height and the ground's footprint.** It is a **living band that
> exists where tall growth stands**: the vertical's middle slice — below the canopy's
> light (the zenith's height, the light's `q`-carry absorbed above), above the ground's
> floor (the bedrock's footprint, the understory is not the floor but the living band
> above it) — a **low-lit, `q`-thinned, `ε²`-quiet zone whose shade is its own regime**.
> It is the vertical's **occupied shadow-zone**: the shade a tall growth casts is a real
> field state, and that state is a place the field holds — the canopy's shadow is not an
> absence and not a density, it is a *thin the light was spent to make*, and on that
> thin's margins the field's own life lives.

**The composition, honestly scoped.** The light, the floor, the hollow, the home, and the
ecology rule are their source docs' landed pieces. **The understory's contribution — that
a tall growth's shade is a *band*, a bound, a regime with a form and a life and an honest
law — is this doc's [design]** over those landed pieces, probe-calibrated against the
vertical's tall-growth mechanics (the zenith's height being a real canopy the vertical
stack ends at) and the ecology's recognition (`§6b` — the shade's life is the field's
own recognized run, never a hidden biome). Where `the-cave.md` makes the deep's openness
a *hollow* and `the-roost.md` makes a being's habitat a *home*, **the Understory makes a
tall growth's shade a *floor* — the canopy floor, the occupied middle the shade itself
is.**

**Not a hidden third regime — the understory's fundamental honesty.** The cave is "not a
hidden third regime" ([`the-cave.md`](./the-cave.md) §1); the bedrock is "not a hidden
bottom regime" ([`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md) §1). The Understory inherits the same
neutrality: a shade-band is **not a new field state**, it is the vertical's own
`q`/`ε²`/`(1−q)` **read at the light's absence** — the canopy's `q`-take above (the
zenith's light spent, [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2), the 
understory's `q` thinned and its `ε²` quiet below (the desert's thin and the cold's quiet
