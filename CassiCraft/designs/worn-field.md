# The Worn Field: Clothing as a Field-State

**Question under design:** what it means to *dress* in the field. The corpus has
instruments you wear (the Weatherglass — `field-instruments.md`), the debt you
carry (the Burden — `the-burden.md`), and a place you enter to clear it (the Still
Room — `field-instruments.md` §2.2). But nothing designs the field as a **second
shell on the body** — a garment that *is* an authored-regime field-state, worn
rather than placed. This document is that shell:

> **A garment is a worn field-state.** It is the `(ξ, ω₀², θ_c, n)` regime tuple
> of `custom-blocks.md` §1 realized as *worn* matter rather than a placed block —
> an authored-regime shell on the body that changes **your own coupling** to the
> field. It is the passive, always-worn twin of the aimed instrument: you do not
> hold it, you are dressed in it; what you wear is a permanent bodily field-op, a
> second shell that is your daily relationship to the law.

The shell is distinct from the Burden (the debt you carry) and the Still Room (a
place you enter), and it is the one you **carry into both** (§3).

This is a **player-interior design**: the passive form of the corpus's active
player (a bounded EY injector + ε² vent + reader — `coherence-magic.md` §1). It
introduces no new physics; it is a designed coupling read/write over the existing
channels, probe-calibrated. The honest line is drawn in §5 and §6 and never
blurred.

## 1. A garment is a worn field-state, stated once

`custom-blocks.md` §1 collapses every material into the regime tuple
`(ξ, ω₀², θ_c, n)`: a material *is* a tuple plus a name, and every behavior —
fall, flow, burn, conduct, hardness, fuel — is a derived column of those numbers
under the law. The registry is the table of tuples the sampler can select from; a
block's "id" is a pointer to a tuple, and a phase change is a threshold crossing
in state within one tuple, never a NEW material.

**A garment extends that act from the ground to the body.** Where a placed block
holds a regime tuple at a location, a garment holds the same tuple **on the
wearer** — and because the player is already a body the field reads and steers,
the worn tuple changes *your own* coupling to the field rather than the field
around a place:

| Placed regime (custom-blocks) | Worn regime (this doc) |
|---|---|
| the `(ξ, ω₀², θ_c, n)` tuple realized as condensing matter at a `worldPos` | the same tuple realized as **worn matter on the body** |
| re-quantizes the local field around a place | re-shapes **the wearer's own body's contribution** to the local field |
| a static fixture the field self-organizes around | a **permanent bodily field-op** — the passive, always-worn twin of the aimed instrument |
| precipitates via a Q4 `{op:"precipitate", tuple, worldPos, …}` write | registers via the same Q4 write, on the body's worldPos, held by the domain's condensation scanner |

The engine-real grounding is exact, not metaphorical. The player is a bounded EY
injector + ε² vent + reader (`coherence-magic.md` §1): their body raises local ρ
and q when they channel, sheds ε² when they vent, reads the published channels at
their position. The worn tuple sits in that read/write path — it changes *how
much* of each the body does at the coupling the tuple encodes. What you wear is
therefore not armor with a stat block; it is **the law's own constants, worn** —
the same `ξ = φ⁶ ≈ 17.94`, `ω₀² = 20.0`, `θ_c = 0.5`, and rung `n` that govern a
regime, applied to the body that wears them.

> **The one-line claim.** You do not hold the field in a garment; you *are*,
> at the field's own constants, a different coupling to it — and the daily
> choice of which constants you wear is the game's permanent, always-on
> relationship to the law.

**The tuple, swung (reverse pointer, two-way).** This §1's worn-regime tuple has
an active twin: [`the-tool.md`](./the-tool.md) §3 realizes the same
`(ξ, ω₀², θ_c, n)` regime **as an alloy in the hand** — "a tool is the worn-field's
tuple realized as a deliberate bite-vs-scar choice you swing," where the garment
re-shapes the wearer's coupling passively, the tool re-shapes the hand's
*perturbation* actively (`the-tool.md` §3 — it cites this §1 as the tuple-and-
framing source). The garment is on you whether you act; the tool is taken up *for*
the act; both are the same held-regime principle applied to body vs hand. The two
docs now cite each other as read-and-answered.

Distinct from everything adjacent: the Weatherglass is an instrument **you wear**;
a garment is the field-state **you wear**. The Weatherglass reads the field; a
garment *is* a regime — it changes the read, the vent, and the injection, not just
their presentation. And it is the passive twin precisely because you do not aim it:
an instrument is a handle you take up for an act; a garment is on you whether or
not you act (§4 — the day's plan).

## 2. The garment vocabulary

Each designed shell is a **real coupling change** over engine-real channels,
flagged **[design]**, probe-calibrated, with an honest cost — **nothing free;
every shell trades** (the no-free-energy cap, §6d). The three shells below are
the designed vocabulary; the tuple is open enough that a cabinet of regimes
(§4) extends the family without new physics.

### 2.1 The EY-shell — the steadying shell

**The regime.** A mid-`ξ`, high-`ω₀²`, low-`θ_c` tuple worn on the body — a
coupling that re-locks the wearer toward the φ-attractor faster than the ambient
field would (`ω₀²` is the lock-restoration rate, `custom-blocks.md` §1 /
`coherence-magic.md` §3).

**The coupling change.** The shell raises the local `q` *at the body* — the 
steadying effect is the body's shed ε² re-locking faster, so the local field
around the player reads calmer:

- **Calmer Weatherglass/Sense.** The shell suppresses the body's own ε²
  contribution, so the Weatherglass's climbing-ε² glow (`field-instruments.md`
  §1.2) runs quieter at the wearer's position — the read is steadier, less jumpy
  at the player's own body.
- **Lower carried ε² — a passive the-burden reducer.** The shed ε² re-locks
  faster, so the accumulated carried-debt (`the-burden.md` §1) grows more slowly
  per channeling act. **[design]** the *shell's* reduction is a designed read
  over the engine-real vent/(1−q) channels; the *growth* of the Burden from a
  given vent is the vehicle's own accumulation (§5).
- **A steadier read = a steadier reader.** The read axis (`coherence-magic.md`
  §1) samples the same calmed locality, so sense/glass at the wearer's position
  present a quieter, more legible field.

**The honest cost.** A steadier body is a *duller* one:
- **Resonance efficiency dips.** The same `ω₀²` that re-locks fast resists the
  deliberate, phase-matched injection of deep channeling (`coherence-magic.md`
  §3.1 — resonance efficiency is bought by matching the local rhythm; a shell
  that re-locks faster fights the held phase). **[design]** a net
  efficiency-multiplier < 1 on the wearer's deep-rung ops — the shell steadies
  the low rungs at the price of blunting the deep ones.
- **You cannot vent sharply.** The reducer turns a sharp deliberate vent into a
  steadier, slower one — which is exactly *not* the vent-discipline / trail-noise
  counterplay of `signature-predator.md` §4.1. The steadying shell makes you
  *cleaner* (higher `M_stability` on whatever trail you do shed), not messier.

### 2.2 The high-ξ weave — the deep-rung worker's dress

**The regime.** A high-`ξ` tuple, `ξ → φ⁶ ≈ 17.94` (`corpus-reconciliation.md`),
worn on the body — raising the effective gravity chord `g = 1+(ξ−1)q` at the
wearer.

**The coupling change.** The weave makes the wearer **heavier, heavier-coupled**:
under the river law `a = −G_N·(π/ρ)·∇(g·Φ)` (`coherence-magic.md` §1, the
canonical steer), a high-`ξ` body feels a stronger pull toward matter — it sinks,
it stands its ground, and it draws the field's condensation toward it.
**[design]** the *magnitude* of the coupling change is probe-calibrated; the
*direction* (higher-`ξ` → heavier) is the law verbatim. This is the **deep-rung
worker's dress** — the miner, the diver (`deep-field-diving.md`), the one who
needs to *hold the field* against an excavation rather than be steered by it.

**The honest cost — mobility.**
- **Slower under the river law.** The heavier couple means the ambient
  `∇(g·Φ)` steer accelerates you harder toward mass and opposes moving *against*
  the gradient — the deep-rung worker pays for grip with drag.
- **A weight signature.** A heavier, heavier-coupled body is *more legible* to
  reads that pick up mass/coupling (`field-hazards.md` pre-warning channels
  read ρ/coupling) — the weave draws a stronger presence than a bare body.

### 2.3 The detuned-weave lining — the mask

**The regime.** A `ω₀²` held at a **private phase offset** from the ambient
φ-lattice, on the body — `coherence-technologies.md` concept 3's **φ-detuned
boundary** (`M ≈ 0`) worn as a lining rather than built as a wall.

**The coupling change.** The lining makes the wearer a **`M ≈ 0` surface to the
Coda's resonance** — exactly the phase-masking counterplay of `signature-predator.md`
§4.2, made wearable:

- The accumulation `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability` (`signature-predator.md`
  §2.2) is suppressed because the trail's **phase-stability `M_stability` is
  driven toward 0** by the lining's private offset — the Coda's resonance finds
  `M ≈ 0` and slides off the wearer's signature. **[design]** the masking is the
  concept-3 `M` refusal worn; the Coda-attraction model it suppresses is the
  already-[design] accumulation of `signature-predator.md` §2 (§6a).

**The honest cost — resonance efficiency.** The phase that hides you is the phase
that lets you work:
- **Your own clean, deep channeling is blunted.** The same private offset that
  refuses the Coda refuses the field's resonance — matching the local `ω₀²` for
  efficient deep ops (`coherence-magic.md` §3.1) is harder in the mask. **[design]**
  a net efficiency penalty on deep-rung ops, mirroring §4.2's cost.
- **Unreadable to phase-matched cooperation.** A masked wearer does not read as a
  clean phase-locked body to a resonator-tutor (`resonance-tutor.md`), a
  phase-locked group (`field-npc-ai.md` §6b — "a group acting phase-locked IS a
  working"), or the Still Room's patient clearing — the lining that hides you from
  the predator also hides you from the helpers.

## 3. The shell vs the Burden vs the Still Room

The corpus now has three body-scales, and they must not blur:

| | It is | It lives | Where it clears / works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Burden** (`the-burden.md`) | the **debt you carry** — a damaged-locality-you-drag, the running scar of your own practice | a player-attached, slow-fading ε² field | clears in a Still Room's patient/coherent time (§2b) |
| **The Still Room** (`field-instruments.md` §2.2) | a **place you enter** — the room-scale Weatherglass, the coherent/patient refuge | a built room/locality | where the Burden clears, where the Coda cannot enter |
| **The shell** (this doc) | the **field-state you wear** — an authored-regime shell on the body | on you, always | carried into both; its coupling runs wherever you are |

> **The shell is the one you carry into both.** You carry the Burden through the
> world; you enter the Still Room to clear it. The shell is the garment that is on
> you in both — the daily relationship to the law that shapes *how* the debt grows
> and *how well* the room clears it.

The honest interplay is the load-bearing check that the shell is a *re-shaping
consumer*, never a bypass:

- **A reducer-shell lowers the Burden's *growth*, never clears it.** The
  EY-shell makes each vent shed less carried debt (§2.1) — but the field's
  patient clearing is still the Still Room's time (`the-burden.md` §2b; a bed is
  a personal Still-Room interval, `sleep.md`). The shell does not skip the loan's
  repayment window; it makes the principal slower to accrue. **[design]** the
  reduction is calibrated so a shell never *mints* recovery — it can only slow
  the debt's accumulation (§6d).
- **A masking-shell hides you from the Coda but weakens your resonance.** The
  detuned lining hides your signature in the desert's flat field (§2.3) — but in
  the Still Room and under the night's clearing it is a net drag: you clear the
  Burden slower there because the masked body does not read as a clean
  phase-locked lock (`sleep.md` §3 — the Coda's overnight risk; `signature-predator.md`
  §4.4 — the high-`q` refuge). **The honest trade is that the mask that protects
  you in the desert taxes you in the refuge.**

So the shell does not outsource the Burden or the Still Room; it *modulates the
rate of both*. Wearing the right shell for where you are and what you intend is
the day's central economic and strategic decision (§4) — and wearing the wrong
one compounds the very costs it should reduce.

## 4. Dressing as an act

### 4.1 The outfit is a statement of intent

Because a garment is a permanent bodily field-op (§1), choosing one is not
equipping a stat; it is **declaring how you intend to relate to the field today**:

- **Farm in the steadying shell.** The EY-shell's calmer read and passive burden
  reduction suit the long, low-rung day of the tide-fed plot (`farm-that-feeds.md`);
  the steadying re-lock matches the farmer's patient, sustained low-ω₀² work.
- **Hunt in the mask.** The detuned lining hides the hunter's signature in the
  desert / scar margins where the Coda dwells — but the reduced resonance
  efficiency means the hunt's deep ops cost more coherence.
- **Mine in the heavy weave.** The high-ξ weave's grip suits the deep-rung worker
  — but the reduced mobility is a real price on the surface haul.

Your outfit, read honestly, is the game's most readable statement of your day's
plan — to the tools that read coupling (the Weatherglass's lean, the weight
signature, the resonance-efficiency drop), a dressed player is legibly "this is
how I am going to move through the field today."

### 4.2 The craft — an authored regime on the body

A garment is not a placed block; it is **harder to author than a block** because
it must be a regime the *local field state actually holds on the body* —
`custom-blocks.md` §2's realizability ("not every tuple is realizable — authoring
is bounded invention within the law"). Dressing is the same bounded-invention act,
on the body:

- **Found.** Some regimes can only be *discovered*, never crafted — the
  `custom-blocks.md` §1 "non-Euclidean ore" pattern: a high-`θ_c` regime that only
  precipitates where `q` accumulates in place, impossible to mine-and-recast. A
  skin of such a regime is a **found garment** — rarer, but a genuine coupling a
  laboratory tuple cannot replicate because its `θ_c` is reached *only* by in-place
  accumulation, not by a crafted density.
- **Invented.** Other regimes come from the material lab (`custom-blocks.md` §2) —
  the same Q4 `{op, tuple, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}` write, aimed at
  the body's `worldPos`, the domain's condensation scanner holding the regime as
  worn matter.

**The found/invented economy.** A wardrobe is the collision of the two: dress
from the skins you find in the field's own extremes, or from the tuples you
author in the lab. Neither mints; each is a real coupling change whose cost is on
the same channels that buy it.

## 5. The honest boundary

The [design]/engine-real line, drawn once and never blurred:

- **Engine-real.** The player's coupling channels are real: the EY injection + ε²
  vent + reader (`coherence-magic.md` §1); the coherence budget `B` and its
  geographic recovery (§1.2); the river law `a = −G_N·(π/ρ)·∇(g·Φ)`; the `M`
  phase-matching of `coherence-technologies.md` concept 3 (over the Coda's
  already-[design] accumulation); the Q4 player-return channel the write rides in
  on.
- **[design] over those channels.** The *worn shell's coupling changes* — how much
  the EY-shell raises local `q`, how the high-ξ weave weights the body, how
  sharply the mask drives `M_stability → 0` — are **designed, probe-calibrated
  surfaces over those real channels**, exactly as the-burden's carried-debt
  aggregation and the-Coda's accumulation are: real channel, [design] read/write,
  probe-calibrated.
- **A consumer + bounded write, never a new channel, never new physics.** A
  garment is a consumer of the publish (it reads `ρ`, `q`, `ε²`, `∇(g·Φ)` at the
  body) **and** a bounded write through Q4 (it registers its regime tuple on the
  body's `worldPos`, held by the domain's condensation scanner) — the same
  bounded-write discipline as `custom-blocks.md` §2, aimed at the body. It does
  **not** add a per-player "wear channel" to the publish (the ≈ 6 MiB snapshot,
  `corpus-reconciliation.md`, is unchanged), **not** add a novel force, **not**
  mint energy.

**Determinism (a hard gate).** Same garment tuple + same field state → same
coupling change. The shell's read and write are a deterministic function of the
worn tuple and the published state it samples — a reloaded world reproduces the
identical coupling from the identical tuple and field. This is the gate that
makes the shell *readable* (a player learns to predict what a garment does) rather
than a hidden buff.

## 6. Honest gates

### (a) The [design]/engine-real line

Drawn in §5 — the coupling channels real, the shell's coupling changes [design]
over them, probe-calibrated. The shell never introduces a new physics claim or a
new published channel.

### (b) PHASE-1-ABLE — a worn field-state is a bounded read/write consumer

A worn field-state is **Phase-1-legible**: a bounded read/write consumer of the
publish + Q4 (an authored regime on the body). It needs none of the medium-late
machinery (no Coda, no Still Room, no active combat) to be a real, testable
coupling change. It is **gated on regime constants landing** — the per-material
`γ/ν/μ` and per-cell `ω₀²`/`ξ` engine work that `custom-blocks.md` §2's registry
sits behind (the `material-regimes.md` §7 gates, flagged open by
`corpus-reconciliation.md` §4). Once a tuple is a real regime the world holds, a
tuple on the body is the same act, phase-1-tested. The **Phase-1 slice**: one
shell's coupling read on the living-terrain demo — wear the EY-shell, read `q` /
the Weatherglass / the vent at the body, verify the coupling change lands as
designed, before any wardrobe economy is built (§7).

### (c) Determinism

Same tuple + same field state → same coupling. A hard gate (§5), the property
that makes the shell readable rather than a hidden state.

### (d) The no-free-energy cap

**A shell trades, never mints.** No garment *generates* coherence, ε², or a
steady read from nothing — every shell is a coupling *change* whose benefit is
costed on the same channels that buy it (§2). A reducer-shell slows the burden's
growth, never produces recovery; a masking-shell hides you from the Coda, never
from the cost of diminished resonance. This is the corpus's standing no-free-energy
discipline (`energy-harnessing.md` §6; `the-burden.md` §6d; `signature-predator.md`
§8e) applied to the body: **no "wear to farm recovery," no garment that
generates.**

### (e) Accessibility

A shell's effect is **readable from the reader/glass, never hidden-only** — the
same field's channels, the same presentational idiom (`field-instruments.md` §2.1,
`sleep.md` §6e). The calmer glow, the weight signature, the resonance-efficiency
drop are all on the published channels a reader/glass already presents — a player
who cannot (or chooses not to) rely on a hidden mechanic loses nothing, because
there is no hidden mechanic.

## 7. Feasibility verdict

**PHASE-1-ABLE, with a Phase-1-legible concept.**

A worn field-state is a *bounded read/write consumer of the publish + Q4* — the
same architectural act as a custom block (`custom-blocks.md` §2), aimed at the
body instead of a location. It introduces no new physics, no new channel, no free
energy. Its feasibility rests on two things, both stated:

- **The regime-constants gate** — a worn tuple is real only when the tuples the
  world holds are real (the `material-regimes.md` §7 per-material/per-cell engine

   holds, own question whether NPC clothing is a designed layer or reserved.

   ε²-elevation rather than its phase-stability — the accessible read (gate §6e)

   publishable.
