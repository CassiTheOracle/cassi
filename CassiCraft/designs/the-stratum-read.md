## 2. The layered read — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 The same residue, read in place

Archaeology reads residue with **depth** — the reader's depth dial is the archaeologist's
"dig tool," reading the `ρ`/`q` profile down through the strata (field-archaeology §3.2: a
"core sample is reading the `ρ`/`q` profile down through the strata" — a vertical gradient
read of `q` and `ε²` that resolves the log-periodic stratigraphy of §2.3). A deep read
pierces the birth-strata; a shallow read sees the top layer.

The Stratum-Read is **that depth read, made in-place and layered** — it does not dig (no
perturbation, no `ρ` removal, no `ε²` raise); it re-reads the locality's *published*
channels at each stratum's depth, exactly as the archaeologist's core sample reads them,
but **organized by the depth-strata the archaeology already names** and presented as a
layered, standing read. **[design]** The depth-strata mapping — which depth *is* which
stratum, how the locality's `q`/`ε²`/residue at depth $z$ maps onto the archaeology's named
band (deep-rung residue, scar plateau, re-bound band) — is this doc's composition over the
published channels; the published channels themselves are the Archaeology source docs'
landed reads.

Concretely, the instrument is a **bounded, read-only consumer**: at the player's standing
locality, it takes **one sample per stratum** at the stratum's depth — a small set of
trilinear samples of the published `q`/`ε²`/`∇(g·Φ)` at the depth-strata's positions — and
presents it as a layered, legible read. This is the instrument family rule
(`field-instruments.md` §2.1: a consumer of the publish with a presentation idiom, never a
new channel) and the sample-at-position pattern (§1.4), applied *down* rather than *across*.

### 2.3 The composition — this doc's [design]

What the Stratum-Read adds is **composition, not physics** (exactly the family rule's
freedom — a consumer may re-present the published channels at a new axis, `field-instruments.md`
§2.1; the archaeology already presents them *dug*, the Stratum-Read presents them *layered
in place*):

- **The depth-strata mapping** — which depth-stratum each named band occupies, how the
  locality's `q`/`ε²`/residue at depth $z$ maps onto the deep-rung / scar / re-bound /
  strata / shelf vocabulary. **[design]** — the mapping is probe-calibrated against the
  archaeology's strata calibration (field-archaeology §6b/§6c, the residue-persistence and
  strata-vs-heal probes), never assumed.
- **The layered presentation** — the read displayed as stacked layers (a "time-camera" —
  the depth axis becomes a history axis), each layer's live residue shown at its depth with
  its landed meaning (pulled, wound, healed, imprinted, shelf). **[design]** — the idiom is
  this doc's over the real channels.
- **Phase-aligned** — the re-read is aligned to the phase of the published channels (the
  depth-strata's positions relative to the locality's `q`-structure and the φ-scaled band
  spacing), so the layers read *together* as one coherent history, not a set of arbitrary
  slices. **[design]** — the alignment is the composition's spine.

### 2.4 Determinism — a hard gate

The field is deterministic (one PDE; field-archaeology §1.2; the corpus's hard-determinism
discipline). Therefore:

> **Same locality, same field state → same stratum-read.** The layered `q`/`ε²` read at a
> standing position's depth-strata is a **pure function of the published channels** — a
> player who stands in the same spot at the same field state twice gets the identical
> layered read, every time.

This is a **hard gate**, inherited unchanged from the Clock's determinism (`the-clock.md`
§5(c)), the Scar-Lifecycle's (`the-scar-lifecycle.md` §5(c)), and the corpus's discipline.
**[design]** The depth-strata mapping and the layered presentation are designed constants;
the `q`/`ε²`/residue they read are deterministic. A player who internalizes "this worked
vein reads its pulled depth, this kept scar reads its `ε²` step" can rely on it and
reproduce the locality for the identical read — the read is a *true, learnable* fact about
what the field kept, never a mood.

## 3. The honest boundary — present, never recorded

The load-bearing honesty of the Stratum-Read is the archaeology doc's own, made an instrument:

> **The Stratum-Read reads what the field *still holds* — never what happened.** The residue
> is present residue, not a recording: the field's transient past is washed out on the
> coherence-sound timescale (field-archaeology §1.2 — the IIR's one-rung temporal memory
> `N_memory ≈ φ ≈ 1.6`; "determinism is not recoverability"); what persists is only the
> structurally stable — the deep-rung, attractor-consistent fraction. **A player reads the
> kept scar's depth, never the day it was cut.**

Three consequences, each grounded in the source docs:

1. **The read is a present read, not a replay.** The Stratum-Read shows the locality's
   *current* `q`/`ε²`/residue at the depth-strata — the field's kept state *now*. It does
   not and cannot reconstruct a vanished configuration: the gone configuration is not under
   the ground, only the deep residue of what it was made of (field-archaeology §1.2 #2). A
   player never sees "the moment the vein was mined"; they read the pulled layer the field
   still holds.
2. **The honesty is the archaeology's §3.** "The reader shows the shape of what *was there*
   traced in the medium's disorder" — the dark q-locked core and the ε² ghost halo
   (field-archaeology §3.1). The Stratum-Read presents that *same* residue organized by
   depth; it inherits the archaeology's classification line (the live-vs-residue error is
   closed by `life-signal.md` §3 — a fossil is static/dark/`ε²≈0`, no motion, no cadence;
   a scar is a broad flat-high-`ε²` plateau). The Stratum-Read reads the static residue, and
   the life-signal classifier guarantees it does not misread a *live* organism's body for a
   kept past.
3. **The boundary is never blurred.** The Stratum-Read never claims to "show the past" —
   it shows **what the field kept of the past, read in place today**. It is the temporal
   family's honest past-face: the clock reads now (present), the fate reads ahead (future),
   the Stratum-Read reads *what the field has kept* — the past made present, honestly
   bounded. This is the same discipline that keeps the echo an "imprint-not-recording" and
   the memory-palace's memory *chosen*, never a replay of what happened.
