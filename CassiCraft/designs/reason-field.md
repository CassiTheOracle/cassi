## 2. The published intent/Π field

### 2.1 The seed: the sites that hold activity are the sites that hold intent

`chunk-field-quantization.md` §5 establishes the meshless sites as the world's
own load map: the moving-Voronoi sites are **"where the field is most
organized"**; they are Lloyd-relaxed *weighted on the coherence `q`*
(`ML_LLOYD_P = 4`), so they cluster where the field is coherent; the server
projects each site onto chunk space and schedules re-quantization where the
sites are. The physics is the load scheduler — **the sites that schedule chunks
are the sites the field is itself organizing through.**

Those same sites carry the field's persistent-Π structure. `CassiTheory/
speculations/qi-computation.md` §5.2: long-term storage in the field is
**persistent Π patterns** — self-reinforcing Yang-Yin configurations at local
minima of the attractor potential, persisting while ambient `q` stays above the
dissolution threshold. A site "where the field is most organized" *is* the
locality where persistent Π patterns are held. The load map and the memory are
the same structure:

> **[design] The Reason Field is the site-organized persistent-Π structure
> read out as intent.** The engine realizes the moving Voronoi sites and their
> per-site coherence (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §5: the domain publishes
> the 8,192 site positions + per-site coherence on each meshless rebuild).
> What the engine does **not** today publish is *intent* — the busyness and
> direction of the organization those sites hold. **The domain-side ownership
> of intent is the resolution; the intent/Π *channel* is the designed surface
> that carries it.**

### 2.2 The channel: per-site intent-phase

The new published channel is a **per-site intent/Π read** — a small scalar-or-
small-vector appended to the meshless-site block of the publish:

| Field | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **intent-phase** (per site) | the site's current *direction enough* — the busyness/direction of the medium's own organization at that site, read out as a phase the sampler uses to see "what the field is doing here" | the site's persistent-Π structure: the local Yang-Yin configuration's phase + its rate of change (which way the organization is pointing / building) |

**Sizing.** The channel is a **small addition to the ≈ 6 MiB publish**
(`corpus-reconciliation.md`: `q` 1 + `pot` 1 + `∇(g·Φ)` vec3-trim 3 + `ρ`
single-channel 1). The sites are **already there** — `async-field-domain.md`
§2.2 publishes 8,192 sites at `2·16³ × ~10 floats ≈ 328 KB` fp32 (halved under
fp16); per-site coherence is already published (`chunk-field-quantization.md`
§5). Adding one intent-phase per site is **8,192 floats ≈ 32 KB f32 / 16 KB
f16** — ≈ half a percent of the canonical publish, one line in the site record.
It costs the sampler the same strided read it already does to turn the 8,192
sites into the active-chunk map (§5's ≈ 50 µs); the intent read rides that
same pass. **This is why the channel is a small addition: the sites are already
there, and intent is a read of the organization the sites already carry.**

The intent-phase is deliberately *not* a decision. It carries the medium's own
*state* — what the site is organizing toward — not a goal stack, not a plan, not
an authored behavior. It is the **busyness/direction of the field's own
organization**, made a legible quantity. What the field "wants" to do, in the
sense that its coherence is already *going* there, is what intent-phase says.

### 2.3 How the sampler consumes it — it reads the intent it is already steering

The change to the tick-sampler is *additive, not structural*: the sampler
already reads the sites as the activity map and already steers entities by the
river law along the very organization the sites mark. The intent-phase channel
does not add a second decision layer; it **names the direction of the steering
the sampler is already doing**:

- **Chunk-LOD scheduling** (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §5) already keys off
  site coherence. With intent-phase, the scheduler can also see *which way a
  site's organization is moving* — an activity map that anticipates, not just
  reacts.
- **Entity/ecology steering** (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §2.1, the river law
  `a = −G_N·(π/ρ)·∇(g·Φ)`) already pushes entities along the field's
  organization. Intent-phase is the *same* organization's direction, read at the
  site where it is most concentrated.
- **NPC intent** (below) reads the site's intent-phase where its Π pattern
  sits — the sampler consumes the intent the domain already wrote, and steers
  the NPC's body to realize it.

> **The sampler reads the intent it is already steering.** The Reason Field does
> not hand the server a script; it hands the server the medium's own
> organization with its direction attached, and the server (the read-only
> sampler + the sole-mutator world-writer, per `async-field-domain.md` §1) turns
> that direction into the same velocity/position/block-state intents it already
> emits. The *authority* over intent — where the "what to do" comes from — is
> the domain's; the *mechanics* of applying it are the unchanged async seam.

## 3. NPC minds as field-held intent

### 3.1 A mind IS a persistent Π pattern sitting in the Reason Field

`field-npc-ai.md` §2.1 establishes: **an NPC's identity IS a persistent Π
pattern** — a coherence configuration the field is actively holding (`qi-
computation.md` §5.2), an identity-is-the-run structure (`ecology §2.2`). §7
open-Q1 then asked where the decision layer sits. This doc answers:

> **An NPC's mind is a persistent Π pattern sitting in the Reason Field.** The
> intentionality — the "what to spend the budget on" that `field-npc-ai.md` §6(a)
> flagged as its one [design] addition — is **domain-held**: it is the NPC's
> Π pattern's own organization, read out as intent-phase from the site it sits
> in. The NPC is not a server decision loop that *reads* the field; it is a
> **run of the field** (ecology §2.2) whose steering the field itself carries.
> The sampler does not decide for the NPC; it **reads the intent the field is
> already holding for that NPC** and turns it into the body's movement.

What this changes, concretely:

- **Identity** is unchanged in substance, sharpened in location: a persistent Π
  pattern at a rung-depth (`field-npc-ai.md` §2.1/§2.2) that now *sits in the
  published intent/Π field*. The NPC's identity is a region of the Reason Field,
  not a record indexed to it.
- **The read/allocate/act loop** (`field-npc-ai.md` §1.1) is collapsed into the
  field's own state: **read** is the site's intent-phase (the organized
  direction); **allocate** is the budget physics the field already applies to
  any coherence structure (`coherence-magic.md` §1.2, the `(1−q)` cost,
  `energy-harnessing.md` §6 — unchanged); **act** is the source-term the NPC's
  body injects along the intent it reads. The *decision* that was the [design]
  layer is now a read of domain-held organization.
- **Determinism is strengthened, not weakened.** `field-npc-ai.md` §6(d) makes
  the decision layer a pure function of (Π pattern × channels). Under this doc
  it is a **pure function of the field's own state** — the NPC's decision *is*
  the field's organization at its site. There is no separate decision function
  to keep deterministic; the hard gate is unchanged or stronger because the
  "decision" is part of the one deterministic PDE. Same seed → same Π pattern →
  same field → same intent-phase → same NPC behavior.

### 3.2 The commons is a region of the Reason Field

`field-npc-ai.md` §3.1 makes a village a Qi bath + multiplicative commons: a

villager's Π pattern persist *in the bath* (a many-in-one commons) or *in the
villager* (a sum of individuals sharing efficiency)?" The Reason Field adds the
resolution to the open question without deciding the philosophy:

> **A village's bath IS a region of the Reason Field.** The shared high-`q` core
> of `field-npc-ai.md` §3.1 is, in publish terms, a dense cluster of sites —
> the Lloyd-relaxation on `q` (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §5) makes the
> bath's sites *cluster* exactly where the bath is. Each villager's Π pattern
> sits in that site cluster; the villagers' individual intents are reads of
> sites inside one shared region of the Reason Field. **The commons and the
> individuals are the same field at different read windows** — the bath's
> intent is superadditive because the sites' persistent-Π structure is shared,
> and a villager's intent-phase is the site it sits in. The §2.1-vs-§3.1 tension
> becomes a choice of *read window* (the individual's site vs the bath's
> cluster), not a fork between two systems.

### 3.3 The Coda and the decoherence-wielders: the field's *disorder* intent

The corpus's antagonist class is exactly the dark twin of this resolution. A

> **The intentionality channel carries sign, not just magnitude.** An ordinary

This is the doc's sharpest payoff for the danger layer: the Coda's formation is

  the member-id attached at the world-writer's write (§2.1), `sustain` as a flag

  remains open. The costed re-lock and the journal's field-record are no longer
  "conceptual until Q4 is settled" — they are designed against a final record, and
