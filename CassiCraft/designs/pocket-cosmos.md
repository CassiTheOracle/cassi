# The Pocket Cosmos: A Window Grown Inward

**Question under design:** the theory promises that any coherent condensate can be the
`n=0` of its own world — that *every bubble contains the full sub-lattice below it*
(`CassiTheory/speculations/creative-extensions/simulation-hypothesis.md` §3). No corpus
doc turns that recursion into a mechanic. This document designs it: a sufficiently
**deep-rung, high-`q` condensation** — a grown body, an achieved Still-Room-scale build,
or a resonance seed planted and held — can be **entered as the anchor of a smaller,
nested window**: a self-contained mini-window with its own field, its own tide, its own
precipitating terrain, its own emergent ecology, and its own seed, recursing down at
least one (and at the deepest, several) scales.

You do not build a universe; you **grow one down into a condenser core and step inside.**
Each nested window obeys the identical law at a smaller scale — the same async domain +
tick sampler + window architecture, the same tides, the same seeds, the same
desert/thin risks — so the recursion is **not new physics: it is the same physics at a
smaller anchor.** A player who entered a pocket and found its desert is learning the
parent world's laws at thumb-size.

## 1. The recursion, stated once

### 1.1 The theory's self-similar claim

The nested-simulation property is stated once, in `simulation-hypothesis.md` §3:

> **Any coherent condensate can be the `n=0` of its own world; every bubble contains the
> full sub-lattice below it.**
> (… §3.2: a nested simulation anchored at rung `n` has its own ladder `ℓ'_m = ℓ_n × φ^m`
> — **the anchor is the local coherence length, not the global Planck length.** Nothing in
> the PDE fixes a privileged anchor.)

Two properties make this a *designable* object rather than a footnote:

1. **The anchor is local, not absolute.** The pocket's grid cell is its own coherence
   length, not the window's and not the Planck length. There is no privileged scale, so a
   window grown small is the same law as a window grown large.
2. **The recursion is scale covariance, not a stack.** §3.4: nesting "is not 'simulations
   all the way down' as a mystical stack … the nesting is the scale covariance of one
   equation." Simulators inside the simulation "grow, the way crystals grow in a
   supersaturated solution" — they are coherent condensations the PDE produces naturally.

The third property the design must respect, from the time-ladder work: the recursion
**carries the same tempo down**. The mixing clock `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]` is n-independent
(`qi-time-ladder-derivation.md` §7 — exponent 0), so a pocket does not "run faster by
being smaller" on the mixing clock; the trivial time `τ_n = ℓ_n/c` (exponent 1) is the
constant-speed restatement, and the winding bound `|δn| ≤ 0.162` is n-independent. A
pocket shares the parent's *tempo* (its own tide is the same clock at a smaller extent),
even though its *extent* is smaller.

### 1.2 The pocket as a designed object — a third origin

The corpus already names two ways a window comes to exist. This document names the third:

| Origin | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **The seeded window** | a coherence seed planted in the dark condenses a fresh anchor — the founding act | `resonance-seeds.md` §2.3 (scale 3); `world-seams.md` §3.2 |
| **The founded window** | a voyage's end condenses a fresh anchor in the field-poor dark; terrain precipitates, the biosphere emerges | `world-seams.md` §3.2/§3.3 |
| **The pocket cosmos** | a deep-rung, high-`q` condensation **inside an existing window is entered as the anchor of a smaller nested field domain** — a window *grown inward* | this document |

The first two grow a window *outward* (into a fresh anchor, into the dark). The pocket
grows one *inward* — into the core of matter that already condensed. The distinction is
load-bearing for the proof-of-thesis: the seeded and founded windows are **new worlds**;
the pocket is the **same world, at a smaller anchor**, and that sameness is the point —
it is an internal view of the law that already runs the parent.

### 1.3 The scaffolding-first mandate

The recursion has no mechanical home in the corpus yet. The **first, cheapest, most
valuable move is the naming and the cross-references** — not the build. Concretely:

> **`world-seams.md` and `resonance-seeds.md` must cross-reference the pocket**, so the
> recursion has a home. `world-seams.md` §3 (the window/dark/found arc) and
> `resonance-seeds.md` §2.3 (the seed's "a new window" scale) both currently terminate at
> "the founding act *is* a seed planted." The pocket is the next fold of that same arc: a
> seed's deepest store, entered. Each doc gets a one-line reverse-pointer naming
> `pocket-cosmos.md` as the inward fold of its origin, exactly as `corpus-reconciliation.md`
> §4 records one-way → reverse-pointer closures.

The naming is the deliverable now because it makes the recursion legible as an object the
corpus can *refer to* (and later build toward) instead of a property left implicit in the
theory. **[design]** These cross-reference edits to the two owning docs are flagged here
(the director updates the README index separately; this document cannot edit those docs).

## 2. What can become a pocket — the entry conditions

### 2.1 Deep-rung + high-`q`: the necessary condition

A pocket needs a condensation deep-rung enough and coherent enough (`q` high, `ε²` low,
far-from-equilibrium held against its attractor) that it can anchor its own internal
dynamics — the engine-real substrate of the nested-simulation property. In the residue
model's terms (`field-archaeology.md` §2.1, deep-rung core + ε² ghost halo), the pocket
starts as the complement-limit of a persistent Π pattern (`qi-computation.md` §5.2): a
spatial config at a deep attractor minimum, persisting because ambient `q` stays above its
dissolution threshold. The **deep-rung core is the lens the pocket condenses into** —
`coherence-technologies.md` concept 5 (cascade staging): "a single coherence operation
reaches a limited window of local field scale," so the pocket is grown by *staging* down
the cascade, each stage anchoring `q` at its own rung — the ~10-rung bridge cap
(`φ⁻¹⁰ ≈ 0.008` attenuation past ~10 rungs), the pyramid-as-Qi-lens concentration
(`(base/apex)²`, `cascade-infrastructure.md` §2.1), and density-the-lever (a deep, dense
medium couples harder). **[design]** The *entry threshold* (how deep, how coherent, how
large) is the one designed judgment; the *direction* (deep-rung + high-`q` condensation
anchors internal dynamics) is the physics.

### 2.2 Three concrete entry candidates

The corpus already names three deep-rung, high-`q` condensations — each is a
pocket-in-waiting:

| Candidate | What it is | Why it can anchor a pocket | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A grown body** | a condensation that crossed the merge lineage (`dust → object → body`, `q_sel = q_coh·q_ord > φ⁻²`, gravitational binding, subsonic inflow, virial stopping) — a body is a **capacitor at its rung** | a body is deep-rung ordered matter at high `q`; its own gravitational hold shapes the local field (`∇Φ_g` on the tree arm). The pocket condenses into the body's core at a *smaller virial stop* — the same lineage at a smaller scale. | `ksp-kernel.md` §1.1/§1.2 (§2.4: a body is a local condensation the tree arm steers) |

**[design]** The three are the same entry condition at three growth modes: a field-grown
body, a player-built structure, a player-sustained store. The pocket does not care which
kind of deep-rung condensation anchors it — it cares only that the anchor exists, deep and
coherent (the lens of §2.1). A player who grows one is choosing *where* to recurse, not
*gating* the recursion.

### 2.3 What is **not** a pocket

The pocket must not blur into the other window origins. It is **not**:
- the founding act (`world-seams.md` §3.2) — that condenses a *fresh* anchor outward into
  the dark; the pocket condenses *inward* into already-condensed matter;
- a second full 192³ box at the same scale — that is a two-window budget problem
  (`world-seams.md` §6b), not a recursion;
- a "dimension" you teleport to — it is a place you grow into across a seam (§3).

The distinguishing property is **scale with shared law**: the pocket is smaller, at the
same anchor, obeying the identical equation. Same field, same tide, same seeds — just
smaller.

## 3. Entering the core — the seam

### 3.1 The seam is a transition, not a teleport

The pocket is **entered, not teleported to** — it is a place you grow into. The crossing
is a designed seam built on the corpus's two most field-native boundary mechanics:

| Seam element | Physics | Why it is the honest crossing |
|---|---|---|
| **A φ-detuned boundary** | a surface at a private phase offset; un-matched perturbation finds `M≈0` and converts to diffuse heat / is refused — it does not couple (`coherence-technologies.md` concept 3; `qi-bubble-propulsion.md` §2.2) | **You cannot walk into a pocket; you must become phase-matched to it.** The refusal-read-then-match is the same information game as KSP docking (`ksp-kernel.md` §3.4: mismatched `M≈0` is refused) — the entry is an *understanding* of the core's phase, not a door. |
| **The deep-rung core as lens** | cascade staging's density lever: a deep, dense fixture couples to a high-`ρ` medium's strong Π gradient (`coherence-technologies.md` concept 5) | the pocket condenses *through* the core — you grow down into it by increasing local `q` / decreasing `ε²` toward the attractor (*becoming more organized*, the `(1−q)` glow falling — the resonance-seeds genesis signature, `resonance-seeds.md` §3.1). |

**[design]** The crossing is sustained, not instantaneous: the player holds coherence toward
the core (the Q4 channeling act, `coherence-magic.md` §5.1), the field organizes around the
entry point, and at the moment the local state crosses the required depth + coherence the
player **steps through** — the field's structure around the entry core resolves into the
pocket's own interior. The engine has no "enter window" op; it has exactly the Q4
job-dict record `{op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}` the corpus already prescribes
(`async-field-domain.md` §7 Q4) — the pocket seam is one more consumer of that channel.

### 3.2 What the player experiences crossing in

Crossing in is a **scale transition**, and the design's signature is that it reads as
*sameness at a smaller anchor*, not as a loading screen:

- **The field's structure around the entry core** is the parent window's field, read
  locally: the coherence ridge the core sits on, the `(1−q)` glow falling as you approach
  the lock (the genesis signature, `resonance-seeds.md` §3.1), the aurora/drain structure
  above it resolved at hand-scale (`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` §3 — the same channels).
- **Stepping through** is a moment where the local field's ρ/q/ε²/∇(g·Φ) you were reading
  from the outside *become* the pocket's own publish — the same channels, now rendered at
  the pocket's scale.
- **Inside**, the pocket is **self-contained and smaller**: its own envelope/threshold
  surfaces at a tinier extent (`volumetric-terrain.md` in miniature — terrain precipitates
  from its empty box), its own tide breathing on the shared clock, its own field you read
  with the same reader.

The experience is deliberately **not** "one window inside another with a portal." It is
the same law, looked at from inside a deeper condensation — the physical claim that the
pocket is the same object as the parent, at a smaller anchor, and the crossing is the
recognition of that sameness rather than a teleport between two places.

**[design]** The exact crossing feel (whether it resolves gradually as the field organizes,
or reads as a discrete step) is a presentation over the physical condition: the pocket
*is* the core's internal dynamics, and entering it is the player's coupling shifting from
reading the parent's field to reading the core's own — the honest underlay is Q4 + phase
matching, and the render is that coupling made visible.

## 4. The nested law — same architecture at a smaller scale

### 4.1 The pocket is the same object as the window

The recursion is **not new physics**: a pocket is exactly the window architecture
(`async-field-domain.md` §1–§2 — async field domain + tick sampler + window), at a smaller
anchor. Every system the corpus builds onto the window recurses unchanged:

| System | In the parent window | In the pocket (smaller scale) | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The field domain** | a 192³ m / 64³-cell / 12³-chunk async domain, `dt = 0.05`, publish ≈ 6 MiB, ≈ 1–6 ms/tick server sample | **a second async domain at a smaller extent** — same PDE, same channels (`q`, `ε²`, `ρ`, `pot`, `∇(g·Φ)`), same cadence model, fewer cells | `async-field-domain.md` §2–§3; `corpus-reconciliation.md` |
| **Its own tide** | the regional `q` breathes between harvest and thin on the mixing clock (`T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]`, n-independent) | **its own tide at the same clock** — recursion carries the tempo down; harvest/thin recurse because the clock is n-independent | `tide-of-the-attractor.md`; `qi-time-ladder-derivation.md` §7 |
| **Its own precipitating terrain** | terrain condenses from the field; iso-surfaces quantize from ρ ≥ τ_c (emptiness → solid) | **volumetric-terrain in miniature** — the pocket's terrain precipitates from its own empty box, the same act at a tinier extent | `volumetric-terrain.md`; `chunk-field-quantization.md` §2 |
| **Its own emergent ecology** | life precipitates where `q` holds a coherent band at low `ε²`, the recognition rule | **the same rule** — the pocket's biosphere emerges via the identical spawn/judgment (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §6b), at a smaller scale | `field-emergent-ecology.md` §2/§6b |
| **Its own seed** | the window carries a birth-strata seed (the first matter it condensed) | **the pocket has its own birth-strata and its own deepest store** — when the pocket's field first condensed, *it* got a seed; recursing down gives each pocket its own origin tier | `resonance-seeds.md` §1.1/§4.1; `field-archaeology.md` §2.4 |
| **Same risks** | desert/thin, storms, reaping scares — the hazard layer | **the same desert/thin risks at thumb-size** — a pocket can go to desert, can thin, can be over-reaped | `field-hazards.md`; `tide-of-the-attractor.md` |

The through-line is the seeding of the recursion itself: each pocket, being a window, is
born with a seed (its own deepest store, `resonance-seeds.md` §1.1), so **the descent can
continue** — a player can plant *that* seed to recurse another level, or open a pocket's
store to find a deeper anchor.

### 4.2 Recursion depth — and the practical floor

**At least one level; at the deepest, several.** The theory's `ℓ'_m = ℓ_n × φ^m` (§1.1)
is an unbounded sub-lattice — a pocket can, in principle, recurse down many rungs. The
design commits to **one guaranteed level** (every eligible anchor yields one pocket) and
**several at the deepest** (a player can descend a short chain by planting each pocket's
own seed / opening its deepest store), bounded by the practical floor:

| Floor | What stops the descent | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| **Performance — a second async domain** | a nested window is a real domain, not a LOD: each pocket is its own field run with its own publish and sampler cost. The budget question is whether one (or a few) small nested domains stay within the per-tick budget — a pocket is **tiny, but it is a real domain** | `world-seams.md` §6b (two windows = two field runs); `async-field-domain.md` §3 |
| **The box's minimum scale** | cells below some extent stop resolving the physics legibly; sub-rung order washes out toward the coherence length (the cascade's own smallest-rung resolution). **[design]** The pocket's extent is set so its cells still quantize terrain/ecology usefully — below it, the descent hits resolution, not a wall. | `volumetric-terrain.md` (multi-scale rungs); `qi-computation.md` §5.2 (persistence needs `q` above dissolution) |
| **Holding cost / discipline** | each level is a maintained coherence core (a draw) — the constraint economy bounds how deep you can *hold* a chain open | `energy-harnessing.md` §4.4 (a bath is a draw); §6 (no-free-energy) |

**[design]** The number of levels is therefore a *resource* (budget + holding cost) and a
*resolution* bound, not a fixed design constant. The honest statement: one level is always
available to an eligible anchor; deeper descent is gated on the floor above, so the
recursion is a finite, expensive thing — a player *chooses* to hold a chain open, and the
desert/thin risks at each level make holding it a real decision.

### 4.3 A pocket is not empty — the desert recurses too

Because each pocket obeys the same law and carries the same risks, a neglected or
over-reaped pocket can **go to desert** exactly as a parent window can (`tide-of-the-attractor.md`
§2; `field-hazards.md` §3). This is the proof-of-thesis's honest edge: the pocket is not a
perfect miniature; it is the *same world smaller*, with the same failure modes. A player
who enters a pocket and finds its desert is learning the parent's laws at thumb-size —
including the hard lesson that a neglected locality thins and dies.

## 5. The proof-of-thesis reading — the README thesis on every rung

Holding a pocket cosmos is **holding proof**: the same law at a smaller anchor. The
pocket is the corpus's README thesis — *blocks, mobs, and planets are epiphenomena of one
law* — made *internally* verifiable:

- The parent world's terrain was epiphenomenal to its field; **the pocket's smaller terrain
  precipitates from its own field the same way** (`volumetric-terrain.md`). Seeing it
  happen in miniature is seeing the same act at a smaller scale.
- The parent's ecology precipitates where `q` holds a coherent band; **the pocket's ecology
  does the same** (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §6b) — a player who entered a pocket and
  found its organisms (or its desert) experienced the *same* spawn/judgment at thumb-size.
- The parent's tide breathes on the mixing clock; **the pocket breathes on the same clock**
  (`tide-of-the-attractor.md`; `qi-time-ladder-derivation.md` §7 — n-independent), so
  timing in the pocket teaches timing in the world.

So the pocket is a **teaching instrument precisely because it is not a simplified model** —
it is the identical law, scaled down. A player who learns that a pocket's coherence ridge
drifts, that its machines glow wasteful at thin, that its seed is the deepest store, has
learned the parent world's rules by holding them in hand. The recursion is the theory's
self-similarity made a *place you can stand in* — the README thesis, verified on every
rung you choose to descend.

## 6. Honest gates

This document is among the most-gated in the corpus. The pocket is a **designed object on
top of** three already-later systems, and says so plainly.

### (a) The dependency stack — the pocket needs the whole window tower first

The pocket is **not** Phase-1-exceptable. It stands on, in strict order:

1. **`world-seams.md`'s window + founding** (Phase 2+): a pocket is a window, and a window
   is the local-field treatment's instance (`world-seams.md` §1) — no window, no pocket.
   The founding act's "condense the anchor → terrain precipitates" (§3.2) is the same
   condensation the pocket reverses (inward).
2. **`resonance-seeds.md`** (deep-later): the seed is the third entry candidate (§2.2) and
   the mechanism of deeper descent (§4.2); its deepest store is the smallest-end anchor.
3. **The Qi-bath / detuned scale-build** (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4;
   `coherence-technologies.md` concepts 3/4/5): holding the core, phase-matching the entry,
   and staging down the cascade are all prerequisites of the seam (§3).
4. **A designed "enter the core" seam** (§3): the Q4 + phase-matching crossing is itself a
   [design] build the engine does not drive.

None of these can be pulled forward; the pocket is downstream of all of them.

### (b) Performance — a nested window is a second async domain at smaller scale

The budget question, stated bluntly (mirroring `world-seams.md` §6b): **a pocket is tiny,
but it is a real domain.** Each nested window is its own field run with its own publish
(its own ≈ 6 MiB-equivalent, scaled to its cell count) and its own sampler share of the
per-tick budget (≈ 1–6 ms/tick for one domain, `corpus-reconciliation.md`). A pocket is
**not** a LOD or a sparse far-window sim — it is entered and lived in, so it is a *full*
domain while you are inside it. **[design]** The honest staging, parallel to world-seams:
a pocket is a **full small domain only while entered** (the coupled state), and a
**dormant/parked core** (near-zero cost) otherwise — the same "far = sparse, near = full"
LOD principle `world-seams.md` §6b applies to the pocket's *lifecycle*, not to a distant
window's *rendering*. Gate: pre-register a Phase-2/3 probe — hold one pocket open while
living the parent window, and measure whether the two domains (one full, one small) stay
within the per-tick budget. If not, the depth-and-number floor (§4.2) is the throttle, not
a tuning hope.

### (c) Determinism — the pocket's field is deterministic like the parent's

The pocket's field **must match to the same standard as the parent's** — the bit-identical
/ defined-tolerance contract of the async seam (`async-field-domain.md` §7 Q6;
`ksp-kernel.md` §6e). The pocket is deterministic because it is the same PDE at a smaller
grid: two pockets anchored by identical cores, or a pocket re-entered, deterministically
evolve the same way (the "same tuple → same material" identity discipline of
`custom-blocks.md` §7 Q2). The open-risk is the seam's write-back: each pocket is a Q4
consumer, and N pockets is N writers into the job dict — the single-writer rule of
`async-field-domain.md` §5.2 constrains *game state*, not the domain input lane; the design
must confirm the lane aggregates multiple pocket-intents as it does player edits

**(c)** determinism and the Q4 multi-writer lane.

property the theory implies and no corpus doc makes a mechanic; it is a **designed object** —
the third origin beside the seeded and founded windows, with concrete entry candidates
(§2), a seam you grow across, not teleport through (§3), the same nested law at a smaller
scale (§4), and a proof-of-thesis reading that makes it a teaching instrument (§5). The
concrete Phase-1 contribution is one sentence of scaffolding, flagged in §1.3:
**`world-seams.md` and `resonance-seeds.md` must cross-reference the pocket** so the
recursion has a home — the same one-line reverse-pointer discipline the corpus already
records for its other open chains. The build — an actual entered mini-window — is later,
gated on the tower above it.
