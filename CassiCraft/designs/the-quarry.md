# The Quarry: The Open Cut — the Deliberate Mining-Ground Where a Window Takes Order from the Deep's Face

**Question under design:** the corpus has the fallow (the *worked* deposit — what
mining *does* to a window, [`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md)), the tool (the
*bite* — the rung-matched work object, [`the-tool.md`](./the-tool.md)), the

the bedrock (the deep's *floor*, [`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md)), and the
cave (the deep's *openness*, [`the-cave.md`](./the-cave.md)). **But no designed
mining-ground — no deliberate place where a window's order is *taken from the

deep's floor and its openness (the bedrock's terminus, the cave's hollow); but
it has **no designed cut where the taking happens by design** — no open cut with
a designed extent, cost, and scar. This document is that design: **the Quarry is
the open cut — the deliberate mining-ground where a window takes order from the

fallow's *place* — the one deliberate spot where the window's one-way run-out
happens **by design**, read honestly on the strata and the archaeology.

## 1. The Quarry, stated once

The corpus's taking of order is everywhere **dispersed and consequential**. The
fallow reads the one-way run-out of what mining *does* to a window
([`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §1/§2a — a worked deposit is gone forever).
The tool bites the vein — a rung-matched perturbation whose bite, wear, and scar
are the work object the whole economy rests on ([`the-tool.md`](./the-tool.md)
§2). The stratum-read records *what lies under* before the excavation
([`the-stratum-read.md`](./the-stratum-read.md) §1 — the past-face, the field's
kept residue read in place). The bedrock floors the deep ([`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md)
§1 — the immovable terminus everything precipitates onto), and the cave opens it
([`the-cave.md`](./the-cave.md) §1 — the deep's hollow, the openness the dense
pile holds).

**But the corpus has no designed mining-ground.** The tool is a *thing in your
hand*; the fallow is a *window-scale depletion*; the stratum-read is an
*instrument that reads*; the bedrock is the *floor*; the cave is the *openness*.
None of them is a **deliberate place where a window takes order from the deep's
face** — an open cut with a designed extent, a designed cost, and a designed scar.
The window's order is *taken* (by the tool), *depleted* (read by the fallow), and
*regretted or stewarded* (its scar healed or kept) — but it is never taken in a
designed, bounded, deliberate ground. **The Quarry is that missing ground.**

> **The Quarry, stated once.** It is the **open cut — the deliberate mining-ground
> where a window takes order from the deep's face.** It has a **worked face** — a
> steep, deliberately-cut exposure of the deep's rung-layers, hardness-as-rung made
> a wall the tool meets. It has a **designed extent** — a bounded volume the cut
> may take; the quarry's take is honest, finite, and scarred, and it is read
> *before* the cut (the-stratum-read's point-records) so the window knows what it
> is taking. And it has a **scar-and-yield** — the cut's edge is the
> scar-lifecycle's **deliberate branch**: a quarry's scar is a *worked* scar (the
> clear that does not scar was never a clear), its yield the order taken, its honest
> cost the depletion the fallow reads. **It is the fallow's *place* — the one
> deliberate spot where the window's one-way run-out happens by design, read
> honestly on the strata and the archaeology.**

The quarry is the **extraction primitive** — the designed act at the center of a
window's material economy. The tool bites; the fallow depletes; the stratum-read
records; the bedrock floors; the cave opens. **The Quarry is the ground where the
tool's bite, at the fallow's honest extent, reads its face on the strata before
the cut, and books its scar-and-yield on the archaeology-to-come.** It is the
deliberate "we take from here, this far, and no farther" — the designed place
where the window's own chosen depletion lives.

Distinct from everything adjacent: the fallow is the *consequence* (the run-out),
the Quarry is the *place* (the deliberate cut). The tool is the *hand object*, the
Quarry is the *ground* the hand object works. The stratum-read is the *read*, the
Quarry is the *reading-then-cutting* that takes. The cave is the deep's *openness*
healed inward; the Quarry is the deep's *openness* cut **outward to a designed
extent** — the kept-scar's edge taken the other way, into a worked cut rather than
a hollow. **Where the tool moves matter and the fallow measures what was moved,
the Quarry is the designed place that makes the moving a *bounded, deliberate,
scarring act* — the extraction primitive the whole economy rests on.**

## 2. The cut — the honest mechanics

The quarry's cut has three honest parts, each grounded in its source doc's real
quantity; **the quarry's form — its face's structure, its take's bound, its
stopping — is this doc's [design]** over the material read, probe-calibrated (§5).

### (a) The worked face — hardness-as-rung made a wall the tool meets

**The landed piece:** [`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §4 fixes that
**hardness is `n`, the rung depth** — "a deeper-rung material is more ordered
(lower ε²) and holds its φ-lock against a wider range of perturbation — that *is*
hardness, physically." §3 fixes the precipitation law: a vein is a region where
`q` accumulates above a band, condensing into a high-`q`, deeper-rung material
selected by the local `q` band. The tool's bite is its rung-depth — a tool whose
rung meets or exceeds the material's rung perturbs at that scale and *takes*
([`the-tool.md`](./the-tool.md) §2a; mining is a perturbation that lowers `ρ` and
raises `ε²`).

**The [design] over it — the quarry's worked face is that rung-ladder exposed as
a cut wall:**

> **The quarry's face is the deep's rung-layers, deliberately cut open.** Where
> the field piles the regime ladder downward (each rung deeper, more ordered,
> lower ε²), the quarry's face is the *steep exposure* of that ladder — a wall
> where the tool meets material at increasing rung depth, hardness-as-rung read
> as the face's strata. **A shallow face** cuts shallow rungs (cheap, low-order
> matter the field re-makes); **a deep face** exposes deep rungs (harder, more
> organized, more stored coherence — and more costly to reach, and its removal
> scars deeper, the tool's honest charge/scar asymmetry,
> [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §3). The face is not a wall of
> blocks; it is the field's rung-gradient read as a cut, and every swing into it
> is the tool's bite meeting a material of knowable rung.

**[design]** — the *face's composition*. What the source docs land is the rung
semantics and the tool-material law ("does this perturbation exceed this rung's
decoherence-resistance?" — [`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §4).
What is this doc's [design] is *that a window has a designed face to cut* — a
steep, deliberately-exposed rung-ladder at a chosen locality, and the *bounded
perturbation* a quarry delivers per swing at scale. **This is probe-calibrated**
(§5b): the exact rung-step the quarry's face exposes and the per-cut scar magnitude
are Phase-1.5 tuning over the material-regimes constants (`material-regimes.md`
§7), not stated numbers. The *semantics* — "a quarry is a designed exposure of
the rung-ladder" — is the design; the *numbers* are measurement.

### (b) The designed extent — the bounded volume the cut may take, read before the cut

**The landed piece:** [`the-stratum-read.md`](./the-stratum-read.md) §1 fixes the
past-face read: the point-records of what lies under, read in place, organized by
the depth-strata the archaeology names; §3 fixes the load-bearing honesty —
**determinism is not recoverability** ("the residue is present residue, not a
recording — a player reads the kept scar's depth, never the day it was cut").
[`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §3.2 names the core sample — the
depth read down through the strata.

**The [design] over it — the quarry's take is bounded, honest, finite, and read
before the cut:**

> **The quarry's take is the bounded volume its face is cut into — honest, finite,
> and scarred — and the window reads its face's point-records *before* the cut.**
> The stratum-read's layered read (the-stratum-read §2) surveys the face's
> rung-layers in place: **the deep-rung layer's stored matter, the scar plateau,
> the re-bound band** — what the field has kept, read at depth, at the cheap,
> leave-in-place end of archaeology's conservation fork (field-archaeology §5.3
> #3). The quarry's **designed extent** is that cut not taken blind: the window
> reads what its face holds, sets the bounded volume it will take, and the take is
> **finite** (the face's stored matter is a real, exhaustible stock — the fallow's
> worked deposit) and **scarred** (the cut's edge is the worked scar of §3). **And
> because determinism is not recoverability, the read before the cut is the honest
> map — never a replay, never a promise that the cut will re-make what it takes.**

**[design]** — the *extent's composition*. What the stratum-read lands is the
layered in-place read (a bounded consumer of the published channels — one sample
per stratum, the ≈ 1–6 ms/tick budget, [`the-stratum-read.md`](./the-stratum-read.md)
§5(b)). What is this doc's [design] is *that the read fences a cut* — that the
surveyed rung-layers bound a volume the window may deliberately take, with the
take honest (finite, scarred, and booked) rather than an unread withdrawal. The
read is the source docs' landed piece; the *extent it fences* is the quarry's
composition, probe-calibrated against the stratum-read's depth-strata mapping
([`the-stratum-read.md`](./the-stratum-read.md) §2.3/§5(b)).

### (c) The cut stops at the deep's terminus — the floor the quarry does not pass

**The landed piece:** [`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md) §1/§2 fixes the
window's absolute floor — a maximal-rung, q-maximal, effectively-immovable
substrate; the tool's bite skates on the bedrock (no alloy's rung exceeds it, the
honest wall, [`the-tool.md`](./the-tool.md) §2a at its flat maximum). §5 fixes the
floor's honesty: "a floor informs, never mints; no bedrock that yields."

**The [design] over it — the quarry's cut is bounded at the bottom by the deep's
own floor:**

> **The quarry's cut stops at the bedrock — the floor the quarry does not pass.**
> Because the bedrock is the maximal-rung regime no alloy's rung exceeds, the
> quarry's deepest face bottoms out there: **the last rung a tool can take is
> above the bedrock's surface, and the cut's floor is the immovable base beneath
> the taken layers.** A quarry does not mine the bedrock — the tool's bite skates
> on it (the bedrock's §2b, the honest wall), so the quarry's depth is bounded by
> the deepest takeable rung, and everything the window took *precipitated onto*
> that floor. **The quarry is the fallow's deepest worked vein read against its
> floor** — the cut's extent bottoms at the bedrock, and the un-touched face
> beneath the taken volume is the window's honest, un-spendable floor
> ([`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §3; [`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md) §3).

**[design]** — the *stopping composition*. The bedrock's immovability is the tool's
skate law made flat-maximum (engine-real / [design]-landed, [`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md)
§5a). What is this doc's [design] is *that a deliberate cut *bottoms*** — that the
quarry's designed extent has a floor the quarry does not pass, mirroring the cave's
openness hitting the same bedrock ([`the-cave.md`](./the-cave.md) §4 — a cave sits
on the bedrock; a quarry's cut bottoms on it). This is probe-calibrated against the
bedrock's maximal-rung claim (`the-bedrock.md` open-Q1 — does the ladder read as
a flat settling band or a gradual fade?) — a *gradual*fade would soften the quarry's
floor to a "deepest practical layer," honest but weaker than the flat bottom.

## 3. The scar-and-yield — the load-bearing design

The quarry's whole honesty lives in its scar. The load-bearing design, stated once
and never blurred:

> **A quarry is the window's own chosen scar — a place where the field's order is
> taken by design, read honestly on the strata and the archaeology.** The cut's
> edge is the scar-lifecycle's **deliberate branch**: a **worked scar**, not an
> accident — the clear that does not scar was never a clear; **a quarry that
> leaves no scar took nothing.** Its **yield** is the order taken — the
> rung-matched bite's harvest, booked (§4). Its **honest cost** is the depletion
> the fallow reads — the worked vein's one-way run-out, at the cut's designed
> extent. And its **read** is on the strata (the face's point-records before the
> cut) and the archaeology (the spent cut's residue after).

**Why it is a worked scar, not a healed one.** [`the-scar-lifecycle.md`](./the-scar-lifecycle.md)
§2.1 fixes the corpus's hard line: *the clear that does not scar was never a
clear* — a shallow wound heals under rest, and a deliberately-spent locality that
left nothing was never deep enough to need clearing. The quarry takes the deep
branch's own language: **a deep wound persists as a fixed defect — a low-`q`
memory the field keeps, a readable, stable boundary** (§2.2), and a wound taken
deliberately and held becomes a place (the scar-kept, §2.3). The quarry's cut is
the deliberate branch applied to a designed ground: **the window cuts a bounded
volume, the cut scars (its `ε²` spent, its `q` drawn — the worked vein's locality,
`the-fallow.md` §2a), and the scar is *kept as a worked scar*** — not healed away
(a quarry that healed flat and left nothing took nothing), not mined-through (the
bedrock stalls the cut), but **held as the edge of a deliberate taking**.

**The yield — the order taken, booked.** The quarry's yield is the rung-matched
bite's harvest ([`the-tool.md`](./the-tool.md) §2a — a tool whose rung meets the
material's meets and takes): the ordered matter drawn from the exposed face, whose
stored coherence is the taken order. It is **booked** the way any op is — the
quarry's deposits of taken order and draws of the cut's work travel the settled
op-record `{member, op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}`
([`schema-that-settles.md`](./schema-that-settles.md) §2.1), attributed per member,
bounded per cell, at the non-forgeable single write. **The yield is not a silent
gain; it is a taken, booked, legible order.**

**The honest cost — the depletion the fallow reads.** The quarry's cut is the
fallow's one-way run-out made **deliberate and local**: the worked face's taken
layers are gone forever (the-fallow §2a — the condition that made the vein no
longer holds where it was worked, and the law does not re-precipitate that vein).
The window reads its own chosen scar on the instruments — the spent `q`, the
raised `ε²`, the read spent-locality the fallow aggregates at window scale
([`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §3; the window that quarries a lot reads
spent, honestly, the wealth-is-remaining-depletables read, [`the-window-pulse.md`](./the-window-pulse.md)
and the fate's scar-read, [`fate-of-a-window.md`](./fate-of-a-window.md) §2). **The
quarry's scarce honesty is precisely that its take is *visible as a take*** — the
worked scar reads spent, and the window cannot hide from its own chosen depletion.

**And the read on the archaeology.** The spent cut's residue is the strata a later
window reads ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §2/§2.3 — a worked
-out vein's locality is the archaeology-to-come; the quarry's taken layers leave
the deep-rung residue the field kept), and the stratum-read reads that residue in
place ([`the-stratum-read.md`](./the-stratum-read.md) §2.2 — the pulled layer).
**A defunct quarry is a kept scar with a readable history** — the window's own
chosen scar, read honestly by its descendants on the same strata and the same
archaeology the window read before the cut.

## 4. The honest economy — a quarry converts nothing

The quarry's economics are the corpus's no-free-energy discipline made the cut's
own law. The load-bearing honesty, stated once:

> **A quarry converts nothing; the cut's yield is the field's own order taken,
> never a mint.** The quarry does not generate coherence, ε², or matter — it is a
> bounded withdrawal that *takes* ordered matter (the deep-rung reaper's
> withdrawal, [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2.5 — removing deep
> rungs de-orders the locality, raises `ε²`, lowers `q`) and *costs* coherence to
> work (the `(1−q)` waste law, §2 — the tool's wear and the face's glow). The
> no-free-energy cap holds: **`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`** (§6) — the taken order never
> exceeds the field's own deposited order, and the cut's scar is the cap made
> visible at the ground.

The three honest guards, each sourced:

1. **No cut that yields.** The quarry never "makes" the taken order worth more. A
   quarry that appeared to mint coherence from cutting — that returned a richer
   take than the face held, that charged a capacitor by cutting, that read the
   worked scar as a harvestable drain (the Coda/Burden-farming closure,
   [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §7e /
   [`the-burden.md`](./the-burden.md) §4) — **is a false cut the field sheds**,
   exactly as a floor that yields or a cave that pays is forbidden
   ([`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md) §5d; [`the-cave.md`](./the-cave.md) §5d).
   The taken order is the face's own order, removed and booked — never created.

2. **The honest partner is the compost.** The quarry's spent face — the taken-out
   matter's residue, the worked layers' depleted locality, the scar-tainted alloy
   the tool bleeds ([`the-tool.md`](./the-tool.md) §2b) — is the compost's feed
   ([`the-compost.md`](./the-compost.md) §1/§4 — the deliberate re-turn of spent
   order, turned back into the field at a loss, giving back what the fallow
   cannot). **A quarry that cuts and never gives back is a window that mines its
   own fallow one-way; the compost is the quarry's deliberate partner** — the
   spent cut's matter returned to a depleted plot, at `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`, over
   patience, never a mint. This is the honest inverse of the extraction: the
   quarry takes order, the compost gives the spent back at a loss.

3. **The taken order's destination is the store.** The quarry's yield feeds the
   granary ([`the-granary.md`](./the-granary.md) §2 — the everyday order-vault
   holding the ordinary harvest against the lean): the taken order is held as
   plenty, the deposit/draw booked the way any op is
   ([`schema-that-settles.md`](./schema-that-settles.md) §2.1), and the store —
   never a mint — holds exactly the order the quarry took, at the `(1−q)` bleed of
   holding it ([`the-granary.md`](./the-granary.md) §6). **The quarry's yield has a
   destination and a book; it does not disappear into a hidden pocket.** A window
   quarries, stores, and spends its taken order through the cold — and the store's
   draw is spent, never free (the-fallow's spent-read, §2a).

**The book — schema-that-settles §2.1.** The quarry's whole economy is booked: the
taken order's magnitude at its rung (`{member, op, worldPos, rung, magnitude,
sustain}`), the deposits to the granary and the draws of the cut's work, per
member at the cell the op names, never merging a contribution and a drain into a
silent zero (§2.3's cell-ownership). **A quarry is legible on the settlement's
book** — what a window took, whose take it was, what it was stored against. The
book is the quarry's honesty made collective: the window that quarries a lot
reads spent on the Board, the same aggregation the fallow reads at window scale.

   softens the face's "rung-ladder exposed" to a deeper-shallower gradient, honest

   hides its run-out. **[design]/[probe]**

   scar persist as the kept scar of the scar-lifecycle, or slowly heal toward the
   attractor? The "a quarry that leaves no scar took nothing" honesty hangs on the
   re-bind verdict (`field-hazards.md` open-Q4 / `the-scar-lifecycle.md` §5b); a
   re-binding cut softens the worked-scar branch to a presentation read.
   **[probe]**
4. **The quarry vs. the compost's re-turn (§4).** What exactly of a spent cut's
   matter is the compost's feed — the worked-out residue (which the fallow's
   hardest one-wayness allows, never the gone ore), the scar-tainted alloy, the
   taken-but-unstored surplus? The compost's honest re-turn line (`the-compost.md`
   §2) bounds what a quarry's spent face can give back, and the line must never
   read as an un-mine. **[design]** — inherits the compost's open-Q4 boundary.
5. **The quarry across a window's decay.** When a window falls, does its deliberate
   cut read as the archaeology-to-come's clearest kept scar (the next window's
   stratum-read reads the defunct quarry's face as history), or does the fallow's
   leaving book the quarry as a sunk, unrecovered loss? Rides the archaeology's
   residue/stability gates and the fallow's departure; the honest default is that
   a defunct quarry is a readable kept scar, never a buried one. **[design]** /
   **[probe]**, later.
