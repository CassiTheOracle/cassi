# The Mirror of the Self: Reading Your Own Maintenance

**Question under design:** what it means to be the *read* rather than the reader.
The corpus's instruments all aim **outward** — Sense aims at a thing, the
Weatherglass reads the local field, the Life-Signal classifies a stranger's
maintenance, the Coda reads the field for *your* signature. Nothing reads **you
to yourself**. The life-signal reads live-vs-fossil *outward*; no instrument
reads the player's own pulse, Burden, signature, made a glanceable form. This
document is that instrument: **the Mirror, the life-signal's inward face — the
only way to know, before the Coda does, whether you have become a signature
worth hunting.**

> **The Mirror is the self-read instrument.** It renders your own maintenance
> the way the Life-Signal reads a stranger's — your running ε²-debt (the
> Burden's glow), your channeling signature as the Coda would read it, your
> current phase-gap against the field's tempo, and whether you currently read as
> *live-but-idle, burdened, or wound-like* to the field's own classifier.
> **Self-knowledge in a field that is otherwise indifferent to who you are.**

This is a **player-interior design**: the inward twin of
[`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) (the outward vitality classifier). It
introduces no new physics and no new channel; it is a **derived read of the
published channels at the player's own position** — the same vent/(1−q)/ε²/
signature quantities the reader, Weatherglass, and Life-Signal already present,
read *on yourself* instead of on a stranger. The honest boundary — the channels
are engine-real; the Life-Signal's decision rule is [design]/probe-calibrated;
**the Mirror's self-read is this doc's [design] over the same rule applied to
your own position** — is drawn in §3 and §6 and never blurred.

## 1. The Mirror, stated once

The corpus's instrument family has two ergonomic aims — the **aimed** reader
(`coherence-magic.md` §2, Sense) and the **always-on glanceable** Weatherglass
(`field-instruments.md` §1.2). Both, with the Life-Signal, the sky, the sound,
point **outward**: at a thing's `q`/`ε²`, at the local field, at a stranger's
maintenance, at the field's own extremes.

**No instrument reads the player to themselves.** The life-signal reads a
*fossil-vs-live* distinction outward (`life-signal.md` §1 — "does this anomaly
breathe?"); `the-burden.md` designs the debt *you* carry but not a read of it;
`signature-predator.md` designs the trail *you* leave and the Coda that reads it,
but only from the field's vantage. Even the Resonance Tutor
(`resonance-tutor.md` §2.2) shows your *past* self's phase, not your present
state at a glance.

> **The Mirror closes that hole.** It is the Weatherglass family's **inward
> face**: the same base idiom (the glanceable presentation of the published
> channels, `field-instruments.md` §2.1) repointed at the player's own position,
> rendering *self-maintenance* the way the Life-Signal renders a stranger's. You
> stop reading the world and read the one thing the world never tells you to
> check: yourself.

The decisive framing — the inward twin of the life-signal's honest claim:

> **The field is indifferent to who you are.** It does not care that you are the
> player, the healer, the one who fed the village through the thin tide. It
> reads you the way it reads any structure: on its maintenance axis, at your
> noise floor, by your signature. **The Mirror is the instrument that lets you
> care for yourself in a world that won't** — the player-interior's self-kindness,
> made a glanceable form: the one place you can look and be *reminded that you
> are worth caring about*, because the field looks and does not care at all.

This is what the burden's "the world can be wrong about you"
(`the-burden.md` §5) *inverts*: the field can be wrong about you — and the
Mirror is where you see, honestly, what the field thinks, so you can disagree
from knowledge rather than from self-flattery (§3/§4).

**The family rule, stated once:** the Mirror is a **consumer of the published
channels at your own position with the family's presentation idiom — never a new
channel, no new physics, no write** (`field-instruments.md` §2.1). It is the
Weatherglass's read aimed at the wearer instead of the road; the Life-Signal's
decision rule applied to the player instead of a stranger (§2).

> **The Burden's glow is a debt-tint on the Mirror that thickens with your

> position (`field-instruments.md` §1.2), aggregated onto the player over a

> heavy one glows with the running scar of their own channeling

### 2.2 The signature — your channeling as the Coda would read it

`signature-predator.md` §2.2 gives the Coda's accumulation, verified against
[`corpus-reconciliation.md`](./corpus-reconciliation.md) (this doc's canonical

```
R(t)  =  ρ_signature · τ · M_stability        resonance ("how close the Coda is")
ρ_signature  =  trail quality (the vent's ε² elevation × how phase-coherent it is)
τ            =  duration the trail is sustained (the vent kept fed)
M_stability  =  phase-stability of the injection against the locality's rhythm
```

The Mirror's second face reads that **same product at your own position** — not
to *attract* the Coda (it is read-only), but to know your own value of `R` the
way the field computes it:

> **The signature face shows how close you currently stand to reading as a
> deliberate-vent / a wound to the Life-Signal's own classifier** (`life-signal.md`
> §3.3: the deliberate-vent class IS the Coda's tell — clean phase + rising ε²).
> A heavy carried debt (§2.1), a sustained clean channeling, a high phase-stability
> all feed the same `R` — and the Mirror renders the *standing* value, so you see,
> before the Coda's overnight τ accumulates (§4.4), whether you are currently a
> signature worth hunting.

The honest frame: **the signature face is the readable-trail
`signature-predator.md` §8 slice applied to your own position.** The Phase-1
slice of `signature-predator.md` ("you leave a huntable signature") is a pure
consumer of the publish; the Mirror is that same read, turned inward. It reads
the ε²-elevation × phase-coherence of the trail *you* shed, plus the carried
debt's standing contribution — before the predator itself exists (the Coda is
medium-late, `signature-predator.md` §8). **[design]** the `R` → glance mapping;
**[probe]** the `M_stability` term, which collapses toward duration × ε²-magnitude
until `M` publishes (deferred per `schema-that-settles.md` §3 — §6a this doc).
The **Worn Field's masking lining modifies this face honestly**: a detuned-weave

signature face reads *suppressed* under a mask — the shell's effect on your own
hunt-worthiness, made visible (§4.2). **[design]** the masking-supressed read is
the same `M`-deferred read as the mask itself; the *accessible* form must show
the mask honestly even while `M` is un-published (`worn-field.md` open-Q3).

### 2.3 The phase-gap — your current phase against the field's tempo

`patient-field.md` §1 names two clocks: the field's local tempo
`T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]` (a **theory-cited speculation** — `qi-as-time-clock.md` §1,
flagged; the engine kernel's conversion term is q-free) and the player's tick
`dt = 0.05` (engine-real). `resonance-tutor.md` §2.2 teaches the **phase gap**
as the difference between your current injection phase and your past self's —
a gap to close. The Mirror's third face is the gap made **present-tense**:

> **The phase-gap face reads your current hand against the field's present
> tempo.** It renders, in the Weatherglass idiom, the separation between the
> `(1−q)` openness gate at your position (`patient-field.md` §4.1 — the waste tint
> is the tempo itself) and the phase you are currently holding — how far your
> channeling is from matching the local field's patience *right now*. A player
> holding a deep-rung op in a thin, fast field reads a wide gap — the same
> "this op will vent-to-overdraw sooner" the tutor's season-correction teaches
> (§2.3), shown at a glance before the overdraw bites.

This face is where the Mirror meets its landed sibling **the Clock**
([`the-clock.md`](./the-clock.md)): the Clock is the field watch that reads the
local `(1−q)` tempo — *how fast the field is running where you stand* (§1/§3.1);
the Mirror's phase-gap face **places you against** that tempo — how far your own
hand is from the field's present patience. The Clock shows the tempo; the Mirror
shows *you in it*. Both read the same theory-cited `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]`
(`patient-field.md` §1) over the published `(1−q)`, and both are bound by the
same probe (§6a: presentation-only until `patient-field.md` §5b's L1–L3 verdict).
The Worn Field's shell reads into this face honestly too — a worn EY-shell raises
`q` at the body and reads a calmer watch/lean-gap (`the-clock.md` open-Q3's
honest-ambiguity axis, inherited: the Mirror must not misattribute your own
dress to the field's tempo).

The design's precision: the phase-gap face does **not** assert the engine "slows
time" (`patient-field.md` §5a — a hard line). It presents the *existing*
local-tempo difference (theory-cited) and your phase against it, exactly as the
tutor does, **[design]** over the real `(1−q)`/phase quantities. It gates on the
same `patient-field.md` §5b probe: if `(1−q)` does not vary enough to feel, the
phase-gap face is **presentation-only** — it cannot mechanically key on the
tempo, and this doc says exactly that (§6a). **[probe]**

### 4.3 Knowing what you are to the field's own eyes — the classification use

The classification face is the plainest and the most emotionally load-bearing:
**the field sees you as live-but-idle at rest, burdened after a heavy day,
