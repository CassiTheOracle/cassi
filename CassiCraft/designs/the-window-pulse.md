## 2. The beating read — the honest mechanics

The pulse composes four **landed reads** — each a real channel, each the property
of its source doc — into **one beating read over the tide**. The composition —
which reads, what weights, the beating form — is **this doc's [design]**,
probe-calibrated.

### 2.1 The four landed reads

| The read | The source's landed piece | The settlement's face | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The bath's `(1−q)` trend** | the Qi bath's maintained gradient — the bath at the center, the productive edges, the deliberate drains (the settlement's high-`q` core, "brittle if it goes down") | **the settlement's maintenance read at scale** — `life-signal.md` §3's maintenance axis applied to the bath; the `(1−q)` waste fraction's trend is the *core health*: falling `(1−q)` (toward the attractor) = the bath being held; rising `(1−q)` = the bath draining | `house-that-steers.md` §1/§2.2; `energy-harnessing.md` §4.4; `life-signal.md` §3 |

### 2.2 The beating composition

The pulse composes the four reads over the tide cycle `[t_k, t_k+T]` — the
census's and the Board's own accounting cycle (`the-census.md` §2.1;
`shared-ledger.md` §5; `the-window-year.md` §2's pulse) — into **one beating
read**:

> **[design] The beating form, stated once.** The pulse is the composed shape
> of the four reads over the tide, read as a **beat**:
>
> - **The bath's `(1−q)` trend** sets the pulse's **level** — the core health
>   the beat rests on (a falling `(1−q)` is a rising floor; a rising `(1−q)` a
>   sinking one).
> - **The lines' mutual drift** sets the pulse's **regularity** — a settlement
>   whose `C(M)` lines move together beats *evenly*; a settlement whose lines
>   drift apart beats *raggedly* (the leading-indicator honesty, §3).
> - **The anchors' held-locks** set the pulse's **hold** — each held tether is a
>   beat that lands; each shed anchor is a missed beat (the tethers).
> - **The commensals' steadiness** sets the pulse's **warmth** — the settled
>   margin's fitted helpers keep the pulse's low side steady.
>
> A **strong pulse** is one that beats level, even, and warm — the bath held,
> the lines coherent, the anchors locked, the margins companioned. A **thin
> pulse** is one that beats low, ragged, and cold — the bath draining, the lines
> drifting, the anchors shedding.

The **weights** — how much each read contributes to the beat, and the *shape*
of the composition (whether the pulse is a scalar aggregate, or a two-axis read
of *level × regularity*) — are **this doc's [design]**, probe-calibrated against
the same living-terrain demo the census's and the Board's slices use
(`the-census.md` §6b; `shared-ledger.md` §6b). The design *prefers* a read whose
**leading indicator is the lines' mutual drift** (§3): a settlement's *coherence
as a body* thins before its *bath's level* does — the same honest pattern the
census names when it says **the people thin before the field does**
(`the-census.md` §3).

**The tide as the beat's period.** The pulse is read per-tide, on the census's
and the Board's accounting cycle (`the-census.md` §2.1; `shared-ledger.md` §5;
`the-window-year.md` §2 — "the tide is the year's pulse"). This makes the beat a
**recurring, readable present** rather than a snapshot: every tide-close is a
settlement *reading its own health as one body*, the way the Board's tide-close
reads who held the core (`the-window-year.md` §3.4). The observances of
`the-window-year.md` §3 are the pulse's **markers**: the festival is the pulse at
its brightest (the settlement at its most freshly bound), the anniversaries the
pulse at its quietest (the settlement remembering that it holds losses too), the
thin-year observances the pulse at its thinnest (the settlement enduring a winter
as one body). The pulse gives the year's rhythm its **health read** — not "when
to act" (the calendar) but "is the settlement *alive as one body* through the
act."

**[design]** — which tide-close reads a beat, whether the pulse is scalar or
two-axis, and how the observances mark it, are this doc's composition dials over
the landed channels. The *channels* are the sources'; the *beat's shape* is
designed.

### 2.3 Determinism — same settlement, same field state → same pulse (a hard gate)

The field is deterministic (one PDE; `field-archaeology.md` §1.2; `life-signal.md`
§6d's hard gate). The pulse inherits it as a **hard gate**:

> **Same settlement, same field state → same pulse.** The bath's `(1−q)` trend,
> the lines' mutual drift, the anchors' held-locks, and the commensals'
> steadiness are all deterministic functions of the published channels and the
> executed op-stream (`schema-that-settles.md` §6b); the composition is a pure
> function of those four reads. A reloaded settlement at the same book and field
> state reads the same pulse, every run. There is no seeded-RNG luck about the
> settlement's health being read one way or another — the bath either holds or
> it doesn't, the lines either cohere or they drift, the anchors either hold or
> they shed. **[design]** The *weights* and the *beat's shape* are designed dials,
> but once set the pulse is deterministic.

## 3. The life/death read — the load-bearing beat

The pulse's load-bearing read is the **life/death boundary**, drawn honestly —
the settlement's own coherence, never a hidden difficulty meter (the same
channels the instruments show, never a secret health stat):

> **A settlement is alive while its pulse beats — the bath holds, the lines
> cohere, the anchors stay locked. A settlement is dying when the pulse thins —
> the lines drift apart, the anchors shed, the bath's `q` falls.**

This is the honest beat: it is a *read of the settlement's own coherence*, the
same channels the Weatherglass, the Board, the reader, and the census show — at
the settlement scale. A settlement whose bath is draining *is* thinner in the
field (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4 — "brittle if it goes down"); a settlement
whose lines have scattered *is* less a body (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2); a
settlement whose anchors have shed *has* lost its tethers (`the-name.md` §2).
The pulse reads none of this hidden — it *composes the visible*.

> **[design] The life/death line.** The *boundary* — where "beating" becomes
> "thinning," where "thinning" becomes "dying" — is a **[design]** threshold over
> the composed reads, probe-calibrated against the same living-terrain demo the
> census (its dormant-threshold dial, `the-census.md` §6b) and the Board (its
> `C(M) < 0` line, `shared-ledger.md` §7 open-Q2) set their dials on. It is set
> with the corpus's two-margins-set-together discipline: a seasonal thin trough
> reads as a **thin pulse** (weakness, honesty — `the-cold.md`'s honesty, the
> tide's thin-floor), never as a death; a genuine, sustained scatter reads as
> **dying** (the settlement's body breaking). The line is a dial, never a
> scripted trigger — a thinned pulse is the fate's decay arc read in the present,
> and the present beats differently from the trajectory (`fate-of-a-window.md`
> §6 — the pulse is *now*, the fate is *forward*).

### 3.1 The leading-indicator honesty (the census's quiet-sign pattern)

The pulse inherits the census's quiet-sign honesty (`the-census.md` §3 — a
shrinking window is not a crash but a season, **the people thin before the field
does**) and applies it to the settlement's *coherence*:

> **The pulse thins *before* the glass shows the settlement failing.** The
> lines' **mutual drift** precedes the bath's fall: a settlement does not stop
> being a body when its bath collapses — it stops being a body when its **lines
> scatter first**. The per-member `C(M)` arcs move apart (the active count
> thins, the dormant rises, the departed's settled entries accumulate —
> `the-census.md` §3) before the bath's `q` visibly drains. The pulse is wired
> so the **regularity** read (the lines' mutual drift) is the first to thin —
> the beat grows ragged *before* it grows low.

This is the census's quiet-sign pattern at the *health* rather than the
*population* scale: **the settlement's coherence as a body thins before its
field does** — the honest early sign that a window's fate is turning
(`fate-of-a-window.md` §1), read in the present beating rather than the
forwarded arc. The **lead time** — does the lines' mutual-drift thinning
*systematically* precede the bath's `q` fall in the living-terrain demo — is a
**[probe]** (`the-census.md` §7 open-Q2's lead-time probe, inherited): a
negative (the drift and the bath turn together) would weaken the
*early-warning* claim to a *coincident* one, and the design prefers the honest
reading either way.

**[design]/[probe]** — the *wiring* (that the regularity read thins first) is
this doc's design; the *measured lead* (how much it actually precedes the bath's
fall) is the probe.

### 3.2 The honest boundary — a read, never a difficulty meter

The pulse is never a hidden difficulty meter. It reads the settlement's **own
coherence**, the same channels the instruments show:

- The **Reader / Sense** (`coherence-magic.md` §2) shows the local `q`/`ε²` — the
  pulse's level is the bath's trend, visible in the field.
- The **Weatherglass / Still Room** (`field-instruments.md` §1.2/§2.2) shows the
  local `q`/`ε²`, the waste tint, the lean — a settlement that reads as a body has
  a healthy, level read; one that is dying reads ragged and cold, from the same
  channels.
- The **Board** (`shared-ledger.md` §2) shows the per-member `C(M)` set — the
  pulse's regularity is the Board's member-lume set read as a *set*: do the lumens
  move together (a body) or scatter (drift)?
- The **Census** (`the-census.md` §2) shows the class counts — the pulse's
  population-side drift is the census's active-down / dormant-up trend.
- The **Observatory** (`the-observatory.md` §2) is the **pulse's seat** — the room
  you walk into to see the window as one body; the pulse is that body's **health
  face**, mounted beside the census's population face and the mirror's
  maintenance read.

A settlement member who never uses a "pulse" surface loses nothing the existing
instruments do not already show, more laboriously — the pulse is an *idiom over
the public reads*, never a second, hidden information source. A pulse that hid
its read would be a hidden health stat the design's own accessibility forbids
(§6e).

## 4. The we/never-I boundary

The pulse's honest scope is its most load-bearing: **it reads *we*, never *I***.

> **The pulse reads the settlement — never any one member.** A member's own
> illness — their low `C(M)`, their wound, their burden's running debt
> (`the-burden.md`) — is **the healer's business** (`the-healer.md` §2), not the
> pulse's. The pulse is the aggregate's read: it composes the *lines' coherence*,
> not any line's value; the *bath's level*, not any one contribution; the
> *anchors' hold*, not any one name's truth. A member who is a drain is the
> Board's and the healer's concern (the Board's misattribution guard,
> `shared-ledger.md` §3.3); the pulse reads whether the *settlement* holds
> together around them.

The honest frame has a real consequence, stated once:

> **A settlement with a strong pulse can hold a sick member; a settlement with a
> thin pulse cannot.** The bathed one is carried — the settlement's coherence is
> strong enough to hold a member who is not contributing, who is wounded, who has
> over-borrowed, *because the body around them still beats*. A member's own
> illness does not thin the pulse; a body that can no longer hold its members
> (the lines scattering, the bath failing) thins it. The pulse is the health of
> the *holding*, never the health of the held.

This is the census's sibling's deepest honesty: the census reads who is here
