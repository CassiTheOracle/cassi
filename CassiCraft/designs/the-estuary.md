# The Estuary: The Salt-Mouth — the Third Water, the Corpus's First Designed Boundary Between Two Designed Places

**Question under design:** the corpus designs the three great waters each as a
distinct regime — the **sea** ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) — the flat coherence
plain, the open water of the tide's pulse), the **marsh** ([`the-marsh.md`](./the-marsh.md)
— the shallow, textured water that hides), the **river** ([`the-river.md`](./the-river.md)
— the living line, the current's `∇(g·Φ)` carry). Each is a *new standalone*: a
place with its own ecology, its own dangers, its own honest law, designed against
the corpus whole. **But the place where *two designed waters meet* — the brackish,
tide-fighting mixing zone of river-meets-sea — has no regime of its own.** The
corpus has never designed a boundary *between two already-designed places*: a band
that belongs to neither water alone, where the sea's salt and the river's fresh
argue, where the tide's rise and fall and the current's flow fight over the same
water, and where the marsh's hiding and the river's revealing both hold in the
blend. This document is that design: **the Estuary is the salt-mouth — the third
water — the regime that exists only where two designed regimes touch.**

The Estuary is deliberately the corpus's **first designed boundary**, not a fourth
standalone water. Where the sea is the open plain, the marsh the hiding shallow,
and the river the living line — each its own regime-complete place — **the estuary
is the intertidal mixing band where those two designed regimes become one thing
that is never one thing**: the river's moving-conduit filament
([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2a / [`the-river.md`](./the-river.md) §2) emptying
its fresh `q` into the sea's salt open water, the tide-of-the-attractor's pulse
([`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §5a) pushing the salt
water up against the current, and the marsh's hiding ([`the-marsh.md`](./the-marsh.md)
§2) taking hold where the band shallows. And its load-bearing honesty is the
boundary's own law: **an estuary is a place that is never one thing — a regime
whose every read is a mixture** (salt and fresh, tide and current, hiding and
revealing), so its order is the *negotiated* kind — the tide's argument with the
current settled by the field's own conservation, never a hidden third physics.

plain. The two are the same liquid regime read at two characters: the river's
coherent flowing filament, the sea's flat uniform plain.

**The [design] over them — the Estuary is where the filament ends and the plain
begins, and the two blend as a density-stratified band:**

**[design]** — the *density-stratified form*: that the river's fresh and the sea's
