# The Reading Ahead: Prophecy as Reading the Field's Momentum

**Question under design:** what reads the *future*. The corpus reads the past
(`field-archaeology.md` — residue only, §1.2), the present (the reader, the
sky, the instruments), the distant present of another window (the Far Mirror,
`field-instruments.md` §3), and composes (`field-music.md`) — but nothing reads
the near-future. This document is that design: **the deep-read oracle** — a
practiced Sense or a built instrument that reads what the field is *becoming*,
the forward read over the same channels the reader already renders, made a
warning-and-opportunity instrument. And because `field-npc-ai.md` gives NPCs
the same read primitives, a village with a deep reader can have a **prophet** —
an NPC whose warnings are *true* because they read the field's momentum, never
a dice roll.

The honest core, stated once before anything is designed:

> **Prophecy is near-future field-reading — the same law's forward tail.** The
> field is deterministic and *currently organizing*, so the near-future is
> **emergent and readable, not a hidden history**. It is not "the exact future"
> predicted from a crystal ball; it is the present's **organized momentum read
> forward** — the growing condensation, the coherence ridge drifting toward a
> threshold, the region about to cross a regime boundary, the desert-margin
> about to take hold, the BH about to tip past self-reinforcing mass. The read
> carries the **uncertainty of a forecast, not the certainty of a fixed fate** —
> and that distinction is the design's whole spine.

## 1. The future's epistemology — the honest core

### 1.1 Determinism gives momentum, not destiny

`field-archaeology.md` §1.2 establishes the honest framing for the past: the
field is **deterministic** (one PDE, the same published snapshot every sample
reads), so in *principle* the present encodes both past and future — but
**determinism is not recoverability**. For the past, that yields archaeology's
conclusion: the transient is washed out, only **residue** persists. For the
future, the same determinism has the *opposite* property, and it is the load-
bearing asymmetry of this document:

> **The past cannot be replayed because the dissolve destroys degrees of
> freedom; the near-future cannot be a hidden history because there is nothing
>  hiding it — it is the *present state's organized momentum carried forward by
> the same deterministic law*.** The field's short-term temporal memory is
> one-rung (`N_memory ≈ φ ≈ 1.6` steps, `qi-computation.md` §5.1), but the *
> force* that carries state forward is not a memory — it is the law itself,
> running the present into the next present.

Two consequences that delimit the honest read:

1. **The read is bounded to the *organized* near-future, not the exact future.**
   The field is "currently organizing" (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §1.1: the
   φ-attractor organizes, so the field has long coherent and long thin states).
   Where organization is *building* — a condensation growing, a `q` ridge
   drifting toward a threshold, a `ε²` front's leading edge, a regional `q`
   trending down — the *direction of motion is legible from the present
   channels*. This is the read's honest domain: **imminent precipitations and
   approaching crossings**, on the deterministic law's forward tail. What is
   *not* in it is the chaotic tail — the exact path of a specific wavefront
   after many coherence-sound crossings, the precise storm track weeks out. The
   read does not claim it.
2. **The read is momentum + threshold-crossing detection over known channels —
   never a crystal ball.** The channels are the ones the reader already renders
   (`corpus-reconciliation.md`: `q` 1 + `ε²` + `∇(g·Φ)` 3 + `ρ` 1, the ≈ 6 MiB
   publish, `coherence-magic.md` §2). The oracle adds one thing: **a time
   derivative / trend + a set of known thresholds** (`τ_c = 0.5` for
   condensation, the desert's `q`-collapse floor of `field-hazards.md` §3, the
   BH's self-reinforcing-mix point, the tide's harvest/thin bands). It reads
   "this quantity is moving toward that threshold" and presents it as a
   scheduled omen. It is a *forecast engine over the reader's own channels*,
   with the uncertainty of any forecast.

### 1.2 The honest boundary, stated once

> **The oracle reads what is *building*, with the uncertainty of a forecast —
> not the exact future, and not a prophecy of a fixed fate.** It extends the
> reader's known channels (q/ε²/∇ drift + threshold-crossing detection) forward
> over a bounded horizon, on the tide's known tempo where a tempo exists
> (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §1.1: the mixing clock `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]`, and its
> honest probe §5a). A forecast can be **wrong and falsifiable** — a rally that
> doesn't hold, a crossing that recedes, a bath that recovers against the
> drift. Determinism gives momentum; **never destiny**. The read's value is
> information about the present's organized direction, which is exactly the
> information a planning life (the omen economy §6) is worth.

This is the same discipline `field-archaeology.md` §1.2 applies to the past,
pointed forward: archaeology reads residue because the past is *gone but
imprinted*; the oracle reads momentum because the near-future is *building but
understood*. Neither is time-travel; both are reading the present for what the
law is doing with it.

## 2. The forward-inverse problem — archaeology's twin

### 2.1 The same lookup, the opposite direction

`field-archaeology.md` §4.4 designs the **mini inverse-problem**: from a found
residue signature, reconstruct the extinct regime — residue → tuple → the
regime's behavior, bounded by realizability. The player reads the past by
*inverting* what the law left. This document runs that same §4.4 lookup **the
other way**:

| | Archaeology (§4.4, backward) | The oracle (this doc, forward) |
|---|---|---|
| Reads | a **residue** (dark q-locked core + ε² ghost halo, §3.1) | a **momentum** (a growing condensation, a drifting ridge, a trending `q`) |
| Inverts / extrapolates | the regime **that was** (residue → tuple) | the outcome **that is building** (momentum → crossing/outcome) |
| Bounded by | the law's realizability (why the regime no longer precipitates) | the law's determinism + the read's horizon (what the present's organization is driving toward) |
| Verifies by | a forward preview matching the found residue's shape | a recorded omen that comes true (the read vindicated) |
| Time direction | past | future |
| Load-bearing honesty | the residue is real, the regime model is [design] | the momentum is real, the forecast is [design] |

**The same deterministic law, run in opposite time directions.** Archaeology's
inverse recovers what *was made* out of what *was kept*; the oracle's forward-
read sees what *is being made* out of what *is moving*. This is the structural
tie: **the oracle is archaeology's twin facing the other time**, and the two
docs deliberately share the §4.4 discipline — both are honest inverse / forward
problems over the same channels, both flagged [design] against engine-real
substrate, both bounded by the law.

### 2.2 What the read detects — the forward channel inventory

The oracle reads the same published channels the reader renders, but with a
**trend + threshold** lens. Each row is a real channel read forward:

| Forward read | Channel + trend | The outcome it signals | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A growing condensation** | `ρ = EY+EI` rising toward `τ_c = 0.5` where `q` is high — a precipitation about to happen | a new solid / ore vein forming (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §2.2; `material-regimes.md` §3: ore precipitates where `q` accumulates) | `coherence-magic.md` §2 Condense; `corpus-reconciliation.md` (τ_c = 0.5) |
| **A ridge drifting toward a threshold** | a `q` coherence ridge moving (the meshless sites advect; the biosphere's basin, `field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.1, migrates with it) toward a « where it tips over » | the basin relocating; ore / spawn-band shifting there | `field-emergent-ecology.md` §4; `chunk-field-quantization.md` §5 |
| **A region about to cross a regime boundary** | a local `ε²` rising toward its dissolution floor, or `q` falling toward the desert's floor | a dissolve / a scar / a desert-margin taking hold | `field-hazards.md` §2.1/§3; `material-regimes.md` §2 |
| **The desert-margin's drift** | a regional `q` trending down toward the `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1` noise-collapse floor, at the tide's trough (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.1) | the desert creeping into a window — preventable only before the trough | `field-hazards.md` §3; `energy-harnessing.md` §7 Q1 |

| **A Qi-bath's health trend** | a maintained bath's core `q` trending down (its standing draw outrunning recovery) | the bath's imminent brittleness — the village's intelligence dropping with it | `energy-harnessing.md` §4.4; `field-npc-ai.md` §3 |

All six are **the reader's own channels read for their direction of motion**,
not for any new quantity. The oracle is *not* a new physics; it is the reader's
static magnetometer read turned into a **trend read plus a threshold roster**.
This is the Phase-1 honesty (§7a): a "what's building here" slice is a pure
consumer that extends the already-published snapshot with a time-difference and
a threshold comparison — no new channel, no write.

### 2.3 The horizon — how far the read reaches honestly

The read's reach is not arbitrary; it is set by when the underlying tempo is
known. The corpus already names the one large-scale cadence — the **tide's
mixing clock** `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]` (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §1.1, and its
honest probe §5a). So:

- **Where a tempo is known** (the regional-`q` tide, the storm's `c_s` front
  approach, the desert's slow creep), the oracle can speak in **scheduled
  omens on that calendar**: "a storm will come in three tides" (the storm `ε²`
  front at `c_s`, `field-hazards.md` §2.1, over a horizon the tide's tempo
  frames), "this harvest will be lean" (the regional-`q` trend toward a thin
  trough, `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3), "the desert will creep this winter"
  (the regional-`q` collapse toward the floor at the trough).
- **Where no tempo is known** (the chaotic tail of a specific wave's exact
  track), the oracle is silent. It does not fabricate a date for a governed
  non-rhythm.

**[assumption]** The exact horizon length in Minecraft time — how many tide-
periods a read can honestly project before the drift's uncertainty swamps the
signal — is a Phase-1 calibration, the forward analog of `tide-of-the-attractor.md`
§5a's period measurement. The *rule* (the read's reach is set by where a tempo
is measured, and it never speaks past a tempo it cannot measure) is the honest
bound.

## 3. The oracle instrument — the deep read, forward

### 3.1 Two forms, one read: the mastered Sense and the built oracle

Following the corpus's instrument idiom, the oracle has **[design]** two forms
that share one read — the forward trend over the reader's channels (§2.2):

| Form | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **The mastered deep-read** | a *practiced* Sense — a player (or NPC) who has mastered the reader and trained it **forward**: instead of "what is here now," they read "what is building here." The Phase-1 momentum slice (§7a) *is* this form — a pure consumer of the publish, no new instrument. | **Phase-1** (the momentum slice) |
| **The built oracle** | a structural instrument — a consumer of the publish with a **forward presentation idiom**, honoring the family rule (`field-instruments.md` §2.1: *every instrument is a consumer of the same publish with a presentation idiom, never a new channel*). A calibrated display of the trends + thresholds + the tide's phase, mounted where a village can read it. | **Phase-1.5+** (gated on the tide probe + archaeology gates) |

The built oracle is the **Weatherglass aimed forward**: the Weatherglass
(`field-instruments.md` §1) is the always-on *present* read; the oracle is the
same base idiom rendered as a **trend, not a state** — the lume not as "how
bright is `q` now" but "which way is `q` moving and how close to the next
threshold", the lean not as "downhill is that way" but "the ridge is drifting
this way". It extends the family's four glanceable forms (§1.2 of the
instruments doc) with a **direction-of-motion** presentation — the same
channels, the same publish, only the *idiom* goes forward instead of present.

### 3.2 The relation to the Far Mirror — present vs. becoming

The corpus's ranged instrument, the **Far Mirror** (`field-instruments.md` §3),
reads another window's sparse publish (~KB, `world-seams.md` gate (b)) into
that place's *present* chart: "this window's `q`-band is healthy, it has an
`ε²` drain growing on its west face, three bodies of which one reads as a young
BH." The oracle differs by **time direction, not range**:

> **The Far Mirror reads another window's *present*; the oracle reads this
> window's *becoming*.** Neither divines a fixed future — the Far Mirror's
> chart is a present sparse state; the oracle's chart is a present organized
> momentum. The two compose: a Far Mirror that *also* carries the oracle's
> trend lens would read "*that* window's desert is creeping toward its trough"
> — a destination-triage upgrade to `field-instruments.md` §3.3's destination-
> decider, telling not just *is it healthy now* but *is its health trending
> away*. **[design]** That cross-ward composition is later; this doc's oracle is
> the local window's forward read first.

The oracle's output is a **scheduled omen on the tide's calendar**: a forecast
of what the field is organizing toward, presentable on the reading of the
reader + the sky + the Weatherglass + field-music's growl/silence
(`field-music.md` §2.2/§2.3 — the oracle makes hazard-audio *forward*: the

| Omen | The read behind it | Presented as |
|---|---|---|
| **"A storm will come in three tides"** | the `ε²` front approaching at `c_s` (`field-hazards.md` §2.1), framed by the tide's tempo | a wave-crossing countdown, not a date-certain track |
| **"This harvest will be lean"** | regional `q` trending toward a thin trough (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2/§3) | a season forecast — energy/materials/ecology all bleed at thin |

## 4. The prophets — the mechanically true voice

### 4.1 An NPC with a deep reader

`field-npc-ai.md` §1.1 gives every NPC the *same three primitives* as the
player: **read** (sample `q`/ε²/∇(g·Φ) from the publish), **allocate** (a
coherence budget B), **act** (inject organized perturbation). Its identity is a
persistent Π pattern at a rung-depth (§2.1/§2.2); its society is a Qi-bath
commons (§3.1). The prophet is the corpus's natural completion of those
primitives:

> **A prophet is an NPC whose Read primitive is trained forward — it reads the
> field's momentum (§2.2), not just its present state.** It has the same
> read/allocate/act loop as every other NPC, but its *read* carries the
> trend-and-threshold lens of the oracle (§2/§3). Its "warnings" — "a storm
> will come in three tides", "this harvest will be lean", "the desert will
> creep this winter" — are **deterministic outputs of its Π pattern × the
> published channels it reads**, exactly like every other NPC's decisions
> (`field-npc-ai.md` §2.3: same seed → same mind, a hard gate). There is no
> dice: **a prophet's omen is a forecast its field-read produces, and it
> comes true when the momentum it read actually holds.**

This is the myth-and-omen dimension **without superstition**: the village's
prophet looks like it speaks for unseen powers, but mechanically it is
**reading the same law the player reads** — just with a read-pattern weighted
forward. The player who has mastered the deep-read (§6.1) can *verify* the
prophet, read the same momentum themselves, and see that the prophet is not
divining — it is the best-reader in the village.

### 4.2 What it can know — the prophet's horizon

A prophet knows, honestly, only what its read's horizon shows (§2.3):

- **Within a measured tempo**: the scheduled omens (storm approach, lean
  harvest, desert creep, bath failure) — the same calendar the oracle renders.
  Its rung-depth (`field-npc-ai.md` §2.2) sets *how deep and how far* its read
  reaches: a shallow prophet reads the near storm front and the local `q` trend
  (days, local); a deep prophet reads the tide's phase and the basin drift
  (seasons, regional). **A deeper lock = a longer, farther, more reliable
  read** — capability scales with rung-depth exactly as it does for every NPC.
- **Not beyond a tempo**: the chaotic tail (a specific storm's exact track weeks
  out), what a *truly* random perturbation will do, the far window's future
  (the Far Mirror reads *present* state, `field-instruments.md` §3 — a prophet
  does not outrange it). **[design] A prophet that reads beyond its horizon is
  a *false* prophet** — and the corpus's determinism discipline (§7c) is what
  makes false prophecy legible as error, not mystery.

### 4.3 How it's wrong honestly — forecasts, not fate

The prophet's wrongness is the doc's probe of its honesty:

| This is honest | This is not |
|---|---|
| A forecast that **does not come true** because the momentum it read **receded** (a rally that didn't hold, a crossing that moved back, a bath that recovered against the drift) | a "fate" that the prophet *must* come true because it was written — fate is the superstition this doc refuses |
| A **revision** — the prophet re-reads, the momentum changed, the omen is updated or withdrawn | a prophecy retrofitted to whatever happened ("I meant the other thing") |
| The omen stated **with its conditions and its horizon** ("if the `q` drift holds, the desert will creep this winter") | a vague, unfalsifiable dread that can be read any way |

The honest engine of this: **a forecast is a claim about the present's
organized momentum, and the present can change**. The player's own actions
(anti-corruption, `energy-harnessing.md` §5.4; a Qi-bath husbandry,
`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.1; not over-reaping, `field-hazards.md` §3.2) are
*themselves* organized perturbations that alter the momentum. A prophet that
read "desert will creep" and then the village husbanded the bath and the desert
did **not** creep was *not wrong — it was heard*. **Good prophecy in CassiCraft
is the omen that changes the outcome**, which is the deepest myth-and-science
convergence: a true forecast is one you can act on, and acting on it is what
keeps it true.

### 4.4 The village oracle — how it's verified

A **village oracle/prophet** is a commons role: a deeper-rung NPC living in the
Qi-bath (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.1), its read sustained by the bath's high-q core
(the bath raises budget recovery and cuts waste, §3.1 — a bath-raised NPC is
the natural deep reader). Its verification is **a recorded omen that comes
true**:

- The village (and the player) **records** the prophet's omen at the time it is
  spoken — the omen *is* a state of the published channels at a moment plus a
  forecast claim.
- When the omen's horizon closes, **the record is checked against the field's
  actual outcome**. A storm that arrived in three tides, a lean harvest that
  came at thin, a desert that crept — each is **the read vindicated**; a
  forecast that receded is **the read honestly wrong**.
- Over time the village (and the player) build a **track record** — the
  prophet's reliability is *measured*, not assumed. A prophet whose omens
  consistently hold is trusted and consulted; one whose reads keep receding is
  heard but weighted down. **Prophecy is a skill with a batting average**, the
  same way the player's own deep-read improves with practice (§6.1).

The myth-and-omen register emerges *because* the mechanism is real: a village
that lives by the tide learns to treat "the prophet says the harvest will be
lean" as **a reading to plan around**, exactly as they treat the reader's
regional-`q` trend (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §4). The prophet is not a
superstition; it is the village's best forward instrument, *personified*.

### 4.5 The prophet gates on the NPC read-primitives

The prophet **does not introduce a new NPC class or a new mind-primitive**. It
is an ordinary `field-npc-ai.md` NPC whose decision layer happens to weight the
forward-read; it rides every existing gate:

- **The read primitives are engine-real** (the publish, the source terms, the
  budget — `field-npc-ai.md` §6a); the *intentionality* (that the read is
  weighted forward) is the same [design] layer every NPC's mind has.
- **Determinism is hard** (`field-npc-ai.md` §6d): a prophet is a pure function
  of (its Π pattern × the channels). Same world → same omens. No randomness.
- **It obeys the commons and the budget** (`field-npc-ai.md` §3, §6e): a
  prophet is a sustaining member of the village's Qi bath; its deep read is a
  maintenance cost, no free reading. A village too poor in coherence cannot
  hold a deep prophet — the prophet's depth is *funded by the bath's health*,
  which is one of the things it reads. A failing commons reads its own failure
  earlier than it can fix it — a melancholy, honest loop.

## 5. The composition tie — the oracle feeds the composer

### 5.1 Music programs the field; the oracle programs *what it is organizing toward*

`field-music.md` §4.1 makes the deepest claim mechanical: a **coherence score**
is a sequence of Q4 field-operation records that, played back, IS a sequence of
field operations — **music is programming the field** (§4.2/§4.3). This doc
completes the sentence with the forward read:

> **If a score programs the field's present, the oracle tells the composer what
> to program — the field's organized future.** The composer who reads ahead
> does not play *blind*; they **play what the field is building toward**: a
> **heal where a threshold is about to cross**, a **hold where a desert is
> about to creep**, a **shepherd's tune aimed at the basin that is about to
> leave** (`field-music.md` §4.2's ecology tie). The oracle is the composer's
> **planning instrument** — the forward read that turns a score from a
> response to a *pre-emption*.

Concretely, the oracle ties to `field-music.md` §4.2's three functional rows:

| What the oracle reads building | The score the composer programs | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| **A threshold about to cross** (a `ε²` rising toward a dissolution floor, or a `q` falling toward a desert floor) | a **shield/heal score** — anti-corruption sustained as the `ε²`/`q` hold (`energy-harnessing.md` §5.4), *ahead* of the crossing rather than in reaction to it | `field-music.md` §4.2 hazards; `coherence-magic.md` §2 Shield/Heal |
| **A desert about to creep** (a trough-trend in regional `q`) | a **Qi-bath-maintenance score** — the herd-chord / held-heal that keeps the region's `q` up through the thin (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.1 husbandry) | `field-music.md` §4.2 ecology; `energy-harnessing.md` §4.4 |
| **A basin about to leave** (a coherence ridge drifting out) | a **shepherd's-tune score** — a coherent phrase that raises `q` where the ridge is heading, drawing the basin / creatures / the biosphere toward the sustained coherence | `field-music.md` §4.2 ecology; `field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.1 |
| **A precipitation about to happen** (a `ρ` rising toward `τ_c`) | a **condense score** — authored at the moment the oracle flags the yield, pre-tuned to become a new regime | `field-music.md` §4.2 energy; `material-regimes.md` §3 |
| **A storm's `ε²` front approaching** | a **calm-maintained score** — the anti-corruption hold that brackets the growth (`field-music.md` §4.2 hazards), played *on the front's approach* | `field-hazards.md` §2.4 |

### 5.2 The honest scope — scores still cost, and the gate holds

Because a score *is a sequence of Q4 op records* (`field-music.md` §4.1), the
oracle changes **what the composer programs, never the cost of programming it**:

- **The no-free-energy gate holds unchanged** (`energy-harnessing.md` §6: no
  conversion yields more than it sinks, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`). The oracle is
  *information* — it tells the composer *when and where* a hold/heal is most
  likely to matter — but the hold/heal still spends its coherence cost. An
  oracle that "pre-empted" a crossing does not mint the coherence that did it;
  it *saved* the field from the crossing, which is a different and honest kind
  of value (§7d).
- **The forecast never removes the need to act.** Reading that the desert will
  creep does not stop it creeping; the score (anti-corruption at cost) is what
  holds it. The oracle is the composer's *planning instrument*, not a free
  solve.
- **A score's effect is still gated on phase-matching** (`field-music.md` §4.3:
  a score works because it is a deliberately organized, phase-timed
  perturbation, `M ≈ 1`). The oracle tells the composer *what* to target; the
  score still has to be played in phase to couple. An oracle-aided pre-emptive
  hold that isn't phase-matched is a forecast that recedes — the §4.3 honesty
  applied to the pre-emptive form.

This is the deepest tie of the whole corpus as the task names it: **the thesis
"intelligence is steering the flow of coherence" becomes, with the oracle, a
steering of the field *by its own forward tail*** — you are not just moving the
flow, you are *conversing with where it is already going*, the way resonance
(`coherence-magic.md` §3.1) teaches channeling *with* the field's natural
rhythm rather than against it. **Prophecy-in-composition is the strongest
resonance, played ahead.**

## 6. Player-facing — learning to read ahead, the omen economy, and the pulse

### 6.1 Learning the deep-read — the same reader, practiced forward

There is no new ability to unlock; there is a **mastery of the existing
reader**. `coherence-magic.md` §2 Sense renders q/ε²/∇(g·Φ) as a magnetometer;
progression is `rung mastery, not spell tiers` (§4). The deep-read is the same
reader's **rung in time**:

- **Base** — read the present: what is coherent here, what is a scar.
- **Practiced forward** — read the *trend*: which way is `q` moving, how close
  to the next threshold, is the `ε²` front approaching. This is the Phase-1
  momentum slice (§7a) — genuinely early-testable, a pure consumer.
- **Mastered** — read the scheduled omen on the tide's calendar: "this region
  will go thin this season", "a storm is three tides out", "the basin is
  leaving". This is the full oracle (§7b), gated on the tide probe and the
  archaeology/regime thresholds.

The pedagogy is the corpus's constant: **mastering the deep-read is learning
the field's dynamics**, not learning a spell. A player who can read ahead has
learned that the field *carries its own momentum* — the deepest lesson the
world teaches — and the omen economy is that lesson made playable.

### 6.2 The omen economy — plan the harvest, time the voyage

The corpus already makes timing a resource (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.3:
"when matters as much as where"). The oracle supercharges it by making the
timing **scheduled instead of felt**:

| Omen acted on | The planning it enables | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| **"This harvest will be lean"** | defer ore trips, Still-Room channeling, authoring to a *better* season — or husband the bath now to hold through the thin | `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2/§3.2; `material-regimes.md` §3 |
| **"The desert will creep this winter"** | anti-corruption *before* the trough, when it is cheapest and the field can still hold (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.1) | `field-hazards.md` §3.3; `energy-harnessing.md` §5.4 |
| **"A storm will come in three tides"** | shelter a region, marshal a calm-score, move the at-risk stock before the front | `field-hazards.md` §2.4; `field-music.md` §4.2 |
| **"Something precipitates here soon"** | position to harvest the yield; time the dig to the ore-fill | `material-regimes.md` §3 |
| **"The basin is leaving"** | move the herd / the settlement's fields toward where the ridge is heading | `field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.1; `field-npc-ai.md` §3 |

**The forecast is information, and information is the economy's rarest
currency.** `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5d is explicit that the tide's gift is
"information and timing, not energy" — the oracle extends that gift from the
present trend to the scheduled future, so the player plans the harvest and
times the voyage **on a forecast, not on guesswork**. The omen economy is the
player's version of the prophet's batting average (§4.4): a player who reads
ahead well makes better plans, and the plans are the payoff.

### 6.3 The narrative register — a world with true omens is a world with a pulse

`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §4 ends on "there is a field with a pulse, and the
pulse is the clock the whole tempo runs on." The oracle is that pulse **made
legible as meaning**:

- **The world does not surprise the prepared.** Because the field's extremes
  are all *readable-before-they-arrive* (`field-hazards.md` §5.1), the oracle
  is the corpus's pre-warning architecture completed: storm, desert, BH,
  bath-failure, basin-drift — every extreme has a scheduled omen, so the world
  stops being a sequence of ambushes and becomes a **place whose turning you
  can feel coming**, the way a culture reads the seasons.
- **Prophecy and science meet in one mechanism.** The village prophet does not
  roll dice; it reads momentum. The myth — "the old one saw the famine
  coming" — is *true*, and it is true because the old one is the village's
  deep reader, its sharpest eye on the field's forward tail. The folklore
  grows *out of* the honest forecast, not in spite of it. This is the truest
  Cassi flavor: **the omen is real, and the reality is what makes it a myth.**
- **A pulse suggests agency at a scale beyond the player.** A world whose
  weather, harvests, hazards, and ecology all turn on one shared rhythm, and
  whose warnings come from a respected reader, *feels* alive in a way a
  spawned-event world does not. The narrative register is deliberate: the
  corpus's "no monster table" (`field-hazards.md` §1) becomes "no ambush
  either" — the player lives in a world that *tells you what it is becoming*,
  and the telling is the prophecy, and the prophecy is the physics.

## 7. Honest gates

### (a) The momentum read extends the reader's known channels — Phase-1 slice

The **Phase-1 "what's building here" slice is early-testable as a pure
consumer of the publish**. The deep-read's base is the reader's own channels
(q/ε²/∇(g·Φ)/ρ, `corpus-reconciliation.md` ≈ 6 MiB, `coherence-magic.md` §2)
read for their **trend** — a time-difference over the published snapshot plus a
**threshold roster** (the condensation `τ_c = 0.5`, the desert's `q`-collapse
floor, the dissolution `ε²` floor, the BH's self-reinforcing point, the tide's
harvest/thin bands). None of this is new physics and none is a write: it is a
bounded sampler-side read inside the ≈ 1–6 ms/tick budget (the same budget the
hazards' detection already claims, `field-hazards.md` §6.3). **A player who
holds the reader and watches the trend is doing the Phase-1 oracle** — no new
instrument, the mastered-Sense form of §3.1. This is the honest, live,
early-testable slice and the whole design's proof-of-concept.

### (b) The full oracle gates on the residue/regime thresholds + the NPC read-primitives

The **scheduled-omen full oracle** (§3.3) and the **prophet** (§4) sit on gates
the corpus already owns:

- **The tide's tempo** — "a storm in three tides", "this harvest will be lean",
  "the desert will creep this winter" all speak *on the calendar*, and the
  calendar is the tide's mixing clock `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]`, whose measurability is
  **the open probe** of `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5a. Until the probe returns
  a real season, the oracle speaks in *trend drift* (Phase-1, §7a), not in
  calendar omens. **The scheduled omen is gated on the tide probe.**
- **The regime/desert/BH thresholds** — the oracle's threshold roster is the
  same set `field-hazards.md` §6.2 flags (where "a living plain" becomes
  "desert", where a BH's draw becomes self-reinforcing, what `ε²` budget merits
  a storm) and `material-regimes.md` §7 gates (per-cell `ω₀²`/ξ). The forecast
  is only as honest as the thresholds it crosses; those are Phase-1.5 tuning,
  gated before the oracle asserts a crossing. **[design]**
- **The prophet's read-primitives** — an NPC with a deep reader is an ordinary
  `field-npc-ai.md` NPC whose decision weights the forward-read; it rides every
  NPC gate (§4.5): engine-real primitives, hard determinism (§6d), the commons
  and the coherence budget (§6e). The *prophet persona* (the village role, the
  track record) is [design] on top, but the *mechanism* (a deterministic
  forward-read NPC) is Phase-1's §7a slice plus the `field-npc-ai.md` NPC.
- **The built oracle instrument** — a consumer of the publish with a forward
  idiom, honoring `field-instruments.md` §2.1's family rule; it cannot exceed
  the publish and it rides the Weatherglass's already-shipped base idiom. No
  new channel, ever.

### (c) The forecast-vs-fate honesty — the read never presents a fixed future

This is a **hard gate**, the load-bearing honesty of the whole design. The
oracle (player) and the prophet (NPC) **never present a fixed future**:

- **Determinism gives momentum, not destiny.** The read is "what is building",
  with the uncertainty of a forecast; a rally can recede, a crossing can move
  back, a bath can recover against the drift. A forecast is **falsifiable and
  can be wrong** — and being wrong is legible as *error* (the prophet's batting
  average, §4.4), never as *mystery washed over*.
- **No "fate" clause.** No omen is phrased or mechanically modeled as an
  unalterable outcome. The corpus's determinism (`field-archaeology.md` §1.2,
  `field-npc-ai.md` §2.3) forces the question and answers it: a deterministic
  field still leaves the player and NPC *agency* — their organized
  perturbations are themselves part of the field the oracle reads, so **the
  forecast is conditional on the momentum holding, and the player's own
  actions (anti-corruption, husbandry, a score) are exactly the perturbations
  that can change it.** Prophecy is a *conversation*, not a sentence.
- **The read never overreaches its horizon (§2.3).** No omen is emitted past a
  tempo the oracle can measure; the chaotic tail is silent. A design that
  violated this (a "fate" for a date it cannot read) would be the superstition
  this doc explicitly refuses.

### (d) No-free-energy — an omen is information, never energy

An omen has **no power to do work**. It is information — a read of the publish
— and information is not energy in this corpus's discipline:

- **The oracle costs nothing and does nothing** — it is a consumer of the
  already-published snapshot, the same read-only discipline as Sense
  (`coherence-magic.md` §5.1) and the Weatherglass (`field-instruments.md`
  §5a). It does not write.
- **Acting on an omen still pays.** A heal where a threshold is about to cross,
  a hold where a desert is about to creep, anti-corruption before the trough —
  every response spends its coherence at the unchanged cost (`energy-harnessing.md`
  §6: `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`). **The oracle saves the field from a crossing; it
  does not mint the coherence that prevented it.** The no-free-energy gate of
  `field-hazards.md` §5.3 and `field-music.md` §4.2 closes any oracle-farm: you
  cannot read a coming hazard, "cause" it, and harvest its drain — the write-
  back caps keep every conversion net-negative, and the pre-emption of a hazard
  is a *saving*, not a *mint*.
- **The prophet costs too.** A deep-reader NPC is a bath-funded member of the
  commons (§4.5) — its deep read is a maintenance cost. **Reading the future is
  not free for the reader either**; it is funded by the coherence of the very
  field it reads, which is itself one of the things it reads.

The honest through-line: **an omen is information; information is the
economy's rarest currency; but information never replaces energy, and the
constraint economy holds exactly as it holds for every field act.** The oracle
is the corpus's ultimate proof that the thesis holds even at the edge of
"seeing the future": it gives you *foresight*, and foresight is not power over
the law — it is the ability to spend your coherence where the law is already
going.

**Medium-late overall; the Phase-1 momentum slice is early-testable.**

**Phase-1 — the momentum read (early-testable, genuinely nothing new).** The
deep-read's base — reading the trend of the reader's own channels
(q/ε²/∇(g·Φ)/ρ) plus threshold-crossing detection over the published snapshot —

concept slice and the seed of the mastered-Sense form (§3.1)**, and it can ship
and be validated off the living-terrain demo before any other gate.

**Phase-1.5+ — the full oracle and the prophets, gated.** The **scheduled-
omen full oracle** (§3.3) and the **built oracle instrument** gate on the same
Phase-1.5 set the corpus already owns: the **tide probe** (`tide-of-the-
attractor.md` §5a — does the regional `q` oscillate at all? the calendar the
omen speaks on depends on it), the **residue/regime thresholds**
(`field-hazards.md` §6.2; `material-regimes.md` §7 — the threshold roster's
honesty), and — for the prophet — the **NPC read-primitives** (`field-npc-ai.md`
Phase-1: a deterministic forward-read NPC is a `field-npc-ai.md` NPC whose
decision weights the read, nothing more). The **prophet persona** (the village
role, the track record, the omen-comes-true verification) is [design] on the
Phase-1.5 mechanism.

**Later — the composition tie and the cross-window oracle.** The **oracle-
feeds-the-composer** (§5) is gated on `field-music.md`'s composition system,
which is itself gated on the Q4 player-return channel (`field-music.md` §7) —
a score is a Q4 op sequence. The **oracle-on-the-Far-Mirror** (reading
*another* window's becoming, §3.2) is gated on the Far Mirror's sparse far-
window publish (`field-instruments.md` §6; `world-seams.md` gate (b), Phase
2+).

**Binding risks, in order:** **(a)** the tide probe — the scheduled omen
speaks on the calendar only if the regional-`q` oscillation is real; until the
probe returns, the oracle is a drift-reading, not a calendar-reading (§7b);
**(b)** the threshold roster — the forecast's honesty is exactly the honesty
of the designed thresholds it crosses (`field-hazards.md` §6.2, `material-
regimes.md` §7), a Phase-1.5 tuning gate; **(c)** the forecast-vs-fate
discipline (§7c) — keeping the read never-fixed is a *hard design rule*, not a
nice-to-have, and it is the one place the design could tip into the
superstition it refuses; **(d)** the no-free-energy gate (§7d) — an omen must
never become a mint. None contradicts the async, dual-world, or regime-collapse
architecture — the oracle is a *consumer* of the publish, adding no channel,
no write, and no physics.

> **The honest statement that makes this doc load-bearing: the corpus reads the
> past (`field-archaeology.md`), the present (the reader, the sky, the
> instruments), and the distant present (the Far Mirror), but nothing read the
> future — and the future was available all along, because the field is
> deterministic and currently organizing, so the near-future is *emergent and
> readable*, the same law's forward tail. The oracle reads what is building;
> the prophet is the village's best forward reader, true because it reads
> momentum, never dice; the composer is fed what the field is organizing
> toward; and the world with true omens is a world with a pulse. The field is
> one law, read in both time directions — archaeology keeps the past, the
> oracle reads the becoming, and both are the same honest reading of a present
> that carries its own past and its own future in its organized state.**

## Open questions

1. **The horizon's length.** How far (in tide-periods / game time) can a read
   project before the momentum's uncertainty swamps the signal? The forward
   analog of `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5a's period measurement, and it sets
   how "scheduled" a scheduled omen can honestly be. Phase-1 calibration
   **[probe]**.
2. **The trend-vs-state classifier.** Does "rising `ε²` trend" robustly
   separate "an approaching storm's front" from "a nearby static scar whose `ε²`
   is elevated but not moving forward"? The forward twin of the live-vs-
   residue classifier (`field-archaeology.md` open-Q3) and the growl-vs-scar
   audio classifier (`field-music.md` open-Q2) — a trend needs a *moving*
   signal, not a static one. Can the trend read reliably distinguish them at
   the noise floor? Phase-1 probe.
3. **The prophet's effect on the outcome.** §4.3 states that a prophet whose
   omen the village acts on may be "heard" (the forecast changes the outcome).
   Is that a *design feature* (a good prophecy is one that alters the future
   it forecasts — planned, kept as the doc's myth-and-science convergence) or
   a *measurement hazard* (a village that over-trusts its prophet's lean-harvest
   omen stops planting and *causes* the lean it feared)? Both are real; which
   the game leans into is a design choice that the omen economy (§6.2) and the
   composer tie (§5) both feel.
4. **Far-oracle vs. Far-Mirror.** Should the oracle's trend-lens eventually
   compose onto the Far Mirror (reading *another* window's becoming, §3.2), and
   does that need a trend-history of a *sparse* far publish (the ~KB record of
   `world-seams.md` gate (b)) to be meaningful? Later; gated on the Far-Mirror
   and world-seams.
5. **The prophet's determinism vs. the field's changes.** `field-npc-ai.md`
   §6d makes the NPC a pure function of (Π pattern × channels). A prophet that
   re-reads and revises its omen because the field changed (the player acted,
   the momentum receded) is *deterministic* (same new input → same new omen),
   but the *revision itself* must be legible as honesty, not as wavering — the
   batting-average verification (§4.4) must weight revisions correctly or the
   "prophet is honest" claim erodes into "the prophet always moves the goal."
   A design decision, not a physics one.
