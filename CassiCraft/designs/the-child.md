## 2. The un-written line's freedom and cost

### 2.1 What a fresh line IS — the four empty surfaces

The Child is defined as much by its *emptiness* as its potential. Each empty
surface is the honest state of a landed piece, read before it has been written:

| Surface | The empty state | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| **The Board's book** | **no `C(M)` row** — a member who has never netted a contribution has no legible book; the first contribution is the first row | `shared-ledger.md` §1.2/§6b (the aggregation; the Phase-1 single-player slice renders one member's line — before the first op, that line is empty); §6c the landed member-id (the *id* exists; the *book* does not yet) |
| **The trace journal** | **no recorded phase** — the recorded-vs-live gap (`resonance-tutor.md` §2.2) is empty because there is no recorded self to gap against; the tutor has nothing to replay | `resonance-tutor.md` §1/§2.2/§7 open-Q2 (the gap; the empty journal) |
| **The mirror** | **a near-clean self** — the carried ε²-debt is zero, the signature is un-raised, the classification face reads live-but-idle | `the-burden.md` §1 (debt zero); `the-mirror.md` §2.4 (the classification face — at birth the child reads *live* and *clean*, the honest baseline) |
| **The family joint** | **no holding** — no hearth co-bound, no joint line `C(F)`; the child owes no joint's maintenance and holds no anchor's weight | `the-family.md` §2.1/§3 (the joint hold requires members already holding; the child is not yet a holder) |

These four are not absences to lament; they are the **honest start**. The corpus's
own determinism makes them *true*: same window, same field state, a fresh line's
first moment is reproducible (§6c).

### 2.2 The freedom — unbounded by a past

A fresh line's firsts are all *free* in a way no reborn or veteran line's are:

- **Every contribution, scar, and name is a first.** The child has no prior
  record to contradict, no carried debt to compound the vent back-up in thin
  (`the-burden.md` §2a), no willed claim to husband (`the-inheritance.md` §3.2),
  no family joint to maintain (`the-family.md` §3.3). It is the only line that
  can start a book *clean* — not amnestied (that is the no-amnesty rule,
  `schema-that-settles.md` §2.1.1, which governs *reborn* lines), but genuinely
  unwritten.
- **The tutor teaches a live hand, not a replay.** Because the journal is empty,
  the child's first lesson cannot be a re-enactment; it is the *founding* of the
  journal (§3.4). The child does not learn from a past self it has never been;
  it learns by *making* the first past hand it will one day feel.
- **The mirror reads clean, honestly.** There is no misattribution risk at birth
  (`the-burden.md` §5 — the world cannot be wrong about a child *yet*, because
  there is no carried weight to misread). The near-clean self is the baseline the
  whole interior stack measures from.

### 2.3 The cost — unknown

Freedom is priced in the only currency the field has: **legibility**. A child is
*unknown*, and the corpus's two social reads both need history:

| The read | Why history is required | The child's cost |
|---|---|---|
| **The election** (`the-election.md` §2/§2.4) | a steward is chosen by reading **patterns over many tides** — running `C(M)`, stewardship history, momentum; "never a single event," never a single tide | the child cannot yet be *read* as a candidate — it has no many-tide pattern to compose; its first contribution is a first row, not a stewardship line |
| **The family joint** (`the-family.md` §3) | a joint hold requires members *already holding* a shared anchor | the child has no record of holding, so the joint has nothing to bind to it; it is not yet a co-holder |
| **The Board's recognition** (`shared-ledger.md` §3.1) | "X is this tide's steward" is the ledger's honest sum of repeated ops | an empty row reads as *nothing*, not as a negative — the child is neither steward nor margin-causer, just unbooked |

The honest shape of the cost, stated once:

> **A child is unbounded by a past and unknown without one.** Its freedom is the
> absence of weight; its cost is the absence of record. The firsts (§3) are the
> bridge: each one *buys* the legibility the stack's social faces require — the
> first contribution starts the election-readable history, the first name starts
> the anchored things, the first trace starts the journal a future self (or the
> township) can read. The child's whole arc is the movement from **unknown to
> known**, made in the field's own bookkeeping terms.

**[design]** The framing of the child as "unknown until it writes a first" is this
doc's designed reading of the landed Board/identity stack; the *records* are
real. No claim here says the engine evaluates "newness" or "unknownness" — it is
what an empty row and an empty journal *mean*, read honestly.

## 3. The firsts — the whole stack, experienced for the first time

The design's texture is that **the corpus's entire stack happens, in miniature,
in the order a fresh line performs it.** Each first is a designed event grounded
in a landed piece, and together they fix the *order* of the fresh line's
books (§5's [design]).

### 3.1 The first contribution — the Board's first row

`shared-ledger.md` §1.2 aggregates a member's net contribution over a tide:
`C(M) = Σ ops (net field-contribution …) budgeted against the bath's Δq/Δε²`.
For a child, the first contribution is the **first row in that book**:

> **[design] The first contribution is the book's first row.** The first op the
> child nets against the bath — a condense at a coherent site, a heal into a
> margin, a maintained shield (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2's contribution
> disposition) — *begins* the child's `C(M)` line. Before it, the book is empty;
> after it, the child has a legible stake in the commons' high-`q` core
> (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4). It is the first movement from unknown to known
> (§2.3): the row the election will one day read as a steward's line's seed.

The Phase-1 legibility: against the landed member-id (`schema-that-settles.md`
§2.1) and the Board's Phase-1 aggregation slice (`shared-ledger.md` §6b — render
one member's contribution from the first windowed demo), **a fresh line's empty
book and first contribution are designable and demoable now** (§6b).

### 3.2 The first scar — the burden's first honest entry

`the-burden.md` §1: every channeled op sheds ε² that accumulates on the player as
a carried debt; `coherence-magic.md` §4.3: overdraw is the full-cascade
discharge. The child's first mistake is not a failure of the design — it is the
**first entry in the burden's honest journal**:

> **[design] The first scar is a first mistake, honestly entered.** The child's
> first vent past its own capacity — an early overdraw, a condense at an
> overfull site that sheds where it should heal — writes the *first row of its
> carried ε²*. The honest registers: (a) it is **small** (a child's carried debt
> is a first op's shed, not a veteran's habit — `the-burden.md` §1's debt scale
> grows with practice); (b) it **clears in a Still Room's patient time**
> (`the-burden.md` §2b), so the first scar is the *first lesson in repayment*;
> (c) it is **teachable** — the child's near-clean self (`the-mirror.md` §2.4)
> reads a small, legible debt it can name and clear, the exact lesson the
> maintenance axis teaches (`life-signal.md` §3). **A child that never scars is a
> line that never acts; the design's texture is that a scar is the first honest
> entry, read as teachable, never as shame.**

**[design]/[probe]** The first scar's *legibility* rides `the-burden.md`'s
Phase-1-testable carried-debt read (§6a) and the mirror's classification face; its
*existence* is just the vent physics — engine-real.

### 3.3 The first name — the beginning of anchored things

`the-name.md` §1: to name a thing is to bind a persistent-Π anchor to it (§2
— you cannot name falsely). `the-inheritance.md` §2.2: a name you leave is a
structure a future run can re-lock. The child's first name is the **beginning of
its anchored things**:

> **[design] The first name is the child's first binding.** The first thing the
> child names — a kept tool, a first hearth-stone, a structure it holds — binds a
> Π-anchor to it (`the-name.md` §1), giving the fresh line its first *thing-that-
> is-its-own* against the field's indifference. It is the first token of
> stewardship: a named thing must be husbanded or it decays (`the-name.md` §2.2;
> `the-inheritance.md` §2.2), so the first name is the child's first commitment
> and its first honest test. It is also the seed of the child's share of the
> family's hearth (`the-family.md` §2.2 — the anchor binds to a real structure),
> the first structure a future will can mark.

**[design]** The order — name after a held thing exists, never before (you cannot
name falsely) — is `the-name.md`'s own bound, inherited unchanged. The first
name's mechanical anchor is LATER (gated on `the-name.md` §6b's frontier); the
*principle* is Phase-1-legible ($6b).

### 3.4 The first trace — the tutor's founding of the journal

`resonance-tutor.md` §1/§2.2: a trace is the Q4 op-record; the tutor replays a
recorded phase as a gap to close; the recorded-vs-live gap is empty without a
recorded self. `the-apprenticeship.md` §1: a veteran's live hand is the in-person
target. The child's first lesson resolves the empty journal:

> **[design] The tutor's first task is not teaching but founding the journal.**
> Because the child has no recorded phase, the first lesson cannot be a
> re-enactment — it **records the live hand**: the child performs its first op,
> and that op's Q4 record (`resonance-tutor.md` §1; `schema-that-settles.md`
> §2.1) becomes the *first entry* of its own journal. The first past hand is
> **recorded live, not replayed**. The next lesson — a day or a season later —
> has a recorded self to gap against, and the tutor's phase-gap (§2.2) starts to
> mean. **The founding-lesson is the moment the journal goes from empty to
> founded: the child stops being the un-written line and gains a past of its
> own, made in the field's own record.** The apprenticeship is the natural first
> teacher (`the-apprenticeship.md` §1 — a veteran's live hold gives the child a
> phase to feel inside, and the shelter's bounded error floor makes the first
> scar safe to make).

**[design]** The *founding* (the first record becomes the journal's first entry)
rides the trace-journal's domain-side publish (`reason-field.md` §4.2) — gated on
that, LATER; the *principle* (a first past hand is a live recording, not a
replay) is Phase-1-legible against the landed Q4 record.

### 3.5 The order — the fresh line's books, fixed

The firsts are not arbitrary; they compose in the honest order the corpus's own

| Order | First | The book it opens | The dependency that fixes the order |

| **4** | **First name** | the anchored things | requires a held structure to name (cannot name falsely, `the-name.md` §2) |

The first contribution and the first trace are the *same first op* — it books on

## 4. The settlement's children

### 4.1 The joint's heirs — the hearth's future lines

`the-family.md` §3.2: the anchor survives one member's death — the family line
outlasts the individual line "the way a name outlives a body." §4: the family is
the commons' first rung. The honest consequence for a settlement is its children:

> **A settlement's children are the joint's future lines — the fresh runs that
> will hold the hearth when the veterans' lines end.** The family's anchor
> outlives any one member (§3.2); what keeps it alive *across generations* is
> the fresh lines that come up to co-hold it. A child is the **heir the joint
> does not yet co-bind but will**: its first name (§3.3) starts the anchored
> things; its first contribution (§3.1) books it into the commons; and when it
> co-binds a hearth with another line (`the-family.md` §2.1), it becomes a
> co-holder — the joint's *next* generation. The veteran's will written for this
> line is `the-inheritance.md` §2.2's bequest with a future target: **a will can
> be written *for* a child that does not exist yet** — the bequest's target, the
> future co-holder of the hearth, is exactly a line whose book is empty now and
> will be the anchor's holder later.

### 4.2 The next stewards — the read that will one day read them

`the-election.md` §2/§2.4: a steward is chosen by reading patterns over many
tides — a history is needed to read a candidate. The child's first tides are the
**seed of that read**:

> **The child's first tides are the history the election will one day read.** The
> election cannot read a fresh line today (§2.3's cost); it *will* read it
> tomorrow, because a child's contributions, scars, and clearings accumulate into
> the many-tide pattern the read requires (§2.4 — patterns over many tides, never
> one event). A settlement that teaches its children well books them as the next
> steward's line early: anti-corruption held against a margin (`the-election.md`
> §2.2's stewardship acts) learned in the apprenticeship (§3.4), practiced as a
> first contribution (§3.1), read as a growing stewardship line a season from
> now. **The child is not only the joint's heir; it is the settlement's
> steward-to-be — the read's future subject, made legible by the books it writes
> now.**

### 4.3 The honest frame — continuity as a designed act

The settlement's response to its own beginning is the design's whole payoff,
stated once:

> **A settlement that welcomes and teaches its children is a settlement that will
> outlast its founders — the corpus's continuity made a designed act.** The
> identity stack designed persistence everywhere *about things* — fossils,
> traces, wills, anchors, lines. None designed the *next people*: the fresh runs
> that will carry the hearth and sit for the steward's read when the veterans'
> lines end. The Child closes that. A settlement's children are its future — and
> the field-honest form of that truth is bookkeeping: a fresh line is welcomed
> because its firsts begin the books that will elect it, hold the hearth it will
> one day co-hold, and pass the craft it will one day hand down. The window that
