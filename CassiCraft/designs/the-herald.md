# The Herald: The Announcing Stranger — The Being Whose Presence Is an Audible Augury, the Field's Own Voice Reading Its News Ahead

**Question under design:** the stranger-object layer has eight named faces, and
every one is a *relation to what is present*. The signature-predator **hunts** a
clean trail ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §1), the blight
**turns** a region's life ([`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md) §1), the feral
**wakes** a kept thing ([`the-feral-instrument.md`](./the-feral-instrument.md) §1),
the scavenger **feeds** the spent ([`the-scavenger.md`](./the-scavenger.md) §1),
the commensal **holds** a patch ([`the-commensal.md`](./the-commensal.md) §1),

**keeps** a named place ([`the-guardian.md`](./the-guardian.md) §1). **None

moment *in silence* ([`the-witness.md`](./the-witness.md) §1); the reading-ahead

living version of what the bell does mechanically ([`the-bell.md`](./the-bell.md) §1),
**but of the field, not of a settlement**. It is the stranger-object layer's
**ninth face, the announcer** — the field's own news-carrier, the witness's loud

*strange* — the beings the field produces that a settlement must read and answer.

  Herald feeds nothing and provides nothing** — it is not a metabolizing run; it
  is a being whose *announcing* is its entire act (§3).
- **Not a companion that holds a patch.** The commensal lives beside the order
  and steadies it ([`the-commensal.md`](./the-commensal.md) §3). The Herald holds

  held *without intent* — it does nothing but be there ([`the-witness.md`](./the-witness.md) §1/§2). **The Herald is the witness's loud twin**: the same

- **Not a keeper that holds a place.** The Guardian *keeps* a named place

  not bound to a place, it holds no order, it turns no trail toward defense. Its

**The through-line of the ninth face:** every prior face is the field doing

## 2. The announcement — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 A being bound to the field's own dynamics — the morphology

structure that crossed the recognition threshold** — the same organism-class
admission every stranger-object with a body inherits
([`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md) §6b):

> A field structure is recognized as a candidate organism when it sustains, for a
> minimum window `T_org`, all three of: **(1) coherence band** — local `q` inside
> `[q_low, q_high]` held above a floor, set between `φ⁻² ≈ 0.382` and `τ_c = 0.5`;
> **(2) decoherence ceiling** — `ε²` stays below a floor (it is *holding* the
> φ-lock); **(3) size / rung-depth** — at least 2–3 distinct cascade layers
> ([`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md) §6b, cited).

The Herald is such a structure — a maintained live coherence (moves, its `(1−q)`
glow pulses at the maintainer's cadence, `ε²` held low;

while its coherence band holds ([`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md) §2.2/§4.2). **No new physics, no new channel**: the Herald is an
organism-class run — the same morphology the scavenger, commensal, mirror, and
witness read on the Life-Signal — with one designed addition. **[design]** The
addition is the **announcing**: the Herald also *reads the field's channels
