— a time-lens over engine-real quantities — and the honest boundary (what is

## 1. The two clocks, stated once

The design rests on exactly two clocks, and the entire feel of the game is the
difference between them.

**The field's local tempo.** The theory's mixing clock is the attractor's
relaxation cadence (`qi-as-time-clock.md`; cited in `tide-of-the-attractor.md`
§1.1):

```
T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]        the n-independent mixing/re-conversion period
λ_mix = λ(1−q)         the openness gate (§2.2): the conversion rate
```

The mixing rate `λ_mix = λ(1−q)` is **high where the field is far from
φ-equilibrium (`q → 0`) and suppressed where the field is coherent (`q → 1`)**.
Where the field is organized, it is *patient*: its re-conversion runs slowly,
so its coherence economics — the harvest, the strata-lifetime, the desert's
creep — play out over long, deliberate arcs. Where the field is decoherent, its
clock races. **The field's tempo is a local quantity: it depends on the local
`q` at each place.**

**The player's tick.** The player's clock is a constant that does *not* depend on
the field. `dt = 0.05` — **one field step per Minecraft tick at 20 Hz**
(`chunk-field-quantization.md` §1.2; `corpus-reconciliation.md`). The server
tick-sampler samples the async domain's *published* snapshot on a cadence, not
per tick (`async-field-domain.md` §2.3 — "the field does not hurry"). The player
is the window's **fastest reader**: a bounded coherence source (`coherence-magic.md`
§1) sampling the publish at 20 Hz, channeling on the tick.

> **The two clocks.** The field's local tempo `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]` is theory —
> a *proposed* reading whose `(1−q)` variation the live sim must confirm (§5a).
> The tick and the publish cadence are **engine-real** (canonical). **Where the
> two differ is the feel**: the field is patient — its coherence economics play
> out over tides and strata-lifetimes; the player is the field's fastest reader —
> the only thing in the window that can outrun its own local tempo.

The design statement, in one line:

> **The field is patient, and the player is the only thing in it that is not.**
> This doc makes that difference *the feel of the game* — a designed time-lens
> over the engine-real tempo difference, presented in the field's own idiom
> (the Weatherglass, the sky, the tutor, the stethoscope), never as an engine
> that "slows time."

The honesty, stated once: the **mixing clock** and the local-tempo *variation* are
theory (`qi-as-time-clock.md` is a speculation, flagged as such); the **tick**,
the **publish cadence**, and **`dt = 0.05`** are engine-real; the **presentation
of the difference** — the feelings, the loans, the patience — is **[design]** over
engine-real tempo. The one designed mechanical choice (whether gameplay actually
keys on the local `(1−q)`) is the Phase-1.5 probe of §5(b).

## 2. The patient field, concretely

The field's patience is not one mood; it is the *same* patience expressed across
the corpus's own time-scales. Each of the four below names a tempo, grounds it in
its source doc, and names the *feeling* the time-lens makes legible.

### 2.1 The tide — tempo: the world's shared season

The tide is the mixing clock `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]` *given a face*
(`tide-of-the-attractor.md`): a long cycle where a region's operating coherence
`q` breathes between **harvest** (organized: high `q`, low `ε²`, cheap machines,
ore precipitating, the biosphere's basins at full strength) and **thin** (q
troughs: machines wasteful off the `(1−q)` floor, organisms shed, the
desert-margin creeps). It is a **pacing lens** — no new engine term — over an
attractor relaxation the field is already living.

The time-lens reading: **the tide is the patient field's shared season.** The
player's *day* is the tide's *blink*. A player who has played one session has
lived a fraction of one season's arc; the tide completes over tides and
multiple sessions, and every system in the corpus — machines, ecology, hazards,
archaeology — runs *against* it as the outer clock (`tide-of-the-attractor.md`
§2's regime table).

> **The feeling the time-lens makes legible: awe.** The whole living world shares
> one large-scale tempo the player can *plan around* but not *set* — husbandry
> with a clock (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.1). The player's timing becomes a
> first-class resource ("when" matters as much as "where"), and learning to read
> the season — from the reader's trend, the sky's aurora, and the `(1−q)` glow —
> is learning the patient field's patience.

Honesty inherited unchanged: the tide's *period* is a [probe] (does the box's
regional `q` oscillate? `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5a), and a "season length" is
a **[design]** dial, not a physics constant. The patient field's awe does not
depend on a confirmed oscillation — even a monotone drift toward organized is a
tempo worth feeling (§5b).

### 2.2 The strata — memory: the field's residue at rung-depth

The field's memory is **structural, not temporal** (`qi-computation.md` §5.2:
persistent Π patterns — configurations at attractor-potential local minima that
persist while ambient `q` stays above dissolution; §5.1 the one-rung IIR is the
honest limit). Field archaeology reads that residue: the deep-rung core + ε²
ghost halo, the strata as condensation history, the fossil as the deep-rung
skeleton of a dissolved organism, the birth-strata as the window's origin layer
(`field-archaeology.md` §2).

The time-lens reading: **the strata are the patient field's memory accumulated.**
A player's *lifetime* is the strata's *instant*. What the player does today —
die, carve, plant, reap — is one layer in a stratigraphy that spans the window's
whole condensation history, readable at rung-depth the way the cosmology reads
powder patterns at block scale.

> **The feeling the time-lens makes legible: smallness.** The player's deeds do
> not vanish; they stratify. The player's own deaths add a *player* stratum —
> a well-traveled window is archaeology of itself
> (`player-remains.md` §4.1). The field's patience is that it *keeps* what the
> player is too fast to see accumulate — the residue holds the deep past the
> player's lifetime cannot outlast. The awe of §2.1 flips here to the humility
> that the player is one fold in a field that remembers at rung-depth.

### 2.3 The pocket — scale covariance: time inside is not time outside

The recursion carries the *tempo* down. The mixing clock is n-independent
(`qi-time-ladder-derivation.md` §7 — exponent 0), so a pocket does not "run
faster by being smaller" on the mixing clock; its *own tide* is the same clock at
a smaller extent (`pocket-cosmos.md` §1.1). A pocket is a window *grown inward*:
its `(1−q)` runs at its own local value, so **time inside a pocket is not time
outside** — the pocket's clock sleeps (or races) at its own coherence, even as it
shares the parent's tempo.

The time-lens reading: **a pocket's clock runs at its own `(1−q)`.** A player who
enters a pocket and finds its desert is learning the parent's laws at thumb-size
(`pocket-cosmos.md` §4.3) — including the field's temporal patience, held in
miniature.

> **The feeling the time-lens makes legible: belonging.** The pocket is the same
> law *smaller* — the recursion is scale covariance, a place you grow into across
> a seam (`pocket-cosmos.md` §3), not a portal. The patience of the field does not
> scale away; it repeats at every rung. Holding a pocket is holding proof that
> the field's patience is one law at every scale — and the player who recurses
> finds themselves *inside* the field's own temporality, the deepest belonging the
> time-lens offers.

### 2.4 The run — life: an organism's lifetime is the field's patience

An organism is a **run the field holds**: a transient coherence configuration the
field actively maintains, ending when the ε² drain outruns the φ-lock — "matter is
conserved, only the configuration is lost" (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2). A
creature's lifetime *is* the field's patience at that creature's rung — the span
the field is willing to hold that particular lock against the drain.

The time-lens reading: **life is the patient field lending its patience to a
moving coherence store.** A bird's flight, a shell's layers, a herd's chord
(`field-music.md` §2.4 — "a herd is a chord") are each the field holding a run
for as long as its local coherence permits. The player's own body is the *same*
object at a deeper rung (`player-remains.md` §1.1) — the field's fastest reader
is also a run the field holds, and its lifetime is the patient field's gift of
patience to the player's rung.

> **The feeling the time-lens makes legible: urgency, tempered by belonging.** The
> run is finite — the field will, sooner or later, let it go. But *because* the
> field is patient, the run is not a countdown; it is an *act* the field is
> choosing to hold. The player's urgency (the §2.1 awe, the §2.2 smallness) is
> softened by the knowledge that the field's patience is what is holding *them*
> — the fastest reader is still a held run, and its lifetime rides the same
> `q`/`ε²` economics every organism does.

## 3. The player as the field's fastest reader

### 3.1 The power — and what the power is

The player's speed is not a power in the spell-slot sense; it is a **field fact**.
The player is a bounded EY injector + ε² vent + a reader of the published
channels (`coherence-magic.md` §1), sampling at 20 Hz. That is the fastest read in
the window — the field's own tempo (theory-cited, §5) relaxes over tides and
strata-lifetimes, but the player's tick does not wait on it.

**Channeling is the power that borrows the field's patience.** Every ability is
one field operation at a chosen rung (`coherence-magic.md` §2): condense, dissolve,
steer, ignite, shield/heal. To *make the world move at the player's speed* — a
wind, a shield, a heal, a door — the player spends coherence budget `B`
(`coherence-magic.md` §1.2). The honest frame:

> **The player's speed is a loan against the local tempo.** Channeling spends
> coherence to compress what the field would do slowly, if at all, into the
> player's 20 Hz present. **You can hurry the field only where you have coherence,
> and the field's patience is what you spend.**

The three abilities that are the clearest loans:
- **Condense** (`coherence-magic.md` §2) — raise `ρ` past `τ_c` where `q` is high:
  the field's patience would precipitate matter slowly at its own cadence; the
  player hurries it into an instant, at budget `B`.
- **Shield / Heal** (§2) — suppress local `ε²`, restore the φ-lock: the field
  heals *by its nature* (anti-corruption is how it recovers); the player spends
  coherence to make the healing happen *now*, in their window, not over the
  field's patient arc.
- **Steer** (§2) — inject a current along `∇(g·Φ)`: the player makes matter move
  at their chosen speed, borrowing the field's patience to direct the river rather
  than ride its own slow flow.

### 3.2 The cost — the loan must be repaid

The loan is never free, and never a mint. Two honest repayment channels, both
inherited:

| Repayment | Mechanism | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| **Recovery is geographic** | the budget `B` refills toward 1 only where the *local* field is coherent — standing in a high-`q` region pulls `B` up; standing in a carved / ε²-rich scar keeps it depressed. **The player who borrows the field's patience to act fast must return to the field's patience to recover** — the loan is repaid by standing where the field is slow and organized. | `coherence-magic.md` §1.2/§3 |
| **The ε² vent** | every channeled op sheds decoherence the field must heal around; overdraw (spending past `B`, or sustaining a deep rung past the vent's capacity) tips the injection from organized to random perturbation and risks a full-cascade discharge. **The field's patience is repaid as the disorder the player leaves it to heal** — and the field heals at *its* pace, not the player's. | `coherence-magic.md` §1.1/§1.2/§4.3 |

The no-free-energy rule holds the whole thing: every conversion yields at most
`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` (`energy-harnessing.md` §6 — amplitude caps in the write-back
lane), so **borrowing patience is a loan, never a mint.** There is no way to use
channeling to *outrun* the field permanently — every act that hurries the field
leaves the player slower to recover (geographic) and the field's locality more
wasteful (the vent), so the net effect is the player repaying the patience they
borrowed.

> **The honest design statement.** Channeling is *borrowing the field's patience*
> — spending coherence to make the world move at the player's speed, at the cost
> of geographic recovery and the ε² vent. The player is the field's fastest reader
> *because* they are willing to spend the field's patience for speed; the field is
> patient *because* it holds the coherence the player's speed runs on.

### 3.3 The tide as the loan's physical limit

The loan is not available equally everywhere. `coherence-magic.md` §1.2 makes
recovery geographic, and the tide (the patient field's shared season) sets the
*yield* of that geography: at **harvest** the vent is small (the field heals
fast — the loan is cheap and repays quickly); at **thin** the vent backs up (the
field heals slowly — the loan is dear, and overdraw risk rises)
(`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2, magic row). So the player's speed is *priced per
season*: **the same channeled act costs more patience at thin tide and less at
harvest** — the loan's interest rate is the field's own patience, which swells
when the field is organized and bites when it is not. A player who has learned to
read the phase knows *when* their speed is cheap to borrow.

### (d) The no-free-energy cap

Borrowing patience is a **loan, never a mint**. Every channeled hurry —
condense, shield, steer, heal — is bounded by the coherence budget
(`coherence-magic.md` §1.2) and by `energy-harnessing.md` §6's amplitude caps

   channeling does not *recover* the spent patience faster by channeling more —
   recovery is geographic (`coherence-magic.md` §1.2), so the loan is repaid by

   timing*, never a source.
3. **The loan's repayment is a real vent.** Every hurry leaves the `ε²` the field

   the field's healing by spending more; overdraw is the hard ceiling that ends
