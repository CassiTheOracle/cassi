## 1. The Marsh, stated once

The corpus's water has a character. [`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b designs the
sea as **a flat coherence plain** — a uniform low-drain region crossed as a
patient act, where a uniform `q` hides the ridges and the fast-reader's
instruments read *less*. The swim ([`the-swim.md`](./the-swim.md)) gives a body's
act in that plain its cost and its craft. The river, the flood, the sea-floor
complete the liquid family. **None designs the shallow.** Between the sea's
carrying plain (deep, uniform, read less) and the land's stepping ground (dry,
visible, read fully), there is a band the corpus never draws: water too thin to
be a plain, too wet to be land — where the uniformity that defines the sea gives
out against the shallow bottom, and texture returns.

The Marsh is that band, stated once and kept:

> **The Marsh is the slow sea — the shallow, textured water that hides — a
> low-flow, low-depth band of the liquid regime where `q` is *patchy*, the sea's
> uniformity replaced by a fine-grained tessellation.** Where the sea reads
> uniformly (a flat plain, a uniform `q`, the reduced reads — `the-sea.md` §2b),
> the marsh is where that uniformity **breaks into texture**: the water thins
> toward the land, the liquid regime's leveling gives out against the shallow
> bottom's structure, and the field's flat `q` resolves into small coherent
> hummocks and thin pools — a tessellated surface, the sea's plain read at its
> shallow edge. And with the texture comes the corpus's first honest *hiding*
> surface: **a marsh hides what the sea shows** — the Coda's trail-read, the
> watcher's clean signature, the walker's ε² wake all diffuse in the marsh's
> patchy `q`, the pattern does not carry, and the hiding is the field's own — the
> tessellated `q` making the signature illegible, honestly, at its texture, never
> a hidden channel.

**Why this is the corpus's missing wetland, honestly.** Every other water the
corpus builds is a *uniform* thing — the sea a flat plain, the river a coherent
filament, the flood an overflow, the sea-floor a boundary. What the corpus has
never designed is the water that is **not uniform** — the wetland whose whole
character is that the field's order is *patchy* there, small coherent bits
against wet disordered gaps. The sea hides the *ridges* (uniform `q` gives the
instruments nothing to lean on); the marsh is the first water that hides
*itself* — the first hiding surface, the first place a creature can be unseen
and unseeable, and the hiding is honest, the field's own texture and never a
mechanic.

The honest scope, stated once: **the sea's regime, the trail-read, the
maintenance axis, the wake, and the ecology are their source docs' landed
pieces; the marsh's composition — the tessellated-`q` form, the hiding, the
one-sided-hiding honesty — is this doc's [design] over them, probe-calibrated.**
The Marsh never claims the engine "has marsh water"; it reads the liquid
regime's published `q` at its shallow, patchy edge and presents the tessellation
as a hiding surface — a consumer of the ≈ 6 MiB publish exactly as the sea and
the swim are, never a new channel, never a write.

## 2. The tessellated q — where the sea's uniformity breaks into texture

**The landed pieces.** [`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b names the sea's defining
read: **a flat coherence plain** — a uniform low-drain `q` across a body of
water (the liquid regime's leveling, material §2's "leveling is the emergent
minimizer of the ε² budget", flattens to a uniform surface) — with the honest
consequence that a **uniform `q` hides the ridges**: the fast-reader's
instruments read *less*, the Lantern lies flat, the Mirror reads you against the
plain. The sea-floor describes the plain's **edge** — where the uniformity first
*breaks* against the deep's structure (the shelf, `the-sea-floor.md` §2a). The
marsh's starting point is the **shallow** edge, the plain's uniformity giving
out against the *shallow bottom* rather than the deep — and what the shelf does
once at the deep's boundary, the marsh does everywhere across a shallow band.

**The [design] over them — the marsh is the sea's regime, taken shallow and
textured:**

> **The Marsh is a low-flow, low-depth band of the liquid regime.** The liquid
> regime is `ρ ≥ θ_c` with `ε²` above the solid floor ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md)
> §2 — dense yet decohered, flows); the marsh is that regime in its shallow,
> slow, near-the-land form — too thin to hold the open plain's uniform low-drain
> `q`, too wet to be dry stepping ground, the water leveling toward the land the
> way the sea levels across its body ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b) but with
> the shallow bottom's structure breaking the flatness point by point.
> **Low-flow**: the marsh is not a moving-conduit filament (that is the river's
> high-`q` band, `the-sea.md` §2a) and not the resting plain; it is the sea's
> slow, shallow water, the filament at its lowest, slowest edge. **Low-depth**:
> the marsh is the plain's shallow shelf, the liquid regime thinning toward the
> land until it stops being water.

**The tessellated `q` — the marsh's form, this doc's [design] core:**

> **Where the sea is uniform, the marsh is *patchy* — the deep's hidden ridges
> surface here.** On the open plain, a uniform `q` hides the ridges
> ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b) — the site-map's organized rises, the
> gradient the wave hides, all flattened away by leveling. In the marsh, the
> leveling **gives out against the shallow bottom**: the water is thin enough
> that the terrain's structure under it — the same organized ridges the sea's
> plain concealed — presses back up into the field. The marsh's `q`-map is
> therefore **tessellated**: small **coherent hummocks** (the shallow ridges'
> tops where the water holds a local high-`q` patch, an edge to organize
> against) and thin **pools** (the water between, lower-`q`, wet but not
> coherent) tile the surface — the sea's uniform plain read at its texture, the
> site-map's ridges surfacing through the shallows (`the-sea-floor.md` §2a's
> shelf at the shallow scale, `chunk-field-quantization.md` §5's organized
> ridges as the hummocks' blueprint).

**The marsh's form is [design], probe-calibrated.** The *tessellation scale* —
how large a coherent hummock, how wide a thin pool, what the fine-grained
patchiness's characteristic size is — is **this doc's [design] dial**, set over
the engine-real `q` channel at the water's shallow edge and **probe-calibrated on
the Phase-1 slice** (§8): whether a bounded shallow band of the liquid regime
actually reads as small coherent hummocks and pools (vs. smearing back to a
thinner uniform plain) is the Phase-1 measurement against `the-sea.md` §5b's
uniform-plain probe. What is landed is that the uniform plain hides the ridges
([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b) and that the plain has an edge where its
uniformity breaks ([`the-sea-floor.md`](./the-sea-floor.md) §2a); **the marsh's
composition — that the break's shallow form is a *fine-grained tessellation* of
hummocks and pools — is this doc's [design]** over those landed pieces, flagged
as such and never asserted as engine-verbatim.

## 3. The hiding — a marsh hides what the sea shows

This is the load-bearing design. **The marsh's hiding is the honest consequence
of its tessellated `q`**: the same pattern-loss the sea's uniformity produces for
the *ridges* (a uniform `q` hides them, [`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b), the
marsh's patchiness produces for *motion itself* — the signature, the trail, the
wake all diffuse in the texture.

**The landed pieces.** The corpus's visibility economy rests on three reads, each
from its source doc:
- **The Coda's trail-read** ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §1/§2): a
  clean signature — an `ε²` pocket with a coherent phase, or a sustained clean
  presence — is legible *only where the field has no order to hide it* (§1.2):
  in a coherent ridge the trail drowns in ambient order, in a scar it is
  noise-on-noise, **in the desert's flat disorder it is the one bright organized
  thing**, the only legible hunt-trail.
- **The watcher's clean signature** ([`the-vigil.md`](./the-vigil.md) §3): a sustained
  low-ε² presence keeps a clean signature — the *most exposed* quiet, the clean
  thing the Coda accumulates on (`R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability`,
  [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §2.2).
- **The walker's ε² wake** ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2c): every crossing
  forges a faint trail — the walker's own `ε²` shed along the route, legible in
  the margins, gone behind you; in a desert a clean crossing is the only
  organized thing in flat orderlessness.

**The [design] over them — the marsh's tessellated `q` diffuses all three:**

> **A marsh hides what the sea shows — the Coda's trail-read, the watcher's
> clean signature, the walker's `ε²` wake all *diffuse* in the marsh's
> tessellated `q`: the pattern does not carry.** On the sea's uniform plain, the
> reads are *reduced but ordered* — the plain hides the ridges, but the flat `q`
> itself reads clean and even, so a signature on it stands out as the one
> structure ([`the-swim.md`](./the-swim.md) §2b, read at the water). In the
> marsh, there is no flat background for a signature to contrast against: the
> `q`-map is already a fine-grained texture of hummocks and pools, so a trailing
> `ε²` pocket, a clean maintained presence, or a walker's wake is **one texture
> among many** — it blur into the patchiness the way the sea's uniformity blurs
> the ridges, but where the sea hides the *terrain's* ridges, the marsh hides
> *motion itself*. **The pattern does not carry** — the trail-read's "the one
> bright organized thing in flat orderlessness" finds *no orderless flat*: every
> low-`q` pool is a small bit of apparent disorder, every hummock a small bit of
> order, and a signature between them is neither bright-against-flat (the
> trail-read's condition, `signature-predator.md` §1.2) nor clean-against-plain
> (the watcher's exposure, `the-vigil.md` §3). **The marsh absorbs the wake a
> walker sheds** (`the-walk.md` §2c's faint trail) — the tessellated `q` swallows
> it into the texture the way the desert's flat silence made it the *only* thing,
> inverted: the marsh's patches make it *nothing particular*.

**The honest frame — the hiding is the field's own, never a channel.**

> **The marsh is not a stealth mechanic; it is the field's tessellated `q` making
> the signature illegible, honestly.** The hiding is not a hidden channel that
> "conceals the player" — it is the *published* `q` read at its texture: the same
> `q`-map the Weatherglass renders, read as patchy; the same `ε²` the reader
> shows, read as blurring into the hummocks' edges. **The marsh's `q`-map is the
> published `q` — never hidden-only** (the accessibility rule, §7e): a player who
> walks into a marsh and hopes to be unseen is reading the same channels the
> reader shows, and the hiding is *there in the channels* — legible as a marsh's
> patchiness, an honest, learnable fact about the field, not a cloak the field
> secretly awards. **The hiding is the field's own**: the tessellated `q` is a
> real arrangement of the published coherence, and the illegibility of a
> signature within it is the honest consequence — the signature is not hidden by
> a mechanic, it is *drowned in the field's own fine-grained order*, exactly as
> the sea's uniform `q` drowns the ridges but now for motion.

**[design]** — the *hiding as diffusion* (that the three reads — trail-read,
clean-signature, wake — do not carry across the marsh's tessellation) and the
*fine-grained illegibility* (a signature becoming one texture among many) are
this doc's designed reading of the landed pattern-loss mechanics: `signature-predator.md`
§1.2's "legible only where the field has no order to hide it" read *against* a
tessellated rather than uniform field, `the-vigil.md` §3's clean-signature
exposure read where no clean flat exposes it, `the-walk.md` §2c's wake read
where the texture swallows it. Each is the source doc's landed pattern-loss,
applied to the marsh's [design] texture, probe-calibrated on the Phase-1 slice.
**What the marsh adds is none of these reads — it is the *tessellation* that
makes them all fail to carry**; that tessellation is this doc's [design] (§2),
and its hiding consequence rides it.

## 4. The honest edge — the hiding is never one-sided

The corpus's visibility economy has always been *asymmetric by habitat*: a
clean signature is loudest in the desert (`signature-predator.md` §1.2), a clean
station is most exposed to the Coda (`the-vigil.md` §3), a walker's wake is
legible in the margins (`the-walk.md` §2c). The marsh is the corpus's **first
symmetric** hiding surface — and the honest edge is that the symmetry cuts both
ways:

> **The marsh hides *everyone* — the Coda's approach is hidden too.** A creature
> that hides its signature in the marsh's tessellated `q` hides the *predator's*
> signature with the same honesty. The trail-read that fails on your wake fails
> on the Coda's approach: the desert-silence tell (`signature-predator.md` §3 —
> the one bright organized thing rising in a dead zone) needs a dead zone to rise
> in, and the marsh is not dead — it is *textured*, so a forming Coda's rising
> `ε²` is one edge among the hummocks' edges, not the only bright thing. Likewise
> the watcher's exposure inverts: a marsh can cover the exposed watcher's clean
> signature, but the same cover hides *what the watcher watches for*. **A marsh
> is as dangerous to the hidden as to the seeker.**
>
> **The hiding is never one-sided, and the accessibility rule holds: *what hides
> you hides what hunts you*.** The marsh is the one surface where the corpus's
> visibility economy is honestly symmetric — not a stealth refuge that favors the
> player against a still-legible predator, but a surface that blurs *all*
> signatures equally. **A player who hides in a marsh must read the same honest
> blindness the predator reads**: you cannot see the Coda's approach through a
> field that hides your own wake; the cover that makes you unseen makes the thing
> hunting you unseen too. This is the accessibility rule held at its most honest
> — the symmetry is not a softener, it is the point.

**Why the symmetry is load-bearing, not a punishment.** The corpus's danger
layer is honest because it is *readable-before-it-arrives* (`field-hazards.md`
§5.1) — a hazard's approach is legible, so a player can respond. A hiding
surface that gave the player cover *without* also hiding the predator would be a
free stealth advantage — a one-sided cloak the field secretly awards, violating
the accessibility rule (`field-instruments.md` §2.1's read-never-a-channel, the
no-hidden-channel discipline). **The symmetric hiding is what makes the marsh an
*honest* hiding surface rather than a stealth mechanic**: it provides no
asymmetric information, only honest concealment, equally applied. A player who
enters a marsh trades *legibility for cover* — they can be unseen, but none of
the corpus's reads carry to guide them, and the danger they sought to hide from
is hidden too. **In the marsh, being unseen and being unseeable are the same
act** — and that is the honest, symmetric, accessibility-preserving design.

**[design]** — the *one-sided-hiding honesty* (that the marsh's cover is
perfectly symmetric, hiding the predator as it hides the hidden) is this doc's
designed reading of the landed accessibility disciplines — the read-never-a-
channel family rule (`field-instruments.md` §2.1), the "readable before it
arrives" danger honesty (`field-hazards.md` §5.1), and the habitat-asymmetric
visibility the marsh's texture contradicts. It is this doc's [design] over them,
honest and never a cloaking gift.

## 5. The marsh's own life — the tessellated habitat

The marsh is not only hiding; it is **a habitat** — and its tessellation is
exactly what makes it one, the fine-grained ecology the sea's plain could not
hold.

**The landed pieces.** [`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md)
§4.2 maps **biome ⇔ regime**: a creature precipitates only where the local field
state admits its coherence-band + size (the recognition rule §6b), so "the local
`(q, ρ, ε²)` regime is a biome". §2.2 gives the lifecycle: a creature is a
coherence structure that crossed the persistence + coherence-band + size
threshold and condensed into a body. §4.2's map lists the coherent ridge (deep,
layered organisms), the living plain (agile, mobile), the scarred well (few or
none), and the precipitation front. The sea's *plain itself* is **sparse**
([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b — a uniform `q` with low drain and low
structure; [`the-sea-floor.md`](./the-sea-floor.md) §2b — the plain's interior has
little for the field to organize *against*, a biosphere's thin).

**The [design] over them — the marsh is the tessellated habitat:**

> **The marsh's hummocks and pools are a fine-grained habitat — the small
> coherent patches the plain's uniformity could not hold.** On the open sea the
> plain's uniform `q` is a biosphere's thin — little for the field to organize
> against, few regimes, transient coherence ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b;
> [`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md) §4.2's sparse
> plain). The marsh's tessellation gives the field **edges**: each coherent
> hummock is a small high-`q` patch — a local coherent ridge at the recognition
> rule's `[q_low, q_high]` band ([`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md)
> §6b) — and each thin pool is a low-`q` gap between. **The marsh's organism-class
> runs are the field's own, at the marsh's scale**: small, shallow, fine-grained
> creatures — the field-emergent ecology's §2.2 runs precipitating in the
> hummocks' patches the way the reef concentrates life at the plain's deep edge
> ([`the-sea-floor.md`](./the-sea-floor.md) §2b), but distributed across the
> marsh's many small patches rather than gathered at one boundary. **The marsh
> is the tessellated habitat** — the fine-grained biome the plain's uniformity
> could not hold and the deep's edge will not reach.

**[design]** — the *tessellated-habitat composition* (that the marsh's hummocks
and pools each admit a small organism-class run, distributing the plain-sparse
life into a fine-grained biome) is this doc's designed reading of the regime⇔biome
map and the recognition rule applied to the marsh's texture; the organism-class
runs themselves are the ecology's landed spans, and which run settles which
patch-band is **[design]** over the recognition band, probe-calibrated. The marsh
**mints no new creatures** — it concentrates existing field-emergent life at a
finer scale, the no-free-energy cap held (§7d).

The field is deterministic (one PDE; [`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §5c;

> **Same marsh, same field state → same hiding.** The tessellation (the hummocks

This is a **hard gate**, not a nice-to-have: the marsh must be a deterministic
function of the published channels, so "this marsh hides motion, that one's

> resource (the shallow hummocks are not deep-rung matter to reap); and it
> yields **no stealth-yield** — the hiding cannot be farmed into a stealth
> advantage, because the hiding is symmetric (§4): what conceals the player
> conceals the predator, so there is no "hide-everywhere" exploit, only honest,
> equal concealment. **The concealment is the field's own texture, never a
> gain** — the §6 cap held, a marsh converts nothing into advantage.

Three concrete guards, all inherited:
1. **No hiding that yields.** The marsh's cover is information-loss, never a
   buffer, a store, or a harvested cloak — no item, no coherence, no stealth-point

2. **No asymmetric cloak.** The hiding is symmetric (§4) — a player cannot farm

## Open questions

1. **The tessellation calibration (§2/§8).** Does a bounded shallow band of the

   textured, *how fine* — a hummock the size of a stride, a pool a few steps
   wide, or a patchiness closer to the coarse grid's cell? The scale is this
   doc's [design] dial ([§2](#2-the-tessellated-q--where-the-seas-uniformity-breaks-into-texture)),
   probe-calibrated against the `q`'/`ε²` variance the Phase-1 slice measures; a
   too-coarse scale reads as a broken plain, a too-fine as noise. **[design]/[probe]**
3. **The wake's-blur magnitude (§3/§8).** Does the tessellated `q` diffuse the
   `ε²` wake enough to *feel* as hiding — a walker's trail genuinely illegible —
   or is the contrast between a wake and the hummocks' edges too small at Phase-1,
   collapsing the hiding to symmetric *presentation* (honest cover, no mechanical
   invisibility)? The §8 slice measures it; both outcomes are honest, and the doc
   states what a weak blur means (§8 risk b). **[probe]**
4. **The one-sided-hiding symmetry's depth (§4).** Is the symmetry *total* — a
   marsh hides the predator's approach exactly as it hides the hidden — or is
   there any honest asymmetric edge (a hummock-top that gives the *stander* a
   vantage the moving predator does not share, the way the sea's Mirror reads you
   against the plain, `the-sea.md` §2b)? The honest default [design] is total
   symmetry (the texture hides all signatures equally, no vantage), the
   accessibility rule held; whether a hummock offers a legitimate stationary
   vantage is a designed dial. **[design]**
5. **The marsh's blight habitat (§6).** Can the water-borne blight's wrong-band
   actually settle the marsh's thin pools as its *patchy* habitat ([`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md)
   §2) — a fine-grained poisoning that the symmetric hiding makes hard to trace —
   or do the pools heal too fast for the blight to hold at shallow depth? Inherits
   the blight's corruption-threshold and sustainability gates, applied at the
   marsh's scale. **[design]/[probe]**
