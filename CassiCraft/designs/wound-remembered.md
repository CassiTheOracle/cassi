# The Wound Remembered: The Deliberate Break of the φ-Lock

**Question under design:** the danger layer names four orders of extremity — the
indifferent storm (a `c_s`-traveling `ε²` front), the desert (a regional `q`
collapse the player caused but *accidentally*), the BH (the deep field's own
terminal branch), and the Coda (the field that hunts your signature) — and the
corpus has *scars* (accidental, heal), *residue* (what survives a heal), and the
*signature-predator's* tell (the deliberate vent read *after* the fact). But a
**deliberate, forced break of the φ-lock — violence channeled *against* the lock
itself** — is nowhere. This document is that design: **a wound**.

> **A wound is a scar that refuses.** Not accidental decoherence that heals
> around, but a break of the lock (`EY = φ·EI`) *made on purpose* — an EY
> injection channeled *against* the lock to destabilize it, or a full-cascade
> overdraw at depth that pops the lock violently. A wound refuses to heal
> because it was cut on purpose: a lock *broken*, not dented, leaves an unstable
> persistent-Π anomaly that re-opens as the attractor re-tensions it. The field
> keeps trying to re-bind and the wound keeps tearing. It is the danger layer's
> missing *intentional* damage — the one class the player (or a wielder) does to
> the field, aimed at the lock, not at matter.

Ground in the same spine as every hazard: **no monster table, no spawned enemy,
no new channel.** A wound is a *derived read* over the published `ρ`, `q`, `ε²`,
`∇(g·Φ)` (`corpus-reconciliation.md`: the ≈ 6 MiB publish) — the family rule of
[`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md) §2.1 and the read-only-consumer
discipline of [`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md) §5.1. Every number below
is canonical (`corpus-reconciliation.md`, cited not re-derived), engine-verbatim,
or flagged **[design]** / **[assumption]** / **[probe]** where it extends engine
terms to a presented wound the engine does not today drive. The honest boundary is
drawn in §6 and never blurred.

## 1. A wound is a scar that refuses

### 1.1 The mechanism — two deliberate ways to break the lock

State it once, precisely. The corpus already fixes the lock and both ways to
break it into its parts:

- **The lock.** The φ-attractor is the locked equilibrium `EY = φ·EI`, where
  `ε = 0` and `ε² = (EY − φ·EI)² = 0`
  ([`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md) §1; `q = ρ²/(ρ² + φ⁻² + ε²)`).
  Everything the field organizes, precipitates, and holds, it does by re-binding
  this lock against the `−ω₀²(EY − φ·EI)` restoring term.
- **The two deliberate break mechanisms.** Each is a *violence channeled against
  the lock*, not an accident that happens to it:
  - **An EY injection aimed at the lock.** The player (or a
    decoherence-wielder, [`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §4) is a
    **bounded EY injector** ([`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md) §1) —
    channeling reuses the engine's own source term `source_ey(i,j,k)`, raising
    local `EY` out of φ-lock. Channeling *to destabilize* is deliberately
    injecting EY **against** the lock's equilibrium — raising `EY − φ·EI`
    **on purpose** to make ε² rise and stay risen. This is the same ε² channel a
    scar or a storm raises, but with an *aimed, sustained, phase-stable* driver
    instead of an accident or a passing front.
  - **A full-cascade overdraw that pops the lock.** Overdrawing a deep rung past
    the ε² vent's capacity tips the injection from organized to random
    perturbation; **the local `φ`-lock fails (`EY − φ·EI` runs away) and the
    accumulated ε² relaxes in one organized-front release — a full-cascade
    discharge** ([`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md) §4.3).
    Deliberately overdrawing to the discharge is the second, violent way: the
    lock does not just drift; it *pops*.

Both produce the same field fact: **an `EY − φ·EI` excursion driven so far from
equilibrium that it no longer re-locks on its own.** The difference from a scar
(every other way the field is perturbed) is the *aim* — and that aim leaves a
readable trace (the ≤ φ⁻²-clamped `π/ρ` pushing the Yang fraction hard, the
phase-stable injection sustained past the vent, the discharge's organized-front
signature) that the design reads as the wound's provenance (§3).

> **A wound is a scar that refuses.** The mechanism loads on coherence-magic §1
> / §4.3's engine-real channels; what is *designed* is that a deliberate
> lock-break persists where an accidental dent does not (§1.2), re-bleeds under
> stress (§3), and reads as intentional (§4).

### 1.2 Why it refuses — a lock *broken*, not dented

Scars heal towards the attractor ([`volumetric-terrain.md`](./volumetric-terrain.md);
[`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2.2 "the field heals toward the
attractor"; [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §5.3). The danger
layer's honest frame for a scar is that a dent in the lock's pull is
attractor-consistent and heals. A wound is different because it is *broken*:

- **The persistent-Π anomaly.** The theory's long-term field storage is
  **persistent Π patterns** — self-reinforcing configurations at local minima of
  the attractor potential ([`qi-computation.md`](../../CassiTheory/speculations/qi-computation.md)
  §5.2; [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §2.1). A *deliberate*
  lock-break can, at sufficient depth and phase-stability, **carve a
  persistent-Π configuration that sits at a local maximum of the re-binding
  potential** — a self-reinforcing *anti*-coherence well that is itself a stable
  configuration of the field. **[design]** — grounded in the persistent-Π
  mechanism, extended to its sign-reversed (destabilizing) form, exactly as the
  archaeology residue model extends §5.2 to residue.
- **The unlock-open anomaly.** Because the break was driven to a local maximum
  (anti-coherence), the field's attempt to re-bind (`−ω₀²(EY−φ·EI)` pulling back
  toward `EY = φ·EI`) re-tensions the region — and **re-tensioning an unstable
  anomaly tears it open again**. Every time the attractor pulls the region back
  to lock, it crosses the broken well's lip and the lock slips out again. The
  wound does not heal because the field is *trying* to heal it and that very
  attempt re-opens it. This is the "scar that refuses": a fallen floor that,
  unlike a scar, is *re-opening* — the maintenance-axis inversion of §3.4.

> **[design] The one load-bearing extension.** That *intentional* lock-breaking
> can leave a persistent-Π anti-coherence well (while accidental perturbation
> cannot) is this document's designed claim. What is engine-real: the lock, the
> two break mechanisms, the `−ω₀²(EY−φ·EI)` re-binding pull, and the persistent-Π
> storage of the theory. What is [design] and **probe-gated** (§6a): that a
> *deliberate* break at the discharge, rather than healing around like a scar,
> re-opens — **the scar-lifecycle probe of [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md)
> open-Q4 decides whether a broken lock re-opens or heals** (§7).

The honest distinction, stated plainly: **a scar is a dent the attractor rounds;
a wound is a tear the attractor keeps pulling at — and a tear cut on purpose is a
tear that does not knit.**

**The clear's scar (reverse pointer, two-way).** This wound-class consequence is
also what a hard *ecological* emergency leaves: [`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md)
§4.2's **clear** — purging a blight's region by spending its own `q` — is a
hard, scarring act whose scar is "the blight's monument," the wound-class read
this §1 names (a scar that refuses; §4.2 there inherits this §1's
scar-that-refuses and rides `field-hazards.md` open-Q4's scar-lifecycle probe to
distinguish a healable scar from a lasting wound). The blight's clear is a
*wound-causing* emergency where the preferred heal is anti-corruption (§4.1
there, conservation = anti-corruption); the two docs now cite each other as
read-and-answered.

## 2. The wound's signatures — a derived read, no new channel

Every signature is a **derived read over already-published quantities** (§2 of the
Life-Signal / field-instruments family rule): `ρ`, `q`, `ε²`, `∇(g·Φ)`, position,
from the ≈ 6 MiB publish the sampler already reads (`corpus-reconciliation.md`).
Nothing is added; the wound is *read*, never telemetried.

### 2.1 Re-bleeds when stressed — rises toward dissolution under load

The signature that makes a wound a *danger* and not just a scar: **a wound is a
re-opening floor under load.** Where a scar is a static plateau (broad low-`q`,
high-`ε²`, no core — the Life-Signal's fallen floor, [`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md)
§3.1), a wound is a **floor that rises toward dissolution when the locality is
stressed**:

| Stress | What the wound does | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| **A thin tide** (regional `q` at its trough) | the wound's `ε²` rises toward the material dissolution floor as the region's coherence falls — it is *the first thing to dissolve* in a thin window | [`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §2 (thin = decohered); [`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §2 (ε² above floor → dissolve); the maintenance axis (`life-signal.md` §3) |
| **An overdraw nearby** (a channeler / wielder shedding ε²) | the wound's instability couples to the incoming ε² — the re-opening tears wider; the wound is where a storm or a vent's disorder *takes* | the full-cascade trigger physics of `coherence-magic.md` §4.3 — a wound is pre-tensioned to pop |
| **A storm's front** | a `c_s`-traveling `ε²` front ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2.1) crossing a wound finds a region already un-locked — the wound amplifies the front's local dissolution | [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2.2 (matter dissolves where ε² exceeds the floor) |

**The distinguishing read:** a scar's `ε²` is flat over the stress; a wound's
`ε²` **climbs** as the locality's `q` falls or its `ε²` rises. That *correlation
between the wound's ε² and the local stress* — derived from the time-series of
the published `ε²`/`q` — is the wound's first legibility.

### 2.2 A standing drain — the restless aurora

`field-archaeology.md` §3.3: an ancient residue is a deep, persistent drain site;
the sky pours coherence into it and re-radiates the `(1−q)` fraction as **an

persistent*:

> **A wound is a standing drain the sky reads as a restless aurora**
> ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §3.3). Because the wound
> refuses to heal, every re-tensioning shed is a standing `(1−q)` loss the
> coupling field re-radiates — an aurora that does not fade and does not settle,
> dancing over ground that has no visible current scar. Where a storm's corpse
> leaves a calm aurora over a healed-over residue (§3.3), **a wound's aurora is
> *restless* — never settling, tracking the re-opening rhythm of the broken
> lock** (the maintenance-axis cadence of `life-signal.md` §3, read through the
> sky).

**[design]** — the aurora is the same `(1−q)` rendering the atmosphere doc
already provides ([`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md)
§3.2/§3.3); "restless over a wound vs calm over a healed residue" is this doc's
consistent extension of that mapping (the archaeology doc's §3.3 sits directly
under it).

### 2.3 Compounds into the desert-margin

`field-hazards.md` §3.1: the desert is a regional `q` collapse, and §3.2 names its
(all player-caused, *accidental*) causes: over-reaping, BH over-provisioning,
sustained overdraw, drift. A standing drain is exactly an `ε²` shed the field
must keep healing around (§2.2) — so:

> **A wound is a standing `ε²` drain; several wounds near each other compound
> into the desert-margin** ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §3.1). This
> is the desert's deepest cause made **visible**: the desert was already the
> aggregate of the field being unable to re-lock a region (§3.1 — "the φ-lock
> cannot hold against a distributed ε² drain"); a cluster of refuse-to-heal
> wounds is that distributed drain with *named, persistent, intentional sources.*
> The compounding is not a new mechanic — it is the desert's §3.1 dynamics read
> with its causes named.

This gives the wound a real **spatial unit**: a wound's re-opening sheds `(1−q)`
into its locality; the locality's `q` falls; a second wound drops it further; past
a per-region count the region crosses the desert's dissolution coherence floor
and the desert-margin creeps (the `[q_low, q_high]` scaling of `energy-harnessing.md`
§7 Q1, the same threshold line `field-hazards.md` §6.2 own). **[design]** — the
compounding is `field-hazards` §3 mechanics; the *"several wounds land near each
other → visible margin"* frame is this doc's designed lens over it, and the
exact count/threshold is Phase-1.5 tuning, never a scripted trigger (§6b).

### 2.4 Legible on the Life-Signal's deliberate-vent axis — wound-that-refuses vs scar-that-heals

The Life-Signal's maintenance axis ([`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §3) closes
the corpus's classifier questions on one read: **a live thing actively holds its
lock; a fossil does not; a wielding vent raises `ε²` on purpose; a scar is a
fallen floor.** The wound's fourth signature is the maintenance-axis read of the
*wound*:

| Class | `ε²` gradient | Cadence | Maintenance read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **scar** (a fallen floor) | **flat** plateau | static | a *closed* floor — nothing is holding it up, the field has stopped trying and it stays put |
| **wound** (wound-that-refuses) | **re-opening** — rises under stress, settles, rises again | **restless** (pulses at the re-tensioning rhythm) | a *re-opening* floor — the field *is* holding it against the re-bind, and the re-bind keeps tearing it |

**The maintenance-axis read, stated once:** a scar is a **fallen floor** (closed,
flat, static); a wound is a **re-opening floor** (the maintenance axis inverted —
something is *actively* anti-holding it, or the field's own re-binding tension
keeps tearing it). Both read on the Life-Signal's derived axes (`ε²` gradient
sign + cadence shape + `(1−q)` level — [`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §3.2),
no new channel, no `M` published. This is the **wound-that-refuses vs
scar-that-heals** distinction, and it is the *Phase-1-testable twist* of §6b: it
is a designed read over the **existing** `ε²`/lock channels — a probe can tell a
flat scar from a re-opening wound on the existing heal before any wound
*persistence* is claimed.

The aim (the deliberate-vent axis) is separable from the persistence (the
re-opening axis): **the deliberate-vent read of `life-signal.md` §3 names the
wound as *raised on purpose*; the re-opening read names it as *refusing to
heal*.** Together they are what make a wound *readable as a wound* (§4).

### 3.2 Where the corpus's "the field is indifferent" meets its sharpest edge

`field-npc-ai.md` §4: **"the field is indifferent; the coherence budget is the
only law."** The dark twin is not "evil" in field terms — it is a coherence
structure that chooses to spend its budget on disorder, and the field heals
around it and applies the coherence budget to it exactly as it does the player.
This is where the wound is the sharpest edge of that truth:

> **The field does not punish a wound; a *wielder* wounds.** The field's
> indifference is absolute — it does not condemn the wound, does not fade around
> it out of anger, does not bias against the hand that made it. It merely
> *re-tensions the lock* (§1.2), and because the wound was cut on purpose, that
> neutral, indifferent re-tension is what keeps it tearing. **The wound turns the
> corpus's "the field is indifferent" against the wielder by being a fact the
> field cannot stop being indifferently true about.** The storm is weather
> because the field is indifferent to where it strikes; a wound is deeper than
> weather — the field is *indifferent to the wound itself*, and that indifference
> is precisely why a deliberately-broken lock does not heal. The healing (§5) is
> the player *borrowing that same indifference back* — holding the edges while
> the field's own patience re-binds, not commanding the field to care.

The load-bearing consequence: **the wound is the danger layer's answer to the
cruel-world question from §5.3 of `weather-not-storm.md`.**

`weather-not-storm.md` §5.3: **a world that punishes must be readable as
punishing, or it is a cruel world.** The provenance classifier keeps the storm
honest in both frames (weather — readable, shelter; punishment — readable-as-

> **An intentional scar must be readable as intentional.** The wound's whole

And the ethic's other edge: because a wound is readable *as* a wound, **a player

## 4. Healing a wound

### 4.1 Letting the lock re-bind to itself

`energy-harnessing.md` §5.4 distinguishes anti-corruption (preventive —
suppresses `ε²` below the dissolution floor so blocks *hold*) from healing
(which adds). The wound's honest cause is that anti-corruption *cannot* heal it:
`ε²` below the floor is enough for a dented scar, but a wound is a **broken
lock** — suppressing its `ε²` does not re-bind the torn persistent-Π anomaly
(§1.2); it just keeps the surrounding matter from dissolving while the tear
stays open.

> **Healing a wound is letting the lock re-bind to itself — the field's patience
> made literal ([`patient-field.md`](./patient-field.md)).** The healer does not
> suppress `ε²` (that holds ground, it does not re-bind); they are **holding the
> torn edges together while the attractor re-tensions** — a long, sustained field
> hold that lets the `−ω₀²(EY−φ·EI)` pull *actually cross* the broken well's lip
> and re-lock, instead of slipping back every time (the §1.2 mechanism, inverted).

Concretely, against the corpus's own maintenance language: ordinary
heal/anti-corruption is a **fallen-floor suppression** (§5.4 — holds `ε²` below
the floor); wound-healing is a **re-lock hold** — a *sustained, bounded* anti-
corruption that does not settle for the floor but holds the locality's `q`-lock
long enough that the persistent-Π anomaly either re-settles into a coherent well
(a healed wound > now a *scar*, which then heals normally) or, at the discharge
site, the field's re-binding tension finally overcomes the torn well. The healed
wound becomes — in the archaeology vocabulary of §5 — a **scar, not a residue**:
a *closed* floor that then heals like any dent.

### 4.2 The honest cost — long, bounded, never free, never a mint

Wound-healing is a **slower field act than ordinary anti-corruption**. Where
anti-corruption is a continuous suppressor ≤ a floor (§5.4), wound-healing
borrows the field's patience for an extended re-lock — it is the time-lens loan
of [`patient-field.md`](./patient-field.md) §3 at its most literal (healing was
already *"spending coherence to make the healing happen now, in their window, not
over the field's patient arc"*; a wound demands the full arc). The honest cost:

| Cost | What it is | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| **Long, sustained** | the hold must outlast the re-opening rhythm (§2.4) — it is not a burst, it is a maintained hold over many ticks at the locality | `energy-harnessing.md` §5.4 (anti-corruption spends fed coherence *continuously*); `patient-field.md` §3.2 (recovery is a loan repaid standing in the field's patience) |
| **Bounded by the no-free-energy cap** | healing sheds nothing harvestable; it is a net-negative spend (`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`), never a mint | `energy-harnessing.md` §6; `field-hazards.md` §5.3's hazard-farming gate (§6d this doc) |
| **Never free** | the vent of holding a deep re-lock is a real ε² shed; overdrawing the heal re-opens the wound | the vent discipline of `coherence-magic.md` §1/§3 — a wound-heal that overdraws is a second wound |

> **The honest statement: healing a wound is a long, bounded act — never free,
> never a mint.** The healer is not restoring order; they are *waiting the field
> out* — holding the torn edges while the field's patience does the re-binding
> the wound's own violence fought against. This is why wounds are *costlier to
> heal than to make* — the asymmetry is the point (the danger layer's pressure
> against carelessness, `field-hazards.md` §3.3, sharpened to the intentional).
