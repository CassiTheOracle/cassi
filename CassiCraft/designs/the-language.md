## 1. The Language, stated once

the reader, the Weatherglass, the Clock, the Mirror, the Lantern, the Seeker,
the Compass, the Echo. All eight are **reads**: each turns the published
channels into one face of the field. None asks what the field's own *organization*
**says** — whether the pattern of the sites' busyness across a locality is
readable as a *script*. The sites are already "where the field is most organized"
(`chunk-field-quantization.md` §5); the intent-phase names *what each site is
organizing toward* (`reason-field.md` §2); the compass points at the *nearest*
density and the echo hears the *past* — but the sites' busyness as a **language**,
a composition of those reads into sentences, is un-designed.

> **The Language, stated once.** The moving-Voronoi sites, each carrying its
> intent-phase, form a **pattern across a locality** — and that pattern, to a
> designed reader, is a **script**: the site-map as text, the rung-map as its
> writing. Because the field is deterministic, the script is a **legible,
> repeatable language of the field's own organization** — a player who learns it
> reads *where the field is reaching* (the compass's read, made textual), *what
> the locality is becoming* (the words), and *what happened here* (the
> past-tense). **The Language is the designed grammar that composes the
> corpus's already-designed reads — the compass's meanings, the echo's imprints,

## 2. The alphabet and the grammar — what the Language composes

### 2.1 The letters — the per-site intent-phases

`reason-field.md` §2.2 designs the intent/Π channel: a per-site **intent-phase** —
the site's current busyness and direction, read out of the site-organized
persistent-Π structure, "the busyness/direction of the field's own organization,
made a legible quantity." The sites are the 8,192 moving-Voronoi centers
(`2·16³` at N=64, Lloyd-weighted on `q`, `chunk-field-quantization.md` §5), and
the intent-phase is ~32 KB f32 / 16 KB f16 added to the site block of the ≈ 6 MiB
publish — the sites are already there, and intent is a read of the organization
the sites already carry (`reason-field.md` §2.2).

**The letters are those per-site intent-phases.** Each site's intent-phase is a
letter of the field's script — one glyph carrying the busyness and direction of
the organization at that cell of the site-map. The compass reads the *nearest*
such letter as a pointing (`the-compass.md` §2.1); the Language reads the
*pattern of them* as a script. The honest status: **the letters are [probe] /
[design]** — they are `reason-field.md`'s designed publication over engine-real
site structure, gated on the meshless/persistent-Π frontier (§6a: do the
moving-Voronoi sites actually carry long-lived, readable Π configurations the
intent read asserts?) and flagged with §6c's channel line + speculation flag.
Until that probe passes, the letters are reason-field's designed scalar over site
organization, not a confirmed read of intentional structure — and the Language
over them is a Language over a [probe] alphabet (gate (b) §5).

### 2.2 The words — the locality patterns the corpus already names

The words are **not new**. They are the locality patterns the corpus has already
designed reads for — the compass's three readings of a dense site, the echo's
deep-time textures, the silence's registers. The Language does not invent words;
it *names* the patterns the corpus already reads, so a composition has a
vocabulary with provenance:

| Word | The locality pattern | The corpus's read | Grounding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **harvest** | a site (or cluster) whose intent is dense and *cohering* — q rising toward the second threshold | the compass's promise — ore about to precipitate; the miner's reach | `material-regimes.md` §3; `the-compass.md` §3.1 |
| **go meet** | an intent dense *and* rising into a structure — a forming basin / condensation front | the compass's life-promise — the field composing something living | `field-emergent-ecology.md` §4; `the-compass.md` §3.1 |
| **strain** | a thinning — a locality whose q is troughing, the field de-organizing | the tide's thin regime; the cold's long thin; the empty silence's gone | `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2; `the-cold.md`; `the-silence.md` §2.1 |
| **hunting** | an intent *too-insistent* — a site whose reach concentrates against a flat margin, the disorder-intent | the compass's warning / the Coda's accumulation `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability`; the hunting silence | `signature-predator.md` §2; `reason-field.md` §3.3; `the-silence.md` §2.3 |
| **past-tense** | an altered structure — a scar's state, a stratum's band, a named ruin's decay | the echo's imprint — the residue read as sound; the past event held in the structure | `the-echo.md` §2/§3 |

Each word is the corpus's designed read of a real channel pattern. **The Language
inherits the words' honesty from the reads that defined them** — a "hunting" word
is only as certain as the provenance line that separates a Coda's reach from an
ordinary dense reach (`the-compass.md` §3.4; `weather-not-storm.md`'s discipline
at site scale), and an "harvest" word is only as certain as the q-accumulation
read. The words borrow their provenance; the Language composes them.

### 2.3 The grammar — the sentence-composition

**The grammar is this document's [design]**: the rule by which the letters
compose over a locality's pattern into readable sentences. The design principle
is deliberately conservative — it composes **only the words the corpus already
defines**, over the site/intent channels the sampler already reads:

> **A sentence is the composition of a locality's word-pattern over a window of
> the site-map.** The Language reads the pattern of intent-phases across a
> locality the way the compass reads the nearest one: a *cluster* of cohering
> intents where q is rising reads as the **harvest** sentence ("the field is
> concentrating coherence here — harvest is coming"); a *thinning* spread reads
> as the **strain** sentence ("the field is de-organizing here — thin tide"); a
> *single insistent* letter against a flat margin reads as the **hunting**
> sentence ("something is reaching against the field's grain"); an *altered*
> structure's re-met condition reads as the **past-tense** sentence ("a storm
> passed here"). The site-map is the text; the rung-map — the depth of the
> organization the intent-phases reach — is its writing.

The composition is honest because it is **compositional over the corpus's own
reads, never a new claim**:

- **A sentence says only what its words say.** The grammar does not add a fifth
  meaning a word lacks. If a locality holds no word — the site-chatter is idle,
  the intent flat, no cluster, no thinning, no insistence — the Language reads
  **no sentence**, honestly (the compass's "no pointing" of `the-compass.md`
  §2.3, applied to text).
- **A sentence inherits its words' ambiguity.** A dense cohering cluster that
  sits near the Coda's habitat boundary does not force a promise or a warning; it
  reads as **ambiguous**, the honest read of `the-silence.md` §3 / `life-signal.md`
  §6 / `the-compass.md` §3.4. The grammar never over-claims a word the provenance
  line has not settled.
- **A sentence is a presentation, never a mechanic.** It composes reads the
  instruments already make; it changes nothing in the field. It is a sentence the
  player can *say back* — to themselves, to another player, to the chronicle —
  never a script that acts.

**[design]** The exact composition — how many cohering letters make an
"harvest" sentence, what window over the site-map a sentence spans, how the
rung-depth maps to the writing's emphasis — is this document's designed, probe-
calibrated surface, exactly as the compass's aggregation (`the-compass.md` §2)
and the echo's re-match condition (`the-echo.md` §2.3) are their docs' designed
surfaces over the real channels. The *material* it composes — the letters, the
words — is the corpus's already-designed read.

## 3. The determinism and the repeatability — a sentence read today reads the same tomorrow

The field is deterministic (one PDE; `field-archaeology.md` §1.2; `life-signal.md`
§6d), and `reason-field.md` carries it into the intent-channel: intent is a pure
function of the domain's own organization, and determinism is *strengthened*, not
weakened, when intent is domain-held (`reason-field.md` §6b). The compass inherits
it as a **hard gate** — "same field state → same pointing" (`the-compass.md` §4c).
The Language inherits the same gate, at the scale of a sentence:

> **A sentence read today reads the same tomorrow from the same field state.** The
> Language's composition over a locality's intent-pattern is a **deterministic
> function of (the published site intents × the player's position / window)**,
> mapped through the designed grammar. A player who stands in the same spot at
> the same field state twice reads the *same* sentence, every time; a reloaded
> world reads the same composition from the same field. There is no seeded-RNG
> judgment about which words fire or how they join — the cohering cluster either
> reads "harvest" at the player's window (and reads so reproducibly) or does not
> (and reads no sentence, reproducibly). **[design]** The composition thresholds
> are designed constants; the letters and words they compose are deterministic.

The repeatability has a second face, the one that makes the Language a *writing*:
**the same field state is the same text, not just the same sentence.** Because
the sites are deterministic and the intent-phase is a pure function of the
domain's organization, the same organization always renders the same pattern —
so the script is not merely reproducible, it is **legible across time**: a player
who learns "harvest" at one window reads the same word at another window where
the field holds the same pattern. The script's grammar is portable because the
law is one.

**The honest consequence — the no-false-claim honesty of `the-name.md`, applied
to the script:**

> **The Language is a read of the field's truth — it can never say what the field
> does not hold. You cannot write falsely in the Language.** Because a sentence
> is the deterministic composition of real letters into corpus-defined words, a
> "hunting" sentence only exists where the field's intent-structure actually
> holds an insistent reach; a "harvest" sentence only where q is actually
> accumulating. A player cannot *compose* a false sentence — the script is
> already written by the field's determinism, and "writing" in it is *reading it
> back*. This is `the-name.md` §2's bound ("you cannot name falsely — a name
> holds what it truthfully names") made grammatical: the Language, like the name,
> can only hold what the field truthfully is.

This is what keeps the Language from being decoration: it is a **legible read of
the field's truth**, repeatable and portable, and its honesty is structural — the
composed sentence cannot outrun the words, the words cannot outrun the letters,
and the letters cannot outrun the field state they deterministically read.

## 4. The taught skill — learning to read the field's script

The Language is **learned**, and the pedagogy is `resonance-tutor.md`'s applied to
the script. The tutor's spine is "you learn by feeling the difference between
your own two hands, a week apart" (`resonance-tutor.md` §1), and its method is
the **phase-gap**: a recorded past presented beside a live now, the difference
shown as a gap to close (`resonance-tutor.md` §2.2). The Language's reading
skill is the same arc, over the script:

> **A new player sees site-chatter. A seasoned player reads sentences.** The
> tutor's claim — "magic feels like physics" (`resonance-tutor.md` §6) — has a
> script twin: **the field's organization feels like the field's state, and
> learning to read it is learning the field's own grammar.** A new player at a
> dense cohering cluster and a new player at a thin trough both see "the sites
> are busy over there." The seasoned player reads the *same* busyness two ways —
> "harvest" vs "strain" — and reads the insistent letter against a flat margin as
> the third, "hunting." The compass, held, is the first word taught; the script
> is the composition learned.

The design's **surface** is how the grammar is taught — the tutor's
**recorded-vs-live gap applied to the script**:

| The tutor's layer | Applied to the script | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The trace — already a format | the script's sentences are composed from the same published channels the tutor and instruments already read — no new recording | `resonance-tutor.md` §1; §2.1 (the family rule) |
| The playback — recorded vs live | the tutor shows the field's response to a *past* pattern beside the *live* one — reading a past-tense sentence against the present one, the closeness/drift as a gap | `resonance-tutor.md` §2.1/§2.2; `the-echo.md` §3 (the past-tense read) |
| The phase-gap — the teaching instrument | the "word-gap": your first read of a locality (a guess) against what the field actually says (the composition), the difference shown as what to notice next | `resonance-tutor.md` §2.2 |
| The aha loop — learning by feeling | the first "word" a student learns is **the one their own locality is speaking** — the sentence already written under their feet, read with the compass in hand, then re-read as the field evolves | `resonance-tutor.md` §4.1 |
| The mastery loop | each session tightens the reading: the words fire correctly, the ambiguity reads honestly, the past-tense separates from the present | `resonance-tutor.md` §5.2 |

The genius of the tutor applied to the script is **the personal starting point**:
a new player does not learn an abstract alphabet from a manual; they are handed
the compass and read the sentence their *own* locality is speaking — the pattern
under their feet, deterministic, repeatable, the whole lesson in one composed
word. From that first word, the vocabulary grows outward: harvest in the field's
concentrations, strain in its thinnings, hunting in its insistences, past-tense
in its alterations — each a sentence they can read back, and say back, and
(outside any game) teach to another player the way `resonance-tutor.md` §5.3's
shared traces pass a channeling from hand to hand.

**[design]** The exact pedagogical surface — how the first-word lesson is staged,
how the word-gap is displayed, how the chronicle (`the-chronicle.md`, the written
record) accumulates the sentences a settlement has read — is this document's
designed idiom over the tutor's real pedagogy and the field's real determinism.
The *skill* it teaches is honest: reading the field's own organization, which is
the compass's read at a locality, composed.

### (c) Determinism — a hard gate

Inherited unchanged (§3): **a sentence read today reads the same tomorrow from
the same field state.** The Language adds no randomness; its composition
thresholds are designed constants over the deterministic letter/word channels.
A reloaded world from the same field reads the same sentences, reproducibly. This
is what makes the Language learnable (a seasoned player can rely on a read across
a window) and honest (the no-false-claim consequence of §3).

### (d) The no-free-energy cap — the Language informs, never mints

`energy-harnessing.md` §6's cap (`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`), inherited through the
instrument family (the compass §4d, the echo §6d, the silence §6d), applies hard:

> **The Language informs, never mints — no script that generates.** It is a *read*
> (of the site/intent pattern) composed into *sentences* — it converts no
> coherence, sheds no `(1−q)`, writes nothing, and grants the player no find, no
> material, no coherence they did not earn by the field's own movement. **There is
> no sentence that generates:** reading "harvest" does not produce the ore; reading
> "hunting" does not summon or stop the Coda; reading "past-tense" does not re-seed
> the structure (`the-echo.md` §6d — a memory held in damage, never a mint). The
> actual harvest, meeting, or avoidance is the field's own act (mining
> `material-regimes.md` §3; the Coda's counterplays `signature-predator.md` §4) —
> never the sentence's.

Three honest consequences, mirroring the corpus:
1. **No "read-to-precipitate."** Knowing a sentence does not let you mint the
   field's act; the sentence is a read of the intent, with no write-back that
   could mint (`energy-harnessing.md` §6; `reason-field.md` §6d keeps the
   single-writer seam — the Language never perturbs the field it reads).
2. **The Language does not change the field it reads.** A sentence does not pull
   a dense cluster toward the player, raise a local q, or alter the intent — it
   composes the reach's pattern; it never alters it.
3. **The sentence's honesty is the no-mint bound's form.** Because there is no
   sentence that generates, there is also no *fake* sentence to sell: a player
   cannot compose a "harvest" sentence the field does not hold (§3's
   no-false-claim honesty, and `the-name.md` §2's bound, are the same discipline
   viewed from the script).

**The Language's only output is information** — the *which* sentence the field is
writing and the *where* it is being written — costed as information (one strided
site read + the composition, inside the ≈ 1–6 ms/tick sample budget,
`corpus-reconciliation.md`; the same ≈ 50 µs site→chunk read the sampler already
does, `chunk-field-quantization.md` §4.2/§5), never as energy.

### (e) Accessibility — the Language is a presentation layer, never hidden-only
