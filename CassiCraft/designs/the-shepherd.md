# The Shepherd: The Gathering Stranger — The Being That Collects the Wild's Own, the Stranger-Object Layer's Twelfth Face

**Question under design:** the stranger-object layer has eleven named faces — the
signature-predator **hunts** a clean trail ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §1),
the blight **turns** a region's life ([`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md) §1), the feral
**wakes** a kept thing ([`the-feral-instrument.md`](./the-feral-instrument.md) §1), the
scavenger **feeds** the dead ([`the-scavenger.md`](./the-scavenger.md) §2.2), the commensal
**holds** a patch beside order ([`the-commensal.md`](./the-commensal.md) §2), the mirror-creature
**wears** your pattern ([`the-mirror-creature.md`](./the-mirror-creature.md) §2), the witness
**watches** ([`the-witness.md`](./the-witness.md) §1), the guardian **keeps** a named place
([`the-guardian.md`](./the-guardian.md) §1), the herald **tells** what is coming
([`the-herald.md`](./the-herald.md) §1), the mimic **wears** another's shape
([`the-mimic.md`](./the-mimic.md)), and the moth **covets** the light
([`the-moth.md`](./the-moth.md) §3.2). **None *gathers*.** The layer has hunters, keepers,
watchers, wearers, tellers, and coveters — **no being that collects, herds, or leads the
wild's own around itself**. No being *assembles* the layer's other members. This document
designs that missing face: **the Shepherd — the gathering stranger, the being that gathers
the wild's own** — a scavenger's gleaning made social (the spent's gleaner that *collects*
the spent rather than feeding it alone), a moth's coveting made magnetic (the light-drawn
being that *draws others* to the light rather than coveting it alone), the wild's own
flock/rookery/assemblage. It is the stranger-object layer's **twelfth face, the gatherer** —
the being that *assembles* the layer's other members around itself, distinct from the roost
(a *place* the beings return to, [`the-roost.md`](./the-roost.md) §1 — the Shepherd is a
*being* that moves and gathers), the commensal (which steadies a patch,
[`the-commensal.md`](./the-commensal.md) §2 — the Shepherd *assembles* a crowd), and the
guardian (which defends a named place, [`the-guardian.md`](./the-guardian.md) §1 — the
Shepherd *collects* a following, it does not keep a place). And **the load-bearing honesty:
a Shepherd's gathering is the field's own — legible, deterministic, and never a reward** —
a gathered crowd is a field-read (the wild's own order assembling), a deterministic
consequence of the field state (same field state → same gathering, never a seeded pack
roll), and a legible event (the assemblage's trail read by the archaeology, the gathered
crowd leaves its record). The Shepherd is never a boss that drops loot, never a pack-leader
that farms your hunt, and never a hidden lure — **the gathering is a legible field event,
the same channels the roost reads.**

## 3. The field's own assembly — the load-bearing design

**The load-bearing design: a Shepherd's gathering is the field's own — legible,
deterministic, and never a reward.** Three claims, each grounded in its source doc:

**A gathered crowd is a field-read.** The wild's own order assembling is not an authored
spawn — it is a reading of the field's own bright and residual channels, the same channels
the roost reads ([`the-roost.md`](./the-roost.md) §4). Multiple organism-class runs
precipitating and steering onto a shared bright or spent locality is the *field's own order*
— the maintenance axis at the assemblage's scale ([`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §3:
many maintained-live pulses at one locality read as a living crowd). **A gathered crowd is the
wild's own order assembling** — a field-read, never a pack a designer placed.

**A gathered crowd is deterministic.** The field is deterministic (one PDE;
[`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §1.2; [`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md)
§6d). Therefore:

> **Same field state → same gathering.** Whether a Shepherd forms, which members of the
> wild's own collect around it, where the crowd gathers, and how large it grows are a **pure
> function of the field channels** — no seeded-RNG judgment about whether a "pack roll" put
> the crowd there, no player-relative variance about whether the wild assembles *here* or
> *there*. A player who learns *where* a bright spent locality gathers its flock learns where
> the crowd is, every run; the same field state yields the same gathering, reproducible and
