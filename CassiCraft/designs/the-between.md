## 1. The Between, stated once

The corpus names the *pieces of the dark* but never the dark itself. `world-seams.md`
sails it, `the-interstitial.md` gives it its three characters, `the-harbor.md` builds a
door into it, `the-atlas-of-windows.md` records what is seen *from* it, `window-guests.md`
and `the-treaty.md` place strangers and compacts at its edge. But nothing answers the
question a cosmology answers: **what is the whole that holds a window?** A window is a
place the field condenses into life; a voyage is a traverse across the dark; but the
dark — the medium all of it floats in — has no designed *account* of itself as a whole.
The corpus has the windows and the crossings, and not the field's own outside.

This document is that account, stated once and kept:

> **The Between is the cosmology of the field's own outside — the dark the windows
> float in, designed honestly as a place.** It is **not another world** — it is the
> field's own un-windowed medium at its sparsest: **lawful** (the interstitial's law
> holds: nothing kills you and nothing feeds you), **empty** (no tide, no window-year,
> no farm — the corpus's windowed machinery does not apply, because the windowed stack
> is the window's own), and **honest** (the same field at the sparsest — there is no
> hidden richness, no secret world, no un-windowed abundance). **It is the corpus's
> macro-face: the cosmology of the space between worlds, designed from the windows'
> own evidence, honestly.**

The load-bearing distinction from every window-neighbor it touches:

- **Not a window.** The between has no `q ≈ 0.947` attractor, no boatable bath, no
  precipitating terrain, no tide — the windowed stack (farm, harvest, settlement,
  stranger-layer, calendar) is the window's own, absent here. [`pocket-cosmos.md`](./pocket-cosmos.md)
  §1.3's "not a dimension" is the between's own distinction drawn one level out:
  the between is **entered by crossing, never teleported to**, and what you find is a
  *samer* law thinned, never a new place.
- **Not the deep.** [`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §6 designs the
  dark's *depths* as the vertical's bottom — a window's own strata and its deepest
  store. **The between is not that**: it is not down, it is *outside*. The deep is a
  window's inward fall into density; the between is the window's outward edge into
  sparseness. The one is a hole in the world, the other is the world's field at its
  thinnest.
- **Not a void to be endured.** [`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md) §3
  states it: the between's emptiness is **a thin to be understood, not a blank to be
  endured**. The between is a *readable* lawfulness — the same two-fluid at its most
  honest, because it has nothing to offer and hides nothing.

**The one-line distinction that makes the Between its own object:** every window-neighbor
designs a *place a person acts in* (lives, crosses, shops, departs, records, compacts).
**The Between designs the *whole those places float in*** — not a place to act, but the
cosmological frame that makes the windowed world legible: the honest outside against
which every window's richness is read. It adds no new place; it names the dark that was
already there, and describes it honestly.

## 2. The lawful dark — the honest mechanics

The between's nature is composed of the corpus's own landed claims, stated as the

**The [design] over them — the between's constitution is that law, stated as
cosmology:**

> **[design] The between's constitution is the interstitial's law, held as the law of

The **[design] line:** the lawfulness (each extreme's death in the thin) is a *documented

you and nothing feeds you" is the whole of the outside's nature — is this doc's designed

## 3. Not another world — the load-bearing honesty

The between's sharpest honesty is a refusal. Every cosmology is tempted to make its
outside a *second world* — a place with its own abundance, its own hidden life, its own
treasures, a secret more valuable than the one the player lives in. **The Between refuses
that by construction.** Stated once:

> **The between is not another world — it is the field's own outside.** A dark that is
> **lawful** (the interstitial's law holds: nothing kills you and nothing feeds you —
> §2a), **empty** (the between has no tide, no window-year, no farm — the corpus's
> windowed machinery does not apply: no harvest, no settlement, no stranger-layer, because
> the windowed stack is the window's own, §3 below), and **honest** (the between is the
> same field, at the sparsest — **the voyage's craft is the walk's craft at the between's
> emptiness**, `world-seams.md` §2 + `the-walk.md` §2; there is no hidden richness, no
> secret world, no un-windowed abundance). **A between that promised a window's wealth
> would be a false dark the field sheds.**

### (a) Empty — the windowed machinery does not apply

The corpus's windowed life all needs a field to be in, and the between's field cannot
hold it. Grounded in each source doc's real gate:

| The windowed machinery | It needs (the landed gate) | In the between |
|---|---|---|
| **The tide** | a regional `q` to breathe on the mixing clock `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]` (`tide-of-the-attractor.md`; `the-window-year.md` §2) | no `q` above the noise floor → **no tide to breathe**, no harvest-thin cycle, no window-year |
| **The farm** | precipitation where `q` holds a coherent band at low `ε²` (`farm-that-feeds.md`; `field-emergent-ecology.md` §6b) | the `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1` floor holds **no coherent band** → no precipitating food, no farm |
| **The settlement / stranger-layer** | the Board, the census, the stranger faces all ride a window's bath and its population (`shared-ledger.md`; `the-census.md`) | ρ below the gas floor, no bath → **no settlement, no stranger-layer** (the between's only people are *transients*: voyagers, guests, treaty-hands, §2c) |
| **The terrain** | condensation cuts iso-surfaces at ρ ≥ τ_c (`volumetric-terrain.md`; `chunk-field-quantization.md` §2) | the between's ρ sits **below every floor** → nothing condenses, no ground to stand on (only the ship's carried hull) |
| **The harvest / found-economy** | deep-rung ordered matter (`energy-harnessing.md` §1.5/§3) | a non-windowed place has **no deposits** — the (1−q) glow is the only light and every reserve is the traveler's own carried coherence (`world-seams.md` §2.3) |

**The load-bearing emptiness, stated honestly:** the between does not *lack* a tide, a
farm, a settlement, a terrain — it has **none, and cannot have any**, because the field
at its sparseness provides none of the organizing thresholds those systems need. **The
windowed stack is the window's own.** This is what makes "empty" honest rather than
deprived: the between is not a barren window; it is *not a window at all*, and so the
window's machinery is simply not its machinery.

### (b) Honest — the same field at the sparsest

The between is not an absence of the field; it is the field at its most readable.

> **The between is the same field, at the sparsest — the voyage's craft is the walk's
> craft applied to the sparsest terrain.** The walk reads the ridges and thins the field
> actually presents (`the-walk.md` §2); a voyager reads the between's only remaining
> structure — the ringing (which windows are near), the star-read (which way to hold
> course, `world-seams.md` §2.2), the `(1−q)` glow (how the carried field is being spent,
> `energy-harnessing.md` §2). **There is no new craft for the between; it is the same
> field-reading discipline at a terrain where the field has nothing to offer and hides
> nothing** (`the-interstitial.md` §3 — "the crossing's craft is the walk's craft"). A
> traveler who can read a window's honesty can read the between's, because it is the same
> honesty — the between has simply removed everything that could obscure it.

**The honest refusal, stated for all time:** **there is no hidden richness, no secret
world, no un-windowed abundance** in the between. The design's commitment is total and
structural: a between that promised a window's wealth — a hidden vein in the dark, a
sub-window civilization, a lootable emptiness — would be a **false dark the field sheds**,
exactly as `energy-harnessing.md` §6's cap rejects a mint and `the-treaty.md` §6d rejects
a net-mint bridge. **The between's gift is the honest contrast it gives a window**
[`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md) §4 **and the pure patience it demands**
(§3 of the interstitial) — information and timing, never energy, never a hidden world.
