## 2. The pair's mechanics — what holding phase IS, and the three honest costs

### 2.1 Holding phase — a working's two-body case (field-npc-ai §3.2 at N = 2)

`field-npc-ai.md` §3.2 is the load-bearing law: **coordination is the docking
mechanic's `M` applied to cooperation** — two NPCs acting in the same phase window
are one organized perturbation; each holds phase with the other, the joint ε² vent
is shared, and the combined injection's `M` sums coherently. **A group acting
phase-locked IS a working.** The apprenticeship takes that law at its two-body
minimum:

> **Holding phase IS the veteran maintaining a single, bounded, organized
> perturbation at the pair's locality** — a sustained EY injection at a fixed rung,
> phase-locked to the local field's rhythm (`coherence-magic.md` §3.1's resonance:
> matching `−ω₀²(EY − φ·EI)` and pushing with `∇(g·Φ)`), emitted as a standing
> `sustain = true` Q4 record (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.2 — a held phase books as
> one compact record, not a re-emit spam). The student channels *inside* that
> window: their condense/dissolve/shield/vent ops read **against the held phase** —
> the same local field the veteran is organizing — so any op the student makes while
> aligned *couples* at the working's `M`, and any vent they shed *drains into* the
> working's shared locality rather than alone.

So the pair is not a teacher and a student doing two separate things; it is **one
working with a deliberately uneven composition** — the veteran holds the frame, the
student's ops ride it, and for the aligned parts of the session the pair's joint
perturbation couples at `M ≈ 1` the way any phase-locked group does (§3.2).
[Holding a low magnitude is deliberate: a **low-magnitude window** keeps the hold
within the veteran's budget physics (§3), so it is *sustainable over a session*, and
keeps the shelter's cost bounded (§2.2). The veteran's hold is a working *at a
chosen low rung*, not a full-power working — the magnitude dial is [design], the
vent-bound legality is engine-real (`coherence-magic.md` §3, `v ≤ h`).]

### 2.2 The three honest costs

The pair is honest precisely because it is **costed on both sides** — the veteran
shelters at their own price, the student channels inside a real frame. Three costs,
each the honest face of a landed piece:

**Cost 1 — the veteran's burden accrues faster.** `the-burden.md` §2 gives the loan
with interest: every channeled op sheds ε² that accumulates on the player as a
carried debt; at thin tide the debt compounds the vent back-up (§2a). Holding a
phase window is a *working*, and a working's ε² is the veteran's to carry. Because
the veteran holds the frame **against a learner's turbulence** — the student's early
vents shed into the shared locality, so the veteran's effective vent is their own
hold *plus* the absorbed novice overdraw — **the veteran's burden accrues faster
than the same veteran channeling alone.** This is the honest version of "teaching
costs the teacher": the veteran's carried ε²-debt grows through the apprenticeship
session, legible on the deliberate-vent axis (`the-burden.md` §2c), cleared only by
the Still Room's patient time (§2b). The veteran is *spending their own patience*
(shaped by the loan's interest-rate physics, `patient-field.md` §3.3) to shepherd a
learner — the price of teaching, priced in the field's own currency. **[design]**
The *rate* of the veteran's faster accrual (how much of the student's absorbed
turbulence lands on the veteran) is a designed, probe-calibrated dial over the
engine-real vent/(1−q) channels (§6a); the *direction* — holding a working against a
learner's turbulence sheds more than holding alone — is the corpus's own working-and-
vent physics (§3.2, §4.3).

**Cost 2 — the student's early vents are sheltered (a bounded error floor).** A
novice overdraws: `coherence-magic.md` §4.3 fixes that channeling past the vent's
capacity tips organized → random perturbation and relaxes in a full-cascade
discharge. The apprentice's early vents would, alone, do exactly that. Inside the
pair, **the veteran's held phase absorbs the worst of a novice overdraw**:

> **The shelter is a bounded error floor.** When the student vents past their own
> capacity, the shed ε² drains into the *working's* organized locality (the veteran's
> held phase) rather than collapsing to a random-perturbation discharge on the
> student's own position. The veteran's hold *absorbs the worst* — the student's
> overdraw is converted from a full-cascade risk into the veteran's carried debt
> (Cost 1) plus a bounded local ε² uptick, instead of the student's own §4.3
> detonation.

This is the corpus's honest answer to "training wheels": **the protection is real
and it costs the veteran.** It is not a shield that makes the student invincible —
it is a *designed, costed transfer*: the student's worst overdraw is paid for by the
veteran's faster burden-accrual (§2.2 Cost 1) at the pair's locality, and it is
*bounded* — the shelter cannot absorb an arbitrarily large overdraw. Past the
shelter's floor (a [design] magnitude, probe-calibrated against the vent/heal rates,
§6), the student's overdraw reverts to their own §4.3 risk; a student who keeps
overdrawing faster than the veteran's hold settles it drains the pair's working and
ends the session. **The shelter is real, bounded, costed, and honestly legible** —
its floor and its cost are the pair's read (§4). The no-free-energy cap (§5d) holds
it: the shelter is *spent* coherence the veteran carries into a debt, never a mint
for the student.

**Cost 3 — the pair's Q4 records (the mentorship flag).** The pair books on the
settled Q4 record `{member, op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}`
(`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1). The veteran's hold is a `sustain = true` record
(§2.2, one compact record); the student's ops are their own records with their own
`member`. **The `mentorship` flag** — this doc's designed extension of the settled
schema (§4) — marks a student's records as *taken inside the veteran's phase window*,
so the ledger reads the pair's joint ops as **one pair's working over the session**,
not as two members' competing or canceling contributions in one cell
(`schema-that-settles.md` §2.3's locality rule, applied to the pair: they book *with*
each other, never as a silent zero). The pair's two `member` lines are the two halves
of a joint working — the student's ops attributable to the student, the veteran's
hold to the veteran, but the *pair* is a legible field state the society can read
(§5).

**[design]** The shelter, its error floor, and the mentorship flag are this doc's
designed gameplay surface over the engine-real vent/(1−q) channels, the §4.3
overdraw trigger, and the settled Q4 record/sustain flag. The three costs are the
honest prices the pair must pay — faster burden for the teacher, absorbed-then-paid
turbulence for the student, and a legible joint book for the town.

## 3. What transfers — the feel, read on the pair's records and the student's maintenance

### 3.1 Not the record — the feel

The tutor's trace transfers your *own past hands* as a recorded target; the
apprenticeship transfers something the record cannot hold — **the feel of your own
hand in phase with an expert's.** The recorded-vs-live gap (`resonance-tutor.md`
§7 open-Q2) is exactly this boundary: a recorded phase is a *static target* the
student closes a gap against; the veteran's held phase is a **live, present frame**
the student's hand sits *inside* — and the difference is precisely what `life-signal.md`
§3.2 names on the maintenance axis: a re-enacted live hold *pulses* (the maintainer
is active); a static replay is *flat* (§3.2, `life-signal.md`'s closure of the
tutor's open-Q2). The apprenticeship is the *live* form that open-question was
reaching toward:

> **The student's own hand, experienced in phase with an expert's, is the craft
> taught the way the field teaches — by resonance.** The student does not *learn a
> recipe* from the veteran ("do condense at rung 3"); they *feel* what the field
> does when their vent and the veteran's held phase are one organized perturbation —
> the `M ≈ 1` working of `magic-systems.md` §1 felt from the inside, at the hand that
> makes it. What the student internalizes is not a sequence of ops but **the phase
> itself** — the timing, alignment, and patience of a matched hand, which
> `coherence-magic.md` §3.1's resonance rule (`channeling is more efficient in phase
> with the field`) is the grammar of.

### 3.2 What progress reads as

Progress is not a skill bar; it is a **measured change in the field**. Two reads,
both designed over the landed stack:

**The pair's own read — staying in phase longer.** The pair's narrative is the
student's ops *inside* the veteran's window. Progress reads as: the student **stays
in phase longer without needing the veteran's correction** — i.e. the fraction of
the session where the student's ops couple at the working's `M` (rather than alerting
the veteran to re-lock the window) rises, and the shelter's absorbed overdraw (*Cost
2*) falls. This is a deterministic read over the pair's Q4 records (§4): the
`mentorship`-flagged student ops, the veteran's `sustain` hold, and the coupling
readable from whether the pair's joint perturbation held `M ≈ 1` over the window.
**[design]** The exact "in phase" threshold (how far a student's phase may drift
before the window breaks) is a probe-calibrated boundary over the real phase-match
`M`-structure; the *quantity* it reads (the student's ops vs the veteran's held
phase) is the pair's Q4 records, deterministic.

**The student's own read — their maintenance, improving over the apprenticeship's
tides.** `the-mirror.md` §2.4 gives the **classification face**: the Life-Signal's
decision rule (`life-signal.md` §3.1's axes — motion, `(1−q)` cadence, `ε²`
gradient, `∇(g·Φ)` alignment) applied to the player's own position. It tells you
*what you are to the field's own eyes* — live-but-idle, burdened, wound-like. For the
apprentice, the honest progress read is that **their own maintenance improves over
the apprenticeship's tides**: a student who starts by leaning on the shelter (their
early overdraws absorbed, their carried ε² elevated toward the deliberate-vent axis
by their own beginner venting) learns, over successive sessions, to hold their own
lock — the classification face reads a student who stays in phase for more of the
session, vents within the heal rate, and ends each apprenticeship lighter (less

learning happened by the field's own measure, not by the veteran's praise** — the
same self-kindness-in-indifference the mirror's whole discipline is built on
(`the-mirror.md` §4.4). **[design]** The progress read over the pair's records and
the mirror's face is this doc's [design]; the maintenance axes it reads and the

The through-line, stated once:

## 4. The pair's book — the mentorship flag over the settled schema

`schema-that-settles.md` §2 settled the Q4 op-record: `{member, op, worldPos, rung,
magnitude, sustain}`, with `member` attached at the world-writer's single non-forgeable
write, `sustain` as a flag (a held op is one compact record), and §2.3's locality
rule (two members in one cell book two lines, never a silent zero). The pair rides
that record with **one designed extension**:

> **The `mentorship` flag.** A student's Q4 records taken inside a veteran's phase
> window carry a `mentorship` flag alongside their `member`, marked by the same
> world-writer that attaches the member-id (the single non-forgeable point,
> `schema-that-settles.md` §2.1) — a *designed tag* over the already-real write,
> exactly as `member` and `sustain` are. The flag names the record as *inside a pair's
> working*: the student's op is attributable to the student (their `member`), but it
> booked *against the veteran's held phase* — so the society's reads (§5) can
> distinguish "a student's early, sheltered work" from "a lone member's contribution
> or drain."

The flag is the pair's legibility. Without it, the locality rule (`schema-that-settles.md`
§2.3) would read a student's early overdraws as that member's *own* drain — a 
beginner's turbulence booked as a fault the student alone made, and the veteran's
absorbed cost invisible. With it, **the pair books as one working over the session**:
each op carries its own `member`, but the `mentorship` flag unifies them into the
pair's joint book — the honest accounting of a costed teaching act. This is the
ledger-level twin of §5's misattribution guard: the boarding is honest *at the
record*, not only at the read.

**[design]** The `mentorship` flag is this doc's designed extension of the settled
schema — one field over the world-writer's write, no new channel, no new subsystem,
the exact class of additive extension `schema-that-settles.md` already
migrates/lands (§6e). The pair's two `member` lines (§2.1's pair) are real; the flag
that binds them for a session is this doc's tag.

## 5. The pair's place — the society's teaching shape

The apprenticeship is not a two-player minigame tucked away; it is a **civic
institution of the field's society** — the shape the corpus's adoption/election
docs give to roles, applied to the oldest human learning pair. Four strands:

**The apprentice becomes the veteran.** `the-inheritance.md` designed the will — the
deliberate bequest of craft across the *re-lock* (§1). The apprenticeship is the
*live* form of that bequest, **before the will's records**: craft that is passed
*hand to hand, in person, while both are alive* is the same continuity
`the-inheritance.md` makes a claim-semantic, enacted as a pair. A player who
apprentices under a veteran and later holds a phase window for their own student is
**the inheritance's living cycle**: the apprentice becomes the veteran, and the
craft's continuity is not a record but a hand held against the next hand. The
apprentice-to-veteran arc is the society's teaching shape — the reason a settlement
with veterans can grow its own.

**The pair feeds the commons.** `field-npc-ai.md` §3.1 makes a village a Qi bath —
a shared high-`q` core that raises every member's efficiency, superadditive
(§3.1). The veteran's held phase is itself a *local working*; the student's early
work inside it is done *in a coherent frame*, so the pair's ops are the pair's
**contribution to the bath** — the student is productive within the commons from
their first sheltered acts, not a beginner idling on the edges. The pair is a
*productive member of the commons together*, feeding the Qi bath the way any working
does (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.2), while the veteran's faster accrual (the price of that
early productivity) is a cost the society's Still Room repays (§2b). The apprenticeship
is thus not a "training penalty" for the settlement — it is the settlement growing its
own coherence, one pair at a time.

**The misattribution guard — attribution honest.** A sheltered student's early
turbulence **must not read as the veteran's drain.** `the-burden.md` §5 is the
guard's origin: a heavy practitioner's carried ε² must never book as a wound or a
margin-causer ("the Board may name a *pattern*, never a *person* off a single
reading"). For the pair, the guard is structural: the `mentorship` flag (§4) books
the student's early overdraws to the *student's* record as sheltered acts, and the
veteran's faster accrual to the *veteran's* record as the cost of holding — so the
Board (`shared-ledger.md` §2) reads the pair as **one working that cost the veteran
and benefited the student**, never as a veteran whose practice drains the bath.
Attribution is honest because the records (§4) and the misattribution guard
(`the-burden.md` §5 / `life-signal.md` §3.3) conspire to keep the pair's joint book
from collapsing into a false blame — the sheltered student is not a fault, and the
teaching veteran is a steward carrying weight, never a drain.

1. **The two-body coupling strength (gate b).** Does a phase-locked pair at exactly
   N = 2 couple as one working the way `field-npc-ai.md` §3.2's group law describes
   at larger N, or is there a minimum group size for a stable organized perturbation?
   The shelter's existence rides the answer — a [probe] over the two-body phase-lock,

   *player-veteran* design, but a `field-npc-ai.md` mentor (a deterministic teacher
   NPC that holds a phase window and corrects) is the natural later form. Is the
   player-holding-a-window case designable independently of the NPC mentor, and does
   the NPC form inherit `field-npc-ai.md` §6d's determinism (a pure function of its Π
   pattern × channels) the same way? The persona is [design]; the *working* it holds
   is real. **[design]**
3. **The shelter's floor vs. honest failure (§2.2).** How much novice overdraw the
   veteran's hold absorbs before the student's overdraw reverts to their own §4.3
   risk — the shelter's exact magnitude — is a probe-calibrated dial. If set too
   high, the shelter is a near-mint (the student never faces their own overdraw); if
   too low, it is a no-op (the student still detonates). The honest boundary is
   "real protection that costs the veteran," and the probe (§6a/§7) must find the
   floor that keeps both. **[probe]/[design]**
4. **The student's progress vs. the veteran's credit (§5).** When the pair's joint
   working feeds the commons, how much of the contribution is the student's and how
   much the veteran's hold? The `mentorship` flag books the student's ops to the
   student; but the *veteran's* hold is what made them productive. Does the flag give
   the veteran credit for the shelter, or does the pair book as one working with the
   veteran's credit being *the teaching act itself* (and its burden) rather than a
   share of the student's output? This is the Board's reading of the pair's civic
   contribution, and it needs the shared-ledger ethics (`shared-ledger.md` §3.3/§6e)
   extended to pairs. **[design]**
5. **The apprenticeship across the pair's deaths (§5).** If the apprentice *becomes*
   the veteran (the inheritance's live form), does the mentorship bond survive a
   death — the student inherits the veteran's role *in the pair* the way
   `schema-that-settles.md` §2.1.1's same-member-line carries a Board place? Or does
   an apprenticeship end with a body, passing only the *felt* craft (not a record)?
   The inheritance's claim-vs-find boundary (`the-inheritance.md` §7.4; `the-burden.md`
   §7.5 — the debt dies with the run) is inherited, not settled here. **[design]**
