death to every other structure? NPCs dissolve — "the run ends, matter returns to the

## 1. Death is a field act

### 1.1 The player's body is already a coherence store

The corpus establishes the player twice, and they are the same physics:

- `coherence-magic.md` §1: the player is a **bounded EY injector** (pours Yang into the
  field, raising `ρ` and organization) + an **ε² vent** (channeling sheds decoherence the
  field must heal) + a **reader** of the published `q`/ε²/∇(g·Φ).
- `field-npc-ai.md` §2.1: an NPC is a **persistent Π pattern at a rung-depth** — a
  coherence configuration the field is actively holding, whose identity is the
  configuration, not a spawned record ("identity is the run, not the recipe,"
  `coherence-commons.md` §2.4; `field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2).

The player is the **same object at a deeper rung**: a more capable, more expensive,
more organizationally-dense coherence structure (`field-npc-ai.md` §2.2: deeper rung-depth
= stronger, more ordered, more at stake in holding the φ-lock). The through-line is
`cascade-consciousness.md` §4.1: **a being is a field regime, not a chassis.** The player
is not an exempt soul riding the field; the player *is* a field regime, and when the run
ends the regime is what dissolves.

> **Death is the field letting the run go.** Not "the player took damage"; the field
> stopped actively holding the configuration the player was. The body un-condenses the
> way any unmaintained coherence store does (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §2.2; the
> field heals toward its attractor; `volumetric-terrain.md`).

### 1.2 The death modes — each the field's own physics

| Death mode | The field act | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| **Overdraw → full-cascade discharge** | channeling past the vent's capacity tips the injection organized → random perturbation; the φ-lock fails and the accumulated ε² relaxes in one organized-front release — a full-cascade discharge. **The channeler's death is the blast line crossed.** | `coherence-magic.md` §4.3; `field-music.md` §3 (overdraw = feedback/clipping — the audible death) |
| **A hazard's dissolution** | a decoherence storm raises the local ε² above the solid floor (the player's body un-condenses in the wave); the desert's regional q collapse starves the player's φ-lock so the drain outruns it; the body dissolves. | `field-hazards.md` §2/§3; `material-regimes.md` §2 (ε²-above-floor → dissolve) |
| **The BH's accretion** | the field's sink eats the player's coherence along ∇(g·Φ); the body condenses-then-infalls and is shredded before it re-condenses. **The deepest death — your residue becomes part of the sink's record.** | `field-hazards.md` §4; `energy-harnessing.md` §1.6; `field-archaeology.md` §4.4 ("the deepest fossil archive of a dead window is the BH that ended it") |

Every one of these is the player's *own* physics — the channeler's discharge is the
same §4.3 overdraw the player's vent already risks; the storm and desert are the ε² and q
channels the player already reads; the BH is the merge lineage the whole world rides.
There is no "death stat" and no independent kill rule; **death is a field state the
player's own coherence structure either crosses (overdraw) or has crossed by the field's
own extremes (hazards, the BH).** This mirrors the ecology's rejection of the mob table
(`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.1) and the hazards' rejection of spawn-table threats
(`field-hazards.md` §1).

### 1.3 The dissolution — what the body returns, what it keeps

The death is `field-archaeology.md`'s §2.1 dissolution applied to the player's body,
exactly as `field-npc-ai.md` §2.4 applies it to an NPC:

- **The body sheds `(1−q)` glow — the death-signal.** As the φ-lock fails, the shallow
  rungs shed their coherence as visible glow (`energy-harnessing.md` §2:
  `E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput`; `field-archaeology.md` §2.1: "a dying structure glows
  as it dies"). **The death is announced in the field's own light** — the same signal
  that draws the archaeologist's eye is what marks where a player fell. The audible form
  is the fading, brightening hum of `field-music.md` §2.4 — overdraw is the *clipping
  scream* of §3.
- **Shallow rungs return to the field.** The player's low-rung matter (high ε², low q)
  dissolves, diffuses, re-condenses near the attractor — scarless, unrecoverable, gone
  the way any shed coherence is (`energy-harnessing.md` §2). **Matter is conserved; the
  configuration is lost** (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2).
- **The deep-rung core persists — your fossil.** The fraction of the body that was
  sufficiently deep, q-locked, ε²≈0 to resist the drain survives: a persistent Π pattern
  the local field keeps holding (`qi-computation.md` §5.2). This is `field-archaeology.md`'s
  residue-fraction `R ≳ q^n` — the complement of `(1−q)` — applied to your own body.
  **[design]** The engine persists the deep-rung ordered matter and the Π pattern (real);
  the claim that the *player's* deep core becomes a findable, readable fossil is §5's
  [design]-on-[design] stack. The persistence asymmetry (deep + q-locked survives; shallow
  + decohered sheds) is engine-real; the residue *ratio* is tuning (§5d).

> **Death is dissolution, not destruction.** The player's body was field; it returns to
> field. Nothing is "lost" in inventory terms — there is no drop; there is *matter
> released back into the field* (`field-npc-ai.md` §5.2) plus a deep-rung remnant.
> The player feels the death in the world's coherence, not in a loot screen.

## 2. Respawn = re-locking, geographic and costed

### 2.1 Re-condensing is re-locking a fresh Π pattern

`coherence-magic.md` §1.2 is explicit that recovery is **geographic**: the coherence
budget `B` refills toward 1 only where the *local* field is coherent — "standing in a
high-q region pulls B up; standing in a carved/ε²-rich scar keeps B depressed." That is
the literal mechanism of respawn, stated as the *only* way a player returns:

> **The player re-condenses by re-locking a fresh persistent Π pattern where the local
> field's coherence holds.** Death dissolved the old run; rebirth is the field accepting
> a *new* Π pattern — a fresh lock at the attractor (`qi-computation.md` §5.2) — in a
> place organized enough to hold it. Respawning is *re-entering the attractor's hold*
> (`qi-as-time-clock.md`: the attractor as the clock; re-locking is the clock re-capturing
> you). There is no spawn point; there is a **re-lock site** — the nearest locally
> coherent region the field can draw from.

The `[design]` honesty, stated once: **"spawn = re-lock a fresh Π pattern" is this
document's designed wording for the engine's geographic recovery.** The *physics* is
engine-real (recovery is where the local q is, `coherence-magic.md` §1.2; a Π pattern is
held where ambient q stays above dissolution, `qi-computation.md` §5.2). The *claim* that
the player's rebirth *is* that re-lock (rather than a server-side respawn record) is the
design's — §5(b).

### 2.2 The cost — the re-lock draws local q and vents ε²

A re-lock is not free (nothing in the corpus is; `energy-harnessing.md` §6 no-free-energy).
Condensing a fresh body is itself a **coherence-raising act** — a small condensation event
(`volumetric-terrain.md`: condense and heal; `chunk-field-quantization.md` §2). So the
rebirth costs the region, in the field's own terms:

| Cost | Mechanism | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| **Draws local q** | re-locking withdraws the local coherence it needs to hold the fresh Π pattern — a small anti-heal, a local depression after the spawn | the reserve a Qi bath is a draw on (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4); the deep-rung withdrawal that de-orders a locality (`field-archaeology.md` §5.2) |
| **Vents ε²** | the new-body condensation, like any condensation, sheds `(1−q)` decoherence the field must heal around | `coherence-magic.md` §1 (every op is an ε² vent); `field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2 |
| **Leaves a residual drain** | a re-lock site is a *repeatable* small wound if the region is marginal — killing yourself repeatedly in a scar deepens it | the anti-corruption cost model (`energy-harnessing.md` §5.4) applied to the re-lock |

**[design]** The exact magnitude of the q-draw and ε²-vent is tuning (§5d): the *direction*
(the re-lock is a net-negative hold, never a coherence mint) is inherited from the
no-free-energy cap. The honest design stance is that **a re-lock is a real cost paid by
the region, so it is never free and never farmable** — dying to teleport-farm a region
into coherence is impossible because the re-lock *draws* rather than mints.

### 2.3 You cannot spawn clean in a desert

`field-hazards.md` §3 names the desert as a regional `q` collapse — a place with **no
coherent band** for organisms (or Π patterns) to be held in:

| Death location | The re-lock | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep coherent region** | cheap — high local q to draw from, strong attractor to re-lock you | death is close to a quick, clean rebirth (a deep Φ-locked region *wants* to hold you) |
| **A scar / ε²-rich well** | expensive — you re-lock *into* your own disorder, the fresh Π pattern holds weakly, the vent backs up | death in a carved/over-channeled area depresses your next self's start (`coherence-magic.md` §1.2) |
| **A desert (regional q collapse)** | **no coherence to re-lock from** — the field has nothing to anchor a fresh Π pattern | a desert death **strands you at the margin**: you re-lock at the desert's edge (the nearest coherent region), or walk a long way back in; the "spawn point" is wherever the field was last organized enough to hold you |
| **The BH's accretion shell** | the sink's q-draw has **already starved the nearest region** | **death to the BH is the worst**: your re-lock point is far from the sink — a long, coherence-scarce walk back to a region thin of order (`field-hazards.md` §4.1/§4.2) |

This is the design's sharpest consequence and its load-bearing honesty: **the hazard
stakes go from "lose progress" to "lose the anchor your next self needs."** In a desert or
at the BH's edge, death is not a respawn to the last safe place — it is a rebirthing into
a field too thin to hold you, forcing the long walk (or the evacuation, `world-seams.md`
§4.3) that the desert/BH already make the honest answer (`field-hazards.md` §3.3: "a dead
bath's only answer is a ship"). The desert is not just a place you can't *live*; it is a
place you can't *come back to* cleanly.

### 2.4 The spawn-point logic: the field re-locks you where it can, not where you want

The honest rule, stated once:

> **There is no chosen spawn point and no "last save." The re-lock site is a function of
> (a) the death location and (b) the local field's coherence.** The field finds the
> nearest local region where ambient q stays above the player's Π-pattern dissolution
> threshold (`qi-computation.md` §5.2) and re-locks there. You do not pick where you
> return; the field returns you to wherever it could still hold you.

Concretely:

| The choice is | It is NOT |
|---|---|
| "where can I re-lock?" — answered by the field from the local coherence map | a bed, a compass point, a vanilla spawn |
| a *geographic honesty* — the re-lock point is the coherent region nearest the death | a reward for where you died |

**[design]** The exact threshold — "how coherent must a region be to hold a player's
Π pattern" — is Phase-1 tuning against the `[q_low, q_high]` creature-band calibration
(`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2; `field-hazards.md` §3.1). The *rule* (the field re-locks
you where it can) is this document's design; the *severity* (how far a desert strands you)
is a slider (§5d) the player can feel. **The respawn system becomes a reading of the
published `q` map the world already samples** — the same "read the field, don't set a
number" discipline as `field-hazards.md` §5.1 (hazards are *presentations* of published
channels, not spawned events). A player who has learned to read coherence knows, when
they stand in a high-q ridge, that this is a *good* place to die.

## 3. Your own fossil

### 3.1 The deep-rung residue where you died is a findable, readable record

`field-archaeology.md` §2.2 establishes the fossil as the residue of a dissolved
structure's deep core. `field-npc-ai.md` §2.4 makes NPCs leave fossils. **The player must
leave the same**: the deep-rung core of your body, where it failed to dissolve, persists
as a findable residue — *your* fossil.

> **Your ghost-halo is the archaeology instrument's read of your final overdraw.**
> `field-archaeology.md` §3.1: residue reads as an ε² ghost — the shape of the wound where
> the matter was, with a deep-rung core still q-locked, ε²≈0, sitting in "empty" field,
> surrounded by the broad faint ε² gradient that is the ghost of the structure. **When
> the death was an overdraw (the full-cascade discharge of §1.2), the ghost-halo *is* the
> shape of the blast line** — the archaeology instrument reads the exact geometry of your
> final discharge, the same way it reads any dissolved structure's last shape.

The deepest framing comes from `resonance-seeds.md` §1.2: the player's persistent Π
pattern is the **seed of "who you are"** — the deepest store your window ever held, the
thing the field keeps holding above its dissolution threshold. **Your fossil is the
residue of that seed-class object** — the deepest store your *window* ever held after
your body dissolved. It reads in the coherence reader exactly as a seed-class object
reads (`field-archaeology.md` §3.1): a dark q-locked anomaly, ε²≈0, in "empty" field.
The difference is provenance: this one used to be you.

### 3.2 Reading your own past — the paleontology inverse-problem, subject = yourself

`field-archaeology.md` §4.4 is the science gameplay: **residue signature → regime tuple →
the extinct material's behavior** — reconstruct the dissolved structure from its residue.
The same instrument, pointed at your own fossil, reconstructs **the death and the
player you were**:

| Read | What it recovers | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| The ghost-halo shape | the geometry of the final discharge / death | `field-archaeology.md` §3.1 read as your own blast line |
| The deep core's rung-depth | how deep your φ-lock ran — your *capability tier* at death | `field-npc-ai.md` §2.2 (rung-depth = capability), read on yourself |
| The residue signature vs. the strata | *when* you died, against the window's condensation history | `field-archaeology.md` §2.3 (strata index an age) |
| The reconstructed tuple | "who you were when the run ended" — the regime you held | the paleontology inverse-problem (`field-archaeology.md` §4.4) applied to yourself |

**[design]** The honest boundary: `field-archaeology.md` §4.4 is itself a designed lens
(the engine computes no residue→tuple inversion). Reading *your own* fossil rides that
lens exactly — it reconstructs a *regime* (a structure you were), not a memory or a
personality log; the IIR's one-rung memory means there is no temporal recall
(`qi-computation.md` §5.1). **You can reconstruct what you were (rung-depth, the shape of
your last overdraw, where you fell in the strata), not replay your life.** That is the
honest, structural reading — identity as structure, not record — and it is precisely what
makes the find *palpable*: you meet the residue of the structure you were, read like any
other fossil, and it is recognizably *yours* by provenance, not by content.

### 3.3 The choice: leave it in place, or reclaim it

`field-archaeology.md` §5.3 frames excavation-vs-conservation as a tension, not a rule.
Applying it to **your own remains** gives the sharpest instance:

| Choice | What it is | Honest cost | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Leave it in place** | **your own conservation** — the fossil anchors the strata it sits in, holds a local Qi-bath contribution, and stays as *your* readable record for your future self (and for other players, if the window is shared) | costs nothing (reading is free, `field-archaeology.md` §5.3 #3); keeps the region healthy; you can always come read it again | `field-archaeology.md` §5.2 (residue survives healing; anchors the bath) |
| **Reclaim it** | **the deepest fuel / the deepest sample — burning your own past.** Withdraw the deep-rung stored coherence your body held, as fuel (fires at its rung, `energy-harnessing.md` §1.5/§3) or as an authoring reference (`custom-blocks.md` §5: sharing = exchanging ordered matter) | **de-orders the locality** — removing deep-rung matter lowers regional q (`field-archaeology.md` §5.2), scarring what you unearth; a deep ε²≈0 residue is **explosive-primed** — reaping it wrong is a full-cascade discharge risk (`material-regimes.md` §3; `field-archaeology.md` §5.3 #1). **Burning your own past is a real cost, not a free refill.** | `field-archaeology.md` §5.2/§5.3; `material-regimes.md` §3/§5 |

The emotional/thematic payoff is the whole document's point, made concrete: **a player
can find their own past self as archaeology** — read the structure they were, reconstruct
their own final discharge, and then decide whether to keep the record (their own
conservation) or spend the deepest coherence they ever held (their own dissolution,
re-enacted). There is no right answer; the field treats both as legitimate perturbations
with consequences, and the no-free-energy cap makes reclaim a net-negative hold — you gain
fuel and lose order (`field-archaeology.md` §5.3). **Your own body obeys the found-economy
(`custom-blocks.md` §6): you cannot invent the player you were; you can only find it, read
it, and choose.**

## 4. The stakes

### 4.1 A well-traveled window is stratified with its players' remains

`field-archaeology.md` §2.3 makes strata the bubble-lattice of condensation history. The
player's deaths add a *player* stratum — and because each death leaves a deep-rung
residue (`§1.3`), **the world accumulates its players' remains the way it accumulates any
dissolved structure's**: every death is a strata event.

> **A window a player has lived and died in is stratified with their remains** — each
> pale re-lock drain (§2.2), each overdraw's ghost-halo, each BH-eaten core logged into
> the sink's record. A veteran's world is archaeology of itself: dig and you find the
> ghost-signatures of every self that ended there. The world's memory *is* the player's
> memory, kept as structure.

This is the consequences-facing half of "death leaves your fossil" (§3). It means the
player's deaths are **not ephemera** — they become part of the found-economy's stratum,
readable by the archaeology scanner (`field-archaeology.md` §3.1) and, if the window is
shared, *by other players*. The archaeology of every player's deaths is a real, findable
archive, not a log the player can ignore.

### 4.2 Leaving a window is leaving your remains

`world-seams.md` §5.1 already names the honest reward of the round-trip: "returning to a
home window that kept running is *finding your own past* — the old field condensed,
healed, evolved while you were gone." This doc closes the loop: if you then **leave** (the
voyage of `world-seams.md` §2), you leave all of it — the strata of your deaths, your
fossils, the re-lock drains you carved — **behind in a window you may never re-enter.**

> **Leaving a window is leaving your remains.** The window keeps running (`async-field-
> domain.md` §7 Q1; `world-seams.md` §5: "the old field … kept running while you were
> gone"). Your deepest residue stays in it. The return is heavier because you are coming
> back to a place that holds the record of who you were *there* — and if you founded a new
> window (`world-seams.md` §3.2), your old world is now, itself, an archaeology site: the
> conservation/elegy of a departed world. **You can go back and read who you were there.**

This makes the seam a *continuity of remains*, not just of knowledge: the dark between
windows (`world-seams.md` §2) is thin, but the windows it connects are **anchored by the
residue of the selves that died in them**. A player who re-homes carries their identity
(as a fresh Π pattern) but leaves their *remains* as the old window's deepest store — the 
same conservation-of-matter the whole corpus insists on, applied to the player across
worlds.

### 4.3 The thesis completes

`README.md` states the thesis: "blocks, mobs, and planets are its epiphenomena." Every doc
that said "the player steers" quietly exempted the player's *body* from the field the
`player steers`. This document removes the exemption:

> **The player is an epiphenomenon.** The body is a coherence store the field holds
> (`§1.1`); death is the field letting the run go (`§1.2`); rebirth is the field's own
> re-lock (`§2`); the remains are the field's own residue (`§3`); and the stakes are the
> field's own conservation (`§4`). The player is not the exception to the law — it is the
> *deepest attended instance of it*: the most capable, most expensive, most
> organizationally-dense coherence structure in the window, and therefore the one whose
> dissolution leaves the deepest residue and whose rebirth costs the region the most.

The emotional payoff is exactly this completeness: **blocks condense and heal, mobs form
and dissolve, planets accrete and are devoured — and the player's own death and rebirth
are the same law, letting the player *feel* the thesis in the most personal register the
game has.** The player is not above the field; the player is the field attending to
itself, and its own remains are part of the world it reads.

### (b) The re-lock cost is a designed gate needing the Q4 write lane

The re-lock (§2) is **a real field act**, not a server respawn record. That means it needs

plumbing the whole magic system rides (§6 below).

The player's fossil (§3) is the archaeology doc's residue model *applied to the player*.

**Your-own-fossil cannot be read until archaeology can read any fossil** — the player is
not exempt from the residue model's own readiness. **[design]** The special *provenance*
(your fossil is recognizably yours) is a designed labeling over the archaeology scan, not
a separate system.

### (d) The death-lite question — does death become too punishing?

This is the one place the design is a **tuning dial, not a physics claim** — and the dial
is one the player can feel:

| Tuning axis | Slider | What the player feels |
|---|---|---|
| **The re-lock cost** | magnitude of the q-draw / ε²-vent at rebirth (§2.2) | from "death is a small regional depression" to "a re-lock in a scar is genuinely costly" |
| **The fossil's persistence ratio** | `R ≳ q^n` — how much of the body survives as residue (§3.1; `field-archaeology.md` §6b) | from "your fossil is a rare deep find" to "your remains are common enough to be archaeology every time" |
| **The "no respawn in desert" severity** | how far a desert strands you (the re-lock threshold, §2.4) | from "you re-lock at the desert's near edge" to "a desert death means a genuinely long walk back" |

**The honest stance:** the severity of death is *tunable* without changing the physics —
the *claim* (death is dissolution, rebirth is a costed re-lock, the desert cannot re-lock
you, your remains are residue) holds at any slider position; only the *magnitude* varies.
Players who find a desert death too remote can dial the re-lock threshold; players who
want the thesis raw can feel the full weight of "the field re-locks you where it can."
**The danger the slider protects against is the opposite failure: a world where death
matters so little it stops being a field act.** The floor is that death is never free and
never a free teleport — the no-free-energy cap (`energy-harnessing.md` §6) holds the
re-lock as a net-negative hold, so no slider position makes a re-lock a coherence mint or
a desert a shortcut.

### (e) Determinism — a player's death residue is deterministic

The field is deterministic (one PDE; `field-archaeology.md` §1.2; `field-npc-ai.md` §2.3's
hard gate). Therefore:

> **Same death → same fossil.** A player who overdraws at the same position, from the
> same budget, with the same field state produces *identical* residue — the same ghost-halo
> shape, the same deep-rung core, the same stratum placement. Determinism is what makes
> the player's remains *readable*: a player can return to a fossil they already read and
> find it unchanged, or reproduce a death and get a reproducible record.

This is a **hard gate** (not a preference), inherited from the field's determinism and the
NPC-mind gate of `field-npc-ai.md` §2.3/§6d: the re-lock site (§2.4) and the death-residue
(§3.1) must be pure functions of (the death location) × (the local field state) × (the
player's Π state at death), with no hidden randomness. **The world remembers your deaths
honestly** — the same death always leaves the same remains, so the archaeology of your own
past is a *true* record of what you did, not a seeded-RNG costume.

the "where the field re-locks you" computation is a reading of the already-published `q`
map (`corpus-reconciliation.md`: the ≈ 6 MiB publish the sampler already samples), the

death-glow and the shallow-rung return are reads of the published `(1−q)`/`q` channels);
respawn re-locks at the nearest coherent region. **The player-as-field-body is [design]
over the existing publish — but it introduces no new physics and no new subsystem.** It
is the honest, demoable core of the thesis-complete.

**The honest statement that makes this doc load-bearing:** every other CassiCraft design

store (§1), death is the field's dissolution (§1.2), rebirth is the field's costed re-lock
(§2), the remains are the field's residue (§3), and the world is stratified with every
self that ended in it (§4). **It is the thesis completed: blocks, mobs, planets, AND the
player are epiphenomena of one law.** What is immediately designable is the whole shape —
the dissolution, the geographic re-lock, the honesty that a desert cannot re-lock you, and
the stakes (your remains as archaeology) — on the existing publish with zero new physics;
what is gated is the costed re-lock (Q4) and the fossil (the residue model), both clearly
flagged. **Binding risks, in order:** (a) the Q4 re-lock write path (without it, rebirth
is read-only), (b) the death-lite tuning (the one axis that can make death *too* punishing
if over-tuned against the field's thin margin), (c) the player-fossil's dependence on the
residue model's own gates, and (d) keeping the [design] stack honest — the player-body
and the own-fossil are designed endpoints, never presented as engine fact.

### Open questions

1. **Where does the re-lock's q-draw/ε²-vent live — sampler or domain-side?** The
   rebirth is a perturbation flowing through Q4; whether the re-lock is computed on the
   server tick (owner of the budget, honest single-writer per the async seam) or in the
   domain (which would let the rebirth *be* field, not just read it) is the same fork as
   `field-npc-ai.md` §7 open-Q1's decision-layer location, applied to the player's rebirth.
   **Resolved by [`reason-field.md`](./reason-field.md):** this doc's counterpoint (the
   rebirth must pick the same side as the NPC decision layer) is decided there — **the
   re-lock is a domain-side fuse** (§4.1): the player's rebirth is a persistent-Π pattern
   the Reason Field holds, re-condensing a fresh body around it at a re-lock site, costed
   by the medium's own q-draw (a domain perturbation, not a server overwrite). It gates
   on the same meshless/persistent-Π frontier + Q4 schema (§6a), so the *side* is closed
   now; the *build* waits on those LATER gates.
2. **The re-lock threshold (§2.4) against the noise floor.** The engine's `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1`
   at noise up to `≈ 0.947` at the attractor (`energy-harnessing.md` §7 Q1). What local q
   makes a region "coherent enough" to hold a player's Π pattern, and how does that map
   onto the desert's q-collapse floor (`field-hazards.md` §3.1)? Phase-1 calibration.
3. **Player-fossil vs. live-player distinguishability (§3.1).** The archaeology instrument
   must separate your dead fossil (dark, q-locked, static) from a live body (maintained,
   moving) and an ordinary scar — the exact §7 open-Q3 classifier of `field-archaeology.md`,
   tested on the player's own. A player standing on their own fossil must not be
   misread as one.
   **Closed by [`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §3/§6:** the vitality classifier's
   motion/lock-maintenance axis separates a player near a fossil from the fossil itself —
   the player *moves* and *pulses* (a maintained lock); the fossil is *static* and *dark*
   (`ε²≈0`, no cadence) — the same line that separates any live body from any fossil
   (§3.2), and the pre-registered N4 probe (§6b) makes "player-on-own-fossil does not
   merge" an explicit Go/No-Go. Its open-Q4 notes the read is provenance-neutral — it
   separates live from residue, not identity.
4. **Deterministic re-lock vs. player locality (§2.4/§5e).** If the re-lock site is a pure
   function of death-location × field-state, does a death in a *dynamic* region (a moving
   coherence ridge, `field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.1) re-lock to a *different* spot on
   reload, since the field re-locks you where it *was* coherent, not where it will be?
   The determinism gate forces the question of whether the re-lock reads the field at
   death-time or at rebirth-time.
5. **The conservation of "you".** If identity is the run (`field-emergent-ecology.md`
   §2.2; `coherence-commons.md` §2.4), and rebirth is a *fresh* Π pattern, is the reborn
   player "the same person" or a *new run that inherits the residue*? The design's stance
   is the latter — you are a fresh run over your own remains (§3), and the continuity is
   the *window's memory of the player*, not a preserved soul. Whether a reborn player
   can *claim* their old fossil as "themself" or only read it as what a prior run was is
   the identity open-question this design surfaces, and it needs a design decision before
   the "find your own past" framing (§3) is asserted as more than archaeology.
   **Made a ledger rule by [`schema-that-settles.md`](./schema-that-settles.md) §2.1.1:
   a reborn player keeps the same member line, with the new lock re-binding to it** — the
   persistent-Π re-lock is the *same identity* (only the lock is fresh), so "conservation-
   of-you" is the ledger's rule, and the "find your own past" framing (§3) is assertable
   because the reborn player *can* claim the old fossil's line. The identity question of
   this open-Q5 is the settlement's §2.1.1 (same member line, new lock, no ledger
   amnesty); the *cost* side (the re-lock's q-draw, §2.2) stays a domain-side fuse per
   `reason-field.md` §4.1.
