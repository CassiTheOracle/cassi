## 3. The load-bearing design — lightning as the weather stack's visible violence

This is the Lightning's whole point: **the weather stack has every register of the
field doing something *over time* — and no register of the field doing something
*at once, at its most energetic*.** The storm gathers and passes; the wind carries;
the cold wears; the rain falls; the blizzard drives; the fog holds; the meteor
arrives. Each is a *state or a motion* the instruments read continuously and the
settlement learns. **The lightning is the one event that is not a state and not a
motion — it is the *release*: the moment the gathered disorder exceeds its hold and
lets go at once, spent in a stroke.**

**The load-bearing design, stated once: the lightning is the weather stack's visible
violence — the same field's most energetic visible event, and its honesty is that
it is *release, not message*.**

|| | The weather stack's register | The lightning's place |
|---|---|---|---|
|| **The storm gathers** | the `ε²` front accumulates and travels, wounding and passing | the lightning is the gathering's **end** — the front's accumulated excess letting go at once |
|| **The wind transports** | the current carries the front, the spores, the signatures | the lightning rides the current's field, but is not a transport — it is the *sharp* event the current's field produces at its extreme |
|| **The aurora sheds** | coherence discharges down channels as a sustained glow | the lightning is that discharge's **violent concentration** — the glow snapped to the flash |
|| **The blizzard erases** | the white buries every read | the lightning is the **spent, gone** release — the white's violent inverse, spent in a flash rather than a wall |
|| **The fog hides** | the held blur softens every read | the lightning is the **sharp inverse** — the one register that spikes a read to its loudest |
|| **The meteor arrives** | a body crosses and lands with a seed | the lightning **flashes and is gone** — no body, no seed, only the spent discharge's scar-etch |

**Why it is release, not message.** The storm is the field's own extreme — the one
hazard that can arrive with no player-caused trigger, the field's own dynamics, not a
punishment (`field-hazards.md` §6.2). Its gathering is a fact about the field. The
lightning is that gathering's release — a *mechanical* consequence of the storm's own
accumulation crossing the field's capacity to hold its φ-lock. There is no will
behind it, no intent, no address to the player: **the charge gathered past its hold,
and it let go.** A lightning that "spoke" — that arrived as a judgment, a warning, a
celebration, a portent — would be a message the field was never sending. The
provenance read the weather doc carries (`weather-not-storm.md` §2) applies to the
flash exactly as to the storm: it is weather if it is the field's own self-limiting
release happening *where the field is*; it is the aimed class only if it tracks the
player's trail (a deliberate decoherence-wielder's vent is the *willful* cousin, and
the provenance read — `(1−q)` signature, restless-aurora sky — tells it apart on the
same channels, `field-npc-ai.md` §4.3 / `weather-not-storm.md` §2.2). The lightning
does not need a new classifier; **it inherits the provenance read of the storm it
