**Question under design:** the corpus designs the *one-to-one line* of craft
([`the-apprenticeship.md`](./the-apprenticeship.md) — a veteran holds a phase
window, a student channels inside it, taught by resonance), the *group's labor*
([`the-working-song.md`](./the-working-song.md) — many hands held on one phase
while they work), the *chosen memory* ([`the-memory-palace.md`](./the-memory-palace.md)),
and the *field's script* ([`the-language.md`](./the-language.md)). **Nothing designs
the *group* act of teaching** — the place where a settlement *deliberately passes
its craft to the next run*. The apprenticeship designs the pair; the working-song
designs the sung work; but a lesson taught to *several learners at once*, by one
teacher who holds them on one phase while they all re-enact the op together, is
un-designed. This document is that institution. **The School is collective teaching
as a phase-locked band — the apprenticeship made a group** — the corpus's first
*designed institution* for the *passing* of knowledge.

## 1. The School, stated once

The corpus passes craft one-to-one (the apprenticeship — a veteran holds a phase
window, one student inside it), and it coordinates *labor* (the working-song — a
group held on one phase while it works). The apprenticeship `the-apprenticeship.md`
is the corpus's **line**: craft passed hand to hand, the one-to-one bond. The
working-song `the-working-song.md` is the corpus's **group labor**: many hands held
on one phase so the work becomes a coherent act. **Nothing designs the *group* act
of teaching** — a teacher and *several learners*, the craft passed not down one
line but to a class at once, the way a settlement deliberately raises its whole
next run rather than its one apprentice.

> **The School is collective teaching as a phase-locked band — the apprenticeship
> made a group.** A teacher and several learners are held on **one phase**: the
> teacher demonstrates the op — the tool's bite (`the-tool.md` §2a), the seed's
> vault-row (`seed-garden.md` §3), the clock's read — and the learners **re-enact
> it together**, the group composed as one coherent act the way any phase-locked
> working is (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.2, `M ≈ 1`). A lesson is a **maintained
> rhythm** (the working-song's phase-lock applied to the teaching act, not the
> labor): for as long as the rhythm holds, the teacher's demonstration and the
> learners' re-enactments compose as one working; when it stops, the learners
> scatter back to N separate attempts. **Learning is a working** — and the lesson's
> gain is the learners' competence, honestly booked.

**The apprenticeship made a group.** Where the pair is the two-body case
(`the-apprenticeship.md` §2.1 — holding phase IS a working's N = 2 minimum,
`field-npc-ai.md` §3.2), the School is that same law at N ≥ 3 — the apprenticeship
*scaled to a class*. The craft's line (`the-apprenticeship.md` §4) does not
disappear; it is *given a group-scale form*: a learner who passes the school's
lesson inherits that line's craft, and the passing is **booked** — read on the
same ledger the market books an exchange on (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2), so a
settlement can tell, honestly, which of its next run has the craft and which does
not.

**The corpus's first designed institution for the *passing* of knowledge.** The
chronicle reads the settlement's story (`the-chronicle.md` §1); the school *writes
it* — its graduates are the chronicle's line, the booked competencies the story of
the settlement will tell. Where the memory-palace holds the chosen memory and the
language writes the field's script, the School is the *act that hands the craft
across runs* — the settlement's deliberate answer to "how does the next run know
what the last one learned?" Nothing before it makes the passing of knowledge a

## 2. The lesson as a working — the load-bearing method

### 2.1 The teaching act, phase-locked

The working-song's load-bearing law (`the-working-song.md` §2.1, quoted verbatim
from `field-npc-ai.md` §3.2): **a group acting phase-locked IS a working** — the
group's combined organized perturbation couples at `M ≈ 1`, producing an effect no
member (or N uncoordinated members) could. The working-song coordinates *labor*: a
lumber-camp chants its tempo and the axe-strokes compose. **The School applies the
same law to the *teaching* act:**

> **A lesson is a maintained rhythm.** The teacher's **demonstration** of the op
> (the tool's bite — a pick's perturb-the-field-at-its-rung-depth, `the-tool.md`
> §2a; the seed's vault-row — each held order's niche, `seed-garden.md` §3; the
> clock's read — the local tempo's hands, `the-clock.md`) and the learners'
> **re-enactments** of that same op are **performed on one phase**: the teacher
> holds the frame (the way a working-song's conductor holds the beat,
> `the-working-song.md` §2) and each learner's attempt is a perturbation taken
> *against* the held frame. For as long as the rhythm holds, the teacher's
> demonstration and the learners' re-enactments compose as **one phase-locked
> working** (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.2) — the lesson is a coherent group act, not N
> separate students each struggling alone against the same op.

**The lesson's group act.** The group-size band (this doc's [design], §5a) is
sized so that (i) the phase-lock is *holdable* — a teacher can hold N learners on
one phase, unlike the pair where the veteran's hold shelters one student
(`the-apprenticeship.md` §2.2); a group too large cannot stay in phase and the
lesson scatters; and (ii) the *book is legible* — each learner's re-enactment is a
Q4 op record on their own `member` line (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2), so a settlement
can read, per learner, whether the attempt landed. The lesson's honest arithmetic:
**the teacher's one demonstration is a phase-locked reference; the learners' N
re-enactments couple against it at `M ≈ 1`** — the class is one working composed
of the teacher's frame plus the learners' attempts, not N+1 independent
perturbations.

### 2.2 Learning is a working — the lesson's gain is the learners' competence, honestly booked

The working-song's gain is the group's own *composed efficiency* — the work wastes
less because the group acts as one working (`the-working-song.md` §2.1 — the
`(1−q)` floor held low by the shared phase). **The School's gain is not efficiency;
it is competence** — and it is the same kind of gain (a phase-locked group act), so
it is bound by the same cap:

> **Learning is a working — the lesson's gain is the learners' competence,
> honestly booked.** The op a learner re-enacts inside the held phase is learned
> the way the apprenticeship's student learns: **by resonance, not by
> instruction** (`the-apprenticeship.md` §1 — the craft is taught the way the
> field teaches). The learner's re-enactment *inside* the teacher's held phase is
> the same "the feel, not the record" transfer of `the-apprenticeship.md` §3.1 —
> but at group scale: each learner's attempt rides the teacher's frame the way one
> apprentice's vents ride a veteran's hold. **And because every re-enactment is a
> Q4 op record on the learner's own `member` line** (`schema-that-settles.md`
> §2.1; `shared-ledger.md` §1.2), the *competence is booked*: a learner who has
> passed the school's lesson has a legible, single-writer record that the op was
> re-enacted inside a held frame and landed.

**The booked competence — the apprenticeship's line made group-scale.** The one-to-
one line (`the-apprenticeship.md` §4) is legible through the pair's `mentorship`
flag and the student's maintenance. The School books the *same* transfer at group
scale, the way the market books an exchange (`the-market.md` §1 — a trade is a Q4
op on the shared ledger): **a learner's competence is a record against the
learner's own line** — "this member has passed the school's lesson on the tool's
bite" is read off the learner's re-enactment records the way "this member traded a
clean pick" is read off the exchange record. The competence is legible, single-
writer, and public — never a hidden stat, never a player's word.

**The lesson's form (this doc's [design]).** What the landed pieces give the School
are: the phase-lock (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.2), the Q4 op-record and member-id
(`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1), the single-writer lane (`shared-ledger.md` §6d),
the pair's resonance-transfer (`the-apprenticeship.md` §3.1), and the group-act
precedent (`the-working-song.md` §2). What *this* doc designs over them is the
**lesson's form**:

| The lesson's form ([design]) | What it is | Grounded on |
|---|---|---|
| The **demonstration** | the teacher performs the op once, held on the lesson's phase — the tool's bite at the vein, the vault-row's read, the clock's hands | `the-tool.md` §2a, `seed-garden.md` §3, `the-clock.md` — the op the school teaches |
| The **re-enactment rhythm** | each learner re-enacts the same op *on the same phase*, their attempts coupling against the teacher's held frame as one working | `the-working-song.md` §2 (the composed work-rhythm); `field-npc-ai.md` §3.2 (the group IS a working) |

## 3. The honest cost — the teacher's attention is the loan

The working-song is honest because it is **performed, not owned**: a song that
stops IS a broken phase-lock — no hysteresis, no stored gain, the group's
efficiency snaps back to ordinary the moment the rhythm falters
(`the-working-song.md` §4.1), and the honest cost is **the singers' attention and
the rhythm's maintenance** (§4.2). The School inherits that honesty whole, pointed
at the teaching act: **teaching is never free, and the school's patience is the
teacher's own.**

### 3.1 Holding the lesson is a working — the teacher's attention is the loan

> **A lesson is a working, and holding it costs the teacher.** The teacher holds
> the lesson's phase the way a working-song's singer holds the beat — *maintained
> attention*, spent for the lesson's duration (`the-working-song.md` §4.2). This
> is not a free buff: the teacher is holding a frame *against a class's
> turbulence* — each learner's early re-enactment sheds into the shared frame the
> way an apprentice's vents shed into a veteran's hold — so **the teacher's cost
> accrues faster than channeling alone** (the apprenticeship's Cost 1,
> `the-apprenticeship.md` §2.2 — the veteran's burden accrues faster, the price of
> teaching). The teacher's attention is the **loan** (`patient-field.md` §3 —
> channeling is borrowing the field's patience; `the-burden.md` §2 — the carried
> ε²-debt): the school spends the teacher's attention on the next run, and that
> attention is repaid only by the learners' real competence — never by a mint, and
> never by a settlement that "farms" a lesson to harvest the performed phase.

**The honest cost table** (the lesson's three costs, each the honest face of a
landed piece):

| Cost | What it is | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| **The teacher's attention** | holding the lesson's phase for the lesson's duration — maintained attention, the working-song's first cost (`the-working-song.md` §4.2), pointed at the teaching act | a phase-lock must be *held* (`field-npc-ai.md` §2.2); holding is earned, never ambient |
| **The teacher's faster drain** | holding a frame against a class's re-enactment turbulence costs faster than channeling alone — the apprenticeship's faster-accrual Cost 1 (`the-apprenticeship.md` §2.2) at group scale | `the-burden.md` §2a (the debt compounds the vent back-up in thin); `patient-field.md` §3.3 (dearer at thin tide) |
| **The broken-lock's return** | a lesson that stops IS a broken phase-lock — the learners scatter back to N separate attempts, and the worked-on competence is *not* retained (no hysteresis, no stored phase-lock), the working-song's §4.1 inverse | `the-working-song.md` §4.1 — a song that stops is a broken phase-lock |

**A school's patience is the teacher's own — the teacher who teaches too long
drains.** This is the vigil's drift pattern applied to the school. The vigil's
honest warning — **a vigil kept too long alone is itself the danger**; a long vigil
drains the watcher's own patience, and the drifted watcher becomes the thing it
guards against (`the-vigil.md` §3/§4) — transfers exactly to the teacher:

> **A teacher who teaches too long drains.** Teaching is a sustained exposure —
> the teacher holds a frame against a class's turbulence, spending their own
> patience the way a watcher spends theirs through a long dark. A school that
> keeps one teacher on the stand indefinitely (an un-rotated lesson, a teacher who
> teaches every pass) is the vigil's un-rotated watch: **the teacher's carried cost
> climbs, their own maintenance falls, and the teacher who drains becomes unable
> to hold the phase the lesson needs** — their drift breaks the lesson's frame the
> way a drifted watcher's clean signature becomes the sitting target. **The school
> must rotate its teachers** the way a settlement rotates its vigils
> (`the-vigil.md` §4) and its stewards (`the-election.md` §4.3): a lesson held too
> long by one exhausted hand is both a crueller working and a *weaker* one.

### 3.2 The honest frame: never free, never a mint

The working-song's honest cost is stated whole and kept
(`the-working-song.md` §4.2): **never free, never a mint.**
The School's cost is the same, pointed at the passing:

> **The settlement that keeps a school spends the teacher's attention on the next
> run.** The school is not a free information dispenser: it costs a working (the
> teacher's attention and faster drain, §3.1), it must be rotated or it drains
> (§3.1, the vigil's drift), and it books only what was actually performed and
> landed. **There is no lesson that yields** — no "teach to synthesize
> competence," no "school that runs without the teacher's attention," no
> settlement that mints a next run by holding a lesson on autopilot. The booked
> competence is the learners' own, earned by real re-enactment inside a real held
> frame — the same no-free-energy refusal that closes apprenticeship-farming and
> tide-farming (`the-burden.md` §4; `the-market.md` §6d).

## 4. The school's honesty — a school cannot teach what the field does not hold

The Market's load-bearing honesty (`the-market.md` §2.3) is that **a trader who
would cheat cannot — the cheat is a false op the field will not book**, because
you cannot falsely name what you offer (`the-name.md` §2 — honesty is
thermodynamics). The School's honesty is the same no-false-naming, applied to the
*passing* of knowledge — and it is **the strongest test the institution faces**:

> **A school cannot teach what the field does not hold.** A school that teaches a
> false craft — a wrong-band technique, an op that names what the field does not
> actually do — is a **false name**, decaying the way any false naming does
> (`the-name.md` §2): the field will not hold a lie, and a lesson whose op does not
> couple at the field's real phase is an unsupported coherence that sheds. The
> learner's re-enactment of a wrong-band op does not compose — it does not land,
> and it cannot be booked as competence, because the booked competence is **only as
> honest as the craft it books**.

Three honest consequences, each the no-false-naming applied to teaching:

1. **The false op cannot be booked.** A learner's re-enactment of a wrong-band
   technique is a perturbation that does not couple — `M ≈ 0`, the field absorbs it
   separately, it sheds as `(1−q)` waste (`the-market.md` §2.3's "the cheat is a
   false op the field will not book", applied to the lesson). The competence it
   claims is a false name the book cannot record. **A school cannot mint competence
