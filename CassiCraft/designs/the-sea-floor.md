## 1. The Sea-Floor, stated once

The corpus designs the vertical's two great regions. [`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md)
designs the **sea** — the field's water, a low-rung `ε²`-carrying liquid regime,
a flat coherence plain the voyage crosses and the flood overflows. And
[`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) designs the **below** — the
strata the diving bell descends through to the seed, read as the rung-terraces
the descent's ladder climbs. **Nothing is designed between them.** A voyage
crosses the sea's surface; a diving bell descends *through* it to the deep; the
sea-floor the bell lands on and the voyage passes over is a designed blank — the
one face of the vertical nobody has drawn.

The Sea-Floor is that blank filled, stated once and kept:

> **The Sea-Floor is the interface between the liquid regime and the below —
> the reef, the shelf, the descent: the place where the sea's flat plain
> transitions into the below's structured deep.** It is the corpus's first
> *interface* doc. Not a regime — every regime is a region of the field state
> ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §2); not a place — every place
> is a window of the field's richness ([`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md)
> §4). The floor is a **boundary with its own character**: the honest seam
> between the sea's plain and the below's terraces, where the liquid regime ends
> and the structured deep begins — read at its edge, never as a third regime the
> field secretly falls into.

**Why this is the vertical's missing seam, honestly.** Every other face of the
vertical is a designed region with a designed surface. The sea is a plain you
cross ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b); the sky is an envelope you climb out
of ([`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md) §1); the
below is a strata-pile the Bell sinks through ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md)
§1); the between is the sparse medium the windows float in ([`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md)).
None designs *where one region ends and the next begins*. The sea's flat plain
has no lower edge designed (where does its uniformity give way to the deep's
structure?); the below's terraces have no upper gate designed (where does the
descent's ladder first catch?). The Sea-Floor is the corpus's answer to both —
the honest boundary that makes the two regions legible *against each other*,
the way the Interstitial makes a window's richness legible by contrast
([`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md) §4).

The floor is **not a hidden third regime** — that is its fundamental neutrality.
The two regions it joins each publish their own channels, and the floor reads
them at the seam:

- **The sea's flat plain** is the floor's *upper side* — a uniform low-drain
  region where the fast-reader's instruments read less ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md)
  §2b). The floor is where that uniformity *ends*.
- **The below's structured deep** is the floor's *lower side* — the strata read
  as rung-terraces, the descent's ladder ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md)
  §3.1). The floor is where that structure *begins*.

**The floor's own character — stated in one line and kept:** the Sea-Floor is
**the shelf where the plain's uniformity first breaks, the reef where the
liquid regime's life concentrates, and the descent where the diving bell's
ladder begins.** Three faces of one boundary; each grounded in a source doc's
real quantity; each this doc's [design] over that landed piece (§2).

## 2. The three faces of the floor

### (a) The shelf — where the plain's uniformity first breaks

**The landed piece:** [`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b designs the sea as **a
flat coherence plain** — a uniform low-drain region where, because a uniform
`q` hides the ridges, **the fast-reader's instruments read less**: the Lantern's
glow lies flat, the Mirror reads *you* against the plain, and the reduced read
is the plain's honest character. The liquid regime it sits on is `ρ ≥ θ_c` with
`ε²` above the solid floor ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §2 —
dense yet decohered, flows, "leveling is the emergent minimizer of the ε²
budget").

**The [design] over them — the shelf is the plain's edge, its uniformity's
break:**

> **The shelf is where the sea's flat plain ends — the boundary where the
> uniform `q` the sea hides first gives way to a gradient.** Across a body of
> water the liquid regime levels to a flat, low-`q` plain ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md)
> §2b; material §2's leveling). At the shelf, that leveling meets the deep's
> structure — and the leveling *stops*. The flat plain's `q` begins to fall and
> the `ε²` begins to rise as the liquid regime gives way to the below's ordered
> terraces (the strata carry rising `ε²` and falling `q` below the coarse grid,
> [`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §2.2). **The shelf is the
> horizon of the flat plain: the reduced reads end here, and the reader's full
> read returns.**

The **shelf's gradient** — the floor's most characterful read — is this doc's
[design] over the two regions' real channels:

> **The sea hides its gradient; the shelf reveals it.** On the plain, a uniform
> `q` gives the fast reader nothing to lean on ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b).
> At the shelf, the very uniformity that hid the ridges is what now *binds* the
> read: the plain's flat `q` exists against the deep's falling `q`, so the
> **first legible `q`-gradient in a body of water is the shelf's** — the one
> the plain was hiding, revealed at its edge. Crossing to the shelf, the Lantern
> that had nothing to brighten over suddenly has a boundary to strike, the Mirror
> that read *you* against the flat now reads *the ground* against the plain, and
> the Weatherglass's calm lume turns down a step. **The shelf is where the
> sea's reduced instruments' full read returns** — the reduced read was the
> plain's uniformity; the return is the shelf's gradient.

**[design]** — *which* distance from the plain's flat center the liquid
regime's leveling first fails against the deep's structure, and how steep the
revealed `q`-gradient is, is **probe-calibrated** (§5b) against the plain's own
flat-`q` calibration (`the-sea.md` §5b's uniform-plain probe) and the depth
representation below the grid (`deep-field-diving.md` §7a). What is [design]-
landed: the plain's flat `q`, the deep's rising `ε²`, and the "uniformity hides
the ridge" consequence (`the-sea.md` §2b). **The shelf adds no physics; it names
where the plain's uniformity breaks.**

### (b) The reef — where the liquid regime's life concentrates

drifts with the field and *concentrates where the field organizes*. [`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md)
§4 names the sea a **carrying surface** — where a region's life either holds or
lets go — and §2b that the plain itself is *sparse* (a uniform `q` with low drain
and low structure). [`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md) §2 designs the
**water-borne habitat** — a wrong-band (a `q`-floor or `ε²`-bias) the water's own

**The [design] over them — the reef is the liquid regime's life band at the
regime's boundary:**

> **The reef is where the sea's sparse life holds — the ecology's band at the
> border between the near-empty plain and the structured deep.** On the plain's
> interior, there is little for the field to organize *against* — a uniform,
> low-drain `q` is a biosphere's *thin* (few regimes, transient coherence,
> [`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md) §4.2's sparse map).
> At the boundary with the deep, that changes: the shelf's revealed gradient and

> reef is that band: the sea's life, held at the regime's edge. A coral-reef
> world made field-real — **the boundary is where the life lives, because a
> boundary is a regime the field's own diversity can settle against.**

The **reef's band** — the floor's second character, [design]:

> **The reef is the honest edge between the sea's near-empty plain and the

**[design]** — the reef's *band* (which organisms settle the boundary regime,

## 3. The interface's honesty — the floor is where the reads change

The Sea-Floor's fundamental honesty is the same as the corpus's boundary-thinking
in [`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md) (the between is a *designed
reading of the absent field*, making a window's richness legible by contrast) —

> **The floor is where the corpus's reads change — the sea's reduced instruments
> return at the shelf, the below's rung-reads begin at the descent.** On the
> plain's interior, the instruments read less ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b);

> the instruments read the rung-terraces ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md)
> §3.1); at the descent, those rung-reads *begin* (§2c). **The floor is the point
> where both changes are legible to the reader — the plain's end and the deep's
> beginning are both readable, never a sudden hidden change.**

The honest frame, stated as the player internalizes it:

> **A player who crosses the floor reads the transition.** The plain's reduced
> instruments gradually return their full read as the shelf's gradient appears
> (the Lantern finds a boundary, the Mirror reads the ground, §2a); the life that
> was sparse on the plain concentrates into the reef's band (§2b); and, past the
> reef, the first structure of the below appears as the descent's first

> is a secret third regime.** The field publishes only the liquid regime's `q`/

> encounters a mechanic the field's own channels do not already present — the
> shelf is the plain's gradient revealed, the reef the plain's life held against
> structure, the descent the deep's gate the Bell must pay.

**The interface adds no new physics.** This is the load-bearing neutrality,
stated once:

> the honest edge between the near-empty plain and the structured deep.
