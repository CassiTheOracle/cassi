## 1. Cooking as composing a meal as a new edible regime, stated once

> **A dish is a composed regime.** The farm (`farm-that-feeds.md`) grows a crop as a
> shallow-rung high-`q` structure riding a maintained band — a single harvest, edible
> stored coherence taken at tide-high. The kitchen's move is *combination*: a cook
> combines harvested regimes — their `(ξ, ω₀², θ_c, n)` tuples (`custom-blocks.md`
> §1) — under the law into a **resultant edible regime**, a composed tuple the eater
> takes in as a meal. Cooking is the same authoring act as placing a custom block,
> turned inward: **a recipe is an authored regime you *eat* rather than place.**

The load-bearing move is the word *compose*. Both ingoing anchors are already
built by the corpus:

- **The crop is a constant-tuple regime.** `farm-that-feeds.md` §2 is explicit: a
  plant is `material-regimes.md` §2's table at crop scale — "a point in `(ρ, q,
  ε²)` plus a constant-tuple `(ξ, ω₀², θ_c, n)`." A harvest is edible stored
  coherence, "the deeper the crop's run holds, the more stored coherence the harvest
  carries into the eater's budget" (§4).
- **A material is a regime, and authoring is dialing a tuple.** `custom-blocks.md`
  §1 collapses the per-material behavior table into the tuple `(ξ, ω₀², θ_c, n)` —
  every behavior (fall, flow, burn, explode, conduct, hard, fuel, appearance) a
  *derived column* of the law, nothing stored. §2's material lab is an in-world
  authoring surface: dial the four constants, then **see the predicted emergent
  signature** — "the lab computes, *from the same formulas the world uses* (the
  registry's derived columns, not a side-simulator), a small live patch of the
  candidate regime exercised inside the frame — the law runs it."

The kitchen is the material lab's logic pointed at the plate. Where the lab dials
an arbitrary tuple to place as a block, the kitchen **combines the tuples it is
given** — the harvested regimes — into a composed one, and previews that
composition in a bounded patch before it is eaten. The distinction is honest and
structural:

| | The material lab (`custom-blocks.md` §2) | The kitchen (this doc) |
|---|---|---|
| Input | a player dials **arbitrary** `(ξ, ω₀², θ_c, n)` | the cook combines **harvested** tuples — regimes the field actually grew |
| Preview | a bounded live patch runs the law on the candidate tuple | a bounded live patch runs the law on the candidate **composition** |
| Outcome | a **placed** regime (a block/body in the world) | an **eaten** regime (a meal that feeds/drains the budget) |
| Economy | found vs invented (`custom-blocks.md` §6) | found recipe vs invented recipe — the same split, on food |
| Gated on | Q4 + the material gates (Phase-1.5) | Q4 + the regime constants + the mouths-eye read (Phase-1-lean) |

The kitchen's preview differs from the lab's in one designed way: the lab previews
*behavior and appearance* (will it fall/flow/burn), while the kitchen previews the
*resultant tuple's edible effect* (feed vs drain, on the eater's `B`). But the
mechanism is the same — **a bounded patch runs the law on the candidate combination
before it is realized** — and that is the honest core this doc builds on.

## 2. The dish as an authored regime you eat

### 2.1 A recipe is a composed tuple

A recipe is a **composition rule over the ingredients' tuples**: combine
ingredient regimes `(ξ₁, ω₀₁², θ_c₁, n₁)` … `(ξ_k, ω₀ₖ², θ_cₖ, n_k)` under the law
into a resultant `(ξ_R, ω₀_R², θ_c_R, n_R)`. What "combine under the law" means, and
the honest line, are stated once:

- **The ingredient tuples are the designed crop constants** — `farm-that-feeds.md`
  §2's crop table (grain `n ≈ 0`, root `n ≈ 1`, bitter green `n ≈ 0` at the thin
  edge, deep-heart `n ≈ 2`), each a shallow-rung regime whose band/rung/harvest-peak
  are **[design]** constants probe-calibrated against the engine-real precipitation
  law (§7).
- **The composition rule — how the ingredient tuples fold into a resultant tuple —
  is this doc's [design], probe-calibrated.** The honest form is a **weighted
  merging of the regime knobs** under the constraint economy: the resultant rung
  `n_R` is the *dominant* ingredient's depth (the deepest stored-coherence carrier
  sets the meal's intensity, `mouths-eye.md` §1.2 deep-rung = intense); the resultant
  resonance `ω₀_R²` and condensation `θ_c_R` fold the ingredients' values toward a
  designed blend; and the `(1−q)` waste law (`energy-harnessing.md` §2) — the meal's
  toll — applies to the composed store as it is taken in. **[design]** The exact fold
  function is a presentation constant, probe-calibrated so a menu is legible, not
  free energy (see §7b and §8).
- **The law it runs under — the precipitation/preview mechanics — is engine-real.**
  The kitchen preview runs the *same* law the world uses (the bounded-patch sync-touch
  path of `async-field-domain.md` §4.2, exactly as `custom-blocks.md` §2 specifies for
  the lab), so the resultant tuple's *realized* behavior is what the preview showed.

A recipe is authored the way a custom block is authored — dialing/combining knobs —
except its realization is a **Q4 write into the eater's budget channel**, not a
precipitation into the world. The dish is a **hand-off of rung access at the mouth**
(`custom-blocks.md` §5: an authored material is "a hand-off of rung access, not a
blueprint").

### 2.2 What a dish does — feed or drain, on the budget channel

`coherence-magic.md` §1.2 fixes the player as a bounded EY injector and ε² vent,
with a coherence budget `B ∈ [0,1]` that recovers geographically. `mouths-eye.md`
§3/§4 makes eating that recovery's *active* channel: **consuming ordered matter
feeds `B`; consuming disorder loads ε² onto the vent and raises overdraw risk.**
The dish is the *deliberate, composed* version of that choice:

| The dish | Resultant tuple's character | Effect on the eater | Grounding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A feed** | shallow `n_R`, high-`q` band, low `ε²`-footprint | **brightens `B`** — a mild withdrawal from the field's stored order into the budget; the composed "pull B up" of `coherence-magic.md` §1.2 made ingestive | `mouths-eye.md` §4.1 (recharge); `farm-that-feeds.md` §4 (high-`q` = bright) |
| **A drain** | deep `n_R`, `ε²`-adjacent, or eaten at thin tide | **loads the Burden / pushes toward overdraw** — a heavy, deep-rung meal at the wrong time is the overdraw boundary on the tongue | `mouths-eye.md` §3.3 (the blast-on-the-tongue); `the-burden.md` §2a (thin compounds) |

The `the-burden.md` tie is the meal's *recovery* end: **what you eat feeds `B` but
can also feed or drain the carried debt.** `the-burden.md` §2b is explicit that the
ε² you shed is a damaged-locality-you-drag-with-you, cleared cheaply in a Still
Room's patient/coherent time. A dish carries this in two directions:

- **A well-composed feed eaten at harvest clears toward the Budget's recovery and
  does not compound the carried ε²** — the meal is organized order the body can
  withdraw, the "coherent rest" of `the-burden.md` §2b made ingestive at its daily
  instance.
- **A heavy deep-rung meal, or a feed eaten wrong (uncontrolled release),
  de-orders into ε²** — a drain on the body *and* a contribution to the carried
  debt, exactly `mouths-eye.md` §3.3's fire-vs-blast distinction applied to a
  composed meal.

The deliberate framing is the point: **a dish is composed *to* feed or *to* drain —
a cook authoring a meal is authoring the eater's budget response.** This is the
recipe/craft hole `farm-that-feeds.md` left open: the farm grows food, `mouths-eye`
eats it, but nothing *combined* the ingredients into a prepared, deliberate
coherence. The kitchen is that combination.

### 2.3 The taste-as-read tie — the flavor IS the field history

`mouths-eye.md` §1/§5 grounds the taste-map: **a material's flavor IS its regime
read** (`custom-blocks.md` §6), and the connoisseur's economy catalogues regimes as
a wine list — deep-rung = intense, high-`q` = bright. For a *composed* dish this is
compounded and made legible:

> **The flavor IS the field history of the ingredients and the cook's hand.** The
> dish's taste reads, at once: each ingredient's regime ("root was stewarded at
> depth", "bitter green grew on the scar's flank", `farm-that-feeds.md` §2), the
> tide's phase ("harvested at tide-high" = rich and near-lossless, `mouths-eye.md`
> §4.3), and the cook's own composition (how the knobs were folded, whether the
> release was controlled or rushed). A mouth that reads the tuple can taste the
> whole chain: grew → combined → eaten, and whether each was done well or ill.

This is `field-music.md`'s score-tie made gustatory: **a recipe is the culinary
score.** `field-music.md` §4.1 is explicit that a coherence score is "an ordered
sequence of Q4 field-operation records" — a composed field act, costed and gated
the same way as every channeled op. A recipe is the same object on the mouth: an
ordered composition of harvested regimes that, eaten, performs a bounded field act
on the eater's budget. The kitchen is where a settlement "plays" its food as
deliberately as it plays the field.

## 3. The kitchen as a mini-lab

### 3.1 The bounded-patch preview, applied to the plate

`custom-blocks.md` §2's material lab is the model, and it is honest about its
mechanism: *"The lab computes, from the same formulas the world uses (the registry's
derived columns of §1, not a side-simulator with separate physics), a preview of
both behavior and appearance: a small live patch of the candidate regime exercised
inside the frame — the law runs it (small bounded locality, the synchronous-touch
path of `async-field-domain.md` §4.2 for player-touched outcome)."*

The kitchen applies that exactly to the plate:

> **A candidate recipe's resultant tuple is *run* in a bounded patch before it is
> eaten.** The cook assembles the ingredients' tuples into a candidate composition,
> the kitchen's bounded frame runs the law on it — the composed regime's `(ρ, q,
> ε²)` signature realized in the patch — and the cook reads the *result*: what the
> composed dish feeds or drains, at harvest vs thin, whether it holds or
> meta-decays toward a stable neighbor (`custom-blocks.md` §3's stability
> classification). The preview is not a recipe card; it is **the law's verdict on
> the composition**, rendered as an edible-read before intake.

The honest difference from the lab — and the reason this is *not* a mint — is
stated in §2.1/§4: the kitchen previews the composition's *edible effect*, and the
composition itself never exceeds what its ingredients held. The preview is a
**diagnostic consumer** of the publish (like the weatherglass family:
`field-instruments.md` §2.1 — a consumer with a presentation idiom, never a new
channel), and the *realization* (the eaten feed/drain) is a **bounded Q4 write** on
the eater's budget channel.

### 3.2 The found-vs-invented economy, on food

`custom-blocks.md` §6 splits the economy: **finding** is cheap (read-only, bounded
by what the field has made); **inventing** is expensive (spent coherence, unbounded
within the realizable space). The kitchen inherits this split for recipes:

| Economy | Cost | Bounded by | The culinary form |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A found recipe** | cheap — the recipe is *documented*, not composed | what the field/a ruin's pantry actually provides | a recipe read off a ruin's pantry (`field-archaeology.md`'s found-only regimes, `field-archaeology.md` §4.1, "regimes the field no longer precipitates") — documented inherited food, free to know, bounded by what exists |
| **An invented recipe** | a bounded Q4 write + the ε² vent — composing is *costly* | the realizable-composition space (the law's verdict on the fold, §3.1) | a new composition dialed in the kitchen lab — the only path to food the settlement has not already made, and the richer end (novel blends do new things) |

The honest cost, stated once: **composing is a bounded field act — a Q4 write plus
the vent — never free, never a mint.** The dialing/combining is a channeling act
(budget-spending, vent-shedding, overdraw-risk-carrying, exactly `custom-blocks.md`
§2's lab-as-channeling-surface), and the composition's *effect* on the eater is
bounded by its ingredients. What a cook does is **move stored order from the
ingredients into the composed dish, minus the `(1−q)` toll**, never create it.

### 3.3 A found recipe from a ruin's pantry vs an invented one from the lab

The two registers close the loop with the corpus's archaeology and craft faces:

- **A ruin's pantry** is where the world's own past composed food — a found recipe is
  a *documented* composition the field (or a dead settlement) already held, readable
  and reproducible for free, but bounded by what archaeology surfaces
  (`field-archaeology.md` §4.1: the found-only deep-time regimes).
- **The kitchen lab** is where the player composes new ones — the invented end,
  costing the Q4 write + vent, unbounded within the realizable-composition space.

Both feed the same field history into the eaters; they differ only in source and
cost. This is `custom-blocks.md`'s exact economy, transposed from blocks to
meals — and it makes a settlement's **table** as legible a craft surface as its
Board of custom blocks.

## 4. The food loop closed

### 4.1 Grow → combine → eat → recover

The corpus's food loop was open at the combination step. This doc closes it:

```
farm-that-feeds (grow)   — a crop, a shallow-rung high-q structure, a harvest
kitchen (this doc)       — harvested regimes combined into a composed edible regime
mouths-eye (eat)         — the dish eaten as a field act; feeds/drains the budget

       └── the harvest *is* the tide's daily instance at the table (tide-of-the-attractor)
```

Each leg is already designed; the kitchen is the missing *spine* that turns a
harvest into a deliberate, composed feeding.

### 4.2 The table as a legible field practice — the feast books

`shared-ledger.md` makes a settlement's society a *reading of the Q4 op-stream*:
every channeled op is already a single-writer record, and the Coherence Board
renders each member's net contribution-to/drain-on the shared Qi bath per tide
(§1.2, §2.2). The kitchen rides that record directly:

> **A meal's contribution/drain books on the settlement's ledger.** Every kitchen
> act (compose, vent, feed, drain) is a Q4 op on the single-writer stream
> (`async-field-domain.md` §5.2 — no forged contributions). A cook who composes
> feeds books as a **contribution** (feeding the shared high-`q` core, the
> `heal`/steward class, `shared-ledger.md` §1.2); a cook who composes drains — or a
> settlement whose table *over-feeds the wrong thing* at thin tide — books as the
> **margin-causer** pattern (`shared-ledger.md` §2.2). The feast is a tide-cycle
> event, and **the feast books are the Board's per-tide read of the table**: a
> settlement's table is as legible a field practice as its Board of blocks, and the
> steward-of-the-tide's daily act is a composed, well-timed meal.

The `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5d discipline holds at the table unchanged: **a
harvest feast is the field's organized state, not a mint; a thin-trough fast is the
same store at a worse `(1−q)` toll** (`mouths-eye.md` §4.3's own words, applied to
the composed meal).

### 4.3 The dish's effect on the eater is deterministic — a hard gate

The field is deterministic (one PDE; `life-signal.md` §6d; `field-npc-ai.md` §2.3;
`mouths-eye.md` §1.1's "same regime, same taste"). The composed dish inherits it as
a hard gate, restated for the kitchen:

> **Same dish, same eater state → same feed/drain.** The resultant tuple is a pure
> function of the ingredients' tuples and the composition rule (no hidden
> randomness); the eaten effect is a pure function of the resultant tuple and the
> eater's current `B`/carried-debt state. A cook who learns "this composition feeds
> a rested eater cleanly at harvest" can rely on it; a settlement that books a
> feast's contribution sees the same books for the same meal, the same eater state,
> every tide. The kitchen's verdict is as trustworthy as a taste reading, because
> it runs the same law on the same deterministic publish.

## 6. Honest gates

### (a) The [design]/engine-real line

§5. The composition rule and the edible-read are [design]; the precipitation law,
the bounded-patch preview mechanics, and the eater's budget/vent are engine-real.

### (b) PHASE-1-LEAN — a consumer of the farm + lab preview + mouths-eye read

A kitchen that **combines harvested regimes into an edible tuple, previews it in a
bounded patch, and eats it to feed/drain the reader's budget** is a consumer of the
existing publish + a bounded Q4 write — **no new physics**:

- **reads** the ingredient tuples (the farm's crop constants, `farm-that-feeds.md`
  §2) and the compose preview (the bounded patch running the law from the ≈ 6 MiB
  publish);
- **writes** the composed meal's feed/drain through the **Q4 feed lane**
  (`coherence-magic.md` §5.1; `mouths-eye.md` §3.4 — consuming is a consumer of Q4
  like every energy act), amplitude-capped by `energy-harnessing.md` §6;
- **rides** the mouths-eye read (the deterministic taste/diagnosis of §2.3) as the
  dish's legible effect.

**The Phase-1-lean slice, stated:** combine two harvested crops (e.g. grain `n ≈ 0`
+ root `n ≈ 1`) into a candidate composed tuple; run it in the kitchen's bounded
patch; eat it to feed the reader's `B` by the resultant feed amount, minus the
`(1−q)` toll. This is gated on (i) the **regime constants** — the crop tuples and
`custom-blocks.md`'s realizable-composition space; and (ii) the **Q4 feed lane** —
the consuming write path `mouths-eye.md` §3.4 gates on. Before both land, the
kitchen is read-only (it can document and preview composed regimes but not eat them),
exactly the lab's Phase-1 read-only slice (`custom-blocks.md` §7). **No new physics;
the kitchen is a consumer of the farm + the lab preview + the mouths-eye read.**

### (c) Determinism — a hard gate

§4.3. Same ingredients, same field state → same dish; same dish, same eater state →
same feed/drain. No seeded randomness; the composed tuple is a pure function of the
ingredient tuples and the composition rule. This is what makes the kitchen
*trustworthy as craft* and the feast *honest as bookkeeping*.

### (d) The no-free-energy cap — a meal is a withdrawal, never a mint

`energy-harnessing.md` §6 inherited unchanged through the meal's route: **no
composition yields more than its ingredients sank; `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` amplitude
caps hold the write-back lane; a dish is a withdrawal that de-orders what was
eaten, minus the `(1−q)` toll; the ambient attractor drift is forbidden to charge a
stored dish.** A cook cannot mint a feed by re-folding the same ingredients any more
than a farm can mint by holding a high tide. The feast's richness is bounded by the
harvest's `q` and the tide — stewardship moves the meal *within* that bound, never
above it.

### (e) Accessibility — the dish's effect is readable, never hidden-only

Every ingredient's regime and every composed dish's feed/drain is a consumer of the
publish (the crop's `q`/`ε²`, the preview's realized signature, the eater's `B`).
**The dish's effect is readable from the reader/glass — the composed tuple's
`(ρ, q, ε²)` rendered on the reader overlay and the Weatherglass/Board, never
hidden-only.** The triad/accessibility rule (`field-music.md` §6; `mouths-eye.md`
§2.1) holds unchanged: a player who cannot taste loses nothing the instruments
already show; a player who can eat reads the same composed field the tool displays.
The feast books (§4.2) put the meal's field meaning on the settlement's public
Board, not in a private "satiation" counter.

## 7. Open questions

1. **The composition rule's numeric fold.** What exactly is the weighted merge that
   turns ingredient tuples into the resultant `(ξ_R, ω₀_R², θ_c_R, n_R)` — which knob
   dominates (deepest `n` as the intensity carrier?), how `ω₀²`/`θ_c` blend, and how
   the `(1−q)` toll is attributed across the composed store? **[design]**, the same
   calibration family as `farm-that-feeds.md` §8.1's crop-band tuning and
   `mouths-eye.md` §7.1's taste-map thresholds. Phase-1 probe.
2. **The feed/drain threshold vs. the eater's state.** How much carried ε² / how low
   a `B` flips a "same dish, same ingredients" from feed to drain — the exact line
   at which a well-composed meal stops brightening and starts loading the vent? It
   inherits `mouths-eye.md` §7.5/§3.3's fire-vs-blast body-scale threshold and
   `the-burden.md` §7.2's interest dial; set after the Phase-1 read measures the
   composed dish's effect and the carried-debt magnitude.
3. **The bounded-patch → live-domain consistency.** The kitchen preview runs the law
   in an isolated bounded frame (`custom-blocks.md` §2's sync-touch path); will a
   composition that previews as a feed in the frame necessarily feed identically in
   the live, continuously-evolving domain (neighboring coherence, ongoing
   perturbation)? This is `custom-blocks.md` §7 Q1's lab-preview/async-consistency
   probe, applied to the plate — same Phase-1.5 measurement, meal-specific verdict.
4. **The feast-book's attribution across eaters.** A composed meal feeds one eater's
   `B`; does the cook's ledger credit book at the *compose* write or the *eat* read,
   and how is a shared feast (many eaters, one cook) apportioned on the Board? Rides
   `shared-ledger.md` §6c's member-id and the per-tide `C(M)` sum.
5. **A found recipe's reproducibility.** A ruin's-pantry recipe is a *documented*
   composition; is it reproducible exactly (same ingredients, same field state → same
   dish, §6c) or does realizability remain contextual (`custom-blocks.md` §7 Q2) —
   a recipe that worked in a deep coherent ruin may fail in rubble? Preferred stance
   **[assumption]**: identity is the composition (fixed); realizability is
   contextual (region/tide-dependent), inheriting `custom-blocks.md`'s split.

## 8. Feasibility verdict

**PHASE-1-LEAN — closes the food loop; no new physics.**

The kitchen composes harvested regimes into an edible tuple, previews it in a
bounded patch, and eats it to feed/drain the reader's budget. It is a pure
**consumer of existing pieces**: the farm's crop tuples (`farm-that-feeds.md` §2),
the material-lab's bounded-patch preview (`custom-blocks.md` §2 — the same
synchronous-touch path that runs the law on a candidate combination), and the
mouths-eye consuming read (`mouths-eye.md` §3) — all riding the published `(ρ, q,
ε²)` and a bounded Q4 write on the feed lane.

**The Phase-1-lean slice:** combine two harvested shallow-rung crops into a
candidate composed tuple; run it in the kitchen's bounded patch to read its feed;
eat it to brighten `B` by the resultant amount minus the `(1−q)` toll, and book the
meal's contribution on the Board. Gated on (i) the **regime constants** — the crop
tuples and `custom-blocks.md`'s realizable-composition space (Phase-1.5
per-material-constants work) — and (ii) the **Q4 feed lane** — the consuming write
path `mouths-eye.md` §3.4 gates on. Before both land, the kitchen is read-only (it
documents and previews composed regimes but cannot feed), the lab's Phase-1
read-only precedent. **No new channel, no new physics, no new engine code** — the
kitchen reads the field the farm grew and writes a bounded feed the mouth already
executed.

**Binding risks, in order:** **(a)** the composition rule's fold (§7.1) — whether
the weighted tuple-merge, probe-calibrated, yields a *legible, non-minting* menu
(the design's grounded premise); **(b)** the feed/drain threshold vs. eater state
(§7.2) — over-tuned, a "same dish" flips from feed to drain between rests and the
kitchen reads as arbitrary; **(c)** the bounded-patch → live-domain consistency
(§7.3) — the kitchen's preview must predict the eaten reality, the lab's Q1 probe
re-gated on the plate; **(d)** the no-free-energy cap held against "fold the same
ingredients to mint a feed" (`energy-harnessing.md` §6, inherited — a meal is a
withdrawal, never a mint). **None contradicts the async, dual-world, or
regime-collapse architecture** — the kitchen adds no physics; it composes what the
farm grew, previews it with the law, and feeds the eater the same stored order the
mouth was already withdrawing, closing the loop that eating opened and the farm
left ungrown.
