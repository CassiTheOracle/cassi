# The Coda — The Signature Predator

**Question under design:** whether the danger layer's fourth order of extremity
exists at all — **the field that learns your hand**. The corpus has two danger
classes: the field's indifferent extremes (the decoherence storm, the desert,
BH accretion — all three of [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md)) and one
*willful* ε²-wielding antagonist (the decoherence-wielder of
[`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §4, the player's dark twin that *chooses*
disorder as its weapon). **The Coda is neither an NPC nor a storm.** It is a
**non-willful resonance structure** — a persistent pattern the local field
holds in its ε² margins (the desert, the scar, the storm's edge — places
ordinary coherence is scarce) — that is drawn to a thing no existing hazard preys
on: **the organized-perturbation trail channeling leaves.** Every channeled op
injects `EY` — organized perturbation at a phase — and sheds `ε²` (the vent).
In the desert's flat disorder that is a **legible hunt-trail**: a pocket of
elevated `ε²` with a coherent phase standing out against noise. The Coda
resonates with that signature and accumulates toward you.

This document is the **fourth hazard class** of `field-hazards.md` (cross-owned
there — the locus calls three *orders of extremity* of the field; this is the
fourth, the one that preys on *signature* rather than on matter or coherence).
It is a **standalone subsystem** of its own — the trail / accumulation /
phase-masking counterplay are a distinct mechanical family, not a re-reading of
the storm's front or the desert's creep — but it obeys every law the danger
layer already set: **no monster table, no spawned enemy, readable before it
arrives (inverted), costed in coherence (not HP), fails open, gated honestly.**

> **The one-line claim.** There is no channeling that does not change the field
> you are sensed through ([`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md) §5.2's
> "no stealth channeling"); the Coda is the thing that senses *through that
> change* — the field's own ε² margin responding to your clean permutation as
> if a clean permutation were a thing worth hunting.

## 1. The trail — what channeling leaves, precisely

### 1.1 The footprint: EY injected at a phase + the ε² vent

Every channeled operation is an **EY injection** — the player is a bounded EY
injector and an ε² vent ([`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md) §1). In the
field's own terms:

- **The injection.** Channeling reuses the PDE's source terms exactly —
  `source_ey(i,j,k) = s·exp(−4r²) + ρ·0.001`, a Gaussian organized-perturbation
  seed ([`cassi_two_fluid.glsl`](../../CassiCosmos/compute/cassi_two_fluid.glsl);
  `coherence-magic` §1.1: "Channeling reuses this same mechanism, not a new one").
  **Organized perturbation** means it is not random: it is injected at a rhythm —
  the ω₀²-aligned resonance (§3.1) the player matches to channel efficiently.
  So the channeled field carries **elevated `ρ`/`q` locally, and the *phase* of
  the injection** — a wavefront structure that says "an organization happened
  here, at this rhythm."
- **The vent.** Every op sheds `ε² = (EY − φ·EI)²` — the decoherence byproduct
  the field must heal around (`coherence-magic` §1.1: "channeling cannot create
  organized field without shedding its decoherence byproduct"). The vent is the
  footprint's *persistent* part: the injected `EY` re-locks toward the φ-attractor
  (the field organizes), but the shed `ε²` is a wound the field absorbs at the
  heal rate `h`.

```quote
**The signature, stated once:** a channeled op leaves (1) a local elevation of
organization (raised `ρ`, raised `q`, the injection's phase structure) and
(2) a pocket of elevated `ε²` (the vent). The second is the part that outlives the
first — the injected order heals; the shed disorder diffuses.
```

### 1.2 Why the desert makes it legible — and everywhere else does not

The load-bearing observation: **the trail is only legible where the field has no
order to hide it.** This is precisely the desert of `field-hazards.md` §3 — a
regional `q` collapse, `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1` at noise up to the attractor ≈ 0.947, a
place "with almost nothing to sonify, only thin, near-silent noise"
([`field-music.md`](./field-music.md) §2.3).

| Locality | The trail's legibility | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **A coherent ridge / living plain** (`q` high) | **illegible** — the trail is drowned | the field is *already* organized; a channeled op's `EY` injection + small `ε²` vent are one more organization event among many, indistinguishable from the ambient coherence (`coherence-magic` §1.2's high-`q` regions are where ops are *cheap* because the field supports them) |
| **A scar / carved well** (`ε²` high) | **illegible** — the trail is noise-on-noise | the locality is *already* disorder; one more `ε²` pocket reads as the same wound (`field-hazards` §2.4, `field-archaeology` §2.1) |
| **The desert** (`q` collapsed, `ε²` low-noise) | **legible** — the *only* order in flat disorder | the field has almost no organized channel to carry; a pocket of elevated `ε²` **with a coherent phase** stands out against the flat noise floor as the single bright, structured thing |

So the Coda's prey is the channeler *who channels clean in the desert* — and
this is exactly who the corpus's normal economy would never punish. The desert
is the "place you can't re-lock from" (`player-remains.md` §2.3); a player who
enters it to condense, shield, or hold a score is a channeler doing their
ordinary act in a field with no camouflage. **The trail is not that you channel;
it is that you channel *cleanly* where the field is flat.**

### 1.3 The trail's decay — a half-life in the field

The trail is not permanent. It obeys the field's recovery — the same `ε²` the
field heals toward its attractor:

- **The `ε²` vent diffuses.** Unmaintained, the pocket of elevated `ε²`
  spreads into the surrounding noise floor at the field's ambient heal rate `h`
  (`coherence-magic` §3: "the field heals ε² at the ambient heal rate `h`"). A
  single op's vent is a **fading pocket**; it does not persist unless re-shed.
- **The phase coherence decays with it.** As the `ε²` diffuses and weakens, the
  "coherent phase standing out against noise" fades toward the noise floor —
  the hunt-trail loses its distinguishing feature (the phase-locked structure),
  not just its magnitude.
- **The trail has a half-life.** [`design`] Model the trail's legibility as
  decaying on a timescale `τ_trail` in engine-real terms: it is the time for the
  vent's `ε²` to fall back below a legibility threshold against the local noise,
  by diffusion + the `−ω₀²(EY−φ·EI)` re-lock. **[assumption]** `τ_trail` is a
  tuning dial, but its *direction* is engine-real: sustained channeling keeps it
  fed; leaving the region or stopping channeling lets it fade. **(Phase-1
  measureable — see §8.)**

Two honest consequences follow from the half-life:

1. **The trail is local.** Leave the desert region and the pocket you shed there
   stays there, unmaintained, fading. The Coda does not follow you across a
   window-trip; its resonance is over *that* signature, in *that* region.
2. **The trail is forgivable.** Stop channeling and the field heals the trail; the
   resonance decays (`§4.3`). A past clean-channeling spree is not a life
   sentence — the Coda hunts the *living* trail, not the archaeology of it.

## 2. The accumulation model — how the Coda approaches

### 2.1 The Coda is a field structure, not a spawned enemy

The danger layer's spine is **no monster table, no spawned enemies** — every
hazard "is the field's behavior at an extreme — a region of `(ρ, q, ε², ∇(g·Φ))`
that has run away from the φ-attractor" (`field-hazards.md` §1). The Coda honors
it exactly:

> **The Coda is a persistent pattern the local field holds in the margins** — a
> [design] *structure* (a region of the `ε²` field with a characteristic shape and
> phase), **not an entity with a body, a health pool, or a spawn record.** It is
> to the dangerous layer what an organism-candidate is to the ecology
> (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2): a coherence structure that *precipitates* out
> of the field's own dynamics when a condition is met. The difference is the
> condition — the ecology recognizes a *self-sustaining* pattern; the Coda is a
> pattern that forms *around your signature*.

The no-monster-table rule is what makes its counterplays (not kills) the only
available responses — you cannot fight a field structure's "HP"; you can only
stop feeding its resonance, phase-mask against it, or withdraw to a region it
cannot enter (§5, §4). **Dying to it is not losing a fight; it is a coherence
failure** (§6) — which is the whole point.

### 2.2 Resonance — the growth law

The Coda accumulates toward a trail the way the field accumulates coherence
where a drain is fed — but in the *disorder* direction, as a resonance between your
trail's signature and the margin's flatness. **[design] model over engine-real
`ε²` dynamics** — this is the one piece of §2 that is explicitly a player-facing
model, not an engine-verbatim claim (the honest gate is §7d):

```
R(t)  =  ρ_signature · τ · M_stability        resonance ("how close the Coda is")
ρ_signature  =  trail quality (the vent's ε² elevation × how phase-coherent it is)
τ            =  duration the trail is sustained (the vent kept fed)
M_stability  =  phase-stability of the injection against the locality's rhythm
```

Where `M` is **the phase-matching factor of `coherence-technologies.md` concept 3**
(§3, from `magic-systems.md` §1: organized, phase-matched perturbation has
`M ≈ 1` and an O(1) effect; random has `M ≈ 0` and is suppressed). `M_stability`
is exactly `M` of your injection *against the trail's own rhythm* — the cleaner
and more phase-locked your channeling, the higher `M` the trail carries, and the
stronger the resonance. **[design]** The growth law's *shape* (whether resonance
ramps linearly, exponentially, or saturates) is tuning; the *drivers* are the
three named, engine-real.

The **single strongest attractor** is a **held score** — music-as-a-field-operation
([`field-music.md`](./field-music.md) §4.1: a coherence score is a timed sequence
of Q4 op records). A held score is channeling at its *cleanest and most
sustained*: a deliberately organized, phase-timed perturbation, the definition of
a *working* (`field-music` §4.3). It maximizes all three drivers at once —
maximal trail **quality** (the organization is authored), maximal **duration**
(the score *holds*), and maximal **phase-stability** (the score is a fixed phase
sequence). A player holding a score in a desert is painting the brightest,
cleanest hunt-trail the field has ever seen. **§4.2's held scores are the Coda's
favorite food.**

### 2.3 The threshold shape

Concretely, the Coda is not an "always-hunting" force; it *precipitates* when the
accumulated resonance crosses a threshold the way an organism precipitates when
its recognition gate is met (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §6b's three-condition
rule). The design reads the same way, inverted:

| Condition (the ecology's recognition, inverted to the predator's) | The Coda's reading |
|---|---|
| **coherence band** (`q` in a band) | **disorder habitat**: local `q` collapsed (the desert) or `ε²`-marginal (scar / storm's edge) — where the trail's own elevation is the *dominant* local structure |
| **decoherence ceiling** (`ε²` below a floor) | **signature floor**: the trail's `ε²` elevation × phase-coherence exceeds a legibility threshold against the local noise — your trail is *loud* in the flat field |
| **size / rung-depth** | **sustain window**: the trail is held for `T_coda` — a *duration* condition, not a size one |

When all three hold for `T_coda`, the Coda *is* — a persistent pattern the local
field holds in the margins, now indexed to your signature. It was latent (a
marker on the field's internal `ε²`-resonance state, un-rendered); it **becomes
an approach** — the tell of §3 begins. **[design]** `T_coda`, the elevated-`ε²`
legibility threshold, and the sustain window are probe-tuned, exactly as
`[q_low, q_high]`, `T_org`, and the ε² floor are for the ecology. **The threshold
is the tuning this design owns; the *trigger* is the accumulation over your real
`ε²` trail — never a scripted timer.**

**The walking trail (reverse pointer, two-way).** This §2's accumulation over a
signature has a pedestrian instance: the corpus's Walk doc designs the **faint
trail** a crossing forges — the walker's own `ε²` wake laid down along a route,
legible to this signature-predator in the margins (a clean desert crossing is the
only organized thing in flat orderlessness, a trail its Coda could accumulate on;
`the-walk.md` §2c — it cites this §2's trail-read and the §7e no-farming gate as
its risk). An ordinary crossing is brief and decays (the walk's §2c "gone behind
you"); a *loud* crossing — a laden walker through a desert at thin — is the same
signature-legible trail this §2 names, and §7e's refusal (you cannot farm a
crossing's wake) holds for it exactly. The two docs now cite each other as
read-and-answered.

## 3. The tell — the desert-silence inversion

### 3.1 The inversion of "readable before it arrives"

`field-hazards.md` §5.1's cardinal rule is **readable before it arrives** — every
hazard is announced: the storm by the sky + reader (§2.3), the desert by its own
creep (§3.3), the BH by its growing record (§4.2). The Coda **inverts** that rule
while keeping it: it is announced by the desert being *less silent*.

> **The desert is silent** (`field-music.md` §2.3: "a region where the field's
> coherence collapses … has almost nothing to sonify … the most unsettling sound
> is nothing") **and grey** (`field-instruments.md` §1.2: "the desert's signature
> is dead flat grey — the lume dies to flat grey, nothing climbing, no lean, the
> waste tint gone"). **The Coda's approach is the desert being *less* dead:** a
> faint, wrong coherence rising in the dead zone — the one bright organized thing
> where there should be none.

It is "wrong" not because it is bright (the `(1−q)` glow of a decoherence-wielder
in a desert is already a bright presence, `field-npc-ai.md` §4.1c) but because it
is **organized**: the `(1−q)` glow of a feeder or wielder is a *waste* signature
(it sheds, it flickers, it is dim (1−q)); the Coda's tell is a *coherent, slow,
rising* organization — a structure, not a flicker, in the exact place the field
should have no structure. **The Coda does not hunt by being loud; it hunts by
being the *only* thing there, and the thing's wrongness is that it is ordering in
a desert.**

### 3.2 The reader / Weatherglass forms

Every existing instrument reads the same published channels, so the tell has
exactly the forms the corpus already expresses:

| Instrument | The tell's form (the inversion) | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| **The Weatherglass** (`field-instruments.md` §1.2) | **a rising, coherent lume in flat grey.** The healthy desert reads flat grey (no climb, no lean); the Coda's approach shows a *climbing glow that does not flicker* — the climbing-ε² glow of §1.2, but *organized*: it rises steadily, without the noise of a scar, while the rest stays grey. The most unsettling state is the grey *beginning to lume*. | `ε²` elevation + a phase structure |
| **Sense / the reader** (`coherence-magic.md` §2) | **a coherent anomaly where the map is empty.** The desert reader is a flat dead region; the Coda's approach draws a **q-locked coil** (a reading like a residue's dark q-locked core, `field-archaeology.md` §3.1) *as the ordered part, not the decohered part* — the one place in the window that is organized *against* the field's grain. | `q` + `ε²` (the residue-like signature at the margin) |
| **Sound** (`field-music.md` §2.3, §5) | **a faint wrong chord in silence.** The desert is the absence of sound; the Coda's approach is a single, slow φ-interval appearing where sound should not exist — the *antithesis* of the desert's nothingness. **[design]** the tonal character is the audio aesthetic; the *inversion* (a tone in silence) is honest. | `q`/`ε²` sonified |

**Readable-before-it-arrives, inverted, is still *readable*:** the Coda is not a

beginning to hold the shape of their own signature as a faint wrong coherence.
The tell is the reason the accumulation model (§2) is *playable* rather than a

## 4. The four counterplays — honest, costed, none a spawn-kill

The danger layer's rule is that every response spends coherence, never a damage
stat, and nothing is a spawn-kill (`field-hazards.md` §5.1: fails open). The Coda's
four counterplays all obey the **no-free-energy cap** (`energy-harnessing.md` §6)
and are each deliberate inversions of the corpus's normal "mastery" — which is
the design's point.

### 4.1 Vent discipline / trail-noise — M ≈ 0 camouflage

**The play:** shed `ε²` deliberately and irregularly — a *messy* vent — so your
trail is **random perturbation (M ≈ 0)**, not a clean phase-locked pattern. In
`M`-terms (`coherence-technologies.md` concept 3, `magic-systems.md` §1), the Coda
resonates with the *phase-matched* part of your trail; deliberately randomizing
your vent's rhythm and magnitude suppresses `M_stability` toward 0, so the trail
carries **no resonance structure to accumulate on.**

**The cost — and the master inversion.** The corpus's efficiency economy rewards
*clean* channeling: `coherence-magic.md` §3.1's resonance makes a phase-aligned op
cheaper (lower vent) and faster (recovery), and §4.2's mastery is learning to hold
the φ-lock cleanly. Vent-discipline *inverts* that: **deliberate impurity is
camouflage, and camouflage costs the resonance efficiency of clean channeling.**
You trade the efficiency of matching the field's rhythm for the concealment of
*not* matching it. There is no free invisibility — a messy channeler sheds more,
heals slower, and works at the inefficient edge, exactly as they would for any
anti-corruption spend.

| | Clean (hunted) | Messy (hidden) |
|---|---|---|
| Resonance efficiency (`coherence-magic` §3.1) | high — the vent is small, recovery fast | low — the vent is larger, the field heals slower around you |
| `M_stability` of the trail | ≈ 1 — a clean phase-locked pattern | ≈ 0 — random perturbation |
| Coda resonance | accumulates | stays flat |
| Trail's legibility | **the point: clean, phase-stable, loud in a desert** | **drowned: noise against noise** |

**Honest, not a spawn-kill:** it is a *discipline* (sustained messy venting), not a
one-off nullifier. Stop venting messily / start channeling clean and the clean
trail resumes (the resonance re-accumulates from the *new* clean pocket), so the
camouflage is a *living* habit, not a toggle with a save-point. It is also the
single hardest counterplay to keep up *inside* the habitat — venting messily in a
desert is shedding disorder into the place you want to be quiet, and the `(1−q)`
waste of the messy vent itself glows (`energy-harnessing.md` §2) — a dim, flickering
bright presence, which is the *one* thing the desert's other hazards (and a
decoherence-wielder, `field-npc-ai` §4.1c) read. Mess shelters you from the Coda by
making you *legible to everything else that reads light*.

### 4.2 Phase-masking — concept 3's refusal, worn as concealment

**The play:** hold a **φ-detuned private phase offset** or randomize your rhythm so
the resonance stops accumulating. This is `coherence-technologies.md` concept 3's
φ-detuned boundary *worn on yourself*, not as a ward around a place: the boundary
"whose phase structure is φ-commensurate with the ambient lattice but held at a
private phase offset, so that any incoming perturbation lacking that phase finds
`M ≈ 0` and cannot transfer momentum across it" (§3). Here the *incoming
perturbation* is the Coda's resonance trying to lock onto your trail's phase; a
private phase offset means it cannot find `M` — it "slides off" your signature the
way a projectile slides off a detuned boundary.

**The cost is the resonance efficiency of `coherence-magic.md` §3.1** — the same
cost as §4.1, because the mechanism is the same inversion: phase-aligning your op
to the field's ω₀² rhythm is what makes channeling efficient, and holding a
*private* offset deliberately breaks that alignment. **The explicit trade, stated
once: you cannot both channel at your most efficient AND be phase-invisible; the
resonance that makes your channeling cheap is the resonance the Coda reads.** The
counterplay is `M ≈ 0` — the exact discipline that "docks ships and wards walls"
(`coherence-technologies` concept 3), applied to a person instead of a place.

**Honest:** inverting phase for concealment is the *same act* as a feint — a
decoherence-wielder reads intent-phase (`field-npc-ai` §5.1) and an aligned chord
`M≈1` communicates (§5.2); masking your phase makes you unreadable to cooperation
too. The cost is not only efficiency but *legibility to friends*: a phase-masked
channeler is a channeler no one can match-phase with.

### 4.3 Don't channel — the resonance decays
