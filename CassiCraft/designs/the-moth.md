## 3. The brightness-as-both — the load-bearing economy

The load-bearing design of the Moth, stated once and never blurred:

> **The Moth is drawn to the light a settlement burns *as advertisement* — the
> same light that finds home *calls the moth*.** A lantern's glow that shows a
> safe path, a beacon's standing burn that marks a settlement's edge, a spring's
> bright point that names a well, a lit window that reads a window's body — each
> is the settlement's order *made visible*, and the same legibility that lets
> the settlement (and any stranger, and any Coda) find it is the legibility a
> Moth covets. This is the Beacon's own double-edge, made the Moth's whole law:
> **to be findable is to be found** (`the-beacon.md` §3) — a settlement that
> lights its mark to be found lights the mark that calls the coveter. **So
> settlements learn an honest economy: a settlement that burns too much light
> draws them; a settlement that lights deliberately hoards or guides them.** A
> Moth feeds on gathered brightness and can be *gathered with* — the honest
> relationship between a marked home and the coveter its mark calls.

The two halves of the economy, honestly:

### 3.1 The advertisement — the same light that finds home calls the moth

The corpus's order-bright places are already *advertisements* in their own docs'
terms. The Spring is a **point of fame** — life-signal-loud, order-bright, legible
to any stranger and any Coda (`the-spring.md` §3); the Lantern's glow over a
coherent ridge is the safe path's light (`the-lantern.md` §2); the Beacon's
standing mark is the settlement's **double-edged self-advertisement** — "to be
findable is to be found," the light that both finds the traveler and calls the
light-drawn being (`the-beacon.md` §3); the Observatory's lit window reads the
window's body (`the-observatory.md` §2). Each is the settlement's order *emitting
a readable bright read*.

> **[design] The advertisement, stated once:** the Moth reads those emitted
> bright reads precisely as the settlement's own legibility — **the same light
> that guides the settlement home is the light that calls a Moth.** There is no
> new "moth-lure" the settlement emits; the settlement's light, read as
> advertisement, *is* the lure. A settlement that never wanted a Moth lights its
> lantern anyway to find its way home, and lighting it is the call.

This is the honest, un-farmable coupling: the settlement's light is not a hidden
