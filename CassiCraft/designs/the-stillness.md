# The Stillness: The Field at Absolute Rest

full, the storm, the desert — and the patient field's tempo
([`patient-field.md`](./patient-field.md)), but nothing designs the **still-point**:
a region so coherent it has *no read at all*. [`the-silence.md`](./the-silence.md)
designed the three registers of the world's voice *stopping* — empty (gone), held
(at rest), hunting (approaching) — but its grammar lacks a fourth: the silence so
complete it is not absence but **stillness**, where the channels go flat *because
everything is already resolved*. This document is that design.

> **The Stillness is the field at absolute rest** — a high-`q` region so perfectly
> held that its channels go flat: `q` pinned at the attractor's center (no
> gradient), `ε² ≈ 0` (no disorder), `ρ` stable, `∇(g·Φ) ≈ 0` (no steering — the
> river law stilled), and the voice is *gone* — not the silence's empty/hunting
> registers but the **patient floor**: the silence so complete it is not absence
> but stillness. It is the opposite of the flood's deafening (too much signal) and
> the cold's thin (too little). **The Stillness is no signal at all, because
> everything is already resolved.**

## 1. The Stillness, stated once

`field-music.md` gives the world a voice — the sonification of the published

`field-instruments.md` §2.2), and the **hunting** silence (the Coda's approach
heard as an absence, `signature-predator.md` §3). That grammar is complete for the
world's *voice* — but not for the field's *state*. The empty silence is the
field's death; the held silence is the field's *poise*; neither is the field's
**resolved end**.

> **The Stillness, stated once.** The field at absolute rest — a high-`q` region
> so perfectly held that its channels go flat: `q` pinned at the attractor's center
> (no gradient), `ε² ≈ 0` (no disorder), `ρ` stable, `∇(g·Φ) ≈ 0` (no steering —
> the river law stilled), and the voice is *gone* — not the silence's empty/hunting
> registers but the **patient floor**: the silence so complete it is not absence but
> stillness. It is the opposite of the flood's deafening (too much signal) and the
> cold's thin (too little): **the Stillness is no signal at all, because everything
> is already resolved.**

The one-line distinction from the silence's three registers, and from the flood and
the cold:

| Field extreme | The field state | The read | The voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| the flood (deafening) | over-perturbed, too much signal | everywhere-loud | the storm's growl (`field-music.md` §2.2) |

## 3. What lives there

### 3.1 The deep-rung basins that hold without maintenance — the equilibrium residue

The still-point's ecology is not a *population*; it is the **residue the field keeps
holding** because it no longer needs to be maintained. [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md)
§2.3/§6b gives the engine-real ground: residue is stable as long as the local `q`
stays above its dissolution threshold (`q_dissolve`, `qi-computation.md` §5.2 —
persistent Π patterns hold "as long as the ambient q remains above the threshold for
pattern dissolution"). At the still — `q` pinned at the attractor, far above
`q_dissolve` — the deep-rung basins **hold without maintenance**, because there is
no `ε²` drain to outrun and no drift to shed them.

> **The still-point's ecology is the strata at equilibrium.** The deep-rung basins
> that persist anywhere the field is coherent (`field-archaeology.md` §6b) are the
> only "life" a still region has — and at the still they are *done being made*: no
> new deposition, because nothing precipitates (no `q` accumulation, `q` is pinned),
> and nothing erodes, because nothing diffuses (`ε² ≈ 0`). **It is the archaeology of
> a *finished* place** — the residue holds exactly what the field organized before
> it resolved, and will hold it indefinitely, because the resolution is exactly the
> state that protects it.

**[design]** The image of a still region as a *finished stratigraphy* — the 
cross-section of `field-archaeology.md` §2.3's log-periodic bands, held frozen at
their deepest rung — is this doc's design; the *stability* (deep-rung residue holds
above `q_dissolve`) is engine-real (`field-archaeology.md` §6b).

### 3.2 The farm at the still — the band met perfectly

[`farm-that-feeds.md`](./farm-that-feeds.md) makes a farm a *cultivated region whose
soil `q` is held inside a crop's precipitation band* (§1.1), harvested at tide-high
(§2). A crop is a shallow-rung regime the field precipitates *when the bed's `q` sits
in its band* (§1.2, the precipitation law). At the still, the band is met *perfectly*
and *permanently*:

> **The farm at the still is a maximal harvest that is also motionless.** The
> crop's precipitation band is met exactly (`q` pinned at the attractor, inside
> every shallow-rung band), so the field precipitates the crop's full stored
> coherence — but because nothing drifts, nothing needs tending, and because
> nothing precipitates *further*, there is no cycle to harvest. **A farm at the
> still has nothing to steward** (`farm-that-feeds.md` §3's weeding, husbanding,
> harvesting — all become no-ops, because `ε²` does not rise and `q` does not
> move). The harvest is maximal and motionless: richer than any stewarded plot,
> and bound tighter than any — because the no-free-energy cap is *trivially* held,
> the field yields what it has organized and nothing more.

**[design]** "A farm whose band is met at the still" is this doc's image over the
farm doc's crop-band model; the *tending-becomes-pointless* consequence is honest
(the stewardship the farm doc designs is only needed where the field drifts),
flagged as a designed reading, not a new farm mechanic.

### 3.3 The bath at equilibrium — nothing to hold, because nothing leaks

[`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §4.4's Qi bath is a maintained
high-`q` core with a radius — a draw that holds its core `q` up, "brittle if it
goes down." The bath's standing cost exists *because it must fight the `ε²` drain
and the field's drift*. At the still, that cost goes to zero:

> **The bath at equilibrium is a bath with nothing to hold, because nothing
> leaks.** The still-point's `q` is pinned — the field itself holds the core
> flat, so the bath's standing draw (the thing a Qi bath must spend against) is
> met by the field for free. This is the *trivial* face of the no-free-energy cap:
> a still region provides nothing to take (see §4), so there is also nothing the
> player must spend to keep it. **The equilibrium is a gift of the field's own
> resolved state, not a build the player maintains** — the same "brittle if it goes
> down" of `energy-harnessing.md` §4.4, inverted: here it cannot go down on its
> own, because the field does not leak.

**[design]** "A bath with no standing draw" is the still-read's implication of the
bath's own economics at the pinned-`q` limit; the *field-holds-it-for-free* claim is
gated on §6b (the flat state reads in the live sim) and is honest only within the
still's boundary (the first drift re-introduces the draw).

## 4. What the player experiences

### 4.1 The awe of the resolved

The corpus's awe is the patient field's — the world's tempo, the field's patience
(`patient-field.md` §1). The Stillness is that awe at its **absolute**: the Weatherglass
flat (`field-instruments.md` §1.2 — every form at its resting read), the clock frozen
(`the-clock.md` §3.1 — the hand that has stopped because `(1−q) → 0`), the mirror
reading **nothing to change** (`the-mirror.md` §2.4 — a self with nothing to maintain),
and the voice gone because all is whole (this doc §1). It is the field telling the
player, in the only way a field can, **that the work of this place is done.**

> **The awe of the resolved.** The reader is calm and flat. The Weatherglass holds
> a single steady lume with no lean. The clock's hand does not turn. The mirror
> reads *nothing to change*. The world's voice is silent — but not because the field
> is dead (the empty silence) or holding its breath (the held silence): because
> **it has nothing left to say, having said everything**. The player standing at the
> still is standing at the field's end-state — the corpus's quietest awe, the
> patient field's patience at its absolute.

### 4.2 The honest design — not a resource, an experience

The still is emphatically **not a resource**. Every reservoir the corpus designs —
the `∇(g·Φ)` gradient (`energy-harnessing.md` §1.1), the `ε²` budget (§1.3), deep-rung
coherence (§1.5), the Qi bath's multiplier (§4.4) — is *flat* here:

- **Nothing to route.** The `∇(g·Φ) ≈ 0` means there is no gradient to ride — the
  coherence-highway is a road through a featureless plain with nothing to carry
  (§2, `coherence-highway.md`).
- **Nothing to harvest.** A farm's crop is at its maximal stored coherence, but
  there is no cycle to tend and no drift to exploit; the harvest is "maximal" and
  motionless (§3.2).
- **Nothing to heal.** With `ε² ≈ 0`, there is no disorder to suppress, no scar to
  heal, no anti-corruption to run (`energy-harnessing.md` §5.4).
- **The no-free-energy cap is *trivially* held.** Because the field provides
  nothing to take, there is no way to read the still into a mint — `output ≤
  φ⁻¹·input` (`energy-harnessing.md` §6) is trivially satisfied by a region that
  yields nothing at all.

> **The Stillness is an experience of the field's end-state, not a store of it.**
> It is where the player goes to *feel* the resolution (the awe of §4.1), to read
> the strata of a finished place (§3.1), and to witness the fate's quietest horizon
> (§5) — never to extract. The field offers nothing here, and that offering-nothing
> *is* the design: the corpus's quietest awe is the one that costs the player
> everything they could earn, which is nothing, because there is nothing left to
> earn.

### 4.3 The brittle edge — the first perturbation is the first event

The silence doc's honesty is inherited and sharpened. A fully still region is
**brittle in the corpus's sense**:

> **The equilibrium is perfect and precarious.** The still has no event-history —
> nothing has perturbed it since it resolved, so nothing has scarred it and nothing
> has *taught it to heal a scar*. **The first perturbation is the first event in a
> place with no event-history: the stillness breaks, and the breaking is the
> region's first scar.** The field that never had to re-organize around a wound must
> do so for the first time — and a region's first scrape is the one it is least
> practiced at closing, exactly the way a Qi bath is "brittle if it goes down"
> (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4: a structure that has never drained is the one most
> at risk when it finally does).

This is the danger-adjacent honesty the corpus insists on (`the-silence.md` §6b —
an ambiguous read is read honestly; `weather-not-storm.md` §2 — a quiet is never
forced into a register): the still is not a passive refuge; it is a **precarious
completion**, and its flatness is the sign of how far it has not yet been tested.
A player who rests in the still rests in a place that has never needed to protect
itself — and the first event that arrives finds a field with no practice.

## 5. The window's still-horizon

### 5.1 The settle arc's end — a fully settled window is a window done

[`fate-of-a-window.md`](./fate-of-a-window.md) §1.1/§1.2 designs the window's arcs
in field terms: a **settling** window's coherence is organizing toward the attractor;
a **thriving** window's bath is high and stable — a *maintained* state, never
permanent. The still-point is the **settle arc's end-state**, the shape the
organizing momentum is tending toward and the thrive's *maintenance* would hold at
its absolute:

> **A fully settled window is a window *done*.** The settle arc's trajectory — the
> regional `q` rising toward the attractor (`fate-of-a-window.md` §1.1) — reaches, at
> the still, the value it has been organizing toward (§2, the pinned attractor). But
> where the thrive is a *maintained* state (a bath the settlement keeps feeding,
> `fate-of-a-window.md` §1.2), **a fully still window is one that no longer needs
> maintenance — the attractor's victory, not a holding action.** It is the fate's
> quietest horizon: not decay (the desert, `fate-of-a-window.md` §1.3's `q`
> collapse), but **completion**.

The honest boundary, drawn as fate-of-a-window draws it: the still is a **trajectory
endpoint, not a scripted outcome** — it is the field's own attractor reached and
held, gated on §6b (the flat state reads in the live sim), and forecast-≠-fate holds
(`fate-of-a-window.md` §6c): a window does not *become* still; it is *read* as having
settled when its `C(W)` and its `q` hold the flat value, and even then it is a `q`
state, not a permanent class (the first event of §4.3 can break it).

### 5.2 What a settlement does there — the design's final question

Because the still is an *end-state* (not decay, not ongoing thrive), it forces the
corpus's most peaceful question — and the design leaves it honestly open:

> **What does a settlement do at the still?** Two honest answers, neither forced:
>
> **Leave.** The still is where the story is finished; a settlement that resolves a
> region to the still has completed that place's work, and the honest departure is a
> seam-crossing (`world-seams.md` §4) toward a *new* settling window — beginning the
> fate's arc again somewhere the field is still organizing.
>
> **Live in the resolved.** The still is also where the *harvest-maximal,
> maintenance-free* life of §3 lives — a settlement can choose to *inhabit the
> resolved*, doing the no-op "work" of §3.2/§3.3 (nothing to tend, nothing to feed,
> because the field holds it all) and reading only the awe. This is the corpus's
> deepest rest: a window that reaches the still and *stays* is a window that has
> finished its story and chosen, quietly, to keep living in the last chapter.

**[design]** Which a settlement does — leave, or live in the resolved — is the design's
**final question, left open** (mirroring the corpus's own "recorded open, owners
cited" discipline, `corpus-reconciliation.md`). The answer is a player/steward choice,
not a rule the field imposes: a still window *can* be broken (§4.3), *can* be departed
(§5.2), *can* be lived in — and the field, having resolved, is indifferent to which.
