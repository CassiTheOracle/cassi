**Question under design:** the stranger-object layer has seven named faces — the
signature-predator (**hunts** a clean trail, [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md)),
the blight (**turns** a region's life against the field, [`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md)),

**eighth face, the keeper** — the corpus's first being with a *territorial
no-trespass* that is morally legible, the field's own warden who holds the order

**The through-line of the eighth face:** every prior face is something the field

5. **The Guardian across the place's honest decay (§2/§4).** When a guarded place is
