## 1. The Zenith, stated once

The corpus designs the vertical's two outer faces. [`the-sea-floor.md`](./the-sea-floor.md)
designs the **middle-bottom boundary** — the interface where the liquid regime
meets the deep. And [`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md)
designs the **sky** — the window's weather, orbits, and auroras, the envelope you
climb out of. **Nothing is designed at the sky's top.** The sky's doc draws the
ceiling as a level set (where the envelope's ρ crosses the gas floor, §1.4) and
opens it outward — above it is the window's vacuum, and past that the between.
The one face of the vertical that is *both* a designed boundary and an open exit
is the face nobody has given character. The Zenith is that blank top filled,
stated once and kept:

> **The Zenith is the vertical's ceiling — the edge of the window's atmosphere,
> where the sky ends.** It is a **thin, structured edge**: the atmosphere's
> `(1−q)` waste escaping the window at its top — the sky's own drain, slow,
> honest, readable, where the auroras are the waste's *light* and the ozone the
> waste's *residue*. It is the corpus's **second *interface* doc** — not a
> regime, not a place, but a **boundary with its own character**, the seam
> between the window's air and the between's thin. And it is the honest edge:
> **you cannot leave through the zenith.** The voyage is the seam's work, not
> the sky's — a window is not a cup with a lid, and the ceiling's honesty is
> that it is the window's *boundary*, not its *door*.

**Why this is the vertical's missing top, honestly.** Every face of the vertical
is a designed region with a designed edge. The sea is a plain you cross
([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b); the below is a strata-pile the Bell sinks
through ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §1); the sky is an
envelope you climb out of ([`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md)
§1); the between is the sparse medium the windows float in
([`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md)). The sea's plain has a designed
*floor* (the sea-floor, the first interface); the below has a designed *bottom*
(the strata, the birth-strata of [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md)
§2.4). **But the sky has no designed *top*** — the ceiling is where the designed
sky *stops being designed*. The Zenith is the corpus's answer: the second
interface, the honest boundary that makes the window's vertical *top* legible the
way the sea-floor makes its middle-bottom legible — and, by contrast with the
between, makes the sky's own richness legible ([`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md)
§4 — the window is where the field is rich, the between where it is thin).

The zenith is **not a hidden ceiling regime** — that is its fundamental honesty,
mirrored from the sea-floor's neutrality. The two regions it joins each publish
their own channels, and the zenith reads them at the seam:

- **The window's air** is the zenith's *lower side* — the envelope with its
  waves and auroras, the atmosphere's ρ/ε²/q story ([`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md)
  §1). The zenith is where that air *ends*.
- **The between's thin** is the zenith's *far side* — the `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1` field
  with no bath, no regime ([`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md) §2a).
  The zenith is where that thinness *begins*, at the window's own top.

**The zenith's own character — stated in one line and kept:** the Zenith is
**the drain where the atmosphere's `(1−q)` waste escapes the window at its top,
the boundary that is the seam between the window's air and the between's thin,
and the edge you cannot leave through.** Three faces of one ceiling; each
grounded in a source doc's real quantity; each this doc's [design] over that
landed piece (§2).

## 2. The ceiling's character

### (a) The drain — the atmosphere's `(1−q)` waste escaping at the top

**The landed piece:** the `(1−q)` waste law — every working field system bleeds
`E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput` as visible glow (`energy-harnessing.md` §2;
`qi-bubble-propulsion.md` §2.5, adopted corpus-wide). The sky's own docs make this
the atmosphere's honest signature: the waste is the glow you read to find where
energy flows (`energy-harnessing.md` §2 — "the glow is the diagnostic a player
reads"), weather reads as the sky's glow before you feel it
(`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` §1.3), and an **aurora is the (1−q) discharge**
along the coupling-field lines into a drain — a region where ε² rises and the
φ-lock fails (`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` §3.1–3.2).

**The [design] over them — the drain is the sky's own slow loss through its
top:**

> **The Zenith is where the atmosphere's `(1−q)` waste escapes the window at its
> top.** The envelope is a working field system — it holds the window's q up
> against the outside thin, and *every* working field system bleeds its
> `(1−q)` fraction (`energy-harnessing.md` §2). That bleed does not vanish at the
> sky ceiling; it exits **at the window's topmost edge** — the atmosphere sheds
> its `(1−q)` waste through the Zenith, the way a wound sheds its glow and a
> machine its loss, **slow, honest, and readable**. The zenith's drain is not an
> event; it is the atmosphere's steady, quiet expenditure — the sky's own slow
> loss, the same law that makes a storm glow (`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`
> §1.3) and a re-entering body skim a bright trail (§2.3), scaled to the window's
> whole ceiling and read as a *drift* rather than a flare.

The **drain's read** — what the waste's escape looks like — is this doc's
[design] over the landed law:

> **The auroras are the drain's light; the ozone is the waste's residue.** The
> waste that escapes through the zenith is the same `(1−q)` glow the sky already
> renders — so the auroral bands at the top of the envelope (the envelope-to-
> vacuum Π gradient's shedding, `atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` §3.2 site 2) are
> not a separate sky feature: **they are the drain's light, the atmosphere's
> `(1−q)` waste re-radiating as it leaves.** And the fraction the atmosphere
> *keeps* — the part of the shed coherence that does not escape but is caught and
> re-locked at the ceiling — is the **ozone: the waste's residue, the sky's own
> strata** (the field-archaeology register — a kept residue, the high-`q` part
> the field holds back, `field-archaeology.md` §2.1). The zenith is readable
> because the drain reads clearly: **bright auroras overhead = the window is
> shedding more of its waste; a thin, dim, ozone-thin ceiling = the atmosphere's
> hold is tight, its loss small.**

**[design]** — the *rate* of the drain (how much of the window's `(1−q)` bleed
exits through the ceiling per unit time, vs. how much the envelope re-locks or
the ozone residue keeps) is **probe-calibrated** (§5b) against the waste-law
calibration and the atmosphere's envelope stability (`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`
§5a). What is [design]-landed: the `(1−q)` waste law at the window scale
(`energy-harnessing.md` §2), the auroral discharge as the glow into drains
(`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` §3), and the residue model (`field-archaeology.md`
§2). **The drain adds no physics; it names where the atmosphere's already-real
waste escapes its top.**

### (c) The edge — you cannot leave through the zenith

**The landed pieces:** [`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) designs the voyage as

between**, not the sky's. And the between is not a void you fall out of a window
into — it is the sparse medium the windows float **in**, reached by *sailing*, not
by climbing to a sky and stepping off.

**The [design] over them — the zenith's honesty is that it is the window's
boundary, not its door:**

> **You cannot leave through the zenith.** The ceiling is the window's *boundary*,
> not its *door* — a window is not a cup with a lid. Climbing to the top of the
> atmosphere and passing the ceiling does not *leave* the window; it opens onto
> the window's own vacuum (ρ below the gas floor, the Kármán-line analog,
> `atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` §1.4) and, past that, the same **thin the voyage
> crosses** ([`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §2.1; [`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md)).
> The zenith does not carry you to another window — **the voyage is the seam's
> work, not the sky's** ([`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §2): you leave a
> window by sailing the dark from a ship, with a coherence budget and a carried
> Qi bath, never by stepping off its ceiling. **The ceiling's honesty is that it
> is the window's boundary — a place you read, stand at, and turn back from, the
> honest edge that marks where the window's designed vertical ends — not a door
> that opens out.**

The **edge's honesty**, [design]:

> **The zenith adds no new door.** Because a window is not a cup with a lid,
> the zenith closes nothing and opens nothing — it *completes* the vertical's
> design (the sky now has a top) without pretending to be a way out. This is the
> design's most important neutrality: **the zenith is the last seam, not the
> first gate.** It does not become an exit, a shortcut, or a fast-travel the
> voyage was supposed to be. The vertical's top is where the designed window
> *ends*, honestly — and the only honest leaving is the voyage's long dark
> ([`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §2).

**[design]** — the *way* the ceiling reads as a boundary-and-not-a-door (the 
presentation that says "here the window's air ends, and beyond is the thin you
sail, not the sky you leave through") is this doc's designed reading over the
