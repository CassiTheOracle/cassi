[`field-music.md`](./field-music.md) turns the published channels into an
informative ambience (the present song); [`the-silence.md`](./the-silence.md)

## 1. The echo, stated once

`field-music.md` §1 designs the world's voice as a sampled, informative
ambience — the present song. `the-silence.md` completed it by designing the
absence — the empty register, the grammar of quiet. `the-bell.md` answers with
*now*. **Nothing designs the *remembered* register — the past event held in the
structure, heard.** The corpus already reads that past: `field-archaeology.md`
is the residue you *read*; `field-hazards.md`'s storm leaves scars; `the-name.md`
binds anchors that decay. Each is a *read*. The echo is the same residue **heard**
— the structure that plays back, through the voice's own channels, what happened
to it.

> **The echo, stated once.** The world's voice is the published channels made
> sound (`field-music.md` §1). The *echo* is those same channels **re-read as a
> history** — the altered state of a structure (a scar, a stratum, a named
> ruin's decay, a wound) sounded through the *same* sonification map, in the
> *remembered* register. Sound is what coherence does (`field-music.md` §2.3);
> the echo is what coherence **did** — the field's memory, made audible.

The fourth register of the voice, set against the three that exist:

|| The register | The read | The face |
|---|---|---|---|
|| `field-music.md` — **present** | the published channels made sound — *what the field is saying now* | the informative ambience, the growl, the hum |
|| `the-silence.md` — **empty** | the same channels read as absence — *what the field is not saying* | the sound-holes: gone / at rest / approaching |
|| `the-bell.md` — **warning** | the instrument stack agreeing — *now* | the ring that says the field is about to act as one |
|| **this doc — remembered** | the same channels' *altered-state history* sounded — *what the field once said here, and what it became* | the scar's growl, the stratum's hum, the ruin's whisper, the wound's song |

**The family rule, held exactly:** like every instrument and register, the echo
is a **consumer of the same publish with a presentation idiom — never a new
channel, never new telemetry** (`field-instruments.md` §2.1; `field-music.md` §6).
It reads no line the sonification and the residue-reads do not already read; it
only *re-sounds* what those reads call "altered." There is no new microphone, no
new sensor, no new physics, and critically **no stored audio file** — there is a
designed ear that hears the structure's *state* as its history, on the same
channels the live voice already sings.

## 2. The honest memory — an imprint, not a recording

### 2.1 The spine, stated once

The design's honest mechanism rests on one refusal: **the echo is not a
recording.** The field is deterministic (`field-archaeology.md` §1.2 — one PDE,
same published snapshot every reader samples), and determinism is not
recoverability (`field-archaeology.md` §1.2: the transient past is dissolved,
washed out, unrecoverable). A structure cannot *record* the event that passed
through it — the field keeps no trace of a dissolved state. What it keeps is
what it *became*.

> **The imprint is not a recording; it is the structure's own altered state, and
> the echo is that state read as sound.** When the storm passes, the scar is not
> a movie of the storm — it is the field, *re-quantized around* the deep-rung
> cores that survived (`field-hazards.md` §2.2 / `field-archaeology.md` §2.1).
> When a named house falls, the ruin's residue is not a caption of the name — it
> is the anchor's decay, the field no longer holding the lie
> (`the-name.md` §2.1). The echo reads **what the structure became**, through
> the same sonification map the voice already uses.

This is the same inversion `the-silence.md` performs on the voice: the silence
reads the quiet the voice already makes; the echo reads the *state* the structure
already is. **The same channels, one more classification-time — present-turned-into-history.**

### 2.2 The altered-state channels — engine-real/[design]-landed, never recorded

The echo's *material* is the structure's real altered state, drawn exactly the
way `field-archaeology.md` §6(a) and `wound-remembered.md` §6(a) draw their
boundaries:

|| Altered state | What it is | Status | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|---|
|| **The scar's shape** | the deep-rung cores that survived the storm + the `ε²` ghost halo of what dissolved around them — a *real* field configuration (`field-hazards.md` §2.2: "the deep-rung fraction resists and persists as residue") | **engine-real** — the structure of the scar is a real field read | `field-hazards.md` §2.2 / §6.1; `field-archaeology.md` §2.1 |
|| **The stratum's composition** | a banded imprint of a deep-rung condensation event, at φ-scaled depth-intervals — the log-periodic stratigraphy (`field-archaeology.md` §2.3) | **[design]-landed** over engine-real deep-rung persistence — the *strata model* is designed, its *composition* is a real read | `field-archaeology.md` §2.3 / §6(a) |
|| **The anchor's decay** | a bound-name's holding or its shed — a true name's structure is a real coherent well; a false name's decay is a real `(1−q)` shed (`the-name.md` §2) | **engine-real** (the holding/shedding is the field's persistence); **[design]** the *naming binding* | `the-name.md` §2 / §4 |
|| **The wound's re-opening** | a re-opening `ε²` core that refuses to heal ("a floor that rises toward dissolution when stressed") | **engine-real** read; **[design]** + probe-calibrated the *persistence* that makes it a wound | `wound-remembered.md` §2.1 / §6(a); `field-hazards.md` open-Q4 |

**The one thing that is never present: a stored audio file.** The structure does
not *hold* a recording of its storm; it holds a scar. The echo is a **consumer
of the published channels** that reads that scar's state and presents it through
the voice's map — the same discipline as every instrument (`field-music.md` §6,
`weather-not-storm.md` §4b). There is no audio memory, no "the game recorded
this," no playback of the lost transient. The echo *is* the altered state, read.

### 2.3 The re-match condition — the same pattern that made it makes it sing

The load-bearing mechanism for *when* an echo sounds. Field music is the present
song — it sounds because the field *is* in a state. The echo sounds when the
field's conditions **re-match** the conditions that made the alteration:

> **The echo sounds when the field's conditions re-met — the same `ε²`/`q`
> pattern that made the alteration makes it sing again.** A scar sings when a
> similar `ε²` front *nears* (the same pattern that carved it, re-passing
> through/over it — `field-hazards.md` §2.3's pre-warning channel, read as a
> re-sound). A stratum hums when the `q`/depth profile it sits in re-approaches
> the profile it was laid down in. A named ruin whispers when the field re-
> tensions its locality (the same organization the name held, re-approached).
> A wound sings when it is *stressed* — the re-bleeding of
> `wound-remembered.md` §2.1 *is* the re-match.

The re-match is not mystical — it is the same field-state coincidence every
instrument reads (`the-bell.md` §2.1's coincidence, `the-silence.md` §3's
register-classification). The echo's *trigger* is a designed, probe-calibrated
**condition** — a threshold on the published channels over the structure's
locality (a local `ε²`-front approaching the scar's own band, a `q`-profile
re-approaching a stratum's) that, when met, drives the scar's state through the
sonification map. **[design]** The re-match condition and its threshold are this
doc's [design], probe-calibrated; the *channels it reads* (the `ε²`-front, the
`q`-profile) are engine-real. The honest mechanism, once: **a ring / hum /
whisper is not a memory firing — it is the same field read the live voice makes,
re-presented at a structure that *remembers* having been in that state.**

## 3. The echo register — the deep-time textures

The register is four textures, each grounded in a source doc's already-designed
state, each a *re-sounding* of what that doc reads:

### 3.1 The scar that sings the storm — `field-hazards.md`

`field-hazards.md` §2 designs the storm as a `c_s`-traveling `ε²` front (the
engine-real `∇ε²` wave), and §2.2 that it "leaves scars — the deep-rung fraction
persists as residue" → the archaeology of the storm (`field-hazards.md` §2.2 /
`field-archaeology.md` §2.1). The echo's first texture is the scar **re-sounded**:

> **The scar that sings the storm.** When a similar `ε²` front *nears* a storm
> scar — the same `c_s` `ε²`-gradient that carved it — the scar's deep-rung core
> and ghost halo pass through the sonification map (`field-music.md` §2.2, the
> growl = a *rising* `ε²`-front whose texture tracks the front's ramp-up), and
> the scar **sounds its passing growl** — re-sounding, in the remembered
> register, the storm that made it. A player who hears the scar's growl after
> the storm has passed, rather than before a new one arrives, has heard a *memory*.

The distinction from the present-song growl (`field-music.md` §2.2) is the
register, told apart exactly as the silence tells `gone` from `at-rest`
(`the-silence.md` §2): **a living growl is a rising, moving front announcing the
arriving storm (that is the present song); a scar's growl is the *same* texture
fired by the *re-match* at a structure that already holds the imprint — it reads
as the storm's *echo*, not its approach** (`life-signal.md` §3.2's maintenance
axis, applied to audio: a moving rising front vs a re-sounded static-state
re-trigger). **[design]** The re-match threshold (which `ε²`-front magnitude
over the scar re-sounds, and how the re-sound reads as "remembered" rather than
"approaching") is the register's tuning, probe-calibrated against the
`life-signal.md` §6b noise-floor line.

### 3.2 The stratum that hums its residue — `field-archaeology.md`

`field-archaeology.md` §2.3 designs the strata: ancient condensation events left
as imprinted bands — a log-periodic stratigraphy, "a cross-section of the world
is a log-periodic stratigraphy." The echo's second texture is the banded imprint
**hummed**:

> **The stratum that hums its residue.** A stratum's banded imprint — its deep-
> rung composition at φ-scaled depth-intervals (`field-archaeology.md` §2.3) — is
> itself a *φ-tuned structure*, and the sonification map is *already* a φ-tempered
> harmonic bank (`field-music.md` §1: sine oscillators at `f_r = f0·φ^r`). The
> stratum's residue is therefore a **φ-addressable tone**: the deep-rung band at
> a given rung-depth hums the φ-interval that band *is*. When the local field's
> `q`-profile re-approaches the profile the stratum was laid down in (the
> re-match, §2.3), the band hums its residue aloud — **the deep-rung imprint's
> tone**, a player's first *hearing* of the archaeology they would otherwise
> scan (`field-archaeology.md` §3.1).

This is the archaeological-audio twin of `field-music.md`'s ghost-halo hum
(`field-music.md` §5, the residue "sonified"): where that doc's hum is the
*present* read of a residue's ghost, the stratum's hum is the *remembered* read
of the band's own rung-composition, sounding under the re-match. **[design]**
The mapping (rung-depth → φ-interval) is inherited unchanged from field-music's
map — the stratum hums the interval the band already *is*; the register's
presentation (that it reads as "old," not "here") is this doc's tuning, gated on
the strata model itself landing (`field-archaeology.md` §6(c), Phase-1.5).

### 3.3 The named ruin that whispers its name — `the-name.md`

`the-name.md` §3.1: a named skeleton is a re-authorable Π the field holds

> **The named ruin that whispers its name.** A named skeleton — still held, still

This is the deep-time face of the name's honesty (`the-name.md` §2.4 —
determinism): a true name's whisper is a pure function of the structure's held
state; a false name's whisper is the same field consequence *heard* — the
structure *cannot* sing a lie, any more than it can hold one. **[design]** The
"name as a tone" mapping (which held-Π structure sounds as which interval) is
over the name's binding (`the-name.md` §4); the whisper's *honesty* is the field's
own (a named ruin's echo is only as true as the name is). Gated on the naming

### 3.4 The wound's song — `wound-remembered.md`

`wound-remembered.md` §1: a wound is a scar that refuses — a deliberate lock-break

The wound's song is the register's *darkest* texture, and it carries the

## 4. What the echo is for

The echo is the corpus's reads made **audible as history** — the archaeological
audio. Three uses, each a designed composition over the register §3:

### 4.1 Reading a structure's history by ear — the seasoned player's skill

`the-silence.md` §4 designs the reading skill: a new player hears "quiet," a
seasoned player hears *which* quiet — the resonance-tutor's pedagogy applied to
the ears (`the-silence.md` §4.1). The echo is that same pedagogy applied to the
**deep-time voice**:

> **A new player hears "a sound." A seasoned player hears *whose*** — the scar
> that growls the passing storm, the stratum that hums the old band, the ruin
> that whispers a name, the wound that will not resolve. The tutor's claim —
> "magic feels like physics" (`resonance-tutor.md` §6) — has a deep-time twin:
> **the echo feels like the field's history, and learning to read it is learning
> to tell a structure's past from its mere presence.** The echo is deterministic
> (§5), so a player who learns the register can rely on it — the same
> reproducibility `the-silence.md` §4.1 and `patient-field.md` §5(c) guarantee.

### 4.2 The provenance read, made audible — `weather-not-storm.md`

`weather-not-storm.md` designs the provenance classifier: weather (the field's
own extreme) vs punishment (an aimed event), read off the storm's published
dynamics. The echo supplies the provenance read's **audible** face, operating
*before* and *alongside* the classifier's verdict:

> **The echo of a scar tells what made it.** A scar's re-sounded growl carries
> the texture of the front that made it — a broad storm-scar growls the passing
> storm's shape (`field-hazards.md` §2); a deliberate-vent/signature scar sings
> the *phase-stable, target-aimed* texture of `weather-not-storm.md` §2.2 (the
> aimed event's coherent-phase signature). A player who *hears* the echo before
> reading the classifier's verdict has heard the provenance read in the
> remembered register. **And with the conservation companion (§4.3), a player
> who hears a scar's echo knows whether what re-met it is weather it can shelter
> from or a signature aimed at it — the cruel-world honesty of
> `weather-not-storm.md` §5.3, made audible.**

**[design]** The echo does not *decide* provenance — it *presents* the altered
state's channel-texture with legibility; the *classification* stays the
classifier's (probe-calibrated, `weather-not-storm.md` §4a). The echo is the
