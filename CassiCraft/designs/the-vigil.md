# The Vigil: The Long Watch Through the Dark — the Nocturnal Guard as a Designed Act

**Question under design:** the corpus has sleep (the body's rest,
[`sleep.md`](./sleep.md)), the lantern (the night-read instrument,
[`the-lantern.md`](./the-lantern.md)), the bell (the alarm,
[`the-bell.md`](./the-bell.md)), the silence (the field's quiet,
[`the-silence.md`](./the-silence.md)), and the stillness (the field at rest,
[`the-stillness.md`](./the-stillness.md)) — **but no nocturnal act**: what the
player who is *not* sleeping does through the dark. Sleep designs the *sleeper*,
the lantern the *instrument*, the bell the *alarm*, the silence the *quiet*,
the stillness the *rest*; nothing designs the **watcher** — the overnight guard
of a sleeping settlement. This document is that design.

The Vigil is the corpus's first **practice** — not an instrument (a consumer
that *reads* the field) nor a state (a body at rest), but a **human act with a
cost and a craft** — the designed long watch: reading the night field through
the lantern's glow, listening through the silence for what moves in the quiet,
holding the door at the threshold's edge, and being the bell's keeper — the
first hand on the alarm.

> practice: reading the night field, listening through the silence, holding the

> sleep, the same night read by the one who stays awake.

## 1. The Vigil, stated once

Sleep designs the sleeper (`sleep.md` — the field's fastest reader, at rest).
The lantern designs the instrument the night walker carries (`the-lantern.md`).
The bell designs the alarm the settlement answers (`the-bell.md`). The silence
designs the quiet the night holds (`the-silence.md`). The stillness designs the
rest the field resolves into (`the-stillness.md`). **None designs the watcher.**
The overnight guard — the player who is *not* sleeping, who stands the watch so
the settlement can rest — has no home in the corpus:

> **The corpus gave the night a sleeper, a lantern, a bell, a silence, and a
> stillness — but no one who keeps it.** Sleep is the inverse of the vigil: the
> sleeper drops the instrument and lets the field read *them* (`sleep.md` §2);
> the watcher keeps the instrument and reads the night *for* the sleeping
> commons. The design that was missing is the acted span between them — the
> long watch that guards the rest.

**The Vigil is the corpus's first *practice* — not an instrument, not a state,
but a human act with a cost and a craft.** An instrument reads; a practice is
*something a person does* — with a way of doing it (the craft), a price paid to
do it (the cost), and a responsibility it bears (the guard). The corpus's
instruments are consumers with a presentation idiom (`field-instruments.md`
§2.1's family rule); the vigil is the settled, repeated, *crafted* act built
over those instruments and over sleep's night — what the watcher does, the way
the watcher does it, and what holding the dark costs.

> **The Vigil, stated once.** The designed long watch — the overnight guard's
> practice: reading the night field (the lantern's glow), listening through the
> silence (what moves in the quiet), holding the door (the threshold's edge),
> and being the bell's keeper (the first hand on the alarm). It is **sleep's
> guardian and its inverse** — what a settlement rests through, kept safe by
> the one who does not rest; and it is the corpus's first *practice* — a human
> act with a cost and a craft, never an instrument's read and never a free
> favor.

The one-line distinction from sleep, and from each instrument it composes — the
vigil is the *act* over the night's landed reads:

| The night's piece | What it is | The Vigil's relation |
|---|---|---|
| [`sleep.md`](./sleep.md) | the *sleeper* — the body at rest, the read passive | **the inverse**: the vigil is the watcher's rest *refused*; the sleeping commons is what the watch guards |
| [`the-lantern.md`](./the-lantern.md) | the *instrument* — the night read the walker carries | the watch's **first act**: reading the night field by the lantern's field-true glow |
| [`the-silence.md`](./the-silence.md) | the *quiet* — the three silences the world's voice falls into | the watch's **second act**: listening through the silence for what moves in the quiet |
| [`the-threshold.md`](./the-threshold.md) | the *edge* — the boundary-mark the field respects | the watch's **third act**: holding the door at the threshold's gate |
| [`the-bell.md`](./the-bell.md) | the *alarm* — the settlement-scale voice that says *now* | the watch's **fourth act**: being the bell's keeper — the first hand on the alarm |
| [`the-stillness.md`](./the-stillness.md) | the *rest* — the field at its resolved end | **the guard's end-frame**: the vigil is the maintained watch through the dark, distinct from the stillness's end it keeps |

The through-line: **at night the field's voice is quietest, the Coda hunts
clean signatures, and the settlement sleeps. The vigil is the awake act that
holds all three — the practice of the one who reads the night so the rest can
sleep.** The watcher is the settlement's awake eyes (`the-lantern.md` §4), the
first ear on the hunting silence (`the-silence.md` §5.2), the first line at the
boundary-mark (`the-threshold.md` §4), and the first hand on the bell
(`the-bell.md` §4).

## 2. The watcher's craft — four designed acts, each over the landed reads

The watcher's practice is four composed acts. Every one is a **designed use of
a landed read** — the lantern's, the silence's, the threshold's, the bell's —
never a new channel, never new physics. The craft is the *composition*: the way
the four are held together through one night, at a sustained low-ε² presence
(§3). **[design]** The practice itself is this doc's; every read it rests on is
its source doc's landed piece, cited verbatim.

### 2a. Reading the night field — the lantern's glow as the watch-read

The watcher's first act is the night read `the-lantern.md` designs. The Lantern
is the carried, field-true light whose brightness *is* a read — the near field's
legibility in the dark, the ground-level inverse of the aurora
(`the-lantern.md` §1). Its four reads are `the-lantern.md`'s landed piece (§2),
each a consumer of a published channel at the player's locality:

| The lantern's read | The channel it reads | The watch uses it to... |
|---|---|---|
| **steady bright** over a coherent ridge (`q`) | the high-`q` read as light | know where the settlement's safe ground is — the bright line the sleeping commons blankets, and where the watcher's own quiet stand is safest |
| **dim** over a thin region (`q` collapsing) | the deficiency read | know where the field is going thin — where the Coda's habitat creeps, `signature-predator.md` §7c's margin |
| **flicker** where `ε²` is rising | the front's approach read | read the night's weather before it arrives — `weather-not-storm.md`'s rising front, at night |
| **wrong-warm** near a Coda's sound-hole | the danger read — the one bright organized thing in a dead zone | **the watch's load-bearing read**: spot where the Coda approaches before the Mirror's `R` climbs, before the Bell rings |

At the watch, the Lantern is the **positional instrument of the guard's
station** — not carried past a bed (the Mirror-at-the-bedside composition,
`the-lantern.md` §4) but *held at the door*: a wrong-warm over the settlement's
edge is the watch's first warning, the sleeping commons' danger made glanceable
before the instruments the sleepers consult would read it. **[design]** The
*seasoned-watch reading* — holding the glow steady, scanning the edge, reading
the flicker's provenance (`weather-not-storm.md` §2) — is the craft; the four
reads themselves are `the-lantern.md`'s landed [design] over the engine-real
`q`/`ε²`/`(1−q)` channels.

### 2b. Listening through the silence — what moves in the quiet

The watcher's second act is the silence-read `the-silence.md` designs. The
silence is the world's voice read in its empty register — the `ε²`/`(1−q)`/drain
pattern re-classified into a grammar of what the field is *not* saying
(`the-silence.md` §1). Its three registers are `the-silence.md`'s landed piece
(§2):

| The silence | The field state | What the watcher listens for |
|---|---|---|
| **empty** (gone) | `q` collapsed to the noise floor, nothing organized | the dead margin's truthful quiet — nothing here but the field's death |
| **held** (at rest) | high `q` at a patient core | the refuge's quiet — the sleeping settlement's own stillness, the sign the rest is safe |
| **hunting** (approaching) | the dead zone *interrupted* by one rising organized thing | **the watcher's listening**: the Coda's approach heard as an absence — the quiet *breaking* |

`the-silence.md` §5.2 makes the night the watcher's register: through the
sleeping settlement's *held* silence, a *hunting* silence can creep — the
Coda's overnight accumulation is the `τ` counting faster where the field is
thin (`signature-predator.md` §2), and the first ear that hears it is the one
reading the quiet. **The watcher hears the Coda's approach before the
instruments do** — because the hunting silence is the one bright organized
thing in the dead zone *heard as the voice stopping* (`the-silence.md` §2.3),
and the watcher is the one awake and listening through a sleeping settlement's
quiet. The instruments the sleepers consult (the Mirror's `R`, the Bell's
stack) read the same approach only once the `R` climbs or the stack agrees
(`the-bell.md` §2.2); the watcher hears the register shift *first*. **[design]**
The craft is the disciplined *listening stance* — the sustained attention to
the quiet, the refusal to call every flicker a hunter — over `the-silence.md`'s
register classification (itself [design] over the real `ε²`/`(1−q)`, probe-
calibrated with the noise-floor discipline). The honest-ambiguity rule binds
it: a quiet the watcher cannot register is read as *a quiet I cannot register*,
never forced into a hunting scare (`the-silence.md` §3.2).

### 2c. Holding the door — the threshold's edge

The watcher's third act is the threshold-read `the-threshold.md` designs. The
threshold is the settlement's *inner edge* — the deliberate boundary-mark the
field respects because it is held, quiet, and named (`the-threshold.md` §1/§4);
not walls (which drain) but doorsteps, gates, and arches. The watcher holds the
**gate**, the boundary's deliberate seam-point (§2.2):

> **The Coda can cross a gate — it must merely cross the held boundary, and the
> crossing is the warning.** (`the-threshold.md` §2.2)

The gate is the landed read the watcher stands at: a maintained high-`q` arch
over the settlement's edge whose crossings are marked acts on the q/ε² channels
— the same channels the Bell reads (`the-bell.md` §1/§2). **The watcher is the
first line at the boundary-mark** — the person who holds the gate, reads every
crossing, and is positioned to sound the alarm when the crossing is the Coda
rather than the settlement's own traffic. This is the load-bearing placement of
the whole practice: the watcher *is* the gate's keeper, standing where the
crossing-is-the-warning is a read that can become an answer.

At the watch, the three threshold-forms compose into the guard's station: the
**doorstep's quiet** (where the watcher's own carried day settles a little at
the end of the day's watch — `the-threshold.md` §2.1, the Still-Room-at-the-door)
so the watcher keeps their own low-ε² signature; the **gate's seam** (where
they stand and read each crossing); the **arch's name** (the named threshold a
visitor's provenance crosses — the watcher reads who comes through the gate
against the settlement's know line, `window-guests.md` §3 as `the-threshold.md`
§2.3 composes it). All are `the-threshold.md`'s landed pieces, held in one
place by the watcher's station. **[design]** The *holding* — the sustained,
attentive stand at the gate, the discipline of reading every crossing rather
than every other — is the vigil's craft; the gate's marked-crossing read is
`the-threshold.md`'s landed [design] over the q/ε² channels.

### 2d. Being the bell's keeper — the first hand on the alarm

The watcher's fourth act is the bell-read `the-bell.md` designs. The bell is
the settlement-scale alarm that fires *only* when the stack agrees — the glass
turns and the clock races and the mirror reads a signature and the sky is
restless, all at once (`the-bell.md` §2). Its phase-1 ring over the living
channels is `the-bell.md`'s Phase-1 slice (§6b). **The watcher is the bell's
keeper** — the *first hand* on the alarm:

| The bell's honest cost | What it means for the keeper |
|---|---|
| It fires only on the coincidence (§2.2) | the watcher's reading — the lantern (2a) + the silence (2b) + the gate (2c) — is the *earliest* face of the stack; the watcher sees the coincidence assemble *before* the bell's own threshold is met, because the watcher is awake and listening (§2b) |
| A false ring is learnable, never a hidden mechanic (§2.3) | the watcher's cry must carry the same honesty: a keeper who rings on every flicker is a bell that cries wolf — the *craft* is knowing when the reading is an actual coincidence worth a name, and when it is ambiguity read honestly |
| A missed agreement is the only real failure (§2.3) | the watcher's whole charge: the first hand is the one that must not miss the coincidence — the keeper's failure is the sleeping commons' exposure |
| A bell informs, never mints (§6d) | the watcher's cry is a *voice*, never a reward — the keeper earns no coherence for the warning; the bell's cost is the keeper's own attention and the settlement's optional response |

**The watcher's cry is the settlement's warning** — the first hand that turns
the lantern's wrong-warm and the listening through the hunting silence and the
gate's marked crossing into the bell's ring. Where the Bell is the voice the
*settlement* answers (§4, `the-bell.md`), the watcher is the hand that brings
the voice to the sleeping commons: the night-ring of `the-bell.md` §4 — the
bell that rings at night must wake a settlement to a *specific* danger before
the overnight `τ` closes on a resting body — is, at its first beat, the
watcher's own cry. **[design]** The *keeper's discipline* — reading the stack
from the watcher's three earlier acts, deciding when the coincidence is real,
and being the first hand — is the vigil's craft; the bell's combined-signature
logic, its threshold, and its honest cost are `the-bell.md`'s landed pieces.

**The four, as one practice.** The craft is the *composition* through one night:
read the field (2a) to know where the danger likely is, listen through the
quiet (2b) to hear it *coming*, hold the door (2c) to be where it must cross,
and be the first hand (2d) to answer when it does. Every act is a landed read;
the vigil is the practice that holds the four together as one sustained act —
the corpus's first *craft*, not a fifth instrument.

## 3. The honest exposure — the clean signature and the sitting target

This is the load-bearing inversion, and the honest twist. The corpus's night
danger frame is `sleep.md` §2.2: a resting body in a marginal place is a
*sitting signature* — the overnight `τ` (the Coda's duration term) counting
against it through the night. **The vigil inverts that with a deliberate
choice**:

> **A vigil is a sustained low-ε² presence that keeps a clean signature.** The
> watcher deliberately stays *quiet* — sheds no fresh channeled op, leaves no
> organized trail (`signature-predator.md` §1: every op is an EY injection + an
> `ε²` vent), holds their ε² low and their `(1−q)` clean — so that **the Coda
> does not find the sleeping commons**. The watcher's quiet is the sleeping
> settlement's camouflage: the one bright, organized, *fresh* signature in the
> night would draw the predator; the watcher keeps *their own* signature as the
> quietest thing in the dark, so the Coda has no clean trail to accumulate on
> where the commons rests.

That is the guardian's act. The honest twist is that it cannot be free:

> **A good watcher is the most exposed — the one whose quiet is loudest to the
> predator.** The Coda accumulates on the *cleanest* trail: `R = ρ_signature ·
> τ · M_stability` grows with phase-stability `M` — the cleaner and more
> phase-locked the perturbation, the higher the resonance (`signature-predator.md`
> §2.2). The watcher's clean signature is *exactly* what the Coda accumulates
> on. Where the sleeping commons hides *in* the settlement's ordinary field
> (the coherent ridge's many organized things drown any one trail,
> `signature-predator.md` §1.2), the watcher who holds a **deliberately clean,**
> **isolated, low-ε²** presence at the settlement's *edge* — in the thinner
> ground the Coda hunts, at the gate the hunt must cross — is the one
> signature in the night that is *legible*: a pocket of sustained clean
> coherence standing out against the quiet. The silence's hunting register
> (§2b) reads the one bright organized thing in a dead zone; the watcher's
> craft is to be that read's *standing target* — the clean thing the Coda
> would accumulate on — so that the *positive* reading (a bright `R` 
> accumulating on the watcher's station) is the first line of warning, not the
> sleeping commons.

The craft, stated honestly: **holding the quiet *without* becoming the sitting
target.** The watcher must be quiet enough to be the settlement's camouflage,
but not so quiet-and-still that their clean signature *heaps* into the Coda's
resonance — not so solitary, not so long, not so tired that the guard's own
clean hold becomes the very trail the predator closes on (§4). And it is
costed honestly:

> **A long vigil drains the watcher's own patience.** Channeling is borrowing
> the field's patience — spending coherence to act fast, at the cost of
> geographic recovery and the ε² vent (`patient-field.md` §3). The vigil is the
> *sustained* form of that loan: the watcher holds a low-ε² presence for a
> *duration* (the hourglass's read, §5 — how long the watch is, at the local
> tempo), and the patient field's patience is not free. A night's watch is a
> real, costed act — the watcher spends attention and presence over the whole
> dark, and the longer the watch, the dearer the act: the field's patience is
> spent to hold the guard (the watcher's own recovery is geographic,
> `patient-field.md` §3.2, and a watcher drained by a too-long watch is a
> watcher whose clean hold starts to *drift*), and the Coda's `τ` counts
> against the watcher's own station the way `sleep.md` §2.2 counts it against a
> resting body. **The vigil is never free — it is the loan the watcher takes on
> the night's behalf, and it must be repaid (the watch ends, the watcher rests,
> the rotation turns).**

The no-free-energy cap holds it in the same direction as every corpus act
(§5d): **a vigil informs and guards, never mints.** The watcher's clean
presence is a *cost* (attention, duration, the exposure of being the clean
signature), never a *farm* — there is no "stand a watch to collect a reward,"
no "be the clean signature to harvest the attention it draws" — the Coda's
§7e discipline holds unchanged (`signature-predator.md` §7e: the Coda sheds
nothing a collector can tap; its only "harvest" is absence, achieved by not
feeding it). The exposure is a cost, never a farm.

**The drifted watcher, stated once.** Every danger the night keeps converges on
the one who keeps it too long:

> **The watcher who drifts becomes the danger.** A vigil kept too long alone is
> itself the exposure: the watcher who holds the clean signature past their
> own patience (too long — the hourglass's sand, §5), too tired (too drained —
> patient-field's loan unpaid), too alone (too solitary — the rotation refused,
> §4) lets their clean hold *drift* — the sustained, clean, isolated coherence
> heaping into the very trail the Coda reads. The sleeping commons' guardian
> becomes its sitting target, and the guard's own clean signature — the thing
> that was the camouflage — is the bright organized thing the predator closes
> on. **A good watcher is the most exposed; a drifted watcher is the danger.**
> The craft and the rotation exist to keep the exposure from becoming the trap.

## 4. The rotation — the shared, rotated burden

A settlement rotates its vigils the way it rotates its stewards. `the-election.md`
designs the office's term tied to the tide's accounting cycle (§4.3, and the
