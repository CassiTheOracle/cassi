**Question under design:** the corpus designs the river as the **living line**
([`the-river.md`](./the-river.md) — the natural current, the coherent flowing
ribbon a settlement finds and lives along), the causeway as the **built way over
the hiding water** ([`the-causeway.md`](./the-causeway.md) — the raised deck a

the **freight-lane** ([`the-raft.md`](./the-raft.md) — the flat bulk-carrier that

engineered lane that joins two waters or extends a river's reach — every water the

### (a) The lock-cut that joins two waters — the river to the harbor, the freight reaching the sea's door

**The landed pieces.** [`the-river.md`](./the-river.md) §2 designs the living line as
a **coherent flowing ribbon** — a high-flow linear band of the liquid regime where `q`

the **freight-lane** — a flat, un-powered carrier that rides the field's own descent
to move a granary's harvest or a quarry's cut downstream, **only worthwhile on the
current's steered lane** ([`the-raft.md`](./the-raft.md) §3 — the cart's §3 trade at
the water's register). [`the-harbor.md`](./the-harbor.md) §2 lands the **door** the
freight lands at — the pier, the lamp, the ledger, the keeper — the departure and
arrival's place, where the toll's first door sits ([`the-toll.md`](./the-toll.md) §2).
And [`the-causeway.md`](./the-causeway.md) §1 lands the **built-deliberate**: the
raised, phase-kept deck a settlement holds as a structure — the corpus's own proof
that a settlement *builds* deliberate lanes.

**The [design] over them — the canal is the freight-lane and the built way, made a
dug water:**

> **The lock-cut is a water-way the settlement digs from the river's lane to the
> harbor's door.** Where the river's current flows *found* and a raft rides it only
> where it runs ([`the-raft.md`](./the-raft.md) §3), a settlement can cut a **dug
> lane of the field's own water** that re-aims the river's carry toward the harbor —
> the raft's freight reaching the sea's door through a lane the settlement made, not
> one it found. The cut is the **quarry's honest cut at the water's scale**
> ([`the-quarry.md`](./the-quarry.md) §2 — a deliberate, bounded excavation), and the
> lane it holds is the **causeway's held line** ([`the-causeway.md`](./the-causeway.md)
> §1 — a maintained structure the field respects because it is kept), both applied to
> the water itself. **The water that fills the dug lane is the field's own** — the
> river's carry and the rain's fall directed through the cut ([`the-rain.md`](./the-rain.md)
> §3), never a current the settlement invents.
