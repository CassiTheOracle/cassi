## 2. The mechanics — what the chord IS, what it does, what it costs

### 2.1 What the chord IS: the multiplicity theorem made a deliberate act

The corpus already names the physics; the Festival makes it *deliberate*:

- **The commons is superadditive** (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.1; `coherence-commons.md`
  §7.2 — the multiplicative budget `∏ 1/(1−q_i)`, the AM-GM evenness direction).
  A village's intelligence **exceeds its members'** because the shared high-`q`
  core raises every member's efficiency.
- **A group acting phase-locked IS a working** (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.2;
  `magic-systems.md` §1 — a working is `M ≈ 1`, random perturbation `M ≈ 0`).
  Two members in the same phase window are *one* organized perturbation; N
  members in a shared phase are a working whose combined injection couples at
  `M ≈ 1`.
- **A coherence score is a deliberately organized, phase-timed perturbation** —
  the definition of a working (`field-music.md` §4.3; the honest scope: a score
  is an ordered list of Q4 op records, and the field responds to the sequence
  the way it responds to any organized perturbation).

**The Festival chord is the intersection of all three:** each member performs
their bounded EY injection, phase-aligned via the composed score, so the group's
combined injection is `M ≈ 1` — the multiplicity theorem `∏ 1/(1−q_i)` made a
**single deliberate act** rather than an ambient property. A village that shares
coherence evenly is already *smarter*; the Festival is that sharing **pointed
in one direction, at one time, as one op.**

Concretely, the chord is `field-music.md` §4's composition system at settlement
scale:

| Layer | Form | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| Each member's part | a bounded EY injection / vent — a `{op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}` record (`coherence-magic.md` §2, §5.1) | engine-real |
| The shared score | the composed sequence that **holds all members in one phase window** — the conductor's phrase, `field-music.md` §4.1 | [design] (composition is `field-music.md` §4's design) |
| The resonance | the whole settlement steering its field toward **one shared pitch** — the φ-tonic of `field-music.md` §1, the attractor's `EY = φ·EI` | engine-physical (the attractor is the tonic) |

**[design]** The *which-chord* (the exact phrase, the tempo, the members'
parts) is composition — `field-music.md` §4's designed surface. **[design]** The
*alignment rule* — how many members, at what phase tolerance, for what
magnitude, until the group couples at `M ≈ 1` — is this doc's design over the
commons law. The *phenomenon* (phase-locked = `M ≈ 1`, `field-npc-ai.md` §3.2)
is the corpus's law; the *orchestration* is design.

### 2.2 What it does: three effects, all grounded

**Raises the settlement's core against a thin tide.** The Qi bath
(`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4) is a maintained high-`q` core with a radius —
everything within it runs at the core's elevated coherence, and it is a draw
("brittle if it goes down"). A Festival is the commons **spending its members'
channeled coherence to re-hold that core**: the coordinated `heal`/`condense`/
`shield` ops (`coherence-magic.md` §2) lift the locality's `q`, cut the `(1−q)`
waste across the whole cluster, and raise capacitor dwell and conduit
efficiency (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4 — the parts are superadditive). **A
Festival at harvest re-raises the core cheaply** — the field is already
organized, the vent small, the `(1−q)` floor low. **A Festival at thin is a
deliberate, costly hold** — the members spend against the thinning field to keep
the margin from creeping (`field-hazards.md` §3; `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.1
— husbandry with a clock, done as one act).

**Re-binds the named structures.** `the-name.md` §3 makes a name a
persistent-Π anchor on a thing — the member-id's principle extended to the
places the settlement cares for (§3.2: the named contribution is legible; §6d:
a name holds, never mints). The Festival **re-locks the settlement's Π-anchors
together**: the coordinated working is applied at the named core — the Hearth,
the Still Room, the bed, the bath's center — so the field re-quantizes around
the named structures as a *kept whole* rather than a scattering of fading ones.
This is the same persistent-Pi holding a name invokes (`qi-computation.md` §5.2;
`the-name.md` §4 — the holding is theory, the binding is design), now applied
**collectively**: the Festival is the settlement's anchors held *in phase with
each other*, the way the field holds any persistent pattern — a re-binding of
the named body of the village.

**Books as one coordinated working on the Board.** Because every member's op
is a single-writer Q4 record (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1 — `{member, op,
worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}`, the member-id landed), the whole
Festival is **aggregatable as one op**: the Board reads a `C(festival)` line —
the settlement's coordinated contribution (or drain, if thin-misjudged) summed
over the tide-cycle, per the `shared-ledger.md` §1.2 aggregation. A Festival
that re-raises the core books as **the settlement's strongest single positive
line** — the multiplicity theorem made visible at the society's scale
(`shared-ledger.md` §3.1: "X is this tide's steward" becomes "the settlement is
its own steward this Festival"). A Festival at thin that overdraws books as the
settlement's honest coordinated drain — a real cost the Board shows.

### 2.3 The honest cost — the Festival is *spent*, never free

The no-free-energy cap (`energy-harnessing.md` §6: **no conversion yields more
than it sinks**, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` amplitude caps in the Q4 lane) is the
Festival's hard bound, inherited unchanged:

> **The members' collective ε² vent is the price of the working.** Every
> channeled op sheds decoherence the field must heal (`coherence-magic.md` §1 —
> the bounded EY injector is an ε² vent); a coordinated Festival sheds that
> vent *in one phase*, so the locality takes the full blast of the settlement's
> collective ε² in a single window. The working's output is bounded by the
> members' channeled input: the re-raised core is worth **at most** the
> coherence the members spent to hold it up, minus the `(1−q)` waste
> (`energy-harnessing.md` §2). **The multiplicity gain is the only amplification**
> — and that gain is the corpus's own law (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.2, `M ≈ 1`), not
> a hidden mint.

Three consequences, all honest:

1. **A Festival cannot mint.** No member's op can produce more coherence than it
   consumes (`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`); the whole Festival's `C(festival)` is bounded
   by the sum of its members' channeled input. The settlement that celebrates
   "for free" is impossible — the celebrating *is* the spending.
2. **A Festival at harvest is a *re-stock*, not a win.** The field is already
   organized; the Festival's re-raising is *maintaining* the core the way a
   well-tended bath maintains itself (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4's "a bath is a
   draw"), against the `(1−q)` waste and the shared drains. It cannot *add* to
   what the tide provided — it spends to hold the organized state's floor.
3. **A Festival at thin is a *sacrifice* — an intentional net draw.** The honest,
   brave version of "celebrate anyway": the settlement deliberately spends its
   members' coherence to hold the core (and the re-bound names) through the
   trough, knowing the book will show a coordinated drain. It is `the-burden`'s
   discipline made collective — a loan the field returns when it heals, not a
   gift.

**The no-free-energy cap holds** throughout: the working's output ≤ the members'
channeled input, the multiplicity gain being the only amplification — and that
gain (phase-locked = `M ≈ 1`) is the corpus's own designed commons law, so the
Festival never bypasses the cap; it exercises the one legitimate multiplier the
law provides.

## 3. The calendar — the tide-high feast day, and the sacrifice

### 3.1 The timing is the tide's

The Festival's rhythm is `tide-of-the-attractor.md`'s, not a separate clock.
The two states the tide names (§2) are physically meaningful and engine-real:
**harvest** (`q` high, `ε²` low, near the attractor — cheap machines, ore
precipitating, the biosphere's basins at full strength) and **thin** (`q` low,
`ε²` high, wasteful — machines glow bright, organisms shed, the desert-margin
creeps). The Festival is the **social face of that season**:

| Tide phase | A Festival here is | The honest cost/benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **Harvest** (the feast day) | **the cheap, rich re-bind** — "harvest the harvest" (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.2) done as one act: re-raises the already-organized core, re-locks the names at near-lossless, books a rich positive `C(festival)` | the vent is small (the field heals fast, `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2 magic row), the `(1−q)` floor low — the celebration is nearly free and the book shows the settlement's strength |
| **Thin** (the sacrifice) | **a deliberate, costly working to hold the settlement through the trough** — the honest brave version of "celebrate anyway": re-raising the core against the thinning field, re-binding the names before the desert-margin takes | the vent backs up (the field heals slowly), the `(1−q)` floor is high — the same act is a real, book-visible drain, a loan the field returns when it heals |

**"Harvest the harvest" is not a resource exploit** (`tide-of-the-attractor.md`
§5d: the tide's gift is *information and timing, not energy*). A Festival at
harvest re-raises the cheaply because the field is organized — it does not
harvest a high tide for free power; it withdraws (and re-holds) the coherence
the field actually organized, bounded by the bed/core's `q` and the
`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` cap. The honest statement from the farm's counterpart in
`farm-that-feeds.md` §5 — "the tide's gift ... is information and timing, not
energy" — applies to the Festival verbatim: **the tide does not *give* the
Festival power; it gives the settlement the *right moment* to spend its
members' coherence at the lowest possible cost.**

### 3.2 The seasonal rhythm — the settlement's celebrations ARE its coherence calendar

Because the Festival's cost and effect both ride the tide's phase, the
settlement's celebrations **are its coherence calendar** — the readable rhythm
of the local field written in the social act:

- **A harvest Festival, every harvest** is the settlement's standing
  re-stock-and-re-bind — the "harvest the harvest" of `tide-of-the-attractor.md`

- **A thin-trough Festival is a *decision*, not a default** — the settlement
  chooses to spend its members' coherence to hold the core (and the re-bound
  names) through the trough. It is `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.1's husbandry
  with a clock, done as one brave act: prevention made *celebratory*, holding

The calendar is **[design] over the tide's measured period**

spent coherence bound its frequency), and when the thin-sacrifice is on the
calendar are this doc's designed choices, calibrated after `tide-of-the-attractor.md`
§5a's probe returns a real season or a drift.

**The calendar-wide extension (reverse pointer, two-way).** This §3's statement —
