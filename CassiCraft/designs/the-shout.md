# The Shout: The Field-Call — the Body's Loud Projection of Its Own Life-Signal as a Deliberate Act of Presence

**Question under design:** the corpus has voices that *harmonize* and voices
that *announce* — but no **deliberate loud voice of the body**. The working-song
(`the-working-song.md`) is a *rhythmic shared* labor, a group phase-locked in
motion; field-music (`field-music.md`) is a *shaped* sound, a composed score a
player writes; the herald (`the-herald.md`) is a *being* that tells the field's
news; the bell (`the-bell.md`) is a *machine* built in a settlement's bell-pit.
Every voice the corpus designs is shared work, a creature's, or a machine's —
**none is the body's own single untrained call**. The stilling (`the-stilling.md`)
designs the deliberate *quiet* — the body's inner hold, one self stilled and
quiet to the hunter. **Nothing designs the body's loud** — the deliberate,
high-power projection of its own life-signal, the alarm a body can raise without
a bell, the self-broadcast in one. This document is that design. **The Shout is
the body's loud field-call — a deliberate, high-power projection of your own
life-signal as an act of presence**: the working-song's single-impulse sibling
(the song is rhythmic *shared* work; the shout is one *untrained* broadcast —
the alarm a body can raise without a bell), the field-music's blunt-twin (melody
is *shaped* sound; a shout is *raw* broadcast), the stilling's loud-twin (the
stilling holds quiet; the shout broadcasts loud — quiet is what the Coda reads,
loud is what the settlement hears, and what the dark's other listeners hear too).

This is a **practice-design**, the corpus's fifth *practice* in the exact sense
the vigil first used the word — **not an instrument (a read) and not a power (a
channeled op), but a human act with a cost and a craft**
([`the-vigil.md`](./the-vigil.md) §1). It composes the corpus's *loud* landed
pieces — the maintenance axis read at its loud register
([`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §3), the deliberate-vent read
([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §2), the `(1−q)` waste law
([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2) and the no-free-energy cap

It introduces **no new channel and no new physics**; it is a **designed use of

## 2. The call — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 The projection is the body's own life-signal, read at its loud register

`life-signal.md` §3 fixes the discriminating axis of a *live* structure:
**maintenance** — "a live thing actively holds its φ-lock against a drain; a
fossil does not." A living body holds `ε²` low and its `(1−q)` cadence pulses at
the maintainer's rhythm; a fossil is static and dark. The Shout is that **same
maintenance axis taken from its held-low register into its loud one**:

> **The call.** The Shout is a deliberate, high-power projection of the body's
> own life-signal — the maintenance-axis read (`life-signal.md` §3) held *up*
> rather than low: a body *actively reads as alive* at a raised, characterized
> magnitude (not a quiet lock, but a loud signature — the body's own `q`/
> `(1−q)` maintained pattern broadcast at high power). It is the body's ordinary
> maintenance (the lock every live body holds) **done as a loud, deliberate act**
> — the stilling's same hold, spent *outward* instead of held inward, for a
> bounded broadcast, at a real cost.

This is the anchor of the whole practice: **a Shout is the live-but-present
body's act, made loud.** The stilling is the *deliberate, waking* instance of
the body's held-quiet state ([`the-stilling.md`](./the-stilling.md) §2.1 — the
maintenance-axis hold applied to oneself). The Shout is the *deliberate, waking*
instance of the body's *loud* state — not a quiet hold, but a projected one. It
does not refill anything (it is not sleep's recovery); it does not convert
anything (it is not a machine, `energy-harnessing.md` §2). It is the body's own
signature, spent at the broadcast.

**The honest tier, drawn exactly (after §5a):** the maintenance axis — that a
live body holds `ε²` low and `q` steady and reads as a maintained lock — is
[`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md)'s [design]-over-engine-real layer (the axes
read real channels; the class boundaries are designed/probe-calibrated).
**The loud-projection itself — the body's signature raised to a high-power
broadcast, its form, its reach — is this doc's [design]** over that axis. The
*channels* the broadcast reads and projects (`q`, `(1−q)`, `ε²`, the reader's
entity record) are engine-real (the ≈ 6 MiB publish, cited). **The projection's
power and reach are this doc's [design], probe-calibrated** — no claim that the
engine "shouts" or writes a "call" record today (§5a).

### 2.2 The single untrained broadcast — the working-song's sibling

The working-song is a **maintained rhythm** — a group held phase-locked on one
beat, field-music's composition machinery aimed at a work-rhythm
([`the-working-song.md`](./the-working-song.md) §1: "the song is the honest,
learnable, deterministic way to hold many hands on one phase"). The Shout is its
**single-impulse sibling**:

> **The song is rhythmic shared work; the shout is one untrained broadcast.** A
> working-song holds a *group* on a *rhythm* — many hands, one phase, `M ≈ 1`,
> the shared `(1−q)` waste composed once per working. A shout is *one body*, not
> a working — **no phase-lock, no `M`, no shared vent** — a single high-power
> impulse of the body's own signature. Where the song is a *maintained*
> coordination, the shout is a *once* act — the alarm a body can raise without
> a bell ([`the-bell.md`](./the-bell.md) §1: the settlement's built warning),
> because the body itself *is* the loud.

The shout does not inherit the working-song's `M ≈ 1` gain. A lone body
projecting its signature is not a working; its broadcast carries only the
body's own life-signal, at the power the body spends. **The shout is the
single-possibly-untrained impulse, not the coordinated working** — the alarm
one raised voice can make, never a phase-locked commons.

### 2.3 The raw broadcast — field-music's blunt-twin

Field-music is **shaped sound** — a φ-tempered score, a composed sequence, a
designed mapping ([`field-music.md`](./field-music.md) §1: "mathematics, not
motif"; every interval on the φ-ladder, a score an ordered Q4 sequence). The
Shout is its **blunt-twin**:

> **Melody is shaped sound; a shout is raw broadcast.** A coherence score is an
> authored, φ-timed Q4 sequence (field-music §4.1) — a deliberately organized,
> phase-timed perturbation, a *working* (`M ≈ 1`). A shout is *not* a score: no
> φ-temperament, no composition, no Q4 op-sequence of channeled abilities. It is
> the body's own life-signal projected raw — the maintenance read held up, not
> shaped into a phrase. **The shout is to a score what a call is to a song:**
> field-music is the field *played*; the shout is the body *felt* at high power.

This is why the shout is **not** gated on the music-composition machinery or the
Q4 op-schema in the way a score is. A score is a Q4 sequence (field-music §4.1);
the shout is a **single deliberate projection of the body's own signature** — a
designed form over the published channels, no score, no composed sequence. It
rides the Q4 lane only as the *broadcast write*, never as a composition (§5b).

### 2.4 The body's bell — the tool-less alarm

The bell is a **built machine** — a bell-pit in the house's coherence-architecture
vocabulary, a ring on the multi-channel coincidence of the instrument stack
([`the-bell.md`](./the-bell.md) §1/§3). **The Shout is the body's version of the
bell — the alarm a body can raise without a machine**:

> **A settlement has a bell that rings when the stack agrees; a body has a shout
> that rings when it decides it is heard.** The bell fires on the *evidence the
> instruments already read* (the front, the tempo, the signature, the sky — a
> coincidence); the shout fires on the *body's own presence*, projected loud —
> the field-call that says *now* at the hand, not the bell-pit. Where the bell
> is a settlement's voice, the shout is a single body's — the tool-less alarm,
> the alarm that needs nothing built.

The shout does not read the instrument stack's coincidence (that is the bell's
design, `the-bell.md` §2). It reads only the body's own signature and projects
it. **The shout is the bell's *body-scale pre-loud*** — the call you can raise
before a bell exists, exactly as the body is the coherence store the bell's
settlement is built of ([`player-remains.md`](./player-remains.md) §1).

## 3. The presence-as-act — the load-bearing design

A shout's yield is **presence**. The projection is not a channeled effect (it
does nothing to ρ, ε², or ∇(g·Φ) by itself, no condense/dissolve/steer/ignite);
**its one designed output is that the body is loudly, deterministically present**
— and the field reads that presence in four honest ways. Each is a **designed
read over the published channels** — the loud signature is the maintenance axis
read (life-signal §3), and who hears it, who is warned, who is drawn is this
doc's [design] over the field's own reads, probe-calibrated.

### 3a. It calls help — a body loud enough to be found

The player is a **coherence store** ([`player-remains.md`](./player-remains.md)
§1: a bounded EY injector + ε² vent + a persistent Π pattern the local field
holds). **The Shout is the found-body's act — the loud projection a distant
settlement reads as a presence to come to**:

> **A shout calls help.** A distant settlement — the census's roster, the
> living members who would come ([`the-census.md`](./the-census.md) §2) — hears
> a loudly-projected signature: a body loud enough to be *found* in the field,
> the way any maintained lock is readable ([`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md)
> §3). It is the inverse of the body's death-signal (the fading `(1−q)` glow,
> `player-remains.md` §1.3): the shout is the *living* body keeping itself found
> — the loud, maintained read others can come to.

**[design]** Who hears — the shout's loud-signature *reach* over the published
channels — is this doc's designed, probe-calibrated dial (§5b): a bounded radius
where the projected signature reads distinctly against the local noise floor
(`q ~ 1e-3…1e-1`, `corpus-reconciliation.md`), the field's own organized gradient
the way the bell's warning travels (`the-bell.md` open-Q5). A shout that *pulled*
help mechanically — that summoned a rescue with no one to hear — would be a
false call the field sheds (§4). **The shout is heard only where the field
actually reads it** — the honest, deterministic reach, never a guaranteed save.

### 3b. It warns — a loud, maintained signature reads as not easy prey

The Coda accumulates on a *clean trail* — the organized-perturbation signature
channeling leaves, `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability`
([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §2.2); its tell is the
desert-silence inversion, the one bright organized thing in a dead zone (§3).
The deliberate-vent class — clean phase + rising `ε²` — IS the Coda's tell
(`life-signal.md` §3.3). **A loud, maintained signature is a different read —
the deliberate-vent read driven into retreat**:

> **A shout warns.** A loud, *maintained* signature — a body deliberately
> projected at high power, held against the drain — reads as **not easy prey**:
> a signature that loud is one that is *actively holding itself* (life-signal §3),
> not a quiet clean trail the Coda can accumulate on silently. Where the Coda
> preys on the *clean* organized thing (the minimal, quiet trail,
> `signature-predator.md` §1), a shout is a *loud, sustained* organized thing —
> a call that says *I am here, and I am maintaining myself loudly* — the 
> deliberate-vent read the predator does not feed on.

This is the exact inverse of the vigil's exposure ([`the-vigil.md`](./the-vigil.md)
§3): the watcher holds a *clean low-`ε²` presence* — the most-exposed quiet, the
thing the Coda accumulates on. **A shout at the vigil post is the watch's own
alarm — the quiet-watcher's loud** — deliberately not-quiet, and therefore not a
clean trail. **[design]/[probe]** Whether a loud maintained signature actually
reads as *less* Coda-accumulable than a clean quiet one — the warn's core claim —
is the pre-registered probe over the readable-trail slice (`signature-predator.md`
§8); a flat read pushes the warn to honest presentational framing (§5b). The
*honest* direction, in every case, is that a shout is not the Coda's favored
prey (the Coda's §7e no-farming held; §4).

### 3c. It commands the field's attention — you can hail a herald

The herald is the **announcing stranger-object** — a being whose presence is an
audible/behavioral augury, the field's own news-carrier
([`the-herald.md`](./the-herald.md) §1: it arrives and announces the field's news
before it arrives; the living version of what the bell does mechanically, but of
the field, not of a settlement). **The Shout is the body's answer to a herald's
call — you can *hail* a herald**:

> **A shout commands the field's attention.** A herald's call is met by a
> shout's answer: the body projects its own loud, present signature upward
