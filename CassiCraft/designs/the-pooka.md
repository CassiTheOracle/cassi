## 2. The riled draw — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 The riled signature — grounded in the maintenance read

`life-signal.md` §3/§3.1 fixes the discriminating axis of a live structure:
**maintenance** — a live thing "actively holds its φ-lock against a drain",
moving, its `(1−q)` glow pulsing at the maintainer's cadence, `ε²` held low, its
motion steering against the river's gradient. The four-class matrix reads each
structure on motion, `(1−q)` cadence, `(1−q)` mean, `ε²` gradient, and `∇(g·Φ)`
alignment. **The pooka is drawn to the maintenance read at its most disturbed —
the panic: the boundary where a maintained live lock *breaks into a riled one*.**

| The maintenance channel (life-signal §3.1) | The quiet, maintained reading | The riled, panicked reading (the pooka's food) |
|---|---|---|
| **motion** | steers to hold position (a bounded steering offset) | **wild motion** — a strong, wandering steering offset; the body fleeing, the walk's controlled stride (`the-walk.md` §2a) collapsed into a bolt, the fall's register (`the-fall.md` §2 — the gradient owning the body bare) |
| **`(1−q)` cadence** | pulses at the maintainer's bounded rhythm | **ragged cadence** — the glow racing out of the bounded pulse, no settled feed-and-settle, an open, irregular climb fall |
| **`(1−q)` mean** | low-to-moderate (glows while feeding) | **rising** — the panic sheds `(1−q)` the way a vent does (`life-signal.md` §3.3, the deliberate-vent class), but unaimed |
| **`ε²` gradient** | flat-low (`ε²` held below a floor) | **raised and rising** — the panic's `ε²` climbing, the decoherence of a body that stopped holding its lock clean |
| **`∇(g·Φ)` alignment** | tracks or actively opposes (the body steers) | **dis-aligned** — the motion runs *with* the gradient's pull (fleeing downhill/into a well) or tears across it without steering; no maintained counter-term |

The riled signature is **the deliberate-vent-like class read on a moving body
that is not deliberately venting** — `life-signal.md` §3.2's own honest
