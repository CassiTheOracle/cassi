# The Dune: The Moving Sand — a Ridge the Wind Walks Across the Desert, the Land That Migrates

**Question under design:** the corpus has the desert as the *static dry regime* — the standing thin, the Coda's home terrain, a window living at the attractor's thin edge ([`the-desert.md`](./the-desert.md) §2) — and the wind as the *flow* — a directional current of coherence/ε² through the air, the weather stack's flow-face ([`the-wind.md`](./the-wind.md) §1). And the corpus has the delta as the *braided land* — the living line's fan, the river's order spent into many ([`the-delta.md`](./the-delta.md) §2). But it has **no moving *ground-form***: no dune, no ridge of sand the wind walks across the desert. Every landform the corpus designs is *stationary* (the desert's dry, the delta's fan, the river's line, the cave's hollow sit where they are), and every moving thing the corpus designs is *air or water* (the wind's current, the sea's flow). **None designs the land itself as a traveler** — a ground-form that migrates on the wind, the desert's own wandering landform, distinct from the stationary dries and the flowing air. This document is that design: **the Dune is the moving sand — the desert's own wandering landform, a ridge that migrates on the wind: the only terrain that is itself a traveler, the field's own carried matter walking the desert.**

The Dune is deliberately the corpus's **only moving landform**, not a second desert and not a ground-wind. It is the desert's *landform*, the wind's *consequence*, and its own *traveler*: no other terrain moves of itself. ## 4. The bury and the yield — the load-bearing honesty

The dune buries what it covers, and the bury-and-yield is honest in the corpus's exact, *symmetric* sense: **the cover is the field's own sand, and it is spent and gone when the dune moves on.** This is the load-bearing honesty of the whole design.

### (a) A dune covers a place — and when it moves on, what it covered is read beneath

The archaeology doc grounds the corpus's deep-time honesty: the field's past is written into its present as **residue** — the fraction the dynamics left behind because it is too deep-rung, too `q`-locked, too ordered to dissolve ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §1/§2; the fossil-residue model is its doc's [design]-grounded model, read through the coherence reader). A **stratum is a band of the field holding the record** — the core sample reads the void the way it reads any stratum ([`the-cave.md`](./the-cave.md) §3). **A dune covers a place.** The wandering ridge over-lays a locality of the desert's thin surface — a scar's bright edge, a husbander's kept patch, a settlement's small mark — and when the dune moves on, **what it covered is read beneath**: the covered place, kept from the `(1−q)` drift and the surface's re-laying, reads as the residue the moving land has held ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §2 — the land's own archaeology, the strata-to-come; [`the-scar-lifecycle.md`](./the-scar-lifecycle.md) §2.3 — the covered place's read at the moving land's register).

> **The yield is the land's own archaeology.** A dune covers a place, and when the wind moves it on, what it covered is read beneath — the residue held under the ridge, read the way the archaeology reads any strata ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §2; [`the-cave.md`](./the-cave.md) §3). The burial is not a deletion and the yield is not a recovery-from-nothing: the dune *moved sand over* a place and *moved sand off* it, and the covered place is the residue the field already held, now read where the ridge no longer hides it.

### (b) The cover is the field's own sand, spent and gone when the dune moves on — never a hidden burial, never a free excavation
