## 2. The falling water — the honest mechanics

### (a) The river's descent, taken at once — the carried current spent as a drop

The River's descent is a **carried** gradient: a raft rides it as a fluid car
(`the-river.md` §3a), a swimmer and a diver work with and against it
(`the-river.md` §3b), and its resistance is the honest cost of the ride. **The
waterfall is that same descent taken *without* carriage — the `∇(g·Φ)` the river
flows down, spent at once as a free vertical drop off the cliff's face.** The fall
is not a new force and not a new water; it is the river's own continued descent at
the one place the ground falls away beneath it. The descent made visible: the 
same field-hydro gradient the settlement can tap with a mill (`energy-harnessing.md`
§1.1 — potential gradients `∇(g·Φ)`, "field hydro") is here *shown* — the 
gradient's magnitude made a spectator's read and a falling water.

### (b) The edge — the vertical's face meeting the plain's level

A waterfall is defined by its **edge**: the cliff where the carrying ground ends
and the fall begins. That edge is the vertical's face meeting a new level — the
sea's flat plain (`the-sea.md` §2b) or a lower terrace the river reaches only by
falling. **The waterfall is the interface between the climb's height and the
plain's level** — the same vertical the climb ascends deliberately
(`the-climb.md` §1) and the fall descends *without* the climb's care — taken by
the water itself. The edge's draw — how steep, how long the drop, where the spray
thins the local read — is a read on the published `∇(g·Φ)` and `(1−q)`, this
doc's [design] over the river's landed descent, probe-calibrated.

### (c) The danger, legible — the fall's drop reads before you commit

The Waterfall's danger inherits the corpus's one pre-read discipline: the climb
reads a wall's height-feasibility before it commits — a coherent face gives good
holds, a scarred or thin face wastes `(1−q)`, and a failed hold is a fall
(`the-climb.md` §3.1/§3.2/§3.3/§4.3). **The waterfall applies the same pre-read
to the water's edge: the fall's drop reads on the same `∇(g·Φ)` the climb reads,
before a body is committed to the water.** The drop-and-the-edge are legible on
the instruments — the gradient's magnitude, the edge's proximity, the fall's
height all published channels — and the honest design is the legible read, never
a hidden slope. **A waterfall that "hid its edge until you were in it" is a false
display the field's determinism sheds** (`the-dispute.md` §2). The same
determinism that makes the drop dangerous makes it *readable*, and the
readability is the honesty.

### (d) The display — the hum of the fall, the field's own voice

The field's voice is the corpus's designed music: auroras hum, the biosphere hums,
each organism hums at its rung and a herd is a chord (`field-music.md` §2.4 — the
biosphere's hum; §1 the sound of the field). **A waterfall's roar is that same
voice at the gradient's edge — the `(1−q)` discharge and the water's fall heard
as the river's loud, legible hum.** The roar is not a message (the field cannot
telegraph, `the-dispute.md` §2); it is the field's own discharge made audible — a
sound the instruments can read as a loud persistent hum at the fall, distinct
from the storm's dissonant growl (`field-music.md` §2.2) and the desert's silence
(`field-music.md` §2.3). **The waterfall is gravity's own display, and its voice
is the field's own.**

### (e) The base — the plain that takes the fall

The waterfall falls **into** the plain — the sea's flat coherence plain
(`the-sea.md` §2b) or a lower reach — and its base is where the descent's spent
energy disperses into the level water. That base is a real place the seacraft
crosses around (`the-seacraft.md` §2a — a seacraft rides the wind's current
across the sea) and a swimmer enters only by choice; the fall's plunge-pool is
the one reach of the plain the crossing deliberately avoids. **The waterfall's
base is the descent's landing — the place the drop's spent `(1−q)` settles into
the plain, honest about what it received.**

### (f) The drop's form — this doc's [design] over the landed primitives

The Waterfall's mechanics are entirely composed from the landed primitives —
