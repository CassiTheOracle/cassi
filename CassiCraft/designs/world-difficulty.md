## 2. The turbulence dial — the honest mechanics

### (a) The dial as a scaling parameter over the landed ambient

`D` is not a new engine law and not a new channel. It is a **world-birth
coefficient** — set when a window is born (like the seed), carried as part of the
sim's ambient, and read back continuously by the instruments. Mechanically, `D`
scales three landed quantities, each already a real, published channel:

| What `D` scales | The landed quantity | The scaling ([design]) | Grounding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The `ε²` noise floor's mean and variance** | the field's ambient coherence (`q ~ 1e-3…1e-1` at noise up to the attractor `q ≈ 0.947`, `energy-harnessing.md` §7 Q1) | `D` shifts where the field's *ambient rest* sits — a low-`D` world rests quiet near the attractor with a tight floor, a high-`D` world rests busier with a wider floor. **The attractor `q ≈ 0.947` is NOT moved** — `D` scales the ambient *excursion around* the φ-attractor, never the equilibrium itself. A wild world's rest is a noisier rest, not a different equilibrium. | the `q` band is engine-real (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §2); the excursion-scaling is [design], probe-calibrated |
| **The weather's frequency** | the storm's season — the density of weather events inside the tide's phase (`weather-not-storm.md` §6e: weather is *seasonal* — more likely at thin tide, never causal) | `D` scales how many weather events the season carries — a calm world's thin tide brings a few storms; a wild world's thin tide brings many. **The tide's *phase* is not changed**; `D` scales the density of the events the phase *frames*. The tide stays the season, never the cause. | the tide-correlation is landed (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2; `weather-not-storm.md` §6e); the density multiply is [design] |
| **The hazard-amplitude ceiling** | the storm's `c_s`-traveling `ε²` front strength — how deep the dissolution-floor crossing goes at its peak (`field-hazards.md` §2) | `D` scales the ceiling on how strong a front can be at its peak and how deep its dissolution reaches. **Capped by the no-free-energy ceiling** — a wild world's stronger fronts are still bounded by `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` (`energy-harnessing.md` §6), so a wild world is never a richer world to harvest. | the front's `c_s = h₀/dt` mechanics are engine-real (`field-hazards.md` §2.1); the amplitude ceiling's scale is [design], held under the cap |

These three are not three dials but **one dial made of one law**: `D` sits on the
ambient and the field does its own thing at that ambient. A calm world's field is
quiet; a wild world's field is busy. The scaling rule that *chooses* the multiply —
how the ladder steps, how the ceiling scales, where the band's excursion lands — is
the [design].

### (b) The dial's ladder or continuum

`D` can be a **continuum or a short ladder** — the corpus's house style prefers the
honest option: a **small named ladder** (calm → moderate → wild → stormbound, or
`D ∈ {0, 1, 2, 3}`), each rung a fixed, probe-calibrated coefficient on the three
landed quantities. The ladder is [design] for the same reason the tide's season
length is [design] ([`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §5b):
the field's ambient is continuous, and turning that continuity into *named*,
*readable*, *reproducible* rungs is the design's contribution. A named ladder keeps
two honesty properties:

- **Determinism is a hard gate** (§5c): same seed + same `D` rung = same ambient
  tempo, same weather statistics, every run. The ladder is a fixed table of
  coefficients, never a seeded-RNG grade, never a roll. A player who re-enters the
  same world at the same `D` reads the same restlessness, every time.
- **The attractor and the cap are invariant** across all rungs. `D` scales the
  ambient excursion, never the `q ≈ 0.947` equilibrium; it scales the front's
  ceiling, never the `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` bound. The ladder changes *what is
  happening*, never *what can be taken*.

The task premise names a continuum ("scale the field's ambient activity"); the design
commits to the **named ladder over a dial** because a named, legible, reproducible
set of rungs serves the accessibility and determinism gates better than a hidden
slider. If Phase-1 measurement finds the continuum is needed (e.g. a per-window
*grade* authored into a window star's sparse publish), that is a later, separate
[design] — the initial rung set is what ships.

### (c) The engine-real anchors — what the floor already is, what the publish already carries

The dial is honest because it scales **quantities the engine already computes and the
publish already carries**. The `ε²` noise floor is the canonical band the field lives
at (`q ~ 1e-3…1e-1` at noise), read every snapshot from the published `q`/`ε²`
channels. The weather's frequency is the classifier's tide-correlation read
([`weather-not-storm.md`](./weather-not-storm.md) §6e), a derived read over the
published `ε²`/`q` time-series. The hazard-amplitude ceiling is the storm front's
peak `ε²`, read from the published `ε²` the sampler already consumes every tick
([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2; [`chunk-field-quantization.md`](./chunk-field-quantization.md)
§2). **No new channel, no new telemetry, no new write** — the dial is a coefficient
over channels the ≈ 6 MiB publish already carries (`q` 1 + pot 1 + `∇(g·Φ)` 3 + `ρ` 1,
[`corpus-reconciliation.md`](./corpus-reconciliation.md) §2), read inside the ≈ 1–6
ms/tick sample budget, at the cost profile of the Weatherglass's single sample
([`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md) §1.4). Exactly the
read-only-consumer discipline ([`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md) §5.1)
and the instrument family rule ([`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md)
§2.1): the dial is a read of the ambient, never a new channel, never a write INTO
the field's state beyond the birth-time ambient coefficient.

**The one honest nuance — what the dial's high setting does to the field.** `D` is
not purely presentational: a wild world's ambient *does* inject more perturbation,
which means the field has more to organize against and more `(1−q)` waste to shed.
That is the honest face of "wild" — the field is genuinely busier, and the waste law
([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2) means that extra activity is
largely spent as glow, not stored as usable order. This is the whole point of §4:
**a wild world is noisy, and its noise is mostly waste.** The dial changes what is
happening, never what can be taken.

## 3. The load-bearing design — difficulty as the world's own tempo

### (a) A wild world is busier but never richer, never meaner

The load-bearing design, stated once and kept: **difficulty is the world's own
tempo — a wild world is a busier field, not a richer or a meaner one.** The three
scalings of §2 compose into a single honest identity:

- **Busier, by the field's own law.** A wild world's ambient floor is higher, its
  weather denser, its fronts stronger — but every one of those follows the same
  `c_s`-front, the same tide-phase, the same re-lock toward `q ≈ 0.947`. The wild
  world is not a different physics; it is the same physics at a higher ambient. The
  player who reads the world's restlessness is reading the field's real
  `ε²`-excursion, not a mood dial.
- **Never richer.** The no-free-energy cap holds at every `D` ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md)
  §6: `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`). A wild world's stronger fronts, denser storms, and
  busier floor generate MORE `ε²` events — and more `(1−q)` waste. The harvestable
  order is **diminished, not increased**: the extra activity is largely the waste
  law's glow, and a "wild world is richer" claim is a false mint the field sheds.
  The coherence turbine's yield is capped the same at every difficulty — the dial
  changes what is happening, never what can be taken. **A wild world is a worse
  place to be, in the honest sense that its field is harder to hold — not a better
  place to loot.**
- **Never meaner.** The weather-vs-punishment classifier is inherited exactly
  ([`weather-not-storm.md`](./weather-not-storm.md) §2, §6). A wild world's storms
  are MORE weather — more frequent and stronger events that happen *where the field
  is*, ordered, bounded, self-limiting — never MORE punishment. And the Coda still
  only trails the player's channeling trail ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md)
  §1/§2): a wild world's higher ambient does not make the world track the player. A
  "difficulty that hunts the player" is a false reading the field's indifference
  sheds.

### (b) The dial is legible ambient, not a hidden stat

The corpus's cardinal rule is **readable before it arrives** ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md)
§5.1); the dial extends it to the ambient itself — **a player reads the world's
restlessness before choosing to stay or leave.** This is the load-bearing
accessibility claim:

- **The ambient tempo reads on the instruments.** The Weatherglass's four forms
  ([`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md) §1.2) — the steady lume at high
  `q`, the `(1−q)` waste tint, the climbing-glow excursions, the lean — all carry the
  ambient's restlessness. A calm world's lume rests quiet; a wild world's lume
  trembles, its waste tint warmer, its climbing glows more frequent. The `(1−q)`
  glow floor ([`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §4) and the
  sky's restiveness ([`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md)
  §3.3) read the same: **the world's restlessness is a field state, legible to
  anyone who reads the field.**
- **Never a hidden difficulty, never a secret world-stat.** The dial is a
  world-birth setting and it stays readable — a player who sits in a wild world and
  ignores the instruments learns it the hard way, but never from a *hidden* source.
  The legibility is structural (the family rule, [`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md)
  §2.1: every instrument reads the same publish) — the ambient tempo is on the field,
  and the field is public.
- **The honest feel:** a calm world is a place you can *let down* — the field's rest
  is a floor you can hold. A wild world is a place you must *stay alert in* — the
  field's rest is a stir you continually read. Both are the same law; the player who
  reads the sky, the lume, and the glow reads which world they are in, and chooses
  accordingly.

### (c) The same law holds at every setting

The load-bearing coherence claim: **there is no dial position where the law
changes.** Three invariants hold across every rung of `D`:

1. **The equations are identical.** The PDE, the source terms, the `c_s`-front, the
   tide — all run unchanged. `D` is a coefficient on the ambient, not a new term in
   any equation.
2. **The attractor is invariant.** Every world re-locks to `q ≈ 0.947`; `D` scales
   the excursion around it, never the equilibrium.
3. **The cap is invariant.** `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` holds at every `D`, so a wild
   world's extra activity is bounded, honest, and un-mintable.

This is what makes the dial *coherent*: a player who understands a calm world
understands a wild one — they are the same law at a different ambient, the same field
at a different restlessness. The dial does not introduce a second physics; it names
the parameter that makes one physics produce calm worlds and wild ones.

## 4. The load-bearing honesty

### (a) Never punishment — the classifier inherited

The dial's first honesty is the danger layer's spine held at every `D`: **difficulty
never aims the world at the player.** The weather-vs-punishment classifier
([`weather-not-storm.md`](./weather-not-storm.md) §2) is inherited exactly: a wild
world's storms are **MORE weather** — more frequent and stronger events that happen
*where the field is*, called by the field's own dynamics (the tide's thin phase, the
regional `q` trough), self-limiting, readable, shelterable. They are never MORE
punishment — nothing the wild dial adds happens *where the player is* because of the
player. And the aimed class never scales with the dial: the Coda still only trails
the player's channeling trail ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md)
§1/§2), so a wild world's higher ambient does not make the world hunt. **A "difficulty
that hunts the player" is a false reading the field's indifference sheds.** The
cruel-world failure mode the classifier closes ([`weather-not-storm.md`](./weather-not-storm.md)
§5.3) is closed here identically: a wild world is hostile by its own restlessness,
readable as such, never a punishment aimed at the player.

### (b) Never a power source — the cap held at every setting

The dial's second honesty is the no-free-energy law held unchanged: **difficulty is
never a power source.** At every `D`:

- **`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §6) is
  the hard bound.** A wild world's stronger fronts, denser storms, and busier floor
  generate more `ε²` events — but every conversion is amplitude-capped net-negative.
  You cannot "set the difficulty high to farm its storms" any more than you can farm
  a storm ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §5.3) or a wind
  ([`the-wind.md`](./the-wind.md) §5d).
- **The `(1−q)` waste law ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2) is
  what a wild world's extra activity mostly IS.** A high-`D` world runs wasteful — its
  busy floor sheds `E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput` as glow. A "wild world is richer"
  claim is a false mint the field sheds: the extra activity is the waste, not the
  order.
- **The coherence turbine's yield is capped the same at every difficulty.**
  ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2.2/§6; [`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md) §1.3) The
  dial changes what is happening, never what can be taken. **A wild world is a harder
  place to hold, not a better place to mine.**

The honest phrasing, stated once: **the dial moves the ambient, and the ambient's
movement is mostly waste. A wild world offers more weather and less usable order —
the opposite of a reward dial, and by design.**

### (c) The field is indifferent — a setting, not a temperament

The dial's third honesty borrows the dispute's ground truth
([`the-dispute.md`](./the-dispute.md) §2): the field cannot lie, cannot be bargained
with, has no will. **World Difficulty is a world-birth setting, not the field
choosing to be hostile.** A wild world is not an angry world; it is a world whose
birth-ambient was set restless. At every dial position the field follows the same
indifferent equations. The player who reads a wild world as "the world has turned
against me" has misread a setting as a temperament — the field is indifferent at
every `D`, exactly as it is indifferent in a dispute (it adjudicates what is held,
never who is right). The dial is a parameter of the sim's ambient, and the sim's
ambient has no will.

### (d) Determinism held

The dial's fourth honesty is the hard gate (§5c): **same world-seed + same `D` =
same ambient tempo, same weather statistics, every run.** No seeded-RNG difficulty
dice, no player-relative variance, no "difficulty roll." The dial's effect is a
deterministic function of the field's real trajectory under the birth coefficient —
reproducible, learnable, and probe-verifiable (the verify gate, §5b). A player who
re-enters the same world at the same `D` reads the same restlessness, every time, and
can *learn* the world rather than reading a mood.

### (e) Never hidden-only

The dial's fifth honesty is the accessibility rule made structural ([`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md)
§2.1; [`field-music.md`](./field-music.md) §6): **the ambient tempo is legible, never
a hidden-only stat.** A player who never "opens a difficulty panel" loses nothing —
the world's restlessness is on the lume, the waste tint, the sky, the sound. The dial
is not a world-stat the game hides; it is the field's own restlessness, read the way
every field read works.

## Open questions

1. **The excursion-scaling's shape (§2/§5b).** Does scaling the ambient floor's mean
   and variance *de-correlate* (a high-`D` world is both noisier and wider) or scale
   the band as a whole? The [design] rule's shape — mean-shift vs variance-widening —
   is a probe-calibrated [design] dial against the real ambient's excursion; the
   Phase-1 slice measures it. **[design]/[probe]**
2. **The weather-frequency dial's gate on the probes (§5b/§6).** If the tide's §5a
   probe returns *drift* (no regional-`q` oscillation) or the provenance probe returns
   verdict-B (every storm carries a source), the weather-frequency dial collapses to a
   *density over sources* rather than a *density over a season*. Does the dial then
   reframe as "the density of what the field's sources do" (honest, weaker), or defer
   its weather-frequency face entirely until the season is real? The doc prefers the
   honest reframe; the decision is open until the probes return. **[design]**
3. **The hazard-amplitude ceiling vs. the no-free-energy cap (§2c/§5d).** Where,
   precisely, does "a stronger front" stop being "the field's own extreme, louder"
   and start violating the `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` cap (a front whose dissolution
   *pays* rather than *spends*)? The ceiling's scale is a [design] dial, held under
   the cap; the exact relationship between the front's peak `ε²` and the cap's bound
   needs a Phase-1 field-side measurement. **[design]/[probe]**
4. **The directed/ambient sibling relation — `the-incantation.md` landed on disk
   while this doc was written.** The corpus's directed-perturbation language doc
   (`designs/the-incantation.md`) was NOT present at write time (I composed this
   doc around the absent sibling), and it is now on disk — a parallel-doc sibling
   that crossed mid-wave. Its §1 blockquote confirms the directed/ambient relation
   this doc anticipated (the incantation "stirs what is already there ... or to
   settle what is there" — DIRECTED perturbation; the difficulty dial sets the
   AMBIENT baseline those acts speak against — a wild world is pre-stirred, harder
   to settle, easier to hide their directed stir in). I did not re-verify every
   §-pointer of the landed incantation at composition (a sibling re-pass is the
   clean next step), but the relation itself holds on its stated spine. Whether the
   incantation's acts read *harder to hear* over a busy floor, and whether a wild
   world's pre-stirring is genuinely "easier to hide in" against the Coda's
   trail-read (which is legible only where the field has no order to hide it —
   [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §1.2), is the sibling question
   this doc holds open for that re-pass. **[design]**
5. **The wild world's "easier to hide in" slide (§4/§6).** A high-`D` world's busy
   floor could read as concealment — the noisier field drowning the Coda's trail-read
   the way the wind carries a signature downwind ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md)
   §1.2's legibility-only-where-quiet line). Is "a wild world hides you" a designed
   consequence (the field's own orderlessness, honest) or an unintended
   difficulty-farming of concealment (the no-free-energy cap's dark twin)? The design
   leans to the honest consequence, but the boundary is a [design] dial against the
   Coda's legibility probe. **[design]/[probe]**
6. **The rung-vs-continuum boundary (§2b).** Is the named ladder the right long-term
   form, or does a per-window *grade* authored into a window star's sparse publish
   (via the Far Mirror's read of a distant window, [`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md)
   §3.2) let a traveler *scout* a destination's difficulty before sailing — a
   cross-window difficulty read that the named ladder serves but the continuum makes
   finer? The initial design ships the named ladder; the cross-window grade is a
   later [design]. **[design]**
