the cold (`the-cold.md`), the fate (`fate-of-a-window.md`), the festival

**the Window's Year** — the corpus's *time doc*, the calendar the settlement keeps.

clock `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]` and its regional `q` season—is the *patient-field* and the

celebrates, mourns, elects, and endures** — the calendar made out of the observances
the corpus's source docs already name, mounted on the three time-scales they already
design.

### 3.1 The festival's day — the bright anchor

## 4. The rhythm of living in it

The Window's Year exists to give a settlement's days a **readable rhythm**. Its
purpose is not to add observances; it is to make the patient field's patience
**livable** — to mount the loan's repayment window (`patient-field.md` §3.3 — "the
same channeled act costs more patience at thin tide") as the year's shape, so the
settlement's relationship to its time is a rhythm it can read and plan around, not a
list of events it must track.

| When in the year | The settlement's act | The calendar marks it |
|---|---|---|
| **The harvest** (tide's pulse up) | **work** — the deep, expensive, `q`-hungry acts: ore-mining, Still-Room channeling, authoring, the deep-rung reaps (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §3.2) | the festival's day at the apex; the work-window the pulse's high makes |
| **The thin** (tide's pulse down) | **rest, husband** — the vent backs up, the loan is dear; efficient deep work is deferred (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2; `patient-field.md` §3.3) | the thin-trough observances, the ordinary "we endure" days |
| **The cold's decade** (the long thin) | **endure** — hold the bath, stretch the pantry, fall the farm, decide whether to leave (`the-cold.md` §3) | the thin-year observances — the dark spans |
| **The festival's day** | **celebrate** — the one bright anchor, the strongest single `C(festival)` (`the-festival.md` §3.2) | the bright anchor |
| **The anniversaries** | **mourn, remember** — the departed's quiet dates, the funeral's re-memory (`the-funeral.md` §3.3) | the quiet beats |
| **The steward's cycle** | **choose** — the tide-account close and reopen, the read of who holds the core (`the-election.md` §3.4) | the governance beat |

**What the calendar IS for, stated once:** it makes the window's three time-scales
(tide, cold, fate) *legible as one lived year* — a year the settlement can feel as a
rhythm: the bright work-crescendo of the harvest, the quiet endurance of the thin,
the decade-long patience of the cold, the drift of the arc toward the window's end.
A settlement that reads its year knows *when* its acts are cheap (harvest), *when*
they are dear (thin), *when* to celebrate (the festival), *when* to mourn (the
anniversaries), *when* to choose (the cycle), and *when* to endure (the cold). The
calendar is the **patient field's patience given a schedule** — the loan's repayment
window mounted as the shape of the year, so the settlement's time is not an
invisible drain but a rhythm it lives.

**The honest composition, once:** the Window's Year **is** the festival doc's
calendar (`the-festival.md` §3 — the tide-high feast day and the thin-sacrifice) **+
the cold's decade** (`the-cold.md` — the dark spans) **+ the fate's arc**
(`fate-of-a-window.md` — the drift), mounted as one object. The festival's calendar

arc are as much part of the year as the bright anchor. **A settlement's days become a
readable rhythm rather than a list of events** — the corpus's time, told as a year.
