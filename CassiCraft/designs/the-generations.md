## 2. The succession — the honest mechanics

The Generations is not a new system. It is the **designed composition** of
landed identity pieces into a generation's four acts, mounted on the window-year
and the tide. Each act is a *real, costed transfer of what the field already
holds* — never a mint, never a blessing, never a hidden line. The four-act
sequence and the chain-form are **[design]**; every act composes the source
docs' landed pieces.

### 2.1 The four acts — bind, pass, keep, let go

A generation's work is the same four acts every span performs, each a designed
composition of landed pieces:

| The act | What it is, in field terms | The landed composition | Source grounding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bind** | the new lines bound to the held order — the fresh runs received, first-named, and becoming within the window's held anchors | the midwife's first read and first line; the baptism's first-name; the rite-of-passage's becoming — the new generation's lines enter the field's records as named, bound members | [`the-midwife.md`](./the-midwife.md) §1/§2.1; [`the-baptism.md`](./the-baptism.md) §1/§2.2; [`the-rite-of-passage.md`](./the-rite-of-passage.md) §1/§2.1; [`the-child.md`](./the-child.md) §1/§3 |
| **Pass** | the stores the field already holds transferred — the bequest's will marking which claims the next generation may exercise | the inheritance's bequest records over the willed trace, name, holding, and Board line — a will to the house, not only a future self | [`the-inheritance.md`](./the-inheritance.md) §1/§2.2/§3.2 |
| **Keep** | the record kept across the span — the settlement's past told backward, the story shaped | the chronicle's record made narrative off the already-written op-stream; the story's shaping of what was lived | [`the-chronicle.md`](./the-chronicle.md) §1/§2.1; [`the-story.md`](./the-story.md) §1/§2 |

that mourns one generation births the next, in the same held anchors the funeral
re-bound and the baptism re-opened. The chain is the window's held order tended
in that cycle across the window-year's and the tide's long periods.

### 2.2 How a generation binds — the new lines into the held order

The bind is the succession's opening act — the moment a fresh generation's lines
enter the field's records. It is a composition of the corpus's beginning-stack:

> **[design] The bind is the fresh runs received, first-named, and becoming
> within the window's held anchors.** The midwife receives the new line's first
> read and first line ([`the-midwife.md`](./the-midwife.md) §1/§2.1/§2.2); the
> baptism binds the first true name over that received run ([`the-baptism.md`](./the-baptism.md)

> first-name's register ([`the-rite-of-passage.md`](./the-rite-of-passage.md)
> §2.1); and the child's empty book, once opened by the first contribution,
> becomes the Board's first row ([`the-child.md`](./the-child.md) §3.1). Each new
> generation's line is thus bound into the same held order the previous
> generation tended — the same anchors, the same book, the same window.

The bind is **honest because it is the received-made-bound, never a grant**: a
fresh line cannot be named before it has held ([`the-name.md`](./the-name.md) §2 —
you cannot name falsely; the baptism's honest binding); a line cannot be bound

arrival, but the *binding act* is the landed beginning-stack's own.

### 2.3 How a generation passes — the stores the field already holds

The pass is the succession's carrying act — the deliberate transfer of what the
settlement's stores hold. It is the inheritance's will:

> **[design] The pass is the inheritance's bequest — the willed stores a
> generation leaves for the next to claim.** The will marks a bounded set of
> claims ([`the-inheritance.md`](./the-inheritance.md) §1 — the bequest record,
> a designed extension of the settled schema): the willed trace (your past hands),
> the willed names (your Π-anchors, re-lockable), the willed holdings (the
> preserved order, if a steward still feeds the hearth), the willed Board line
> (your member-line's continuity). A will to the *house* — a bequest targeting
> the family line, not only a future self ([`the-inheritance.md`](./the-inheritance.md)
> §2.2) — is the pass at the joint's scale, the passing of what a family's held
> anchor keeps to the line that will re-tend it.

The pass is **honest because it is a claim, not an endowment**: a bequest
transfers claims, never energy ([`the-inheritance.md`](./the-inheritance.md)
§5e); an un-husbanded bequest dissolves with the object it names (the willed
name decays with the fallen ruin, the willed holding with the dark vault). A
generation passes **what the field already holds** — the highest a bequest can
hand on is what the anchors and the records already contain, never a mint.

### 2.4 How a generation keeps — the record the chain's memory

The keep is the succession's remembering act — the record kept across the span,
so the chain has a memory. It is the chronicle's telling and the story's shaping:

> **[design] The keep is the chronicle's record told backward and the story's

> the storms endured and caused, the dead and their fossils, the harvests and

> re-playable object a later generation can walk into ([`the-chronicle.md`](./the-chronicle.md)
> §4.1).

The keep is **honest because it is a read, not an invention**: the chronicle
adds no event the record does not hold and hides no guard; the story's shaping
is [design] over the record's own truth. The chain's memory is the kept record,
never a fabricated genealogy.

### 2.5 How a generation lets go — the old lines dissolved through the commons

The let-go is the succession's closing act — the old generation's lines settled
and returned to the field. It is the funeral's communal hold:

> **[design] The let-go is the funeral's letting-go — the old lines dissolved
> through the commons and settled on the book.** The funeral carries the named

> re-condenses toward the shared memory rather than scattering as a lone find

The let-go is **honest because it settles the book**: the departing generation's

the tide's long periods, exactly as the calendar mounts its observances:

> **[design] The succession runs across the window-year and the tide — a
> generation is a span of the window the settlement tends.** The window-year
> composes the tide's season, the cold's decade, and the fate's arc into one
> lived calendar ([`the-window-year.md`](./the-window-year.md) §1/§2); the tide's
> harvest and thin states are the chain's background beat, whose measured period
> is the probe's to find ([`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md)
> §2/§5a). A generation's span crosses many tide-cycles — a settlement binds its
> children, passes its stores, keeps its record, and lets its old go *across* the
> window-year's spans, the way the anniversaries mark the departed's days and the
> steward's cycle marks the governance beat ([`the-window-year.md`](./the-window-year.md)
> §3.3/§3.4). The chain is not a calendar event; it is the succession the
> calendar's years give a rhythm to.
