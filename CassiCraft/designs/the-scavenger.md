# The Scavenger: The Denizen of the Spent — The Fit Creature That Lives on What a Window Discards

**Question under design:** the stranger-object layer has four named faces — the
signature-predator (a *non-willful resonance* that preys, [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md)),
the blight (a *region's life turned* against the field, [`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md)),
the decoherence-wielder (a *deliberate mind* that chooses evil, [`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md)
§4), and the feral (a *kept thing that woke*, [`the-feral-instrument.md`](./the-feral-instrument.md)).
Every danger the corpus has written is a **predator or a turn** — something that
*preys* (the Coda on a signature), *turns* (the blight's corruption, the wound's
refusal), *chooses* (the wielder's evil), or *wakes* (the feral's order). **Nothing

designs **the Scavenger**: the being that lives in the fallow's worked-out veins,
the blight's cleared scars, the cold's thin — a **fitted** creature that does not
prey on the field's life (it has no interest in the living), but **fits the field's
waste** — the residual `ε²` of the spent locality. It is the stranger-object layer's
**melancholy fourth face**, and the corpus's **first *fitted* creature**: neither
predator, nor turn, nor waking — the honest answer to "what eats the spent."

## 1. The Scavenger, stated once

The stranger-object layer, as the corpus has drawn it, is the layer of the *strange*
— the beings and turns the field produces that a settlement must read and answer.
Until now it has held four faces, and **every one is a predator or a turn**:

| Face | What it is | It is a… |
|---|---|---|
| The **signature-predator** (Coda) | a **non-willful resonance structure** the local field holds in its `ε²` margins, drawn to a channeling signature (`signature-predator.md` §1) | **predator** — it preys on a clean trail |
| The **blight** | a **living-wrong region** — a region's organisms pushed past their band corrupt into a standing drain (`the-blight.md` §1) | **turn** — the field's own life turned |
| The **decoherence-wielder** | a **deliberate mind** that chooses ε² as weapon (`field-npc-ai.md` §4) | **choice** — a will that chose evil |
| The **feral** | a **kept thing that woke** — persistent-Π that outlived its keeper's intent (`the-feral-instrument.md` §1) | **waking** — a kept structure that became a will |

The Scavenger is the fifth — and the corpus never names it, because nothing in the
danger vocabulary *fits*. It is not a resonance that preys, not a region's life
turned, not a mind that chose, not a kept thing that woke:

> **The Scavenger, stated once.** It is the **denizen of the spent** — the being
> that lives in the fallow's worked-out veins, the blight's cleared scars, the
> cold's thin. It is a **fitted** creature: it does not prey on the field's life
> (it has no interest in the living), it **fits the field's waste** — the residual
> `ε²` of the spent locality, the low-`q` residue the field keeps (the kept-scar's
> edge, the fallow's worked vein, the clear's scar). It is the honest consumer of
> the field's residue — a scavenger fed on your waste does not hunt you — and a
> window's scavenger population is the honest census of its spent. It is neither
> predator, nor turn, nor waking: **it is the corpus's first fitted creature, the
> melancholy fourth face of the strange, and the honest answer to "what eats the
> spent."**

The distinction from all four cousins, drawn exactly:

- **Not a resonance that preys.** The Coda forms *around your signature* in the
  `ε²` margins and hunts the clean trail of a channeler (`signature-predator.md`
  §1/§2). The scavenger needs no signature — it is *already there*, a run the
  field holds in the spent locality, and it has nothing to hunt. The Coda's habitat
  is the thin-living margin (the desert, the storm's edge) where a player can still
  *cause* a clean trail; **the scavenger's habitat is below even that** — the
  spent locality the field has already written off. The Coda preys on the living-ish
  margin; the scavenger fits the corpse-of-work.
- **Not a region's life turned.** The blight is a *living population* corrupted — a
  wrong-band turning the field's own organisms into a drain (`the-blight.md` §1).
  The scavenger is not a population that turned; it is a population that **was
  always a consumer of the spent**. The blight's clears leave the scavenger's
  habitat (the clear's scar, `the-blight.md` §4.2 — "the region purged, a scar
  left"); the scavenger moves *in* where the blight was cleared *out*. It is not a
  turn; it is a fitting.
- **Not a mind that chose evil.** The decoherence-wielder is a deliberate, willful
  agent with no-lawful habitat. The scavenger has no will — it is an organism-class
  run the field holds (below, §2), a coherence structure that fits the residue like
  an organism fits its band. It chose nothing; it *fits*.
- **Not a kept thing that woke.** The feral is a *named, kept* structure — a house,
  palace, or vault held long past its keeper and acting on its order
  (`the-feral-instrument.md` §1). The scavenger is **not kept and never wakes**: it
  was never named or bound, it is not a bound object but a wild organism-class, and
  it does not act on an intent — it simply lives, and dissolves, in the spent
  locality. Where the feral is a kept thing that *became a will*, the scavenger is
  a wild thing that *was always a cost*.

**The through-line of the fifth face:** every fixed danger is something the field
*makes at an extreme* and the settlement must *answer* — the danger layer's house
style is "no monster table, every hazard is the field at an extreme"
(`field-hazards.md` §1). The scavenger is the first non-hazard in that layer: it is
not a danger to answer, it is a **signal to read** — and that is exactly what makes
it the corpus's first *fitted* creature. The field's waste has a consumer; the
settlement reads how much waste it has made. §3 draws the lawfulness, §4 the signal.

## 2. The fitting — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 The organism-class run — grounded in the ecology

The scavenger is first an **organism-class** — a run the field holds, in exactly the
sense `field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2 defines life:

> "An organism is not a recipe (a species table) but a **run** — a configuration the
> field is actively maintaining. When the run ends, the creature is not 'killed';
> the field stops holding the configuration and it returns to the medium."

An organism precipitates where the local field state admits its coherence-band + size
(`field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.2's biome⇔regime mapping — a creature precipitates
only where `q` is in a coherent band and `ε²` low). The **recognition rule** (§6b)
is the ecology's core judgment: a field structure is recognized as a candidate
organism when it sustains, for a window `T_org`, all three of (1) a **coherence
band** (local `q` in `[q_low, q_high]`), (2) a **decoherence ceiling** (`ε²` below a
floor), and (3) a **size/rung-depth** (spanning ≥2–3 nested cascade layers). It is
the throttle that keeps the morphology-soup from precipitating into thousands of
bodies (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §6c — the ≈ 2,000-entity cap, `corpus-reconciliation.md`).

**The scavenger's band is the *residual* one.** The ecology's recognition rule is
designed against the *living* band — the high-`q`, low-`ε²` region where a coherent
ridge or living plain precipitates thriving morphologies. The scavenger does not
precipitate there; **its coherence band is the spent locality's residual low-`q`,
high-`ε²` residue.** This is the inversion of the ecology's recognition gate, read
at the spent margin, exactly the way the signature-predator inverts the recognition
rule's meaning for the predator's habitat (`signature-predator.md` §2.3 — the
ecology's three-condition rule read *inverted*).

| The ecology's recognition condition (living) | The scavenger's read (spent) |
|---|---|
| **coherence band** — `q` in `[q_low, q_high]`, held *above* a floor | **residual band** — `q` *below* the living band (the spent locality's `q` collapsed near or under the noise floor, `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1`), but **not the dead-zone vacuum**: high enough that the residual `ε²` is a stable, persistent pocket rather than the absolute no-`q` of a fully dead window |
| **decoherence ceiling** — `ε²` below a floor (holding the φ-lock) | **residual decoherence** — `ε²` *above* the healthy ceiling, at the spent level the field keeps and does not re-bind (the kept-scar's plateau, `the-scar-lifecycle.md` §2.2), but **not the storm's raw wavefront** (the scavenger's habitat is the residual, never the active `c_s` front) |
| **size / rung-depth** — spans ≥2–3 nested cascade layers | **edge-scaled** — the scavenger is small (fits the spent locality's margins), spanning enough rungs to count as an organism but tuned to the *spent* structure's shallower, residual layers rather than the living organism's deep internal cascade |

**[design]** — which residual-`ε²` range the scavenger locks to is **this doc's
band**, probe-calibrated against the ecology's recognition rule (§6b) and the
spent localities' measured residual (`the-fallow.md`, `the-scar-lifecycle.md`). The
*direction* is sourced: an organism-class run that precipitates where the field holds
a stable config; the scavenger precipitates where the field holds a *spent* config
(the residual `ε²` it keeps, not the life it has lost). §5b.

### 2.2 Why it does not prey — the honest niche

The signature-predator preys on the **organized-perturbation trail channeling
leaves** (the clean, phase-stable `EY` injection a channeler sheds in the flat
desert — `signature-predator.md` §1.1). It needs a **living-ish margin** — a player
who can still *cause* a clean trail (§7c: "the Coda must be able to hunt where a
player can still cause a clean trail — a fully dead window has no player to hunt").

**The scavenger's habitat is below even that.** In a fully spent locality there is
*no life to prey on* — no clean trail, no living band, no channeler. There is only
the residual `ε²`: the worked vein's spent `q` (`the-fallow.md` §2a), the kept
scar's plateau (`the-scar-lifecycle.md` §2.2), the clear's scar (`the-blight.md`
§4.2). The scavenger is the being that *fits* that residue — and because there is
**nothing to prey on** in a spent locality, it does not prey:

> **[design] The niche, stated once:** the scavenger does not prey because there is
> nothing to prey *on* in a spent locality. It lives on what a window *leaves* —
> the residual `ε²` the field keeps (the low-`q`, high-`ε²` residue of the worked
> vein, the kept scar, the clear's scar, the cold's thin-multiplied spent) — a
> consumer of the field's residue that **converts nothing and fits what the field
> leaves**. It is the corpus's honest answer to "what eats the spent."

The scavenger is an organism-class on the energy law's own waste: `E_waste =
(1−q)·E_throughput` (`energy-harnessing.md` §2). In a spent locality `q` is low, so
the residue (the `(1−q)` fraction the field wastes) is large — the scavenger's
fitted food. But — the load-bearing honesty — **it is a *consumer of waste*, never
a converter into life**: it does not turn the residue into new organization (that
would be a mint, closed by §5d), it *holds* against the residue's drain, exactly as
any organism's metabolism is maintaining its φ-lock against the `ε²` drain
(`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2). A scavenger's run *is* the residual `ε²` held
as a coherence structure; when the run ends it dissolves back into the same field
it was made of — a *fitted* creature that converts nothing.

### 2.3 The three spent localities — each grounded in its source doc

The scavenger's habitat set is precisely the corpus's named spent localities — each
a **real, sourced spent state** from its own doc:

| Spent locality | The spent state (source doc) | The scavenger in it |
|---|---|---|
| **The fallow's worked-out vein** | a mined deposit's locality — its `q` withdrawn, its `ε²` raised by the mining perturbation, the vein gone forever (`the-fallow.md` §2a — "a worked deposit is gone forever") | the worked vein's spent-locality residual is the scavenger's home; the depleting stock's residue is where the scavenger precipitates |
| **The blight's cleared scar** | a blight cleared by spending the region's own `q` — a hard, scarring act whose scar is "the blight's monument" (`the-blight.md` §4.2; the clear's scar as a deep-wound kept scar, `the-scar-lifecycle.md` §4.1) | the *cleared* scar's residual `ε²` — the scavenger moves in where the corruption was purged, fitting the spent ground the clear left |
| **The cold's thin** | the long-thin as a lived season — `q` held near the noise floor over many tide-cycles, every op wasting more (`the-cold.md` §1, §2) | at the `q` floor, spent localities multiply (every worked vein, every scar, every thin-strained region holds more residual `ε²`); the scavenger population swells — the denizen of the spent's *season* |
| **The kept scar's edge** | the deep wound's residue the field keeps — a low-`q`, high-`ε²` plateau with a stable, readable boundary (`the-scar-lifecycle.md` §2.2) | the kept scar's **edge** — the transition from the spent plateau to the surrounding re-bound field — is the scavenger's *edge*: it fits the boundary where the residual meets the heal, but not the scar-kept place it avoids (`the-scar-lifecycle.md` §2.3) |

**The scavenger avoids the scar-kept place.** The deliberate branch of the
scar-lifecycle — a wound taken deliberately and held becomes a *place* the
settlement adopts as foundation (`the-scar-lifecycle.md` §2.3) — is a *maintained*
locality (the settlement's adopted foundation, built-on with `q` up). A maintained
locality's coherent band is **not the scavenger's food** (§2.2) — so the scar-kept
place is precisely the spent-adjacent ground the scavenger does *not* inhabit. Its
edge, not its kept foundation; the spent plateau, not the adopted it.

> **[design] The honest boundary of the fitting:** the scavenger inhabits the spent
> — the residual `ε²` the field keeps and does not re-bind — and **never the
> maintained**. A locality the settlement maintains (a held bath, a scar-kept place,
> a living plain) has no residual `ε²` for it to fit. The no-free-energy cap (§5d)
> is the *grammar* of this: a maintained locality has no waste to feed a consumer,
> so a scavenger has no niche there. The settlement reads its own spent, not its
> life, in the scavenger's count.

## 3. The lawfulness — the honest character

### 3.1 Lawfully harmless to the maintained

The danger layer's house style is "no monster table, every hazard is the field's
behavior at an extreme" (`field-hazards.md` §1). The scavenger obeys it — it is a
field structure, never a spawn — and then takes the discipline one step further: it
is the layer's first being that is **lawfully harmless to the maintained**.

> **[design] The lawfulness, stated once:** a scavenger fed on your waste does not
> hunt you. It has no interest in the living — a maintained locality's coherent band
> is not its food (the residual `ε²` is), so a settlement that maintains its
> locality has nothing a scavenger wants. **The scavenger is not a danger; it is the
> danger layer's first *non*-hazard row — a signal to read, never a threat to
> answer.**

Where the predator's tell is the desert-silence inversion (the *only* bright
organized thing in flat disorder, approaching — `signature-predator.md` §3), the
scavenger has **no approach**: it is *already there*, a stationary residual
structure. It does not accumulate toward you, it does not precipitate on your
trail, it does not threaten your lock. Its presence is a **property of the spent
locality**, not of you — the same deterministic property as the kept scar's edge
(`the-scar-lifecycle.md` §2.2) or the worked vein's spent ground (`the-fallow.md`
§2a). **[design]** The scavenger is the first being whose *read* is not "a danger
approaching" but "a cost already spent."

### 3.2 The honest edge — a starving scavenger thins, it never turns

The closest thing to a sharp edge in the scavenger's lawfulness is its **starvation
response** — and the honest design keeps it from crossing into the blight's turn:

| | A starving scavenger | The blight's turn (the cousin) |
|---|---|---|
| What happens | it **thins** — the population's honest read (fewer scavengers as the residual `ε²` is exhausted or heals away, `§4`) | a maintained run *corrupts* — its growth stays, its feeding turns to the region's own coherence (`the-blight.md` §2) |
| Why | a scavenger has no *band to turn* — its habitat is the spent, and when the spent heals (a worked vein re-binds, a scar's plateau closes, `the-scar-lifecycle.md`) there is nothing left to fit; it dissolves back into the field (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2) | the blight's corrupted run is one whose **band was held wrong** — the wrong-band persisted past a threshold and the run stopped dying back |
| The honest lesson | **the blight's turn is the wrong-band, never the hunger.** A scavenger's starvation is not a corruption — it is a *dissolution* (a run the field stops holding when the residue is gone) | the blight's corruption is a *maintained* wrong — the run actively fed on the region rather than shedding |

The load-bearing honesty: **a starving scavenger is still not a hunter.** It never
"decides" to prey on the living when its waste runs out — that would require a band
to turn (the blight's mechanism), and the scavenger has none. It thins, in the most
honest sense: the population's count drops as the spent heals, until the locality
either recovers (and the scavengers dissolve with the residual) or is re-spent (and
they return with it). This is the attack on the "creature that turns predator when
starved" trope — the corpus's answer is that **starvation turns the fitted creature
into dissolution, never into predation**, because its niche is the waste, not a
living margin.

### 3.3 The danger layer's non-hazard row

The scavenger earns a row in the danger layer's threat table — as a **non-hazard**:

| Hazard | Field channel | Pace | Readable before it arrives | Response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Decoherence storm | a moving `ε²` front | fast | auroras / reader | shelter, discipline |
| The desert | a regional `q` collapse | slow | reader darkens | prevent, husband |
| BH accretion | a `∇(g·Φ)` sink | slow→terminal | sky falls | feed / starve / leave |
| The Coda | a resonance structure | accumulation | desert-silence inversion | don't feed the trail |
| **The Scavenger** | **a residual `ε²` structure of the spent locality** | **stationary** | **already there (no approach)** | **nothing — read the count** |

The row's honesty is that the scavenger is **not a hazard to answer but a signal to
read** — the landscape answers with the count, exactly as a worked vein answers with
its spent ground and a kept scar answers with its edge. The table gains the corpus's
first row whose "response" is *read*, not *respond* — which is precisely the
melancholy fourth face.

## 4. The signal — the load-bearing read

### 4.1 A window's scavenger population is the honest census of its spent

The scavenger's load-bearing role in the corpus is not the creature itself — it is
**the read**. Just as the Census aggregates the Board's per-member lines into a
population reading (the settlement's pulse — `the-census.md` §1) and the Fallow
reads a window's depletions forward as one run-out (`the-fallow.md` §3), the
scavenger population is the **spent's census**: the honest aggregate of the spent
localities' fitted denizens.

> **[design] The signal, stated once:** a window's scavenger population is the
> honest census of its spent. **Many scavengers read as a window that has been used
> hard** — the fallow's worked-out veins, the blight's cleared scars, the cold's
> thin all hosting their fitted denizens, each a spent locality's residual `ε²`
> holding a run. **Few scavengers read as a window kept clean** — a maintained
> locality has no residual `ε²` (§2.3), so a clean window's spent is claimed and

### 4.2 The spent's population face

Where the Census is the *society's* population face (the people thin before the

### 4.3 Read, never a difficulty meter — the honest frame

The scavenger count must never be *gameable difficulty* — it is not a threat to be
reduced, a resource to be farmed, or a meter the settlement "passes." Three honest
frames hold:

1. **A read, not a penalty.** A high scavenger count is not a punishment — it is a
   *diagnostic* of a window that has been used hard (the fallow's face), read the
   way the Fallow's `C(W)` is a designed read, never a sentence
   (`fate-of-a-window.md` §6(c)'s forecast-≠-fate discipline; `the-fallow.md` §3.2).
   A window with many scavengers is a window that has *spent* — the count names the
   cost honestly, it does not assign blame.
2. **Conservation is the inverse.** Just as conservation = anti-corruption holds a
   region's biome alive (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.3), **healing the spent** —
   anti-corruption that restores a worked vein's `q`, holds a clear's scar to the
   field's patience (the shallow heal, `the-scar-lifecycle.md` §2.1), re-binds the
   kept scar — *dissolves* the scavenger count with the residual `ε²` it fed on.
   The count falls when the window stops being spent; the settlement reads its own
   recovery in the count's thinning (§3.2).
3. **Never a harvest.** The scavenger yields nothing (§5d) — there is no "kill the
   scavenger for its drops" because it has no body worth reaping and its presence is
   a read, not a resource. The no-free-energy cap and the §7e farming closure
   (`signature-predator.md` §7e — the same refusal held for the scavenger: you
   cannot farm a scavenger's presence, and its "harvest" is only its absence, which
   is the healed-and-not-spent.)
