scale. **None of them *says* — none takes the read and turns it into a voice.**

The corpus has instruments for every *read* and none for the *act*. The
storm-glass guild that would have said "now" never landed; the settlement hears
its field, sees it, times it, reads its own signature — but has **no voice**
when all of it agrees. The instruments read *what the field is*; nothing
*answers* — nothing says *now*, to a whole settlement, on the evidence the
instruments already read.

> **The Bell, stated once.** The settlement-scale alarm — the composed

> coincidence, and the Bell is the sound that says *the field is about to act as
> one*. Not read, but answered: the instrument family's one missing function —

The one-line distinction from every sibling instrument — the Bell is the

## 2. The combined signature — the Bell's logic

### 2.1 The coincidence is the warning

The Bell's load-bearing principle, stated once: **each channel alone is the
field's ordinary texture; the coincidence is the warning.**

| Channel | Alone, it is... | Combined with the rest, it is... |
|---|---|---|
| **The glass turns** — a rising `ε²`-front (`weather-not-storm.md` §2; `field-hazards.md` §2) | **weather** — the field's own extreme, ordered, self-limiting, happening *where the field is*. A glass-turn is a weather event; the honest response is shelter (`weather-not-storm.md` §5.1). | one face of "the field is about to act as one" |
| **The clock races** — a thinning tempo (`the-clock.md` §1/§3.1) | **a thin locality** — the vent backs up, the Coda's `τ` counts faster, a fast-tempo bed is dangerous. A racing clock is a local fact. | a second face — the field is *running fast* where the front is rising |
| **The mirror reads a signature worth warning about** (`the-mirror.md` §2.2; `signature-predator.md` §2) | **your own maintenance** — an inward read of your `R`, your burden, your phase-gap. A rising signature is a fact about the player. | a third face — something is *accumulating toward the settlement's read* |
| **The aurora is restless** — a discharge over a drain that dances (`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` §3.3) | **the sky reading a local drain** — a scar, a decoherence well, a wounded body. A restless aurora is the field's own diagnostic. | a fourth face — the sky reads, *before the front arrives*, that the field is about to act |

**The coincidence is the warning.** Four independent published channels moving
together — the front rising, the tempo racing, the signature climbing, the sky
restless — is not the field's ordinary texture; it is the field's reads
*agreeing* that its own economics are about to move as one. A glass-turn is
weather; a racing clock is a thin locality; a climbing signature is the player's
own hand; a restless aurora is the sky's diagnostic. **All at once** is the Bell's
only warrant — and it is the one event the Bell is tuned to catch.

### 2.2 The Bell fires only on the coincidence — it does not cry wolf

The Bell's honesty depends on its selectivity. Because each channel alone draws
a *designed, proportionate* response (shelter from the glass-turn, care in the
racing locality, self-maintenance on the mirror, the aurora is its own read),
**the Bell must not ring on any single channel** — or it would ring on every
storm and become noise. The Bell's logic is the **AND of the coincidence**:

> **The Bell rings only when the stack agrees** — the glass turns (a `c_s`-front
> rising) **and** the clock races (a thinning tempo at the same locality) **and**
> the mirror reads a signature worth warning about (a settlement-scale `R` rising
> toward a threshold) **and** the sky is restless (the aurora dancing over the
> drains) — in one window. Any single channel alone gets the ordinary response;
> only the agreement gets the Bell.

The **[design]** is the *which-coincidence* — which combination, at what
magnitude, over what window, reads as "the field is about to act as one" rather
than "four things happened near each other." The **probe** (§5a/§6) is what
calibrates that threshold over the real channels against the corpus's
noise-floor discipline — the same pre-registered, honest-verdict pattern
`weather-not-storm.md` §3, `life-signal.md` §6b, and `tide-of-the-attractor.md`
§5a use.

### 2.3 The noise-floor honesty — a false ring is a learnable event, never a hidden mechanic

The Bell can be wrong, and the design says so plainly: a coincidence that was
*nothing* — a front that passed, a tempo that settled, a signature that decayed
un-fed, an aurora that calmed — rings the Bell and nothing follows. That is a
**false ring**, and it is a **learnable, honest event**, not a hidden mechanic:

- **It is honest because it is never fabricated.** The Bell reads only real
  published channels; a false ring is a misread of honest evidence (four real
  reads agreed, and the agreement didn't hold), never a scripted scare.
- **It is learnable because it is deterministic.** Same channels, same field
  state → same ring (§5/§6c). A settlement that learns the Bell's *specific*
  coincidence (which storms ring it, which near-misses don't) internalizes the
  threshold through repetition — the same way a player learns the provenance
  read (`weather-not-storm.md` §4c's "the player can be wrong").
- **It is honest because it is [design], probe-calibrated.** The false-ring rate
  is a dial tuned against the real channels, pre-registered the corpus's way. A
  Bell that rings too often is a *calibration defect* the settlement (and the
  design) records and corrects — not a hidden game event.
- **It never punishes.** The corpus's `field-hazards.md` §5.1 "fails open, not
  closed" rule holds: a false ring costs only the settlement's attention (its
  responding acts are each *optional*, §4); a *missed* coincidence — a Bell that
  did not ring when the stack agreed — is the failure mode that matters, and it
  is calibrated to avoid (the threshold is set to ring on the *agreement*, broad
  enough to catch the real coincidence, narrow enough not to cry wolf on every
  weather event).

The honest framing, once: **the Bell's threshold is what the settlement tunes by
living with it; a false ring is a learnable event; a missed agreement is the only
real failure.**

## 3. The bell-pit — the built chamber tuned to the combined signature

settlement's **answer made physical** — the way the house's walls hold a

the stack agrees.

The honest line, drawn in `house-that-steers.md` §5a and kept here: the field's
channels are **engine-real**; the **geometry's steering effects** — that the
sloped wall concentrates this way, that the chamber holds this read, that the

`(base/apex)²` concentration is measured, not asserted (`house-that-steers.md`
§5b's Phase-1 hearth slice).

A **bell-pit on a coherent site** — placed where the settlement's field is

grain) reads against the field's own gradient and hears mostly noise — the

### 3.2 The ring is field-music made urgent

The bell-pit's voice is **field-music's composition** (`field-music.md` §1/§6):

The **[design]** is the composition — which phrase is urgent, how it escalates

**The honest line, once:** the bell-pit's structure is the house's vocabulary
