## 2. The three reads of the breaking

The dawn's read is **three reads over two published channels**  — each a
consumer of the engine-real `q` and `(1−q)` (and the landed Coda/waste/patience

### 2(a) The re-birth — the morning's `q` recovery as a legible event

The night is the field's quiet drain. `energy-harnessing.md` §2 fixes the waste
law: every working system bleeds `E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput` as visible
glow, and the fraction is large where the field runs low-`q` — a thin region, a
scar, a decoherence well. At night the locality's `q` sits low (the thin-tide
state, `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2; `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1` at the noise floor vs. the
attractor `q ≈ 0.947`, `corpus-reconciliation.md`), and the waste fraction
`(1−q)` is correspondingly large — **the night is the drain and its waste is
the glow it sheds.**

The first read of the breaking is the **re-birth**: the morning's `q` recovery
as a legible event.

> **The re-birth, read once.** As the locality's coherence recovers toward the
> `q ≈ 0.947` attractor, the waste fraction `(1−q)` **falls** — the night's
> large wasteful glow thins toward the day's near-lossless run. The Dawn reads
> that fall as the *breaking*: **the morning's `(1−q)` waste read at its fall**
> (`energy-harnessing.md` §2 — `E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput`, the same waste
> the Weatherglass's waste tint shows by day, `field-instruments.md` §1.2),
> rendered not as a lower waste *level* but as a *turning* — the night's quiet
> drain ending, the waste wasting less.

**The honest ground.** The `q` recovery itself (the field healing toward its
attractor on the tide's and the environment's timeline) is the source docs'
landed, engine-real physics — the same recovery sleep's standing-still-in-
coherent-ground rides (`sleep.md` §1, `coherence-magic.md` §1.2) and the farm's
bed `q` returning into the crop's band (`farm-that-feeds.md` §2). **[design]**
What the re-birth adds is the *event*: the transition's trend (the low-`q`/
high-`(1−q)` trough giving way to the rising `q`/falling `(1−q)`) read as one
legible breaking, over the window §3. The source docs give the recovery and the
waste; this doc gives the recovery's *presentation as a becoming*.
