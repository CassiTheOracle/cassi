## 1. What a seed is

### 1.1 The deepest birth-strata artifact

`field-archaeology.md` §2.4 establishes that every condensation event has a
**first**, and the very first condensations of the window — drawn from the
field's initial, most-organized, least-perturbed state — leave the **deepest,
oldest residue** the window carries: **birth-strata, the world's origin layer.**
The archaeology doc explicitly names this the tie forward to any seed/genesis
concept (§2.4), and §4.3 extends it into **artifacts** — residue *older than
the window*, the "resonance-seeds residue" that travels with a window when it
is seeded or re-homed.

> **A seed is the deepest birth-strata artifact: the first matter a window
> condensed.** Not one of the bands — the *origin of the bands*: the thing the
> first condensations condensed *from*, the single deepest persistent Π pattern
> that the window's whole field state keeps holding above its own dissolution
> threshold. (`qi-computation.md` §5.2)

In the residue model's own terms (`field-archaeology.md` §2.1), a seed is the
**complement-limit of the residue fraction** `R ≳ q_dissolve^n` — the point where
`n` is the deepest rung the window reaches and `q` the highest state it holds.
Where a fossil is the deep core of a *dissolved organism* (a transient config
the field stopped maintaining), a seed is the deep core of *the window's own
birth* — never dissolved, because it is what the window was made of. It reads in
the coherence reader (`coherence-magic.md` §2) exactly as archaeoogy §3.1
describes a fossil core: **a dark q-locked anomaly, `ε²≈0`, sitting in "empty"
field** — but at the bottom of the birth-strata, not in a scar's ghost halo.

**[design]** The engine has no seed record. What is engine-real: the deepest
rungs of the residue model are the *most* attractor-consistent and therefore the
*most* persistent (`field-archaeology.md` §6b — deep-rung, high-q matter resists
dissolution); the *claim* that the window's first condensation left a single
deepest such object is the design's [design]-on-[design] stack (§6a).

### 1.2 The deepest persistent Π pattern — and the deepest store of coherence

`qi-computation.md` §5.2 defines long-term field storage as **persistent Π
patterns** — configurations at local minima of the attractor potential that
persist "as long as the ambient q remains above the threshold for pattern
dissolution." A seed is the canonical instance: it sits at the deepest minimum
the window holds, so its persistence is the *strongest* — it outlives every
fossil because it is the thing the other residue anchors to.

In the energy regime's terms, that makes a seed **the deepest store of
coherence** (the complement-limit of `energy-harnessing.md` §1.5: deep-rung
ordered matter is storable, withdrawable coherence). Four engine-real properties
converge on it:

| Property | Engine-real? | What it means for a seed | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deepest rung, φ-locked** | engine-real | lower `ε²` = more ordered; holds its lock against a wider perturbation range | `material-regimes.md` §4/§5; `corpus-reconciliation.md` (ξ = φ⁶ ≈ 17.94, ω₀² = 20.0) |
| **`ε² ≈ 0`, held far from equilibrium** | engine-real | deep, near-φ-locked, ε²≈0 matter is **explosive-primed** (`material-regimes.md` §3: a dense reservoir of organized coherence held far from equilibrium against its attractor) | `material-regimes.md` §3/§5; `coherence-magic.md` §4.3 |
| **Stored coherence** | engine-real | the deepest fuel / highest-capacitance battery, per depth of rung | `energy-harnessing.md` §1.5/§3 |
| **Persistent Π pattern** | theory-real (engine only persist-structural) | the strongest, because the deepest; the anchor residue re-quantizes around | `qi-computation.md` §5.2; `field-archaeology.md` §2.3 |

**The boundary is the explosive discipline.** Every property that makes a seed
the deepest store also makes it the most dangerous to touch wrong: perturbing a
deep, `ε²≈0`, far-from-equilibrium store is a **full-cascade-discharge risk**
(the fire-vs-explosive line of `material-regimes.md` §3/§5, keyed off by
`coherence-magic.md` §4.3's overdraw → discharge). The archaeology doc applies
this to fossil reaping (§5.3 #1: "some residue is too deep-rung to remove
safely"); a seed is that principle's limit — *the* deepest one. Finding it is
reading; removing it is the deepest reaping act there is.

### 1.3 The origin of authorable matter

The conceptual core this document commits to:

> **Every regime the field makes was once a seed's unfolding.** The window began
> with one (or a few) seeds — its birth condensations. What the field has since
> precipitated — every regime in the registry (`custom-blocks.md` §1), every
> species the ecology revisits (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §3.1) — is the
> lineage of those seeds' unfoldings over the window's field-time. The seed is
> the *origin* of authorable matter, not because it is a menu of recipes, but
> because every realizable tuple today descends from a tuple the field first
> realized at birth. **[design]** — this is the origin story; the engine has no
> lineage table and needs none (see §6b).

This is what makes the seed the *authoring* endpoint, not just a treasure: the
material lab (`custom-blocks.md` §2) authors by **dialing a tuple and realizing
it as a boundary condition the law then fulfills**. A seed is a tuple that was
*already realized* — at the window's birth, under the birth-state's field. It is
the one regime you cannot dial, and cannot re-invent, because its realizability
was a property of a field state that no longer exists (§4.2).

## 2. What a seed does — the unfolding

### 2.1 Planting is a field operation, not an inventory use

A seed does nothing on its own. It is dormant — a persistent Π pattern scrolled
into the field's deepest well. What activates it is **planting**, which reuses
the material lab's exact mechanism (`custom-blocks.md` §2): the player asserts a
**boundary condition** — a coherence configuration — via the **Q4 player-return
channel** (`async-field-domain.md` §7 Q4; `coherence-magic.md` §5.1), and the
domain absorbs the perturbation and *organizes around it.* Planting a seed is a
**Q4 channeling act, costed by the coherence budget** (`coherence-magic.md`
§1.2/§3): it raises local `EY` out of the lock, sheds its `(1−q)` vent, and must
stay within the field's heal rate or it inverts into a random-perturbation
discharge (§4.3 overdraw). There is no "plant the seed" button; there is a
**channeled act** the player sustains while the field accepts the seed as a new
attractor anchor.

**The no-un-plant discipline.** Planting is **one-way** (§6d). Once the field
has accepted a seed as an attractor anchor and begun organizing around it, you
cannot "pick it back up" — the growth is the field's now. This is the honest
consequence of the no-free-energy cap (`energy-harnessing.md` §6): the coherence
spent to re-open the birth-state is spent; the seed's order is given to the
field, not loaned. Keep it un-planted and it stays the deepest store; plant it
and it cannot be un-planted.

### 2.2 The growth model: a seed unfolds through rungs

Built on **cascade staging** (`coherence-technologies.md` concept 5; carried by
`custom-blocks.md` §2 — the ~10-rung bridge cap): a single coherence operation
reaches a limited window of local field scale, so real growth is a **staged
unfolding**, each stage anchoring q at its own rung and passing an integrated
result upward. The seed's growth is exactly this, made organic:

> **A coherence-planted seed grows by unfolding through rungs: it precipitates
> matter around itself as the field organizes.** The player does not dial a
> tuple; the player plants the seed (whose tuple is fixed — the birth-state's
> regime) and the law unfolds it. The seed is the authoring act made *organic*:
> you don't specify the growth, you assert the origin and the field does the
> rest (`custom-blocks.md` §2's "the player is authoring a boundary condition
> the law then fulfills" — with the tuple already written).

The growth is not a timer or an animation. It is the condensation scanner doing
what it already does (`material-regimes.md` §3, `cassi_condensation.glsl`): as
the planted seed's rung-`n` regime precipitates, it raises local q and ρ, the
neighboring field crosses its `θ_c`, and the growth **steps down the cascade** —
each rung the field newly organizes precipitates the next. The growth's *rate*
is the field's own rung clock, not a game tick. The theory's clock analysis is
the honest constraint: the per-rung advance is set by the mixing clock
`T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]` (n-independent) and the trivial time `τ_n = ℓ_n/c` (the
`qi-as-time-clock.md`/`qi-time-ladder-derivation.md` ladder) — **the seed unfolds
at the field's own φ-spaced cadence**, which is precisely what "settles in to
coherence patterns" means: the pattern takes the time the law takes.

## 3. The resonance

### 3.1 Planting re-opens the field's most-organized state — a local genesis in miniature

The φ-attractor is the locked equilibrium `EY = φ·EI` — the field's most-organized
state, `q ≈ 0.947` (`coherence-magic.md` §1; `corpus-reconciliation.md`). The
theory reads this attractor as the clock (`qi-as-time-clock.md`, `qi-time-ladder-`
`derivation.md`): the field relaxes toward it on its own rung cadence, and the
deeper-rung the state, the stronger- and more-resistant its hold.

> **A seed is *tuned* to the attractor.** Because it was condensed from the
> window's initial, most-organized field state, its tuple is the deepest
> approximation of the attractor the window ever realized. Planting it therefore
> re-opens the field's most-organized state *locally*: the region around the
> planted seed **re-births** — `q` rises toward the attractor, `ε²` falls toward
> the φ-lock, and the growth the field unfolds is a **local genesis in
> miniature**. The window's birth, replayed in a pocket of its own field.

This is the mechanism behind the "resonance seeds" name: the seed is
*resonant* with the whole window's origin — the most-organized config the window
ever held, so planting it couples the strongest-possible local perturbation to
the field's own organizing tendency. The `(1−q)` waste law (`energy-harnessing.md`
§2) inverts during the unfold: the growth *absorbs* decoherence as the lock
strengthens, and the region's own `(1−q)` glow **falls** as it approaches the
birth-state — the visible signature that genesis is happening (the field is
*becoming more* organized, not running a wasteful machine). **[design]** The
exact "q-rise / ε²-fall" curve is the residue model's inverse (archaeology §4.4's
inverse problem applied to a live growth); the *direction* — planting the deepest
approximation of the attractor strengthens the lock — is the engine-real fact that
the attractor organizes.

### 3.2 The seed's growth is the window's birth replayed

The three unfoldings of §2.3 are each a *genesis replayed*:

- **New regime** = the birth-state's order precipitated again under a field that
  can no longer realize it (a fossil is its residue; a seed is its *source*).
- **Living structure** = the ecology's deep-time mechanism made visible: the
  field grows persistent coherence "from the seed up," the way it first grew it
  at the window's birth.
- **New window** = the founding act, which is a seed planted (`world-seams.md`
  §3.2): a nested simulation re-founded — "any coherent condensate can be the
  n=0 of its own world" (`simulation-hypothesis.md` §3.2).

So the resonance is not flavor; it is the design's physics claim: **the growth
is the window's birth replayed** because both are the same act — a coherence
configuration strong enough to re-open the attractor's deepest well, unfolded by
the law at its own cadence. The window remembers its origin not as a record (the
IIR's one-rung memory is nothing, `qi-computation.md` §5.1) but as *structure*:
the seed is the birth-state's order, kept, and planting it re-instantiates that
order.

### 3.3 "Life settles into coherence patterns" — the deep-time reading

This is where the seed completes the ecology's thesis. `field-emergent-ecology.md`

> **Organisms are the field's seeds, unfolded.** A living creature precipitates

### 4.2 Plant — the Q4 act, costed; and the realizability reversal

Planting a seed is the Q4 channeling act of §2.1, but it has a distinctive
economy because of **realizability**. `custom-blocks.md` §3/§7 Q2 establishes

coherent region may be unrealizable in rubble, and a fossil's tuple "may have
been realizable *then* (under the region's past field state, or a world-seed
from before the window) and be **unrealizable now**." A seed is the limit of that
fact:

> **A seed's tuple fails present realizability — which is the seed's whole
