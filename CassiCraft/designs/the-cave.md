**Question under design:** the corpus designs the deep as everywhere *full*. The
below is the strata-pile the diving bell sinks through ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md)

base everything precipitates onto ([`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md) §1/§2), the

stays low, and the dense organization is simply *not there*. It is formed
honestly (a cave is a wound that healed *hollow*), and it reads as itself (a
cave is a stratum that holds *nothing*, and that nothing is its honest content).
The Cave is the deep's openness *made legible*.

## 1. The Cave, stated once

The corpus designs the deep as a **pile**. [`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md)
designs the below as the strata the Bell sinks through — dense, crushing,
bright-with-disorder outside the shell (§1/§2.2). [`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md)
designs its absolute floor as the maximal-rung, q-maximal, effectively-immovable
substrate where "the field's organization is so dense and so settled that no op
can move it" (§1). [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) designs the
deep's content as residue — deep-rung coherent wells, fossil cores, strata
bands, all *dense* with kept order (§2). **Every designed face of the below is a
face of its fullness** — the strata-pile, the dense settled base, the
rung-terraces the Bell descends through. The corpus's below has no *caves*.

The honest gap: the field is organized *wherever it can be*, but the discipline
that makes the deep dense is the *same* discipline that lets a region's
organization **depart** — a wound leaves a low-`q` defect
([`the-scar-lifecycle.md`](./the-scar-lifecycle.md) §2.2), a worked vein is gone
forever ([`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §2a), a BH sweeps the matter out of
its region ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §4.2), a flood's aftermath
leaves a locality the field can no longer re-organize ([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md)
§2/§4.1). The deep is not only full; the deep is the set of *places where
organization is not* too. Nothing designs that set. The Cave is that hollow
filled, stated once and kept:

> **The Cave is the vertical's first volume — a designed hollow the strata open
> into.** It is a **void in the dense pile, a coherence *absence* the field
> holds**: the one place in the below where `q` falls, `ε²` stays low, and the
> dense organization is simply *not there*. It is the corpus's first *volume*
> face — a hollow with its own character — the sea-floor's boundary-with-character
> register ([`the-sea-floor.md`](./the-sea-floor.md) §1) applied to *openness*
> instead of to a seam. The deep is everywhere designed as full; the Cave is the
> deep's openness, made legible.

**Why this is the vertical's missing face, honestly.** Every region the corpus
designs *below* is a region of organization. The below is the strata-pile
([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §1); the terraces are its dense
rung-ladder (§3.1); the bedrock is the immovable base the pile rests on
([`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md) §1). The one downward quantity the corpus
has not designed is the **absence of that organization** — a region where the
field's own logic, through its own landed mechanics, has *hollowed the pile*. A
cave is that absence with a boundary: a volume of the deep that holds nothing,
kept coherent (in the sense of *held, stable*) by the surrounding strata's read
of it. It is the below's answer to the zenith's paired emptiness at the top — but
where the zenith is the *between* the sky sheds into (a thin the window is *under*
[`the-zenith.md`](./the-zenith.md)), a cave is a hollow *inside* the deep the
window's field holds.

**Not a hidden third regime — the cave's fundamental honesty.** The sea-floor is
"not a hidden third regime" ([`the-sea-floor.md`](./the-sea-floor.md) §1); the
bedrock is "not a hidden bottom regime" ([`the-bedrock.md`](./the-bedrock.md)
§1). The Cave inherits the same neutrality: a hollow is **not a new field state**,
it is the deep's own `q`/`ε²`/`ρ` **read at a deficit**. The dense pile publishes
its channels; the hollow reads the same channels *falling* — `q` down, `ε²` low,
`ρ` dropping — never an unknown third state the field secretly falls into. A cave
is a **reading of the deep's own channels where they read empty**, exactly as the
sea-floor is a reading of the plain's and the deep's channels where they meet.

**The cave's character — stated in one line and kept:** the Cave is **the
quiet (the deep's one still place — the `ε²` low is the below's stillness), the
cold (the dense pile's absence of organization), and the shelter (a hollow the
dense world cannot press — the honest underground).** Three faces of one hollow;
each grounded in a source doc's real quantity; each this doc's [design] over
that landed piece (§4).

## 2. The honest formation — a cave is a wound that healed *hollow*

The cave is **not a spawned void.** The corpus's hard rule is that the world is
deterministic and the field organizes where it can
([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §1; the no-spawn discipline of
[`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §1). A hollow cannot be *placed*; it must
be **formed** — and the corpus's own landed mechanics form it. The through-line,
stated once:

> **A cave is a wound that healed *hollow*.** The scar-lifecycle's closing —
> the deep wound that does not re-bind — does not always leave a sealed scar; it
> can leave a *void*, the locality healed *around* an empty where the organization
> was spent. Each formation below is a designed consequence of a landed mechanic:
> the cave is not put into the world; the world's own dense-pile logic, through
> its own depletions and sweeps, empties a volume and holds the hollow where the
> fullness was.

Each of the four formations is honest and grounded. **The cave's composition —
that these are *caves*, that their hollows are the deep's openness, that they
cohere into one volume-face — is this doc's [design]** over the landed pieces
(§5a). The formations are the source docs' real events.

### (a) The kept-scar's edge taken inward

**The landed piece:** [`the-scar-lifecycle.md`](./the-scar-lifecycle.md) §2.2
designs the kept scar as a deep wound whose `ε²` the local field cannot re-bind —
"a fixed defect, a low-`q` memory the field keeps," with "a readable, stable
boundary." §2.3 names the third fate: a wound taken deliberately and held becomes
*a place* — the scar-kept, a locality the settlement builds on, never mines from.

**The [design] over it — the kept scar, turned inward, heals *hollow*:**

> **The cave is the kept scar's edge taken *inward*.** Where the scar-lifecycle's
> deep branch leaves a *plateau* — a fixed low-`q` defect with a readable edge
> (§2.2) — a locality whose spent organization was hollowed rather than merely
> flattened closes as a **void** instead of a defect: the same deep-wound
> persistence, but the `ε²` spent carved a volume the field re-quantizes *around*,
> leaving an empty the edge binds. The stratum-read reads it the way it reads any
> stratum — a band of the pile that holds *nothing* ([`the-stratum-read.md`](./the-stratum-read.md)
> §2.2). **The kept scar's edge is the cave's wall; the cave is the wound whose
> fixing closed a hollow.**

**[design]** — *which* deep wounds hollow rather than plateau, and whether the
healing re-quantization re-seals a void or holds it, is probe-calibrated against
the same healing-threshold dial as the scar-lifecycle (§3.1 there) and the
strata-vs-heal probe of [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §6c. What
is [design]-landed: the kept scar's stable readable edge (§2.2), the field
re-quantizing *around* residue (§2.1 there), and the scar-kept place's
build-on-never-mine adoption boundary (§2.3). The cave inherits the wall the scar
already lands; the hollow *behind* it is this doc's composition.

### (b) The emptied deep-rung store

**The landed pieces:** [`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §2a designs the worked
vein as "a worked deposit is gone forever" — the mining perturbation spends the
locality's `q`, raises its `ε²`, and the precipitation law *does not re-precipitate
that vein*. §2c designs the pulled deep-rung: the Bell's collection is a
withdrawal that "de-orders the region it came from… the region heals around the
empty store" ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §6.3).

**The [design] over them — the depletion's *space*:**

> **The cave is the emptied deep-rung store's space.** When a deep deposit —
> a worked vein, a pulled deep-rung store, a mined-to-depth reserve — is withdrawn,
> the locality **heals *around* the empty** ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md)
> §6.3: "the scar closes, the field re-organizes — but the deep-rung matter
> itself is not re-precipitated"). The emptied volume is not a re-filled scar; it
> is a **hollow the dense world re-quantizes around** — the fallow's one-way
> depletion, read as a volume. A cave is the honest shape of a worked-out vein
> *once the vein is gone*: not a re-sealed floor but the depletion's space, the
> emptiness the field now holds.

**[design]** — the cave as the *retained* form of the emptied store (rather than
a healed-flat scar) is this doc's reading of the [design]-landed depletions
(`the-fallow.md` §2a/§2c's gone-forever mechanics) over the 'heals around it'
persistence of deep-field-diving §6.3 — **probe-calibrated** against the fallow's
worked-deposit verdict (its open-Q1: does a worked vein stay gone forever?) and
the residue-persistence calibration (`field-archaeology.md` §6b, which decides
whether an emptied store holds its hollow or slowly re-binds toward the pile).

### (c) The BH's swept wake

**The landed pieces:** [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §4 designs the BH
accretion as a `∇(g·Φ)` sink that "never stops": `mass += acc_rate · qi_local ·
cell_vol`, eating local `Qi`, lowering `q` in the shell around it, and pulling
matter inward along the river law until the "accretion front is a moving
dissolution boundary" (§4.2). §4.4 names the deep end: "what a very deep descent
reaches is the field's terminal" — and `deep-field-diving.md` §7a/§4 names the BH
as the descent's *hard floor*.

**The [design] over it — the swept-out region behind the hole:**

> **The cave is the BH's swept wake.** A BH that accretes through a region does
> not leave the region's full pile behind; it **sweeps the organized matter out
> along `∇(g·Φ)`** (§4.2 — the dissolution boundary, the shredded-then-infall),
> and the shell around the sink reads **emptied** — the `q` draw, the pulled
> matter, the local desert the accretion makes (§3.2's BH-draw cause). Where the
> sweep is complete and the BH has moved on (or been starved, §4.3), the region it
> passed through is the deep's **swept wake** — a volume the dense organization
> once filled and now does not. **A cave is the honest hollow left behind a hole
> that ate and left.**

**[design]** — the *retention* of the swept wake as a hollow (rather than the
field re-filling it, which the attractor's `ω₀²` re-bind tends to do — `field-hazards.md`
§3.3) is this doc's composition, probe-calibrated against whether a drained region
ever re-binds (§4 there, open-Q4 — the scar-lifecycle verdict). What is
[design]-landed: the BH's `q`-draw (lowering regional `q`, §4.2), the matter
pulled inward (the river law), and the accretion's local desert (§3.2). The cave
is the sweep's *after* — whether it holds depends on the same re-bind measurement
the scar-lifecycle rides.

### (d) The flood's aftermath's undercut

**The landed pieces:** [`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §2 designs the over-bloom
— ore *over-precipitates then re-dissolves*, the shallow ecology over-crowds, the
machines run past useful toward the `(1−q)` waste. §3 resolves it honestly: the
surfeit *wastes upward* — "a self-limiting bloom, never a power source." §4.1
names the aftermath: the over-blown region over-precipitates then **re-dissolves
into a churn**, the over-bloom's crowding, the wrong-bright `(1−q)` glow shed —
leaving "a locality whose deep coherence has been *spent* by the overshoot
itself" (the-fallow reading, §2d).

**The [design] over it — the water's hollow:**

> **The cave is the flood's aftermath's undercut.** The flood sheds upward — the
> surfeit re-dissolves into the band the field can hold (the-flood §3) — and the
> over-blown region's deep coherence is *spent by the overshoot*
> (the-fallow §2d). **The undercut is the hollow a surfeit carves when it leaves**:
> the surfeit over-precipitated and shed, the crowd over-bloomed and went, and the
> volume whose organization the overshoot consumed reads **undercut** — the empty
> where a churn of over-abundance was, at the deep's edge the water's excess
> scoured. A cave is the water's hollow: the aftermath of too-much, held as an
> emptied volume of the deep.

**[design]** — the undercut reading (a surfeit's shed leaving a *hollow* rather
than a re-bound plain) is this doc's composition over the flood's [design]-landed
over-precipitate-and-re-dissolve (§2) and the fallow's flood-aftermath depletion
(§2d) — **probe-calibrated** against the tide probe's verdict (`tide-of-the-attractor.md`
§5a, whether the regional `q` accumulates a real harvest that can overshoot) and
the flood's over-bloom shape (its open-Q4).

**The through-line — the hollow is the field's own, formed.** Each formation is a
landed mechanic read *as a void*: the deep wound that healed hollow (a), the
one-way store that emptied and stayed empty (b), the sweep that left (c), the
shed that undercut (d). The cave does not add a mechanic to the field; it **names
the emptiness the deep's own dynamics produce** — the same honesty that makes a
hazard "the field's behavior at an extreme, not a spawned enemy"
([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §1).

## 3. The hollow's honesty — a cave is a stratum that holds *nothing*, and that nothing is its honest content

The cave's honesty is that it **reads as itself** — never as a hidden region, never
as a mystery the field's channels cannot carry. The through-line, stated once:

> **A cave is a stratum that holds *nothing*, and that nothing is its honest
> content.** The corpus reads the deep by *strata* — the archaeology's core sample
> reads a vertical `ρ`/`q`/`ε²` profile down through the bands
> ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §3.2); the stratum-read re-reads
> that residue in place as a layered read ([`the-stratum-read.md`](./the-stratum-read.md)
> §2). **A cave is the same read pointed at a band that comes up empty** — a
> stratum whose content is the `q` falling, the `ε²` low, the `ρ` dropped. The
> emptiness *is* the layer's content; reading it is reading the cave.

Three reads, each grounded in a source doc's channel, each reading the hollow's
edge the way the corpus's own instruments read the deep:

### 3.1 The Bell's descent reads the terraces thinning to a boundary

The Bell's descent reads the below as the rung-terraces the ladder climbs
([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §3.1 — the under-structure where
`ρ` rises toward the deep-rung matter, the rising order the descent is survey of).
At a cave, that read **changes**: the terraces do not keep rising; they thin to a
**boundary** — the dense pile's organized bands give way to a `ρ`-drop and a
`q`-fall at the hollow's edge. The Bell's descent survey (the archaeology core
sample as its pre-descent map, §5 there) reads the cave the way it reads any
strata-boundary — the edge is the deep's own `q`/`ε²`/`ρ`, read thinning. **The
cave's wall is where the rung-terraces end.**

### 3.2 The strata-read reads the hollow's edge

The stratum-read is "a bounded, phase-aligned re-read of a locality's current
`q`/`ε²`/residue channels organized by the depth-strata the archaeology already
names" ([`the-stratum-read.md`](./the-stratum-read.md) §1) — the deep-rung layer's
pulled matter, the kept scar's wound-depth, the re-bound's healed band (§2.2, each
a real landed channel). **A cave is the stratum-read's emptied band**: standing at
the hollow's edge, the layered read's deep-rung band comes up *empty* — the `q`
that fell, the `ε²` that stays low, the `ρ` that dropped — the round of pulled /
wound / healed **read as a hollow stratum**. The stratum-read does not invent a
cave channel; it reads the deep's own kept state organized by depth, and at the
hollow that state is *nothing*, read faithfully.

### 3.3 The archaeology's core sample reads the void the way it reads any stratum

The archaeology's core sample is "reading the `ρ`/`q` profile down through the
strata — a vertical gradient read that resolves the log-periodic stratigraphy"
([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §3.2). **The core sample reads a
cave's void the way it reads any stratum**: the profile drops, `q` falls below the
surrounding bands, `ε²` stays low, and the layer reads as a band that holds
*nothing* — a coherent well of *absence*, the archaeology's residue-model reading
(§2) pointed at its negative. The archaeology never flags a cave as "a hidden
thing"; it reads the void as a stratum whose content is that it is empty — the
same determinism-is-not-recoverability honesty (§1.2): the cave is present residue
of an absence, read as present field state, never a replay of the wound that made
it.

**The honest boundary, stated once:** the cave's reads are **the deep's own
channels** — `q` falling, `ε²` low, `ρ` dropping — the same channels every deep
instrument shows (the reader/Sense of [`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md)
§2, the strata profile of [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §3.2,
the layered read of [`the-stratum-read.md`](./the-stratum-read.md), the descent
survey of [`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §5). **A cave is never a
hidden region** — it is a *legible deficit* in the deep's own published state,
readable by every instrument that reads the deep. Adding "the cave" to the world is
not adding a secret; it is naming the emptiness the channels already show.

## 4. The hollow's character

The corpus's first *interface* doc established the register: a boundary **with its
own character** — not a regime, not a place, an honest seam between two designed
