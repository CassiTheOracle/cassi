**Question under design:** what a stranger *is* in a window, as a designed object.
[`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) designed the voyage and another-window's
arrival; nothing designs the **traveller as a designed object** — a stranger whose
carried field-history arrives and changes your window. The design, stated once:

> **A traveller is a walking provenance.** They carry their home window's
> field-history with them: the residue of their own past (their carried ε²-debt
> and persistent-Π identity, [`the-burden.md`](./the-burden.md)), their
> origin-window's order (pre-window artifacts older than your window,
> [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §4.3), and their own signature
> (the maintenance-axis read of a body raised by another field,
> [`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md)). When a stranger crosses the seam, their
> **foreign field-history arrives and perturbs yours** — and the Guest is the way
> to make *another player / another window* a felt, field-true encounter:

> **You meet not just a person but the field that raised them.**

This document is the **traveller's face** the seam leaves undrawn. It is a
**[design]** over the corpus's own carried channels (the ε² the vent sheds, the
persistent-Π identity, the maintenance-axis signature read) and the corpus's own
arrival machinery (the re-home policy, the Board's book, the Coda's accumulation).
It invents no new channel and no new physics; it *reads* what a stranger already
carries, in your window's field, and gives that carried history a legible,
booked, felt presence.

## 1. A traveller is a walking provenance, stated once

[`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §1.2 states the load-bearing physics: a
window is a **local anchored field**, and the global is the **measured mixture of
the local fields** — "the window's content is a *history*, not an endpoint." The
corpus already holds that a *person* is, in the field, a carried thing: a heavy
channeler drags a **damaged-locality-you-drag-with-you** — an accumulated ε² field,
the running scar of their own practice, the living form whose terminal is the
corpse's fossil ([`the-burden.md`](./the-burden.md) §1). An identity is a
**persistent Π pattern** the field holds ([`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md)
§2.1; [`reason-field.md`](./reason-field.md) §3.1). The schema binds that identity
to a **member line** that survives death — same member, new lock
([`schema-that-settles.md`](./schema-that-settles.md) §2.1.1).

The Guest design composes those two already-true statements into the third the
corpus never drew: **what a stranger carries when they arrive.**

> **A traveller is a walking provenance.** They carry their home window's
> field-history with them. The residue of their own past — the ε² they shed to
> act, accumulated as a carried debt, and the persistent-Π identity the field of
> their *home* window holds for them. The origin-window's order — the
> pre-window artifacts of the field that raised them, residue older than the
> window that visits you. Their own signature — a maintenance-axis read of a body
> actively held by a field *other than yours*.

**The honest framing, stated once.** The field is **local** — the law runs in
windows, not across the seam ([world-seams §1.2](./world-seams.md)). A guest's
*history* is **real**, but it is not carried as a separate "guest record" the
engine writes. It arrives as the **same channels the corpus already publishes**,
shaped by a different origin and read in your window's field:

> **The perturbation is what the corpus's own channels already carry, arriving
> with a stranger.** A guest's deep-rung Burden is their real carried ε² — the
> same ε² the reader and the Glass already render, arriving darker-deeper than a
> resident's, because it was shed into a field that kept more of it. Their
> artifact is the archaeology §4.3 residue — the same q-locked core + ghost halo
> the core sample reads, but older than your window by provenance. Their
> signature is the life-signal's maintenance read — the same pulsing-live /
> dark-residue / climbing-vent classes, but a cadence your field did not set.

So the Guest is not a new substance; it is **a provenance on the channels the
field already carries**. The design's contribution is the *reading* that turns a
stranger's ordinary carried ε², their ordinary pre-window residue, and their
ordinary maintenance cadence into a **legible, booked, felt encounter with the
field that raised them**. **[design]** — the *reading as provenance* is this
doc's designed surface over the engine-real/theory-ratified channels; the carried
channels themselves are real, and never presented as a "guest record."

## 2. What arrives

Three carried things, each grounded in the corpus's own channels. Each is a
*foreign* instance of a channel your window already reads.

### 2.1 The Burden — a guest's deep-rung debt reads as *foreign* weight

[`the-burden.md`](./the-burden.md) §1 establishes the carried debt: a heavy
channeler carries an accumulated ε² field — the damaged locality they drag with
them, the running scar of their practice, legible on the deliberate-vent axis
(§2c) and drawing the Coda (§2d, `R = ρ_signature·τ·M_stability`). The Burden's
persistent-Π identity (§1.2) is what the member-id derives from.

**The Guest's first carried thing is that Burden, read in *your* field:** a
guest's deep-rung carried ε² is **foreign weight** — it was shed into a *different*
field, at a *different* tide, under *different* ambient `q`. In your window it
reads as:

| What carries | Your window reads | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| The guest's carried ε² (their shed, accumulated debt) | a **deep-rung elevation** on the ε² channel — closer to the deliberate-vent line than a resident's, because their carried debt is *old* (long-sustained by their home practice) and *deep* (shed under the home field) | the-burden §1/§2a/§2c |
| Their persistent-Π identity | a **lived member line** that is *not of your window* — the field of your window holds them as a maintained lock (they move, they pulse), but the Π pattern they hold is home-window-raised | the-burden §1.2; schema §2.1.1; life-signal §3.1 |
| Their vent cadence | a maintenance rhythm tuned by **their** home window's tempo — the guest's cadence is not your window's | the-burden §2c; patient-field §4.3; life-signal §3.1 |

**The honest frame.** The guest's Burden is **not a drain on your bath** — it is
*their* carried weight, arriving in your field (the-burden's own ethics guard
applies: "the world can be wrong about you"; shared-ledger's misattribution
guard must not read a guest's carried ε² as a resident's drain, §3 of this doc).
It reads as a **standing signature the window did not set** — legible, weighted,
but not blameworthy. **[design]** The "foreign weight" framing — *how* the deep
old ε² reads against your window's baseline — is a designed read over the real
carried ε²; the *direction* (deep, old, deliberate-vent-proximal) is the honest
consequence of a debt shed under a different field.

### 2.2 The order — pre-window artifacts, something your window cannot re-precipitate but a guest *is*

[`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §4.3 establishes **artifacts** —
deep-rung residue *older than the player's window*, the pre-window order that
travels with a window when it is seeded or re-homed. Since a window is a local
field ([world-seams §1.2](./world-seams.md)) and the local-field treatment makes
the window's content a history, the archaeology doc flags the artifact tier as
the pre-window residue "the resonance-seeds residue" forward-ties to
([`resonance-seeds.md`](./resonance-seeds.md) §1.1/§4.3).

**The Guest's second carried thing is that pre-window order, arrived on a person.**
A traveller is a walking provenance in the archaeology doc's own sense: they carry
their **origin-window's order** — the deep-rung residue of the field that raised
them, older than your window — and arriving, they are *that foreign order made
present*:

| Carried | What it is | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| The guest's **origin-window residue** | pre-window artifact residue on/with them — the deep `q`-locked, `ε²≈0` structure of a field your window never condensed | archaeology §4.3; §2.1 (residue = deep-rung core) |
| A realistic tuple **your window cannot re-precipitate** | an artifact/seed-regime whose realizability was a property of the guest's home field-state, not yours — *something your window cannot re-precipitate, but a guest is* | resonance-seeds §4.2 (realizability reversal); custom-blocks §3 |
| The resonance-seed principle, carried | the guest's presence *is* a resonance-seed arriving — foreign order that re-opens a local state your field could not | resonance-seeds §3.1; §2.3 |

> **The guest IS the resonance-seed they carry.** A seed is a foreign order your
> window cannot re-precipitate, made findable/plantable ([resonance-seeds
> §4.2](./resonance-seeds.md)). A guest is that same foreign order, *arriving on a
> person*: not planted, but present — a walking artifact, a resonance-seed that
> has crossed the seam to meet you. Their origin-window's order perturbs your
> field the way the theory's local-field mixture predicts two organized cells
> meeting at their empty remainder ([world-seams §4.3](./world-seams.md)) — a
> foreign coherence your window reads but cannot re-precipitate.

**[design]** This is the most speculative *carried* claim (it inherits
archaeology §4.3's own gating on the window-relocation policy and resonance-seeds'
deep-later stack). The honest lower bound that survives the flag: **a guest's
presence is pre-window order, in the archaeology doc's own sense** — they carry
residue older than your window by provenance, and that residue reads as a foreign
structure in your field, no matter how far the seed-planting mechanics are gated.

### 2.3 The signature — a body raised by another field, read on the maintenance axis

[`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) establishes the vitality classifier: a live
body *actively holds* its φ-lock (moves, feeds, `(1−q)` glow pulsing at the
maintainer's cadence); a fossil does not; a deliberate vent *raises* ε²; a scar
is a fallen floor (§1, §3.1). Its deliberate-vent class **IS the Coda's tell**
(§3.3).

**The Guest's third carried thing is their signature — and a guest's maintenance
cadence is *their* window's, not yours.** Because maintenance is a *time-series*
read (the pulse, the cadence, the gradient over a window, life-signal §2/§3.2),
and because the cadence is set by the tempo of the field that raised the body,
a guest's live read is a **foreign rhythm** in your window:

| Read | A resident | A guest |
|---|---|---|
| **maintained live coherence** | pulses at your window's tide, harvest-vs-thin re-based cadence | pulses at their **home window's** cadence — a rhythm your field did not set | life-signal §1/§3.1; patient-field §4.3 |
| **the deliberate-vent axis** | a resident's deliberate vent is *your* field's disorder, raised on purpose | a guest's carried ε² elevates them toward the vent line even without acting — *foreign* weight on your deliberate-vent axis | the-burden §2c; life-signal §3.3 |
| **the Coda's tell** | a resident clean-channeler in the desert | a guest in the margin is the same bright structured thing — but a **foreign signature** | signature-predator §3; §2.3 of this doc |

**The honest framing.** The signature read is the life-signal's Phase-1
maintenance axis over the real published channels (position, `(1−q)`, `ε²`,
`∇(g·Φ)`) — engine-real axes, [design] decision rule ([life-signal §6a]). A
guest's read uses the *same* classes; what makes it *foreign* is that the
**maintenance cadence** — the pulsing rhythm, the re-based threshold — belongs to
their home field, arriving into yours. **[design]** The "foreign cadence" framing
is this doc's designed presentation over the real maintenance-channel time-series;
it inherits life-signal's Phase-1 noise-floor probe for the class boundaries.

## 3. The visitor line — the Board books a guest with provenance, not as a resident

The Board ([`shared-ledger.md`](./shared-ledger.md)) is the settlement-scale
Weatherglass over the Q4 op-stream: it renders, per member-identity, each player's
running net contribution to / drain on the shared Qi bath (§2). Every op is an
immutable Q4 record carrying the **member-id** at the world-writer's single,
non-forgeable write ([schema-that-settles §2.1](./schema-that-settles.md)); the
member line survives death — same member, new lock (§2.1.1).

### 3.1 The principle extended to a foreign line

The schema's §2.1.1 rule, stated once: **a member is a continuous line, bound to
a persistent-Π identity, that death does not break** — same member, new lock, no
ledger amnesty. The Guest extends that same-line principle **to a *foreign*
identity**:

> **A guest books on your window's book as a visitor line: a continuous identity
> with provenance, from elsewhere — not a resident.** The Board's per-member rule
> is "one member, one line"; a guest is a *different* line, but the same *kind*
> of line — a persistent-Π identity, own home, own history — so it must book as
> **a line that is not-of-your-window**, tagged with a provenance flag, never
> merged into the window's resident books.

Concretely, the extended board row (the schema's `{member, op, worldPos, rung,
magnitude, sustain}` gains a designed provenance flag — **[design]**, over the
settled record, not a new canonical field):

| Resident row | Visitor row (guest) |
|---|---|
| `{ member: <window-line> }` — a resident's line, same member, new lock | `{ member: <foreign-line>, provenance: <origin-window> }` — a guest's line, their own home-id, marked *from elsewhere* |
| books to the window's per-tide `C(M)` aggregate | books to the **same** window's book (a contribution to / drain on *your* bath is real) but is **attributed to a visitor line** — never folded into a resident's `C(M)` |
| steward / margin / dissolved among the window's identities | a fourth, distinct identity: **the visitor** — reads exactly what they contributed to *your* bath, kept separate |

### 3.2 The honest accounting and the misattribution guards

A guest's contribution/drain **books on the window's book** (their `heal` into
your collapsing margin *is* a contribution to your bath; their sustained overdraw
*is* a drain) — but it is **marked as theirs, from elsewhere**. The Board's
misattribution guards extend exactly where the corpus already draws them:

| Guard | Why a guest needs it | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| **A guest's foreign ε² must not read as a resident's drain** | a heavy visitor carrying Burden sheds carried ε² in your field; the Board's margin-causer line must not blame that carried weight on a resident (or on the guest as a resident) | shared-ledger §3.3/§6e; the-burden §5 |
| **The visitor line never becomes a resident line** | a guest who stays must, at some designed point, *become* a resident — but only by a designed decision (settling), never silently by duration of book | shared-ledger §2.2 (the four identities); §2.1.1's same-line rule is *not* auto-extended to a foreigner |
| **The card-counting is honest** | a guest's `C(M)`-round is real but carries the provenance tag so the window's economy (who held the core, who drained it) is not distorted by a visitor's foreign weight | shared-ledger §1.3; §6e |

**The ethics, stated once.** The visitor line is the Board's misattribution
classifier applied to a stranger: **the Board may name a pattern (a visitor's
sustained net effect on the window's bath); it must never read a guest's carried
foreign ε² as a resident's drain, or as the guest's own *wounding* of your field.**
A guest's Burden is the cost of the field that raised them, not a blame signal
(the-burden's steward-with-weight guard, extended to a stranger). And because the
visitor line is provenance-tagged, a guest who leaves closes their line the way a
dissolved resident's closes ([shared-ledger §2.2](./shared-ledger.md)) — the
the window's book remembers *that a field visited*, without carrying its debt as its
own. **[design]** The provenance flag and the "becomes-resident-by-decision, not
by-duration" rule are this doc's designed extension over the settled record; the
misattribution-classifier floor is inherited verbatim from shared-ledger §3.3/§6e.

**The crossing (reverse pointer).** The first thing a visitor's provenance crosses
is the corpus's newest boundary-mark: [`the-threshold.md`](./the-threshold.md) §2.3
designs the **named arch** as the boundary of the visitor line — "the arch is the
first thing a visitor's provenance crosses; the visitor books as a guest from the
moment their carried Burden/order/signature passed a *named* edge" (it cites this
§3's visitor line as the booked event). The arch is where this visitor line
*starts* — the boundary that makes the crossing an act rather than an arrival; the
two docs now cite each other as read-and-answered (§2.3 there books this §3's line).

## 4. The encounter — meeting the field that raised them

The design's surface: how the fused, field-true encounter is *presented* — all
consumers of published channels, family-rule-obedient ([field-instruments §2.1]
(./field-instruments.md)).

### 4.1 The reader and the Glass read a guest's provenance

The coherence reader (`coherence-magic.md` §2) and the Weatherglass
([field-instruments §1.2](./field-instruments.md)) already render the ε²/`q`/
`(1−q)`/`∇(g·Φ)` channels. Reading a **guest** through them is reading their
provenance — the family rule, applied to a person:

| Surface | Reads | What a guest shows |
|---|---|---|
| **the reader (Sense)** | the ε² ghost + core of what it sees | a guest's deep-rung carried ε² and pre-window residue read as a **foreign structure** — a dark old core + the deliberate-vent-proximal elevation of the field that raised them (archaeology §3.1; the-burden §2c) |
| **the Weatherglass** | the four glanceable forms | a guest carries a **fifth glance** — the burden-tint of the-burden §3, thickened to *foreign* depth: the glass reads "this body was raised heavy elsewhere," a provenance your window's residents do not show |
| **the visitor line (Board)** | the Q4 op-stream | the settlement reads the guest as **a contribution/provenance row**, not a resident — the provenance presentation of §3 in the Board's own idiom |

**The family rule, stated once.** Every presentation above is a *consumer* of the
publish with an idiom — **never a new channel, never a new write**; a guest's
provenance is *read*, exactly as the reader reads any ε²/`q` structure. The guest
is not a new data type the engine must produce; they are the *same* published
channels, read with the question "where was this raised?"

### 4.2 The sky and the Coda respond to a foreign signature

The two passive responses of the window to a foreign signature, both grounded:

- **The sky.** [`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md)
  §3.3 renders a body's field structure at scale — a ground scar draws an aurora,
  a drain glows. The-burden §3 gives the sky-over-you: a heavy channeler carries a
  restless read where they walk. **A guest in your window carries a foreign
  aurora** — the (1−q)/ε² of the field that raised them, rendered over *your* sky
  at a cadence and tint your window's residents do not set. The sky, like the
  Glass, reads provenance passively: the field cannot help showing what arrived.

- **The Coda.** [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) — the fourth
  hazard class, a non-willful resonance structure that accumulates on a clean
  channeler's ε² trail (`R = ρ_signature·τ·M_stability`, §2.2), its tell the
  desert-silence inversion (§3). A heavy carrier — resident or guest — is a
  stronger standing signature (the-burden §2d). **A guest in a margin draws the
  predator like a resident heavy channeler would, but the trail is *foreign*:**
  their deep old carried ε² presents a higher `ρ_signature` and a sustained `τ`
  than a fresh resident could, and the Coda's resonance accumulates against a
  signature your window did not generate. The Board's Coda-attribution guard
  (shared-ledger §5/§6e) extends: **the predator's resonance over a guest's trail
  must not read as the guest's own overdraw — it is the margin responding to
  foreign order**, and the provenance tag is what keeps that honest.

> **Meeting a Guest is meeting the field that raised them.** Their Burden's
> *weight* (deep, old, foreign ε²), their artifact's *foreign order* (pre-window
> residue your field cannot re-precipitate), their signature's *foreign cadence*
> (a maintenance rhythm your sky did not set) — all read in your field's own
> channels and currency. The encounter is *field-true* because it is the field's
> own channels, carrying a provenance: you meet the person, and through the same
> read you meet the window that shaped them.

### 4.3 A guest names themselves — the stranger's name as provenance

[the-name.md](./the-name.md) establishes naming as a field act: a persistent-Π
anchor bound to a thing, the member-id's principle extended from players to
things; **you cannot name falsely** (§2). A guest's name — *their* name, not yours
to give — is provenance in the the-name sense:

> **A guest names themselves, and the name is a provenance flag on an adult
> identity.** Because a name holds only what the field truthfully holds
> (the-name §2), a guest's self-given name — bound to *their* persistent-Π
