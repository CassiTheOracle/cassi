# The Healer's Craft: Medicine as a Designed Practice, and the Healer's Toll

**Question under design:** the corpus designs the wound — `wound-remembered.md` —
as a scar that refuses (a deliberate break of the φ-lock that re-opens as the
attractor re-tensions it); it designs the *debt* — `the-burden.md` — as a carried
ε²-loan with interest, cleared in a Still Room's patient time; it designs the
*death* — `player-remains.md` — as dissolution and a fossil; and it designs the
*recovery budget* — `coherence-magic.md` §1.2's `B` — recovering geographically.
But nothing designs **the healer as a practice**: the person who holds another's
torn edges. This document is that design.

> **The Wound-Healer is a designed practice with its own honest toll.** Healing a
> wound (`wound-remembered.md` — the deliberate-break scar that refuses) or a
> deep Burden (`the-burden.md`) is not a free, channeled operation folded into
> ordinary Shield/Heal. It is the **long bind**: the healer holds the torn edge of
> another's persistent-Π, drawing their ε²-debt *into themselves* so the other's
> field can re-settle. **The healer's toll is the genuine new cost** — every long
> bind leaves a little of the hurt inside the healer: their own Burden rises,
> their own read dims toward the wound's color, and their own clearing needs the
> Still Room's patient time. **The healer pays what the healed were spared** — and
> the craft of healing well is the discipline of the bind: how long, how deep,
> when to stop, when the toll is too high.

This is a **practice-design**: the player-interior face of medicine, standing on
the medicine the corpus already prices but does not make a *craft*. It introduces
no new physics and no new channel; it is a **designed read over the existing ε² /
vent / (1−q) channels** (the family rule), turning the wound-heal and the burden's
transfer into a *held, costed, legible act* with a toll. The honest boundary — the
healing op and the vent channels are engine-real / [design]-landed; the wound's
persistence is `wound-remembered.md`'s [design] (probe-gated on the scar lifecycle);
**the long bind's transfer mechanics, the toll, and the discipline are this doc's
[design]** over that stack — is drawn in §5 and never blurred.

## 1. The Healer's Craft, stated once

`coherence-magic.md` §2 fixes **Shield/Heal** as the player's sixth op: suppress
local `ε²`, inject opposing Yin to re-lock `EY = φ·EI` in a region, a sustained
B-bounded hold (`§1.2`), net-zero over the hold or the vent backs up. This is
ordinary *anti-corruption + healing* — it holds a scar's floor and helps a dent
re-bind (`energy-harnessing.md` §5.4). `wound-remembered.md` §4 shows what a
*wound* demands beyond that: not floor-suppression (which only holds ground, it
does not re-bind) but a **re-lock hold** — a long, bounded act that holds a
broken lock's torn edges together while the attractor's `−ω₀²(EY−φ·EI)` pull
crosses the broken well's lip and the persistent-Π anomaly re-settles. And
`the-burden.md` prices the cost of sustained magic as a **carried loan with
interest**: every op sheds ε² that accumulates on the channeler, cleared only in
a Still Room's patient time.

**The corpus has the medicine; it does not have the healer.** `wound-remembered.md`
§4 designs the *healing op* (the re-lock hold); `the-burden.md` designs the *debt*
the channeler carries; `coherence-magic.md` prices *whoever* performs Shield/Heal
at its B-cost. None asks who *holds the other's torn edge*, or what that *person*
becomes. The Wound-Healer is that missing practice:

> **The Healer's Craft is medicine made a designed practice with its own honest
> toll. The long bind is the deliberate-break healing act: the healer holds the
> torn edges of another's persistent-Π — a wound-remembered's refusal, or a deep
> Burden's carried field — drawing their ε²-debt *into themselves* so the other's
> field can re-settle against the attractor. It is slower than ordinary
> anti-corruption, it is bounded by the no-free-energy cap (the heal is spent,
> never a mint), and it is never free. The healer pays what the healed were
> spared: every bind leaves a little of the hurt inside them.**

The decisive framing — the healer as the corpus's caretaker, against the field's
indifference:

> **The field is indifferent** (`field-npc-ai.md` §4: "the coherence budget is
> the only law"). It does not care that a wound refuses, that a heavy channeler
> carries a debt, that a broken lock tears. **The healer is the one who chooses
> not to be indifferent** — who takes the other's ε²-debt into themselves so the
> field's own patience can do the re-binding instead of the field letting it tear
> again. The toll is the honest price of that choice: **the field is indifferent;
> the healer chooses, and pays, not to be.**

**[design]** What is engine-real / [design]-landed: the Shield/Heal op and the
vent channels (`coherence-magic.md` §1/§2); the player as a bounded EY injector +
ε² vent; the carried-debt read and its Still-Room clearing (`the-burden.md`
§1/§2b); the wound's re-lock-hold healing frame (`wound-remembered.md` §4). What
is this doc's [design]: **the long bind — its ε²-transfer mechanics, its toll,
and its discipline — a designed gameplay surface over that landed stack**,
probe-calibrated (§5). No claim here pretends the engine writes a "healer" or a
"bind" record today.

## 2. The long bind

### 2.1 What the bind IS — the deliberate-break healing act

`wound-remembered.md` §4.1 is the load-bearing frame: **healing a wound is letting
the lock re-bind to itself** — not suppressing `ε²` below a floor (that holds
ground, it does not re-bind) but **holding the torn edges together while the
attractor re-tensions**, a long, sustained field hold that lets the `−ω₀²(EY−φ·EI)`
pull actually cross the broken well's lip and re-lock. Because the wound was cut
on purpose, the ordinary heal cannot close it — the field's own re-tension keeps
tearing it open (§1.2). The healer breaks that cycle by *holding*.

The long bind is that hold, made a person-held act:

> **The long bind is the deliberate-break healing act.** The healer takes the
> wound-remembered's broken persistent-Π edge — or a deep Burden's torn carried
> field — into their own hold, at the locality, over a sustained window many
> ticks long (the time-lens loan of `patient-field.md` §3 at its most literal:
> healing was already *spending coherence to make the healing happen now*, and a
> wound demands the full arc — `wound-remembered.md` §4.2). **The healer holds the
> torn edges of the other's persistent-Π while the attractor re-tensions**, and
> while they hold, the other's ε²-debt transfers into them. The bind is slower
> than ordinary anti-corruption because it is *not* a floor-suppression — it is a
> re-lock hold that must outlast the wound's re-opening rhythm (`wound-remembered.md`
> §2.4) before the persistent-Π anomaly re-settles into a coherent well (a healed
> wound becomes — in the archaeology vocabulary — a scar, which then heals
> normally, `wound-remembered.md` §4.1).

The honest mechanics — the ε²-debt transfer:

> **The other's clears; the healer's rises.** The wound-remembered (or the
> burdened) person's carried ε² is not erased by the bind — it is *moved*. The
> healer, holding the torn edge, becomes the locality's vent: the shed ε² that
> the broken lock keeps re-opening into accumulates **on the healer** the way a
> heavy channeler's practice accumulates on them (`the-burden.md` §1 — a
> player-attached, slow-fading ε² read). The other's field re-settles against the
> attractor and *their* read clears toward the field's heal rate `h`
> (`coherence-magic.md` §1.2); the healer's carried ε² rises by the transferred
> amount. **This is `the-burden.md`'s channel, reversed** — where a channeler
> sheds debt into their own carried field by acting, the healer draws *another's*
> carried field into theirs by holding. **[design]** The *transfer mechanics* —
> the amount of the other's ε²-debt the bind moves onto the healer, and at what
> rate per hold — is this doc's designed read over the engine-real vent/(1−q)
> channels, probe-calibrated (§5a); the *direction* — holding a torn edge sheds
> into the holder, per the working-and-vent physics of `field-npc-ai.md` §3.2 and
> `coherence-magic.md` §3 — is the corpus's own law.

### 2.2 Bounded by the no-free-energy cap — the heal is spent, never a mint

`energy-harnessing.md` §6 is the load-bearing cap, inherited unchanged (`output ≤
φ⁻¹·input`): no conversion yields more than it sinks, and nothing is a net mint.
The long bind holds it as its own spine, in the specific register the healer's
toll demands:

> **The heal is spent, never a mint.** The long bind converts the healer's held
> time and coherence into the other's re-settled field at **best** `output ≤ φ⁻¹·
> input` — the heal's effect is bounded by what the healer channeled in, minus the
> `(1−q)` waste (`energy-harnessing.md` §2). The transferred ε² is *carried* by
> the healer, then cleared by the Still Room's patient time — it is not shed into
> a harvestable drain, not converted into power, not an asset. **And the bind
> cannot resurrect**: a wound's persistent-Π run that has *dissolved* — the
> death, `player-remains.md` — is not a torn edge to hold; **a healer cannot raise
> the dead** (`the-funeral.md` §2.3/§6d), because there is no input whose `output ≤
> φ⁻¹·input` could re-condense a dissolved Π run at full coherence. **The honest
> limit is the cap's own law: the bind re-settles what is still, stubbornly, *in*
> the field — it cannot re-create what the field let go.**

The one honest amplification, and it is not a mint: the multiplicatively-aligned
commons — a group acting phase-locked IS a working (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.2), so a
healer holding a bind *with* the patient's own moderated re-hold (or a settlement
singing a dirge for the worst wound, `the-funeral.md` §2) couples at `M ≈ 1` the
way any phase-locked working does — the multiplicity gain of `magic-systems.md`
§1, the corpus's own law, never a free-energy mint.

**The toll is the honest proof the heal was spent.** The cap says a heal that
cost nothing is a cheat; the healer's toll — the risen Burden, the dimmed read —
is the visible, legible proof that the dead-but-refusing was genuinely held and
genuinely paid for. A heal with no toll would violate §6; the toll is how the
world books the spent coherence honestly (§4, §5d).

**The bind's duration (reverse pointer, two-way).** This §2's held act has a
timer: [`the-hourglass.md`](./the-hourglass.md) §3 reads **the long bind's
length** for the healer — "the Hourglass at the bind tells the healer how long
the bind can be held before the toll turns, the bind's honest bound"
(`the-hourglass.md` §3, grounded on this §2's bind and §3's Still-Room clearing).
Where this §2's discipline fixes *how long/deep/stop* as the craft's dials, the
sand makes the honest duration legible at the local tempo (released when it runs
out, before the healer's own overdraw, `coherence-magic.md` §4.3); the two docs
now cite each other as read-and-answered.

## 3. The healer's toll

### 3.1 The genuine new cost — a little of the hurt stays

The long bind is a transfer, and a transfer leaves a residue on the receiver.
Every bind leaves a little of the hurt inside the healer:

| The toll | What it is | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| **The healer's Burden rises** | the transferred ε² accumulates on the healer as a carried, slow-fading debt (`the-burden.md` §1) — the running scar of the healing they do | `the-burden.md` §1/§2a (the channel, reversed); §2b it clears only in patient time |
| **The healer's read dims toward the wound's color** | a practicing healer's carried ε² elevates their own read on the deliberate-vent axis — at the noise floor (`q ~ 1e-3…1e-1`) they can read *wound-like* to the field's own classifier | `the-mirror.md` §2.4 (classification face), §2.1 (Burden's glow); `the-burden.md` §2c (a heavy channeler reads like a wound at distance) |
| **The healer's clearing needs the Still Room's patient time** | the toll is cleared by coherent rest — the Still Room (`field-instruments.md` §2.2) and its nightly body-scale instance, the bed (`sleep.md` §1/§3) — a withdrawal from acting, not a further op | `the-burden.md` §2b; `sleep.md` §1/§4; `patient-field.md` §3.2 |

The mirror here is load-bearing: **a practiced healer is a carrying, exhausted,
quietly-wounded figure.** Their own classification face reads *burdened* after a
day of binds, and *wound-like at the edge* if they have overbound
(`the-mirror.md` §2.4) — not because they are a wound, but because they carry
what the healed were spared. The mirror's honest correction is `the-burden.md`
§5's: the reveal that the "wound" is *the healer's own carried debt, stationary on
them, not a rising structure in the field* is the classifier's honest answer — the
same count that lets a heavy channeler stop mistaking themselves for the world's
wound (`the-burden.md` §5).

**[design]** The toll's *magnitude* — how much of each bind's transferred ε²
sticks as the healer's carried debt, and at what decay in coherent rest — is a
designed, probe-calibrated dial over the engine-real vent/(1−q) channels (§5a);
the *direction* — a long bind raises the holder's carried ε² — is the corpus's own
vent-and-carried-debt law (holding a working's phase sheds into the holder,
`the-apprenticeship.md` §2.2 / `field-npc-ai.md` §3.2).

### 3.2 The discipline — the craft of healing well

Because the toll is real and compounding (`the-burden.md` §2a, the interest — the
carried debt re-opens on the next vent in thin ground), healing well is not a
power level; it is a **discipline of the bind**:

| The discipline | What it means | The honest consequence of failing it |
|---|---|---|
| **How long** | the hold must outlast the wound's re-opening rhythm (`wound-remembered.md` §2.4) but not be held forever — a bind held past the re-settle point stops healing and only feeds the healer's toll | holding too long transfers more than the wound needed — the healer's Burden rises past the honest cost |
| **How deep** | the bind is taken at a rung — deeper holds re-bind faster / over a larger volume but shed more ε² (`coherence-magic.md` §4.2, the depth⇔blowback ladder) | a deepen-beyond-capacity bind tips the healer's own injection toward overdraw |
| **When to stop** | the discipline's core: the craft of healing well knows when the wound is re-settled (healed to a scar, `wound-remembered.md` §4.1) and releases. **A healer who overbinds harms themselves** — the toll does not repay; it compounds | not stopping means carrying debt the bind never needed to move |
| **When the toll is too high** | a healer already at the overdraw edge (`coherence-magic.md` §4.3 — vent past capacity tips organized → random and relaxes in a full-cascade discharge) must not bind — **overdrawing at the wrong moment, in thin ground, is how a healer becomes a second wound** | the discharge is the healer's own full-cascade risk, exactly `coherence-magic.md` §4.3 |
| **The patient-craft tie** | the bind borrows the field's patience (`patient-field.md` §3); a healer who respects the patient arc heals more for the same toll than one who forces it | forcing the re-lock against the field's patience costs extra vent (`patient-field.md` §3.3 — the interest swells at thin) |

> **The craft of healing well is the discipline of the bind: how long, how deep,
> when to stop, when the toll is too high.** Healing is not a free channeled op —
> it is a held act whose cost is the healer's own carried weight, and the practiced
> healer is the one who has learned to pay exactly what each wound needs and no
> more. The healer who overbinds is not more powerful; they are **more wounded** —
> exhausting themselves into the very overdraw the healed were spared
> (`coherence-magic.md` §4.3).

**[design]** The discipline's dials (the bind's duration/deep/stop thresholds, the
"too high" line) are this doc's designed, probe-calibrated thresholds over the
engine-real vent/heal/sustain mechanics (§5a); the *direction* — overbinding
harms the healer — is the corpus's own overdraw-and-interest law stated as the
healer's practice.

## 4. What a healer is

### 4.1 The practice's place

The healer is not a lonely mechanic; the corpus's own systems give it a place:

| The place | What it is | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| **The veteran of the bind** | the craft taught **hand to hand** — the apprentice-healer learns by holding *small* binds first (a fledgling scar, a light Burden) under a veteran's phase window, exactly as the apprenticeship's pair-bond transfers craft by resonance rather than instruction | `the-apprenticeship.md` §1/§2.2 (the pair-bond, the veteran's faster accrual); `resonance-tutor.md` (the recorded twin) |
| **A settlement's medicine** | a window with a healer can *afford* the wound's healing — where a lone wound would tear (or a heavy Burden would sit), a healer's bind lets the field re-settle. The **Coherence Board** books the heal as a contribution with the toll legible; a heavy healer books as **steward-with-weight**, never a drain | `shared-ledger.md` §6e (the misattribution guard — "must not read the debt as a drain"); `the-burden.md` §5 (steward-with-weight) |
| **The toll as the corpus's caretaker cost** | the field is indifferent; **the healer is the one who chooses not to be** — the settlement that reads its heavy healers' carried weight as *their reason to be rested and fed in the Still Room* (rather than shunned) is the corpus's intended, honest society | `the-burden.md` §5 (the social harm guard); `field-npc-ai.md` §4 ("the field is indifferent…") inverted by the healer's choice |

### 4.2 The Board reads the toll — steward-with-weight, never a drain

`shared-ledger.md` §3.3/§6e's guard, applied to the healer, is the practice's
civic honesty:

> **The Board may name a *pattern* (a sustained net drain over many tides); it
> must never name a *person* off the carried debt's elevated read alone.** A
> heavy healer books as **steward-with-weight** — the debt is a *practice cost
> that heals in a Still Room*, not a signature of wounding. The toll is legible
> on the Board (the heavy healer's carried read, `the-burden.md` §2c) as the price
> of the medicine the settlement receives; the ledger must not read that weight as
> a margin-causer's drain. **The healer's toll is booked as contribution-with-weight,
> and the settlement that reads it correctly is the design's honest society.**

### 4.3 The honest figure of the healer

> **The healer is a carrying, exhausted, quietly-wounded figure — and the
> settlement that keeps one is a settlement that chose the wound's healing over
> the field's indifference.** Not a hero of spent points, but a veteran of the
> bind: their own read dims toward the wound's color, their own Burden rises with
> every life they hold together, their own clearing is the Still Room's patient
> time. They pay what the healed were spared — and they are worth resting, feeding,
> and honoring because the field would not have paid it at all.
