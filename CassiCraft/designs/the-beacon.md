# The Beacon: The Sown Standing Light — the Settlement's Fixed Mark of 'Here Is a Place'

**Question under design:** the corpus has three lights. The lantern
([`the-lantern.md`](./the-lantern.md)) is the **carried** night-read — a light you hold
and walk, the near field's legibility in the dark around you, the ground-level inverse
of the sky's aurora (§1/§2). The bell ([`the-bell.md`](./the-bell.md)) is the
**alarm** — a mechanical *sound* that rings only when the stack agrees, answering
*now* to a whole settlement (§1/§3). The observatory ([`the-observatory.md`](./the-observatory.md))
is the **window-read** — a room where a settlement reads its field together, the Board's
book made spatial (§1/§2). **None is a fixed, standing light a settlement plants and
burns to mark itself across the dark.** The lantern *carries*; the bell *sounds*; the
observatory *reads*. No structure *sows* a light — a stationary, deliberate, always-lit
mark at the settlement's edge that says *here is a settlement, and here is where the
light obliges*. This document is that design: **the Beacon, the sown standing light — a
fixed, projected high-`q` light a settlement plants at its deliberate edge and burns to
mark itself across the dark.**

channel and the place-mark structure (Phase-1.5+), with the **Phase-1 slice stated** (a
bounded projected-light-mark consumer over the published channels). It is the corpus's

lantern (carried), the bell (alarm), and the observatory (read). It introduces no new
channel and no new physics: it is a designed consumer over the light the corpus already
renders and the architecture and identity machinery it already designs.

## 1. The Beacon, stated once

The corpus carries a light (the lantern, read at the hand), rings an alarm (the bell,
sounded at the agreement), and reads a window (the observatory, composed in a room).
Each is a relation of a *person* to the field: the lantern is held, the bell is answered
by a settlement's attention, the observatory is entered. **None is the settlement's own
standing mark** — a fixed light that asks nothing of a hand or a room or a moment, that
simply *is*, planted at the edge and burning to say *here*.

> **The Beacon, stated once.** It is the **sown standing light** — a fixed, projected
> high-`q` light a settlement plants at its deliberate edge and burns to mark itself
> across the dark. It is the corpus's *sown* light: the fixed, deliberate mark of *here
> is a settlement, and here is where the light obliges.* It finds the traveler who reads
> the way-home by it, and it calls the light-drawn being who covets it — **to be findable
> is to be found**, the honest cost of being a marked home. And it is a held structure
> that converts nothing: the mark is the settlement's own act, never a gain, never a mint.

The one-line distinction from every sibling — the Beacon is the corpus's *fixed, standing,
self-advertising* light:

| Instrument / structure | It asks | It does | Carried / fixed |
|---|---|---|---|
| The lantern ([`the-lantern.md`](./the-lantern.md) §1) | *what is the field here, in the dark?* | reads the near field at the hand, casts it as glow at the walker's feet | **carried** — the-lantern, a held tool |
| The bell ([`the-bell.md`](./the-bell.md) §1) | *when do we answer?* | rings *now* on the multi-channel coincidence, to the whole settlement | fixed, but **alarm** — a sound, transient |

| **The Beacon (this doc)** | ***who is here?*** | **projects the settlement's mark *out* into the dark, always lit** | **fixed** — a standing, sown light |

The Beacon is not a *greater* lantern, a *louder* bell, or a *wider* observatory. It is
the corpus's light *in the other direction*: the lantern reads inward to the walker's
feet, the observatory reads inward to the room, the bell answers inward to the
settlement — **the Beacon signals outward, into the dark, and asks nothing in return but
to be seen.** A settled place is a place that says *we are here*; the Beacon is that
sentence made a fixed, projected light.

**The honest status, once:** every piece the Beacon composes is its source doc's landed

## 3. The double-edged mark — the load-bearing honesty

The load-bearing honesty of the Beacon, stated once and never blurred:

> **A beacon is a light that finds home and a light that calls.** It *finds* — a traveler
> on the walk crosses toward the gradient and reads the settlement's light as the
> way-home; a voyager returning from the dark reads the window's near-light the way a
> distant window reads as a star, at the scale of the door. And it *calls* — the
> light-drawn being covets it; a predator reads the bright place as a gathering, the
> marked home as a target. **To be findable is to be found** — the honest cost of being a
> marked home, the settlement's own self-advertisement, never a hidden-only beacon.

The two halves of the composition, honestly:

### (a) The found — the Beacon finds the traveler home

[`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2 lands the crossing: a walker reads the terrain's
`∇(g·Φ)` at foot-pace, steps with the lean, times the thin, carries little. The
settlement's light is the crossing's **answer** — the thing the gradient organizes toward.
A traveler on the walk, cheap along a ridge descent, rises over the lip and sees the
settlement's fixed light: *there is the way home.* And a voyager returning from the dark
reads it the way the corpus already reads far light — [`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md)
§2.2: **a distant window reads as a star** — the boundary-layer discharges of another
anchored field, resolved at range. The Beacon is that read at the near scale: **a window's
own near-light**, the standing mark of the very window the voyager left, held at the
harbor so the return is *legible as a return*. The found read is not a navigation-mint —
it is the settlement's presence made readable, the way the window-star makes the far
window readable, but at the door's distance.

> **The found, honestly:** the Beacon is the settlement's *legibility outward* — the light
> that says to the crossing walker and the returning voyager *here is the place you were
> going.* It is the way-home made a standing light, and it never acts on its own: it
> finds only those who are already reading the field toward it.

### (b) The called — the Beacon is the bright place as a gathering

[`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §2 lands the trail-read: the Coda
accumulates toward a clean signature — `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability` — and
`the-spring.md` §3 lands that a **bright, sustained, order-loud source is exactly the
fixed landmark a Coda can find.** The Beacon's mark is the *brightest sustained
order-loud source a settlement keeps* — it is, in the corpus's own terms, the 
**point of fame** (`the-spring.md` §3) made built. And the point of fame is a double mark:

> **The called, honestly:** the same standing light that finds the traveler makes the
> settlement *legible to what hunts.* A predator reads the bright place as a gathering —
> a clean, sustained, order-loud mark is the settlement *advertising its own presence* to
> the field's signature-reader. **To be findable is to be found**: the Beacon is not a
> hidden light, and a settlement that lights its edge is announcing, to every reader of
> the field — traveler and Coda alike — *here is a place, with a maintained edge and a
> held core to covet.*

This is the Beacon's load-bearing honesty, and it is the settlement's own act, not a
hidden mechanic. The double-edge is the **explicit price of the self-advertisement**: a
settlement may choose a hidden-only existence (no beacon, no standing mark — but then it
is *unfindable* to its friends and *illegible* as a home), or it may light its edge and
accept that the light serves both directions. **There is no beacon that only finds.**
That is the honest cost of *being a marked home* — and it is exactly the corpus's
privilege of the `the-spring.md` point-of-fame: a source that hid its brightness would be
a secret wealth the field's own legibility forbids. The Beacon is the same forbidding,
made the settlement's deliberate, standing choice.

## 4. The held light — the design's load-bearing bound

The Beacon's design is a held structure that converts nothing. This is the load-bearing
bound, stated once and never blurred:

> **A Beacon converts nothing — no mark that yields; the light is a held structure,
> never a mint.** The projected light is the settlement's own maintained coherence read
> as a standing signal — the same `q` the house's room holds up, the same `(1−q)` waste
> the settlement spends to keep it (`energy-harnessing.md` §2/§4.4). **A beacon that
> "projected" power — that marked across the dark for free — would be a false light the
> field sheds**, exactly as a house that "generates" `q` is a lie (`house-that-steers.md`
> §5d) and a spring that *makes* order is a well the field sheds (`the-spring.md` §5d).
> The mark is the settlement's own act, never a gain, never a navigation-mint.

Three honest consequences, the corpus's no-free-energy discipline applied to the sown
light:

1. **The Beacon never projects a yield.** It informs (it finds the traveler, announces
   the place) and it holds (it is a standing draw), but it *produces nothing* — no
   coherence, no boost, no navigation except the legibility of the settlement's own
   presence. **There is no "build a beacon to travel faster" and no "beacon that mints
   the way-home."** A beacon that reads as making the crossing cheaper would be a
   false light — the marked edge is the settlement's self-advertisement, and it never
   grants the field's own gradient a discount it did not already have.
2. **The found read is a legibility, never a mint (the walk's cap inherited).** The
   walker who reads the way-home by the Beacon is receiving *information* — the
   settlement's presence made visible — never energy (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5d:
   information is not energy). The walk's own no-free-energy cap (`the-walk.md` §4d)
   holds the Beacon's reach exactly: no descended gradient becomes stored coherence,
   no standing light charges a traveler (`energy-harnessing.md` §6). **The found is
   free *information*, never free *movement*.**
3. **The called is a cost, never a farm.** The double-edge is a liability the settlement
   *pays* (the Coda can read the mark, `the-spring.md` §3; `signature-predator.md` §7e's
   no-farming gate) — the mark is never a lure the settlement profits from. There is no
   "light a beacon to attract and reap," because the field sheds nothing a collector can
   tap from a clean mark — the called is absence (stop feeding the Coda, mask, withdraw
   to the high-`q` refuge, `signature-predator.md` §4), never a harvest. **The highest a
   Beacon can announce is the field's own presence at the edge; the cap encodes it.**

The no-free-energy cap holds the Beacon as a hard bound, inherited through the house's
`holds-never-mints` (`house-that-steers.md` §5d), the lantern's `informs-never-mints`
(`the-lantern.md` §5d), and the spring's `provides-nothing-beyond-its-own-welling`
(`the-spring.md` §5d), and it is the reason the Beacon is exactly as cheap as a lantern
(one standing sample, §5) and exactly as honest: it informs, never mints — a held light,
never a false one.
