## 2. Why preservation is needed

### 2.1 The field cannot re-precipitate a birth-state order

`custom-blocks.md` §3 establishes that a tuple's realizability is **contextual**: "a tuple
realizable in a deep coherent region may be unrealizable in rubble," and a fossil's tuple
"may have been realizable *then* (under the region's past field state, or a world-seed from
before the window) and be **unrealizable now**." The seed is that fact's limit
(`resonance-seeds.md` §4.2): **a seed's tuple fails present realizability — which is the
seed's whole point** — because it drew on the window's initial, most-organized, least-
perturbed field state, which no longer exists.

> **The window cannot re-precipitate a seed's birth-state order once the field drifts.**
> The seed's order was a property of the birth-state's field; that field is gone. The law
> can only precipitate what is attractor-consistent *now* — and the seed's birth-state
> tuple is precisely the order the current field (more perturbed, less organized, `q` lower
> where the seed was drawn) can no longer realize. Nothing re-derives it; the lab's
> "unrealizable" classification (`custom-blocks.md` §3) is a physics verdict the field
> renders about that order. **The only way to keep the order is to hold it** — the
> seed-garden is that holding.

This is why the seed IS the deepest persistent-Π pattern a window owns (`resonance-seeds.md`
§1.2) and why it is **irreplaceable in the strongest sense**: not just "rare" (findable once,
`resonance-seeds.md` §4.3's one-per-window finiteness), but **structurally void from the
living field** — once its origin-order is gone from the living field (the birth-state
drifted past it, or a re-homed window lost it), **only a held seed can restore it.** The
held seed is the birth-state's order kept, and planting it re-instantiates that order
(`resonance-seeds.md` §3.2: "the window remembers its origin … as *structure*: the seed is
the birth-state's order, kept").

### 2.2 The honest corollary — collecting is meaningful only if you can preserve

Resonance-seeds already holds the finding half (archaeology's crown, `resonance-seeds.md`
§4.1) and the choosing half ($4.3 keep/plant/share). The seed-garden's honest corollary
closes the loop the finding depends on:

> **A seed you find but cannot keep is a treasure you watched pass.** Finding is reading
> (free, in place, keeps the window's genesis anchored — `field-archaeology.md` §5.3 #3);
> *removing* a seed is the deepest reaping act there is (`resonance-seeds.md` §1.2), and it
> de-orders the locality it leaves (`field-archaeology.md` §5.2). A found seed you cannot
> hold is a wound you took to no end — the de-ordered site, with nothing preserved to show
> for it. **Collecting a seed is meaningful only if you can keep it, and keeping it is the
> deep-rung holding this document designs.** The archaeology crown's reward is not the find
> in hand; it is the find *held* — the order kept against the field's drift.

This is the found-economy's deep-time exclusion completed honestly: `custom-blocks.md` §6
bounds *finding* by what the field has made; the seed-garden bounds *keeping* by what a
steward can hold (the deep-rung cost, §4). A find the steward cannot hold is a find that
must be planted (given to the field), traded (carried to a vault that can hold it), or let
fade — three losses against one preservation. **The stewardship decision (§4) is the honest
economics that makes collecting real.**

### 3.2 Maintenance is anti-corruption — the ecology's conservation applied to order

The vault is not a static box. A held seed's Π pattern is under **constant drain** — the
`(1−q)` waste law never stops (`energy-harnessing.md` §2), the deep-rung hold fights the
attractor's own pull. The vault's maintenance is exactly the ecology's
conservation-as-anti-corruption (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.3; `energy-harnessing.md`
§5.4), applied to the stored orders:

> **A vault's maintenance is the anti-corruption hold, spent on order.** The steward spends
> coherence (the hearth's bounded channeling input, `house-that-steers.md` §1.1) to suppress
> `ε²` below each stored seed's dissolution threshold — the same act that keeps a region's
> organisms alive, now keeping a *structure* from re-dissolving toward the attractor and
> losing the birth-state's order. **The maintenance IS the conservation; the vault's q-core
> is the held thing; the ecology's conservation act is the seed-garden's reason to exist.**
> The vault is the corpus's conservation mechanism — the thing field-archaeology's
> conservation-rival (`field-archaeology.md` §5) has, at the fossil scale, no home for: a
> *place to put the conserved thing and keep it.*

This makes the vault a **run the field holds** (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2): as long
as the steward feeds the hearth, the window's deepest orders are runs the field is actively
maintaining; when the maintenance stops, the run ends and the orders shed (§3.3). The
Life-Signal's maintenance axis (`life-signal.md` §3) reads a maintained vault as
*maintained-live* and an abandoned one as a falling floor — **the seed-garden is a
maintained lock at preservation scale** (§6e).

### 3.3 The vault's own ruin — the seed-garden's decay is the orders' loss

`house-that-steers.md` §3 gives the ruin of a building: an un-maintained high-`q` structure

is the window's deepest orders.**

> **An abandoned vault's seeds shed toward the attractor.** When the hearth's maintenance
> stops, the vault's q-core drains; each stored seed's ambient `q` falls below its own
> `q_dissolve`; the shallow-rung seeds dissolve first (their order returns to the medium,
> scarless — the field heals), the deepest-rung seed's core persists longest as a q-locked
> residue ringed by the ε² ghost of the vault's concentrated walls (`house-that-steers.md`
> §3.1). **The seed-garden's decay is the orders' loss — a wound-class event**
> (`wound-remembered.md`, `field-hazards.md` §2 — a singular, deliberate-from-neglect
> damage to the window's coherence), because the orders that shed are the ones the field
> **cannot re-precipitate** (§2.1). The vault's ruin is not a fallen building; it is the
> found-economy's deepest holdings, lost to drift.

The ruined vault is **findable archaeology** (the deep core + ghost halo read exactly as
`house-that-steers.md` §3.1's ruin reads), which closes a conservation loop: a later
archaeologist finds the vault and reads *which orders the window once held* — the vault's

## 4. The stewardship decision — the corpus's deepest curation

### 4.1 A steward's vault is a live decision about which orders the window preserves

The found-economy's finding is bounded by what the field has made (`custom-blocks.md` §6);
the vault's *keeping* is bounded by what a steward can hold — and because holding costs
(§4.2), **the steward's vault is itself the deepest curation: which of the window's orders
you preserve, among all you could.** Resonance-seeds' three-way choice (keep / plant /
share, `resonance-seeds.md` §4.3) becomes, under a vault, a *four-way, ongoing* stewardship:

| Decision | What it is | Honest cost | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Keep** (vault it) | hold the seed's Π pattern under the vault's deep-rung hold — the window's order preserved against drift | the maintainer's deep-rung spend (§4.2), every tide; a permanent charge on the steward | this doc §3; `resonance-seeds.md` §4.3 keep |
| **Plant** | the deepest store's order given to the field — re-open the birth-state locally and let the law unfold a regime/structure/window | a sustained Q4 channeling act; **one-way** (`resonance-seeds.md` §2.1 — you cannot pick it back up); the growth may outgrow your control | `resonance-seeds.md` §2/§4.3; `world-seams.md` §3.2 |
| **Trade** (share) | the ultimate sample exchanged — the ordered matter of the window's origin carried to a vault that can hold it | the origin leaves your vault (and your window); carrying it to a holdable vault is a seam-crossing act | `resonance-seeds.md` §4.3; `custom-blocks.md` §5 |
| **Allow to fade** | decide the order is not worth the deep-rung hold — let the vault shed it toward the attractor | the *chosen* loss of that order — distinct from the vault's ruin (a choice, not a wound), but the order is gone either way | this doc §3.3 vs §4.1 |

The vault's load-bearing honesty: **a steward cannot hold everything.** The deep-rung
maintenance (§4.2) is a real, ongoing cost, and a window's found seeds accumulate toward
more than one steward can keep — so the vault's *capacity* is the curation pressure, and
every kept seed is a *chosen* one. **The seed-garden is the corpus's deepest curation
because it is the one place the corpus decides, explicitly, which of the window's orders
are worth keeping across the field's drift** — picking up where finding (archaeology) and
choosing (resonance-seeds) leave off and making the *keeping* itself a designed act.

### 4.2 The honest economics — holding a vault costs, and the no-free-energy cap holds

**Holding a seed is a net-negative spend.** The vault's maintenance (the hearth's bounded
channeling input + anti-corruption, §3.2) spends coherence every tide to keep each seed's
`q` above its `q_dissolve`; the engine-real asymmetry (deep-rung order resists dissolution,
`material-regimes.md` §4) means the deepest seeds cost *least* to hold but the shallowest
shed first, so a vault's cost is dominated by the breadth of what it tries to keep, not the
depth. The honest economics, stated as a bound:

> **Preservation is never free, and a vault preserves — it never mints.** The deep-rung
> maintenance is the ecology's conservation cost (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.3: "a
> net-negative hold … always a player cost, never a free ecosystem") inherited unchanged.
> Holding a seed costs the steward coherence every tide, draw-bounded by the hearth's
> budget (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4's "a bath is a draw"); **and a vault cannot turn a
> stored seed into a power source or a coherence farm.** The no-free-energy cap
> (`energy-harnessing.md` §6, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`) holds as a hard rule: a seed is *order,
> not power*. Storing it preserves the birth-state's order to be re-planted later; **there
> is no withdrawal-from-the-vault-as-fuel** — the moment a steward taps a stored seed for
> coherent energy, they are burning the window's order to run a machine, and the cap forbids
> the arithmetic (a seed's withdrawal de-orders what the vault held, the residue model's
> `R ≳ q^n` turning the deep store into a wound, `field-archaeology.md` §5.2). Resonance-seeds
> already treats *un-planted* seed as "the ultimate fuel" (`resonance-seeds.md` §4.3) — but
> under the vault, the *stored* seed is order held for an eventual plant, **never a
> withdrawable battery**. The vault and the find-economy's burn-line are strictly separate:
> a vault preserves order; it never mints, and it never consumes its charges as fuel.

This is the doc's load-bearing cap, stated once: **a seed is order, not power — a vault
preserves, never mints.** The stewardship decision (§4.1) is therefore not a wealth
decision (no stored fortune), it is a *priorities* decision (which orders are worth the
ongoing hold) — which is precisely what makes the vault's curation the corpus's deepest.

### 4.3 A seeded window's vault is the birth-order's own archive

Because every window is a planted seed (`resonance-seeds.md` §2.3, `world-seams.md` §3.2),
a vault in a fresh or young window holds that window's **birth-order's own lineage** — the
first condensation's deepest store, kept against the drift that will eventually make it
un-realizable. The steward who vaults a window's first found seed is, in effect, **archiving
the window's own origin** — the same preservation act as the birth-strata it was drawn from
(`resonance-seeds.md` §1.1), made deliberate and portable instead of incidental. This is the
vault's most valuable keep: not a rare unknown seed, but **the window's own genesis, held**
so that when the founding field drifts, the origin is still plantable to replay it.
**[design]** — this is the origin-story reading of the vault, consistent with
`resonance-seeds.md` §4.4.

## Open questions

1. **The vault's hold vs. in-situ hold.** A seed held in the vault is removed from the
   birth-strata (the reaping cost of `field-archaeology.md` §5.2) — is a vaulted seed's
   Π pattern held *as stably* as the same seed left un-reaped in situ (the birth-strata's
   own deep coherent well, `resonance-seeds.md` §1.1), or permanently less, the way a
   re-authored house's core can be permanently below a fresh build (`house-that-steers.md`
   open-Q4)? Determines whether vaulting is a preservation or always a loss, however held.
   **[design]**
2. **The capacity bound — how much a vault can hold.** Is a vault's capacity (how many
   seeds at what depth) a [design] dial — a deliberate curation pressure — or does it follow
   from the deep-rung maintenance (a finite coherence budget can hold only so many orders
   above their `q_dissolve`, `energy-harnessing.md` §4.4's draw)? The stewardship decision
   (§4.1) is honest only if the bound is real. **[design]** — the house's §5b calibration
   applied to capacity.
3. **The held seed's own drift.** A seed's order, even held at `q ≈ 0.947` and `ε²≈0`, sits
   under the attractor's slow pull on the field's own rung clock (`qi-as-time-clock.md`, the
   mixing clock `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]`, the winding bound `|δn| ≤ 0.162`). Does a vaulted seed
   *bleed* its deepest order over very long holds (a partial, honest loss even when
   maintained), or is the hold at the true deepest rung stable effectively forever as long
   as `q` stays above `q_dissolve` (`qi-computation.md` §5.2)? Sets whether "deepest order
   preserved" is literally forever or a very-long-but-finite hold. **[design]/[probe]**
4. **The conservation-for-its-own-sake choice.** Is vaulting a seed with no intention to
   plant — keeping the window's order for the *record* — a legitimate conservation, or must
   a held seed eventually be spent (planted or traded) for the vault to be an honest
   economy? The archaeology-conservation default (read in place, keep the region healthy,
   `field-archaeology.md` §5.3) and the vault's escape-cargo tie (§5) pull toward the former;
   the no-free-energy bound (§4.2) pulls toward the latter (a kept order with no use is a
   permanent net-negative hold). **[design]**
5. **Inherited provenance (§4.3).** A re-homed or newly-founded window inherits pre-window
   artifact residue (`field-archaeology.md` §4.3) — does its vault preserve the *carried*
   orders (the seed of the window it came from) or only the *founded* order? Depends on
   `resonance-seeds.md` open-Q5 and the unresolved window-relocation policy
   (`async-field-domain.md` §7 Q1, owned by `world-seams.md` §4.2), exactly as archaeology's
   §6c flags. **[design]** — later, with the relocation policy.
