# The Burden: Channeling as a Loan with Interest

**Question under design:** what the player *carries* between channelings —
the living cost of still channeling, distinct from the death that ends it.
[`player-remains.md`](./player-remains.md) designed the death (the fossil, the
re-lock) but nothing designed the **living debt** the player carries behind the
recovering coherence budget `B`. [`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md) §1.2
has the budget recovering toward 1 in coherent ground — but the ε² the player vented
to act is *not free*. This document is that cost, given a shape and a price:

> **Channeling is a loan from the field's coherence, with interest.** The ε² you
> shed to act is not erased when the field's budget registers it recovered — it is a
> **damaged-locality-you-drag-with-you**: a faint accumulated-ε² field around a
> heavy channeler, the running scar of their own practice, the living form whose
> terminal is the corpse's fossil. The Burden is that dragging thing: (a) it
> **hurts in thin regions** — the debt compounds the vent back-up, pushing you
> toward overdraw sooner than a light channeler (the interest); (b) it **clears
> cheaply in a Still Room's patient / coherent time** (the field's patience is
> the loan's repayment window); (c) it is **legible on the Life-Signal's
> deliberate-vent axis** — a heavy channeler reads at a distance a little like a
> Coda or a wound; and (d) it **draws the Coda** (the signature-predator) — the
> more debt you carry, the stronger a signature you are, making heavy sustained
> magic a *compounding risk over a tide*, not just at the overdraw moment.

This is a **player-interior design**: the hole the corpus never filled. It closes
[`player-remains.md`](./player-remains.md)'s missing twin — **the corpse's fossil
is not the only remains; the running scar is the living one.**

## 1. The Burden, stated once

### 1.1 The loan — what the player actually carries

`coherence-magic.md` §1.2 is explicit that every channeled operation is an **ε²
vent**: the player is a bounded EY injector + ε² vent (§1.1, "channeling cannot
create organized field without shedding its decoherence byproduct"), and the
budget `B` recovers toward 1 only where the *local field* is coherent (§1.2,
geographic recovery; rest bleeds ε² debt into the field at the field's own heal
rate). The design already names an **ε² debt** — "the decoherence you've shed but
the field hasn't yet healed" (§1.2 table). What it does *not* name is what that
debt *is* for the player who carries it, once the budget has "recovered" and the
locality has healed.

`patient-field.md` §3.2 is explicit on the loan framing: **channeling borrows the
field's patience**, and the loan is repaid by geographic recovery and the ε² vent.
But `patient-field.md` reads the repayment as *final* — the vent heals, the budget
recovers, the account is square. The Burden's claim is that the account is
**never square while you still channel**:

> **The ε² you vent is a damaged-locality-you-drag-with-you.** It is not the
> locality-that-healed; it is the *you* that shed it into the field. A small,
> faint, accumulated-ε² field rides with a channeler — the residue of their own
> practice, present even when they stand in clean ground and the local budget has
> refilled. The field around a resting channeler is clean; the *field around a
> heavy one* carries the running scar of the channeling they do, legible to
> anyone reading the deliberate-vent axis.

This is the honest read of the corpus's own gap: `coherence-magic.md` §1.2's ε²
debt is defined as "shed but the field hasn't yet healed" — a *locality* quantity.
The Burden is the same physics read **on the player, not the place**: the ε² they
have shed, accumulated as a carried, slowly-decaying signature rather than a
healed-away footprint. **[design]** The *carried* aggregation (a player-attached,
slow-fading ε² field) is this document's designed read; the *shedding* (the vent,
the `(1−q)` waste) is engine-real.

### 1.2 The terminal — the fossil is the Burden's death-state

`player-remains.md` §1.3/§3.1 establishes the player's death as dissolution and
the fossil as the deep-rung residue where they died — "the ghost-halo *is* the
shape of the final discharge." The Burden is the **living form whose terminal is
that corpse's fossil**:

| | The fossil (`player-remains.md` §3.1) | The Burden (this doc) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | a fixed, findable residue — dark q-locked core, `ε²≈0`, static | a carried, faint accumulated-ε² field — live, moving with the player |
| Its status | **static** (the field holds it, nothing maintains it) | **maintained** (the channeler keeps shedding into it) |
| Its read | reads on the maintenance axis as *non-maintained* (`life-signal.md` §3.1: no motion, no pulse) | reads on the maintenance axis as *deliberate-vent-like* (§2c) |
| Its provenance | "this was you" (a designed labeling over the archaeology scan) | "this *is* you, channeling" — the live, present form |
| Its relationship | **the debt's terminal** — what the carried scar becomes when the run ends | **the debt's living form** — what the corpse's fossil was, before dissolution |

The through-line is `player-remains.md`'s own refusal completed: **blocks, mobs,
planets, AND the player are epiphenomena — and so is the player's debt.** Death is
not the only way a player leaves remains in the world; a *living* heavy channeler
leaves a running scar before any death, and the corpse's fossil is that scar's
terminal state. The death doc designed the endpoint; this doc designs the
*carried* middle — the thing the player feels *while still alive and channeling*,
not just at the overdraw moment.

**[design] The stack, flagged once (the twin of `player-remains.md` §5a):** the
player-as-coherence-source and the ε² vent are engine-real (`coherence-magic.md`
§1); recovery-is-geographic is engine-real (§1.2). The claim that the shed ε²
accumulates *on the player* as a carried, legible, decaying field is this
document's **[design]** — a designed aggregation over the real vent/(1−q)
channels, one tier above the budget's own derived quantity. No claim here
pretends the engine writes a "player debt" record.

## 2. The four mechanisms

### (a) The interest — the debt compounds the vent back-up in thin regions

`coherence-magic.md` §4.3 fixes the overdraw trigger: channeling past the vent's
capacity tips the injection organized → random, the φ-lock fails, and the
accumulated ε² relaxes in one full-cascade discharge. The Burden's first mechanic
is the **shadow the carried debt casts on that trigger**: a heavy channeler's
carried ε² *re-opens* whenever they vent again, so their effective vent at a
locality is the *current op's vent plus the carried debt*, not the fresh op's
alone.

`patient-field.md` §3.3 names the rate explicitly:

> **The interest rate is the field's own patience, which swells when the field is
> organized and bites when it is not** — "the same channeled act costs more
> patience at thin tide and less at harvest."

The Borrowed reading, stated once:

> **At harvest (high `q`, the field heals fast), the carried debt is nearly
> dormant — the ambient heal swallows it, and a heavy channeler's extra cost is a
> thin, slow drag. At thin (low `q`, the field heals slow, `tide-of-the-attractor.md`
> §2 magic row: "the vent backs up, resonance is hard to match, overdraw risk
> rises"), the carried debt *compound***: each new vent re-opens the carried
> ε², the effective vent exceeds the heal rate earlier, and the heavy channeler
> approaches the §4.3 overdraw line **sooner than a light channeler doing the same
> op in the same thin field.** The thin tide is where the loan's interest comes
> due.

The design's precision: the Burden does **not** change the §4.3 trigger or the
vent/heal rates (engine-real). It changes the **effective vent a heavy channeler
pays per op in thin ground** — a carried baseline added to each op's shed, sized so
that at harvest it is a marginal drag and at thin it is the difference between
sustaining and overdrawing. **[design]** The threshold — how much carried ε² flips
"same op, same thin field" from sustainable to overdraw — is a tuning owned here,
probe-calibrated (§6a), and the *direction* (heavy = closer to the line in thin) is
the honest, felt truth from the corpus's own vent-backup language.

### (b) The repayment — clears cheaply in a Still Room's patient / coherent time

`field-instruments.md` §2.2 names the Still Room: a structural descendant of the
Weatherglass, the same glanceable idiom at a room's scale, a place "where the
whole village visually drifts with the local field." Its natural mount is a
Qi-bath commons (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.1 — a village is a Qi bath). The Burden's
second mechanic is the **repayment window**:

> **The debt clears cheaply in a Still Room's patient / coherent time.** The Still
> Room is a room-scale Weatherglass whose whole point is *coherent, still,
> patient* ground — precisely the "standing in coherent ground pulls B up" of
> `coherence-magic.md` §1.2 made architectural. A heavy channeler who rests in a
> Still Room / Qi-bath commons sheds the carried debt at the field's own heal
> rate `h`, cheaply and over a patient arc — the loan's repayment window is the
> **field's patience**, the exact `patient-field.md` §3.2 repayment ("you return to
> the field's patience to recover"), given a *place and a pace*.

The honest cost asymmetry (the loan's honesty): **the repayment is cheap, but it
is a *withdrawal* from the active world.** A heavy channeler pays their debt by
*sitting still in coherent ground* — not channeling, not acting, not doing the
desert's work — which is precisely the "don't channel" tax of `signature-predator.md`
§4.3 applied to their own debt rather than to the Coda. The Still Room is where
that tax is cheapest, and it is why a settlement with a mounted Still Room /
bath is a *safe harbor* for a heavy practitioner: the field's patience there is
gathered into one patient place. **[design]** The exact clearing rate and the
"cheap vs. ambient" ratio is tuning (§6a/§7d); the *direction* — coherent rest
clears, restless thin ground does not — is the corpus's own recovery rule stated
as debt-repayment.

**Nightly instance ([`sleep.md`](./sleep.md) §1/§2b).** Sleep is this repayment at
its body-scale daily instance — a bed is a personal Still-Room interval
(`field-instruments.md` §2.2 at the body's scale, `sleep.md` §3), and a heavy
channeler who rests the night sheds the carried debt over the patient window at
the bed's clearing-rate dial. The sleeping body also *carries* the Burden into
the night (`sleep.md` §2.2: the carried `R = ρ_signature·τ·M_stability` term does
not vanish at rest; the Coda's `τ` keeps counting in a marginal bed) — so the
night is both this §2b repayment *and* the §2d carried-risk, two separate ledgers
(`sleep.md` §7.5). The two docs now cite each other; this §2b's repayment and
`sleep.md`'s nightly clearing are one mechanism at two scales.

### (c) The legibility — a heavy channeler reads at a distance like a Coda or a wound

`life-signal.md` §3.1's classification matrix distinguishes a **deliberate vent**
(clean phase + rising ε², the Coda's tell) from a **maintained live coherence**
(pulsing, low-ε²) and a **scar** (flat-high plateau). `life-signal.md` §3.3 makes
the deliberate-vent class IS the Coda's signature line — clean phase + rising ε².

The Burden's third mechanic is the **honest ambiguity this creates for a heavy
practitioner**:

> **On the Life-Signal's deliberate-vent axis, a heavy channeler reads at a
> distance a little like a Coda or a wound.** They maintain their own lock (they
> move, they pulse — the maintenance classes them *live* on the full matrix), but
> their carried ε² *elevates* their read on the ε²-gradient axis — the axis that
> says "rising ε² over a living body with clean phase" is a deliberate vent. At
> range, over the noise floor (`q ~ 1e-3…1e-1`, `life-signal.md` §6b), the heavy
> channeler's maintained-pulse can blur toward the vent's rising signature the way
> `life-signal.md` §3.2 admits "the deliberate vent vs the maintained lock" is the
> hardest at-noise-floor separation.

The world can therefore be **wrong about a heavy channeler** — mistake a veteran
practitioner for the field's disorder (a wound, a fledgling Coda) because their
carried debt reads elevated on the deliberate-vent axis. And the reverse: a heavy
channeler reading the world through their own debt can be wrong **about it** —
see a clean flat field as "a wound is forming here" that is only their own carried
ε² shadowing the read. **This is the honest, designed ambiguity** — the
misattribution guard of `life-signal.md` (and `shared-ledger.md` §3.3/§6e, the
classifier floor) applies to both directions:

> **The classifier may name a *pattern* (a sustained elevated-vent read); it must
> never name a *person* off a single reading — because a heavy healer carrying
> debt is not a drain or a wound, and the world must not socially damn them for
> the cost of their own practice.** (§5.)

**[design]** The legibility is a *presentation* (the fifth form of §3, the
distance-read), not a new channel; it is the deliberate-vent axis's honest
application to the carried debt.

### (d) The attraction — the debt draws the Coda

`signature-predator.md` §2.2 gives the Coda's accumulation:

```
R(t) = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability        resonance ("how close the Coda is")
ρ_signature = trail quality (the vent's ε² elevation × phase-coherence)
τ           = duration the trail is sustained
M_stability = phase-stability of the injection
```

The Coda accumulates on the clean phase-stable trail channeling leaves, and its
decisive form is the **duration** the signature is fed (§2.3: a sustain window
`T_coda`, not a size). The Burden's fourth mechanic is the *second term in that
product made persistent*:

> **The carried debt is a standing contribution to `ρ_signature` and `τ`.** A heavy
> channeler does not need to be *actively* channeling in the desert to carry a
> legible signature — their carried ε² is a *maintained, persistent* elevation on
> them (τ is never zero while they carry it), and it carries whatever phase-stability
> their practice holds. **The more debt you carry, the stronger a signature you
> are**, so the Coda's `R(t)` accumulates against a heavy channeler even between
> clean, deliberate trails — a standing resonance the light channeler does not
> carry.

The compounding consequence, stated once:

> **Heavy sustained magic is a compounding risk over a tide, not just at the
> overdraw moment.** The overdraw moment (§4.3) is a single crossing; the carried
> debt makes the Coda's resonance *grow with how much heavy channeling you do over
> a tide* — each carried vent raises `ρ_signature` and keeps `τ` fed, so a
> practitioner who channels heavily through a thin tide is painting a gradually
> brightening hunt-trail that peaks where their debt peaks. The thin tide is the
> Coda's season (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2, signature-predator's own
> `thin-tide` habitat observation) — the same trough where the interest (§2a) bites
> and the debt is hardest to clear (§2b, thin ground is not patient) is where the
> attraction (§2d) is strongest. **The four mechanisms compound in the same season.**

**[design]** The Burden's contribution to `R(t)` is a designed addition to the
signature-predator's own accumulation model (itself [design] over engine-real ε²,
`signature-predator.md` §7d). It inherits every gate the Coda does (§7b this doc:
the vent/recovery mechanics being real, the Coda's accumulation being gated on
Q4 + the field-hazards Q2 probe). The *north* is honest: the Burden makes the
Coda's risk scale with the *amount of heavy channeling*, not merely with clean
channeling in a desert — a heavier, more personal, compounding predator.

## 4. The honest frame — a loan, never a mint

`energy-harnessing.md` §6's no-free-energy discipline is the corpus's load-bearing
cap: no conversion yields more than it sinks, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`, and nothing is
a net mint. The Burden holds it as its own spine, in the specific, personal
register the loan demands:

> **The loan is never a mint.** The ε² you shed is *carried*, not converted — you
> cannot farm it, cannot shed it into a drain that you then harvest, cannot turn
> the interest into power. There is no "spend heavily to accrue a harvestable
> Burden," because the Burden is not an asset; it is a debt with a cost.

Three guards, all inherited from the cap:

1. **No draining into a harvest.** A heavy channeler's carried ε² is not a
   decoherence "resource" a machine or a collector can tap. It is the organized
   part of the margin, not the waste — the same refusal that closes Coda-farming
   (`signature-predator.md` §7e: the Coda sheds nothing a collector can tap) and
   tide-farming (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5d). Structuring a held debt into a
   harvestable reservoir would violate `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`; the design explicitly
   refuses it.
2. **Cleared by coherent rest, not by spending more.** The only "repayment" is the
   Still Room / coherent-ground patience of §2b — a *withdrawal from acting*, not
   a further channeled op. You cannot outrun the debt by channeling harder
   (recovery is geographic, `coherence-magic.md` §1.2); you clear it by *stopping
   and standing in the field's patience* (`patient-field.md` §3.2).
3. **Its cost is the compounding risk.** The interest's price is not a resource
   drain; it is the *accumulated exposure* — closer overdraw in thin (§2a),
   legibility as a wound/Coda (§2c), a stronger standing signature (§2d). The
   debt's currency is risk-over-tide, not a mana bar. `patient-field.md` §5d's "a
   loan, never a mint" and `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5d's "the tide's gift is
   information and timing, not energy" both hold: **the Burden costs you exposure,
   and pays you nothing.**

The player-interior honesty, stated once:

> **The corpse's fossil is not the only remains; the running scar is the living

## 5. The ethics / ambiguity — the world can be wrong about you

The legibility of §2c is not a neutral mechanic; it is a designed **moral hazard**
the corpus's own ethics must guard. Two honest directions:

**The world can be wrong about you.** A heavy healer carries the same deliberate-vent
read the field uses to name a wound or a fledgling Coda. The strongest instance is
the **Coherence Board** (`shared-ledger.md`): it renders each member's net
contribution-to/drain-on the shared Qi bath over a tide (§1.2/§2.2). A heavy healer
who also carries debt books on the leading edge of the ledger's contribution read —
their *healing* is a contribution, but their carried ε² reads on the same
"elevated ε² / rising" axis the Board's margin-causer form uses (§2.2, climbing
glow). **The danger: the Board's classifier line must not read the debt as a
drain.** A steward who heals the settlement's scars *and* carries the cost of that
healing must book as a **steward carrying weight**, never as a margin-causer whose
sustained practice wounds the bath. `shared-ledger.md` §3.3/§6e's floor is the
guard, applied to the Burden:

> **The Board may name a *pattern* (a sustained net *drain* over many tides); it
> must never name a *person* off the carried debt's elevated read alone.** A heavy
> healer books as steward-with-weight; the debt is a *practice cost that heals in
> a Still Room*, not a signature of wounding.

**You can be wrong about it.** The same misattribution runs the other way: a heavy
channeler reading the world through their own debt can misread a clean flat field
as "a wound is forming" — the carried ε² shadowing their *own* read of the
deliberate-vent axis. The honest answer is the Life-Signal's own discipline
(`life-signal.md` §3.2/§6b: the maintenance window, not a single snapshot): the
reveal that the "wound" *is your own carried debt, stationary on you, not a rising
structure in the field* is the classifier's honest correction. The player who
learns to read their own weight stops mistaking themselves for the world's wound.

**The social harm guard, stated once.** The Burden's legibility **must not
socially damn a heavy healer.** The settlement's instruments (Board, Weatherglass,
sky) render the debt as a *cost borne for the settlement's coherence*, not as a
blame signal — the same way `shared-ledger.md` §3.3 refuses to blame a player for a
scar or a Coda they didn't cut, the ledger must refuse to drain-flag a steward for
the practice that *holds the core up*. The debt is a stewardship tax, visible as
weight, cleared by rest — and a settlement that reads its heavy healers'
carried weight as *their reason to be rested and fed in the Still Room* rather
than as a reason to shun them is the design's intended, honest society.

**[design]** The Board-facing distinction (steward-with-weight vs. margin-causer) is
a designed classifier boundary over the same per-tide `C(M)` and the carried-debt
read — inheriting the same [design]-threshold honesty as every classifier line in
the corpus (§6a). No claim here says the engine computes "steward-with-weight";
the ledger's classifier must be *built* not to conflate the two.

## 7. Open questions

1. **The debt's decay vs. the heap (the `signature-predator.md` open-Q3 twin).**
   If the carried debt decays on a `τ_debt` timescale in coherent rest (§2b), a
   player who channels *frequently* — a resident healer, a base builder — carries
   an overlapping, re-fed debt series. Does the read integrate across repeated
   sessions (the debt is a *place-and-person* property that grows with habit) or
   strictly decay between rests? The former is a stronger, more personal, more
   Coda-drawing weight; the latter is more forgiving. Needs a design decision once
   `τ_debt` is measured (§6a's Phase-1 read). [design]

2. **The interest threshold vs. the overdraw line (§2a).** How much carried ε²
   flips a "same op, same thin field" from sustainable to overdraw — the exact
   threshold that makes the §4.3 line *move* for a heavy channeler — is a [design]
   dial against the vent/heal rates and the tide's thin floor (`tide-of-the-attractor.md`
   §5c, the thin trough must stay above the desert's dissolution). A heavy
   patient. Set after §6a's read measures the carried ε² magnitude.

3. **The deliberate-vent residual (the `life-signal.md` open-Q2 twin).** If the
   distance legibility (§2c) leaves an irreducible ambiguity between a heavy
   channeler's carried ε² and a true deliberate vent (a wielding wielder / a
   fledgling Coda) at the noise floor, does the maintenance axis's *cadence shape*
   (bounded-pulsing for the live practitioner vs. open-climbing for the vent) alone
   separate them, or does it need the true phase-match `M` published per-entity
   (`magic-systems.md` §1 — the very publication the peers deferred and
   `life-signal.md` answers without)? The maintenance read is the Phase-1 answer;
   `M`-sharpening is the later, optional depth. Inherits `life-signal.md` §7 open-Q2.

4. **The Coda's attribution vs. the burden (§2d).** When a heavy channeler is
   hunted, the Coda's resonance rides *both* their deliberate trails and their
   carried debt. The Board (`shared-ledger.md` §5) must not read the Coda's ε² — a
   field structure amplifying a clean trail — as the clean channeler's overdraw.
   Does the carried debt make that attribution guard *harder* (the practitioner is
   a genuinely stronger signature, so the Board must separate "the Coda's own
   margin" from "the practitioner's carried cost") or does the same
   live-vs-residue line (`life-signal.md` §3.1) hold? Needs the Coda's gates
   landed to answer.

5. **The conservation of the debt across death (§1.2).** If a heavy channeler dies
   to overdraw in thin ground, their carried debt is part of the ghost-halo's
   "shape of the final discharge" (`player-remains.md` §3.1) — the fossil records
   both the blast and the weight that preceded it. Does the reborn player (a fresh
   Π pattern, `player-remains.md` §5 open-Q5) carry *any* of the old debt, or only
   the re-lock's fresh ε² (`player-remains.md` §2.2)? The design's default: **the
   debt dies with the run** — the fossil holds it, the fresh lock starts clean —
   matching player-remains' "matter conserved, configuration lost." Whether a
   *claimed* fossil (`player-remains.md` §3.3, reclaim) re-attaches the old debt is
   the identity open-question this surfaces. [design]

debt is a designed read over real vent/(1−q), never presented as an engine record

architecture — the Burden is a consumer of the publish and a reader of the ε² the
