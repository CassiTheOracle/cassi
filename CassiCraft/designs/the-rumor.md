## 2. The told-unverified — the honest mechanics

legible state**. Each leg composes a landed channel; the *composition* — which
circumstances birth a rumor, how it is carried, how a verdict reads — is [design].

### 2.1 The birth — from a real misread, a partial read, a gap in the read

The rumor does not appear from nothing. Its honest birth is one of three, each a real
read of the published channels:

| The birth | What actually happened | The channel it mis-states | Landed in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A real misread** | an instrument's partial read is taken for a settled one — the compass's needle points at a dense site ([the compass](./the-compass.md) §3.3 — the *meaning* of the direction is [design], the ambiguity inherited) and a player tells "something is forming out at the ridge" before the provenance read settles *what* is forming | the intent-channel / the dense site | `the-compass.md` §3.3/§4a; `weather-not-storm.md` §1 (the provenance discipline) |
| **A partial read** | a read that is *true but thin* is told as *settled and whole* — a half-skimmed Weatherglass ([`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md) §1.2 — the glanceable idiom is a read, not a verdict) becomes "the core is turning" before the Observatory reads the whole window as one body | `q`/`(1−q)` — the glanceable channels | `field-instruments.md` §1.2; `the-observatory.md` §2 |

### 2.2 The carrying — voices, NPCs, the partial instruments

The rumor is carried by the corpus's real carriers — never by a new "gossip engine."
Three carriers, all landed:

- **Players.** A player reads a partial instrument, forms a claim, tells it — in the

- **The partial instruments themselves.** The instruments do not *say* a rumor, but a

**[design]** The *carrying mechanics* — how often, how far, at what cost a claim

## 3. The load-bearing design — the rumor as the human layer over the legible field

1. **The unverified claim is honest to design because the field is the referee, not

## 4. The load-bearing honesty

### 4.1 The field cannot lie — the deception is the human/instrument layer

[`the-dispute.md`](./the-dispute.md) §2 is the whole ground, held absolutely:

> **The field cannot lie the way a witness can — a rumor is never the field
> mis-stating itself; it is PEOPLE (or misread instruments) mis-stating the field.**
> The field is deterministic ([`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §6d) and the read
> is honest ([`shared-ledger.md`](./shared-ledger.md) §6d), so a "rumor the field
> whispered" is a false framing — the corpus's own dispute doc sheds it (§2). The
> deception, where there is one, lives in the interpretation layer: a partial read
> taken for a settled one, a silent line filled by a claim, a true read turned into a
> wrong one. The field states; the teller mis-states; and the read resolves.

The hardest honest consequence, stated once: **there is no such thing as the field
lying to you.** Every rumor in this design is a *telling about* the field, never a
>*telling of* the field. A claim that reads as "the field hid X" is itself a false
>rumor — the field hides nothing (`the-observatory.md` §6e's accessibility).

### 4.2 A rumor never mints — the no-free-energy cap

[`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §6 (`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`), inherited
through the whole settled-telling stack ([`the-story.md`](./the-story.md) §6d, the
chronicle §6d, the dispute §6d, the echo §6d), held as a hard bound:

> **A rumor informs, never mints — no rumor that pays.** Spreading a rumor is a real
> act with a real cost: the telling (a Q4 op the book can hold), the attention (the
> listeners' own coherence, spent on the claim), and the hearing. No rumor that "paid"
> its teller, that farmed attention or order from nothing, is a true rumor the field
> sheds — a claim that yielded a competence, a re-raise, or a recovery it did not earn
> in the field is a false naming, and it fades (`the-story.md` §2.1, whole). The rumor's
> *only* honest output is information — a told claim that either books (true) or sheds
> (false) at the read. Meaning is information, never energy.

Three honest consequences, mirroring the corpus: **no rumor that yields** (there is no
rumor whose spreading produces a gain); **no false rumor to sell** (a false claim sheds,
so a settlement cannot mint a past, a threat, or a recovery it did not live); and **no
rumor that mints a next run's craft** (a rumor teaches nothing the read does not hold —
its resolution is the settlement's own read, not the claim's invention).
