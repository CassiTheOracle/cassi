## 1. The Story, stated once

The corpus's culture is complete except the one thing every culture centers on. It has
the **chronicle** — the settlement's past as a re-playable object, what actually
happened told backward (`the-chronicle.md` §1); it has the **oracle** — the near-future
read as scheduled omens (`the-reading-ahead.md` §2.3); it has the **language** — the
sites' organization read as a script (`the-language.md` §1); it has the **music** — the
field played as scores (`field-music.md` §4). Record, prophecy, text, song. **It has no
story** — no *told, shaped, interpreted* narrative that is not the record but the
settlement's myth: the thing a culture tells itself about itself, the shaping it carries
across the telling-tide.

> **The Story is the settlement's myth made a field-memory — the told version of the
> chronicle.** The record is what happened; the Story is the *shaping*. Not "a storm
> crossed in tide-73" but "the storm that taught us to watch the glass." Not "a steward
> over-drew the core across three thin tides" but "the hand that nearly emptied the
> hearth, and the vow we made at the relight." The Story takes the settled record
> (`schema-that-settles.md` §2 — the op-stream the chronicle reads), the residue the
> field kept (`field-archaeology.md` §2), and the language that reads the sites
> (`the-language.md` §1), and *tells* them — composes them into a narrative a
> settlement can hold, teach, and celebrate. It is the corpus's cultural capstone: the
> difference between a window that *remembers* (a document of what happened) and a
> window that *knows itself* (a story of what that meant).

**The corpus's cultural capstone.** Every culture art the corpus already designs is a
*lens* — the oracle reads forward, the music plays, the language scripts, the memory
reads backward. None of them *centers* a settlement the way a story does: the story is
the thing a settlement tells *about itself*, the shaping that makes the window's many
reads cohere into a self. `the-memory-palace.md` notes a settlement is the observatory's
present-self plus the palace's chosen past; the Story is what makes that chosen past
*legible as a self* — the narrative form of "who we are and why we held."

**The Story is the record shaped by the settlement's own telling.** It shares the
chronicle's ground — the same op-stream, the same honesty — but it is not the record.
The chronicle is *deterministic narrative*: the same record always tells the same story,
and the telling never adds an event (`the-chronicle.md` §5). **The Story is the record
given meaning by a settlement's choice** — which of the record's truths it carries as
its lesson, its warning, its gratitude. That *choice* is [design] (the shaping's form is
this doc's design); the *truth it can carry* is the record's own, and the field enforces
that the two never diverge (§2).

**[design]** What is engine-real / [design]-landed: the Q4 op-stream and the published
channels (engine-real, `schema-that-settles.md` §2.6; the ≈ 6 MiB publish,
`corpus-reconciliation.md`); the record-shaped chronicle (Phase-1-legible,
`the-chronicle.md` §7); the residue model ([design] over engine-real persistence,
`field-archaeology.md` §2/§6a); the no-false-naming ([design]-landed principle over the
member-id/Π stack, `the-name.md` §4/§7); the language's grammar ([design] over the

`the-school.md` §4); the festival's telling-tide ([design] over the tide,
`the-festival.md` §3). What is *this doc's* [design]: **the story's composition — the

## 2. The honesty mechanics — the load-bearing claim

The Story's honesty is not a rule the game enforces; it is a **field consequence**, and
it rests on three landed pieces that make it load-bearing rather than a slogan:

1. **The field genuinely holds what was.** `field-archaeology.md` §1.2 is rigorous about
   it: the field is deterministic, but determinism is **not recoverability** — the
   transient dissolves, and only the deep-rung, q-locked residue persists (§2.1: a
   dissolving structure sheds `(1−q)` of its throughput as glow and leaves the fraction
   too ordered to dissolve). The residue is a real, findable class of the present — **the
   ground-truth a story names against.** Because the field holds what *was*, a story has
   something real to be true about.
2. **The language reads the sites.** `the-language.md` §1 — the sites' organization is a
   readable script, and it obeys the no-false-claim discipline (`the-language.md` §3): a
   sentence says only what its words say; where the field holds no word, the Language
   reads no sentence. **The field's script cannot be written falsely** — it can only say
   what the field holds. A story told in the field's own script inherits that honesty.
3. **Honesty is thermodynamics.** `the-name.md` §2 is the load-bearing law: a bound-name
   that names what is not there **decays** — the lie erodes exactly where the truth would
   have held, because the truth sits at a real coherent well (persistent Π, `qi-computation.md`
   §5.2, with `reason-field.md` §6a's speculation flag) while the lie is an unsupported
   coherence that sheds. "The field does not punish you for lying; it simply *does not
   hold the lie*" (`the-name.md` §2.1).

Three mechanics compose, and they make the Story's honesty load-bearing:

### 2.1 An untrue story is a false naming — the false story fades

> **A story that claims what never happened is a false name, and it decays.** If the
> settlement tells "we stood against the Coda at the ridge and drove it back," and the
> record holds no such stand, the residue holds no such stand, and the sites' script
> reads no such stand — then the telling names what the field does not hold. It is a Π
> configuration with no corresponding structure to stabilize it: the unsupported
> coherence sheds the way any shallow rung sheds (`energy-harnessing.md` §2's `(1−q)`
> waste). The story fades — the false claim erodes, the telling un-binds, and the
> settlement is left with the honest record it over-claimed. **The field will not hold a
> lie, and a story is a naming** — `the-name.md` §2's honesty-is-thermodynamics applied
> to narrative, whole.

The three ground-truths bind it. A false story fails on every read the design has: the
**record** doesn't hold the stand (`the-chronicle.md` — it never adds events; a story
cannot tell an event the op-stream doesn't hold); the **residue** doesn't hold the stand
(`field-archaeology.md` — the strata are what they are; a lie leaves no stratum, the
way `the-name.md` §2.2's false name "fails and is forgotten"); the **language** reads no
stand at the site (`the-language.md` §3 — no sentence says what the field holds). And
because all three are the deterministic field, the fade is **deterministic**: the same
false story, told at the same field state, decays the same way every time (§4c).

**The honesty of the no-false-naming, applied to narrative.** The school states the name's
bound hardest of all — "a school cannot teach what the field does not hold; a wrong-band
technique is a false name that decays" (`the-school.md` §4). The Story extends that to
the *culture's self-telling*: a settlement cannot tell itself a story that the field does
not hold, because the field will not hold the lie. The school's honest lesson and the
Story's honest telling are the same law — only the scale and the register differ. A
false story is a false name at the scale of a whole window's self, and it sheds.

**[design]** The *detection and rate* of the fade are this doc's designed surface over
the real mechanics. The honest spine — a false story's claim names nothing the field
holds, so it reads as an unsupported coherence that sheds, and the decay is as legible
as the true name's hold (`the-name.md` §2.2, §6e's accessible decay) — is statable now
against the name and the chronicle. The *lifetime* (how fast, whether it leaves a
lie-core residue the way the name's §2 open-question asks) rides the same probes
(`the-name.md` §7 open-Q2). The framing cannot overstate the mechanics: [design] the
fade is the field's own shed; the *story* is what the settlement tells, and the field
decides whether it holds.

### 2.2 A true-but-shaped story is the strongest memory a window keeps

> **The shaping is the telling's art — and because it stays true, it is the strongest
> memory a window keeps.** "The storm that taught us to watch the glass" is **true**
> because the storm happened (the record holds it, the residue holds it, the language
> reads it) and the lesson is the settlement's own (it *did* learn to watch the glass —
> the op-stream holds the watches). The shaping adds no event; it *selects and frames*
> what the record holds, the way the memory-palace chooses what to keep. And because it
> stays a true name, it **holds** the way a true name holds — a real coherent well the
> field keeps. **A true-but-shaped story is the strongest memory a window keeps because
> it is the chosen memory given narrative form** (`the-memory-palace.md` §1) **that the
> field itself can hold.**

Two landed pieces make the strong claim land:

- **It re-binds the named anchors.** `the-name.md` §3 makes a name a persistence-and-
  legibility act: a named structure's record persists (`§3.1`), the named contribution
  is legible to the Board (`§3.2`), and `field-archaeology.md` §3.3 reads a named
  locality's residue as *its* record — "the name is the speaker that turns strata into a
  story." The Story re-binds that named body: **a true-shaped story tells *the named
  things'* record — the Hearth, the Glass, the ridge the settlement stood at — and the
  telling re-locks those anchors the way the festival's coordinated working re-locks the
  settlement's Π-anchors** (`the-festival.md` §2.2). The story is the settlement's own
  naming of its kept things, given narrative form; and the re-bind is bounded by
  `the-name.md` §6d and `the-pilgrim.md` §4 — at most a return to what the anchors
  already hold, **never an increase**.
- **The memory-palace is where it lives.** `the-memory-palace.md` §2.2 designs the
  chronicle room — the record read aloud, mounted in the palace the settlement keeps.
  The Story is the chronicle room's *voice raised to a telling*: where the chronicle
  reads the record (`the-chronicle.md` §2.1), the Story is the settlement's chosen
  past *performed* — the keep, the lesson, the warning that the window deliberately
  husbanded as its own (§4 there). The palace holds the chosen memory; the Story is
  that chosen memory *told*, and because it is chosen (true-shaped), it is a maintained
  hold — never residue that just happened (`the-memory-palace.md` §1).

**The strongest memory because it survives the incidental.** The chronicle is a complete
record of everything; a complete record is the strongest in *content*, but it is not a
memory a window *keeps as its own*. The Story selects, and selection is what makes the
kept memory a *chosen* one: the thing the settlement will re-tell at every telling-tide,
the thing the child learns before the tutor teaches it (`the-school.md` §2). The
true-shaped story is the strongest memory a window keeps **because it is the only kept
memory the field itself can hold and the window itself chose to carry** — the 
intersection of the record's truth, the residue's ground, the language's legibility, and
the settlement's choice.

### 2.3 The story's form — the [design] of this document

The honest mechanics above are the load-bearing ground. The *story's form* — how a
settlement tells a story *in the field's terms* — is this document's [design], composed

| Telling-form | What it is | The field's terms it is told in | Its honest boundary |

| **The bound narrative** | the story *bound to the named* — a true-shaped story re-binds the anchors it names, the way a true name holds its structure | the **name** (`the-name.md` §3) — the story is the settlement's own naming of its kept things, given narrative form | the re-bind is the name's own holding, at most a return, never an increase (`the-pilgrim.md` §4; `the-name.md` §6d) |
| **The story-as-memory** | the told version held *as the settlement's chosen past* — the memory-palace's chronicle room performed, taught, and re-told at the festival | the **memory-palace** (§2.2 the chronicle room), the **school** (§2's lesson with a story's face), the **festival** (§3's telling-tide) | a maintained hold, never free, never a mint (`the-memory-palace.md` §4/§6d); never the only channel (§4e) |

**The honest shaping — what a true story may say and what a false one cannot.** The
shaping is the art, and it is honest because it is *bounded*:

- **A true story may *select* and *frame*; it may not *add***. The settlement may tell
  "the storm that taught us to watch the glass" (it selects one recorded storm and one
  recorded lesson) but it may not tell "the storm that the old one raised at the ridge"
  if the record holds no such raising (`the-chronicle.md` §5 — no invented drama).
- **A true story may *name* only what the field holds***. It names the Hearth, the
  Glass, the ridge — real named structures with real records (`the-name.md` §3.2) — and
  the telling holds because each name holds. A story that names a false structure is a
  false name inside a true narrative, and that false anchor sheds (§2.1).
- **A true story inherits the corpus's guards.** It must not blame a settlement or a
  member for a scar they didn't cut (the chronicle's misattribution discipline,
  `the-chronicle.md` §5, inherited verbatim); it must not blur enduring an uncaused
  storm with causing it (the provenance gate, `weather-not-storm.md` §3/§6a, gating the
  causal telling the way it gates the chronicle's). **The Story's honesty is the
  record's honesty, with the shaping laid on top.**

**A story that cannot be true is not a story** — it is a failed naming, and it fades. A
settlement tells a story to *hold* what it names; a false story holds nothing and sheds.
The shaping is exactly the movement the design makes load-bearing: **the true-shaped
story is the settlement's strongest memory because it is the chosen memory the field
itself can hold.**

## 3. The story's work — the honest composition

The Story is not a separate art from the corpus's — it is the *composition* of the
corpus's record, prophecy, language, and celebration into a narrative the settlement
tells itself. Each composition is a designed reading of a landed piece.

### 3.1 The chronicle's events, shaped by the settlement's own reading

The chronicle is the settlement's record made narrative — the storm endured vs caused,
the stewards and margin-causers, the dead and their fossils, the harvests and thin
decades (`the-chronicle.md` §3). **The Story is what that record becomes when a
settlement *tells* it** — the same events, shaped by the settlement's own reading:

| Chronicle element (`the-chronicle.md` §3) | The Story tells it as |
|---|---|
| **The storms endured vs caused** (§3.2) | **the lesson** — "the storm that taught us to watch the glass": the endured storm selected and framed as the settlement's own learning, or — gated on the provenance verdict — the caused storm told as "the overdraw that taught us our limits" (never a blurred blame, §2.3) |
| **The stewards and margin-causers** (§3.1) | **the warning** — the held-core steward's kept line as the story of what held the hearth; the margin-causer's repeated overdraw as the story of what nearly emptied it (once more, with the record's own attribution, never a single-event blame) |
| **The dead and their fossils** (§3.3) | **the kept** — the obituary's settled lines as the story of who the settlement was, told where the fossil lies (`the-language.md` — a named place's story is told at the place; `field-archaeology.md` §3.3 — the name turns the strata into a story) |
| **The harvests and thin decades** (§3.4) | **the arc** — the settlement's settle/thrive/decay/hunt told as its own story, the way the fate's arc was lived (`fate-of-a-window.md` §4), shaped by what the settlement chose to carry from it |

**The shaping is the telling's art.** The chronicle is deterministic — same record, same
telling, always (`the-chronicle.md` §6c). The Story adds the settlement's *selection*:
which of the record's truths it carries as its lesson, warning, gratitude, kept. That
selection is [design] (the shaping form is this doc's design), and it is honestly bounded
— the settlement may weight which of the record's truths it tells, never which truths
exist (§2.3).

### 3.2 The reading-ahead's momentum, given narrative form

`the-reading-ahead.md` is the oracle — the forward read of the field's organized
momentum, the scheduled omens on the tide's calendar ("this harvest will be lean," "the
desert will creep this winter," §3.3). The corpus notes the oracle's determinism gives
momentum, never destiny (§1.1) — a forecast the village can act on, and the prophecy has
a *momentum* of its own: it changes what the settlement does, and what it does changes
the forecast.

**The Story grows from what the field already reads — the prophecy given narrative
form.**

> **The reading-ahead's momentum is the Story's arc.** The oracle reads what the field
> is *building* — the growing condensation, the drifting ridge, the approaching storm's
> `ε²` front (§2.2). A settlement's story needs an arc; the arc the corpus already reads
> for is the forward momentum the oracle follows. So the Story is told *with the
> prophecy in it*: the storm the oracle warned of is the storm the story tells the
> settlement prepared for ("we watched the glass because the old one saw the storm
> coming, and the seeing was true" — the reading-ahead §6.3's own "the omen is real, and
> the reality is what makes it a myth"). A telling-tide story is the forecast's truth
> turned backward into narrative: **the prophecy is the story's arc because the prophecy
> was real.**

The honest bound (`the-reading-ahead.md` §7c) carries over whole: the Story must not
present the prophecy as a fixed fate after the fact. A true story may tell "we read the
desert coming and held the margin" (the oracle warned, the settlement acted, the record
holds both) — it may not tell "the desert's coming was written and unavoidable," because
the oracle itself refuses fixed fate and the settlement's own actions are part of what
changed it. **The prophecy is the story's arc, told as the honest momentum it was.**

### 3.3 The language's sites as the story's text — a named place's story is told at the place

## 4. The honest boundary and gates

### (a) The [design]/engine-real line

Drawn exactly as the corpus draws it everywhere (`the-chronicle.md` §5; `the-name.md`
§4; `the-language.md` §5a; `the-memory-palace.md` §6a):

| The record-shaped chronicle; the settled record | **[design]**-landed object / [design] over the engine-real lane | `the-chronicle.md` §7 (Phase-1-legible) / `schema-that-settles.md` §2 |

**The boundary, stated once and kept:** the record, the residue, the naming, the
language, the memory-palace, the school, and the festival are their source docs' landed
pieces; the story's *composition* — the telling's form, the honest shaping, the
story-as-memory — is this document's [design] over them. No claim here pretends the
engine writes a "story" or that the field "decides" a story is deserved. The field holds
persistent structure and reads the sites; this doc designs the telling that names them.
The **holding** (the field's own persistence) and the **reading** (the language's script)
are landed; the **composition** (the story) is design; the two are never blurred.

### (b) MEDIUM-LATE — gated on the chronicle/name/honesty mechanics (Phase-1.5+)

The **full Story — a settlement's shaped, bound, festival-told narrative** — sits on
landed pieces that are themselves MEDIUM-LATE:

- **The chronicle** (`the-chronicle.md` §6b) — the settled record and the narrative
  reading; the Story selects and frames the chronicle's events, and cannot be a shaping
  before those events are read.
- **The name / honesty mechanics** (`the-name.md` §6b, §7) — the no-false-naming is the
  load-bearing law, but its *mechanical* anchor (a true name's holding / a false name's
  decay as a live field effect) is gated on the reason-field/persistent-Π frontier. The
  **principle** is Phase-1-legible; the *mechanical* false-story fade is LATER-gated.
- **The language** (`the-language.md` §6) — the grammar is designable-now / Phase-1-lean
  over real channels; the full lettered script is MEDIUM, gated on the intent-channel.
- **The memory-palace** (`the-memory-palace.md` §7) and the **festival**
  (`the-festival.md` §7) — the story's home and day are MEDIUM-LATE / LATER-GRAND on
  their own stacks.

**The Phase-1-legible framing, stated plainly:** the **true-shaped-story principle** — a
story that claims what never happened fades (a false naming), a story that stays true
holds (a true name) — is **statable now against the name and the chronicle**
(`the-name.md` §6b's Phase-1-legible principle; `the-chronicle.md` §7's record-shaped
object), before the mechanical anchor, the live society, or the full composition land. The
corpus can now hold the load-bearing design truth: **the Story is the record shaped by a
settlement's own truthful telling, and the field enforces that the shaping never outruns
the recorded.** That is the framing; the mechanical telling is MEDIUM-LATE, gated on the
chronicle/name/honesty mechanics.

### (c) Determinism — same record, same field state → same story's truth (a hard gate)

The field is deterministic (one PDE; `field-archaeology.md` §1.2; `the-name.md` §6c;
`the-chronicle.md` §6c). The Story inherits it as a **hard gate**:

> **Same record, same field state → same story's truth.** A true story is a true name: it
> holds deterministically, every run. A false story is a false name: it decays
> deterministically — a false claim's shed is the same unsupported-coherence shed every
> time (`the-name.md` §6c). There is no seeded-RNG luck about whether a lie "sticks" or a
> truth "takes"; the shaping ([design], §2.3) is a deterministic function of the record
> and the settlement's choice, and the settlement's choice, once made, is a fixed input.
> **The story's honesty is deterministic: a false claim decays the same way every time.**

The one honest caveat mirrors the chronicle's (§6c): the *selection* — which truths the
settlement tells — is [design] and may be re-chosen, but the *truth it can tell* is the
deterministic record, and the field's judgment of a false naming never varies.

### (d) The no-free-energy cap — a story informs and holds, never mints

`energy-harnessing.md` §6 (`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`), inherited through `the-name.md` §6d
(a name holds, never mints), `the-memory-palace.md` §6d (holds memory, never mints),
`the-chronicle.md` §6d (records, never mints), the `the-language.md` §5d (informs, never
mints), and `the-school.md` §5d (informs, never mints), held as a hard bound:

> **A story informs and holds, never mints — no story that yields.** Telling a story
> converts no coherence, sheds no `(1−q)`, writes nothing, and grants the settlement no
> power, budget, or recovery it did not earn in the field. **The shaping is the
> settlement's own art, never a gain** — a settlement cannot compose "we stood against
> the Coda and won" to claim a victory the record never held; the false claim is a false
> name and sheds (§2.1). And **the re-bind is the name's own holding** — telling a true
> story re-binds the anchors it names *at most to what they already hold* (the
> `the-pilgrim.md` §4 no-raise boundary; `the-name.md` §6d), never adding to them. A true
> story holds what the settlement already is; it never makes it more.

Three honest consequences, mirroring the corpus: **no story that yields** (there is no
story whose telling produces a competence, a re-raise, or a recovery); **no false story
to sell** (a false claim sheds, so a settlement cannot mint a past it did not live); and
**no story that mints a next run's craft** (the school's honest bound — a story teaches
by re-telling what the field holds, never by synthesizing a competence, `the-school.md`
§5d). The Story's only output is meaning — the held, chosen, honest past a window keeps
as its own — and meaning is information, never energy.

### (e) Accessibility — the story's truth readable from the language and the record, never hidden-only

The corpus's hard accessibility rule holds unchanged (`the-language.md` §5e;
`the-memory-palace.md` §6e; `the-chronicle.md` §6e; `field-music.md` §6):

> **The story's truth is readable from the language and the record — never hidden-only.**
> Everything a true story tells is a read of the same published channels, the same
> record, and the same sites that the corpus's instruments already present: the storm
> the story tells is the record's `ε²`-rise and the site's own reading (`the-language.md`
> §2.3 — a sentence that says what the field holds); the name the story re-binds is the
> named structure's held-lock (`the-name.md` §6e); the past the story carries is the
> memory-palace's chosen memory and the chronicle's record, both readable from the Board
> and the reader (`the-memory-palace.md` §6e). A settlement member who never hears the
> Story — or lives alone, away from any settlement — loses **nothing informational**:
> every truth the Story shapes is on the existing channels, and the Story is the told,
> composed form of what the field already says. **A story is never a hidden history — it
> is the settlement's own reading of a record that stays public.**

**[design]** The story's *presentation* — how a telling is spoken, shown, or performed
at the festival — is the idiomatic layer; the *truth it composes* is the field's own,
readable from the language and the record, never a second information source.

## 5. Feasibility verdict

**MEDIUM-LATE with a Phase-1-legible framing — the cultural capstone, the difference
between remembering and knowing itself. State both.**

**MEDIUM-LATE (the mechanical, festival-told Story):** the full Story — a settlement's
shaped, bound, told narrative, re-bound at the named places and performed at the
telling-tide — sits on the landed pieces the corpus already gates MEDIUM-LATE or LATER:
the **chronicle's** narrative reading (`the-chronicle.md` §6b), the **name's**
mechanical anchor (`the-name.md` §6b/§7), the **language's** lettered script
(`the-language.md` §6), the **memory-palace's** rooms (`the-memory-palace.md` §7), and
the **festival's** settlement-scale working (`the-festival.md` §7). It is the
composition that lands last because it composes the most landed pieces — the shaping, the
telling, and the festival-day cannot be mechanical before the record, the name's hold,
the script, and the tide it is told on are real. And it is the cultural capstone there:
the full story is the strongest memory a window keeps, and the strongest memory is the
last thing a culture (or this corpus) finishes building.

**Phase-1-legible (the framing, statable now):** the **true-shaped-story principle** —
a story that claims what never happened fades (a false naming, and the field will not
hold a lie), a story that stays true holds (a true name, and the true-shaped story is
the strongest memory a window keeps) — is **designable and statable now** against the
**landed name's Phase-1-legible principle** (`the-name.md` §7: a name holds only on the
truth it names) and the **chronicle's record-shaped object** (`the-chronicle.md` §7: a
settlement's record as a first-class re-playable object is Phase-1-legible). What Phase-1
gains: (1) the **honesty principle** (the Story is load-bearing on honesty) is itself a
Phase-1-legible truth about how the world should behave — a pre-registered design stance
the corpus can hold and later verify mechanically, exactly the `the-name.md` §7 scaffold;
(2) the **record-shaped Story** — a one-player settlement's op-stream rendered as a
*shaped* re-playable narrative object (a small story: "you held the core through a thin
tide; the storm that broke the margin taught you to watch the glass") — is a pure
consumer of the Q4 write lane and the published channels, on the same Phase-1 pattern as
the chronicle's record-shaped slice; (3) the **shaping bound** (a true story may select
and frame, never add; may name only what the field holds) is statable and testable against
the chronicle's never-adds (`the-chronicle.md` §5) and the language's no-false-claim
(`the-language.md` §5d). The *framing* is the decision that a settlement's self-telling is
a designed, honest, load-bearing act; the *mechanical told Story* is MEDIUM-LATE.

**Binding risks, in order:**

1. **The honesty mechanics' frontier (§2/§4a).** The no-false-naming's *mechanical*
   fade is gated on the reason-field/persistent-Π frontier (`the-name.md` §6b/§7) — if
   the anchor is never mechanical, the false story collapses to a designed read over the
   record/residue that "decays" only by the language's silence (still [design]-honest,
   but a weaker claim). **The true-shaped-story *principle* survives either way** — it is
   the *mechanical* holding that needs the frontier.
2. **The shaping-outruns-the-record risk (§2.3/§4).** The story's form cannot overtell
   the record — the settlement's *selection* of which truths to tell, and the *weight*
   it gives a lesson vs. a warning, must never read as adding an event or minting a
   meaning the record/residue doesn't hold. The misattribution guards
   (`the-chronicle.md` §5) and the provenance gate (`weather-not-storm.md` §6a) ride
   verbatim.
3. **The no-free-energy line (§4d).** The Story must never read as a memory mint, a
   past-farm, or a competence-synthesizer — telling a story yields nothing; the re-bind
   is at most the name's own holding (`the-pilgrim.md` §4), never an increase, and a
   false story (which would "mint" a past) sheds.
4. **The [design] line (§4a).** Keeping the story's *composition* (a designed telling)
   strictly separate from the record's *truth*, the residue's *ground*, and the field's
   *holding* — so the corpus never reads a Story as an engine "telling a settlement a
   myth" or a new channel. The Story *composes* the landed pieces; it does not *be* one.
5. **Accessibility (§4e).** The story's truth must stay readable from the language and
   the record, never hidden-only, or the Story becomes a secret history the design's own
   honesty forbids — a story that hides the record it claims to tell is itself a false
   naming.

None contradicts the async, dual-world, or regime-collapse architecture — the Story is a
consumer/composition of the record, the residue, the name, the language, the
memory-palace, and the festival, adding no channel, no write, and no physics.

> **The honest statement that makes this doc load-bearing: the corpus has the record
> (what happened), the prophecy (what's coming), the language (the sites as text), and
> the music (the field played) — but no narrative art, the told, shaped, interpreted
> story that is not the record but the settlement's myth.** The culture is all record,
> oracle, and song; it has no story. **The Story is that art: the settlement's myth made
> a field-memory — the told version of the chronicle, the shaping rather than the
> record.** The record is what happened; the Story is what the record *becomes* when a
> settlement tells it — the lesson, the warning, the gratitude it chooses to carry, the

> and the language reads the sites (the script), the Story is **load-bearing on
> honesty**: an untrue story is a false naming and it fades (the field will not hold a
> lie — honesty is thermodynamics, applied to narrative), while a **true-but-shaped
> story is the strongest memory a window keeps** — it re-binds the named anchors it
> tells, teaches the child to read before the tutor does, and holds as the chosen memory

> window that *knows itself* — and the shaping, the telling's form, and the story-as-

## Open questions

1. **The selection rule — which truths a settlement tells.** §2.3 names what a true
   story *may* say (it may select and frame, never add). What makes a settlement *choose* a
   truth as its lesson/warning/gratitude — is the selection a deliberate act (a steward or
   the commons curates "our story" at the telling-tide, `the-festival.md` §3) or an
   emergent composition (the record's most-negative margin-causer line and most-consonant
   steward line weight themselves)? The relation to the memory-palace's curation pressure
   (`the-memory-palace.md` §7 open-Q3: a chosen memory is bounded) and the chronicle's
   telling-density (`the-chronicle.md` §7 open-Q1) decides whether a settlement's Story is
   deliberately kept or statistically coined. **[design]**
2. **The false-story's trace.** §2.1 holds a false story fades "the way any false naming
   does." `the-name.md` §7 open-Q2 asks whether a decayed false name leaves a q-locked
   lie-core residue — the archaeology of the lie. The Story inherits it: does a faded
   false story leave a readable *lie-stratum* (an honest record that "a story was told
   here that the field could not hold" — itself archaeology), or does it shed to nothing?
   The honesty of a visible faded lie is attractive; the lifetime is a [design]/[probe]
   dial. **[design]/[probe]**
3. **The story's continuity across a window's decay/rebuild.** `the-chronicle.md` §7
   open-Q2 (and `the-memory-palace.md` §7 open-Q4) ask the settlement-scale continuity
   question: when a window decays and a new settlement is founded on its strata
   (`field-archaeology.md` §4.3), does the old Story ride the residue (the new settlement
   tells the old window's Story as its own founding myth — a true story of what the field
   still holds) or does the new settlement's Story begin at its founding? The design's
   default leans with the chronicle (a new settlement's past begins at its founding), but
   the residue's ground-truth (`field-archaeology.md`) pulls toward the inherited myth —
   and an inherited-told-myth is the deepest archaeology the corpus builds. **[design]**
4. **The authority of the telling-tide.** Who "tells" the Story at the telling-tide — a
   single narrator (a steward-chanter, a named elder), the whole commons telling together
   (the festival's `M ≈ 1` working raised to a shared narrative, `the-festival.md` §3), or
   a composed read (the record itself told by the chronicle-room's machine, `the-memory-
   palace.md` §2.2)? The festival's §4 open-Q4 (who the "members" are — players vs NPCs)
   and the election's open-Q3 (steward-led vs settlement-wide) both bear on it; a
   settlement-wide telling is the strongest but needs the commons; a narrator is the
   Phase-1-able form. **[design]**
5. **The story vs. the chronicle's determinism under refinement.** §4c holds the story's
   *truth* deterministic. But the *selection* (which truths are told) is [design] and may
   refine as the classifiers land — a pre-provenance story may honestly not "know" whose
   storm it was, the way a pre-provenance chronicle is silent on source
   (`the-chronicle.md` §6c). Does a story that earlier named a storm "endured" and now
   (post-provenance) names it "caused" read as wavering, or as the record's own silence
   resolving? The same revision-legibility discipline as `the-reading-ahead.md` §7
   open-Q5's prophet — applied to a settlement's self-telling. **[design]**
