   another, the blight's wrong-band a third) — the announced read's *named-warning*
   form (`the-herald.md` §2) — or does the raised flame warn *danger* without

   honest default is the beacon's high-`q` mark and the balefire's raised `(1−q)`
   flame are the same edge's two registers — the balefire *is* the beacon's mark
   raised into alarm, never a second light. Whether a settlement may hold both

   *answer* it draws inherits the neighbor-systems' gates, exactly as the bell's
