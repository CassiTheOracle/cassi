# The Diving Bell of the Deep Field

**Question under design:** the one spatial axis the corpus leaves hollow — *down*.
Every vehicle in the corpus goes up (ships, rockets, the long dark between windows —
[`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md), [`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md)); nothing
designs the downward journey through the strata. Yet the corpus keeps pointing down:
archaeology reads a deep core sample but cannot safely reach the deepest residue
([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §5.3 #1 — "some residue is too
deep-rung to remove safely"); the deep-rung reaper scars the terrain it reaps
([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2.5); and the fire-vs-explosive
line makes a too-deep harvest a full-cascade blast ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md)
§3/§5). This document designs the object that answers all three with one craft:
**a rigid body sheathed in a Qi-bath core + a φ-detuned skin, that descends through
the strata carrying its own held coherence.** You do not rip the fossil out from
above — you descend to it inside a shell that refuses the surrounding disorder,
collect ordered matter at a controlled rate, and surface.

It is the **down-facing twin of the condensation-drive rocket**
([`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md) §2.2): the rocket injects EY to climb the
envelope's ρ-gradient; the Bell holds a detuned shell to sink through it and the
crust's ε². Where the rocket spends stored coherence *upward*, the Bell spends it
*holding a pocket of order around the operator* so the crushing ε² and the danger
of disturbing deep-rung order never reach them.

## 1. The down-direction — the hollow axis

**The corpus has an up and a sideways, not a down.** [`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md)
is a rocket pointed at the sky; [`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) is ships sailing
the long dark *between* windows; [`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md)
is the envelope you climb out of. Every field operation that moves matter does so
*by leaving the ground* — thrust, orbit, voyage. Nothing carries a player *into* the
strata as a designed act.

Yet the corpus's own economics keep terminating down there:

- **Archaeology reads a depth it cannot safely reach.** A "core sample" is a vertical
  `ρ`/`q` profile down through the strata — a *deeper-rung read* costing coherence
  budget ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §3.2) — and it resolves the
  deepest residue: the birth-strata (§2.4), and ultimately the **seed**, the deepest
  object a window owns ([`resonance-seeds.md`](./resonance-seeds.md) §1.1/§4.1). But
  archaeology's own ethics stop short of *removing* it: **"some residue is too
  deep-rung to remove safely"** (§5.3 #1). A deep-rung, near-φ-locked residue is
  explosive-primed; perturbing it wrong is a **full-cascade blast**, not a controlled
  burn (§5.3 #1; [`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §3). The corpus reads
  the deepest thing and then *cannot take it*.
- **The deep-rung reaper scars the terrain it reaps.** Withdrawing deep-rung matter
  de-orders the locality — raises `ε²`, lowers `q` — so a too-deep harvest is a
  landscape wound, not a pile of blocks ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md)
  §2.5). The reaper's by-product is a desert-seed (§3.2 of
  [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md): over-reaping is a desert cause).
- **The fire-vs-explosive line makes a too-deep harvest a blast.** Deep-rung, `ε² ≈ 0`,
  near-φ-locked matter held far from equilibrium against its attractor is
  **explosive-primed**: perturbed wrongly it discharges as a full-cascade shock
  ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §3). The boundary is a *rate/threshold
  ratio, not a material flag* (§3b, §5) — the same overdraw trigger as
  [`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md) §4.3.

**The answer this document designs:** you do not rip the fossil out from above,
perturbing the field your tool can only reach through. **You descend to it, inside a
shell that refuses the surrounding disorder, and collect ordered matter at a rate
you control.** The Bell makes the corpus's most dangerous open thread — the deepest
store — reachable *from inside its own shell*, turning the deep-rung reaper into a
controlled, contained extraction and the full-cascade blast into a *held* boundary the
shell never crosses.

This is a genuine, designed craft — not a mining gadget. It is a **rigid body
vehicle** ([`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md) §2.1) on the same continuous domain the
rocket rides, with one axis reversed.

## 2. The shell — Qi-bath core + φ-detuned skin

### 2.1 The two composed pieces, both already designed

The shell is **not a new physics**. It is the composition of two corpus pieces plus
the existing rigid-body vehicle model:

| Layer | Piece | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core** | **Qi bath** | a maintained high-`q` core with a radius — everything within it runs at its core's elevated coherence; a *regional efficiency multiplier*, not a new reservoir | [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §4.4; [`coherence-technologies.md`](./coherence-technologies.md) concept 4 |
| **Skin** | **φ-detuned boundary** | a surface held at a *private phase offset* so any incoming perturbation lacking that phase finds `M ≈ 0` and cannot transfer momentum across it — it *refuses* rather than tanks, converting organized kinetic energy to diffuse heat (glow) | [`coherence-technologies.md`](./coherence-technologies.md) concept 3 (the `M≈0` refusal, its §2b/§3a) |
| **Body** | **rigid vehicle** | six-DOF + angular, integrated by the sampler against the local pot gradient and field values — a field operation, not a voxel contraption | [`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md) §2.1 |

A body is a **capacitor at its rung** ([`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md) §1.2): the
merge lineage's ordered matter *is* stored coherence. The Bell is a **capsule of
that same substance** — a moving, self-holding order pocket — with a bath at its
core (raising the operator's working coherence and cutting `(1−q)` waste across the
whole cluster, energy §4.4) and a detuned surface (refusing the surrounding `ε²` and
disorder from coupling in, coherence-technologies §3b).

### 2.2 What it refuses, what it costs

**It refuses the crushing ε² and the danger of disturbing deep-rung order.** The
descent below the safe-reap boundary is dangerous for two distinct reasons, and the
shell answers each:

1. **The surrounding disorder** — the strata below the coarse grid carry rising
   `ε²` and falling `q` (the deep end of the material regime; the dissolution floor,
   [`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §2). A player who descends *exposed*
   shed their own coherence as they go — they become the scar they are in
   (the ε²-vent of a channeler mid-scar, [`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md)
   §1.2). The **detuned skin refuses this** (`M ≈ 0`): the outside disorder
   converts to diffuse heat/glow at the boundary and never reaches the operator.
2. **The deep-rung store's blast risk** — disturbing a deep, `ε² ≈ 0`,
   far-from-equilibrium store *wrong* is a full-cascade discharge
   ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §3). The Bell's skin is the same
   boundary the KSP kernel uses for **docking phase-matching** (§3.4): the operator
   collects *through* the shell, phase-matched, at a rate the field can re-lock —
   so the collection is a **controlled organizational discharge (fire/fuel)**, never
   the uncontrolled full-cascade (blast) of §3/§5.

**What it costs — honestly.** The shell is a **standing draw**:

- A Qi bath is *maintained*, not free — it must hold its core `q` up, a standing
  cost that makes the cluster *brittle if it goes down* ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md)
  §4.4). The Bell carries that bath *with* it, at depth, where the field is least
  inclined to help — so the hold is at its most expensive where the descent is
  deepest.
- The skin is a **draw, not a free shield**. Holding φ-structure *costs nothing*
  in the theory (the attractor funds it, coherence-technologies §3a) — but a boundary
  that is actively *refusing* a rising `ε²` does work, and that work is a
  `(1−q)`-glow bleed (the refuse-converts-to-heat of §2b, concept 3). A Bell in a
  deep, disordered region is *visibly glowing*, and a bright shell is a wasteful one
  (the same `(1−q)` diagnostic, coherence-technologies §4c).
- The **no-free-energy cap** ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §6)
  binds: **a descent is a net-negative hold.** The Bell spends stored coherence to
  hold its pocket of order against a field that would otherwise crush it; it never
  *mints* — the harvest is *ordered matter carried out*, not coherence generated.
  The "product" of a descent is a **withdrawal**, priced exactly like the deep-rung
  reaper's (§2.5) — de-ordering what it removes — but *contained*, so the scar and
  the blast-risk are held at the shell instead of spreading through the terrain.

**The un-contained hand-scale (reverse pointer, two-way).** This §2.2's contained
withdrawal-and-scar is what the corpus's primitive work object does *without* a
shell: [`the-tool.md`](./the-tool.md) §2c designs **a deep tool's bite as the
charge/scar asymmetry held at the hand** — "a deep tool cuts down to deep matter
but scars the locality it cuts into more... the tool is the un-contained hand-scale
face of the same withdrawal; the Bell collects through a contained shell, holding
the scar at the shell and letting the region heal around it (§2.2/§6.3), the tool
does none of that" (`the-tool.md` §2c — it cites this §2.2's net-negative hold and
§6.3's containment as the craft-scale it descends to). The tool is the hand's
deep-withdrawal; the Bell is its craft-scale containment; the two docs now cite
each other as read-and-answered.

### 2.3 The operator is a coherence source inside a self-held pocket of order

The player is a **bounded EY injector and an ε² vent**
([`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md) §1): their body *is* a coherence store
in field terms. Inside the Bell, the operator is not channeling against the open
field — they are **the active maintainer of the shell's core**, feeding the Qi bath
their own coherence budget `B` (§1.2) while the bath and skin multiply its effect.
The Shell's economics are the operator's coherence budget scaled up: the bath
raises the working `q` (cutting `(1−q)` waste everywhere inside, energy §4.4), the
skin holds the boundary, and the operator keeps both fed. A diver runs out of
*coherence budget*, not air — the honest depth limit is the same budget economics
that bound the voyage's reach ([`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §2.3).

## 3. Descent mechanics — the rocket's twin

### 3.1 The symmetry

The rocket and the Bell are **one mechanism, two directions**:

| | Rocket (ksp §2.2) | Bell (this doc) |
|---|---|---|
| The device | a condensation drive pointed **at the sky** | a held shell pointed **down** |
| What it does with EY | **injects** organized perturbation to *climb* the envelope's ρ-gradient | **holds** a φ-detuned shell to *sink through* that ρ-gradient and the crust's ε² |
| The reaction | the river law pushes the vehicle **up** along `∇(g·Φ)` | the field's condition *below* is refused by the skin (`M ≈ 0`), so the Bell's own weight (its capacitor's `ξ`-coupled mass) carries it down |
| Fuel | withdrawn stored coherence, spent as thrust | withdrawn stored coherence, spent as the *hold* (bath + skin) |
| Waste | the `(1−q)` glow of a wasteful engine | the `(1−q)` glow of a shell refusing the outside |
| Terminal risk | thrust-vs-blast overdraw (ksp §2.3) | full-cascade discharge if the collection rate exceeds the field's re-lock (this doc §3.3) |

**The crux:** the rocket *escapes* the envelope by injecting; the Bell *enters* the
crust by holding. Both are coherent operations on the one two-fluid field; neither
is a Newtonian push. The descent is the shell **sinking because the field cannot
couple to it** — the `M≈0` refusal means the outside carries no organized exchange
that could hold the Bell up, so its own coherence-coupled weight takes it down
through a medium that cannot support it. It is propelled *by* the field's
indifference to it — the same privacy that makes a detuned boundary a shield
([`coherence-technologies.md`](./coherence-technologies.md) §3a) is what lets a
shell fall through strata.

**[design] — the descent path below the coarse grid.** The Bell does not move the
1 m gameplay grid's blocks; it moves *through* the raw field the grid is a
sub-cell sample of ([`volumetric-terrain.md`](./volumetric-terrain.md): a block is
the field integrated over its meter). What a descent encounters, from the corpus's
own layers:

1. **The envelope.** The ascent side — descending from the surface, the Bell
   first crosses the same ρ/ε²/q column the sky is the other face of
   ([`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md) §1.2): terrain
   `ρ ≥ θ_c` at the top, the under-structure where ρ rises toward the deep-rung
   matter below.
2. **The coarse grid's underside.** The gameplay grid is 1 m blocks, a **coarser
   quantization** over the field ([`chunk-field-quantization.md`](./chunk-field-quantization.md)
   §1.3/§1.4). The Bell slips through the interstices the grid does not own —
   the field the quantizer ignores between cells. **[design]** Below the grid,
   the strata are the residue material of [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md)
   §2 — the log-periodic condensation bands, the fossil cores, the ghost halos — read
   as a volume rather than a wall.
3. **The deep residue.** The deepest thing a descent reaches is the birth-strata
   and ultimately the **seed** ([`resonance-seeds.md`](./resonance-seeds.md) §1.1/§4.1).
   This is the corpus's deepest store and the Bell's crown target. What a very deep
   descent may also encounter is the **BH** — the end of the cascade
   ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §4) — the field's own terminal, where
   the descent and the accretion front meet.

### 3.2 The controlled-collection rate — holding the fire-vs-explosive line

**The safe-reap boundary of [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §5.3 is
what the Bell honors by construction, not by luck.** The boundary is a rate/threshold
ratio: a *controlled organizational discharge* is fire (fuel); an *uncontrolled
full-cascade discharge* is a blast ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md)
§3b/§5; [`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md) §4.3). The Bell's collection is
a **withdrawal through the shell, metered so `ω₀²` restoration can feed the drain**
— the same condition that keeps a rocket's thrust from becoming a blast
([`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md) §2.3). If the operator forces more collection
than the local field can re-lock, the controlled release crosses into the full-cascade
regime — but now **inside the shell**, at the store, which the detuned skin contains.
The blast-risk is not *eliminated*; it is **localized to the one thing the shell is
built to hold**. A diver who over-collects detonates their own Bell, not the terrain.

**[design] — the metering.** The concrete collection rate (how much deep-rung
coherence per field-step the field can re-lock at a given depth to stay on the
fire side of the line) is a Phase-1.5 measurement, exactly like the reaper's scar
magnitude ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2.5 is a *withdrawal*
whose blast line material-regimes sets, but whose per-depth number is tuning). The
*semantics* — "collection held below the re-lock rate = safe, above = contained blast" —
is the design; the *number* is measurement.

### 3.3 The surface return — re-condensing into the envelope

[`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md) §3.3 names landing as **re-condensing into the
envelope** — the vehicle descends through the gas floor into `ρ ≥ θ_c` and grounds
against the condensation threshold. The Bell's return is the reverse of its own
descent: it stops holding the refusal (the skin weakens, `M` opens), the surrounding
field begins to couple again, and the Bell **re-enters the organized envelope** —
its hull, once a refused shell, becomes a body the field steers, exactly as a rocket
returns from orbit. There is no separate "surface physics"; the Bell rides the same
ρ-gradient, reversed. **[design]** The skin's "opening" on return is the same
free-phase-parameter toggling as docking phase-matching ([`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md)
§3.4) — read the envelope's phase, re-tune the shell to it, and the field couples the
returning Bell the way it couples any incoming body.

## 4. The Bell as a gate site — the density lever

Concept 5's central rule is the **lens-vs-coupler split**: a wide surface structure
concentrates ambient coherence by geometry (a lens for low-ρ air); a **deep, dense
fixture couples to a high-ρ medium's strong Π gradients**
([`coherence-technologies.md`](./coherence-technologies.md) §5a). The key line,
verbatim from the concept: *"Placing a node underwater is not 'closer to the core'
in rung terms — it is a **denser Qi medium** with more raw field to gate. **Density,
not depth, is the lever.**"*

A deep dense medium has **stronger Π gradients** — so a deep descent is a **better
cascade site, not just a mine**. The Bell does not descend only to *extract*; it
descends to *gate*. The bottom of the strata is where a cascade-staged base
(concept 5, the ~10-rung bridge limit carried by [`custom-blocks.md`](./custom-blocks.md)
§2) finds its densest coupling medium — the region with the most organized field
per unit volume, where a gate node's throughput is highest.

**The Bell is therefore simultaneously the deep-rung reaper *and* the deep-rung
gate** — and a descent is a **construction act**, not just a mining trip:

- **Reaper:** it carries the withdrawn ordered matter up (the classical harvest, at
  the safe rate of §3.2).
- **Gate:** it *places* a cascade node at the density lever's strongest point
  — a fixture the deep medium's Π gradients can gate most efficiently. A Bell that
  descends, anchors a staged node, and returns has **built** something the deep
  medium now routes through, not merely emptied a deposit.

This is the honest reason a descent is *worth its net-negative cost* (§2.2): it is not
only a mine, it is the construction of the corpus's best gate site — the bottom of
the cascade's density ladder. `[design]` The *placement* rule (build on the dense,
organized sites) is Phase-1 (coherence-technologies §5d — the build-on-coherent-sites
rule is Phase-1); the *live staged node at depth* is the late-phase realization that
needs the depth representation.

## 5. Navigation — the descent/looting map

Two maps compose, both already in the corpus:

1. **The meshless-site map is the descent map.** The moving-Voronoi sites are "where
   the field is most organized," weighted on coherence `q` (`ML_LLOYD_P = 4`), and
   they are the chunk-activity/LOD scheduler ([`chunk-field-quantization.md`](./chunk-field-quantization.md)
   §5; [`README.md`](../README.md) "the physics is also the load map"). For the Bell,
   these sites are **where the field concentrates order** — both the *descent path*
   (follow the coherent sites down; they cluster where the field is organized enough
   to descend *through*) and the **loot map** (ore/coherence precipitates where q
   accumulates, and deep-rung residue anchors the coherent sites ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md)
   §2.3 — residue *is* a persistent Π pattern, a coherent well the field holds). A
   diver reads the site map to know *where* the deep order is concentrated before
   descending. **Qi-bath siting follows the sites** — the shell's own bath stays
   fed by holding the descent near coherent sites, exactly as a base is best built
   where the sites cluster (coherence-technologies §5b).
2. **The archaeology core sample is the pre-descent survey.** Before committing a
   Bell (a net-negative, budget-costing hold) to a descent, the operator runs the
   archaeologist's core sample — a vertical `ρ`/`q`/`ε²` profile that resolves the
   stratigraphy ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §3.2). This **is**
   the survey: it tells the diver what the descent will find (the fossil cores, the
   ghost halos, the birth-strata), which strata are safe to reap (§5.3), and where
   the seed/artifacts sit. The reader (Sense, [`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md)
   §2) is the instrument for both — a read-only Phase-1 channel that needs no Bell
   to operate. **Descent is survey-driven**: read the residue signature and the site
   map first, descend only where the survey justifies the hold's cost.

**[design]** The depth map — resolving the site map *below* the coarse gameplay
grid, through the strata volume — is flagged in §7(a): the sites are published at
the field resolution, but a *strata-volume* representation of the deep residue the
Bell moves through is the one piece the corpus has not built.

### 6.2 The feel — the down-facing voyage, the long dark inverted

The KSP rocket's voyage is **the long dark between windows** — crossing an empty
measure where ρ is below the gas floor, the ship the brightest thing there
([`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §2). The Bell's descent is **that voyage
inverted**: not a *horizontal* crossing of emptiness, but a *vertical* descent into
an *over-full* medium — where ρ and ε² rise together, and the shell is the only
pocket of order. The feel is symmetric:

- **Up (the long dark):** thin, quiet, empty; the ship's own `(1−q)` glow is the
  brightest thing; the horizon is finite and expensive ([`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md)
  §2.3/§2.4).
- **Down (the deep field):** dense, crushing, bright-with-disorder outside the shell;
  the Bell's glow is its own held order lighting the dark strata as it refuses their
  disorder; the depth limit is a coherence budget, not a distance.

Both are **coherence-budget journeys, not free movement** — the "fuel" that makes each
*go* is the same deep-rung stored coherence withdrawn as a hold, and both are bounded
by the no-free-energy cap (no conversion yields more than it sinks,
[`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §6). The descent is the long-voyage
arc turned down: **descend → survey the store → collect at the safe rate → surface with
what the field keeps.**

## 7. Honest gates

The shell pieces already exist — **nothing below is new physics**; the Qi bath
(energy §4.4), the φ-detuned boundary (coherence-technologies concept 3), and the
rigid-body vehicle (ksp §2.1) are all designed. What a *descent* needs are the gates
below.

### (a) Condensed bodies / a depth representation below the coarse grid **[design]**

The Bell moves through **strata beyond the 1 m gameplay grid**. The corpus's
dual-world grid is a sub-cell quantization of the field ([`volumetric-terrain.md`](./volumetric-terrain.md);
[`chunk-field-quantization.md`](./chunk-field-quantization.md) §1.3/§1.4) — but a
travelable "depth" the Bell descends *through* (the condensation bands, the residue
volume, the birth-strata) is **not built**. This is the largest honest gap: the
strata-vs-heal probe and the residue-persistence calibration are already gated as
Phase-1.5 in [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §6b/§6c — the Bell
inherits both, plus needs a *continuous strata volume* (not a core-sample line) to
descend. **Flagged [design] all the way.** The Bell cannot move "down" beyond the
grid until this depth representation exists.

### (b) The fire-vs-explosive line held at depth — the controlled-rate discipline

The safe-collection rate (§3.2) is the boundary at-depth; the *rate* (how much
withdrawal the local field can re-lock to stay on the fire side) is a Phase-1.5
measurement, exactly as the reaper's scar magnitude. Gate: **pre-register a probe** —
at a deep-rung deposit, sweep the collection rate and measure where the controlled
organizational discharge crosses into the full-cascade regime (`ω₀²` restoration can
no longer feed the drain). The Bell's design holds the line by *metering*, but the
line's position is measurement, not a stated number.

### (c) The sync-path contract for player-touched descent

A descent is **player-touched, outcome-critical** (the operator is inside the shell,
steering and collecting) — so it rides the **sync `block=true` path** for that one
vehicle's locality, bounded by `JOB_STEP_CAP = 64` (≤ 0.25 s) and, under the tree arm
/ at a deep contact, `TREE_JOB_STEP_CAP = 8` ([`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md) §4.1/§4.2).
Free-fall Bell craft (autonomy, unattended) ride the async path. The collection op is
a **Q4 player-return input** — `{op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}` pushed by
the sampler, never the Bell touching physics state (§4.3). Gate: measure a worst-case
descent-contact sync stall against the stated cap; it must be a hitch, not a freeze —
identical contract to the KSP kernel's gate (d).

### (d) No-free-energy — a descent is a net-negative hold

The Bell is **a hold, never a mint**. The shell's bath + skin and the operator's
budget are spent to hold order against a field that would crush it; the harvest is
**ordered matter withdrawn**, priced exactly like the deep-rung reaper's withdrawal
([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2.5) and bounded by the §6 cap
(**no conversion yields more than it sinks**, output ≤ φ⁻¹·input). A descent must
not be exploitable as a coherence generator: collecting a deep store de-orders the
region (§6.3), the hold costs standing coherence at depth (§2.2), and the collected
matter is a *withdrawal of order*, not a source. Gate: confirm the write-back
amplitude caps hold so a Bell cannot turn a deep deposit into a coherence farm —
the same economic gate as [`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §2.3's length-limiter.

### Phase-1 slice — the principle, testable, no new physics

**Phase-1 renders the *principle*: deep-rung matter is best reached from inside its
own shell — as a regional anti-corruption hold around a deep deposit.** Concretely,
before the depth representation exists, the Bell's *idea* is already Phase-1-testable:
a **maintained Qi-bath core + detuned boundary held around a deep deposit** is a
**preventive anti-corruption act** keeping the deposit's `ε²` below dissolution and
its local `q` up — the exact anti-corruption mechanism of [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md)
§5.4, rendered as a *shell* instead of a suppressor. This needs **no new physics**:
the Qi bath (§4.4) is designed Phase-1 infrastructure, the detuned boundary is a
Phase-1 static fixture (coherence-technologies §3d), and "hold ε² down / q up around
a site" reads the same published channels. **The Phase-1 Bell is a regional
anti-corruption hold that protects a deep deposit from the surrounding disorder and
from a too-deep reaper's blast** — the shell's refusal, demonstrated on the ground,
before the descent exists. The downward *journey* through strata is the later phase
this doc designs; the principle is Phase-1-earning.

## 8. Feasibility verdict

**Medium-late: buildable before the world-structure ideas, and the shell is
already designed; what gates it is the depth below the grid.**

By construction this document is the **down-facing twin of the KSP kernel** — it
reuses the kernel's rigid-body vehicle model, its sync-path contract, its
coherence-budget economics, and its fire-vs-explosive discipline, with the axis
reversed. The shell composes **three already-designed pieces** (the Qi bath of
[`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §4.4, the φ-detuned boundary of
[`coherence-technologies.md`](./coherence-technologies.md) concept 3, the rigid-body
vehicle of [`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md) §2.1) — **no new physics is asserted.**
The Bell de-risks the corpus's most dangerous open thread: the deepest store
([`resonance-seeds.md`](./resonance-seeds.md)) becomes reachable from inside its own
shell, the deep-rung reaper becomes a contained extraction, and the full-cascade
blast becomes a *held* boundary.

**What it needs to exist**, in order:

1. **A depth representation below the coarse grid** (gate (a)) — the strata volume
   the Bell moves through, plus archaeology's residue-persistence and strata-vs-heal
   calibrations (both already gated Phase-1.5 in [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md)
   §6b/§6c). This is the absolute dependency: **no depth, no descent.**
2. **The fire-vs-explosive rate measurement** (gate (b)) — where the safe-collection
   rate crosses into full-cascade, at depth.
3. **The sync-path contract** (gate (c)) — the Q4/sync discipline for player-touched
   descent, inherited unchanged from the KSP kernel.
4. **The no-free-energy cap held** (gate (d)) — a descent is a net-negative hold.

**Timing.** The Phase-1 slice — **the principle rendered as a regional
anti-corruption hold around a deep deposit — is Phase-1 feasible with no new physics**
(it is anti-corruption, §2.3, wrapped in a shell). The full downward *journey* is
**medium-late**: it lands after the KSP kernel's prerequisites (the tree arm,
condensed bodies, the Q4 channel, the sync path — [`ksp-kernel.md`](./ksp-kernel.md)
§7) because a Bell is a rigid-body vehicle, and after archaeology's depth gates
(residue model, core sample) because a descent is survey-driven. It is **buildable
before the world-structure ideas** ([`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) founding-a-
window, the seed's full arc) — the Bell can reach and contain deep-rung stores before
those objects exist, and it *supplies* many of them (the deep fuel, the relics, the
seed). The binding risks, in order: the depth representation (gate a), the rate
measurement (gate b), then the sync and economic contracts (c, d) which are inherited,
not new. The architecture is sound because the collapse is real: **a descent is a
coherence budget spent to hold a pocket of order against the deep field's disorder,
and the harvest is ordered matter withdrawn — never a mint.** There is no separate
"down physics"; there is the one two-fluid field, and descending into the strata is
climbing its ρ-gradient upside down, inside a shell that refuses what it cannot hold.

## Open questions

1. **Strata-volume vs. core-sample depth (gate a).** The corpus reads depth as a
   *line* (the archaeology core sample, [`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md)
   §3.2). A Bell descends through a *volume*. Is the deep residue a continuous volume
   the shell sinks through, or a set of discrete fossil cores / coherent wells
   ([`field-archaeology.md`](./field-archaeology.md) §2) the Bell navigates between?
   The former is a new representation; the latter composes existing site-map logic.
2. **The descent's BUDGET shape.** The hold cost scales with depth (the bath works
   hardest where the field is least inclined to help, §2.2). Does the deep hold grow
   monotonically more expensive to the point where the seed (§6.3's crown) is
   nearly unreachable — or does a *coherent* deep region (a birth-strata well) hold
   cheaper because the bath rides existing order? The answer sets whether the deepest
   store is a deliberate endgame or an accidental one.
3. **Collection-rate calibration (gate b).** The safe rate is measurement — but *what
   quantity* the throttle meters (withdrawn EY per field-step? the skin's `(1−q)`
   bleed? the sink's local `ε²` rise?) needs a concrete definition before the blast
   line can be pre-registered.
4. **Bell determinism across the async/sync loops.** A descent is mostly async (free
   descent) with player-touched sync contacts (collection, docking out). The KSP
   kernel's gate (e) flags vehicle determinism across the two loops as a Phase-2
   measure; the Bell inherits it and adds the skin's phase-toggling (descent vs.
   return, §3.3) as a determinism-critical coupling to audit.
5. **The Bell vs. the BH.** What a very deep descent reaches is the field's terminal
   ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §4 — the BH, the end of the cascade).
   Is a "descent into a BH's archive" ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §4.4 —
   what it ate) a real Bell run, or is the BH's surface logically the floor past which
   no shell holds (§2.2 — the hold's net-negative becomes unsustainable as `ε²`
   saturates)? The honest default [design]: the BH is the descent's *hard floor* — the
   point where refusal costs more than any store is worth — but the archive-read
   stays open as a late-phase possibility.
