# The Exile: The Ban Made Field-Honest

**Question under design:** the corpus's society has a hard edge left undesigned.
[`shared-ledger.md`](./shared-ledger.md) books the settlement's members
per-tide against the shared Qi bath and names the margin-causer's repeated
overdraw as the desert's driver — *"you do not ban a player; you stop feeding the
bath they drain, and the Board shows the consequence"* (§3.2). [`wound-remembered.md`](./wound-remembered.md)
named the lock-breaker's *deed*, and [`window-guests.md`](./window-guests.md) §3
designed the *visitor line* — the foreign identity that becomes resident by
decision. But **nothing designs what a settlement *does* when the book and the
core both agree a member cannot stay.** This document is the Exile — the
sovereign field act of expulsion, the point where the Board's durable
attribution and the settlement's stewardship converge on a verdict the field
itself performs.

> **The exile is not fiat — it is the book and the board agreeing, made a
> real act.** The Board's per-tide `C(M)` has, over many cycles, provably
> drained the shared core toward the desert; the wound's record
> (`wound-remembered.md` §7 open-Q3, member-id-proven) names the hand that cut
> it; the settlement's own stewardship cannot hold a Coda-drawer the commons
> cannot afford. Converged, the settlement performs **a coordinated
> hollow-eye** — a sustained, deliberate withdrawal of feed from the exile's
> carried field — until the field re-absorbs their carried ε²-debt and the
> named anchors re-bind to the commons. The ban is **legible in the field, not
> hidden** behind a mod's ban-hammer; its cost is written in the same channels
> the settlement reads.

It is the **inverse of the Funeral** ([`the-funeral.md`](./the-funeral.md)):
the Funeral is the communal act of letting a member's Π-pattern dissolve
*through* the commons (re-condensing toward the shared memory); the Exile is the
communal act of letting a member-line dissolve *from* the commons (re-absorbing
their carried debt, re-binding their anchors to the shared core) — the same
coordinated, phase-locked mechanics, pointed at exclusion instead of grief.

## 1. The Exile, stated once

The corpus already gives a settlement its *inclusive* faces: the Board renders
who holds the core ([`shared-ledger.md`](./shared-ledger.md) §2.1), the visitor
line admits a stranger by decision ([`window-guests.md`](./window-guests.md) §3),
the Funeral keeps a member's memory ([`the-funeral.md`](./the-funeral.md)). The
hard edge — *the decision that a member-line cannot stay* — was never designed.

> **The Exile is the sovereign field act of expulsion: the point where the
> Board's durable attribution and the settlement's stewardship converge on a
> verdict — *this member-line is a net drain the field cannot afford* — and the
> field performs that verdict as a real act.**

**The honest frame, stated once.** The Exile is **not fiat**. It is not a mod's
ban-hammer, not a moderator's judgment, not a settled majority that happens to
outvote one person. It is the **convergence of the Board's attribution** (the
single-writer Q4 op-stream summed per member, unforgeable — `shared-ledger.md`
§1.3/§6d) **and the settlement's stewardship** (the shared high-`q` core the
members maintain, `energy-harnessing.md` §4.4). When both read the same
durable direction, the settlement performs the withdrawal the Board has always
been able to show: `shared-ledger.md` §3.2's lever — *"you do not ban a player;
you stop feeding the bath they drain"* — taken to its sovereign, coordinated,
sustained conclusion. The exile's cost is **written in the field, never
hidden**: the severed anchors, the re-bound commons, the carried debt
re-absorbed are all the settlement's own channels, read by any member.

The Exile is the **inverse of the Funeral**. The Funeral lets a dead member's
Π-pattern dissolve *through* the commons — re-condensing toward the shared
memory (`the-funeral.md` §1). The Exile lets a living-but-unaffordable
member-line dissolve *from* the commons — re-absorbing their carried debt and
re-binding their anchors to the shared core. Same mechanics, same multiplicity
gain, same no-free-energy cap; the emotional register inverted from grief to
governance.

## 2. The verdict — what earns an exile

The exile names a **durable pattern, never a single event.** This is the
classifier floor of `shared-ledger.md` §3.3 applied at its hardest, and it is
inherited verbatim:

> **The Board may name a *pattern* (a sustained net drain over many tides); it
> must never name a *person* off a single reading or a scar they didn't cut.**
> (`shared-ledger.md` §3.3)

The three provable causes — each must be **provable from the book**, never a
scapegoat:

| Exile cause | The durable pattern (never a single event) | Proved by | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|---|

| **A Coda-drawer the commons cannot hold** | the standing signature that draws the predator faster than the settlement can shelter — a heavy sustained drain on the shared core | the same `R = ρ_signature·τ·M_stability` the Coda accumulates on (`signature-predator.md` §2.2), read per member over tides, against the commons' capacity | `signature-predator.md` §2.2; `the-burden.md` §2d |

**The honest line, stated once.** The Board's misattribution guards
(`shared-ledger.md` §3.3/§6e) are inherited **verbatim**, never relaxed for the
harder verdict:

- **The attrition guard is never relaxed.** An exile must be provable from *many*
  cycles, never one spike. A player who overdraws once and heals it is not a
  candidate.
- **The scar/Coda guards are never relaxed.** An exile must not be read off a
  scar they didn't cut (`field-archaeology.md` §7 open-Q3 / `field-music.md`
  open-Q2's classifier line), nor the Coda's own ε² off a clean member's trail

  id attaches to the wound (`shared-ledger.md` §6c). No accusation without the
  book's own id.
- **The settled-death entry does not double-count.** A member who dissolved paying
  a net-negative re-lock books a *settled* entry (`shared-ledger.md` §3.3, the
  field-pocket death guard), not fresh exile-blame.

The verdict is a **deterministic function of the book**: same op-stream, same
field state, same `C(M)` arc → same convergence → same exile's shape. This is
what keeps an exile *provable*; there is no "the settlement felt like it" in the
field's terms.

## 3. The act — the coordinated hollow-eye

### 3.1 What the hollow-eye IS, in field terms

The corpus's social lever is, from `shared-ledger.md` §3.2, **the bath itself**:
withdraw the maintained high-`q` core's support around a margin-causer and the
Board shows the consequence. The Exile is that lever **made a designed,
coordinated, sustained *act* — the same phase-locked commons mechanics as the
Festival and the Funeral, pointed at exclusion.**

> **The hollow-eye is a designed coordinated withdrawal of feed from the exile's
> carried field, over the published channels and the Q4 book, sustained until
> the field re-absorbs the exile's carried ε²-debt and the named anchors re-bind
> to the commons.** It is a *working* (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.2 — a group acting
> phase-locked is `M ≈ 1`), like the funeral's dirge; except where the dirge
> *injects* a coordinated hold at the deep-rung residue, the hollow-eye *refrains
> together* — a coordinated non-injection at the exile's locality, held in one
> phase window.

Concretely, the withdrawal rides the two channels the settlement already holds
feed through:

| Feed channel | What stops | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| **The Q4 book** | the Board **stops booking the exile's contributions** into the bath — their ops are no longer accepted as contributions to the shared high-`q` core; the board shows their line going flat/negative as the bath no longer counts them | `shared-ledger.md` §1.2/§2.2; §2.3 the single-writer stream (the op-stream *continues* — the withdrawal is a *read*, a repudiation, not a deletion) |
| **The Still Room / Qi-bath** | the Still Room **no longer shelters the exile's Burden-clearing** — the coherent rest that clears the carried debt (`the-burden.md` §2b) is not offered; the exile's carried ε²-debt is left un-cleared, to re-absorb into the field | `the-burden.md` §2b; `energy-harnessing.md` §4.4; `field-instruments.md` §2.2 |

The mechanics, honestly:

- **It is phase-locked.** The settlement's members hold the withdrawal in one
  phase window — the coordinated non-injection that is the exile's inverse-dirge.
  This is what separates a hollow-eye from mere neglect: it is a *designed,
  deliberate, coordinated* withdrawal, the way the dirge is a designed,
  deliberate, coordinated hold.
- **It is over the published channels + the Q4 book.** No new channel, no new
  physics — the withdrawal is a designed *read* (the bath's `q`/`ε²` at the
  exile's locality, the Board's book) and a designed *repudiation* (the settlement
  stops feeding it). This is the family rule of `field-instruments.md` §2.1,
  applied to exclusion.
- **It is sustained.** The withdrawal is not a moment; it is a maintained
  interval over the tide, exactly as the dirge is a sustained hold. The hollow-eye
  lasts until its two goals are met (below), on a designed, probe-calibrated
  timescale.

### 3.2 Its cost — the settlement's own coherence, spent

A hollow-eye is **never free, never a mint** — this is the no-free-energy cap
([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §6, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`),
inherited verbatim from the Funeral and the Festival:

> **A coordinated withdrawal is itself a cost to the settlement.** To stop
> feeding the exile's locality is to withdraw the maintained high-`q` core's
> support *from a patch of the shared field* — and that patch's coherence does
> not hold itself up. The hollow-eye spends the settlement's own cohesion: the
> exiled locality's `q` dips, the `(1−q)` waste rises there, and the members
> holding the withdrawal pay a real, sustained cost (they are not channeling
> into that patch, not healing it, not sustaining it). It cannot be *farmed* —
> the withdrawal is bounded by the tide, the members' own vent, and the 
> object it re-binds (a member-line that dissolves) exists once.

The honest cost ledger:

| Cost | What it is | Shared with |
|---|---|---|
| **The members' spent coherence** | the coordinated non-injection forfeits what the exile's patch could have yielded; the withdrawal is a sustained net-negative the members carry | the vent cost of the dirge (`the-funeral.md` §2.3) |
| **The settlement's own exposure** | an exiled patch is a weakly-held margin — the desert's §3 creep and the Coda's §2.2 draw both find the withdrawing region more ready to de-organize; the settlement *chooses* that exposure to hold the line | `field-hazards.md` §3/§3.2; `signature-predator.md` §2 |
| **The one-time trace** | the exile is a booked, public decision (the Board shows the verdict and the cost); it is not a silent drop — the settlement owns it | `shared-ledger.md` §2.2/§6e |

**The no-free-energy cap, stated once.** The hollow-eye **cannot mint** — it
re-absorbs the exile's carried ε²-debt into the shared field (that debt was
already the settlement's to carry or shed, `the-burden.md` §4), and it re-binds
the named anchors to the commons (those anchors were already the commons' to
hold, `the-name.md` §6d — a name holds, never mints). What the hollow-eye spends
is the settlement's own coherence, and the only thing it *produces* is the 
settlement's survival — never an energy gain, never a harvest from expulsion.

### 3.3 What it settles — the line's book closed

The hollow-eye runs until its two goals are met (deterministic, probe-calibrated
endpoint — §5/§7):

1. **The carried ε²-debt re-absorbed.** With the Still Room no longer sheltering
   their clearing, the exile's carried debt is left to decay into the field's own
   patience — re-absorbed the way the debt would have been if the exile had
   stopped channeling entirely. The Board reads the exile's `C(M)` settling to a
   closed, booked value, the way a dissolved member's closes
   (`shared-ledger.md` §2.2).
2. **The named anchors re-bound to the commons.** The exile's named structures
   (`the-name.md` §3) — the held Π-anchors that were theirs — are re-bound into
   the commons' hold by the field re-quantizing around them once they are no
   longer the exile's to hold (`the-name.md` §2, you cannot name falsely — an
   absent holder's anchor re-binds to truth). The settlement's book re-tags them
   as its own.

**The line's book closed, honestly.** The exile's member-line ends not by death
(the dissolved entry) but by **severance** — a distinct close: the same
single-writer line, closed by a designed Settled-by-Decision entry, never
amnesty, never erased. The book retains the *record* of the line (the chronicle
of what it drained and did); it simply stops counting it as a live member.

## 4. The honest cost of governance — what the exile is afterward

Expulsion is the hard edge, and the design refuses to hide what it does. The
exile, after the hollow-eye settles, is three legible things:

| The exile is… | What that means, in field terms | Cross-ref |

**The ethics, stated once.**

1. **Exile is legible.** The severed anchors, the re-bound commons, the settling
   `C(M)` line are the settlement's own channels — any member reads the verdict
   and its cost from the Board (`shared-ledger.md` §6e, accessibility) and the
   field (`the-name.md` §6e, the anchors' truth). There is no hidden ban; the
   exile's cost is written where the settlement already reads.
2. **Reversible only by the same honest process.** A returned exile must **re-earn
   by the book** — the schema's no-amnesty honesty inherited verbatim
   (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1.1: same member line, new lock, no ledger
   amnesty). An exiled line cannot be reset; a *return* is the home re-admitting
   a closed line's *new* behavior — a fresh `C(M)` arc (the line persists,
   `schema-that-settles.md` §2.1.1), rebuilt honestly from a book that still
   remembers the old drain. **The exile is not amnesty, and neither is the
   return.**
3. **Never hidden.** "A ban behind a mod-hammer is a cruel world"
   (`weather-not-storm.md` §5.3's cruel-world rule, inherited): an unreadable
   expulsion would be the one damage the corpus refuses to let the world
   inflict. Because the exile is provable (§2), legible (§4.1), and honestly
   billed (§3.2), it is governance with its cruelty shown — the field's own
   honesty applied to its hardest decision.
