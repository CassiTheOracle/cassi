## 1. The Rain, stated once

The corpus's weather stack is all extremes and absences. The **storm** is a `c_s`-traveling
`ε²` front — a wound that passes ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2). The
**cold** is a static long-thin at the `q` floor — the dry season that wears
([`the-cold.md`](./the-cold.md)). The **flood** is the q-surfeit — the harvest-tide
accumulation pushed past its enriching limit, the deluge that drowns
([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md)). And every water-event is a **deluge or a want**: the
flood that *drowns*, the drought-edge that *recedes*, the `q`-collapse that starves. **No
form is the day's ordinary water — the gentle, sustaining fall that *gives*.** The Rain is
that form, stated once and kept:

> **The Rain is the nourishing weather — the regular, sustaining precipitation that
> *gives order back* rather than drowning it.** It is the **flood's gentle twin**: the
> same q-surfeit source ([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §2) read at **a yield the field
> can take** — the flood condensed into a mild, recurring event, where the flood is the
> catastrophic surplus and the Rain is the same source at a yield that feeds. It is the
> weather stack's **harvest-weather** — the one form that gives more than it takes: a
> soft high-`q` fall that feeds the farm's beds ([`farm-that-feeds.md`](./farm-that-feeds.md)
> §2), waters the fallow's season ([`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §3), is carried on
> the wind ([`the-wind.md`](./the-wind.md) §3), and is the reason the farm and the fallow
> have a season at all.

**Why this is the weather stack's missing face, honestly.** Every other weather shape is a
*push* — the storm wounds, the cold wears, the flood drowns, the desert starves. None is a
*give*. The corpus's provenance classifier ([`weather-not-storm.md`](./weather-not-storm.md)
§2) already draws the honest line — weather is ordered, bounded, self-limiting, happens
*where the field is* — but every *designed* weather reads as the field's extreme, not its
ordinary care. **The Rain is the corpus's first weather that is not an extreme: a fall that
feeds.** The flood doc even presupposes it without naming it — the deluge is a *surfeit*
overshooting a band, and a surfeit is only meaningful against a baseline that *feeds*; the
flood's "harvest that drowns" presumes the ordinary rain that grows the harvest first.

The honest scope, stated once: **the flood's q-surfeit, the farm's crop band, the wind's
carry, the marsh's blur, the maintenance axis, the fallow's season, the provenance
classifier, and the water cycle are their source docs' landed pieces; the rain's
composition — the gentle fall's form, the nourishing-but-wet honesty, the flood's beginning
— is this doc's [design] over them, probe-calibrated.** The Rain never claims the engine
"has rain"; it reads the q-surfeit's source at a milder yield, the crop band's need, the
current's carrying, and the wet's blur — a consumer of the ≈ 6 MiB publish exactly as the
wind and the flood's Phase-1 slice are, never a new channel, and at most a bounded Q4 write
for a tended collector, never a new physics.

## 2. The gentle fall — the flood condensed into a mild, recurring event

### 2.1 The landed source — the flood's q-surfeit, read at a yield the field can take

**The landed piece.** The flood is the regional `q` held *above* the enriching band — the
harvest-tide q-accumulation pushed past the limit where it stops *enriching* and starts
*engulfing* ([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §1/§2). Its honest resolution is that the
surfeit **wastes upward** through the `(1−q)` waste — a self-limiting bloom, never a power
source ([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §3). Every flood-feature is the corpus's own
high-state physics held long enough to oversaturate: ore over-precipitates then
re-dissolves (material §3), machines hum past useful toward the `(1−q)` waste (energy §2),
the resonance deafens until every read saturates, the shallow ecology over-blooms.

**The [design] over them — the Rain is the same source at a yield the field can take:**

> **The Rain is the flood's gentle twin: the same q-surfeit source, at a mild, recurring
> yield — a soft high-`q` fall where regional `q` rises *within* the enriching band, feeds,
> and recedes, never crossing the flood's overshoot.** Where the flood is the long-thick
> that holds above the band and drowns, the Rain is the *short, gentle thickening within
> the band* — a fall of water that raises regional `q` enough to feed a bed's precipitation
> band without pushing the locality toward the surfeit threshold ([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md)
> §2's band). The distinguishing quantity is **yield-rate**, not source: the flood and the
> Rain are the **same** q-surfeit source, and the difference is how fast the water falls
> relative to what the field can absorb — a Rain is the accumulation the field takes;
> a flood begins when a Rain is forced faster than the take.

The *rain's form* — **its fall's rate, its per-event yield, its recurrence** — is this
doc's **[design]** over the water channels, probe-calibrated exactly as the flood's
surfeit threshold is this doc's-of-the-flood's [design] over the tide's measured
accumulation ([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §5). What is landed is the q-surfeit source
itself and the harvest-tide accumulation it over-runs; **the gentle fall is the designed
reading of that source at a milder rate**.

### 2.2 The rain's feed — the beds and the fallow's season

**The landed pieces.** A crop is a shallow-rung regime precipitating where the bed's `q`
sits in a band ([`farm-that-feeds.md`](./farm-that-feeds.md) §2); the farm's water is what
holds that band — a thin tide starves the bed unless stewarded (§5), a harvest-tide peaks
it. The fallow is the window's season of depletion, and the one-way run-out's opposite is
the return a season's water brings ([`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §1/§3).

**The [design] over them — the Rain is the farm's water and the fallow's return:**

| The rain feeds | What it waters | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| **The farm's beds** | a regular high-`q` fall holds a bed's `q` in the crop's band between tides — the farmer's weeding/husbanding writes are *cheapened* by a fall that holds the band's floor, the crop's run held and deepened | [`farm-that-feeds.md`](./farm-that-feeds.md) §2 (the bed's `q` band); §5 (the harvest depends on the season's water) |
| **The fallow's season** | a depleted-bed locality's `q` is raised by the fall's feed, letting the bed re-seed and re-hold its band — the rain is the *return* that turns a fallow into a re-growth, the boundary of what a season's water can bring back | [`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §1 (the one-way depletion vs. the seasons that return); §3 (the fallow's relation to the fate) |
| **The wind's carry** | a fall of rain is *moved* across the window by the current — the same `FieldVel` transport that carries a storm's front, a spore-band, or a signature ([`the-wind.md`](./the-wind.md) §3) now carries the gentle fall; a settlement reads the wind to know where the rain is going | [`the-wind.md`](./the-wind.md) §1/§3 (the flow-face, the transport's honest carry) |

**[design]** — *which* yield feeds which bed, how strongly a fall holds a band vs. the
farmer's own husbanding, and how the wind's carry distributes a fall across the window is
this doc's designed composition over the landed crop-band, fallow-season, and carry
channels — probe-calibrated. The engine-real core is the q-accumulation that raises a
bed's `q`, the crop band's need, and the current that moves the weather; **the rain's
gentle fall is the designed reading that gives the farm its water and the fallow its
return.**

### 2.3 The rain's provenance — nourishing weather, never a punishment

The corpus's provenance classifier distinguishes weather from punishment by whether an
`ε²`/`q` extreme is ordered, bounded, self-limiting, and happens *where the field is* vs
persistent and aimed *where the player is* ([`weather-not-storm.md`](./weather-not-storm.md)
§2). The Rain inherits that read as its **own provenance, honestly self-classified**:

> **The Rain is nourishing weather, never a punishment — the provenance classifier's
> sibling applied to the gentle fall.** A rain is the field's own water at a yield it can
> take: self-limiting (it peaks and passes), causally resident *where the field is* (the
> high-`q` band the fall thickens), and readable in the same channels as everything else
> (the `q`/`ε²` the reader already shows). A settlement never reads a rain as a
> punishment — it is the classifier's clearest "weather" verdict, the self-limiting,
> field-located fall, the gentlest face of the same world whose storm is weather and whose
> deluge is the rate pushed too far. **The Rain does not need a new classifier; it is the
> provenance classifier's nourishing verdict** — the one weather that the classifier would
> name weather *without hesitation*, because it is the field's own care, not its extreme.

## 3. The nourishing-but-wet honesty — the field's own give, never a mint

This is the load-bearing honesty. The Rain *gives* — it is the weather stack's one form
that provides — but the corpus's two hard rules hold it with the same rigor they hold the
storm and the flood:

### 3.1 Rain is nourishing but wet — the marsh's blur on a smaller scale

**The landed pieces.** A marsh's tessellated `q` hides motion — the Coda's trail-read, the
watcher's clean signature, the walker's `ε²` wake all *diffuse* into the texture, the
pattern does not carry ([`the-marsh.md`](./the-marsh.md) §3). The life-signal's maintenance
read separates live from fossil, vent from scar at the noise floor ([`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md)
§3); a rainy *walk* — a crossing that sheds a faint `ε²` wake ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md)
§2c) — depends on that wake's legibility to the maintenance read.

**The [design] over them — the rain's wet cost:**

> **Rain is nourishing but wet — it feeds a plot but muddies a signature.** A rainy walk
> crosses the field through a raised, mild high-`q` fall, and the same elevated-`q` wetness
> that feeds the beds blurs the walker's `ε²` wake — the marsh's tessellated blur on a
> smaller scale ([`the-marsh.md`](./the-marsh.md) §3): the wake does not carry as the one
> clean thing the maintenance read leans on, because the fall's own mild high-`q` texture
> is a *small* extra order the wake must stand out against. **A rainy walk loses some
> life-signal legibility — the blurred read at the noise floor** ([`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md)
> §3.2): a farmer who walked through a rain to reach a plot reads the bed's pulse a
> little less cleanly, the wake's faint trail a little less legible, for a few steps after
> the wet clears.

**The honest frame — the cost is the field's own, a real exchange, never a hidden**
**penalty.** The blur is not a "rain debuff"; it is the published `q`/`ε²` read at its mild
high-`q` texture — the same channels the reader always shows, read wet. The wetness is the
honest other-half of the rain's give: **the form that feeds the plot is the same fall that
dims the read** — nothing about a rain is one-sided. A settlement that reads the rain's
wet cost reads the same `q`/`ε²` it reads everywhere else, never a hidden-only mechanic.

### 3.2 No free energy — the Rain gives back at the field's own yield, never a mint

The corpus's single hard rule ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §6,
`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`) holds the Rain without exception, stated in its register:

> **The Rain gives back at the field's own yield, never a mint.** The rain's feed is the
> **water cycle's own return** ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2c — evaporation-to-rain as
> the envelope wave's round-trip): a fall of rain is the field's organized water returning
> to the land, raised by the same q-accumulation that makes the harvest. It provides
> **nothing beyond the field's own return** — a harvest taken after a rain is bounded by
> the bed's `q` and the tide, exactly as at any other time ([`farm-that-feeds.md`](./farm-that-feeds.md)
> §4 — yield is bounded by the bed's `q`; stewardship moves it within that bound). **There
> is no fall that yields:** a rain cannot be *farmed* into a store — the `(1−q)` waste law
> (§2) holds through the wet like any field state, the ambient pump is forbidden to charge
> a capacitor, and the feed is the field's own round-trip coherence, never a settled gain a
> settlement can bank.

Three concrete guards, all inherited:
1. **No rain-farm into a water-mint.** The fall's feed cannot be captured as stored
   coherence — a machine placed in a rain taps the `(1−q)` waste the same as anywhere,
   amplitude-capped by `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`, net-negative, the wet converting nothing.
2. **The feed is the field's own return.** A harvest after a rain is the water cycle's
   coherence, `the-sea.md` §2c's round-trip — the same charge the field organized on its
   own, never a gain the rain *added*. **A rain provides nothing beyond the field's own**
   **return** — the farmer harvests what the field grew, at the field's own yield, wet or
   dry.
3. **The wet's blur is information-loss, never a gain.** The muddy-signature cost (§3.1)
   is a loss of legibility, never a resource — it yields no coherence, no item, no
   advantage; it is the honest price of the give, the `(1−q)`-shaped other hand.

**The through-line is the corpus's both-sides honesty already proven by the flood.**
[`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §3 shows the cap holding against the temptation of
*abundance* — a q-surfeit must not mint, the deluge wastes upward. The Rain completes the
same both-sides honesty from the *giving* side: **the field's own water, even when it
feeds, gives back only what the field itself returned** — the harvest-weather is not a
generator, it is the water cycle's ordinary, honest hand.

## 4. The flood's beginning — the survival read

This is the load-bearing design. The flood doc's deluge **presupposes a boundary it never
names**: a "flood" is a surfeit that *overshoots a band* ([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md)
§2), and a band is only overshot by a rise that was, a moment before, *in* the band — a
rising water that was, before it became a deluge, ordinary rain. The flood's whole
honesty — "the harvest becomes the flood when it overshoots" ([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md)
§4.1, the farmer's warning) — rests on a settlement being able to *read the moment the
rising water stops being rain.* The Rain is that boundary, named:

> **A too-long rain is where the flood begins — rain is the flood at a safe rate, and
> reading whether a rising water is still rain or has become the flood is the survival
> read a settlement makes.** The Rain and the flood are the *same* channels read at a
> **rate**: both are a high-`q` water rising in a region. The Rain is that rise at a rate
> the field absorbs and recedes from; the **flood begins when the rate outpaces the
> take** — the rise stops receding, the regional `q` climbs *past* the enriching band, the
> accumulation crosses from "a yield the field can take" into "a surfeit it cannot"
> ([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §2). **The same channels read the two** — the rate of
> the `q`-rise, the trend's direction relative to the band's top — and the **rate read is
> the Rain's honest edge**, the readable-before-it-arrives discipline
> ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §5.1) applied to water.

**The rate read — the flood's approach readable-before-it-arrives, never a hidden**
**difficulty meter.** The corpus's cardinal rule is that hazards are *readable before they
arrive* ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §5.1; the sky reads first, then the
reader). The Rain's flood-beginning is a designed instance of that rule:

| The read | What a settlement reads | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| **The fall's rate** | the `q`-trend over a bounded window — is the water falling *within* the band (a rain, absorbing and receding) or *climbing toward and past* the band's top (the rate outpaces the take)? | [`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §2 (the enriching band; the surfeit threshold); the rain's form §2 this doc |
| **The band's top** | the enriching band's top edge — the flood doc's surfeit threshold, read as the line a rain crossing it stops being a rain | [`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §4.1 (the farmer's warning — the harvest becomes the flood when it overshoots) |
| **The recede** | a rain recedes (the `q` falls back into the band); the absence of recede — the accumulation holding — is the read that a rain has become a flood's beginning | [`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §4.1 (rise → bloom → shedding; a flood sheds, a too-long rain that stops shedding is the flood's onset) |

**[design], probe-calibrated, on the established readable channel.** The *threshold* —
how fast the `q`-rise, how far past the band's top before "rain" reads "flood's
beginning" — is this doc's [design] dial over the published `q`-trend, probe-calibrated
against the flood doc's surfeit threshold and the tide's measured accumulation
([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §5; `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5a). What is landed is
the enriching band, the surfeit threshold's overshoot, and the readable-before-it-arrives
discipline; **the rain→flood rate read is the designed boundary that names what the flood
doc presupposes.** It is **never a hidden difficulty meter**: the read is the same
`q`/`ε²` trend the Weatherglass and the reader already show, read over the long horizon —
a settlement that reads the rate reads the same field the flood doc's rising band reads,
one scale of water down.
