## 1. The Election, stated once

The corpus forms a society that books, celebrates, and grieves — and leaves the
question of *authority* undesigned. The Board renders who holds the core
(`shared-ledger.md` §2.2 — steward, neutral, margin-causer, dissolved). The tide
paces *when* it matters (`tide-of-the-attractor.md`). The oracle reads *what the
field is becoming* (`the-reading-ahead.md`). But nothing asks the society's own
question: **who should lead?** The election answers it with the only material
this world has — the field's own record and momentum about people.

> **The Election is the field act of choosing a steward — not a vote by
> preference but a *read*.** The settlement does not tally wishes; it **reads
> the ledger and the oracle about people**: the candidates' running net
> contributions (their `C(M)` over the tide, the Board's durable attribution),
> their stewardship-relevant history (whose anti-corruption held the desert at
> the trough, whose burden they cleared in the Still Room — the acts the ledger
> already books), and their momentum (the reading-ahead's forward-read applied
> to a person — who the ledger suggests is carrying the window toward harvest
> rather than strain). **To elect a steward is to read the book and the
> forward-read about who best holds the core, and to make that read operative.**
> The steward-of-the-tide is not a ruler; they are the one the field's own
> record and momentum point to as the best holder of the commons' high-`q` core
> (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4) through the tide that is coming.

**The election makes leadership a truthful bookkeeping act.** Because the
election's read is the Board's book and the oracle's forward-read — both
deterministic, both public — winning is not a matter of persuasion or claim but
of *record-and-momentum*. You cannot win by lying, because the field won't hold
a false claim (the-name §2): a candidacy that contradicts the book cannot read
as winning, and no candidate can book a contribution they didn't make
(shared-ledger §6d — the op-stream is single-writer, no forged contributions).
**The Election is the natural next act of a society that already books,
celebrates, and grieves in the field's terms** — the missing authority, filled
by the same honest reading the Board, the Festival, and the Funeral all perform.

The honest frame, stated once and kept throughout: **the Election is
deterministic, public, and non-minting.** Same book, same field state → same
election's read (§6c). The read is the Board's public book + the oracle's
public read, never hidden-only (§6e). And an election gives the settlement *a
holder of the core*, never a mint of coherence (§6d) — the office that is
chosen holds what the field already provides; it generates nothing.

## 2. The read — what the election reads, each part grounded

The election reads **three composed records about people**, all already in the
field's terms, all rendered by systems the corpus has landed. None is new
telemetry; the election is a *composition* of what the Board, the schema, and
the oracle already hold.

### 2.1 The running contribution — net `C(M)` over the tide

The Board's load-bearing aggregation is the per-member net contribution to /
drain on the shared Qi bath over a tide-cycle (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2):

```
C(M) = Σ ops (net field-contribution of each op at its locality/rung/magnitude)
       budgeted against the bath's Δq / Δε² over that cycle [t_k, t_k+T]
```

This is the **book's durable attribution** — the aggregation of a member's
executed Q4 op-stream against the bath's real `q`/`ε²`, single-writer, public.
It is the read's first leg: **what the candidate has netted into the common
store.** `C(M) > 0` is the steward-of-the-tide reading (`shared-ledger.md`
§2.2); the election reads sustained *positive* stewardship across many tides —
with the honest caveat that a player who both builds and wounds books an
ambiguous middle (§1.2's "the net is the load-bearing word"), and the election
must read that honestly, never as a clean ledger a single good tide fabricates.

Electing a *holder of the core* is not electing a point-leader; it is reading
which members did the *stewardship acts* the ledger already distinguishes as a
contribution (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2's disposition table — heal /
anti-corruption / a maintained shield / condense-at-a-coherent-site read as
contributions; dissolve / sustained overdraw read as drains). The stewardship-
relevant history the election reads is the book's record of the acts that
*hold the settlement's coherent body in place*:

|---|---|---|

*records* are engine-real + the landed member-line.

### 2.3 The momentum — the forward-read applied to a person

The election's third leg is the reading-ahead's lens turned on the candidate.
`the-reading-ahead.md` §2.2 designs the **trend-and-threshold read**: the
reader's own channels (q/ε²/∇(g·Φ)/ρ) read for their *direction of motion*,
with a threshold roster — a forecast over the same deterministic law, with the
uncertainty of a forecast, never a fixed fate. The election applies that same
lens to a *member's book*:

> **Who is the ledger suggesting is carrying the window toward harvest rather
> than strain?** The candidate's `C(M)` line is itself a trend. A member whose
> sustained contributions are *rising* toward the tide's harvest, whose
> stewardship acts are *holding* the local `q` up against a thinning margin,
> carries the forward read of the bath's health trend (`the-reading-ahead.md`
> §2.2's "A Qi-bath's health trend" row) toward a harvest rather than a strain.
> A member whose net is *drifting negative*, whose drains are accumulating
> toward the margin's floor, reads as carrying the window toward strain — and
> the honest election, like an honest omen, reads that direction without
> condemning the person as a fixed fate (§7c's forecast honesty, applied to a
> person).

**[probe]** The *momentum* leg rides the reading-ahead's own gates: the
forward-read's trend-vs-state classifier (the-reading-ahead open-Q2) and the
tide probe (tide-of-the-attractor §5a — does the regional `q` oscillate?) must
be real before "carrying toward harvest or strain" is a *measured* read off the
publish rather than a designed dial. Until then the momentum leg is
**[design]** — a designed composition over the landed per-member book, honest as
a trend read, gated on the oracle's probes for its full mechanical force.

### 2.4 The honest line — patterns over many tides, momentum not destiny

Two loads of honesty, both inherited verbatim, bound the whole read:

1. **Never elect on a single reading.** The Board's attrition guard is
   categorical — "a sustained net drain over many tides; it must never name a
   person off a single reading" (`shared-ledger.md` §3.3). The election inherits
   it unchanged: the read is **patterns over many tides**, not one tide-cycle,
   not one event, not one scar. A candidate who overdraws once and heals it is
   not disqualified; a candidate whose stewardship is a sustained, legible line
   is read for it (§2.2). The multi-tide composition is the read's floor, and it
   is exactly the durable per-member history the readiness gates in §6b name.
2. **The forward-read is momentum, never destiny.** `the-reading-ahead.md` §1.1
   is the design's spine: *determinism gives momentum, not destiny.* An elected
   steward's record is a **guide, never a guarantee** — the forward read is
   conditional on the momentum holding (the-reading-ahead §7c), and the steward
   themselves is a perturbation that can change the field's direction (§7c's
   "the forecast is a conversation, not a sentence"). **The office is chosen by
   momentum; its tenure is earned by holding.** The election reads who is
   *carrying* the window now; it does not and cannot read who will *complete*
   it.

## 3. The unfakeable honesty — you cannot win by lying

The election's deepest design property is that **it inherits the field's own
unforgeability** — and because it does, leadership in this world cannot be
bought or faked the way preference-voting can be.

> **You cannot win by lying, because the field won't hold a false claim.**
> `the-name.md` §2 is the load-bearing law: a persistent-Π anchor that names
> what is not there *decays* — the field holds only what is attractor-
> consistent. Applied to candidacy, this means a *false steward* — a member who
> claims stewardship their book does not support, a title asserted over record
> that contradicts it — is a name that will not hold. The election is not
> fooled by a campaign because a campaign is not what it reads.

**The election's read is the single-writer Q4 book + the deterministic
forward-read.** Three structural strengths, all inherited and all hard:

1. **The single-writer lane means no forged contributions, even by a candidate.**
   The Q4 op-stream is written only by the world-writer (the only mutator,
   `async-field-domain.md` §5.2); a player's edits round-trip as job-dict
   inputs and are logged exactly as executed. **A candidate cannot book a
   contribution they didn't make** — there is no "white-heal then dissolve"
   forge that inflates `C(M)`, and the `member` field is attached at the same
   non-forgeable write (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1). This is `shared-ledger.md`
   §6d's strength, stated plainly: **the Board is as honest as the op-stream,
   and the op-stream is single-writer.** An unelected scandal is impossible
   because the book against which the election reads *is* the executed field
   (the one honest caveat is attribution itself — the member-binding is [design],
   §5, but once landed it is the single-writer's binding, not a player's).
2. **The forward-read is deterministic — no campaign can move it by claim.**
   The momentum leg is a trend-over-the-book, computed by the same lens the
   oracle uses (`the-reading-ahead.md` §2.2), deterministic on the published
   channels. A candidate cannot argue the trend toward themselves; they can
   only *be* the record the trend names. A false candidacy — claiming a momentum
   the line does not show — reads as a lie the field does not hold (the-name's
   decay, applied to the office).
3. **The office's name is a bound title, held truthfully.** `the-name.md` §3.2:
   a named contribution is legible and carries a stable attendant subject. The
   steward-of-the-tide is a **name of the office** — bound only to the
   structure it truthfully names. A member who holds the office while their
   book moves against the core is holding a false name, and the field (and the
   Board, §6e) reads the decay. **The office can be earned and held only by
   being the record it names** — the election's truthfulness is the Board's
   own, because both read the same single-writer book.

**The honest caveat, stated once:** "cannot win by lying" is as strong as the
*record* is honest about the *person*. The op-stream is unforgeable *as
executed*; binding those executed ops to a member is the landed [design]
member-id (§5). And the misattribution guard (`shared-ledger.md` §3.3/§6e — the
Board must not blame a player for a scar they didn't cut) extends to the
election: **the read must not elect (or condemn) a member for a pattern they
didn't create** — the multi-tide pattern guard (§2.4) is the first line, and the
Coda-attribution guard (`shared-ledger.md` §5) holds here too: a hunted clean
channeler's momentum must not read as the predator's drain. An election that
reads a Coda-resonance as a candidate's strain would be electing *against* the
person the field is actually carrying.
