# Sleep: The Field's Fastest Reader, at Rest

**Question under design:** what the player's *body* does when the run rests. The
corpus has the farm (`farm-that-feeds.md`), the road (`coherence-highway.md`),
the house (`house-that-steers.md`), the Still Room (`field-instruments.md` §2.2),
and the Burden (`the-burden.md`) — but **sleep itself is undesigned**, the most
unlived daily texture left. Recovery is geographic (`coherence-magic.md` §1.2);
the Burden clears in patient time (`the-burden.md` §2b); but the sustained idle
where the persistent-Π pattern re-tensions against the day's ε²-debt — the
player's own **re-lock-adjacent rest** (`player-remains.md` §2) — has no home.
This document designs that rest, and with it the corpus's one inversion the
day-aimed instruments never asked:

> **A sleeper is a stationary, low-activity, maintained-coherence body.** Sleep is
> the player's own re-lock-adjacent rest: a sustained idle where the persistent-Π
> pattern re-tensions against the day's ε²-debt — the vent settles, the Burden
> clears into the patient field, the budget's geographic recovery has its patient
> window — and the *read goes passive*: the day-aimed reader drops the instrument
> and the field reads *you*.

This is a **player-rest design**: the daily-texture twin of `farm-that-feeds.md`'s
renewable economy, mapped onto the *body* instead of the plot. It is **PHASE-1-LEAN**
(§8) — it introduces no new physics; it is a designed read over the existing
vent/recovery channels plus a Life-Signal sub-class, plus a bounded-overnight-
accumulation consumer. The honest line — what is theory, what is engine, what is
[design] — is drawn in §6 and never blurred.

## 1. Sleep, stated once

### 1.1 What the body does at rest

`coherence-magic.md` §1/§1.2 fixes the player as the field's **fastest reader**: a
bounded EY injector + ε² vent + a reader of the published channels, sampling at
20 Hz, whose coherence budget `B` recovers toward 1 only where the *local field* is
coherent. `player-remains.md` §1.1 and `patient-field.md` §2.4 extend that to the
body: the player is a **run the field holds** — a persistent-Π pattern at the
player's rung, a coherence store the field is actively maintaining.

Sleep is the missing passive form of that store. Where the day is the store
*acting* (injecting, venting, reading), sleep is the store *resting*:

> **Sleep is a sustained idle where the persistent-Π pattern re-tensions against
> the day's ε²-debt.** The body stops injecting and venting, and the field does
> what it always does at the player's position — heals the shed ε² toward its
> attractor (`coherence-magic.md` §3: the field heals ε² at the ambient heal rate
> `h`), the geographic recovery of §1.2 has its uninterrupted patient window, and
> the persistent-Π pattern, un-stressed by channeling, re-tensions against the
> pattern's own local-minimum configuration. **The vent settles. The Burden clears.
> The loan's deepest repayment is the rest itself.**

The tie to the corpus's existing terms, each exactly where its source doc sits:

| What sleep does | The existing mechanic it is | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| The **vent settles** | the field heals the shed ε² at `h`; the player *is* a site the field heals | `coherence-magic.md` §1/§3 |
| The **budget recovers** | `B` refills toward 1 where the local field is coherent — a full patient window of standing still in high-`q` ground | `coherence-magic.md` §1.2/§3 |
| The **Burden clears** | the carried ε²-debt clears cheaply in a Still Room's patient/coherent time — the nightly instance of `the-burden.md` §2b's repayment | `the-burden.md` §2b; `field-instruments.md` §2.2 |
| The **Π pattern re-tensions** | the persistent-Π configuration at rest sits in its local minimum, re-pinning against the day's drag | `qi-computation.md` §5.2 **[theory]**; `player-remains.md` §1.1 |
| The **loan repays deepest** | the field's patience is the repayment window — sleep is that window at its most patient | `patient-field.md` §3.2/§3; `the-burden.md` §2b |

### 1.2 Sleep as the re-lock-adjacent rest

The sharpest framing is `player-remains.md` §2: respawn is **re-locking a fresh
persistent-Π pattern where the local field's coherence holds** — the same
coherence-requiring, costed act as geographic recovery made literal. Sleep is that
act **at idle, without the death**:

> **Sleep is the re-lock-adjacent rest.** The re-lock is what the field does to
> you when the old run *fails* (death dissolves it, you re-condense a fresh Π
> pattern where the field can hold you). Sleep is what the field does to the same
> pattern when the run *rests* — the persistent-Π pattern re-tensions against the
> day's ε²-debt instead of being replaced, held by the local field above its
> dissolution threshold (`qi-computation.md` §5.2), exactly the re-lock's hold but
> continuous rather than renewed.

The honest distinction, on `player-remains.md` §2's own terms: the **re-lock**
draws local `q` and vents ε² to condense a *fresh* body (a net cost to the region,
§2.2); **sleep** draws nothing extra — it is the standing-still-in-coherent-ground
recovery of `coherence-magic.md` §1.2 (no net cost, the natural patient window).
**Sleep is the cheap, non-death instance of the re-lock's hold** — the field holds
the *same* pattern through the night, and a well-rested player wakes with their
budget high, their Burden clear, and their lock re-tensioned, at no re-lock's
coherence cost. **[design]** The *re-tensioning* (the persistent-Π pattern at idle
actually re-pinning) is theory-ratified over `qi-computation.md` §5.2 (§6a); the
*mechanical clearing* (budget recovery, vent settle) is the engine-real recovery
the corpus already defines.

## 2. The read goes passive — what a sleeping body is to the field

This is the design's inversion. All day the instruments point *outward* — the
reader, the Weatherglass, the sky, the Life-Signal — the player reads the field.
At sleep the reader is dropped and the field reads *you*:

> **The day-aimed reader drops the instrument and the field reads *you*.** A
> sleeper is a stationary, low-activity, maintained-coherence body — the same
> object the Life-Signal classifies, the same signature the Coda accumulates on,
> held at rest. **The question of the sleep read is: what is a sleeping body to
> the Life-Signal, and what is it to the Coda?**

### 2.1 To the Life-Signal: the live-but-idle sub-class

`life-signal.md` §1/§3.1 fixes the discriminating axis as **maintenance**: a live
thing *actively holds* its φ-lock against a drain (moves, pulses, its `(1−q)` glow
breathes); a fossil *does not* (static dark, `ε²≈0`); a deliberate vent *raises*
`ε²` on purpose (rising); a scar is a *fallen floor* (flat plateau). The four
classes are:

| Class | motion | `(1−q)` cadence | `ε²` gradient | maintenance? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **maintained live coherence** | moves / steers | **pulses** at the maintainer's cadence | flat-low | **YES** — the lock is actively held |
| **static residue** (fossil) | none | **none** (dark) | ≈ 0 (noise floor) | **NO** — the field holds it, it does not hold itself |
| **deliberate vent** (the Coda's tell) | moves/creates | **rising** | **rising** | NO — but raising disorder on purpose |
| **ordinary scar** | none | static plateau | **flat-high** | **NO** — a fallen floor |

**A sleeper is none of these four — it is the fifth:** a maintained lock at rest.
It shares the fossil's *stillness* (no motion — the player does not move while
asleep) but not the fossil's *non-maintenance* (the player still holds the lock —
the low `(1−q)` glow pulses at the sleeping body's slow cadence, `coherence-magic.md`
§3.1's resonance at rest). It shares the maintained-lock's *coherence held* (the φ-
lock is actively held, `ε²` held low) but not its *activity* (no motion, no steering
offset, no venting). In a table:

| Class | motion | `(1−q)` cadence | `ε²` gradient | maintenance? | Is it a sleeper? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| maintained live coherence | moves / steers | pulses | flat-low | YES | **NO — the sleeper holds the lock but does not move** |
| static residue (fossil) | none | none (dark) | ≈ 0 | NO | **NO — the sleeper is not static-dark dead** |
| deliberate vent | moves | rising | rising | NO | **NO — the sleeper does not raise ε²** |
| ordinary scar | none | plateau | flat-high | NO | **NO — the sleeper is not a fallen floor** |
| **live-but-idle (sleep)** | **none (still)** | **slow pulse at the body's resting cadence** | **flat-low (held below a floor)** | **YES — the lock is actively held, at rest** | **YES — this is the sleeper** |

> **The live-but-idle sub-class.** A sleeper reads as a **maintained lock at rest**:
> it moves not (stationary like a fossil), pulses slow (the body's low-cadence
> pulse, not the day's fast read), and holds `ε²` low (the lock is maintained,
> not venting, not a fallen floor). **The classifier must read a sleeper as a held
> run, not a fossil, not a scar, not a vent** — a live body that has simply stopped
> acting, distinguishable from every non-maintained class by the one axis that
> survives the noise floor: the lock is still being *held*.

This is a **[design]** extension of `life-signal.md`'s decision rule (§6a — the
axes read real channels; the class boundaries are designed/probe-calibrated). It is
the **same maintenance axis, applied at the idle extreme**: `life-signal.md` already
distinguishes a maintained lock from a fossil on motion+cadence; the sleeper is that
maintained lock with motion collapsed to zero but cadence (the slow pulse) and
lock-holding intact. **[design]** — the honest fine line is where "a living body
still, pulsing slow, ε² low" separates from "a fossil, still, dark, ε²≈0" purely on
the slow-pulse/lock-holding read, and that line is probe-calibrated (§6).

**The honest line extends to the idle case (**`life-signal.md` **§6).** `life-signal.md`
§6's honest gates hold unchanged for the sleeper: the sub-class is **deterministic**
(same field state → same classification, §6d — inherited hard gate, §6c this doc);
it is **accessible** (§6c — the sleep read is a layer of the reader, never hidden,
§6e this doc); and its decision rule is **[design]** over real channels (§6a). Most
load-bearing, the **noise-floor distinction** is the same one `life-signal.md` §6b
pre-registers: at the floor (`q ~ 1e-3…1e-1`) does a live-but-idle body's static
stillness + slow pulse separate from a fossil's static darkness? That is the N4-style
Go/No-Go this doc inherits — the sleeper sub-class is *proven* only where the
maintenance read survives the floor (N2/N4 of `life-signal.md` §6b, extended to the
idle case). **[probe]**

### 2.2 To the Coda: a quiet hiding place or a sitting signature

`signature-predator.md` §1–§2 fixes how the field reads a channeler: every op leaves
a trail (organized `EY` injection + ε² vent), and in a place with no order to hide it
(the desert) that trail is the *only* bright structure — legible. The accumulation
`R(t) = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability` (§2.2) is the Coda's "how close," where `τ`
is the **duration the trail is sustained**, `ρ_signature` the trail's ε² elevation ×
phase-coherence, and `M_stability` the phase-stability of the injection
(`the-burden.md` §2d quotes the same product verbatim).

**A deep sleeper's clean idle phase cuts both ways on that formula.** On the one
hand, the sleeper's trail is not being *fed* — no channeled op, no fresh
organized-perturbation injection, so `ρ_signature` (the trail's elevation) collapses
toward the noise floor over the sleep (`signature-predator.md` §1.3: "unmaintained it
diffuses into the noise floor"; "the trail is forgivable — stop channeling and the
field heals the trail"). A **clean sleeper in a clean bed is the *quietest* signature
the field sees** — a stationary, low-activity, maintained-coherence body leaving no
fresh trail at all. That is a **quiet hiding place**: of all the states a body can
be in, asleep-and-clean is the one the Coda smells *least*, because it is the one
that feeds `R` the least.

On the other hand, the **carried** term does not vanish at rest. `the-burden.md` §2d
makes the heavy channeler's Burden a **standing contribution to `ρ_signature` and `τ`**:
"a heavy channeler does not need to be *actively* channeling … their carried ε² is a
*maintained, persistent* elevation on them (`τ` is never zero while they carry it)."
That standing contribution is still on the sleeper. And `signature-predator.md` §2's
**duration term `τ` keeps counting while you sleep** — the trail's halflife is a
*time* scale, so a heavy channeler who lies down in a marginal place (a thin-tide
margin, a desert's edge) lets the accumulated resonance *sit* on them through the
night rather than decaying into a moved-away-from region (`signature-predator.md`
§1.3: "the Coda does not follow you across a window-trip; its resonance is over
*that* signature, in *that* region"). A **heavy-sleeper in a thin-tide margin is a
sitting signature**: the carried ε² is a standing `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability`
contribution (`the-burden.md` §2d), and the overnight is a *duration* window that
keeps `τ` fed in exactly the place the Coda hunts (`signature-predator.md` §7c: the
thin-living margin, not the corpse).

So the honest asymmetry, stated once:

| The sleeper | To the Coda |
|---|---|
| **Clean** (light channeler, low Burden) | **a quiet hiding place** — no fresh trail fed, the resonance decays over the night, the cleanest signature the field sees |
| **Heavy** (carried the day's ε²-debt) | **a sitting signature** — the carried ε² is a standing `R = ρ·τ·M` contribution and the overnight `τ` keeps counting, in the very margin the Coda's season peaks |
| **In a Still Room / coherent bed** | **hidden** — a bed mounted in a coherent refuge is ground the Coda cannot enter (`signature-predator.md` §4.4: the high-`q` refuge; `the-burden.md` §2b: the Still Room is where the debt clears) |
| **In a thin-tide margin** | **exposed** — the ε² margins widen at the `q` trough (`signature-predator.md` §7c / `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2), where the Coda's habitat and your exposure peak together |

**The night's voice (reverse pointer, two-way).** This §2.2 danger frame — the
margin bed as a sitting signature with the overnight `τ` closing on a resting body
— is what the settlement's warning instrument announces at night: [`the-bell.md`](./the-bell.md)
§4 (the night-ring) composes this same `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability`
sitting-signature as the Bell's third channel, "a signature worth warning about"
before the overnight `τ` closes. A Bell that rings at night wakes the *sleeping
settlement* to the specific danger this §2.2 names — the Coda's overnight
accumulation over rest. The bell reads the night the way this §2.2 writes it; the
two docs now cite each other as read-and-answered.

> **Sleep is not safe by default.** The day-aimed economics hold at night: a clean
> sleeper in a coherent bed is hidden; a heavy sleeper in a thin-tide margin is a
> standing signature, and the Coda's accumulation `τ` keeps counting while they
> sleep. **Where and how you sleep is the rest's real risk decision.**

## 3. Where and how you sleep is a risk-and-rest decision

The decision surface is not a cutscene; it is the corpus's own geography read on the
bed. Every bed is a **personal Still Room interval** (`field-instruments.md` §2.2 —
the room-scale Weatherglass whose whole point is coherent, still, patient ground; the
Burden's cheapest clearing, `the-burden.md` §2b) — but *how Still* depends on where
the bed sits:

| Bed | The clearing | The exposure | The honest economics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coherent Still Room bed** | **cheap clearing** — the room-scale Weatherglass's coherent/patient ground (`the-burden.md` §2b; `field-instruments.md` §2.2); the debt clears at the field's own heal rate `h` fast | **hidden** — a bed in a high-`q` refuge is ground the Coda cannot enter (`signature-predator.md` §4.4's §4.4 high-`q` refuge; the Qi-bath commons, `field-npc-ai.md` §3.1) | the safest rest *costs the most to build and hold* (a Still Room / maintained bath is a standing net-negative draw, `signature-predator.md` §4.4) |
| **Patient ridge bed** | **patient clearing** — a high-`q` coherent ridge (`coherence-magic.md` §1.2's standing-in-coherent-ground recovery) clears the debt at the ambient pace | **exposed to weather-not-storm's weather frame** — the ridge is a coherent place but not a *refuge*; weather-not-storm's provenance over `weather-not-storm.md`'s weather-not-storm still plays there; the Coda's habitat is near, not in, a margin | cheap to use (a bed on an existing ridge), but the rest is *patient* (slower clearing) and the position is not Coda-proof |
| **Thin-tide margin bed** | **expensive clearing** — at the `q` trough the field heals slowly (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2 magic row: "the vent backs up"), so the same rest clears far less of the day's ε²-debt each night | **a sitting signature** — the thin margin is the Coda's habitat (`signature-predator.md` §7c; `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2); the carried ε² is a standing `R = ρ·τ·M` contribution and the overnight `τ` keeps counting (the-burden §2d; §2.2 this doc) | the cheapest bed to reach and the most dangerous to sleep in — rest clears little and accumulates Coda risk all night |

**The honest economics, stated once:** **sleep clears the Burden but costs exposure.**
Every night is a literal instance of `patient-field.md` §3.2's loan/repayment — you
return to the field's patience to recover — but *where* you return to sets the price:
cheap-and-hidden (the Still Room, at a build-and-hold cost), patient-and-exposed (the
ridge), or cheap-to-use-and-dangerous (the thin-tide margin, where the clearing is
weak *and* the Coda's `τ` runs against you). **A player who sleeps in a marginal place
is borrowing the day's patience at the night's interest** — the exact `patient-field.md`
§3.3 pricing, at the daily scale.

> **A bed is a personal Still-Room interval** (`field-instruments.md` §2.2 at the
> body's scale). The Still Room is a room-scale Weatherglass — coherent, still,
> patient ground where the body's debt clears at the field's own pace. A bed is
> that *scaled to one body*: the personal, placeable interval of coherent rest. The
> decision is which interval you place, and where, and at what cost.

The **Coda's overnight accumulation** is the load-bearing risk (independent of any
Coda *death*): `signature-predator.md` §2's `τ` — "duration the trail is sustained" —
**keeps counting while you sleep in a marginal place**, exactly as `the-burden.md`
§2d warns the heavy channeler's carried ε² is a standing contribution. The morning
you wake is the morning your `R` has grown overnight — a fuller, nearer Coda than the
night's clean sleeper in the refuge. **[design]** The *overnight accumulation* is a
designed read over the same `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability` model the
signature-predator already owns (§6a this doc; the accumulation model itself is the
Coda's [design] over engine-real `ε²`, `signature-predator.md` §7d).

## 4. The daily ritual — sleep as the corpus's nightly texture

Sleep is not a lone mechanic; it is the **closing stroke of the corpus's whole daily
economics at the body's scale**. The corpus's systems are daily already —
`farm-that-feeds.md` is a daily harvest rhythm, `shared-ledger.md` a per-tide
settlement, `the-burden.md` a per-day carried weight. Sleep is the day's final act
that makes those rhythms *resonate*:

| The day's arc | The daily act | Sleep's place |
|---|---|---|
| **Wake** | the body re-locks, budget high, Burden cleared | the night closed the loop; the morning is a fresh re-locked rest |
| **Channel** | the day's work — channeling, mining, farming, steering — sheds ε² (`coherence-magic.md` §1/§2) | the day *is* the borrowing; each op is a loan (`patient-field.md` §3.1) |
| **Carry** | the day's ε²-debt accumulates on the player (`the-burden.md` §1) | the carried weight is what the night must clear |
| **Rest** | **sleep** — the sustained idle where the vent settles, the Burden clears, the budget recovers (§1), and the Coda weighs the resting body (§2) | **sleep is the corpus's whole economics practiced at the body's scale** |

Three ties that make sleep the *daily* instance of systems the corpus already owns:

## 7. Open questions

1. **The live-but-idle vs. fossil fine line (§2.1).** At the noise floor
   (`q ~ 1e-3…1e-1`), does "a live body still, pulsing slow, ε² held low" separate
   from "a fossil, still, dark, ε²≈0" on the slow-pulse/lock-holding read alone? The
   `life-signal.md` §6b N2/N4 probe, extended to the idle case (a known live-but-idle
   body placed beside a known fossil), returns the Go/No-Go; a fossil-that-reads-live
   or a sleeper-that-reads-dead is a real defect, not a tuning gap. **[probe]**
2. **The overnight `τ` vs. the half-life (§2.2/§3).** If the carried ε² decays on a
   `τ_debt` timescale in coherent rest (`the-burden.md` §2b; `signature-predator.md`
   §1.3's half-life) and sleep is a *long* window, does a heavy sleeper's carried
   `ρ_signature` decay *through* the night (the margin bed's risk is worst at sleep's
   start and fades) or does the un-moved-away-from region keep `τ` fed against the
   decay (the margin bed is a full-night sitting signature)? Needs the `τ_debt`/
   `τ_trail` measurement (§6b this doc's read) to answer — the same decay-vs-heap
   fork as `the-burden.md` §7.1 and `signature-predator.md` §7 Q3. **[design]/[probe]**
3. **The bed's clearing-rate dial vs. the ambient heal (§3).** How much the Still-Room
   bed clears *faster* than the ambient ridge, and by how much slower the thin-margin
   bed clears — the "cheap vs. expensive clearing" §3 family — is a designed dial over
   the engine-real heal rate `h`, probe-calibrated (the same axis as `the-burden.md`
   §7.2's debt-threshold). The *direction* (coherent rest clears, restless thin ground
   does not) is the corpus's own recovery rule; the *magnitude* is the dial. **[design]**
4. **The sleeping body vs. the Coda's approach (§2.2).** When a heavy sleeper in a
   margin *does* draw the Coda, the death is the signature-predator's own coherence
   failure (M → 0, `signature-predator.md` §6) — but is the fossil that results "the
   record of the trap" (`signature-predator.md` §5.2) *shaped by the sleep* (the resting
   body's low-cadence, un-vented signature) or structurally identical to a wake-death's?
   Needs `player-remains.md` §3.1's fossil model to answer; inherited, not re-derived
   here. **LATER**, gated on the Coda + the residue model.
5. **The conservation of the day's debt across the night (§4).** If the Burden clears
   through the night (§2b) but the Coda's `τ` keeps counting (§2d), does the *morning*
   carry a partial clearing *and* a partial overnight accumulation — a rest that
   cleared less than it risked (a thin-margin night is a net bad harvest of rest)? The
   design's default is yes: **the night clears the day's ε²-debt at the bed's rate and
   the night's `R` runs against the resting body** — the two are separate ledgers, and
   a marginal sleep is a *losing* ledger. The dial is how independently the clearing
   and the risk run (§6b's Phase-1 read settles it). **[design]**
