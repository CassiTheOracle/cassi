## 3. The order spent into many — the load-bearing design

**The branching is the field's own `(1−q)` dissipation, never a hidden maze.** This
is the load-bearing design: the delta is not a network that *mints* — it is the
single line's coherent carry **spent into many**, the field's own conservation
dividing one carrying filament into several thinner ones, honestly.

**The landed pieces.** [`the-river.md`](./the-river.md) §2 lands the river's carry
as the coherent filament's `∇(g·Φ)` descent — the field's own transport, a raft
rides the flow, never a mint (the river provides nothing: the flow is the field's
own, `the-river.md` §4/the-cap). [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md)
§2 lands the `(1−q)` waste law, `E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput` — every working
field system wastes this fraction as it runs — and §6 lands the no-free-energy cap,
`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` — no conversion yields more than it sinks, no reservoir becomes
a net-energy mint. The estuary's wealth already obeys the cap (the mixing feeds at
the field's own yield, never a mint — `the-estuary.md` §4).

**The [design] over them — the fan's total is the line's own order spent at the
`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` bound:**

> **Each delta channel carries less than the line did, and the fan's total is the
> river's own order spent at the `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` bound — never a network that
> mints.** The single coherent carry ([`the-river.md`](./the-river.md) §2) dividing
> into many thinner carries is the field's own conservation: the `(1−q)` waste law
> (`E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput`, `energy-harnessing.md` §2) holds the branching's
> loss — the fan is the line's order *spent into many*, each arm dissipating the
> usual fraction, nothing gained by the dividing. **The delta converts nothing** —
> the fan mints no new carry, no hidden channel carries more than its share, no
> braid is a hidden corridor the field secretly awards; the fan's total is bounded
> by the line's own order, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` (`energy-harnessing.md` §6), never
> a network that produces. And the fan is **deterministic** — same river state,
> same field state → same delta, never a seeded channel roll.

**[design]** — the *order-spent frame*. The `(1−q)` waste law and the `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`
cap are energy-harnessing's landed pieces; the river's carry is the-river's landed
piece. **This doc's [design]** is *that the river's branching is exactly that waste
law read at the fan* — that a delta is the line's order spent, its total held at the
cap, its many arms each thinner than the line, **never a hidden bounty and never a
hidden maze**. This is the load-bearing honesty: **a delta that "yielded" — a fan
that minted its arms, a hidden channel that carried a secret extra, a braid that
read as a shortcut — would be a false network the cap forbids**, exactly as a
fertile estuary that farmed its own blend is a lie the cap forbids (the-estuary §4)
and a river that "produced" is a false line (the-river §4).

### The determinism hard gate, stated once

The fan is a **pure function of the river's flowing band** — the same field state
yields the same fan, the same channels, the same banks, reproducible and
teachable. **Same river state → same delta** ([§5c](#5c-determinism--a-hard-gate)).
There is no seeded channel roll: a player who learns where the fan's arms braid,
which bank hides, and how the water crosses learns it *every* run, exactly as the
river's current and the estuary's blend are deterministic facts about the field.
