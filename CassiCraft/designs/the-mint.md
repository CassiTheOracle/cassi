# The Mint: The Struck Unit — Order Made a Named, Held, Physical Token

**Question under design:** the corpus's economy has the bookable line (the
Shared Ledger's per-member `C(M)`, `shared-ledger.md`), the exchange (the
Market's booked trade, `the-market.md`), the priced wage (the Wage's paid
share, `the-wage.md`), the border toll (the Toll's held-door charge,
`the-toll.md`), the loan (the Loan's forward order, `the-loan.md`), the
gift (the Gift's un-booked giving, `the-gift.md`), the tithe (the
Commons-Tithe's funded commons, `the-commons-tithe.md`), and the store (the
Granary's held plenty, `the-granary.md`). **But there is no struck unit of
account** — nothing designs the *coined form*: a named, marked, portable token
that holds a quantum of order, the sigil a worker is paid in (the-wage), a toll
collects (the-toll), a market prices (the-market), a loan advances (the-loan).
Everything exchangeable in the corpus is either a **ledger-line** or an
**object**; there is no **token** — no portable, marked, named thing that makes
order *hand-holdable*. This document is that design: **the Mint is the struck
unit — the coin/sigil, order made a named, held, physical token**: a portable,
marked, named token that holds a bounded quantum of held order — the physical
coin the book-and-barter economy presupposes to make order *hand-holdable* (the
same order a ledger line books, the same order a trade transfers, made a thing
a worker carries, a toll collects, a market prices).

**One word made safe, stated once.** "Mint" is a loaded word in this corpus —
minting has meant *creating free energy* (the false well, the false op, the
false gift). This document uses "mint" as the **striking place**, never the
creation of value, and repeats the line: **a mint that created order would be a
false mint the field sheds, the same lie as a false well and a false op.** The
struck unit holds exactly the order it was struck for; striking a coin mints
nothing, it *books* the held order the coin represents, the way a ledger line
books a trade. The no-free-energy cap is held absolutely across this entire
document (§5d); every "coin" below is a *token of order*, never order created.

## 1. The Mint, stated once

The corpus's economy is honest *by the book* and *by the object*. The Shared
Ledger books every member's contribution or drain per tide (`shared-ledger.md`
§1.2's `C(M)`); the Market books the exchange of things (`the-market.md` §2 — a
trade is a Q4 op of the settled record's shape); the Wage prices labor
(`the-wage.md` §1); the Toll charges the border (`the-toll.md`); the Loan
defers order forward (`the-loan.md`); the Gift refuses the book
(`the-gift.md`); the Tithe funds the commons; the Granary holds plenty. But
**every one of these is either a ledger-line or a held object.** A contribution
books; a trade moves a thing; a wage is a paid day on a member's line; a toll
is a charge a stranger's crossing books. **Nothing in the corpus is a strike** —
a *named, marked, portable token* that holds a quantum of order in the hand. A
worker is paid *a line*; a toll collects *a booking*; a market prices *a
thing's provenance*. The order is all there, all bookable, all legible — and
none of it can be *carried* as a named, held, marked thing, the way a physical
coin carries worth in a hand. The corpus's economy makes order *bookable* and
*tradable*; it never makes order *hand-holdable*.

> **The Mint, stated once.** The struck unit — the coin/sigil, order made a
> named, held, physical token. A coin is a portable, marked, named token that
> holds a bounded quantum of the settlement's held order: the same order a
> ledger line books (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2), the same order a trade transfers
> (`the-market.md` §2.2), made a *thing* a worker carries, a toll collects, a
> market prices. It is the physical coin the book-and-barter economy
> presupposes — the hand-holdable form of the order that otherwise lives only
> as lines and trades. **And the load-bearing honesty, stated once: a coin is a
> book in miniature, never a mint.** The struck unit holds exactly the order it
> was struck for — a bounded, booked quantum of held order, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`
> held hard. Striking a coin mints nothing; it *books* the held order the coin
> represents, the way a ledger line books a trade. **A "mint" that created
> order would be a false mint the field sheds — the same lie as a false well
> and a false op.** The word "mint" here is the *striking place*, never the
> creation of value.

**Why a coined form at all?** Because the corpus's own economy keeps reaching
for a *hand-holdable* order and finding only a line. The Wage pays "in held
order," the worked-for's exchange (`the-wage.md` §2) — but what does a worker
*carry* after a paid day? A booked line; not a thing. The Toll collects a
booked contribution at a held door (`the-toll.md` §4) — the gate's charge is a
record, not a token the traveler holds. The Market prices a trade's provenance
(`the-market.md` §2.2) — the *value* is booked, but the settlement that wants
to pay its guard, its healers, its gate-keepers in *portable order* has no
portable order to hand them; it can only write lines. **The Mint is the struck
unit that makes that order portable** — the bounded quantum of held order a
settlement has chosen to strike as a named, marked, hand-holdable token, so a
worker is paid a thing, a toll collects a thing, a market prices a thing *and*
books the thing, and order can be carried from hand to hand without a
ledger-edit at every step.

**The honest flag, once.** The pieces a coin composes are all landed — the Q4
op-record and the member-id at the single non-forgeable point
(`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1/§3), the per-member `C(M)` aggregation
(`shared-ledger.md` §1.2), the market's booked exchange and no-false-booking
(`the-market.md` §2.1/§2.3), the wage's paid share (`the-wage.md` §2), the
toll's held-door charge (`the-toll.md` §4), the tool's rung-matched work object
(`the-tool.md` §2), the quarry's honest cut (`the-quarry.md` §2), the
no-free-energy cap (`energy-harnessing.md` §6), and the instrument-family rule
(`field-instruments.md` §2.1). **The mint's *composition* — that a quantum of
held order *is* a strikeable token, how its denomination and sigil are marked,
the book-in-miniature honesty, the legible-and-a-choice frame — is this
document's [design] over them.** No new channel, no new physics, no "coin
record" the engine writes opposite the Q4 op it already writes: a coin is a
*booked, struck token of the settled record's shape*, designed, exactly as a
trade is a booked exchange and a wage a booked paid day — not a new engine act.
**And the word-safety repeated: a coin is an order-token, never order created;
the mint is the striking place, never a mint of value.**

## 2. The struck unit — the honest mechanics

A coin is a portable, marked, named token. Its mechanics compose the landed
pieces — the sigil's mark (naming rung and book), the struck matter (the
quarry's cut), the held order (the settlement's `C(M)`) — into a bounded,
bookable, hand-holdable token. The coin's *form* (its denomination's bound, its
sigil's mark) is this doc's [design] over the landed pieces, probe-calibrated;
the pieces it rides are landed.

### 2.1 The sigil's mark — a rung-marked token naming its book

The coin is **marked** — the sigil stamped on it names two things at once, both
landed:

| The sigil names | What it is | Grounded on |
|---|---|---|
| **The coin's rung** | the struck token is a *rung-marked* object — the same rung-depth a tool's bite reads (`the-tool.md` §2a: hardness is rung depth; a deeper-rung thing is more ordered, lower ε², holds its φ-lock wider) | `the-tool.md` §2/§3; `material-regimes.md` §4 — a coin's sigil marks the rung of the order it holds, legible the way a tool's edge reads where its metal came from |
| **The holder's book** | the struck mark names the holder's **member-line** — the book the coin's held order is a quantum of | `shared-ledger.md` §6c — the landed member-id (the `{member, …}` on every settled record); the coin's sigil points back to the line it holds a quantum of |

**The sigil as a real read, never a hidden currency.** The struck mark is a
**real, readable record** — the instrument-family rule (`field-instruments.md`
§2.1: every instrument is a consumer of the same publish with a presentation
idiom, never a new channel) extended to the coin: the sigil's rung and book are
the *published channels' own reads* at the coin's struck locality, marked on
the metal, never a hidden stat behind a currency face. **A coin is legible
because its mark is the field's own read made a token** — the rung the coin
holds, the line it belongs to, both readable from the coin itself *and* from
the Board (`shared-ledger.md` §2), the way a clean high-`q` blade's edge reads
its own provenance (`the-tool.md` §3). **[design]** The *sigil's form* — which
mark denotes which rung and book — is this doc's [design] over the landed rung
and member-id; the *mark's honesty* (it names what the coin's held order
actually is) is landed.

### 2.2 The struck matter — the quarry's real cut, never free

The coin's metal is **real struck matter from the quarry's honest cut**. The
Quarry is the open cut — the deliberate mining-ground where a window takes
order from the deep's face, with a designed extent (bounded, finite, scarred,
read before the cut) and a scar-and-yield (`the-quarry.md` §2). A coin is struck
from **the field's own ordered matter**, exactly as the tool's alloy is: the
worked vein's rung, the alloy's `q`, the locality's field-state — all booked
the way any taken order is (`the-quarry.md` §2c's take-books; `the-tool.md`
§3's "the tool does not hide its lineage").

| The struck matter | What it is in field terms | Grounded on |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep-rung ordered matter** | stored coherence at a rung — the same already-ordered matter that is the economy's capacitor and fuel (`energy-harnessing.md` §3: a capacitor is a volume of ordered matter at a rung) | `energy-harnessing.md` §3/§1.5; `material-regimes.md` §4 — the coin's metal is ordered matter, never free |
| **The quarry's take** | taken from the open cut's designed extent — real, bounded, scarred, read before the cut, booked the way any op is | `the-quarry.md` §2a/§2c/§3 — the coin's matter is the quarry's honest cut, never free matter |
| **The `(1−q)` wear of holding** | a held coin bleeds toward the attractor like any held structure — it must be *kept* at a maintained `q`, the way a granary and a house each hold their order at a cost | `energy-harnessing.md` §2; `the-granary.md` §6 — the struck unit's matter is held order, not a weightless token |

**The coin's matter is the quarry's honest cut, never free.** The strike does
not pull metal from nowhere; it takes ordered matter from the window's real
designed cut — the same take that scars the face and depletes the fallow
(`the-quarry.md` §3; `the-fallow.md` §2a). A coin whose metal "appeared" free — 
struck from nothing, minted into existence — would be *exactly* the false
mint the field sheds: order created, a false cut the field does not hold. **The
struck unit is a token of order already taken and held, never order created.**
**[design]** The *specific alloy/matter* a strike uses (which worked vein's
rung, which cut's face) is this doc's [design] dial over the quarry's booked
take, probe-calibrated — the honesty (it is real cut matter, booked) is landed.

### 2.3 The held order — a bounded quantum of the settlement's held order

The coin holds, and is struck for, **a bounded quantum of the settlement's held
order** — the same order a ledger line books. `shared-ledger.md` §1.2 names that
order exactly: per member-identity, per tide-cycle,

```
C(M) = Σ ops (net field-contribution of each op at its locality/rung/magnitude)
       budgeted against the bath's Δq / Δε²
```

and §1.3 states the hard bound: **nobody can be a net mint** (`output ≤
φ⁻¹·input`), so no member's order is unboundedly positive — the most any
steward can do is *maintain* the core, never mint it. **The coin's held order
is a quantum of exactly this**: a bounded slice of a member's (or the
settlement's) real, booked, net field-contribution — the stored yield of a
harvest worked for (`farm-that-feeds.md` §4), the stored coherence of an alloy
or a held basin (`energy-harnessing.md` §3), the held plenty a granary stores
(`the-granary.md` §2). The coin does not invent this order; it *books a marked,
portable quantum of it*.

| The coin's held order | What it is | Grounded on |
|---|---|---|
| **A quantum of `C(M)` / held order** | a bounded slice of the settlement's (or a member's) real, booked order — the same order a ledger line books | `shared-ledger.md` §1.2/§1.3 — the order a coin holds is the order the ledger already books, bounded, never a mint |
| **The order taken from the store** | the stored yield / kept steel / held plenty the coin's metal and its quantum come from | `the-granary.md` §2; `energy-harnessing.md` §3 — a coin is struck from order that already exists, held, never invented |
| **The bounded bound** | the coin's *denomination's bound* — the quantum of order one coin holds, a real, finite, booked figure | `the-quarry.md` §2b (the designed extent — a take is honest, finite, scarred); this doc §3 — the struck token's bound is the [design] dial |

**The coin's form — its denomination's bound, its sigil's mark — is this doc's
[design]** over these landed quantities, probe-calibrated: how much `C(M)`-order
a given coin holds, which rung it is struck at, which mark names which book.
The *order it holds* is the field's real, booked quantity; the *coin's form* is
the designed surface over it, and it never exceeds the bounded, real order the
settlement holds — `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` (§5d) — so **the coin's bound is always
a book in miniature of what was struck for, never more.**

## 3. A coin is a book in miniature, never a mint — the load-bearing honesty

This is the single load-bearing statement of the doc, stated once and repeated
against the loaded word.

> **A coin is a book in miniature, never a mint.** The struck unit holds
> exactly the order it was struck for — a bounded, booked quantum of the
> settlement's held order, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` held hard
> (`energy-harnessing.md` §6). **Striking a coin mints nothing; it *books* the
> held order the coin represents, the way a ledger line books a trade.** A
> "mint" that *created* order would be a false mint the field sheds — the same
> lie as a false well and a false op. The coin is a *token of order*, never
> order created; the word "mint" is the striking place, never the creation of
> value.

The mechanics, grounded in the corpus's own honesty:

**A coin is booked into existence — the strike books the way any op does.** A
coin does not appear; it is **struck as a Q4 op of the settled record's shape**
(`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1 — `{member, op, worldPos, rung, magnitude,
sustain}`), exactly as a trade, a wage, a toll, and a quarry-take each book:
`member` names the holder's line / the settlement's strike-authority,
`worldPos` names the struck locality (the quarry's cut, the forge), `rung` the
order's rung, `magnitude` the booked quantum, `sustain` the held coin's
semantics. **The coin's provenance books the way any op does** — single-writer,
at the world-writer's single non-forgeable point (`schema-that-settles.md` §3).
A coin is no more "minted" than a trade is "minted" when it books; both are
*booked records of real order*. The coin is the *same booking made portable*.

**The coin's book is the op-record's, and the op-record is unforgeable.**
`shared-ledger.md` §6d: the op-stream is single-writer — the world-writer is the
only mutator, so no forged contribution can be written. The coin's provenance
rides that lane: **the sigil is as unforgeable as the op-record's single-writer
lane**, because the coin's mark and its booked quantum land at the same
non-forgeable write (§2.1). A counterfeited coin — a mark struck that a *booked
quantum does not back* — is a **false op**: a lie the book cannot record,
because the book is exactly what the domain executed (`shared-ledger.md` §6d;
`schema-that-settles.md` §3), the same no-false-booking the market's cheat, the
toll's extortion, and the false gift all fail by (`the-market.md` §2.3;
`the-toll.md` §4; `the-gift.md` §2.3). **A coin is never a counterfeitable
line, because a coin is a booked record — and the record is single-writer.**

**The no-free-energy cap, held hard — the coin converts nothing.** `energy-harnessing.md`
§6's `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` is the coin's whole truth: the struck unit holds
*exactly* the order it was struck for, never more; striking a coin transfers a
quantum of already-held order from the settlement's book to the coin's form and
carrier's hand — it does not create coherence, does not add to the bath, does
not "pay a gain." **There is no coin that yields** — no strike worth more than
its quantum, no settlement that "mints to farm," no coin whose holding
enriches the holder by existing (the coin's metal bleeds `(1−q)` like any held
structure, §2.2, so holding costs, never pays). And against the misreading the
loaded word invites: **a coin is a token of order, never order created; a
"mint" that created order would be a false mint the field sheds**, the same
lie as a false well (a source that "wells" free) and a false op (a record that
"books" what was not executed). **The strike is the act of making order
portable — never the act of making order.**

**The honest register against the false mint.** The book does not *punish* a
false mint with a rule — there is no sheriff, no fine. Its honesty is stronger
and quieter, exactly as the market's is (`the-market.md` §2.3): **the false
mint cannot be done, because the false op is not bookable.** A strike that
books a coin its quantum does not back finds the coin does not hold — the mark
names no rung the metal reaches, the line it claims is not the order it stands
for, and the book reads the truth of what was actually struck. **The false coin
is a false op the field will not book — never a gain, never a mint.**

## 4. A coin is legible and a choice — the load-bearing design

### 4.1 A coin is legible — the sigil is a real, readable record

The coin is not a hidden currency and not a counterfeitable line; it is the
**field's own read made a token**. Per the instrument-family rule
(`field-instruments.md` §2.1) and the corpus's never-hidden-only discipline:

> **The coin's sigil and its provenance are readable from the instruments —
> never hidden-only.** The struck mark names the coin's rung (legible off the
> published `ρ`/`q`/`ε²` at the struck locality, the way a tool's edge reads its
> metal's history, `the-tool.md` §2b/§3) and its holder's book (legible off the
> Board's `{member, …}` line, `shared-ledger.md` §2). The coin's provenance —
> which quarry cut its metal came from, which member's or settlement's held
> order it is a quantum of — books the way any op does
> (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1), readable from the Board and the instruments
> the settlement already shows. **A player who never uses a "coin" surface
> loses nothing**: the order a coin represents is the same `C(M)`/provenance
> the ledger and the glass render, more laboriously. **There is no hidden
> currency, no secret struck value, no second book.** A coin that hid its mark
> or its provenance would be a hidden currency the design's own honesty
> forbids, exactly as a hidden market price (`the-market.md` §6e), a hidden
> toll (`the-toll.md` §5e), and a hidden store (`the-granary.md` §6e) are
> forbidden throughout the corpus.

The coin's honesty is therefore *transferred to the thing*, the way the gift's
is moved to the held thing (`the-gift.md` §2.3): the sigil is stamped from the
field's own channels, so the coin *carries* its truth in the metal rather than
hiding it behind a face. **A coin is legible because it is a book in miniature
— and a book's entries are read, never whispered.**

### 4.2 A coin is a choice, never a necessity

The book-and-barter economy works without coins. `shared-ledger.md` §1.2
books every contribution and drain; `the-market.md` books every exchange; the
Wage, the Toll, the Loan, the Tithe all book their transfers as Q4 ops of the
settled record's shape. **None of it needs a coin to function** — the ledger's
lines and the market's trades stand alone. This is the coin's honest place:

> **A coin is a choice, never a necessity.** The book-and-barter economy works
> without coins — the ledger's lines and the market's trades stand alone
> (`the-market.md` §1; `shared-ledger.md` §1.2). The coin is the
> *hand-holdable* form a settlement chooses to strike **when it wants order
> portable** — the wage's hand-holdable form (`the-wage.md` §1: the paid share
> a worker may be paid in), the toll's held-door charge (`the-toll.md` §2: the
> crossing's charge a toll collects), the loan's advanced order (`the-loan.md`
> §1) — **never a forced medium, never a hidden tax.** A settlement that never
> strikes a single coin is not poorer, not wrong, not outside the economy: it
> simply books order as lines, and order flows as lines. **The coin exists only
> where a settlement chooses portability over book-keeping convenience — and
> the choice is always the settlement's to make or refuse.**

**The coin's hand-holdable form, placed against its siblings.** The Wage says a
worker is paid in held order (`the-wage.md` §2) — the coin is *that order made a
thing a worker carries*. The Toll charges a crossing (`the-toll.md` §4) — the
coin is *that charge made a thing a traveler can pre-carry and pay*. The Market
prices a trade's booked provenance (`the-market.md` §2.2) — the coin is *that
booked value made a priced, portable token*. The Loan defers order forward
(`the-loan.md` §1) — the coin is the *present-tensed hold* the loan's deferred
order contrasts with (the coin is itself a present quantum, never a promise).
The Gift refuses the book (`the-gift.md` §1) — the coin is the *booked
alternative*: the struck unit is the ledger's, never the gift's un-tallied form.
Each sibling books a transfer; the coin makes *one of those bookable quanta
portable*, by a settlement's own choice, and the siblings' honesty (no-false-
booking, no-mint, no-hidden) is inherited unchanged.

**The honest flag, once.** The coin's legibility (the sigil as a real read) and
its choice (never a necessity) are this doc's [design] framing over the landed
record, member-id, rung, provenance, and instrument-family rule — the *honesty*
it frames is the field's own (the single-writer lane, the no-free-energy cap,
the family rule), and the framing never pretends the engine "makes coins"
beyond the Q4 op it already writes.
