([`the-market.md`](./the-market.md)) books the exchange; the Gift
([`the-gift.md`](./the-gift.md)) refuses the book; the Tithe funds the commons.

now, I will pay the order back next harvest." The Burden

## 1. The Loan, stated once

The corpus gives the economy its present-tensed faces. The Market books the
exchange — *"my harvest for your pick,"* the two hands' Q4 op, trustworthy by
construction because the field will not book a false claim
(`the-market.md` §1/§2.3). The Gift refuses the book — the un-booked giving, the
honest inverse of the market (`the-gift.md`). The tithe/commons funds the shared
core (`shared-ledger.md` §2.1). But every one is *now*: a thing changes hands, or
is given, or is pooled, **this tide**. Nothing defers the exchange across a season.

The Burden designed the *body's* forward-debt — the carried ε² loan, the running
scar of practice, priced as compounding risk over a tide and cleared only by the
field's patient rest (`the-burden.md` §1/§2). It is the player's *own* forward
obligation to the field. **The economy's forward-debt — the obligation *between
members*, deferred across a term — is undesigned.** The Loan is that gap, closed:

> **The Loan is credit as a field act — forward order-borrowing, the honest
> cousin of the Burden.** A member borrows order against *future* contribution:
> an alloy's stored coherence, a held basin's `q`, a seed's origin-order —
> the lender's *own*, transferred forward, not created — booked on the shared
> ledger with a **term**: the loan comes due at "next harvest," the window-year's
> own beat, the tide's season as the loan's calendar. And it carries **interest in
> the field's own currency — patience**: the borrower repays as *future
> contribution*, and the interest is the same honesty the Burden's is — a thin
> tide stretches the term, a harvest shortens it; **the loan's interest is the
> time the field takes to repay, honestly variable, never a fixed rate.** The
> economy's first *temporal* instrument — the exchange no longer exists only in
> the present; it can be *deferred across a season*, with a price the field
> itself sets.

**The honest flag, once.** The pieces a loan composes are all landed — the Q4
op-record (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1), the landed member-id at the
non-forgeable point (§3), the ledger's per-member `C(M)` per tide
(`shared-ledger.md` §1.2), the Burden's patience-interest (`the-burden.md` §2a
/ `patient-field.md` §3.3), the tide's harvest/thin states and its probe
(`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2/§5a), the window-year's calendar beat
(`the-window-year.md` §3.4), the oath's held-or-ruptured book (`the-oath.md` §3),
and the exile's honest read of a severed line (`the-exile.md` §4). **The loan's
*composition* — that a forward exchange *is* a booked op carrying a term, that
its interest is the field's patience, that a default is read honestly on the
book — is this doc's [design] over them.** No new channel, no new physics, no
"loan record" beyond the Q4 op the world already writes. A loan is a *booked
forward exchange*, and the design's whole honesty is that it *uses* the field's
own book the way the market, the burden, the oath, and the board already do.

**Why the Loan is the market's temporal sibling, stated once.** The market is the
exchange *booked now* — two lines re-allocate the same coherence, a transfer never
a gain (`the-market.md` §6d). The Loan is that same exchange *deferred across a
term* — the same two lines, the same single-writer book, the same coherence, only
the *timing* changes: the lender's order moves forward to the borrower this tide,
and the borrower returns order (more of it, or the field's patience) by the
term's close. **Where the market says "what changed hands, honestly," the Loan
says "what was promised, honestly, and when it comes due."** They are the same
law — the book's own readability — pointed at two tenses.

## 2. The forward book — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 A loan is a Q4 op carrying a term — the exchange deferred

A trade is a Q4 op of the settled record's shape `{member, op, worldPos, rung,
magnitude, sustain}` (`the-market.md` §2.1; `schema-that-settles.md` §2.1), the
two hands' lines bound at the world-writer's single non-forgeable write. A loan
is **the same op, with a designed field that names the term**:

```
{ member, op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain, term }
```

where the record still uses the fields the schema already carries, and **`term` is
the one designed addition — the season the loan comes due**:

| Record field | What the loan writes into it | Landed meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `member` | **the two hands the loan binds** — the borrower's and lender's member-ids at the single non-forgeable write | `schema-that-settles.md` §2.1/§3 — a loan's two lines are attributable because the book is |
| `op` | the **forward transfer** — a designed op over the bookable set, the exchange deferred | the op names the field act; a loan is an op of the bookable set pointed forward |
| `worldPos` | the **exchange's cell** — where the order moves, at cell granularity | `schema-that-settles.md` §2.3 — the cell-ownership rule; two members' books never merge silent |
| `rung` | the **borrowed order's rung** — the alloy's stored-coherence rung, the held basin's `q`, the seed's vault-order | the item's provenance books its rung (`the-market.md` §2.2; `seed-garden.md` §3) |
| `magnitude` | the **borrowed order's magnitude** — the stored coherence the lender transfers forward | the honest mass of order, booked, never made up (`farm-that-feeds.md` §4) |
| `sustain` | the **held loan's semantics** — a standing forward-op, kept until the term's close | `schema-that-settles.md` §2.2 — the held op books as one standing record |
| `term` | **[design] the repayment's season** — the tide-account's close, "next harvest" | `the-window-year.md` §3.4 (the steward's cycle, the book's close and reopen); `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2 (the harvest/thin states) |

**[design] The `term` field is this doc's contribution.** The schema settles the
record's fixed fields; the *term* — that a Q4 op carries a designed forward-date —
is the loan's composition over the landed record, exactly as `the-market.md`
designs the exchange-op's semantics over the same lane (§2.1, its open-Q1 dial).
Once the term's semantics are set, the booking is deterministic (§5c).

**The loan's form over the op-record, stated once.** The market books the exchange
now; the loan books the *forward* exchange — the same op, with the added truth of
a term. **The borrowed order is the lender's own, transferred forward.** An alloy
is stored coherence at its rung (`energy-harnessing.md` §3; `the-tool.md` §2); a
held basin is a maintained high-`q` region (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4); a seed
is an origin-order (`seed-garden.md` §3/§4.2). Each is **already the lender's, with
a booked provenance** — the loan transfers that order's *book* forward, this tide,
on the single ledger the settlement already reads. The lender does not gain; the
borrower does not mint; the order simply *moves* across a season, its truthful
magnitude and rung booked at the write.

### 2.2 The term is "next harvest" — the window-year's own beat, honestly variable

The loan's calendar is **the window-year's**: the tide is the year's pulse
(`the-window-year.md` §2), the steward's cycle is the tide-account's close and
reopen (§3.4), and the harvest/thin states are the calendar's two feet
(`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2). The loan comes due at the **season's beat**:

> **The loan's term is "next harvest" — the tide-account's close, the window-year's
> own beat.** A loan is not "due in N hours" (a server timer, outside the field);
> it is due at the **settlement's book-close**, the same beat the steward's cycle,
> the festival's day, and the anniversaries ride (`the-window-year.md` §3.4). The
> borrower borrows an alloy at one harvest and repays by the next — **the calendar's
> own beat is the loan's clock.** [design] *Which* harvest (the full cycle's apex)
> is the term's reference, and the exact season-length dial, is this doc's choice
> over the tide's measured period (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5b; the beat's
> length is the §5a probe's verdict, never set here).

**The term is honestly variable.** This is the load-bearing honesty of the loan's
book — the term is **not a fixed length; it is the field's repayment window,
which the tide stretches or shortens**:

| State | The term | Grounded in |
|---|---|---|
| **At harvest** (q high, `ε²` low — the field organized) | the term **shortens** — the field heals fast, the vent is small, resonance is matchable; the borrower's future contribution comes *easier*, so the loan is due sooner and the interest forgiven by the field's generosity | `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2, magic row ("channeling is efficient, the ε² vent is small"); `patient-field.md` §3.3 ("costs less patience at harvest") |
| **At thin** (q low, `ε²` high — the field wasteful) | the term **stretches** — the vent backs up, the field heals slowly, the borrower's honest contribution is harder to make; the loan's due-date rides back with the harvest it needs | `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2, magic row ("the vent backs up, overdraw risk rises"); `patient-field.md` §3.3 ("costs more patience at thin"); `the-burden.md` §2a (the debt compounds in thin) |

The honest read, stated once:

> **The loan rides the tide because the repayment rides the harvest.** A borrower
> repays by *future contribution* — working the borrowed order forward against the
> field. That work is cheap at harvest (the field is organized; `tide-of-the-attractor.md`
> §3.2 "harvest the harvest") and dear at thin (the field is wasteful). So the
> loan **cannot name a dead, fixed due-date the field ignores**: a thin tide
> stretches the term because the repayment the term prices has not been *possible*
> yet — the harvest hasn't come; a harvest shortens it because the work was done
> and the order returned early. **The patience-interest and the variable term are
> one mechanism: the field's own economy sets how long a promise may take.** [design]
> The *exact* mapping from the tide's `q` phase to the term's stretch/shorten is a
> designed dial over the tide's measured period (§5b) — the direction (thin
> stretches, harvest shortens) is the corpus's own vent-backup and heal-rate truth.

## 3. The patience-interest — the load-bearing currency

This is the Loan's single load-bearing statement, stated once and never blurred:

> **A loan is repaid as future contribution, and its interest is the same honesty
> the Burden's is — the borrower's future `(1−q)` waste is the lender's risk.**
> The Burden prices a channeler's carried debt by the field's own patience: the
> rate is the field's patience, which bites at thin and forgives at harvest
> (`the-burden.md` §2a; `patient-field.md` §3.3 — "the same channeled act costs
> more patience at thin tide and less at harvest"). The Loan points that same
> sentence *between* members: when a borrower takes an alloy forward, the lender
> takes the honest risk that the borrower's return is blocked by the very field
> the order came from — a thin tide's stretched term *is* the interest paid in
> time; a harvest's shortened term *is* the interest forgiven by the field's
> generosity. **The loan's interest is the *time* the field takes to repay —
> honestly variable, never a fixed rate, no hidden APR; the patience IS the
> price.** (`patient-field.md` §5d — a loan, never a mint.)

**Why the patience is the interest, concretely.** Two quantities tie it to the
corpus's real channels:

1. **The borrower's future `(1−q)` waste is the lender's risk.** Every working
   act wastes `E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput` (`energy-harnessing.md` §2) — at thin
   the field runs bright-and-wasteful, and the borrower's honest contribution
   yields less per unit of work than it would at harvest. A loan lent at harvest
   and repaid across a thin tide returns *less useful order per effort*; the
   lender's expectation was the harvest-magnitude return, and the thin field's
   waste is the gap. **That gap is the interest the field charges, in the field's
   own currency — patience.** The borrower's `(1−q)` waste — the same quantity the
   Burden carries on the body (`the-burden.md` §1) — reads as the *loan's* honest
   risk.
2. **The term is the patience made a number.** A thin tide stretches the term
   (§2.2), and that stretch *is* the price: the lender's order is out of hand
   longer, the borrower is obligated longer, the book holds the promise longer —
   time owed, in the field's own beat. A harvest shortens it, and the shortening
   is the interest forgiven by the field's generosity — the loan closed early,
   the patience not spent.

**The honest frame — never a fixed rate, no hidden APR.** The corpus's economy
already refuses hidden pricing (`the-market.md` §3.2: no hidden pricing engine, no
market-maker). The patience-interest obeys it absolutely:

> **The Loan has no interest rate in the ledger's own arithmetic — no "5% per
> tide," no compounding schedule, no hidden APR.** Its price is the *term* — the
> season's beat the tide sets — and the borrower's own honest future contribution.
> A player who reads the loan's price reads **how long** it is for and **with what
> the borrower will pay it back**, never a number the field secretly multiplies.
> **The patience IS the price** (`patient-field.md` §3.3): the length is the
> interest, legible on the book, never a rate behind it. A loan that priced its
> interest as a hidden APR would be exactly the secret economy the market's
> honesty forbids.

**[design] The patience-interest is this doc's composition over the burden's cost
model — cited, never re-derived.** The *rate* is `the-burden.md` §2a's (the field's
own patience, which bites at thin) and `patient-field.md` §3.3's (the same
channeled act's cost at each tide) — landed pieces. What the Loan adds is the
*application*: that this same patience prices a *between-member forward book*, not
only a body's carried debt. No claim here pretends the engine writes an "interest
rate"; the term and the pledged contribution are the [design] over the landed
patience.

## 4. The honest edges

### (a) A loan is not a mint

`energy-harnessing.md` §6's no-free-energy discipline is the corpus's load-bearing
cap (`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`; no conversion yields more than it sinks). The Loan holds
it as its own spine:

> **A loan is not a mint.** The borrowed order is **the lender's own** — the
> alloy's stored coherence, the basin's held `q`, the seed's origin-order —
> *transferred forward*, this tide, on the single book. **The loan does not create
> coherence; it defers its book.** The sum of the two members' `C(M)` after a loan
> is the same coherence that existed before, re-allocated across a term, exactly
> as a market trade is a transfer never a gain (`the-market.md` §6d). The cap
> holds: `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` bounds the forward transfer the way it bounds any op.

Four guards, all inherited from the cap:

1. **No borrowing into a mint.** A loan cannot be the lever that turns one order
   into two — the borrowed magnitude is the lender's, transferred, and the
   borrower's return is their own future contribution, not the lender's order
   duplicated. **A borrower cannot lend the borrowed order on to create more** —
   that is the market's no-gain, pointed at the forward book.
2. **No interest as free energy.** The patience-interest is *time*, never a
   resource yield — a harvest-favorable term does not mint coherence from the
   exchange; it only closes the loan sooner, and the book never reads a loan as a
   net gain (`the-market.md` §6d; `the-window-year.md` §5d — the calendar informs,
   never mints).
3. **A default is a loss honestly booked, never a gain.** When the borrower cannot
   repay, the book reads the un-returned order as **gone from the settlement's
   economy** — the lender's order was lent and is not returned. There is no hidden
   fee, no interest "recovery" that turns a default into income; the default is a
   real, booked loss, exactly as a spent deposit is gone forever (`the-fallow.md`
   §2a — a worked deposit is gone forever). **The no-free-energy cap means there is
   no way to make a default profitable.**
4. **The borrower's future waste is not collectable.** The lender's risk (§3) is
   the borrower's `(1−q)` waste — but that waste is not a reservoir to be tapped
   back. The same refusal that closes Coda-farming and tide-farming
   (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5d) closes default-farming: the borrower's thin-
   tide waste is the interest's honest description, never a debt the lender can
   harvest (`energy-harnessing.md` §6; `the-burden.md` §4 — the loan is never a
   mint).

And a default that cannot be *made good* over the honest stretch of the term is
the economy's edge — the **exile's read**:

> **A loan that cannot be repaid is the exile's edge.** When the term has stretched

## Open questions

1. **The term's reference season (§2.2).** A loan comes due at "next harvest" — but
   *which* harvest is the term's reference, and what exactly does the tide's `q`

   §5a's verdict decides the period the dial reads.
2. **The patience-interest's shape (§3/§2.3).** "Repay as future contribution,
   more of it by the field's patience" — how much future contribution closes a loan
   of magnitude M over a term of N tides? Is it the borrower's honest `C(M)` arc
   against the borrowed magnitude (a proportional close), or the borrowed order's
   worked-yield enrichment (a return above magnitude, the patience as the
   difference)? The default leans to the latter (you return order the patience
   enriched, exactly as the field's harvest enriches); the exact interpolation is
   [design]. **[design]** — a dial against the tide probe, `the-burden.md` §2a's
   rate model, and `the-fallow.md`'s yield-vs-run-out honesty.
3. **The lender's risk vs. the settlement's book (§3/§4b).** When a borrower
   defaults, the lender bears the honest loss (their order is gone); does the
   *settlement* share that risk (the commons absorbs the lost order the way a death's
   re-lock draws are absorbed, `shared-ledger.md` §1.2), or is the default purely
   between the two lines? The design's default is the two-line book (the settlement
   stops lending but does not socialize the lender's loss — the loss is the lender's
   honest risk for lending forward). The boundary is [design] against the Board's
   per-member read. **[design]**
4. **What can be lent (the borrowed-order scope, §2.1/§2.3).** A loan transfers
   *order* — an alloy's stored coherence, a held basin's `q`, a seed's origin-order.
   Can a borrower lend a *service* (a kept act, a held anchor — the oath's bookable
   keepings, `the-oath.md` §4.1) forward, as a promise repaid by future service,
   rather than a thing? The honest register is that a service-loan is a vow (a
   bookable keeping, `the-oath.md` §3) — but the boundary between "a lent thing,
   provenance-booked and returned" and "a pledged keeping, pledge-booked"
   is a [design] dial against the pledge-line's read, inheriting `the-market.md`
   open-Q3's non-tangible boundary. **[design]**
5. **The loan's relation to the fallow (§4b).** `the-fallow.md` designs the window
   whose loans default — the economic fallow's face. Does a settlement with many
   defaulted loans read as *spent* (the un-returned orders are real depletions,
   booked the way a worked vein is gone forever), and is that spent-read the
   fallow's leading indicator for the credit economy, the way the spent stock leads
   the decay arc? The default's honest booking (§4b) and the fallow's run-out
   honesty (§2a) are the same bookkeeping; whether default-saturation is the
   credit-economy's one-way depletion is a [design] fork this doc leaves open for
