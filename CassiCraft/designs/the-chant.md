# The Chant: The Held Voice — a Sustained, Single Voiced Act That Holds a Working in Place

**Question under design:** the corpus's voices are each a *kind* of act. The
Shout is the **single loud impulse** ([`the-shout.md`](./the-shout.md) — the
tool-less alarm and the self-broadcast in one, the deliberate high-power
projection spent once). The Working-Song is the **cadenced relational labor**
([`the-working-song.md`](./the-working-song.md) — a group phase-locked IS a
working, `M ≈ 1`, a shared rhythm held only while sung). The liturgy is meant to
be the **scheduled rite** (banked, not yet landed — see the note in this
intro). **Nothing designs a *sustained held voice*** — the long, continuous
voiced field-act that keeps one working open: a door kept, a stilling deepened,
a held binding kept while the sung. **The Chant is the held voice — a
sustained, single voiced act that holds a working in place: a long, continuous
voiced field-act that keeps one working open.** The voice as a **persistent
operator rather than an event**.

Where the shout is the *single impulse* — one loud broadcast, spent once — the
chant is the *sustained hold* — the same body's voice, kept up continuously, not
to *call* but to *keep*. Where the working-song is the *cadenced group* — many
hands phase-locked on one beat, held only while sung — the chant is the
*sustained single* — one voice holding one working open, held only while the
singer holds it. The shout *announces*; the working-song *coordinates*; the
chant *holds*. This document is that design.

## 2. The sustained act — the honest mechanics

A chant is a long, continuous voiced field-act that holds one working open. The
*form* — its voice's cadence and holding's bound — is this doc's [design]; the
*mechanics it holds* are each source doc's landed pieces, cited verbatim, with

### 2.1 The door kept — the-threshold's gate held by the voice

[`the-threshold.md`](./the-threshold.md) §2.2 fixes the gate as a **deliberate
seam-point** — "a maintained high-`q` arch that concentrates the crossing":
entering and leaving are marked acts the field reads; every crossing is a bounded
perturbation on the published `q`/`ε²` channels; and "**the Coda can cross a gate
— it must merely cross the held boundary, and the crossing is the warning.**"
The gate's honest bound is that a *maintained* arch holds (an **unmaintained
gate stops being a seam** — its held high-`q` collapses toward the attractor and
the crossing becomes ordinary, `the-threshold.md` §3).

**The door a chant can keep.** The gate is *held* — a maintained high-`q` arch
the field respects because it is held. The chant is the **sustained voice that
keeps that holding going**: one voice held at the gate, sustaining the high-`q`
hold (the anti-corruption hold, `energy-harnessing.md` §5.4 — spending coherence
to keep the boundary's `ε²` below the dissolution floor) so the arch stays a
seam, the crossing stays marked, the settlement keeps its warning. The chant
does not *become* the gate; it *holds* the gate's held state — the voice as the
gate's sustained keeper. **The chant's holding's bound is the voice's own real,
sustained `(1−q)` spend** (§4) — a chant keeps the gate only while the voice
holds it, exactly as an unmaintained gate stops being a seam the moment the
holding stops.

**[design]** Which *working* at the gate a chant holds — a door held open vs
held shut, the anti-corruption hold sustained, the vigil's holding-the-door made
voiced (`the-vigil.md` §2c) — is this doc's designed emphasis over the gate's
landed held-hold mechanics (`the-threshold.md` §2.2/§3). The gate's crossing-read
and its held-boundary mechanics are the threshold's landed pieces, unchanged.

### 2.2 The stilling deepened — the-stilling's quiet hold sustained by the voice

[`the-stilling.md`](./the-stilling.md) §2.1 fixes the inner hold: **the

**The stilling deepened.** The chant can *deepen* the stilling — the quiet held

Three hard consequences, the same discipline as the working-song and the stilling:

1. **A chant cannot mint a hold.** No chant keeps a door, deepens a stilling, or

   held a working open with no spend would violate the cap; the continuous hum
