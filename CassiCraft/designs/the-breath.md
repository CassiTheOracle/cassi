## 1. The Breath, stated once

`deep-field-diving.md` designs the answer to "how do we reach the deep" as a
**craft** — a rigid body sheathed in a Qi-bath core + φ-detuned skin, carrying its
own held pocket of order down through the strata (§1/§2). It is the right answer,
and it is a *craft* answer: it protects a descent by interposing a shell, so that
the surrounding ε² and the blast-risk never reach the operator (§2.2). **But the
corpus has no design for the body that descends *unprotected* — the diver who
goes beyond the shell's reach, the cave-explorer who holds a breath into the
hollow, the swimmer who dives the sea's floor.** Every one of them carries
something down that no craft holds for them: a finite, personal, depleting supply
of held coherence. The corpus named the *debt* the body carries
([`the-burden.md`](./the-burden.md) §1 — the ε² a heavy channeler drags) and the
*work object* the body swings at depth ([`the-tool.md`](./the-tool.md) §2a — the
rung-matched bite), but it never named the **store** the body draws that debt and
that bite *from* — the bounded reservoir that makes a bare descent possible at
all, and that runs out. This document is that store, stated once and kept:

> **The Breath is the body's finite reservoir at depth — a bounded supply of held
> coherence a diver carries down, the corpus's "what you carry in your own
> chest."** It is the primitive under every unprotected descent: a dive beyond
> the shell's protection, a breath held into a cave's hollow, a swimmer diving
> the sea's floor — each spends the diver's B-reservoir as *air*. **A deep dive
> without a bell is a breathed descent where every rung deeper costs lungs of
> held coherence.** And its honest risk is the body's own: **run out of Breath
> at depth and the body's lock fails** — the coherence-failure death of
> `player-remains.md` §1/§4, the deep's own drowning.

**Why this is the corpus's missing face, honestly.** Every vehicle in the corpus
designs *up* or *across* — the rocket, the voyage, the long dark between windows
(`deep-field-diving.md` §1) — and deep-field-diving designs the *downward craft*:
the Bell's protected descent. What no document designs is the *unprotected* form
of that same vertical: the body that descends with nothing between it and the
deep but its own held coherence. The Bell answers "how do I reach the deep
*safely*" — **the Breath answers "is it even possible to reach the deep *at
all*, with just the body?"** It is the honest depth-pressure on the body itself,
the thing the craft exists to relieve, and the reason the craft is worth
building: **a bell is a thing you build because a held breath is finite.**

**The Breath is distinct from the shell it is the twin of:**

| | The Bell's shell (`deep-field-diving.md` §2) | The Breath (this doc) |
|---|---|---|
| What protects the descent | a **craft** — a maintained Qi-bath core + φ-detuned skin, the shell refusing the outside | **the body's own held coherence** — no shell, no bath, no refusal; the body itself is the store |
| The descent's cost | the shell's **standing draw** (bath + skin, §2.2) | the body's **reserve spent as air** — a bounded reservoir that depletes with depth |
| The failure | contained **at the shell** (a diver who over-collects detonates the Bell, §3.2) | the **body's lock fails** — the coherence-failure death (`player-remains.md` §1/§4) |
| The relation | the *craft's* protected descent | the *body's* own unprotected reserve — the reason the Bell matters |

The through-line: deep-field-diving designed the *downward craft*; the corpus's
whole body-economics (`the-burden.md`, `the-tool.md`, `player-remains.md`,
`life-signal.md`) designed what the *living body* carries, swings, dies, and
reads — but never the store those acts draw on when they happen *below the
shell's protection*. **The Breath closes that gap: the body's own finite
reservoir at depth — the air under every bare descent, and the honest wall that
makes the bell a real craft rather than a redundancy.**

## 2. The reservoir — the honest mechanics

The Breath is a **bounded supply of held coherence the body carries down**. Its
three sources are the corpus's own landed pieces; its *composition* — the size
and the draw — is this doc's **[design]**, probe-calibrated.

### 2.1 The carried reserve — the burden's cost at body-scale

`the-burden.md` §1/§1.1 fixes what the player actually carries: channeling is a
**loan from the field's coherence** — the player sheds ε² to act, and that shed
ε² is not erased when the budget recovers; it is "a damaged-locality-you-drag-
with-you," "the running scar of their own practice." §4 fixes the honest frame:
**"The loan is never a mint. The ε² you shed is *carried*, not converted — you
cannot farm it… clear it by *stopping and standing in the field's patience'."**
`deep-field-diving.md` §2.3 names the same reserve at the Bell's scale: the
operator is "the active maintainer of the shell's core, feeding the Qi bath their
own coherence budget `B` (§1.2)," and **"a diver runs out of *coherence budget*,
not air — the honest depth limit is the same budget economics."**

The Breath is that budget, read at its bare-bodied, at-depth register:

> **The Breath is the body's carried coherence budget, made the finite store a
> bare descent draws on.** What the burden calls the *carried cost* — the ε² the
> body represents and sheds, the budget `B` that recovers geographically
> (`coherence-magic.md` §1.2) — is, at depth, **the body's air**. A diver who
> descends unprotected draws the reserve down with every rung; the draw is the
> burden's carried-cost logic at body-scale, and the honest mirror of the Bell's
> statement: **what you carry down is a real, spent cost** (`the-burden.md`
> §1/§4), not a free reserve the field lends.

**[design]** — the *reservoir's form* begins from the burden's landed carried
ε²-debt (a real, player-attached, decaying read over the vent/`(1−q)` channels,
`the-burden.md` §6a) and `deep-field-diving.md` §2.3's budget `B` (the operator's
coherence budget that bounds the Bell's reach). **That the body's own $B$ is a
*depleting store at depth* — a bounded reservoir whose size is a probe-calibrated
function of the body's carried coherence and whose *draw* scales with depth — is
this doc's designed composition over those landed quantities.** The size (how much
held coherence a body carries down) and the draw (how fast each rung spends it)
are this doc's dials, Phase-1 probe-calibrated (§5b), never asserted. What the
source docs land: the carried ε²-debt is real and decaying; the coherence budget
`B` is real and recovers geographically; the Bell's reach is bounded by that
budget. The Breath states that **the unprotected body's own depth-reach is
bounded by the same budget, spent as air.**

### 2.2 The bite the reserve powers — the tool's work at depth

`the-tool.md` §2a fixes the tool's relationship to depth: **"Your tool's rung must
meet the material's rung. A coarse-rung pick perturbs the field at a coarse

matter but scars the locality it cuts into more"** — the charge/scar asymmetry held
at the hand, "the un-contained hand-scale face of the same withdrawal" the Bell
contains at craft-scale.

The Breath is the reserve that **makes the bite possible at depth, and that the
bite depletes**:

> **The breath is what a bare diver spends to *have a bite at all*.** The tool at
> depth (`the-tool.md` §2a) is the body's own rung-matched work object; but
> swinging a rung-matched bite deep, where the surrounding ε² presses and there is
> no shell to refuse it, spends held coherence the body must carry down. **The
> breath is the tool's fuel at body-scale** — the reserve that lets a diver reach
> the deep matter a rung-matched tool cuts, and the reserve the cutting draws
> down. A diver whose breath runs dry loses the *reach to bite* before their
> limbs tire: below the shell's protection, the body cannot hold its own
> coherence together and operate a deep tool at once.

**[design]** — the *coupling* (that a rung-matched bite at depth spends the breath
as well as the tool's own scar, `the-tool.md` §2c) is this doc's designed read
over the tool's landed bite-and-scar, probe-calibrated. What is landed: the tool
bites at its rung and scars the locality it cuts (`the-tool.md` §2a/§2c); the
reserve the deep bite draws from, at body-scale, is this doc's composition.

### 2.3 Spent as air — every rung deeper costs lungs of held coherence

## 3. The hollow's breath — the load-bearing design

The Breath's three unprotected-descent forms are each a designed composition over
a landed piece. The load-bearing claim is the same in all three, stated once:

> **The body's reserve is what a hollow descent, a sea-floor dive, and a bare
> strata descent each spend — and the depth of each is bounded by the reserve,
> not by stamina or will.**

### (a) A breath held into a cave's hollow

`the-cave.md` §3 designs the cave as **"a stratum that holds *nothing*, and that
nothing is its honest content"** — the deep's openness, a coherence *absence* the
field holds, the one place below where q falls, ε² stays low, and the dense
organization is simply not there (§1). Its reads are the deep's own channels (§3,
§5e); its honesty is that the hollow is a deficit, never a source (§5d — "a cave
provides nothing").

**The [design] over it — the breath is what the hollow costs the body, and what
the hollow cannot refund:**

> A cave is the deep's one *empty* volume — and emptiness, for a bare-bodied
> explorer, is **the loneliest place to run dry**. Where the dense strata press,
> the cave holds nothing to press *against*, but it also holds nothing to support
> the body's own held coherence either: **a breath held into a cave's hollow
> ($\text{the-cave}$ §3) is a reserve spent with no field to help refill it** —
> the hollow's low-`q` read (the quiet, the cold, the shelter, §4) is precisely
> *not* a coherent refuge (the cave provides nothing, §5d). The explorer who
> holds a breath into the hollow trades the body's finite air for the emptiness's
> shelter — and the shelter is the absence's own, never a gain.

**[design]** — the *hollow's non-refund* (that a cave, for all its shelter, is not
a coherent ground the breath refills in) is this doc's designed read over the
cave's landed "provides nothing" (§5d) and its low-`q` emptiness (§3), probe-
calibrated. What is landed: the cave is a `q`-fallen, `ε²`-low hollow (§1/§3);
the cave provides nothing — no hollow that yields (§5d). The Breath holds that
**"the shelter is the absence's own"** applies to the body's reserve too: a hollow
does not refill a held breath, because it has nothing to give.

### (b) A swimmer diving the sea's floor

`the-sea-floor.md` §2c designs the descent as the diving bell's ladder beginning —
"the below's entrance, the first rung-terrace, the gate that costs order to enter."
`the-swim.md` §2a designs the swim as **"the expensive walk"** — "a stroke spends
the water's `(1−q)` the land's stride does not have" — the liquid regime's own
waste, the drag a bare body pays to move through water.

**The [design] over them — the shallow breath, the dive that costs the water's
own `(1−q)` and the body's reserve:**

> The swimmer who dives the sea's floor (`the-sea-floor.md` §2c) makes the descent
> at the breath's *shallow* form: the water's `(1−q)` drag (`the-swim.md` §2a) is
> the shallow breath — a stroke that already spends the medium's waste against a
> body crossing the flat plain. **Diving is the stroke *turned down*: below the
> sea-floor's shelf, the same body pays the water's `(1−q)` and then the depth's
> press, both drawn from the breath the swimmer carried through the stroke.** The
> swimmer who crosses the plain to the reef (`the-sea-floor.md` §2b) has spent
> part of the shallow breath on the crossing; the dive that follows draws the
> rest on the descent.

**[design]** — the *descent-through-the-stroke* (that a sea-floor dive spends the
water's `(1−q)` as the shallow breath and then the depth's press as the deep
breath, one continuous draw) is this doc's designed read over the swim's landed
stroke-cost (`the-swim.md` §2a) and the sea-floor's landed descent
(`the-sea-floor.md` §2c), probe-calibrated. What is landed: the stroke pays the

**[design]** — the *breath-as-lock-hold* (that the body's maintained lock at depth

**[design]** — the *draw-as-maintenance* (that the breath's level is the
