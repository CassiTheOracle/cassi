# The River: The Living Line — the One Great Linear Landscape, the Corpus's Path-and-Home-At-Once

**Question under design:** the corpus's great places are each a *regime-doc* — the
**marsh** ([`the-marsh.md`](./the-marsh.md) — the wide-slow, the shallow textured
water that hides), the **sea** ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) — the flat coherence
plain, the slow uniform carrying surface), the **desert** ([`the-desert.md`](./the-desert.md)
— the dry-thin, the living regime of absence), the **spring** ([`the-spring.md`](./the-spring.md)
— the fixed-well), the **sea-floor** ([`the-sea-floor.md`](./the-sea-floor.md) — the
vertical's first interface), the **cave** ([`the-cave.md`](./the-cave.md) — the
deep-hollow). Every great place has a regime except one: **the river is treated only
as transport infrastructure** — [`coherence-highway.md`](./coherence-highway.md)'s
q-filament is an *abstract route* ([`coherence-highway.md`](./coherence-highway.md) §1),
and [`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) names rivers only as *moving-conduit bands*
([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2a — one line of a three-water note). No document
designs the river as a *living place*: its current's gradient, its banks' fertile
life, its people — **the river as a habitable linear region**, a marsh-like regime
that is *long* instead of *wide*. The corpus has the river-as-road; it has no
river-as-home. This document is that design: **the River is the living line — a
full regime-doc for the one great linear landscape, drawn explicitly against the
conduits** (the highway's maintained abstract filament and the sea's single
moving-band note): the place itself — a long, live strip of easy-order: a **fertile
bank** (a linear farm-band, [`farm-that-feeds.md`](./farm-that-feeds.md)'s soil
following the water), a **current that carries** (a softer, wetter relative of the
highway's gradient — a raft rides the flow the way a cart rides the road,
[`the-cart.md`](./the-cart.md); a swimmer or diver works with and against it,
[`the-swim.md`](./the-swim.md)/[`the-dive.md`](./the-dive.md)), and a **people of
the water's edge** (river-settlements, fords, ferries, mills — [`the-gatekeeper.md`](./the-gatekeeper.md)
and [`the-toll.md`](./the-toll.md) at the crossings). The honest design is the
current's **two-face**: a river *gives* (fertile, navigable, life-giving) and
*takes* — **a rising river is where the flood begins** ([`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md)),
and **a river's far bank is a real boundary** — the crossing is the moment
([`the-threshold.md`](./the-threshold.md) at water-scale). The river's long life is
the corpus's one place that is *a path and a home at once* — a habit the traveler
and the settler both read the same way.

## 1. The River, stated once

The corpus's water has a character, designed at its wide and its flat and its fixed
faces. The marsh is the **wide-slow** — a shallow, textured band where `q` is patchy
and the water hides ([`the-marsh.md`](./the-marsh.md) §2). The sea is the **flat
plain** — a uniform low-drain region crossed as a patient act ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md)
§2b). The spring is the **fixed-well** — a bounded place where the field's order
rises of itself ([`the-spring.md`](./the-spring.md) §2). The desert is the
**dry-thin** — the window living at the attractor's thin edge ([`the-desert.md`](./the-desert.md)
§2). And the river appears in that family **only as a line of moving water** — the
sea doc names it as a moving-conduit band ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2a, one
sentence in a three-water note), and the highway reads it as its conduit idiom grown
by the field ([`coherence-highway.md`](./coherence-highway.md) §1.1). What the corpus
designs of the river is the *way across it* and the *conduit it is*; it never
designs the water itself as a place to be.

## 2. The living line — the river's form

The River is a **long, live strip of easy-order**: the marsh's wide-slow at its
long-fast twin, the desert's wet-counter, the linear farm-band's fertile banks, and
the strip's own ecology. Each half is grounded in its source doc's real quantity;
**the river's form — its band's width, its current's structure — is this doc's
[design]** over the water channels, probe-calibrated.

### (a) The long-fast twin of the wide-slow — the coherent ribbon

**The landed pieces.** [`the-marsh.md`](./the-marsh.md) §2 designs the marsh as the
sea's **shallow, textured edge**: a low-flow, low-depth band of the liquid regime
where `q` is *patchy* — small coherent hummocks and thin pools where the shallow
bottom's structure breaks the plain's flatness point by point. The sea's flat
plain is uniform `q`, low drain ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2b). The river of
[`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2a is the one water the corpus already designs as a
**moving-conduit band**: a high-`q` filament carrying *flow* the way a manufactured
conduit carries coherence, held by the liquid regime itself — "a corridor the field
itself maintains, the highway's §1.1 conduit where the field is the roadman."

**The [design] over them — the river is that conduit band, made a *long, live
strip* rather than a line of transport:**

> **The river is the marsh's wide-slow at its long-fast twin.** Where the marsh is
> a low-flow, low-depth band where `q` is *patchy* (the tessellated-q of
> [`the-marsh.md`](./the-marsh.md) §2), the river is the same liquid regime read at
> its opposite end: a **high-flow, linear band where `q` is a coherent ribbon** —
> the shallow bottom that breaks the marsh into hummocks and pools is replaced by a
> running gradient that holds one continuous high-`q` filament, the sea's moving-
> conduit band ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2a) laid out into a place you travel
> *along* and *live beside*. Where the marsh hides motion in its patchy texture
> ([`the-marsh.md`](./the-marsh.md) §3), the river's coherent ribbon *carries* it —
> the current is legible, ordered, flowing; the strip is the field's water organized
> as a line rather than a plain.

**The river's band-width — this doc's [design] dial, probe-calibrated.** How wide a
ribbon the linear regime holds (a band a few strides across, or a reach a settlement
lines) is this doc's [design] over the published `q`-structure of the moving-conduit
band, probe-calibrated against the Phase-1 linear-regime slice (§5b) — the same
honest tier as the marsh's tessellation-scale dial ([`the-marsh.md`](./the-marsh.md)
open-Q2) and the spring's bounded-extent dial ([`the-spring.md`](./the-spring.md)
§2.2). What is landed is that the liquid regime holds a coherent flowing filament
([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2a); **how wide the habitable strip beside it reads
is the living line's own [design], probe-calibrated and never asserted.**
