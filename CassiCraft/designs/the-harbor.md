# The Harbor: The Settlement's Door to the Between

**Question under design:** the corpus designs the **voyage** (the crossing between
windows, [`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md)), the **walk** (the un-roaded crossing,
[`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md)), the **atlas** (the traveler's record of every window
seen, [`the-atlas-of-windows.md`](./the-atlas-of-windows.md)), and the **threshold**
(the boundary-mark within a window, [`the-threshold.md`](./the-threshold.md)) — but
nothing designs **where a voyage leaves from**: the settlement's own door to the
between. Today a voyage begins in an *undesigned* place: a player walks to the
window's edge and leaves. The crossing has a designed texture (the
[`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md)'s thin, ringing, lawful between), and
the departure is a real costed act ([`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §2.3's
coherence budget) — but the *place* the departure happens in, and the act of leaving
as a settlement's own moment, has no design. This document is that design.

> **The Harbor is the settlement's door to the between — the designed departure
> point where a window's voyage begins:** a named, kept structure built at the
> window's edge, holding the seam's anchor. It is a pier into the thin where the
> voyage's *last* moments happen in the settlement's own place: its pier is the
> last maintained ground a traveler stands on, its lamp the last light (the
> lantern's honest reach held over the seam), its ledger the last book (the
> departure booked at the harbor — the Q4 op-record's `worldPos` at the pier), and
> its keeper the voyage's last voice (the watcher's farewell, the vigil's craft at
> the door). The Harbor is the voyage's **home-ward act**: the departure that is a
> settlement's own ceremony — the atlas's first page is written here, the treaty's
> crossing begins here, the leaving is booked here. And its honest edge is the
> corpus's already-designed truth: **the harbor is a door, never a shortcut** — the
> voyage's cost is unchanged; the harbor makes the departure legible, never
> cheaper.

The Harbor is the **movement stack's settlement-scale face** and the corpus's first
*door*. It is the Threshold's sibling — the boundary-mark made a *place* — built at
the window's edge (the outer law-edge of [`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §1.3/§4.3),

*where a voyage leaves from* its place and its ceremony.

## 1. The Harbor, stated once

The corpus's movement stack is a stack of *crossings*: the walk crosses the un-roaded
gradient ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §1), the sea's river carries a crossing
([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §2a), the voyage crosses the dark between windows
([`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §2). Each has a designed **medium** — the
interstitial's thin/ringing/lawful between ([`the-interstitial.md`](./the-interstitial.md)
§2), the sea's flat plain (§2b) — and the voyage has a designed **record** (the
atlas's pages, [`the-atlas-of-windows.md`](./the-atlas-of-windows.md) §2). But **no
crossing has a designed *place of departure*** — the last ground a voyager stands on
before they leave the settlement is un-designed. The walk ends wherever the player
stops walking; the voyage begins wherever the player points their ship past the
window's edge. The departure — the single most consequential act in the movement
stack — happens *nowhere in the corpus*.

The Harbor is that nowhere filled:

> **The Harbor is the settlement's door to the between — the designed departure
> point where a window's voyage begins.** It is a **named, kept structure built at
> the window's edge, holding the seam's anchor**: a pier into the thin where the
> voyage's last moments happen in the settlement's own place. It is the Threshold's
> sibling — the boundary-mark made a *place* (the doorstep/gate/arch of
> [`the-threshold.md`](./the-threshold.md) §2 taken from the settlement's inner edge to
> the window's outer edge, [`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §1.3/§4.3) — and it is
> the corpus's first *door*: not a wall, not a border, but a kept opening through
> which the leaving is a designed act.

**Why a door, and why a *place*.** The corpus already refuses walls — a wall is a
concentrating element when sloped but its open edges drain ([`house-that-steers.md`](./house-that-steers.md)
§1.1/§2.2, via [`the-threshold.md`](./the-threshold.md) §1), and a settlement's edge
is made of held, quiet, named structures, not a sealed ring. The Harbor is that same
honesty turned outward: where the Threshold marks the edge *as a boundary*, the Harbor
marks the edge *as a place to leave from*. A door is not a gap in the wall that leaks
the settlement's gradient; it is a **kept threshold whose crossing is the departure**
— the settlement holds it (the pier is maintained ground), reads it (the lamp holds
light over the seam), books it (the ledger is the settlement's record of the leaving),
and keeps it (the keeper is the departure's last watch). The leaving that today drifts
un-designed across the window's edge is, at a harbor, a **ceremony the settlement owns**.

**The movement stack's settlement-scale face.** The corpus's crossings each have a
personal and an infrastructure face: the walk is the pedestrian's relation to the
gradient ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §1), the highway the road's held line, the
voyage the ship's coherence-budget crossing. The Harbor is the *settlement's* face of
that stack:

> **The walk leaves a footprint; the voyage leaves a harbor.** Every crossing is an
> act that marks a place. The walker leaves a faint `ε²` wake on the ground
> ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2c — the trail, gone behind you); the voyager, at a
> harbor, leaves a *harbor* — a booked departure, a kept door, a record of who went,
> from where, with what. Where the walk's mark is the individual's passing wake, the
> voyage's mark is the settlement's own kept structure: **the place a window sends
> its people out from.** The Harbor is the settlement-scale face of the movement stack
> because it is the one face the stack had not yet drawn.

**The honest statement, held throughout:** the Harbor adds **no new voyage, no new
physics, no cheaper crossing** (§4). It is the departure's *place* — the leaving's
ceremony, the book's anchor. Every mechanic that follows is a designed composition
over the voyage's landed cost, the thin's landed medium, the lantern's landed reach,
the record's settled schema, the book's landed aggregation, and the vigil's landed
craft — none of which it changes.

### (a) The pier — the last maintained ground, the boundary-mark made a place

**What it is.** The pier is the Harbor's ground: **the last maintained ground a
traveler stands on before the voyage begins, built into the thin.** It is the
Threshold's doorstep idiom ([`the-threshold.md`](./the-threshold.md) §2.1 — a held,
quiet pocket) extended to the window's outer edge: where the doorstep is a quiet place
at the threshold *entering* the home, the pier is a kept place at the edge *leaving*
the window.

> **The pier is the last maintained ground a traveler stands on.** It reaches into
> the thin (the-interstitial's medium at the window's edge — ρ below the gas floor, q
> low), but it is *built*: a kept high-`q` hold the settlement maintains the way it
> holds a doorstep's quiet or a gate's arch ([`the-threshold.md`](./the-threshold.md)
> §2.1/§2.2 — the maintained structure the field respects because it is held). The
> pier is where a voyager's feet leave the settlement's maintained coherence — the
> last ground that is *the window's own place* — and the boundary-mark made a place:
> the moment a traveler steps off the pier, they stand in the settlement's edge
> facing the seam, and the departure begins.

**The [design] line.** The *pier as a place* — a maintained structure at the window's
edge holding the departure's last ground — is this doc's [design], over the landed
field-poor interstitial (the medium it reaches into) and the doorstep/gate idiom (the
maintained structure it is). It is the same tier as the doorstep's quiet pocket
([`the-threshold.md`](./the-threshold.md) §2.1/§6b): a designed reading of a maintained
high-`q` hold over the published `q`/`ε²` channels, probe-calibrated. **[probe]** Whether
a kept pier *reads* as distinct maintained ground against the thin's edge — whether its
high-`q` hold is legible above the `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1` noise floor (the thin's floor,
[`corpus-reconciliation.md`](./corpus-reconciliation.md)) — is the same measurement the
threshold's doorstep slice pre-registers (§5b).

**The sea-side form.** Where the settlement is a sea-side one, the pier is literally
*over the water into the between* — the sea is the designed middle the window's own
edge crosses ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md) §1/§2b), and the harbor's pier is the kept
ground over that plain reaching toward the seam. The maritime form is a *local reading*
of the same pier: the pier over water is the sea's flat plain crossed by a built
extension of the settlement's maintained ground, never a new regime ([`the-sea.md`](./the-sea.md)
§5d — water is never a mint; the pier over it is the same kept structure).

> **The lamp is the last light — the lantern's honest reach held over the seam.** It

**The honest reach, stated once.** The lamp is the lantern's **honest limit** made a

## 3. The voyage's home-ward act

The composition this doc exists to name — the Harbor as the departure's *settlement*

> **The Harbor is the voyage's home-ward act: the departure that is a settlement's
> own ceremony.** A voyage from a harbor is a *settlement's* departure — the atlas's
> first page is written here, the treaty's crossing begins here, the leaving is booked
> here — the book shows who left, from where, with what. Where the voyage itself is
> the traveler's coherence-budget crossing ([`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §2.3),
> the *departure* is the settlement's act of sending: a named pier, a kept light, a
> booked record, a last voice — the window's own place from which its people go out.

**The atlas's first page.** [`the-atlas-of-windows.md`](./the-atlas-of-windows.md) §2
charts each window as a page composed from the landed records at the traveler's scale.
The Harbor is where that composition *begins*: the departure booked at the pier is the
first stroke on the page — "I left this window from its harbor" — the page's origin
that the map's drawing, the chronicle's story, and the named places then fill. **The
atlas's first page is written here.**

**The treaty's crossing begins here.** [`the-treaty.md`](./the-treaty.md) §1 fixes the
treaty as a paired persistent-Π bridge-spanning the seam, the crossing-place that makes
two windows' relation legible. A treaty-window's voyages leave *from its harbor* — the
harbor is the treaty's crossing place, the end of the window at which the bridged
crossing begins. The treaty's traffic (`the-treaty` §2.2 — the cross-window line) books
whose departures start at which harbor.

**The leaving is booked here.** The composition's spine is the record: the departure
booked at the harbor (the Q4 op's `worldPos` at the pier, §2c) is the settlement's
honest book of its voyaging — who left, from where, with what — the Board's book read
at the settlement's door ([`shared-ledger.md`](./shared-ledger.md) §2).

**Movement stack's settlement-scale face, stated once.** The walk leaves a footprint
([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2c — the passing wake, gone behind you); the voyage
leaves a **harbor** — a booked, kept, named departure the settlement holds. The Harbor
is the settlement-scale face of the movement stack precisely because a crossing's
*deepest* act — the leaving — is the one the settlement itself performs, in its own
place, and the harbor is that act made a structure.

   harbor only *marks* the place a voyage already begins. A window without a harbor can
   still be left (a voyage is not gated on the design having built the settlement's door)

3. **The honest frame — a window whose harbor has gone quiet.** Because a harbor is a
   **kept** structure (the pier's maintained hold, the lamp's held station, the ledger's
   book, the keeper's watch), it reads the settlement's voyaging life:

> **A window whose harbor has gone quiet is a window whose departures have stopped —

**The harbor reads; it never mints.** The quiet-harbor read is information and timing,
