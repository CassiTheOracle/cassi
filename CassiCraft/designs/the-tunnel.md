## 2. The under-hill cut — the honest mechanics

### (a) The tool's bite along the line — the tunnel is dug by the mining perturbation, repeated horizontally

The Tunnel's cut is the **tool's bite, repeated along the horizontal**: the same
rung-matched perturbation the shaft applies down and the quarry applies at the
face (`the-tool.md` §2(a) — a pick's bite is its rung-depth meeting the
material's rung; `the-quarry.md` §2(a) — hardness-as-rung made a wall the tool
meets), applied as a line through the hill instead of a face or a descent. The
spoil is real and the wear is real: `(1−q)` is spent with every bite
(`energy-harnessing.md` §2 — the waste law), the alloy's held regime wears by
exactly that spending (`the-tool.md` §2(b) — the wear = the waste law as
durability), and the carried field at the head of the cut is the same carried
field that shafts a descent (`the-carry.md` §2 — the pack's honest weight). **A
tunnel's opening is a line of finished bites — no bite mints, no bite digs free.**

### (b) The carry's weight, horizontal — the tunnel is a freight-line sideways

Where the shaft hauls its spoil **up** (`the-shaft.md` §2(b) — the carry's
weight, vertical), the tunnel hauls its spoil **out** — the same honest weight,
pushed or carried along the horizontal's line. The passage stays a freight-line:
what the cut removes, the carry must move, and the carried matter is never free
(`the-carry.md` §4 — carried matter, never free). The load and the gradient's
trade that a cart rides downhill (`the-cart.md` §3) finds a level twin here — a
tunnel's floor is a designed level, so its spoil-hauling is the carry's honest
labor, not a free ride down a gradient the hill's own shape could lend. **The
tunnel's cut costs its spoil threefold — the bite, the wear, the carry — and
none of the three is free.**

### (c) The dark, entered — a tunnel's dark is the between's darkness, contained and legible

The Tunnel's darkness is its defining possession and its defining honesty. The
between is the field's own un-windowed outside, the lawful dark the windows
float in (`the-between.md` §1/§2); **a tunnel's dark is that darkness
*contained* — a bounded passage through the hill where the light's `q`-carry is
cut off and the instruments read the field's own thin close, but the outside
itself is never entered** — a tunnel is not the between, it is a room of the
deep's thickness with the sky's light withdrawn. The dark is legible *because*
it is the field's: the same `(1−q)` the light's carry spends to reach a lung of
the hill reads on the same channels the instruments publish (`field-instruments.md`
§2.1 — every instrument a consumer of the publish). **A tunnel does not make its
own dark hidden; it makes the field's dark readable.**

### (d) The floor it never needs — a tunnel crosses above the bedrock's terminus

The Tunnel is a **crossing, never a descent**: it does not need to reach the
bedrock's floor (`the-bedrock.md` §1) the way a shaft might, because its point is
not depth but passage. The under-hill line runs **above** the absolute floor it
never needs, its walls cut through the hill's own rung-graded stone, and its
terminus is the far open ground, not the deep's bottom. **A tunnel's floor is its
own level, not the window's** — the passage ends where the far light reads clear,
and the bedrock's floor is a boundary it crosses without ever needing to touch.
This is the horizontal's freedom: the shaft's freight descends to the floor; the
tunnel's freight travels between floors.

### (e) The pre-read discipline — the passage's hold read before the dark is entered

The climb's honesty is that you read a wall's height-feasibility before you

## 3. The load-bearing design — why the straight crossing is honest to design

### (a) The tunnel is the shaft's horizontal twin — the line turned from descent to passage

The Shaft is the corpus's **dug descent** — the vertical's engineered line, the
mine's straightness made a place, cut straight down to the deep's floor
(`the-shaft.md` §1). **The Tunnel is that same engineered line turned sideways —

They are twins because they are the same designed honesty oriented differently:
the shaft organizes the descent vertically, the tunnel organizes the crossing
horizontally; the shaft's straightness makes the deep reachable by design, the

The hill the Tunnel cuts through is the field's own structure — the deep's stored

shaft's face (`the-quarry.md` §2(a), `the-shaft.md` §1). A tunnel is honest to

### (c) The way it buys is never a mint — it is the far ground's own openness, reached

The Tunnel's whole point is the far open ground — the crossing one hill's depth
