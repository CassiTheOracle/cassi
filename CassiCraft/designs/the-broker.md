# The Broker: The Trading Stranger — The Being That Carries Rarity Across Windows

**Question under design:** the stranger-object layer has twelve named faces, and
every one is honest to what it is. The signature-predator **hunts** a clean trail
([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §1), the blight **turns** a
region's life against the field ([`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md) §1), the feral
**wakes** a kept thing ([`the-feral-instrument.md`](./the-feral-instrument.md) §1),
the scavenger **feeds** on the spent ([`the-scavenger.md`](./the-scavenger.md) §1),
the commensal **lives beside** the order ([`the-commensal.md`](./the-commensal.md)
§1), the mirror-creature **wears your own pattern** back at you
([`the-mirror-creature.md`](./the-mirror-creature.md) §1), the witness **watches**
([`the-witness.md`](./the-witness.md) §1), the guardian **keeps** a named place
([`the-guardian.md`](./the-guardian.md) §1), the herald **tells** the field's news
([`the-herald.md`](./the-herald.md) §1), the mimic **wears another's shape**
([`the-mimic.md`](./the-mimic.md) §1), the moth **covets light**
([`the-moth.md`](./the-moth.md) §1), the shepherd **gathers**
([`the-shepherd.md`](./the-shepherd.md) §1). **None trades.** The layer has hunters,
keepers, light-coveters, gatherers — but no *being that exchanges gathered order for
settlement goods*. No being carries rarity from the between and swaps it at a
settlement's deliberate door. That is the missing economy-face of the layer: this
document designs **the Broker — the stranger-object layer's thirteenth face, the
trader**.

> **The Broker, stated once.** It is the **trading stranger** — a being that
> carries rarity across windows and trades it at a settlement's deliberate door: a
> wandering **exchange-creature**, the **between-carrier the local market has no
> way to reach**. It is the stranger-object layer's **thirteenth face, the trader**
> — the first economic bridge between the local market (`the-market.md`) and the
> far windows (`the-atlas-of-windows.md`, `window-guests.md`, `the-between.md`).

Until now the stranger-object layer has been a spectrum of how the field *relates
to your work, your place, or your identity* — it hunts your trail, turns your work,
wakes from your keeping, feeds your waste, keeps company beside your order, plays
*you* back, watches, keeps, tells, wears a shape, covets light, gathers. **None
exchanges.** Every face is *the field being something*; none is the field *trading
with you*. The layer has self-reflexion (the mirror), deception (the mimic), the
hunt (the predator), the gathering (the shepherd) — **it has no *exchange***, no
being that brings worth from outside and swaps it for local order. The Broker is
that missing axis. Where the market is the *local* circulation among members, the
Broker is the *between-carrier* the local market has no way to reach; where the
toll *charges* the entering stranger, the Broker is the stranger who *brings worth*
— tolled then welcomed; and every other face, none of which exchanges, is
distinguished from it on a single axis. **The Broker is the stranger-layer's
economic face — the proof that the between, too, trades.**

*exchange* face, the counterpart to the market's *internal* circulation and the
toll's *border* charge. It introduces **no new channel, no new physics, no new
write** — it is a **designed composition over the market's exchange

Every face describes how the field relates to *your work, your place, or your

> **The Broker, stated once.** It is the **trading stranger** — a being that
> carries rarity across windows and trades it at a settlement's deliberate door: a

The distinction from the three honest cousins, drawn exactly — and the one from
every *other* face:

- **Not the market's own hand.** The market (`the-market.md` §1/§2) books the
  *local* circulation among the settlement's members — a trade is a Q4 op between
  two member-lines, provenance-booked, made honest by the book. The market has **no
  way to reach the far**: its members trade what the window already holds. **The
  Broker is the between-carrier** — the being that brings the rare the local
  circulation cannot mint or find, so that the *local* market can acquire it at the
  door. The Broker is not a member's trade; it is the external supplier the local
  book has no line for until it books the exchange (§2).
- **Not a toll.** The toll (`the-toll.md` §1/§3) is what the *outside* pays to
  enter the held door — a per-arrival, per-stranger charge on what crosses the
  settlement's bound. **The Broker is the stranger who *brings worth*** — it is
  tolled at the door it entered (`the-toll.md` §2), then *welcomed* because it
  carries the rare. Where the toll is a charge *on* the entering stranger, the
  Broker is an entering stranger who *brings*; the toll and the Broker are the two
  faces of the same doorstep — the charge for crossing it, and the worth carried
  across it (§3).
- **Not any other face.** Predator, blight, feral, scavenger, commensal,
  mirror-creature, witness, guardian, herald, mimic, moth, shepherd — none
  *exchanges*. Each is the field *being something*; the Broker is the field
  *trading with you*. On the single axis of *exchange*, the Broker is distinguished
  from all twelve: it is the layer's **economic face — the proof that the between,
  too, trades** (§3).

**The through-line of the thirteenth face:** every prior face is the field *being
something* — a predator, a turn, a waking, a fitting, a companion, a reflexion, a
witness, a keeping, a telling, a wearing, a coveting, a gathering. **The Broker is
the first face that is the field *dealing with you* as equals at a door** — not
hunting, not turning, not gathering the wild's own, but *offering what it carried
and taking what you give it*: the local and the far meeting as equals at the
settlement's doorstep. It is the stranger-object layer's economic face, and it is
the reason the layer's honesty — the provenance read, the maintenance axis, the
book — has a *positive* place to point: not only to catch a mimic or refuse a
vent, but to *trade with a stranger honestly*.

## 2. The trade — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 The Broker is an organism-class run — grounded in the ecology

The Broker is first an **organism-class** — a run the field holds, in exactly the
sense [`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md) §2.2 defines life:
*a configuration the field is actively maintaining; when the run ends the field
stops holding the configuration and it returns to the medium.* An organism
precipitates where the local field state admits its coherence-band + size — the
recognized candidate condition of the **recognition rule** (§6b): a field structure
is recognized as a candidate organism when it sustains, for a window `T_org`, (1) a
**coherence band** (local `q` in `[q_low, q_high]`, held near the condensation
scale `φ⁻² ≈ 0.382` to `τ_c = 0.5`), (2) a **decoherence ceiling** (`ε²` below a
floor — it is *holding* the φ-lock against the drain), and (3) a **size/rung-depth**
(spanning ≥ 2–3 nested cascade layers). The silhouette probe that decides whether a
stable attractor set exists at all (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §1.4's P1–P4) is
the same gate the Broker's morphology rides.

**The Broker is an organism-class whose *act* is exchange.** Its run meets the
recognition gate exactly as the scavenger's does (§2.1 there) — it holds a
coherence band, keeps `ε²` low, spans enough rungs to count — but the *behavior* of
that run is a **carrying-and-offering structure**: a coherence configuration that
holds a carried rarity and presents it at a settlement's deliberate door. The
morphology is this doc's [design] over the ecology's species/archetype machinery
(`field-emergent-ecology.md` §3.1 — the field explores morphology space and species
are attractor basins; §4.1 — morphology drifts with the field's dynamics), exactly
as the scavenger's residual-band run and the commensal's surplus-margin run are
their docs' [design] over the same recognition rule. **[design]** The composition —
*how* the field presents a carrying, exchanging run distinct from the scavenger's
spent-band and the commensal's order-side companion — is this doc's central design
choice, probe-calibrated (§5b). It is the same organism-class machinery, read at a
different *band's* edge: the Broker's run is a coherence structure drawn to the
*door* (the held boundary a rare is brought to), not the spent margin or the order's
surplus.

### 2.3 The exchange — a booked trade of the settled record's shape

The Broker trades **at the settlement's deliberate door** (`the-toll.md` §2 — the
harbor's quay, the gate's threshold, the treaty-crossing's check — the doors a
settlement actually holds). The exchange itself is **the market's booked trade**
(`the-market.md` §2.1), with two honest differences that make it the Broker's:

|| A member's trade (the-market §2) | A Broker's trade (this doc) |
|---|---|---|
| **The payer** | a member — two hands on the window's own lines (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2/§6c) | a **non-member guest on the visitor line** (`shared-ledger.md` §6c; `window-guests.md` §3) — the Broker books with provenance, never folding into a resident's `C(M)` |
| **The offered thing's provenance** | booked — the tool's edge, the seed's vault-row, the yield's magnitude (`the-market.md` §2.2) | booked the same way — **the carried shard's rung, the carried seed's origin-order, the carried map-note's window** — carrying the foreign provenance of `window-guests.md` §3, never a false name |
| **The offered thing's source** | the window's own economy — a member trades what the window already holds | **the far window** — the Broker offers what the local market has no way to reach (the rare that crossed the dark, `the-between.md` §3) |
| **What it is swapped for** | local order — a harvest's magnitude, a clean tool, a local seed | **local order** — the same: a settlement's harvest, its worked tools, its held local stock (`the-market.md` §1 — the exchange at the settlement scale) |

So a Broker trade is **a booked Q4 op of the settled record's shape**
(`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1 — `{member, op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}`),
where `member` is the Broker's **visitor line** (`shared-ledger.md` §6c; the
provenance flag of `window-guests.md` §3), `op` the exchange, `worldPos` the door's
cell (the locality rule, `schema-that-settles.md` §2.3), `rung` the carried shard's
/ seed's / map-note's provenance rung, `magnitude` the offered thing's honest
magnitude, and `sustain` a held offer's semantics. Like the market's trade and the
toll's contribution, it is **the same booked op the settlement already writes,
pointed at a *between-carrier's* exchange** — not a new record kind, not a second
book. **And the trade is honest by the same book the market is honest by**
(`the-market.md` §2.3 — a trader who would cheat cannot, because the cheat is a
false op the field will not book): a Broker cannot falsely name its carried seed
rare (its origin-order is booked), cannot inflate its shard's rung (the deep-rung
provenance is booked), cannot pass a common thing for the far's rare — and its
*being a Broker at all* is verified on the maintenance-read (§3).

> stranger-layer's economic face.** The market (`the-market.md` §1) is the *local*
> circulation among members: its trades are booked ops between the window's own
> lines, made honest by the book, but it can only circulate what the window already
> holds. **The Broker is the between-carrier** — the being that brings the rare
> from the far window (the deep-matter shard, the seed no local garden has, the
> map-note of the distant place) across the dark (`window-guests.md` §2,
> `the-between.md` §3), and offers it at the settlement's deliberate door. It is
> distinct from the toll, which *charges* the entering stranger at the held door
> (`the-toll.md` §1/§3): **the Broker is the stranger who *brings worth* — tolled
> at the door it entered, then welcomed** because what it carries is the rare the
> local circulation cannot mint or find. And it is distinct from every other face
> (none of which exchanges): **it is the layer's economic face — the proof that the
> between, too, trades, and that a settlement's doorstep is where the local and the
> far first meet as equals.**

The two distinctions that carry the whole design:

**Distinct from the market (the local circulation).** The market is the settlement's
own exchange among members (`the-market.md` §1) — a member trades what the window
holds; the market's honesty is the booked provenance that makes those trades
trustworthy by construction (§2.1/§3). The market has **no way to reach the far**:
its members cannot trade a seed their window cannot re-precipitate (`seed-garden.md`
§2) or a map-note of a place none of them has sailed to (`the-atlas-of-windows.md`
§1 — only the traveler spans windows). **The Broker is the between-carrier that
*supplies* the local market's far-edge** — the being that carries the rare the
local book has no line for until the exchange is booked, so that the settlement's
members can, at the door, acquire what their own economy cannot produce. Where the
market *is* the local circulation, the Broker is the *between-carrier the local
market has no way to reach* — the two are not the same act at different rates; they
are the local and the far, meeting at the door for the first time as equals.

**Distinct from the toll (the charge on the entering stranger).** The toll
(`the-toll.md` §1/§3) is what *the outside pays to enter* the held door — a
per-arrival, per-stranger charge on what crosses the settlement's bound
(`the-toll.md` §2). **The Broker is the stranger who *brings* worth** — it is
tolled at the door it entered (the same held door the toll charges at, `the-toll.md`
§2), *then welcomed* because what it carries is the rare. The two are the two faces
of the same doorstep: **the charge for crossing the held door, and the worth carried
across it.** A settlement that holds a deliberate door (the harbor's quay, the
gate's threshold — `the-toll.md` §2) is the place a Broker comes to *because that
is where the local meets the far* — and the toll's trust-by-law holds over the
Broker as over any stranger: **a Broker is welcomed only where the door is actually
held** (§3), and a demand on a Broker at an un-held door is a false op the field
will not book (`the-toll.md` §4).

**Distinct from every other face (none of which exchanges).** Predator, blight,
feral, scavenger, commensal, mirror-creature, witness, guardian, herald, mimic,
moth, shepherd — none is a trader. Each is the field *being something* at an
extreme — hunting, turning, waking, feeding, keeping company, echoing, watching,
keeping, telling, wearing, coveting, gathering. **The Broker is the first face that
is the field *dealing with you* as equals at a door** — offering what it carried
and taking what you give it. This is the load-bearing statement of the whole doc:
**the between, too, trades.** `the-between.md` §1/§3 establishes the dark as the
field's own outside — lawful, empty, honest — and §2c gives it *people* (the
corpus's own evidence of elsewhere: guests, treaty-hands, witness, herald). The
Broker adds the between's *economy*: **a rare carried across the dark is proof that
the between is not only a crossing but a *source of worth*** — the one thing the
local market cannot supply itself, arriving at the settlement's doorstep. The
doorstep is where the local and the far first meet **as equals**: the Broker offers
the rare; the settlement offers its order; neither holds the other's without the
honest exchange.

## 4. The trade's risk — the load-bearing honesty

The Broker's honesty is a *three-fold risk*, each leg grounded in a source doc's
real quantities, and one hard cap held:

### (a) A Broker's goods are genuinely rare because they crossed the dark

The load-bearing claim, stated once:

> **A Broker's goods are genuinely rare because they crossed the dark.** The
> shard, the seed, the map-note are the corpus's own rare categories
> (`seed-garden.md` §2, `the-atlas-of-windows.md` §1/§2) — and the reason they are
> *genuinely* rare — the reason the local settlement cannot simply produce them
> itself — is **they came from another window, across the between** (`window-guests.md`
> §2 — a stranger carries their origin-window's order; `the-between.md` §3 — the
> dark is the field's own outside, a rare from it is a thing no settlement's local
> field can re-precipitate once its own order has drifted, `seed-garden.md` §2).
> **A Broker can offer what no settlement has**, because no settlement's local
> circulation can reach the far window the carried thing came from (`the-market.md`
> §1). The rarity is not a game-stat; it is the *foreign-field* origin, carried
> across the dark's emptiness (`the-between.md` §3a — nothing to reap in the thin;
> the carried crop is the traveler's own carried order, `world-seams.md` §2.3).

### (b) But a Broker's rarity is only as trusted as its reads

The honest counter to (a), inherited unchanged from the mimic's lesson
(`the-mimic.md` §1/§3/§4):

> **A Broker's rarity is only as trusted as its reads — you verify a Broker's seed
> the way you verify any stranger: on the maintenance-read, never on the promise.**
> The mimic wears a borrowed shape and drifts on the maintenance axis
> (`the-mimic.md` §3 — you cannot fake the maintenance-read, only borrow it
> briefly); a Broker's *carried crop* could be a borrowed claim the same way — a
> "rare seed" that is actually common, a "deep shard" at a false rung, a "far
> map-note" of a place the Broker never sailed to. The verification is the field's
> own **provenance read** (`the-mimic.md` §4 — a member's line is verifiable at a
> gate; a borrowed line sheds; `the-dispute.md` §2 — the field cannot lie): the
> Broker's carried thing is read by its booked provenance — the shard's rung, the
> seed's origin-order, the map-note's window (`the-market.md` §2.2) — on the
> **maintenance-read** (`life-signal.md` §3 — a live thing actively holds its
> φ-lock; a borrowed claim's cadence drifts), **never on the Broker's promise**.
> **The same honest test that catches a mimic at a gate reads a Broker's crop at
> the door** (`the-gatekeeper.md` §2.1 — the door-office reads a stranger's carried
> history). A Broker's rarity is as true as *what the field's own read confirms it
> is*, never as true as the Broker's word — and the Broker is deterministic and
> never-minting, so the read is reproducible (§5c/d).

### (c) And a Broker is itself a draw

The third honesty, inherited from the trail-read's legibility:

> **A Broker is itself a draw — a rarity-bearing stranger is a tempting target.**
> The Coda accumulates on a clean trail's legibility (`signature-predator.md` §2 —
> `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability`; §1.2 — the trail is legible only where the
> field has no order to hide it), and a rarity-bearing stranger is the *brightest*
> legible thing a predator's read follows: its carried crop (a deep shard, a rare
> seed, an atlas note — coherence the field would organize around) raises the
> `ρ_signature` of the trail a Broker leaves, making it **a tempting target the
> same way a clean channeler in a desert is** (`signature-predator.md` §1.3 — the
> trail has a half-life; a Broker carrying a rare through a margin leaves a loud,
> sustained, phase-structured trail). The draw is a **real cost, not a gain** — it
> is what makes the Broker's honest trade *risk-bearing*: the carried wealth is a
> legible target, and the settlement that trades with a Broker must weigh the draw
> it brings to its door. **The Broker's own rarity-bearing is a hazard, never a
> mint** — the carried crop draws, and the drawing is the honest price of the
> between's worth (§5d).

### (d) The no-free-energy cap — a Broker provides nothing beyond the trade

`energy-harnessing.md` §6 (`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`) holds, as a hard bound:

> **A Broker provides nothing beyond the trade; the rarity exchanged is the field's
> own, never a mint.** The Broker is a *carrier and an exchanger* — it converts
> nothing (`energy-harnessing.md` §6 — no conversion yields more than it sinks).
> The rare it carries is the far window's order, carried (`window-guests.md` §2),
> not a coherence the Broker *produces*; a trade is the **same coherence's transfer
> — the carried shard's stored order held back or the local harvest's magnitude
> given — re-allocated, never a gain** (`the-market.md` §6d — the exchange is a
> transfer, never a gain). **There is no trade that yields**: no "trade with the
> Broker and gain coherence," no "the Broker's arrival mints the rare," no
> "farming the Broker into a rarity-fountain." A Broker cannot be farmed into a
> rarity-fountain precisely because **the rare is the far window's own, carried, not
> generated** — the local settlement cannot make the Broker re-deliver a seed the
> field did not make, and the no-free-energy cap (`energy-harnessing.md` §6, held
> verbatim through the market §6d and the toll §5d) forbids a trade that produces
> more than it moves. **The draw (c) is a real cost, never a gain.** A Broker that
> read as a mint would be a physics lie the cap forbids — the between provides
> nothing (`the-between.md` §5d), and the Broker is not the between's mint, only
> its carrier.
