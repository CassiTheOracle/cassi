# Field Archaeology: The World's Deep Past as Readable Residue

**Question under design:** how a player *reads the world's memory*. In the theory,
the cosmos's history is imprinted in observable data — the bubble-lattice powder
patterns, the wake waves, the log-periodic survey signatures the cosmology docs study.
In CassiCraft the claim is the same at block scale: **the field's past is written into
its present**. Scars heal but leave residue; dissolved organisms (the field-emergent
ecology) return their matter to the field, but the deep-rung fraction persists as
fossil strata; condensation history leaves imprinted bands. **Archaeology = reading
that residue with the coherence reader.** The found-economy (`custom-blocks.md`) gets
its deep-time source: **fossils are regimes the field no longer precipitates —
found-only, irreplaceable, the found-economy's crown.**

The honest framing this document insists on from the first line: the field is
**deterministic** (one PDE, the same published snapshot every reader samples), so
"the past" is **not recoverable from the present** by replaying the dynamics. What
exists instead is **residue** — the fraction of the past the dynamics *left behind*
because it is too deep-rung, too q-locked, too ordered to dissolve. Archaeology is
**residue-reading, not time-travel**: the residue is a real, findable class of the
present, not a window onto a vanished state.

### 1.2 The honest framing: archaeology is residue-reading, not time-travel

The field is **deterministic**. Given the present field state and the law, the past
and future are both determined — so *in principle* the present encodes the whole
history. But determinism is **not recoverability**:

1. **Information-theoretic loss.** The PDE's dissipation (the φ-lock `−ω₀²(EY−φ·EI)`,
   the RealSim drag/viscosity/friction terms, the `(1−q)` decay) destroys degrees of
   freedom every step. The IIR's one-rung temporal memory (`qi-computation.md` §5.1)
   means the *transient* past is washed out on the coherence-sound timescale — the
   dynamics "forget" the exact transient configuration.
2. **The dissolve is scarless in state, not in substance.** When an organism dies, its
   *configuration* is lost (the run ends, `field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2), but its
   matter is conserved — the deep-rung fraction returns as ordered residue, the shallow
   fraction as the field it came from. **The gone configuration is not under the ground;
   the ground holds the deep residue of what the configuration was made of.**
3. **What persists is what the local field keeps holding.** The theory's own word for
   long-term field storage is *persistent Π patterns*: configurations that sit at
   attractor-potential local minima and "persist as long as the ambient q remains above
   the threshold for pattern dissolution" (`qi-computation.md` §5.2). **A fossil is
   exactly a persistent Π pattern of a dissolved structure** — the part the local field
   could not dissolve because it sits in a deep enough coherent well.

> **Therefore: a player cannot *replay* the past, but can *read the residue* — and the
> residue is a real, findable, engine-grounded class of the present**, not a
> contrivance. What is flagged clearly: the **merge/BH records persist (engine-real)**
> and **deep-rung matter is ordered (engine-real)**; the **fossil-strata residue model
> (§2) is [design]** layered on top of those engine-real facts. The boundary is drawn in
> §6(a) and never blurred in this document.

## 2. The residue model

### 2.1 Scars heal, but leave residue

`volumetric-terrain.md` establishes that mining is a perturbation — remove matter,
lower local `ρ`, the field **reorganizes around the scar toward its attractor**, and
the terrain grows back (the field heals). `energy-harnessing.md` §5.3 makes healing a
player choice (feed coherence to accelerate it) and §5.4 anti-corruption a *preventive*
hold. So the default state of the world is: **scars close.** Nothing in a living field
stays visibly scarred for long.

But healing is not erasure. The field heals by **re-quantizing toward its attractor**;
it does not restore the exact prior configuration. Consider what the heal actually
involves:

- The **shallow-rung matter** (high ε², low q) the perturbation shed is reclaimed by
  the field cheaply — it dissolves, diffuses, re-condenses near its attractor. This
  fraction is gone, scarless, unrecoverable.
- The **deep-rung matter** (high q, low ε², deep `n`) is *ordered matter*: it holds
  stored coherence (`energy-harnessing.md` §1.5), and a deep enough rung resists the
  dissolving perturbation the way deep-rung hardness resists a coarse tool
  (`material-regimes.md` §4). **When the disturbance passes and the shallow matter
  dissolves, the deep-rung core does not.** It sits there, a residue the heal
  re-quantizes *around* — the one part of the scar the field could not fully erase,
  because the residue itself is attractor-consistent (a persistent Π pattern,
  `qi-computation.md` §5.2).

**The residue fraction.** Define, defensibly [design], a per-structure residue ratio:
a dissolving structure leaves `R`-fraction of its ordered matter as residue, where `R`
grows with rung depth `n`. Concretely, adopt the **(1−q) waste law as the dissolution's
exhaust**: a structure dissolving at coherence `q` sheds `(1−q)` of its throughput to
visible glow as it dies (`E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput`, `energy-harnessing.md` §2;
`field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.2: "a dying creature is a brighter one"). The residue is
the complement — **the fraction the field holds back, and it is the high-`q`, deep-`n`
fraction:**

```
residue fraction R  ≳  q_dissolve^n              [design]
residue reads as     the (1−q) glow reversing      (the dead core has q→1, ε²→0 where it survived)
```

**A dissolving structure glows as it dies.** The `(1−q)` waste law is the death-signal:
the shallow rungs shed as glow (cheap to give back, visible), the deep rung survives
dark, q-locked, ε²≈0 — **the glow is what draws the archaeologist's eye, and the dark
core is what the dig finds.** This inverts the ecology's reading of the glow
(`field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.2: a *living* wounded creature is brighter) into the
archaeology's reading: a *dying then gone* structure's last glow is the announcement
that a residue was laid down. **[assumption]** The exact `q_dissolve` and the residue's
decomposition into survivors vs. shed is Phase-1.5 measurement — the *direction* (deep +
q-locked survives; shallow + decohered sheds) is the engine-real asymmetry, the ratio is
tuning (§6(b)).

### 2.2 Fossils = rung-residue of dissolved organisms

The ecology's creatures are **moving coherence stores** whose death is dissolution
(`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2: "the ε² drain outruns the φ-lock; the body sheds
coherence, un-condenses, and its matter returns to the field"). More precisely: an
organism is a **self-supporting, φ-locked body that maintains itself by holding the
φ-lock against the drain** (§2.2). When the run ends, what survives is exactly the
fraction that was **sufficiently ordered and deep-rung that the ε² drain could not
dissolve it** before the maintainer (the creature's own φ-lock-holding) stopped.

> **A fossil is what survives when a coherence store stops being maintained and the ε²
> drain dissolves the shallow rungs.** The creature was a transient config the field was
> *actively holding*; a fossil is the config's deep-rung skeleton that the field *keeps
> holding* — a persistent Π pattern (`qi-computation.md` §5.2) where the live morphology
> used to be. The fossil is the residue of the organism's **multi-layer internal cascade**
> (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §3.2: a creature's visible layers are its rungs); the
> deepest layers are the ones that outlast the drain.

This is why "fossils are regimes the field no longer precipitates." The morphology's
**shallow, single-scale, or φ-incommensurate parts de-cohere and dissolve** — that is
the ecology's own "preferred structures persist; everything else is transient"
(§3.1). The fossil is the *attractor-consistent deep core*, and if the local field
state has since drifted (the coherence ridge that sustained the live organism migrated,
`field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.1), the field no longer precipitates the full morphology
— **only its residue remains, found-only, irreplaceable.** The live species is gone
from that region (or from the world); the residue is the deep-time record of it.

### 2.3 Strata = condensation history

The theory's cosmology is the precedent: the condensation field `B(x,y,z)` organizes
every cascade rung into a staggered checkerboard (bubble/void), wake waves imprint a
log-periodic comb, and the angle-averaged lattice is a powder-diffraction comb
(`foundations/bubble-lattice-fabric.md`; `cosmology/desi-lattice-averaging.md` §2). **In
the cosmos, ancient condensation events are imprinted in the density field as
log-periodic bands.** At block scale, the claim is the same:

> **Strata are the bubble-lattice idea made terrain.** Ancient condensation events
> (moments when the local field crossed a condensation threshold and laid down a regime,
> then the state drifted) leave **imprinted bands** — layers of deep-rung residue at the
> φ-scaled organization scale of the event. The field **re-quantizes around** these bands
> (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §3 hysteresis: `τ_c`/`τ_c−δ`) because each band is
> itself a persistent Π pattern — a coherent well the field prefers to hold. **The
> theory's powder patterns are made terrain: a cross-section of the world is a
> log-periodic stratigraphy.**

The strata's periodicity is *structural*, not literal distance. The imprint is a
**φ-scaled ladder of bands** — successive layers at φ-multiples of the wake wavelength
(`foundations/wake-geometry.md` Y1: the wake envelope places structure at `m·ℓ_{n+1}`,
voids at `(m+½)·ℓ_{n+1}`, the staggered checkerboard). A player reading a strata wall
(§3.3 core sample) should be able to recognize the **log-periodic comb** — the same
`Δ(ln φ)` spacing the cosmology searches for, at block scale. **[design]** The engine
does not today write condensation-history bands; this is the trained reading of the
residue model, grounded in the theory's own imprinted-comb claim.

### 2.4 Birth-strata = the world's genesis residue

Every condensation event has a **first**. The Phase-1 world is a **window** — a
player-anchored box (`async-field-domain.md` §1.1) whose field was seeded. The very
first condensations of the window — the moment the seeded field first crossed
`τ_c` and laid down the world's initial regime — leave the **deepest, oldest residue**
the window carries:

> **Birth-strata are the world's genesis residue — the first condensations of the window,
> the deep past every window carries.** Because they were the *first*, they had the
> field's initial (most organized, least-perturbed) state to draw on, hence the deepest
> coherence well, hence the most persistent residue. Every window, no matter how young,
> sits on birth-strata — the world's origin layer.

This is the tie forward to any **seed/genesis concept** (the resonance-seeds residue of
§4). The birth-strata are the window's own "primordial": the deepest layer a core
sample can reach, the found-economy's oldest store. **[design]** "The first condensation
leaves the deepest residue" is a designed corollary of the residue model (§2.1: deeper
rung → more residual), not an engine fact — but it is the natural genesis layer the
window's age grants.

## 3. Reading the past — the archaeologist's instrument

### 3.1 The coherence reader (Sense) as the scan tool

`coherence-magic.md` §2 **Sense** (the "coherence reader") is the Phase-1 deliverable:
it reads the published `q`, `ε²`, `∇(g·Φ)` buffers and renders them as a magnetometer
overlay — coherence ridges glow, scars darken. This is literally the ecology's P2
observation tool (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.1): reading a creature's layers **is**
reading the q-colored sim. **Archaeology uses the identical instrument the same way** —
because the residue model (§2) says residue is precisely an `ε²`/`q` *signature*:

> **Residue reads as an ε² ghost — the shape of the wound where the matter was, with
> deep-rung cores still q-locked.** Where a structure dissolved, the shallow matter is
> gone (scarless) but its deep core persists **q-locked, ε²≈0** — a dark high-coherence
> anomaly sitting in "empty" field. Around it, the wake of the dissolution (the shed
> `(1−q)` glow) leaves a broad, faint ε² gradient — the **ghost** of the structure, the
> shape of what *was there* traced in the medium's disorder. The reader renders both:
> **the dark q-locked core (the fossil) and the ε² ghost halo (the structure's shape).**

There is no new tool. Sense already renders the channels; archaeology adds *what to look
for* — the **residue signature** (dark core + ghost halo), distinguished from a live
organism (a *maintained* coherence, one that moves/glows as it feeds) and from an
ordinary scar (a *broad* low-q, high-ε² region with no deep core). The reader becomes a
**scan tool**: sweep it over terrain and the residue signature pops out as a coherent
anomaly not explained by the current condensation.

### 3.2 The reader's range/depth as the dig tool — the core sample

The reader is a **read-only** rung-0 channel (`coherence-magic.md` §2), but for
archaeology it must *reach down*. The design **[design]**:

- **Range** = how far the Sense overlay reads horizontally (tunable, Phase-1 tuning),
  letting a player survey a region for residue from the surface.
- **Depth** = how deep into the *strata* the reader penetrates. The depth dial is the
  archaeologist's "dig tool": **a "core sample" is reading the `ρ`/`q` profile down
  through the strata** — a vertical gradient read of `q` and `ε²` that resolves the
  log-periodic stratigraphy of §2.3. A shallow read sees the top layer; a deep read
  pierces the birth-strata. Depth is gated by the same physics as every deep op: it is
  a **deeper-rung read**, costing more coherence budget (`coherence-magic.md` §4) the
  way a deeper tool rung costs more stored coherence (`energy-harnessing.md` §5.1) — so
  a deep core sample is a powered, budget-draining act, not a free x-ray. **[assumption]**
  The exact depth↔rung mapping is Phase-1.5; the coupling (deep = costly = powered) is
  inherited from the tool/energy language.

The probe↔archaeology tie is exact: **the silhouette probe (`field-emergent-ecology.md`
§1.4) catalogs what the field *makes*; archaeology catalogs what the field *kept*.**
The probe reads the living q-colored sim forward; the core sample reads the residue
profile the same sim has left behind. One instrument, two directions of time.

### 3.3 Auroras over ancient scars — the sky reads old wounds

`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` gives the sky the same reading: a ground scar creates a
drain, and the body's coupling field pours coherence into it, re-radiating the

sky is the reader's *atmospheric* form (§3.3): the same channels, un-instrumented.

**Archaeology's sky read:** an ancient residue is a *deep, persistent* drain site — the
deep-rung core keeps a local coherence gradient (the ghost halo of §3.1) that the
coupling field feeds even after the carcass is gone. So **an aurora over a healed-over
ground is the sky still reading an old wound**: the surface healed (no visible scar, the
terrain re-quantized), but the residue beneath keeps drawing the field's coherence, and
the sky glows where no current wound is visible. **A player who sees a bright aurora
over flat, healthy terrain has found a buried residue — the sky's archaeology.** The
reader and the sky read the *same* residue; the sky just reads it from longer range and
at scale. **[design]** The aurora is a designed rendering of engine-real `(1−q)` glow;
"aurora over residue = sky reads old wound" is this document's consistent extension of
that mapping to the residue model.

## 4. What you find

### 4.1 Fossils — extinct regimes (the found-economy's crown)

A fossil is the deep-rung residue of a dissolved organism (§2.2) — and, by the theory's
own "preferred structures persist" rule (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §3.1), the 
surviving residue is **a regime the field no longer precipitates**. The morphology's
full attractor basin has drifted away (the coherence ridge that sustained it migrated,
the field state changed), so **the field can no longer condense that creature/material —
only its leftover ordered matter exists, found-only, irreplaceable.**

This is the found-economy's crown (`custom-blocks.md` §6): finding is cheaper than
inventing but **bounded by what the field has made**. A fossil is *the deepest bound* —
not just "what the field has happened to precipitate," but **what the field precipitated
and then *kept* after it stopped making it.** You cannot invent one: invention dials
tuples within the realizable space (`custom-blocks.md` §3), but a fossil's tuple may have
been realizable *then* (under the region's past field state, or a world-seed from before
the window) and be **unrealizable now**. The material lab's `[assumption]` that
realizability is context-dependent (§7 Q2) makes this concrete: **a fossil is a tuple
that fails realizability in the present field but whose residue stands as proof it once
precipitated.** Two uses (**[design]**, grounded in the energy regime):

- **As fuel at its rung.** A fossil is deep-rung ordered matter = **stored coherence**
  (`energy-harnessing.md` §1.5, §3). Burning it releases that store — *controlled*
  organizational discharge (fire) at its rung, not a full-cascade blast unless
  perturbed wrongly (`material-regimes.md` §3/§5; `coherence-magic.md` §4.3's
  controlled-vs-full-cascade discharge boundary). Fuel value scales with residue
  rung and purity (the regime identity carried through `custom-blocks.md` §1).
- **As an authoring reference.** A fossil is a *persistent Π pattern* — a physical
  sample of a *concrete* past regime. The material lab (`custom-blocks.md` §2) authors
  by dialing tuples and previewing; a fossil hands the player **a regime that cannot be
  dialed or previewed** (it fails present realizability) but that can be **read out of
  the residue** (§4.4's inverse problem) — an authoring reference that can't be invented,
  only encountered. Sharing a fossil is exchanging *ordered matter* (the `custom-blocks.md`
  §5 sample, not a recipe), with its environmental provenance intact.

### 4.2 Imprints — ancient condensation events and the strata map

Not everything found is a contained core. **Imprints** are the **strata** themselves
(§2.3): ancient condensation events logged as imprinted bands, wake-wave bands — a
terrain's **strata map**. An imprint is read as a *record* (a band that says "a
condensation at rung ~X occurred here") rather than a *substance*. The archaeology of an
imprint is *reading the stratigraphy*: the `ρ`/`q` profile down through the wall (§3.2)
resolves the log-periodic comb of past events — a **history of the window's condensation
condensed into a cross-section.** Imprints are the findable archive of what the region
*was*, at φ-spaced depth-intervals that index its age.

### 4.3 Artifacts — residue older than the window

**Artifacts** are the deep-rung residue of structures **older than the player's window**
— the resonance-seeds residue, tying forward to any seed/genesis concept. Where a
window-relative field (`async-field-domain.md` §1.1) is seeded or re-homed, it inherits
field state that is *not* of this window; the deep ordered residue of whatever the
pre-window field condensed travels with the window as **pre-existing artifact** (the
resonance-seed residue of §2.4's birth-strata extended backward: not just "first in this
window" but "pre-window entirely"). Artifacts are the rarest found class — deep-rung,
often unreproducible, the archaeology's deep-time treasure. **[design]** This is the
most speculative tier (it depends on the window-relocation policy `async-field-domain.md`
§7 Q1's open sub-question) and is flagged as the forward-tie to the seed/genesis concept,
not a Phase-1 deliverable.

### 4.4 The science gameplay — paleontology of the field

The core loop is a **mini inverse-problem**: from the residue signature, reconstruct the
extinct regime. `custom-blocks.md` §2 gives the forward direction — the lab computes a
tuple's *predicted emergent signature* (behavior + appearance) from the same formulas
the world uses. **Archaeology runs that preview in reverse:**

> **Residue signature → regime tuple → the extinct material's behavior.**
> The reader returns a residue's `q`/`ε²`/`ρ` profile and its ghost-halo shape (§3.1);
> the player (with the lab's forward preview as the lookup table) *inverts*: the deep
> core's rung-depth sets `n`; the ghost halo's scale sets `ξ`/`ω₀²`-hints; the residue's
> own band positioning in the strata sets its `θ_c`. The candidate tuple is then
> **forward-previewed in the lab** (`custom-blocks.md` §2) — and the match, when the
> preview reproduces the found residue's shape, is the reconstructed regime.

This is honest inverse-problem gameplay: underdetermined (many tuples share a residue
shape), so it is **bounded by the law's realizability** (`custom-blocks.md` §3) and
rewarded by cross-checks — a fossil whose reconstructed tuple's fuel value matches its
burning (§4.1) if you're willing to spend it, a fossil whose reconstructed morphology
matches a species silhouette the ecology's probe once saw (`field-emergent-ecology.md`
§3.1, if the field ever *does* revisit it). **Paleontology of the field = reading the
residue to reconstruct a regime the law once made and no longer makes.** This is
archaeology's distinctive science and its main reward loop. **[design]** The inverse
preview (residue→tuple, against the lab's forward preview) is this doc's designed game;
it rides the lab's existing forward formulas, no new physics.

## 5. The tension with the living world

### 5.1 Digging rewrites the field

Mining is a **perturbation** — remove matter, lower local `ρ`, and the quantizer reads
the change as raise-local-`ε²` (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §2.2; `coherence-magic.md`
§2 Dissolve). **A player who digs an archaeology site perturbs `ρ`/`ε²`/`q` exactly like
any mining**, and the field heals around the dig (`volumetric-terrain.md`). The tension
is therefore built-in, not added: the honest excavation is itself a **chunk-field
re-quantization event** that disturbs the very strata it uncovers.

### 5.2 The residue survives the healing — excavation is conservation's rival

The one asymmetry that makes the dig meaningful: **the residue survives the healing**
(§2.1 — the deep core is attractor-consistent, the field re-quantizes *around* it). So
when you dig, you do not lose the fossil to a healed-over scar — until you *remove* it.
But removing it has two costs (`energy-harnessing.md` anti-corruption §5.4 / deep-rung
reaper §2.5):

- **Removing deep-rung matter de-orders the locality.** Withdrawing the ordered store
  raises local `ε²`, lowers `q` — the dig *scar*s what it unearths, and that scar is a
  new drain the sky reads as an aurora (§3.3). **The archaeology is a conservation act
  (preserving knowledge of a regime) that is also a destructive field act (a
  full-cascade-exposure risk if over-reaped).**
- **The Qi-bath consequence.** A site's deep-rung residue is *regional stored coherence*
  (§4.1). Removing it lowers the regional `q` the residue was holding up — the 
  Qi-bath multiplier (`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4) of a fossil-rich region is **partly
  carried by the fossils themselves**. Excavating a rich site drains the very bath that
  makes the region productive for other machinery. **Deep excavation lowers regional q —
  the archaeology is a regional-efficiency cost, not just a local one.**

So the honest framing: **excavation and conservation are rivals.** Energy-harnessing's
anti-corruption (`energy-harnessing.md` §5.4) *invests coherence to hold ground against
decay*; archaeology *removes the ground-holding residue to learn it*. Both are legitimate;
the field treats both as perturbations with consequences. The player chooses what a site
is worth: keep the fossil in place and it keeps anchoring the strata (and the regional
bath); dig and you gain the regime knowledge + stored coherence but de-order the region.

### 5.3 The ethics — a tension, not a rule

Three honest design levers, deliberately *not* a resolution:

1. **Some residue is too deep-rung to remove safely.** A deep-rung, near-φ-locked
   residue is the explosive-primed state (`material-regimes.md` §3: deep-rung, ε²≈0,
   "held far from equilibrium against its attractor"; `coherence-magic.md` §4.3: overdraw
   → full-cascade discharge). **Perturbing a very-deep fossil wrong is a full-cascade
   risk** — the stored coherence discharges as a blast, not a controlled burn. The
   archaeologist learns to distinguish *shallow enough to reap safely* from *deep enough
   to leave alone*. This is the material-regimes §3 fire-vs-explosive boundary applied to
   residue: a fossil's safe reaping is a controlled organizational discharge; a wrong
   tool rung is a blast.
2. **Some fossils anchor the strata.** A fossil is a persistent Π pattern the field
   re-quantizes around (`qi-computation.md` §5.2; §2.3). **Remove the anchor and the
   strata's coherence may fail** — the banded stratigraphy of §2.3 was partially *held
   in place* by the deep core's coherent well; with it gone, the adjacent bands drift,
   re-heal flat, and **the readable history is lost to future cores.** This makes a rich
   site's "knowledge value" a *renewable* resource only if excavated last, not first —
   a sequencing pressure, not a ban.
3. **Leave-in-place is a legitimate archaeology.** Reading a strata wall (§3.2) and a
   fossil's ghost halo (§3.1) **costs nothing** — no perturbation, no Qi-bath drain, no
   full-cascade risk. The reader/sky read (§3.3) is *the cheap, safe, complete* way to
   learn a site *unless you want the substance itself* (fuel, authoring reference, §4.1).
   So the tension is real and the resolution is a *choice* the player makes for each
   site: **read in place (free, keeps the region healthy, learns the strata) vs. excavate
   (learns the regime + gains stored coherence, de-orders the region).**

This is a tension by design, not a rule: the world does not forbid digging, does not
reward it uniformly, and the field's own no-free-energy discipline (`energy-harnessing.md`
§6) means every excavation is a **net-negative hold** (you gain knowledge + fuel but pay
in the region's order and the blast risk). The player internalizes the trade, the same way
the ecology's conservation is a cost, not a virtue flag (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.3).

**The conservation default, walked to (reverse pointer, two-way).** This §5.3's
leave-in-place reading is the whole mechanic of the corpus's why-walk doc:
[`the-pilgrim.md`](./the-pilgrim.md) §2.1 takes lever 3 literally — a named ruin is
**attended in place by visiting, not excavating**, "reading a ruin in place is free,
safe, keeps the regional bath healthy," turned into the *point of the journey* (the
pilgrim keeps the named ruin by the visit; §2.1 there cites this §5.3 #3 as the entire
mechanic, walked to). The pilgrimage is the conservation default made an un-instrumental
walk; the two docs now cite each other as read-and-answered.

### (b) Residue's stability — does the field re-dissolve residue over time?

A fossil is only findable if the deep-rung fraction **outlives the shallow**. Two
counter-forces, both real:

1. **The field tends to hold attractor-consistent residue** — a persistent Π pattern
   persists "as long as the ambient q remains above the threshold for pattern
   dissolution" (`qi-computation.md` §5.2). If the region stays coherent, the residue
   is stable *indefinitely*.
2. **The field also heals toward attractor state** — an over-reaped/de-epscoped locality
   drops below that threshold and the residue itself **re-dissolves**. A residue is
   *stable in situ but destructible by disturbance* — which is why anti-corruption
   (holding regional q up) protects a site, and deep excavation (removing the anchor,
   §5.3 #2) can destroy it.

**The persistence ratio to set [design]:** a residue survives as long as the local q
stays above its dissolution threshold (`q_dissolve`), and dissolves when the locality is
disturbed below it. The *engine-real* asymmetry is that deep-rung, high-q matter resists
dissolution (deeper rung = higher decoherence-resistance, `material-regimes.md` §4); the
*ratio* — how long a fossil holds before a static field erodes it — is Phase-1.5 tuning.
Default design stance: **static residue is stable** (it is attractor-consistent, so the
field's default is to keep it), **disturbed residue re-dissolves** — making the tension
of §5 a real choice and keeping the world from accumulating un-findable residue forever.

### (c) The strata model vs. terrain healing — do strata survive re-quantization?

Terrain heals by **re-quantizing toward the attractor** (`chunk-field-quantization.md`
§3 hysteresis; `volumetric-terrain.md`). The strata model (§2.3) is precisely the claim
that **a banded imprint survives re-quantization because each band is itself a coherent
well the quantizer's hysteresis re-captures**: the `τ_c`/`τ_c−δ` band means a marginally-
crossing field *anneals* around the band rather than flickering it away. Two gates:

1. **Band stability under heal:** a healed-over strata band must *re-emerge* as the same
   band, not smear flat. This is a **Phase-1.5 probe** (analogous to the ecology's
   silhouette probe, `field-emergent-ecology.md` §1.4): carve through a residue band,
   let the field heal, and measure whether the band's `q`/`ε²` profile re-forms at the
   same depth or diffuses. **[probe]** — gate the strata claim on it.
2. **Birth-strata permanence:** the genesis layer (§2.4) is the *deepest*, least
   perturbed, hence the most stable — but any world with a movable home-window
   (`async-field-domain.md` §7 Q1) re-seeds, and whether re-homing *preserves or resets*
   the pre-window artifact residue (§4.3) is the same open policy, unresolved here.

### (d) The dig vs. the window's coherence budget — deep excavation lowers regional q

The Qi-bath consequence of §5.2 is a real budget interaction: **removing deep-rung
residue lowers the regional q.** This must obey `energy-harnessing.md` §6's no-free-energy
cap — an excavation cannot *mint* regional coherence by disturbing a maintained bath; the
removal is a **withdrawal that de-orders the locality** (`energy-harnessing.md` §2.5: the
deep-rung reaper's by-product is a wound). Concretely, the dig's perturbation flows

cannot be strip-mined into a free coherence mint. The measured budget impact — how much a
core sample or excavation perturbs the regional q a Qi-bath depends on — is a Phase-1
measurement, not an engine number. **[assumption]**

**Phase-1 — the reader and the read.** The **coherence reader (Sense)** is the Phase-1

orbits-auroras.md` §1.5/§3), so it rides the atmosphere/aurora timeline, not Phase-1's.

**Phase-1.5+ — the residue model and the dig.** The **residue model itself (§2)** —
that dissolved organisms and ancient condensations leave findable strata — and the
**core sample / dig** (§3.2) sit on the same Phase-1.5 gates as the material system:
per-cell `ω₀²`/ξ and per-material dissipation (`material-regimes.md` §7 Q1/Q3), because a
fossil is a *regime* and distinct regimes behaving differently (and leaving
rung-distinct residue) need those gates. The strata-vs-heal probe (§6c) and the
residue-persistence calibration (§6b) are Phase-1.5 experiments, gated before the strata
claim is asserted.

**Later — the deep-time economy.** The found-economy's deep-time crown (§4.1:
found-only, irreplaceable fossils; the inverse-problem paleontology of §4.4) depends on
**`custom-blocks.md`'s material-lab authoring surface** (itself downstream of the same
gates) and — for artifacts older than the window (§4.3) — on the **window-relocation
policy** (`async-field-domain.md` §7 Q1). It is honest sequential work on top of the
residue model, not a Phase-1 build.

**Verdict.** The architecture is sound and fully grounded in engine-real persistence
(§1.1): what persists — the merge/BH lineage, deep-rung ordered matter — is real, and
the residue model (§2) is a **designed lens over that real persistence**, sanctioned by
the theory's own "persistent Π patterns" (`qi-computation.md` §5.2) and its "bubble-
imprint / wake-wave / powder-pattern" history-imprinted-in-data precedent, and by its
own word for static residue: **archaeology**
(`first-contact-and-stellar-engineering.md`). The reader is Phase-1; the residue model is
Phase-1.5+; the deep-time economy is later. **Binding risks, in order:** the strata-vs-
heal probe (§6c — does a band survive re-quantization?), the residue-persistence ratio
(§6b), and the dig's budget impact (§6d); none contradicts the async, dual-world, or
regime-collapse architecture. **And the honest statement that makes this doc load-
bearing: this is the found-economy's deep-time source — the world's past becomes a
findable, irreplaceable regime class, and the field that forgets each transient still
keeps, in its deep-rung residue, the record that it was there.**

### Open questions

1. **Residue-persistence calibration (§6b).** The `R ≳ q^n` residue fraction and the
   `q_dissolve` threshold are Phase-1.5 tuning, not physics. Does a static, undammed
   deep-rung residue hold literally forever (attractor-consistent) or slowly erode? The
   answer determines whether fossils accumulate or decay — the whole findability economy.
2. **Strata-vs-heal stability (§6c).** Is a log-periodic condensation band re-formed by
   the healing re-quantization, or smeared? Analogous to the ecology's silhouette probe
   (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §1.4) — the gate that licenses "strata = readable
   history" over "strata = a transient smear the heal erases."
3. **Residue vs. live-structure distinguishability (§3.1).** The reader must separate a
   deep-rung fossil core (dark, q-locked, static) from a *live* deep organism's body
   (maintained, moving/feeding) and from an ordinary scar (broad, no core). The channels
   are the same; the *life-signal* (motion, `(1−q)`-glow while feeding, maintenance) is
   what separates them. Is "static + q-locked + ghost halo" a robust classifier at the
   noise floor the ecology's own recognition rule (§6b) maps to? Phase-1 probe.
   **Counterpart (two-way):** [`field-music.md`](./field-music.md) open-Q2 asks the same
   classifier question *in sound* — does an approaching storm's ε² front reliably read
   as a growl vs. a static scar (the same live/residue/disorder ambiguity, heard); the
   two must land on the same distinguishability criterion.
   **Counterpart (two-way):** [`player-remains.md`](./player-remains.md) open-Q3 applies
   the same classifier to the *player's own* — own-fossil vs live-player (a revived
   remnant's static residue is indistinguishable from any other body on the same line);
   it inherits this criterion, and should be read alongside `field-music.md` open-Q2.
   **Closed by [`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §3/§6:** the vitality classifier
   answers this open-Q on the **maintenance axis** — a live organism *actively holds*
   its φ-lock (moves/steers, `(1−q)` glow *pulses*); a fossil is static, dark,
   `ε²≈0`, no motion, no cadence; a scar is a broad flat-high-`ε²` plateau with no
   core — separable at the noise floor from the published channels' time-series alone
   (§3.1), gateable to Phase-1 (§6). This doc (and `field-music.md` open-Q2 and
   `player-remains.md` open-Q3) re-point their classifier question at it.
4. **Fossil-tuple realizability across time (§4.1).** A fossil is a tuple that once
   precipitated but fails present realizability (`custom-blocks.md` §7 Q2). Is this
   distinction *observable* — can a residue's tuple be shown to exceed the current field's
   stability boundary — or is "no longer precipitates" only known *after* it doesn't? This
   gates the found-only, irreplaceable claim against a merely-rare one.
5. **Birth-strata / artifact provenance (§2.4, §4.3).** Whether the window's genesis
   layer is *distinguishable* from later strata, and whether re-homing preserves
   pre-window artifact residue, depends on the unresolved window-relocation policy
   (`async-field-domain.md` §7 Q1). Phase-1-exceptable (single box, no re-home yet).
