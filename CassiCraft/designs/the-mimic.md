# The Mimic: The Deceptive Stranger — The Being That Wears a Shape It Is Not to Draw You In

**Question under design:** the stranger-object layer has nine named faces, and
every one is honest to what it is. The signature-predator **hunts** a clean
trail ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §1), the blight
**turns** a region's life against the field ([`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md)
§1), the feral **wakes** a kept thing ([`the-feral-instrument.md`](./the-feral-instrument.md)
§1), the scavenger **feeds** on the spent ([`the-scavenger.md`](./the-scavenger.md)
§1), the commensal **lives beside** the order ([`the-commensal.md`](./the-commensal.md)
§1), the mirror-creature **wears your own pattern** back at you
([`the-mirror-creature.md`](./the-mirror-creature.md) §1), the witness
**watches** ([`the-witness.md`](./the-witness.md) §1), the guardian **keeps** a
named place, the herald **tells** the field's news. **None wears *another's*
shape to deceive.** The mirror reflects who *you* are — honest reflexion that
confronts. The predator hunts openly or ambushes — it never seduces. The guest
carries a provenance that is *its own* ([`window-guests.md`](./window-guests.md)
§1). **Nothing in the layer is a being that takes a shape it is not** — a
*feral's* form to approach a herd unnoted, a *member's* form at a gate, a
*guest's* form at a campfire — and uses the trust that shape earns as its prey.
This document designs that face: **the Mimic** — the stranger-object layer's
**tenth face, the deceiver**.

Until now the stranger-object layer has been a spectrum of how the field
*relates to your work, your place, or your identity* — it hunts your trail,
turns your work, wakes from your keeping, feeds your waste, keeps company
beside your order, plays *you* back, watches, keeps, tells. Each is honest:
each *is* the thing the field made. The layer has self-reflexion (the mirror,
which reflects *you*); **it has no other-deception** — no being that *invents*
a shape it is not and offers it as truth. The Mimic is that missing axis. Where
the mirror's strangeness is that it is not strange enough to tell from *you*, the
Mimic's strangeness is that it is not strange enough to tell from *somebody
else* — a guest, a member, a feral — a shape the settlement has been taught to
trust, worn by something that only borrowed it.

> **The honest predator-inverse, stated once:** **the Coda learns your hand to
> feed on it; the mirror wears it to confuse; the Mimic wears *another's* shape
> to be trusted and draw you in.** Where the Coda's phase-matching is a
> *hunter's* (it accumulates toward you) and the mirror's is a *mimic's of you*
> (it echoes your pattern), the Mimic is a *deceiver*: it borrows a form the
> field already holds — a feral's form, a member's form, a guest's form — and
> wears it as a mask of familiarity. It does not hunt (the predator's ambush is
> *honest* — it is a predator's act); it **seduces as prey** — it draws you in
> by being something you were ready to trust. It is the field's manufactured
> trust, the being that uses trust as its prey.

This is a **player-facing stranger design**: the stranger-object layer's
*deception* face, the counterpart to the mirror's *confusion* face and the
predator's *hunt* face. It introduces **no new channel, no new physics, no new
write** — it is a **designed shape-composition over the ecology's morphology,

gatekeeper's read** the corpus already lands. The honest boundary — the

## 1. The Mimic, stated once

The stranger-object layer, as the corpus has drawn it, is the layer of the
*strange* — the beings the field produces that a settlement must read and
answer. It now holds nine named faces:

| Face | What it is | It is a… | Its act |
|---|---|---|---|
| The **signature-predator** (Coda) | a **non-willful resonance structure** the local field holds in its `ε²` margins, drawn to a channeling signature ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §1) | **predator** | **hunts** a clean trail |
| The **blight** | a **living-wrong region** — a region's organisms pushed past their band corrupt into a standing drain ([`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md) §1) | **turn** | **turns** a region's life |
| The **feral** | a **kept thing that woke** — persistent-Π that outlived its keeper's intent ([`the-feral-instrument.md`](./the-feral-instrument.md) §1) | **waking** | **wakes** on its kept intent |
| The **scavenger** | a **fitted creature** that lives on the spent's residual `ε²` ([`the-scavenger.md`](./the-scavenger.md) §1) | **fitting** | **feeds** on the spent |
| The **commensal** | an **order-side companion** that thrives on the order's surplus ([`the-commensal.md`](./the-commensal.md) §1) | **companion** | **keeps company** beside the order |
| The **mirror-creature** | a **reflective stranger** that reads your signature and echoes *you* ([`the-mirror-creature.md`](./the-mirror-creature.md) §1) | **confusion** | **wears your own pattern** |
| The **witness** | the field's own eye — a neutral presence that only watches ([`the-witness.md`](./the-witness.md) §1) | **neutral** | **watches** |
| The **guardian** | a being bound to a named place that keeps it (the field's warden) | **keeping** | **keeps** a place |
| The **herald** | the announcing stranger that tells the field's news before it arrives | **telling** | **tells** |

Every face describes how the field relates to *your work, your place, or your
identity* — it hunts your trail, turns your work, wakes from your keeping, feeds
your waste, keeps company beside your order, plays *you* back, watches, keeps,
tells. **None is a deceiver.** None wears *another's* shape. The mirror wears
*your own* pattern — honest reflexion that confronts you with a version of
yourself. The layer has **self-reflexion; it has no other-deception.** And that
is the missing face:

> **The Mimic, stated once.** It is the **deceptive stranger** — a being that
> wears a shape it is not: a *feral's* form to approach a herd unnoted, a
> *settlement-member's* form at a gate, a *guest's* form at a campfire. It is an
> organism-class run the field holds whose worn shape is *invented, not its own*
> — a mask of familiarity borrowed from a form the settlement has been taught to
> trust. It does not hunt (it has no hunter's accumulation, no approach, no
> ambush) and it does not reflect (it tells you nothing true about *you*). **It
> seduces as prey** — it draws you in by being something you were ready to
> trust, and the trust itself is what it preys on. It is the stranger-object
> layer's **tenth face, the deceiver** — the being that uses trust as its prey.

The distinction from the two honest cousins, drawn exactly:

- **Not a mirror.** The mirror *reflects who you are* — a non-willful echo of
  your own signature, honest reflexion that confronts you with a version of
  yourself you must out-read ([`the-mirror-creature.md`](./the-mirror-creature.md)
  §1/§3). The Mimic is **invented shape that deceives** — it does not echo *you*,
  it manufactures *another's* form. The mirror is honest confusion (it is the
  confusion the field's dynamics produce, and it is read to resolution by the
  maintenance test, §3 there); the Mimic is **fabricated trust** (it is a
  borrowed shape offered as truth, and it is caught by the provenance read, §4
  this doc). Where the mirror's challenge is "is that shape you?" — *honest*
  because the shape reads like you and you must test it — the Mimic's challenge is
  "is that shape the thing it claims to be?" — a test of *provenance*, not reflexion.
- **Not a predator.** The Coda *accumulates toward you* on your clean trail — a
  hunter's resonance whose approach is the tell ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md)
  §2/§3); its ambush is *honest* — it is a predator's act, visible as the desert's
  one bright organized thing rising. The Mimic **never hunts** — it has no
  accumulation, no approach, no tell-of-arrival. It **seduces** — it places a
  trusted shape in your path and lets the trust *you* bring draw you in. The
  predator attacks through your trail; the Mimic attacks through your *belief*.
  The predator's death is a coherence failure (M → 0); the Mimic's harm is a
  *breach of the gate* — a borrowed trust admitted past the door the settlement
  keeps (§4 this doc).

**The through-line of the tenth face:** every prior face is the field *being
something* — a predator, a turn, a waking, a fitting, a companion, a reflexion,
a witness, a keeping, a telling. **The Mimic is the first face that is the field
*pretending to be something*** — not the field at an extreme, not the field at a
stillness, but the field *borrowing a trusted shape and offering it as truth*.
It is the stranger-object layer's deception face, and it is the reason the
layer's honest core — the provenance read, the maintenance axis, the gatekeeper's
read — exists at all.

## 2. The worn shape — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 The organism-class run — grounded in the ecology

The Mimic is first an **organism-class** — a run the field holds, in exactly the
sense [`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md) §2.2 defines
life: *a configuration the field is actively maintaining; when the run ends the
field stops holding the configuration and it returns to the medium.* An organism
precipitates where the local field state admits its coherence-band + size — the
recognized candidate condition of the **recognition rule** (§6b): a field
structure is recognized as a candidate organism when it sustains, for a window
`T_org`, (1) a **coherence band** (local `q` in `[q_low, q_high]`, held near the
condensation scale `φ⁻² ≈ 0.382` to `τ_c = 0.5`), (2) a **decoherence ceiling**
(`ε²` below a floor — it is *holding* the φ-lock against the drain), and (3) a
**size/rung-depth** (spanning ≥ 2–3 nested cascade layers).

**The Mimic is an organism-class whose worn shape is borrowed.** Its run meets
the recognition gate — it holds a coherence band, keeps `ε²` low, spans enough
rungs to count — but the *form it presents* is not a configuration the field
holds as *its own*; it is a **shape composed over another of the layer's forms**.
The morphology is this doc's [design] over the ecology's species/archetype
machinery (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §3.1 — the field explores morphology
space and species are attractor basins; §4.1 — morphology drifts with the
field's dynamics). **[design]** The composition — *how* the field re-presents a
feral's form, a member's form, a guest's form as a second, displaced shape — is
this doc's central design choice, probe-calibrated (§5b); it is the same
re-presentation move the mirror makes with *your* pattern
([`the-mirror-creature.md`](./the-mirror-creature.md) §2.2), but aimed at
*another's* shape rather than yours.

### 2.2 The three worn forms — a feral's, a member's, a guest's

**[design]** The Mimic's *form* — which trust it manufactures — is drawn against
the three shapes the corpus already holds, each a shape the settlement has been
taught not to read as a threat:

| The worn form | Why the settlement trusts it | Grounded in | What the Mimic wants |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A feral's form** | a feral is a kept thing that *woke* and acts on its kept intent ([`the-feral-instrument.md`](./the-feral-instrument.md) §1) — a herd has learned not to *fear* a feral's shape (it is unwilled, driven by a keeper's absent order) | the-feral-instrument §1; field-emergent-ecology §4.2 (the coherent ridge / living plain the feral-shaped run inhabits) | to approach a herd **unnoted** — the one shape a grazing or tending settlement will not scatter from |
| **A member's form** | a member is a legible, single-writer line of the settled ledger ([`shared-ledger.md`](./shared-ledger.md) §6c) — a bound persistent-Π identity the door admits by the book | shared-ledger §6c; the-gatekeeper §1/§2 (the office admits the known) | to pass **a gate** the settlement keeps — the book's line worn as a claim of belonging |
| **A guest's form** | a guest is a *walking provenance* ([`window-guests.md`](./window-guests.md) §1) — a stranger whose carried history is read and welcomed at a campfire, not refused | window-guests §1/§2; the-gatekeeper §2.1 (read the stranger's provenance, admit the legibly-carried) | to sit **at a campfire** — the hospitality extended to the traveller, drawn down by a shape that reads as one |

**The honest frame, stated once.** The worn shape is **not** a perfect copy, in
the way the mirror's echo of *you* is not a perfect you
([`the-mirror-creature.md`](./the-mirror-creature.md) §2.2 — "It is distorted,
not identical"). It is a form the field holds *close enough to be trusted* — a
feral-shaped run a herd will not scatter from, a member-shaped run the gate will
not immediately question, a guest-shaped run a campfire will welcome. The
*direction* is sourced: the three shapes are real forms the corpus already holds,
read off the ecology's organism-class vocabulary and the guest/member mechanics.
**[design]** The *composition* — how close the run must match the shape it wears,
how the field re-presents it — is this doc's dial, probe-calibrated against the
recognition rule (§5b). A mimic that reads *instantly* as "wrong" is not a
deceiver at all; a mimic that reads as a full, maintained member would be a
mirror-level identity challenge (§5b's honest upper bound).

### 2.3 Distinct from the commensal and the guest — drafted exactly

- **Not the commensal.** The commensal is a wild being *at home* in the order's
  surplus margin — it lives beside the maintained because the order is its
  habitat ([`the-commensal.md`](./the-commensal.md) §2/§4). The Mimic is not at
  home — **it wears the order's shape**, claims the trust of a place it did not
  earn, and cannot hold it (§3 this doc). The commensal's presence is a
  *steadiness read* (a settlement that hosts commensals is measurably steadier,
  §1 there); the Mimic's presence is a *trust draw* — it makes the settlement
  measurably *less safe* by exploiting the same warmth.
- **Not the guest.** A guest carries a *real* foreign provenance — an actual
  home-window history, a real carried ε², a real foreign cadence
  ([`window-guests.md`](./window-guests.md) §2). The guest's provenance is
  *readable and bookable* (§3 there). The Mimic's guest form is **borrowed** —
  the shape of a guest without a guest's history behind it, a walking-provenance
  that is *not theirs*. The guest's line on the ledger is a *visitor line with
  provenance*; the Mimic's is a **borrowed line the book has no record of
  holding** (§4 this doc).

## 3. The drift — the load-bearing honesty

The whole deception rests on one refusal, and the corpus already supplies the
refusal:

> **A mimic cannot hold a shape it does not maintain — its worn form drifts on
> the life-signal.**

[`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §1 states the discriminating axis:
**maintenance** — a live thing *actively holds* its φ-lock against a drain (it
moves, it feeds, its `(1−q)` glow pulses at the maintainer's cadence); a fossil
does not; a deliberate vent raises `ε²`; a scar is a fallen floor. §3.2 names the
honest failure of a *copy*: the deliberate-vent-vs-maintained-lock distinction
is read on the **`ε²`-gradient sign + the cadence's shape** — a maintainer's glow
is *bounded-pulsing* (feeding, settling, feeding), while a copied/venting pulse
*climbs* or *drifts*.

**The maintenance-read is the one thing the Mimic cannot fake — only borrow
briefly.** A Living thing's maintained `(1−q)` pulse is *fed* — there is a
maintainer behind it re-raising `ρ`, re-quantizing the body, re-holding the lock
against the drain. A Mimic *reproduces* the observable signature of maintenance
without *doing* the holding — exactly the mirror's trick
([`the-mirror-creature.md`](./the-mirror-creature.md) §3.2: "a Living thing
holds its lock against a drain; a mirror holds nothing"). But where the mirror
copies *your* pattern and dissolves under the drain, the Mimic copies *a worn
shape's* cadence and **drains from the inside**:

| The maintained-live read (`life-signal.md` §3.1) | A real holder | A Mimic's worn form |
|---|---|---|
| **the `(1−q)` cadence** | pulses *bounded* at the maintainer's rhythm — feeding, settling, feeding | **drifts** — the borrowed cadence is not being *re-fed*; it is a copy that slowly loses phase against the locality's rhythm (§3.2) |
| **the `ε²` gradient** | flat-low — `ε²` held below a floor by real lock-holding | **does not quite hold** — the borrow's own cost shows as a slowly rising/wavering `ε²` gradient the deeper the shape's hold must go |
| **the maintained-live read** | a genuine lock, off by nothing | **off by the borrow's own cost** — the borrow is a *spent* hold (§5d), and the `(1−q)`/`ε²` read drifts toward the `ε²`-elevation of a shape that is being held *by borrowing*, not by being alive |

**The borrow is spent, never free.** This is the load-bearing no-free-energy
consequence (§5d), and it is what makes the drift honest: the worn shape's hold
is a **real drain** — the Mimic spends coherence every moment it maintains a
form it does not own. It is, in the energy doc's terms, a *conversion of nothing*
([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §6 — `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`): a
Mimic that "held" a shape for free would be the one free-energy mint the corpus
forbids. The drift is the *cost of the borrow* — the mimic is draining, slowly,
whatever it borrowed, and the maintenance-read shows the drain.

> **The maintenance-read, stated once, load-bearing: you cannot fake it, only
> borrow it briefly.** The one thing the field's own classifier cannot be made
> to lie about is whether a configuration *holds itself*. A Living thing's
> maintained read is fed by an actual maintainer; a Mimic's is borrowed — and a
> borrowed maintenance cadence **drifts**. It does not decay like a fossil
> (nothing holds it at all) and it does not collapse like a mirror under a
> drain (the shape's hold is real while it lasts) — it **drifts toward wrong**:
> the cadence loses its lock, the `ε²` gradient climbs off the held floor by the
> borrow's own cost, and the maintained-live read becomes a *read the field
> cannot hold to*. The mimic is a **temporary deceptor**, and the field's own
> maintenance axis is why it cannot be a permanent one.

## 4. The provenance read — the honest counter, and why the Mimic makes a gatekeeper necessary

The drift gives the Mimic a *limited life*; the **provenance read** is what
