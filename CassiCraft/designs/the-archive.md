**Question under design:** the corpus designs memory everywhere as *curated*.
The Chronicle is the *shaped narrative* — which threads of the record get told,
composed as a story ([`the-chronicle.md`](./the-chronicle.md) §1/§3). The
Memory-Palace is the *chosen memory* — what a settlement deliberately keeps, in
a maintained built place ([`the-memory-palace.md`](./the-memory-palace.md)

at each place ([`the-stratum-read.md`](./the-stratum-read.md) §1/§2). None is
the **raw, complete record**: the settlement's full un-curated past — the
ledger's entire history, every point's stratum-trace, every named thing's full
life — the un-shaped totality the Chronicle shapes and the Palace chooses from.
This document is that design. **The Archive is the settlement's raw record,
held complete — the un-curated everything that the shaped and the chosen both

## 1. The Archive, stated once

The corpus designs memory everywhere as *curated*. [`the-chronicle.md`](./the-chronicle.md)
makes the record *narrative* — the settlement's story, the ops *told*
(`the-chronicle.md` §1: "the shared-ledger's book *told*"). [`the-memory-palace.md`](./the-memory-palace.md)
makes memory *chosen* — the kept things, the record read aloud, held in a
maintained built place ("the past-self library," `the-memory-palace.md` §1).
[`the-stratum-read.md`](./the-stratum-read.md) reads the *point*-records — the
field's kept residue at each place, organized by depth (`the-stratum-read.md`
§1). Every one is a *selection*: the Chronicle selects which threads to tell,
the Palace selects which memories to keep, the Stratum-Read selects which places
and depths to read. **None holds the whole** — the complete, un-curated past the
selections operate on: the ledger's entire op-stream, every point's
stratum-trace, every named thing's full life, all held raw, un-sorted into story
or choice.

> **The Archive, stated once.** The complete, un-curated record — the
> settlement's raw past in full: every op the ledger ever booked, every point's
> stratum-trace, every named thing's full life, all un-sorted into story or
> choice. It is the memory stack's **under-layer** — the distinct hold the
> curated layers stand on — and it closes the stack: **the shaped (the
> Chronicle), the chosen (the Palace), and the raw (the Archive).** The Chronicle
> *shapes* it (choosing which threads to tell, never adding events the raw
> doesn't hold); the Palace *selects* from it (keeping the chosen anchors,
> never minting them); **the Archive is the everything** — and its honest cost
> is the burden of the raw: an archive is heavy, and it cannot be fully false.

The one-line distinction from every sibling memory-layer — the Archive is the
one that is *neither* shaped *nor* chosen:

| Layer | It is | It does to the raw |
|---|---|---|
| **The Chronicle** (`the-chronicle.md`) | the shaped narrative — the record made story | **shapes** it — chooses which threads to tell, never adds events the raw doesn't hold (§5) |
| **The Memory-Palace** (`the-memory-palace.md`) | the chosen memory — the kept past made architecture | **selects** from it — keeps the chosen anchors, at a maintained cost (§4) |
| **The Stratum-Read** (`the-stratum-read.md`) | the point-read — the kept residue at each place | **reads** it at a place/depth — a slice, not the whole |
| **The Archive (this doc)** | the **raw** — the complete, un-curated record | **holds** it whole — no story, no choice, the everything kept |

The Archive is not a new memory-system; it is the **raw material** the existing
ones shape and choose from. Where the Palace *chose* a memory and the Chronicle
*shaped* a narrative, the Archive *holds what happened* — the ledger's full
history, the strata's full trace, the named lives' full record — with no
selection, no composition, no telling. It is the memory stack's under-layer: the
distinct hold that makes the shaped a *story about something* and the chosen a
*memory of something*, rather than a fiction and an ornament.

## 2. The everything — the honest mechanics

The Archive holds the full raw record. Three strands compose it, each grounded
in its source doc's real quantities; the Archive's *composition* — how the raw
is held, how it is read back — is this doc's [design] over them.

### 2.1 The ledger's entire op-stream — the book, held complete

`shared-ledger.md` §1.2 establishes the load-bearing fact: every channeled op in
the game lands on the same public, ordered, single-writer stream — the Q4
op-record `{member, op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}`
(`schema-that-settles.md` §2), batched through the player-return lane at the
sampler/world-writer cadence. That stream is "a **book of account** before any
Board exists" (`shared-ledger.md` §1.1): each record carries the op's *field*
meaning (condense/dissolve/heal/shield/steer/ignite, `coherence-magic.md` §2),
its cost, and its disposition. Per tide-cycle `[t_k, t_k+T]`, the Board
aggregates it per member into the net `C(M)` (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2) — the
*society's* read of the stream.

**The Archive holds the stream's whole, un-aggregated history.** Not the Board's
per-tide net `C(M)` — that is already a shaped read (a per-member sum over a
season). The Archive holds **every booked op, in order, for every member, over
every tide** — the full op-stream the Board *sums* and the Chronicle *tells*. The
single non-forgeable op-record's full history (`schema-that-settles.md` §3 — the
world-writer's single write is the only point a record is ever real from) is the
Archive's atomic strand: no op is dropped, re-ordered, or re-shaped. **[design]**
The Archive's *form* here — the unbounded, ordered, per-op hold over the whole
stream — is this document's composition over the landed record; the *records
held* are the schema's own.

### 2.2 Every point's stratum-trace — the strata, kept raw

`the-stratum-read.md` §1/§2 establishes the point-record: at every locality, the
field's kept residue reads as a layered re-read organized by the archaeology's
depth-strata — the deep-rung layer's pulled matter, the kept scar's wound-depth,
the re-bound's healed band, the strata's imprinted bands (`the-stratum-read.md`
§2.2; [`the-fallow.md`](./the-fallow.md) §2a; [`the-scar-lifecycle.md`](./the-scar-lifecycle.md)
§2.2). Each layer is a real, landed channel (a designed read over engine-real
deep-rung persistence, `field-archaeology.md` §2/§6(a)); the Stratum-Read
*presents* them layered at a standing position ("the time-camera,"
`the-stratum-read.md` §2.3/[design]).

**The Archive holds every point's stratum-trace raw.** Where the Stratum-Read is
the *instrument* that reads a locality's residue *in place* (a bounded, one-sample-
per-stratum read, `the-stratum-read.md` §2.1), the Archive is the *complete
record* of those traces across the whole settlement — not a present read at one
standing position, but the full, un-curated set of what each place's field state
kept, held as the raw the Stratum-Read's layered presentation composes. **[design]**
The Archive's *composition* here — the all-places, raw, un-layered hold of the
stratum-traces — is this doc's over the landed residue channels; the *traces
held* are the field's own kept state, read raw.

### 2.3 Every named thing's full life — the named anchors' residue as its record

`the-name.md` §1/§3.3 establishes the named anchor: to name a thing is to bind a
persistent-Π anchor to it (theory-ratified holding over a [design] binding,
`the-name.md` §4), and **a named locality's residue reads as *its* record, not
anonymous** — the deep-rung residue at a named site is that named thing's own
accumulated history ("a named place's strata read differently," `the-name.md`
§3.3). The named thing has a *full life* in the record: its anchoring, its
maintenance, its decay, its ruin's kept record, its contribution's legibility
(`the-name.md` §3).

**The Archive holds every named thing's full life raw.** Where the Palace's
*room of anchors* holds the named structures a settlement *chose* to keep
(`the-memory-palace.md` §2.1), the Archive holds the named things' complete
records — the anchored, the named-but-forgotten, the decayed, the un-chosen —
all of what a name's anchoring recorded, un-sorted into the kept and the
discarded. **[design]** The Archive's *composition* here — the all-named, full-life,
un-curated hold — is this doc's over the name's landed record; the *named lives
held* are the anchor's own persistence, read raw (with `the-name.md`'s
speculation flag carried on the *holding*, `reason-field.md` §6a).

### 2.4 The form — how the raw is held, how it is read back

**[design]** The Archive's *form* — the everything's shape — is this document's
composition over the three landed strands. It is a **hold, not a read**: an
un-curated, complete, ordered store of the ledger's op-stream, the point-strata,
and the named lives, kept whole. It is read back **raw** — un-shaped, un-chosen:
a querier reads "what the book actually booked," "what this place's field state
actually kept," "what this named thing's life actually was," never a composed
narrative, never a selected memory. The Archive's own idiom is the *opposite* of
the Chronicle's telling and the Palace's showing: it presents the record as
itself, complete, with the labor of reading — sorting, shaping, choosing — left
to the reader, exactly as the corpus's accessibility rule says the raw channels
are always readable, more laboriously, by hand (`the-language.md` §2; the
family rule, `field-instruments.md` §2.1, held as a *consumer* never a new
channel). The Archive holds neither a story nor a choice; it holds what happened,
and the reading is the reader's.

## 3. The burden of the raw

The load-bearing honesty: **an archive is heavy**. The full raw record is a
standing draw — held the way the memory-palace's chosen memory is held
(`the-memory-palace.md` §4), at un-selected scale. The palace keeps a
*chosen* past and pays a maintained cost for it ("a palace is a standing
maintenance op, never free, never a mint," `the-memory-palace.md` §4/§6d); the
Archive keeps the *everything* — every op, every place, every named life — and
so it pays the palace's cost **with no curation to bound it**. That is the
honest consequence:

> **The un-curated everything is the one record that cannot be cheap.** A story
> can be told once; a chosen memory can be kept small. The raw, complete record
> is a standing draw — a hold that must be maintained against the `(1−q)` waste
> of the field (`energy-harnessing.md` §2: the field's natural de-order, paid by
> every held structure, held like a conduit's or a bath's idle charge,
> `energy-harnessing.md` §4.1/§4.4) — exactly the palace's maintained hold
> (`the-memory-palace.md` §4) at the everything's scale. **A settlement holds a
> full Archive deliberately**, because holding the raw is a real, standing cost
> — and a settlement that lets its Archive decay loses what it chose not to pay
> to keep.

And the no-free-energy cap, held hard over the Archive (the corpus's single rule,
`energy-harnessing.md` §6, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`):

> **The Archive is a store, never a mint.** It holds the record; it converts
> nothing. Holding the op-stream, the strata, and the named lives produces no
> coherence, sheds no `(1−q)` that yields, writes nothing into the field, and
> grants the settlement no power, budget, or recovery it did not earn in the
> field (the same "holds, never mints" as the palace's §6d and the name's §6d).
> There is **no record that yields**: reading the Archive does not re-mint a
> booked op's coherence (that would double-spend it under the cap — the exact
> reason a replayed Chronicle never re-executes ops, `the-chronicle.md` §6d), does
> not re-precipitate a stratum's pulled matter (the fallow §2a — a worked deposit
> is gone forever), does not re-bind a decayed name's holding. **The Archive's
> holding cost is real; its output is information about what happened, costed as
> a read, never as energy.** There is no "build an archive to farm the raw" — an
> archive converts nothing, exactly why it is honest and heavy.

Three concrete guards, inherited:

1. **The hold is a standing draw.** The Archive is a maintained store, on the
   palace's maintained-cost model (`the-memory-palace.md` §4) at un-selected
   scale — a real, readable consequence of neglect, never a quietly-free gadget.
2. **An un-kept record decays.** What the field keeps is what is kept
   (`field-archaeology.md` §2 — the residue that survives is the fraction too
   deep-rung to dissolve). An Archive that is not held loses the raw the same
   way a statute residue re-dissolves when disturbed (the residue-persistence
   gate, `field-archaeology.md` §6b): the everything that is not maintained is
   the everything that is lost.
3. **The raw is never a currency.** The recorded past is information about what
   happened, never energy — the same no-free-energy frame that closes a chronicle
   farm (`the-chronicle.md` §6d) and a palace farm (`the-memory-palace.md` §6d)
   closes an archive farm.

## 4. The raw's truth

The load-bearing composition: **an archive cannot be fully false**. The corpus
lets a settlement shape a story (the Chronicle chooses which threads to tell,
`the-chronicle.md` §3/§5) and choose a memory (the Palace keeps the chosen
anchors, `the-memory-palace.md` §2); neither can *falsify the raw* the way either
selects from it.

> **You can shape a story (the Chronicles chooses which threads to tell);
> you can choose a memory (the Palace keeps the chosen anchors); but the raw
> record is what it is — and an archive cannot be fully false.** The op-stream is
> unforgeable because it is single-writer: every record is written at the
> world-writer's one non-forgeable point (`schema-that-settles.md` §3; the
> no-false-booking of `shared-ledger.md` §6d — no forged contributions, the book
> is exactly the domain's executed op record). The stratum-traces are the field's
> own kept state, read raw — the medium cannot lie the way a witness can
> (`the-dispute.md` §2.1). **A settlement that lets its Archive decay loses the
> very truth the Chronicle and Palace claim to keep**: the shaped narrative
> without its raw ground is a story with no field-true base (the Chronicle's
> honesty is that it "never adds events the record doesn't hold,"
> `the-chronicle.md` §5); the chosen memory without the raw is a selection with
> nothing it was chosen from.

The raw's truth is the corpus's *field-true* claim, made the record's ground:

| The shaped | The chosen | The raw |
|---|---|---|
| the Chronicle *tells* — selects which threads, composes them, but **never adds an event the record doesn't hold** (`the-chronicle.md` §5's honest boundary) | the Palace *keeps* — selects which memories to hold, but **cannot mint a memory it does not hold** (`the-memory-palace.md` §6d) | **the Archive *is what happened*** — the op-stream's no-false-booking (`shared-ledger.md` §6d), the strata's field-state (`the-stratum-read.md` §2), the named lives' record (`the-name.md` §3.3) |

**The Archive is the dispute's raw ground.** Where `the-dispute.md` §2.1
adjudicates by checking claims against the book, the present-state, and the
provenance ("the field cannot lie the way a witness can"), the Archive is what
that check is *made against*: a claim is field-true because the raw record holds
what was executed, held, and lived — never because a story told it or a memory
kept it. A dispute over the record is read to resolution against the Archive;
the Archive is the under-layer that makes the field's answer *true*, not merely
decided. A settlement that lets its Archive decay does not merely lose a store —
it loses the raw ground its own narrative, its own kept memory, and its own
judgment all claim to stand on.

## Open questions

1. **The Archive's "capacity" — the resolution of the everything's scale (§3).**
   The full raw hold is heavy, and a settlement's un-curated past grows without
   bound. Is the Archive's scale an *emergent* standing draw (the raw costs what
   the raw is; a long-settled window's Archive is simply heavier) or a designed
   bound (a [design] cap on how much raw a settlement can hold, with the older
   decay the way an un-kept residue does, `field-archaeology.md` §6b)? The
   palace's capacity question (`the-memory-palace.md` §7 open-Q3) and the
   chronicle's telling-density (`the-chronicle.md` §7 open-Q1) are the siblings;
   the difference between a *chosen* bound and an *honest weight* is this doc's
   sharpest dial. **[design]**
2. **The raw vs. the decayed — what "held" preserves and what it cannot (§3/§4).**
   The Archive holds the record that was *written* and the state the field
   *kept* — but the field's determinism-is-not-recoverability (`field-archaeology.md`
   §1.2; `the-stratum-read.md` §2.4) means the transient past is not under the
   ground. Does the Archive hold the raw *op-stream* (what was executed, stored)
   and the raw *strata* (what the field kept) while the *transient configuration*
   — what the field washed out — is gone even from the Archive, or does the
   Archive's complete claim extend to a designed retention of the transient? The
   honest default leans to "hold what was written and what was kept; the washed-out
   is not the Archive's either" — the Archive is the raw record, not a recovery
   of the unrecoverable. **[design]**
3. **The Archive's relation to the decay it documents (§3, `field-archaeology.md`
   §4.3).** When a window decays and a new settlement is founded on its strata
   (`field-archaeology.md` §4.3; `the-chronicle.md` §7 open-Q2's new-chronicle),
   does the old settlement's Archive survive as the raw under-layer of the
   new — the complete record of what the strata the new settlement sits on were —
   or does it decay with its window, leaving only the residue the field kept? The
   Archive's decay model and the hand-off to the next settlement's ground are the
   same choice as the palace's across-decay (`the-memory-palace.md` §7 open-Q4).
   **[design]**
4. **The Archive's standing-draw calibration (§3).** How heavy is "heavy,"
   concretely — the standing draw of the full raw hold sized against the canonical
   ≈ 1–6 ms/tick server budget and the ≈ 2,000-entity / ≈ 6 MiB publish
   (`corpus-reconciliation.md`), on the palace's maintained-cost model
   (`the-memory-palace.md` §4) at what multiple? The *shape* is this doc's honest
   claim (a real, readable, maintained cost); the *size* is a Phase-1.5
   measurement. **[probe]**
5. **The Archive's read-back form (§2.4).** How a querier reads the raw "back" —
   the raw op-stream's un-shaped presentation, the stratum-traces' un-layered
   read, the named lives' un-curated record — and how that presentation stays
   honest to the *raw* (never drifting into the Chronicle's composed form or the
   Palace's chosen presentation) is this doc's [design] surface, probe-calibrated
   against the accessibility rule's "readable, more laboriously, by hand".
   **[design]**
