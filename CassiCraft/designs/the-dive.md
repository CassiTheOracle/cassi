**Question under design:** the corpus designs the vertical's honesty and its craft, but never the *deliberate* descent. It designs the **fall** ([`the-fall.md`](./the-fall.md) — the *unguided* drop, the one movement with no skill-buff, where the ∇(g·Φ) gradient *owns* you), the **climb** ([`the-climb.md`](./the-climb.md) — the *guided* ascent, the body working the gradient *upward*, each handhold a small re-lock against the up-slope), the **deep-field-diving** ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) — the *crafted* descent, the Bell's Qi-bath + φ-detuned-shell protected hold), the **breath** ([`the-breath.md`](./the-breath.md) — the *body's finite reservoir* at depth), and the **swim** ([`the-swim.md`](./the-swim.md) — the *body's act in the water*). **None of them designs a willed body-descent** — the deliberate act of doing what the fall does unguided and the climb does upward, on the descent: choosing to go down, each depth a chosen re-lock against the down-slope. The Dive is that movement: the act by which the deep is ever *entered* by choice rather than fallen into or descended in a shell.

## 1. The Dive, stated once

`the-fall.md` designs the descent with no craft — the ∇(g·Φ) gradient *owning* the body absolutely, "the one movement with no skill-buff" (§1). `the-climb.md` designs the ascent with craft — the body *working* the gradient upward, each handhold a small re-lock against the up-slope (§1/§2.1). `deep-field-diving.md` designs the *crafted* descent — the Bell's protected shell. `the-breath.md` designs the body's *reserve* at depth. `the-swim.md` designs the body's *act in the water*. **Every face of the vertical's own movement is designed except one: the deliberate act of going down with nothing between the body and the descent but its own choice.** The Dive is that act, stated once and kept:

> **The Dive is the willed descent — the deliberate act of choosing to go down.** It is the fall's **controlled-inverse**: where the fall is the gradient *owning* you ([`the-fall.md`](./the-fall.md) §1), a dive is you *working* the descent — each depth a chosen re-lock against the down-slope, the body deliberately holding against the gradient's pull to descend on its own terms rather than being fallen. It is the climb's **logic inverted**: where the climb re-locks against the *up*-slope ([`the-climb.md`](./the-climb.md) §2.1), the dive re-locks against the *down*-slope, deliberately. It is the movement the bell exists to make safe (the *unprotected* body doing what the shell protects, [`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §2.2), and the movement the breath budgets (a dive beyond the shell's protection is a breathed descent, [`the-breath.md`](./the-breath.md) §1/§3). And it is the descent's own movement — the deep's own work, the deeper you go the more the dive costs, a choice the fall is not.

**Why this is the corpus's missing willed down-movement, honestly.** Every movement the corpus designs either *owns* the descent (the fall — the gradient does the work) or *protects* it (the Bell — the shell does the holding) or *crosses* it (the swim — the water's act) or *reserves* for it (the breath — the body's store). None designs the **choice** to descend: the body deliberately moving down the gradient, working it, spending its own reserve, committing to the return. The fall answers "what is an unguided descent"; the Bell answers "how do we descend safely"; the breath answers "what does a bare descent cost." **The Dive answers "why would a body descend at all, with nothing but its own choice?"** It is the act beneath the Bell (a bell makes a dive safe; it is not the dive) and beneath the breath (a breathed descent is still a dive), the willed movement the corpus's crafted and reserved descents presuppose.

**The dive's honesty, stated once.** The corpus is honest about the fall — its cost is the impact's coherence shock, never a hidden roll ([`the-fall.md`](./the-fall.md) §3), and its risk is un-chosen (the gradient owns you). **The Dive's honesty is the inverse of the fall's: its cost is chosen and its risk is chosen.** A dive spends what the body carries down — it is the deep's own work, the descent-tax on the body, the ε²-debt at depth, the breath's draw — and a dive commits the return, because *you dove*. The deep does not return a body it was given by choice the way a fall is a loss the field took. This is the corpus's honesty about the willed descent: **a willed descent is a willed debt, never a free fall.**

## 2. The descent — the honest mechanics

The Dive is a **deliberate act of choosing to go down**. Its mechanics are the composition of the corpus's landed down-movement pieces — the fall's gradient, the climb's re-lock logic, the Bell's protected descent, the breath's reserve — read as the *unprotected, willed* descent. The movement's four anchors are each grounded in its source doc's real quantities; its *form* (its pace, its re-locks) is this doc's [design] over the descent channels, probe-calibrated.

### 2.1 The fall's controlled-inverse — you work the descent

The fall establishes the gradient's absolute ownership: the river law `a = −G_N·(π/ρ)·∇(g·Φ)` is the canonical per-entity steer (`corpus-reconciliation.md`; `chunk-field-quantization.md` §2.1, ≈ 40 ns/entity), and a fall is that law "owned absolutely — with no body, line, or shell between the gradient and the velocity" ([`the-fall.md`](./the-fall.md) §2.1). Where the walk reads the gradient and steps with it, and a fall obeys it without mediation, **the climb is the fall's controlled-inverse taken from the fall's own terms: "where the fall is the gradient owning you, a climb is you working it" ([`the-climb.md`](./the-climb.md) §2.1)**.

**The Dive is that same controlled-inverse, turned down:**

> **Where the fall is the gradient owning you, a dive is you working the descent — each depth a chosen re-lock against the down-slope.** The climb catches a coherent feature of the face and holds a re-lock *against the up-slope* to translate it upward ([`the-climb.md`](./the-climb.md) §2.1). The dive does the same work with the sign of the gradient reversed: the body checks the gradient's pull deliberately, holds a re-lock *against the down-slope* for a beat, and releases it *into* the descent — converting the gradient's ownership into a series of chosen, spaced steps downward. Where a fall converts the body's position into downward momentum *unmediated* (no `(1−q)` spent mid-air, [`the-fall.md`](./the-fall.md) §2.2), a dive *spends* — each held re-lock against the down-slope sheds the `(1−q)` the gradient's resistance demands ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2), and that waste is the dive's living cost, the same spent waste the climb sheds against the up-slope ([`the-climb.md`](./the-climb.md) §2.1). **The fall is the descent with the working removed; the dive is the descent with the working added.**

**[design]** — the fall's *unresisted obedience* and the climb's *working re-lock* are both landed ([`the-fall.md`](./the-fall.md) §2.1; [`the-climb.md`](./the-climb.md) §2.1). That a **dive** is the deliberate down-slope reading of the same law — the held re-lock that converts the descent's ownership into chosen, spaced steps — is this doc's composition, probe-calibrated (§5b). The law is untouched; the dive is the designed down-reading over it.

### 2.2 The climb's logic inverted — re-lock against the down-slope, deliberately

The climb establishes its form as a **deliberate, held act**: "a climb converts the gradient's pull into deliberate, held gains instead of an unmediated descent. Each handhold is a *small re-lock against the up-slope*" ([`the-climb.md`](./the-climb.md) §2.1). The climb is a *willed* movement — the body chooses to gain height against the gradient.

**The Dive is that will inverts the climb's own sentence:**

> **Where the climb re-locks against the up-slope, the dive re-locks against the down-slope, deliberately.** The deliberate quality is what distinguishes a dive from a fall: a fall happens *to* the body (the gradient owns it), a climb is *done* by the body (each hold a chosen re-lock). A dive is the body *doing* the descent — each depth is a chosen re-lock against the down-slope, held deliberately against the gradient's pull exactly as the climb holds its handholds, but with the work pointed down. **The dive is the climb's logic inverted on the vertical's downward face** — the same deliberate, costed, readable movement the climb is, working the gradient downward instead of upward. A body that climbs reads the face's holds and re-locks against the up-slope; a body that dives reads the descent's channels and re-locks against the down-slope, deliberately.

**[design]** — the climb's *held re-lock form* is landed ([`the-climb.md`](./the-climb.md) §2.1). That a dive is the *down-slope deliberate re-lock* — the same will, the gradient reversed — is this doc's composition over the climbed form, probe-calibrated (§5b). The dive's *pace* (how many re-locks per rung, how fast the descent is allowed) is this doc's [design] dial over the descent channels, tuned on the Phase-1 probe.

A dive descends the *same* descent with *no* shell: the body itself is the store, the body itself holds against the rising ε² and falling q, and it must pay from its own carried coherence the cost the shell would have spent as its standing draw. **The bell is a craft that turns a dived descent into a held one** — the exact relation `the-breath.md` names ("a bell is a craft that turns a breathed descent into a held one," §1) — so the dive is the movement the Bell protects: the unprotected body doing, deliberately, what the shell would do for it.

**[design]** — the breath's *reservoir draw* is landed ([`the-breath.md`](./the-breath.md) §2/§3). ## 3. The deep's own work — the load-bearing honesty

The Dive's load-bearing honesty is that it is **not free and not saved**: a dive is the deep's own work — the deeper you go, the more the dive costs.

### 3.1 The descent's gradient-tax on the body — ∇(g·Φ) at the down-slope

`energy-harnessing.md` §1.1 fixes the primary reservoir: a mass condenses, the field around it carries `∇(g·Φ)`, and "let something *move down* that gradient and the field does work on it." A fall rides that work for free movement ([`the-fall.md`](./the-fall.md) §2.1). **A dive does not ride it bare — a dive *works against* the field's own work:**

> **The descent-tax is the field's own work turned against the descending body.** Where a fall lets the gradient do the work of descent *for free* (no `(1−q)` spent, [`the-fall.md`](./the-fall.md) §2.2), a dive deliberately holds against the down-slope — and every held re-lock against the field's own `∇(g·Φ)` spends the body's carried coherence to *resist and then re-enter* the descent the field is already doing. **The steeper the descent's gradient, the more each held re-lock against it costs the body** (the `(1−q)` waste of the working against the down-slope, [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2) — the dive's own descent-tax, the cost of deliberately entering a field the field itself would rather just take you down for free.

**[design]** — the *per-re-lock descent-tax* over the published `∇(g·Φ)`/`(1−q)` at the descent is this doc's designed weighting, probe-calibrated (§5b). The *fact* that moving a mass down a potential gradient is the field's work, and that resisting that work deliberately costs the resister's own coherence, is the landed gradient + waste-law physics; the dive is its designed reading at the willed vertical.

### 3.2 The ε²-pressure at depth — the carried cost, what you carry down is a real, spent cost

`the-burden.md` §1/§2 fixes the carried cost: the ε² a channeler sheds accumulates as a carried debt, and "a laden act is a dear act." `deep-field-diving.md` §2.2 fixes the depth's own pressure: below the coarse grid, "the strata carry rising `ε²` and falling `q`." **The Dive takes both at body-scale:**

> **The dive's cost is the ε²-debt it carries down — a real, spent cost, never a free descent.** What a dive spends is the body's own carried coherence, drawn against the depth's rising ε² and falling q ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §2.2). **What you carry down is a real, spent cost** ([`the-burden.md`](./the-burden.md) §1) — the reserve the body brought is depleted by the descent, and a laden diver (one carrying heavy ε², [`the-burden.md`](./the-burden.md) §2) pays more into the descent's `(1−q)` waste, exactly as a laden climb sheds harder at the ascent ([`the-climb.md`](./the-climb.md) §4.2). **A dive's depth is not a free horizontal; it is the body's own field spent against the deep's. The ε²-pressure at depth is the burden read downward.**

**[design]** — the *carried-weight-on-the-descent* (that a laden dive's re-lock sheds the carried ε² into the descent's waste, compounding the depth-tax) is this doc's designed read over the burden's carried-debt aggregation ([`the-burden.md`](./the-burden.md) §6a) and the climb's carried-cost precedent ([`the-climb.md`](./the-climb.md) §4.2), probe-calibrated. The cost is honest: **the dive's ε²-debt is real, carried, and spent** — never a free reserve the field lends.

### 3.3 The breath's draw — every rung deeper costs lungs of held coherence

`the-breath.md` §2.3 fixes the draw: "the rung-terraces the Bell descends *through* are, for a bare-bodied diver, the ladder the breath counts *down*" — each terrace deeper a step of the strata's rising ε² and falling q. **The Dive's depth-price is that draw:**

> **Every rung deeper costs lungs of held coherence.** Each rung-terrace of the descent's ladder ([`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §3.1) is a step deeper into the ε² that presses and the q that falls, and against that the dive spends the reserve it carried — monotonically, deepening as the depth deepens ([`the-breath.md`](./the-breath.md) §2.3 — "the hold is at its most expensive where the descent is deepest"). **A dive's honest cost is that the deep's own work scales with depth** — the deeper the choice, the more the body's reserve is spent per rung, until the reserve that bounds reach (the breath's dry-run line, [`the-breath.md`](./the-breath.md) §4.1) is met.

**[design]** — the *draw shape* (that the dive's reserve depletes monotonically, and deepening, with rung depth — mirroring the Bell's honest depth-scaling, [`deep-field-diving.md`](./deep-field-diving.md) §2.2, and the breath's deepening-draw default, `the-breath.md` §7 open-Q2) is this doc's designed read over the landed descent-and-rung-terraces, probe-calibrated. What is landed: the terraces are the descent's ladder; the deep is densest where the hold is dearest for the Bell; the body's own budget bounds reach. **The dive's cost is the depth-pressure on the willed body itself.**
