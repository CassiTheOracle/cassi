## 2. The three waters

### (a) The river as a moving-conduit band — ride the gradient instead of walking it

**The landed piece:** [`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §4.1 defines the **conduit** — *"a manufactured high-`q` filament that raises `ξ·q` along a path, making that path the field's preferred channel for routing coherence."* [`coherence-highway.md`](./coherence-highway.md) §1.1 raises that conduit idiom to **infrastructure scale** — a maintained high-`q` line across the land that a cart rides along `∇(g·Φ)` for free. [`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2a establishes the gradient at foot-pace: walking a ridge downhill along `∇(g·Φ)` is the field doing the work of moving you, `a = −G_N·(π/ρ)·∇(g·Φ)` (≈ 40 ns/entity, `corpus-reconciliation.md`).

**The [design] over them — a river is that conduit at *nature's* scale, carrying flow instead of only coherence:**

> **A river is a moving-conduit band — a high-`q` filament carrying *flow* the way energy's conduits carry *coherence*, at the scale the field maintains itself rather than a player.** Where the road's conduit is *manufactured* (a player holds the line's `q` up, `coherence-highway.md` §1.1), a river's conduit is *grown*: the liquid regime's own low `ξ`, low `ω₀²` identity ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §6) makes the field hold a coherent, flowing filament of its own — a **corridor the field itself maintains**, the highway's §1.1 conduit where the field is the roadman.

The load-bearing move is the **fluid-drift**: a river is the walk's `∇(g·Φ)` descent made *fluid*:

> **You ride a river's gradient instead of walking it.** The walker steps with the lean and the field does the work ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2a); a river *is* the lean made a medium — the player stops footing the descent and lets the filament's own flow carry them along the gradient, **at river-pace, the way a cart rides the road's haul for free** ([`coherence-highway.md`](./coherence-highway.md) §1.1) but without the road's held-line cost. Where the walk *trades speed for freedom* ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §1.2), the river *trades control for flow*: the field carries you, but it carries you *its* way, down the gradient it chose — you ride a corridor you did not build and cannot redirect.

**[design]** — the *flow-as-conduit* (that the liquid regime holds a flowing, coherent filament the way a manufactured conduit does) and the *fluid-drift ride* (that a body on a river is carried like a cart on a road) are this doc's designed surfaces over the conduit idiom and the `∇(g·Φ)` haul, **probe-calibrated** — the same tier as the highway's ride-downhill slice. **A river is a corridor the field itself maintains** (the conduit idiom at nature's scale); it is never a player-built line.

### (b) The sea as a flat coherence plain — the patient crossing where the instruments read less

**The landed pieces:** the **liquid regime** is `ρ ≥ θ_c` with `ε²` above the solid floor ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §2 — dense yet decohered, flows); the **timing** of a patient act is the Hourglass's duration read ([`the-hourglass.md`](./the-hourglass.md) — how long a moment takes at the local tempo); the **loan** a patient crossing spends is `patient-field.md` §3's patience ([`the-walk.md`](./the-walk.md) §2b — crossing thin spends more `(1−q)` per stride's patience).

**The [design] over them — a sea is a flat coherence plain, the honest middle:**

> **A sea is a flat coherence plain — a uniform low-drain region.** Across a body of water the liquid regime holds a *flat* `q` (it cannot pile into ridges — the river law's `∇(g·Φ)` + the fluid's leveling minimizer, [`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §2's "leveling is the emergent minimizer of the ε² budget", flatten to a uniform surface) and a *low* `ε²` drain (water's tensile ~0, low rest-q — it does not scar the way dry land does). **Crossing it is a patient act** — the Hourglass's duration, [`the-hourglass.md`](./the-hourglass.md) — with **no desert's waste** (the `(1−q)` floor is *low* — low drain means cheap movement, not the desert's wasteful `q → 1e-3…1e-1`) and **no ridge's cheapness** (the uniform `q` hides the gradient that makes a walk or a road cheap — there is no step with the lean on a flat plain).

**The instruments read less — the honest consequence of a uniform surface:**

> **A uniform `q` hides the ridges — the fast-reader's instruments behave differently over water.** The Weatherglass's lume reads a flat, consistent brightness (uniform `q` — high, calm, no climbing glow because `ε²` is low); the **Lantern** ([`the-lantern.md`](./the-lantern.md) §2) has no ridge to brighten over and no thin to dim against — its ground-glow lies *flat and even*, the safe path's light with nowhere to go; the **Mirror** ([`the-mirror.md`](./the-mirror.md) §2) reads you *against* the plain: on a uniform carrying surface your own maintenance is the only structure — a burdened player over a sea glows in a way the flat field does not. **The instruments read the same channels, but the plain's uniformity makes *you* the read**, not the terrain.

**[design]** — the *flat-plain crossing* (a uniform low-drain region crossed as a duration act) and the *instruments-read-less* consequence (the Lantern's flat glow, the Mirror's you-against-the-plain) are this doc's designed surfaces over the liquid regime's leveling and the instruments' real channel reads, probe-calibrated. **[probe]** — whether the liquid regime actually holds a *uniform* enough `q` across a body of water to read as a plain (vs regional `ε²` variation) is a Phase-1 measurement over the published channels (§5b).

### (c) The water cycle as envelope-waves made matter — a storm is a wave, a flood is the wave made water

**The landed pieces:** [`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md) §1.3 — the envelope is a *wave-bearing medium*; the two-fluid waves `∂²EY/∂t² = c²∇²EY − ω₀²(EY−φ·EI)` (and EI mirrored) *are* the weather; **wind = `FieldVel`**, the medium's own velocity written per cell. [`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §3 — the precipitation law: *ore precipitates where q accumulates above a band*; the regime condenses where q rises. [`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §2/§3 — the surfeit (regional `q` held above the enriching band), the shedding through the `(1−q)` waste.

**The [design] over them — the water cycle is the envelope's waves precipitating into the liquid regime:**

> **A storm is a wave; a flood is the wave made water.** The atmosphere's envelope waves ([`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md) §1.3) do not stop at the sky's floor — where a coherent wave band crosses into a region whose `q` accumulates above the liquid regime's own condensation line, it **precipitates into water**: matter condensing out of the wave the way ore condenses out of a q-band ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §3), now as the liquid regime. **The water cycle is that precipitation made a cycle**: evaporation is the liquid regime's `ε²` re-radiating up into the envelope as wave (the wave the atmosphere already carries); rain is a wave condensing back down; a **storm** is the wave *while it is still a wave* (a coherence pulse traveling at `c_s = h₀/dt` through the envelope/air, [`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md) §1.3); a **flood** is the wave that has crossed into water *and overflowed its medium* — the surfeit of [`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §2, the regional `q` held above the liquid regime's own enriching band, making the sea itself overflow.

**The flood's relationship, inherited and stated once:** the flood is **not a separate regime**; it is **the sea's overflow** —

> **The sea is the flood's medium; the flood is the sea's overflow.** Every flood-feature of [`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md) §2 is the liquid regime pushed past its band: over-precipitation then re-dissolve is water over-condensing then re-shedding; the deafening resonance is the saturated liquid surface; the shedding (§3, the `(1−q)` waste) is how a flood recedes. **The design does not build a second water; it reads the flood as the sea's own abundance-extreme** — the same water, the same `q`-accumulation, read over the long-thick horizon exactly as the cold reads the thin. The flood doc's gates (§6b) are the sea's gates for its overflow; the sea adds no overflow of its own.

**[design]** — the *precipitation into water* (that a coherent wave condenses into the liquid regime the way ore condenses by q accumulation) and the *cycle as precipitation* (evaporation-to-rain as the wave's round-trip) are this doc's designed reading of material §3's precipitation law applied to the water regime, probe-calibrated. The **flood is inherited** — the sea is its medium, the flood is the overflow, unchanged from [`the-flood.md`](./the-flood.md).
