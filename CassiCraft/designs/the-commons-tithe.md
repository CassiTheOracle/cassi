# The Commons-Tithe: The Settlement's Collective Contribution, Made Honest — How a Window Funds Its Shared Order

**Question under design:** the corpus designs the shared order's *mechanics* — the
Market books the exchange (`the-market.md`), the Gift refuses the tally
(`the-gift.md`), the Loan advances the forward order, the Ledger's Coherence Board
renders who contributed (`shared-ledger.md` §2) — but **nothing funds the commons**:
the maintained shared things — the house's bath (`house-that-steers.md` §2.2), the
Observatory's lamps (`the-observatory.md` §1), the School's upkeep
(`the-school.md` §2.2), the harbor's lamp (`the-harbor.md` §2b) — have designed
mechanics but **no designed income**. The corpus tells a settlement what the
commons *is*; nothing tells it how the commons is *paid for*. This document is
that missing face: **the Commons-Tithe, the settlement's collective contribution
made honest** — the periodic, bookable, everyone-pays contribution that funds
the shared order: a small, regular `C(M)` contribution (a fixed share of each
member's yield, booked on the ledger the way any op is) whose *use* is the
commons' own. **The tithe is the superadditive commons' financial face.**

The load-bearing fact, stated once:

> **The shared order has a shared cost — and the tithe is that cost made
> bookable.** A settlement's shared things (the bath held by the houses, the
> Observatory's lamps, the School's lessons, the harbor's lamp) are *maintained*,
> never free: each is a held structure whose coherence the settlement keeps up or
> loses (`house-that-steers.md` §3 — an unmaintained hold collapses toward the
> attractor). The Commons-Tithe is the periodic, bookable contribution that pays
> that maintenance: a fixed share of each member's yield, booked on the ledger the
> way any op is (the settled Q4 op-record's shape, `schema-that-settles.md` §2.1),
> whose *use* is the commons' own — the shared things' shared cost made legible.
> **Voluntary but expected, never forced and never free** — a member can decline,
> and the decline is honestly read; a member who tithes reads as a contributor
> (the census's active class); and the tithe is never free — the contribution is
> real, never a mint, the no-free-energy cap held. **The tithe is the commons'
> price, and the commons is worth it** — the superadditive gain
> (`field-npc-ai.md` §3) gives back more than the tithe costs, and a settlement
> that under-tithes reads a thinning commons honestly (the window-pulse's fall).

## 1. The Commons-Tithe, stated once

The corpus's shared order is *designed to be maintained* — the house that holds
the room's `q`-core (`house-that-steers.md` §1.1), the Observatory that reads the
window as one body (`the-observatory.md` §1), the School that passes the craft to
the next run (`the-school.md` §1), the harbor's lamp that lights the departure
(`the-harbor.md` §2b). But the corpus has never asked **what pays for that
maintenance**. The Market books the exchange of things (`the-market.md`), the
Gift refuses the tally (`the-gift.md`), the Ledger renders who contributed
(`shared-ledger.md` §2) — but none of them is an *income*: none of them is a
recurring, everyone-pays contribution whose whole purpose is to fund the shared
things' shared cost. The shared order has a shared cost, and that cost has no
booked payer.

The Commons-Tithe is that payer:

> **The Commons-Tithe, stated once.** The settlement's collective contribution
> made honest — the **periodic, bookable, everyone-pays contribution that funds
> the shared order**: a small, regular `C(M)` contribution (a fixed share of each
> member's yield, booked on the ledger the way any op is) whose *use* is the
> commons' own — the bath's maintenance, the Observatory's lamps, the School's
> lessons, the harbor's lamp. It is the **superadditive commons' financial face**:
> the superadditive gain (`field-npc-ai.md` §3) — the shared high-`q` core that
> raises every member's efficiency — is real, and the tithe is the bookable share
> that *funds* the maintained structures the gain rides on. **Voluntary but
> expected, never forced and never free.**

**The commons is superadditive; the tithe is its price.** `field-npc-ai.md` §3.1:
a village is a Qi-bath commons — the shared high-`q` core raises each member's
budget recovery and cuts each member's `(1−q)` waste, so **a village's
intelligence exceeds its members'** (the AM-GM evenness theorem, coherence-commons
§7.2: a commons sharing coherence evenly is smarter than the same coherence
concentrated in a few). That gain is real and it is *maintained*, never free
(`field-npc-ai.md` §3.3 — the commons can die; `house-that-steers.md` §2.2 — the
bath is "brittle if it goes down"). **The tithe is that superadditive gain's
funded cost** — the small, regular, everyone-pays share that keeps the structures
holding the commons up. Where the corpus's other society-faces read or book the
commons, the tithe *pays for it* — the one act whose whole meaning is the shared
order's income.

**The one-line distinction from every sibling act:**

| Act | It does | Its relation to the book |
|---|---|---|
| **The Market** (`the-market.md` §2) | books an exchange of things | a booked one-off op, re-allocating existing value between members |
| **The Gift** (`the-gift.md` §2.1) | gives an un-booked transfer | the book's deliberate silence — no `C(M)` reads giver or receiver |
| **The Loan** | advances the forward order | a booked forward op with interest |
| **The Coherence Board** (`shared-ledger.md` §2) | renders who contributed | the *read* of the book, per member, per tide |
| **The Commons-Tithe (this doc)** | **funds the shared order** | a **periodic, booked `C(M)` contribution** whose use is the commons' own — the book's giving, the commons' income |

The tithe is the corpus's **commons-funding doc** because it is the one place the
book's contribution stream is pointed *at the shared things themselves* — not at
an exchange, not at a person, but at the maintained structures the superadditive
commons rides on. It is the settlement's collective contribution made honest:
regular, bookable, everyone-pays, and legible on the same ledger the settlement
already reads.

## 2. The funded commons — the honest mechanics

The tithe is a small, regular `C(M)` contribution. Its *mechanics* compose three
landed pieces — the booked op-record's shape, the per-member `C(M)` read, and the
superadditive commons' maintained cost — into a recurring, bookable contribution.
The composition — the share's size, the period, the booking — is this doc's
[design]; the pieces it rides are landed.

### 2.1 The tithe's form — a fixed share of each member's yield, booked the way any op is

**What it is.** A tithe is a **small, regular `C(M)` contribution**: each member,
per tide-cycle, books a fixed share of their tide's yield onto the settlement's
book, as a Q4 op-record of the settled shape. Its *form* is this doc's [design],
over the settled op-record (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1) and the per-member
`C(M)` read (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2):

| The tithe's form ([design]) | What it is | Grounded on |
|---|---|---|
| **The share** | a **fixed share of each member's yield** — a designed fraction of the member's booked contribution over the tide (a small, honest cut of the `C(M)` they actually produced), never a fixed sum the member must earn out of nothing | `shared-ledger.md` §1.2 (the per-member net `C(M)` per tide — the yield the share is a fraction of); the no-free-energy cap — the share can only be a fraction of what the member actually produced, never more |
| **The period** | **per tide** — the same accounting cycle the Board and the census read on (`[t_k, t_k+T]`, `tide-of-the-attractor.md`); the tithe is a tide's-booked share, never a per-op tax, so a lively-margin member and a lean one both tithe to the book | `shared-ledger.md` §5 (the tide as the accounting cycle); `the-census.md` §2.1 (the same `[t_k, t_k+T]` window) |
| **The booking** | the contribution is **booked as a Q4 op-record of the settled shape** — `{member, op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}`, where the tithe writes a contribution line the Board's `C(M)` reads, exactly the way a maintained heal or a house's construction books (`house-that-steers.md` §4) | `schema-that-settles.md` §2.1 (the settled record); `shared-ledger.md` §1.2; the `sustain` flag (§2.2 there) for a standing tithe |

**The tithe's bookable shape, stated once.** A tithe is *not* a new record kind —
it is the same booked op the settlement already writes, pointed at the commons:
a `sustain`-flagged, periodic contribution line on each member's `member` line,
whose `magnitude` is the fixed share of their tide's yield, whose `worldPos`
names the shared structure it funds (the bath cell, the Observatory, the School,
the harbor), and whose `rung` is the funded structure's held depth. **The tithe
books the way any op does** — `the-market.md` §2.1's settled-record honesty
applied to a *recurring* contribution instead of a one-off exchange. It is the
market's regular sibling: the market books the one-off exchange, the tithe books
the periodic contribution.

**The [design] dials.** The share's size (what fraction of a tide's yield counts
as the tithe) is a **[design]/[probe]** dial against the bath's real `q`-maintenance
cost (below, §2.2) — set so a healthy member's tithe funds the commons without
starving their own production, and an open question (§7). The period (per-tide)
and the booking (as a `sustain`-flagged op) are [design] over the settled record.
What is *load-bearing and not design*: that the tithe is a **booked `C(M)`
contribution** (the shared order's income), that it is **periodic** (a tide's
recurring share, not a one-off), and that it is **everyone-pays** (a share of
*yield*, so a member cannot be "exempt" from contributing — though a member can
*decline*, §3).

## 3. The voluntary-but-expected honesty — the load-bearing honesty

This is the design's sharpest honesty, and it is the corpus's own, inherited from
the exile's read (§4) and the census's active class (§2.1):

> **The tithe is voluntary but expected, never forced and never free.** A member
> *can* decline — and the decline is honestly read, never punished, never hidden:
> the way the exile's book shows a declining member's line honestly
> (`the-exile.md` §4), the tithe's book shows a declining member's unpaid share
> honestly. **Never forced** — there is no debt, no garnishment, no difficulty
> meter that rises for a non-tither; the common's lever is the bath itself, never
> a rule. **Never free** — for the member who tithes, the contribution is real
> (a booked share of their actual yield, never a mint); and for the commons, the
> funded upkeep is a real cost the tithe pays — a commons that under-tithes reads
> honestly as a thinning commons (§4).

**Never forced.** The tithe is not a tax the book enforces. A member who withholds
books an *unpaid* share, and the book shows it the way it shows any absence:
**honestly, never as a penalty.** `the-exile.md` §4's principle — a declining
member's read shows the decline, never a hidden punishment, never a difficulty
meter — is inherited verbatim. There is no "tithe debt," no "contribution
reputation score," no rule that raises a non-tither's cost over time. The
settlement's only lever on a non-tither is the bath-centered non-coercive lever
of `shared-ledger.md` §3.2 — *stop feeding the bath they drain, and the Board
shows the consequence* — never a fiat garnishment. A member who declines reads as
a **dormant or fading line** (the census's own classes, `the-census.md` §2.1/§2.2),
never as a criminal.

**But expected.** The tithe is a *social norm made legible*, not a law. Because
it is the shared order's funded cost, a settlement *expects* each member's share —
and the book makes that expectation readable: on the Board, a tithing member reads
as a **contributor, a member of the active class** (`the-census.md` §2.1 — "the
active, members who booked a contribution this tide"), while a declining member's
line reads as the absent share it is. The expectation is not a rule enforced but
a **truth made public**: the commons is worth it (§4), and the book shows who
funds it and who does not — legibly, honestly, never a punishment. This is
exactly the exile's register (`the-exile.md` §4 — legible, never hidden, the cost
written where the settlement already reads), applied to the *mild* end of the
member-line: not exclusion, but honest visibility.

**Never free — a contributor's tithe is real.** For the member who tithes, the
contribution is **real** — a booked share of their *actual* yield, never a mint,
the no-free-energy cap held (§5d). The tithe cannot be a "free contribution"
that a member books without having produced the yield it is a share of: the
single-writer lane (`schema-that-settles.md` §3) means a tithe's `magnitude` is a
deterministic share of the member's real booked `C(M)`, forged by the same no-
false-booking that makes the market's booked exchange honest (`the-market.md`
§2.3). A member cannot tithe more than their yield produced, and a member cannot
*claim* a tithe without the share having been real. **The tithe is the book's
giving** — the commons' own income, honest and legible — precisely because it is
booked, in the settled record's shape, with the same single-writer honesty that
no fake contribution can enter the ledger.

## 4. The commons' price — and the honest frame

The tithe's load-bearing *composition* — the reason the whole mechanism is honest
and not a tax dressed as piety — is that **the commons is worth its price**:

> **The tithe is the commons' price, and the commons is worth it.** The
> superadditive gain (`field-npc-ai.md` §3) gives back **more than the tithe
> costs**: the bath's steadiness (the raised recovery, the cut `(1−q)` waste,
> the deeper locks every member can hold), the Observatory's read (the window as
> one body, the early warning), the School's competence (the passed craft, the
> settlement's own future, honestly booked), the harbor's door (the kept
> departure, the lamp that lights it). **A settlement that under-tithes reads a
> thinning commons honestly** — the window-pulse's fall: the bath's `(1−q)`
> trend rising, the lines' mutual drift, the pulse thins when the funding thins,
> through the same channels.

**The gain exceeds the price.** `field-npc-ai.md` §3.1's multiplicity theorem is
the whole honest arithmetic: a shared high-`q` core is a *regional-efficiency
multiplier* — each member's budget recovery rises, each member's `(1−q)` waste
falls, so the village is **superadditive** (the whole's intelligence exceeds the
parts'). The tithe is a *small, fixed share* of each member's yield; the gain it
funds is the *multiplier* that share keeps holding up. The restoration, the
deeper locks, the cheap vents, the passed craft, the lit departure — the funded
commons returns **more than the tithe costs**, which is exactly what makes a
rational member tithe rather than hoard. It is the corpus's already-designed
truth — the AM-GM evenness theorem, `coherence-commons.md` §7.2: a commons sharing
coherence evenly is smarter than the same coherence concentrated in a few — made
into the tithe's economic justification: **the even share (the tithe) funds the
even commons (the bath), and the even commons is the smarter one.**

**Under-tithing reads honestly as a thinning commons.** This is where the tithe
ties into the window-pulse (§2/§3) without inventing a mechanic: the pulse reads
the four landed reads the corpus already publishes — the bath's `(1−q)` trend,
the lines' mutual drift, the named anchors' held-locks, the commensals'
steadiness (`the-window-pulse.md` §2.1). **A settlement that under-tithes is a
settlement whose funded structures go unmaintained** — the bath's `(1−q)` rises
as the anti-corruption funding thins, the Observatory's keeping dims, the
school's rhythm scatters, the harbor's lamp goes dark — and the pulse reads that
**through the same channels**, honestly: the pulse thins when the funding thins.
The census's quiet-sign pattern is inherited unchanged (`the-census.md` §3 — the
people thin before the field does); the tithe is the *funding* face of that
honest read. Under-tithing is not a "penalty the game inflicts"; it is the
**commons' shared cost going unpaid and the shared things' maintained order
visibly thinning** — the field's honest consequence, never a hidden difficulty
