# The Gift: The Un-Booked Giving — the Economy's Only Generosity, the Honest Inverse of the Market

**Question under design:** the corpus designs the exchange's honesty — the
Market books every trade on the Shared Ledger (`the-market.md`), the ledger
makes every contribution or drain legible per member (`shared-ledger.md`), the
Oath binds within-window promises (`the-oath.md`), the Festival celebrates the
commons' joy (`the-festival.md`), and the census reads the population off the
book (`the-census.md`). But **every exchange that books is an exchange the
society can audit — and nothing designs the deliberately un-booked transfer**:
the giving that refuses the tally. The economy has trust-by-law; it has **no
generosity**. A settlement that only ever settles is coherent but cold.
This document designs the **Gift**: the deliberate **no-trace transfer** — a
contribution made **outside the ledger's op-class** — the honest inverse of the
market, and the corpus's one act whose truth is that it *cannot* be audited.

**The load-bearing composition, stated once:**

> **The Gift is the deliberate no-trace transfer: one player gives another
> *order* (a meal, an alloy, a held basin) with an explicit refusal to book it,
> so the Board's `C(M)` records neither giver nor receiver.** The market's
> honesty comes from *being booked*; **the Gift's comes from *not being*** — an
> act whose whole truth is that it cannot be audited. The no-false-booking rule
> is intact: **you cannot fraudulently give** (a false gift is a false name that
> decays — the thing given is still the thing it is, read by the receiver's own
> instruments, never by the ledger); **but you can truly give (un-tallied)** —
> the one act the book cannot record, and therefore the one act that cannot be
> corrupted *by* being recorded.

This document is the corpus's **generosity doc** — the honest frame that closes
the economy's emotional gap: the un-counted kindness the ledger can't see but
the field's held continuity does, the citizen-moment the superadditive commons
actually runs on, the tide-high generosity the Festival and the Window's Year
are made of.

## 1. The Gift, stated once

The corpus's economy is honest *by the book*. A trade books on the ledger as a
Q4 op-carried exchange (`the-market.md` §2.1); the ledger renders every
member's net contribution or drain per tide (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2); the
name refuses false offers (`the-name.md` §2); the oath and the festival compose
their own booked acts (`the-oath.md`, `the-festival.md`). **Every exchange
that books is an exchange the society can audit.** But the economy's honesty
has never been pointed at its *generosity* — and generosity is precisely the
giving that refuses the tally:

> **The Gift is the deliberate no-trace transfer: one player gives another
> *order* (a meal, an alloy, a held basin) with an explicit refusal to book it,
> so the Board's `C(M)` records neither giver nor receiver.** It is the
> economy's **only generosity** — the one act that *gives* rather than *binds,
> books, or settles* — and the **honest inverse of the market**: where the
> market's trust is the book's own readability (`the-market.md` §3), the Gift's
> truth is that it is *unreadable by law*.

**The honest flag, once.** The pieces a gift composes are all landed — the Q4
op-record and the member-id at the single non-forgeable point
(`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1), the ledger's per-member `C(M)` aggregation
(`shared-ledger.md` §1.2), the no-false-naming that a false offer decays
(`the-name.md` §2), the no-free-energy cap (`energy-harnessing.md` §6), the
festival's spent-never-free giving (`the-festival.md` §2.3), and the commons'
superadditive hold (`field-npc-ai.md` §3). **The Gift's *composition* — that a
transfer *can* be deliberately un-booked, *how* a player marks it so (its
form, §2), what the refusal *means* (§2.2), and why that is the corpus's most
human act (§3) — is this document's [design] over them.** No new channel, no
new physics, no "gift record" the engine writes *against* the Q4 ops it already
writes: a gift is the world's own *refusal to book*, designed, over the ledger's
landed honesty. The engine writes the Q4 op-stream; the gift is the deliberate
absence of a contribution line on it.

The Gift is the corpus's **generosity doc** because it is the one place the
field's honesty is pointed at the *gift* — the giving that Minecraft's barter
and the corpus's booked exchange both bypass, the act whose whole truth is that

## 2. The un-booked honesty — the load-bearing mechanism

### 2.1 The market's honesty is being booked; the Gift's is *not* being

The Market's load-bearing claim is that a trade is trustworthy *because* it is
booked — every exchange's truth lands at the single non-forgeable point, so
**a trader who would cheat cannot: the cheat is a false op the field will not
book** (`the-market.md` §2.3). The Gift inverts that exact source of honesty:

> **(The Gift's honesty is the market's, from the *not-booking* side.)** The
> market is honest because the book records its truth; **the Gift is honest
> because the book *cannot* record its truth** — there is nothing to forge,
> inflate, or mis-record, because the giver and receiver deliberately refuse
> the tally. A trade's honesty is *readable* by law; a gift's honesty is
> *invulnerable for being unreadable* by law. **The one act the book cannot
> record is the one act that cannot be corrupted *by* being recorded.**

This is the design's load-bearing inversion, stated once and never blurred:
**the market's trust comes from the book; the Gift's comes from the book's
refusal.** Where a trade is a Q4 op of the settled record's shape (`the-market.md`
§2.1), a gift is a *withheld* op — a transfer that does not write a contribution
line the Board's `C(M)` would read (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2). Neither giver nor
receiver books to the settlement's book; the ledger sees the order *move*, but
refuses to own who gave and who took.

### 2.2 The refusal's form — a transfer marked un-booked ([design])

The market's exchange *is* a bookable op of the settled record's shape
(`the-market.md` §2.1 — `{member, op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}`).
The Gift must be the deliberate *absence* of that book, and its form is this
doc's [design] (the brief's `[design]` — the gift's *form*): the explicit
refusal, how a player marks a transfer as un-booked.

> **[design] The Gift is a transfer *marked un-booked*: the giver performs the
> transfer—passing the order into the receiver's held state—but opts the
> contribution *out* of the op-record's bookable line.** The Q4 lane still
> carries the *movement* the world executed (the item is physically in the
> receiver's hand, its provenance still read by the receiver's instruments); but
> the record a gift writes **lacks the bookable contribution** — no `C(M)`
> line, no per-member read, no steward-or-margin disposition. The gift's
> record, if it books at all, books the *refusal* — a no-trace flag where a
> contribution line would be. That flagged-absence is the whole mechanism:
> there is nothing on the Board to audit *because* the Board was refused the
> entry.

The concrete UI/mechanics of *marking* (the gesture: a held item offered with a
"this is a gift" toggle, a refused-book chit, a no-ledger handover) is **[design]**
— the exact surface is a Phase-1.5 dial against the goods mechanics. What is
*load-bearing and not design* is the *semantics*: the gift's un-talliedness is
**deterministic** (§6c) — a marked un-booked transfer stays un-booked; it is
**non-minting** (§6d) — the order moved is the same coherence's transfer, never
a gain; and it is **accessible** (§6e) — the thing given is still readable.

### 2.3 The no-false-booking rule is intact — you cannot fraudulently give

This is the sharpest honesty the Gift owns. The ledger's no-false-booking is
the single-writer truth that **the op-stream is unforgeable, exactly what the
domain executed** (`shared-ledger.md` §6d; `schema-that-settles.md` §3). The
Gift's *refusal to book* must not read as a loophole that lets a lie through.
It cannot — because the check is not the book, it is the *thing*:

> **You cannot fraudulently give — a false gift is a false name that decays.**
> Because the no-false-naming extends to goods (`the-market.md` §2.2; `the-name.md`
> §2 — honesty is thermodynamics), a gift's *thing* is still the thing it is,
> **read by the receiver's own instruments, never by the ledger.** A scar-tainted
> pick given "as clean" is still a scar-tainted pick — the receiver reads its
> alloy-`q` on the edge (`the-tool.md` §2), the same read a booked trade would
> give, *before* any book. A common seed given "as rare" is still common — the
> receiver's own read of the order's provenance is not fooled by the giver's
> refusal to book the claim. **The refusal of the book cannot launder a lie,
> because the thing given is still the thing it is.**

The gift's honesty is not weaker for being un-booked — it is *transferred to the
receiver*. What the ledger would have enforced by recording the truth, the Gift
enforces by leaving the truth in the thing, where the receiver reads it directly.
**The no-false-booking rule is intact — it just moves from the book to the held
thing.**

And the counter-direction is the design's point about the positive:

> **But you can truly give (un-tallied).** A *true* gift — a clean high-`q` blade
> given as what it is — is the one act the book cannot record, and **therefore
> the one act that cannot be corrupted by being recorded.** No entry can inflate
> its worth (it books nothing), no entry can misattribute it (it books no membe-
> r), no thief can forge it (there is no record to forge). **The gift's truth is
> exactly that it is un-auditable — and that is why it is trustworthy.** A
> settlement that only ever books can prove what happened; a settlement with the
> Gift has the one act whose goodness cannot be recorded, only be. That is the
> honest distinction that makes the Gift the economy's only *generosity* rather
> than a mere transfer.

## 3. The economy's warmth — the honest composition

### 3.1 The Gift closes the emotional gap — a book that only settles is cold

The corpus's economy, before the Gift, is entirely a *settlement* economy: every
exchange is booked, every contribution legible, every act auditable
(`the-market.md`, `shared-ledger.md`). **That economy is coherent — and cold.**
A settlement whose every giving is a tally is a settlement that has *trust-by-law*
but no *generosity*: it can prove every trade, and it can never *gift*. This is
the gap the Gift closes:

> **A settlement that only books and never gives is coherent but cold.** The
> Gift adds the one economic act the book cannot greet: the giving that refuses
> the tally. It is not a new ledger column; it is the ledger's deliberate
> silence — the act whose warmth is that it will not be owned as a bookable
> contribution. **The Gift is what makes an honest economy *warm*: the kindness
> the law cannot audit, held only by the receiver and the field's continuity.**

### 3.2 What the Gift is made of — the festival's tide-high generosity, the giving tide

The Gift is not a cold transaction; it is the citizen-moment the corpus's own
joy and time docs already reach for:

- **The Festival's generosity is tide-high because it gathers the commons' whole
  channeled input into *one* phase-aligned act** (`the-festival.md` §3 — the
  settlement's strongest single `C(festival)`, spent, never free). The Gift is
  that generosity's **individual instance** — the single phase-aligned giving
  that carries the same tide-high willingness to *spend* for another, at the
  two-person scale instead of the settlement's. The festival is the commons
  giving together; **the Gift is the member giving alone — and the festival
  needs the Gift as its felt microform, the giving the tide-high celebration is
  *built out of*.**
- **The Window's Year's giving tide** (`the-window-year.md` §3) dates the
  observances that make a settlement's year a rhythm; the festival's day is the
  bright anchor, and the Gift is the anchor's **daily texture** — the giving
  that marks the ordinary days *between* observances, the un-dated kindness that
  makes the calendar's generosity a lived thing rather than a marked event.
  **The Gift is the tide's generosity *at hand*, the giving the calendar's
  bright anchors presume.**

Both compose onto the festival's hardest bound: **a festival is spent, never
free** (`the-festival.md` §2.3 — the members' collective ε² vent is the price).
The Gift inherits it verbatim (§6d): a true gift spends the coherence the giver
actually holds — it is a *withdrawal*, never a gain, never a mint. The
generosity is real precisely because it costs.

### 3.3 The citizen-moment the superadditive commons runs on

The deepest warmth-claim, grounded in the corpus's own society law:

> **The Gift is the un-counted kindness the superadditive commons actually
> runs on.** The commons is superadditive because the shared high-`q` core
> raises every member's efficiency (`field-npc-ai.md` §3.1; the multiplicative
> budget `∏ 1/(1−q_i)` of `coherence-commons.md` §7). But that superadditivity
> is not merely a ledger's positive balance — it lives in the **held continuity**
> of members who maintain each other's living: the un-booked heal that holds a
> partner's anchors, the un-tallied share that keeps a member's lock through a
> thin tide. **Those ordinary, un-counted kindnesses are the field's held
> continuity — and the Gift is their design.** The ledger cannot see them (they
> book no contribution), and that is precisely why they are generosity rather
> than accounting; the field's held continuity does see them (the maintained
> core is real), and that is why they are *real*, not sentiment.

The honest frame, once:

> **The Gift is the one act the law's book cannot audit — and that is exactly
> why it is the corpus's most human act.** The economy's trust is the book's;
> its generosity is the book's *refusal*. A settlement that only books proves
> and never gives; a settlement with the Gift has the one act whose goodness is
> not ownable by any record — the un-counted kindness that cannot be corrupted
> by being recorded. **The design's claim is deliberate: the most human act in
> this field is the one the law cannot tally.**

## 4. The gift's relation to the book — the honest edges

The Gift is defined by what it is *not*, and each edge is a deliberate refusal
grounded in the corpus's own honesty.

### 4.1 A gift is not a market's evasion — it is not a hidden trade

The sharpest possible misreading is the gift as a *loophole*: "book nothing, so
the law can't see it — a way to trade secretly." The honest edges kill that
reading:

| | A **gift** | A **market evasion** (a hidden trade) |
|---|---|---|
| The receiver | **can decline** — a gift is refused easily, a trade must deliver | is bound to a booked exchange's terms (`the-market.md` §2.4 — a non-delivery is a ruptured within-window vow) |
| The thing's provenance | **still read by the receiver's own instruments** — the scar-tainted pick read as scar-tainted, the common seed read as common (`the-tool.md` §2; `the-name.md` §2) | is *booked* so the law knows it (`the-market.md` §2.2) — the evasion tries to *hide* it |
| The honesty | the receiver's direct read — never hidden-only | a hidden trade's whole dishonesty is that it *wants* the thing hidden |

**A gift is not a hidden trade** because nothing is hidden *from the receiver*:
the receiver declines freely, and reads the thing's truth on their own
instruments *before* accepting. What the gift refuses to book is not the thing's
truth — it is the tally, the attribution, the contribution. **The gift is the
book's silence about *who gave and who took*, never a silence about *what was
given*.** The thing's truth is never hidden-only (§6e); only its audit is.

### 4.2 A gift is not an oath's bound promise — it is deliberately un-bound

The Oath designs the promised giving between two lines: a named promise, a
pledge-line that books the keeping, a rupture when the promise unresolves
(`the-oath.md` §1/§2.2/§3.2). The Gift is the deliberate inverse:

> **A gift is not an oath.** It is **deliberately un-bound** — **no pledge-line,
> no rupture.** The oath's giving is *promised* (a keeping the field books and
> reads as held-or-failed); the Gift's giving is *freely made* (a contribution
> the field deliberately does not book, so there is nothing to break). **The
> un-bound giving is the design**: a gift carries no promise to keep, no line to
> fail, no rupture to read — it is the giving that is *done*, not the giving
> that is *owed*. Where the oath makes a gift *costed and held*, the Gift makes
> a giving *free of the bond entirely* — and that freedom is not a weakness but
> the definition. The settlement's book shows neither a held pledge-line nor a
> failing one; it simply shows no line, because the Gift asked for none.

Where the oath is "the within-window compact made field-true" (`the-oath.md`
§1), the Gift is **the within-window giving made un-compacted** — the one act
that is the honest opposite of a promise: it can never be broken because it was
never bound.

### 4.3 A gift cannot launder an exile's standing or a stranger's provenance

The Gift's un-talliedness must not read as a way to *slip* a person past the law:

- **An exile's standing is not laundered by a gift.** An exiled member's lines
  are severed by decision, and `schema-that-settles.md` §2.1.1's no-amnesty
  holds (`the-exile.md` §4). A gift *outside* the Book cannot re-book a severed
  line — the gift books nothing, so it cannot restore standing any more than it
  can confer it. A gift given to an exile is just a gift (the thing's truth is
  still the thing's); it does not make them a resident, and it does not hide the
  severed line the book still reads. **The Gift's silence about giving is not a
  silence about *being* — the exile stays exiled, the book stays honest.**
- **A stranger's provenance is read by the receiver, never by the book.**
  `window-guests.md` §3 books a foreign line with a provenance flag, never folded
  into a resident's book. A gift to or by a guest carries the same honesty: the
  guest's carried provenance reads on the receiver's instruments (the foreign
  ε², the foreign cadence — `window-guests.md` §2), *not* through the gift's
  refusal of the book. The Gift cannot make a stranger read as a resident
  (there is no book entry to fold), and it cannot hide a stranger's provenance
  (the receiver still reads it directly). **The gift is the receiver's read, not
  the book's — and the receiver's read is never fooled.**

**The gift is the book's deliberate silence, and the silence is honest.**
Stated once in each direction: the silence is honest *about the giving* (nobody
claims a false gift, because the thing is still the thing, §2.3), and the silence
is honest *about the people* (an exiled line stays severed, a stranger's
provenance stays foreign, because the book was not asked to lie and did not).

## Open questions

1. **The mark's scope (§2.2).** Is the un-booked mark a property of *any*
   transfer (a per-gift toggle on any held-order handover), or a bounded *gift
   class* (a distinct gesture — "offer as a gift" — separate from a trade's
   offer, mirroring the market's exchange-op dial)? The honest default leans to
   the latter (a distinct gave-as-gift form, so the refusal is legible and not
   accidentally silently booked); the exact scope is a [design] dial against the
   settled op set. **[design]**
2. **What a gift can carry (§2.1).** The brief names a meal, an alloy, a held
   basin — *order*: stored coherence, not power (`seed-garden.md` §4.2's
   "a seed is order, never power"). Can a gift carry a *kept skill* (a vow's
   service, `the-oath.md` §2), a *named place's* upkeep, a *debt's* relief
   (`the-burden.md`)? The honest boundary against the oath (a promised service is
   a pledge-line, not an un-bound gift) is a [design] dial; the gifts of *stuff*
   are Phase-1.5-real. **[design]**
3. **How un-talliedness is read on the Board (§2.2/§6c).** Is a gift's
   non-contribution a *transparent absence* (the Board shows the order moved and
   a "gift" mark where a `C(M)` line would be — the no-trace flag's display) or
   a *true absence* (the Board shows no record at all, as if the order never
   booked)? The market's cell-ownership rule (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.3) and
   the board's readability (`shared-ledger.md` §6e) pull toward the former (the
   *world* shows the thing moved; only the tally is refused); the exact display
   is [design]. **[design]**
4. **The gift's receiver-read authority (§2.3).** "The thing given is still read
   by the receiver's own instruments" — is that read the receiver's *own* reader
   (their held lens, `field-instruments.md`), or does the field's held state at
   the receiver offer a *shared* read? The honest default is the receiver's own
   instruments (the family rule, a consumer of the publish); whether a gift
   *also* leaves a field-visible held-state mark (the basin's held lock reads as
   held in the receiver's hands) is a [design] dial over the-name's anchor
   (§6e). **[design]**
5. **The gift's place in the warmth's clock (§3.2).** The Window's Year dates
   the observances; the festival's day is the bright anchor. Is the Gift an
   *un-dated* giving (the ordinary day's kindness, never on the calendar) or does
   a *giving season* (a tide-year observance of many un-booked gifts) mount on the
   calendar's bright anchors? The former is this doc's honest default (the
   best kind is the one the calendar cannot catch); the latter inherits
   `the-window-year.md` §3's composition dial and would make the gift a dated
   generosity rather than the tide's gift *at hand*. **[design]**
