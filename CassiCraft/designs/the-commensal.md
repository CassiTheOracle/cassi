## 1. The Commensal, stated once

The stranger-object layer, as the corpus has drawn it, is the layer of the
*strange* — the beings the field produces that a settlement must read and answer.
Until now it has held four faces, and **every one is adversarial or
neutral-harsh** — the predator that *hunts* it, the blight that *turns* it, the
waking that *outlives* it, the scavenger that *records* its spent:

| Face | What it is | It is a… |
|---|---|---|
| The **signature-predator** (Coda) | a **non-willful resonance structure** the local field holds in its `ε²` margins, drawn to a channeling signature (`signature-predator.md` §1) | **predator** — it preys on a clean trail |
| The **blight** | a **living-wrong region** — a region's organisms pushed past their band corrupt into a standing drain (`the-blight.md` §1) | **turn** — the field's own life turned |
| The **feral** | a **kept thing that woke** — persistent-Π that outlived its keeper's intent (`the-feral-instrument.md` §1) | **waking** — a kept structure that became a will |
| The **scavenger** | a **fitted creature** that lives on what a window discards — the residual `ε²` of the spent locality (`the-scavenger.md` §1) | **fitting** — the denizen of the spent |

None is positive. The corpus's friendly beings are the village NPCs — the
*field-intent* creatures that serve a settlement's bath
([`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §1/§3) — but the NPC is the *servant*:
it has a field-intent, a budget, a deliberate steering that turns the field's
order into *choice*. There has never been a **wild** creature that lives
cooperatively beside the order — no friendly, non-NPC being, no neighbor that is
at home in the same coherent region you tend.

The Commensal is that being. It is the fifth face of the strange, and the first
that is warm:

> **The Commensal, stated once.** It is the **order-side companion** — the
> creature that thrives *on* the field's order rather than against or off it:
> at home in a coherent region (a high-`q` ridge, a settlement's bath-edge, a
> reef's floor), it eats the field's surplus harmlessly (a herd that grazes the
> precipitation, a bird that nests in a still-room's eaves, a reef-warden on the
> sea-floor's reef) and, in exchange, **maintains a small bit of the order it
> lives in** — its own maintained `(1−q)` glow and movement are a *bounded
> anti-corruption assist* to its patch: it weeps a tiny basin clean, its passage
> keeps a thin-lock steady. It is not a pet and not a servant — a being that
> lives beside because the field's order is its habitat. It is the stranger-object
> layer's **first positive face**, the corpus's **warmth**: **the field does not
> only turn against you (predator, blight) or fail around you (scavenger); it
> also keeps company when you hold it well.**

The distinction from all four cousins, drawn exactly:

- **Not a predator.** The Coda forms *around your signature* in the `ε²` margins
  and hunts the clean trail of a channeler (`signature-predator.md` §1/§2). The
  commensal needs no signature — it is *already here*, a run the field holds in
  the *ordered* margin, and it has nothing to hunt. The Coda's habitat is the
  thin-living desert where a player can still *cause* a clean trail; **the
  commensal's habitat is the maintained converse** — the coherent ridge, the
  bath's fringe, where the field is organized and your presence is a neighbor,
  not a trail. The predator preys on the living-ish margin; the commensal grazes
  beside it.
- **Not a turn.** The blight is a *living population corrupted* — a wrong-band
  turning the field's own organisms into a drain (`the-blight.md` §1). The
  commensal is not a population that turned; it is a population that **was always
  a consumer of the surplus**, and its assist (the anti-corruption hold, §3) is
  the *opposite* of a drain — the blight's healing act (`conservation =
  anti-corruption`, `field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.3) at the smallest scale. It
  is not a turn; it is a tending.
- **Not a waking.** The feral is a *named, kept* structure that woke, acting on
  a keeper's outlived intent (`the-feral-instrument.md` §1). The commensal is
  **not kept and never wakes**: it was never named or bound, it is a wild
  organism-class, and it does not act on an intent — it simply lives, and
  dissolves, in the ordered locality. Where the feral is a kept thing that
  *became a will*, the commensal is a wild thing that *was always at home*.
- **Not a scavenger.** The scavenger is the order's *spent* twin — the fitted
  creature that lives on the residual `ε²` of what a window discards
  (`the-scavenger.md` §1/§2). The commensal is the *order's* twin: it lives on
  the surplus of what a window *maintains*. The scavenger fits the spent's
  residual `ε²`; **the commensal fits the order's surplus** — the bright twin of
  the live-but-fitting read (§2). Neither preys; but where the scavenger is the
  melancholy census of waste, the commensal is the warm read of order held well.
- **Not a servant.** The NPC is the *field-intent* creature — a run with a
  deliberate budget that steers toward a village's good (`field-npc-ai.md`
  §1/§3). The commensal has **no will to serve** — it is not a field-intent
  creature, it has no budget to allocate, it does not choose a settlement's good.
  It is an organism-class whose *fitting* is the neighbor: it lives beside the
  order because the order is its habitat, and its assist is not service but the
  mere act of being a maintained run in a maintained patch (§3). The NPC serves;
  the commensal simply lives, and in living holds.

**The through-line of the fifth face:** every prior face is something the field
*makes at an extreme and the settlement must answer* — the danger layer's house
style is "no monster table, every hazard is the field at an extreme"
(`field-hazards.md` §1). The commensal is the first face in that layer that is
*not* a threat to answer or a signal of waste to read — **it is company to keep.**
It is the stranger-object layer's first positive face: the field, held well, does
not only threaten and record; it *keeps company*. §2 draws the order-side fitting,
§3 the bounded assist, §4 the friendly stranger.

## 2. The order-side fitting — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 The organism-class run — grounded in the ecology

The commensal is first an **organism-class** — a run the field holds, in exactly
the sense `field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2 defines life:

> "An organism is not a recipe (a species table) but a **run** — a configuration
> the field is actively maintaining. When the run ends, the creature is not
> 'killed'; the field stops holding the configuration and it returns to the
> medium."

An organism precipitates where the local field state admits its coherence-band +
size (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.2's biome⇔regime mapping — a creature
precipitates only where `q` is in a coherent band and `ε²` is low). The
**recognition rule** (§6b) is the ecology's core judgment: a field structure is
recognized as a candidate organism when it sustains, for a window `T_org`, all
three of (1) a **coherence band** (local `q` in `[q_low, q_high]`), (2) a
**decoherence ceiling** (`ε²` below a floor), and (3) a **size/rung-depth**
(spanning ≥2–3 nested cascade layers). It is the throttle that keeps the
morphology-soup from precipitating into thousands of bodies
(`field-emergent-ecology.md` §6c — the ≈ 2,000-entity cap).

**The commensal's band is the *ordered* one.** The ecology's recognition rule is
designed against exactly this band — the high-`q`, low-`ε²` region where a
coherent ridge or living plain precipitates thriving morphologies
(`field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.2). The commensal precipitates **inside that
band**, at its *surplus margin* — not the deep q-locked core (the most organized
point, where the φ-attractor's own structure holds without needing a small
fitted runner) but the **margin where the order sheds a small surplus and a small
runner can fit it**. This is the inverse of the scavenger's fitting, and the two
read as bright/dark twins:

| The scavenger's fitting (spent) | The commensal's fitting (ordered) |
|---|---|
| **residual band** — `q` *below* the living band (near or under the noise floor, `q ~ 1e-3…1e-1`), but not the dead-zone vacuum | **surplus margin** — `q` *inside* the living band, held high (near the ordered attractor, toward `q ≈ 0.947` at the coherent ridge), but at the margin where the maintenance sheds a small surplus — not the deepest, most self-holding core |
| **residual decoherence** — `ε²` *above* the healthy ceiling, at the spent level the field keeps and does not re-bind | **ordered low-`ε²`** — the φ-lock *held* (the maintained locality's low-`ε²`), with a tiny, bounded `(1−q)` bleeds at the margin the field maintains against |
| **edge-scaled** — small, fitting the spent locality's shallow residual layers | **margin-scaled** — small, fitting the ordered locality's *edge* run — enough nested layers to count as an organism, tuned to the shallow, low-maintenance layers of the coherent margin rather than the deep organism's full internal cascade |

**[design]** — which ordered-margin range the commensal locks to (how far from
the coherent ridge's deepest core toward its edge, how much `(1−q)` surplus marks
the band) is **this doc's band**, probe-calibrated against the ecology's
recognition rule (§6b) and the maintenance axis (`life-signal.md` §3). The
*direction* is sourced: an organism-class run that precipitates where the field
holds a *living* config; the commensal precipitates where the field holds an
*ordered* config and sheds a small surplus — the bright twin of the scavenger's
residual fit (`the-scavenger.md` §2.1). §5b.

### 2.2 The surplus — what it grazes, honestly

The scavenger feeds on the spent locality's *large* residual `ε²` — the
`(1−q)` fraction the field wastes where `q` is low (`the-scavenger.md` §2.2). The
commensal's food is the *same* waste law at its **smallest**: the `(1−q)` surplus
of an ordered, maintained locality. From `energy-harnessing.md` §2:

> **The `(1−q)` waste law is the machine-loss floor.** Every working field system
> wastes `E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput` as visible glow. A machine in a low-`q`
> region bleeds most of its throughput to glow; **a machine on a coherence ridge
> runs near-lossless.**

Because a maintained locality runs at high `q` (the Qi bath of
`energy-harnessing.md` §4.4, a coherent ridge near `q ≈ 0.947`), its waste
fraction is *small but nonzero* — every working ordered system still sheds the
residual `(1−q)` of its own throughput. **That is the commensal's surplus.** It
grazes the ordered run's own negligible waste — the tiny `(1−q)` glow at the
margin of a held bath, the precipitation's edge where condensation sheds a small
surplus, the reef's floor where the boundary's life concentrates
(`the-sea-floor.md` §2b) — and **never a drain**:

> **[design] The surplus, stated once:** the commensal does not prey and does not
> spend — it **grazes the order's own negligible waste**, the `(1−q)` surplus of
> a maintained locality (`energy-harnessing.md` §2), a herd-that-grazes-the-
> precipitation, a bird that nests in a still-room's eaves, a reef-warden on the
> reef's floor. It feeds on the ordered run's surplus — never on the run itself,
> never on the order's life, never on your trail. It is a *consumer of surplus*,
> and in feeding it converts nothing and yields nothing: a grazing is a hold, not
> a harvest (§3/§5d).

The load-bearing honesty — this is the *inversion* of the scavenger's niche:
where the scavenger converts nothing but holds against a *spent* residue's drain
(`the-scavenger.md` §2.2), the commensal converts nothing and *holds the order
itself*. It is a consumer of surplus whose consumption *is* its maintenance — it
grazes the tiny `(1−q)` the field sheds at the margin, and its own maintained run
(in feeding, in moving, in glowing) is the small anti-corruption act that keeps
that margin's patch ordered (§3).

### 2.3 The three habitats — each grounded in its source doc

The commensal's habitat set is precisely the corpus's named *ordered
localities* — each a **real, sourced maintained state** from its own doc:

| Ordered locality | The maintained state (source doc) | The commensal in it |
|---|---|---|
| **The coherent ridge / settlement's bath-edge** | a maintained high-`q` core with a radius — the Qi bath raises the `(1−q)` floor across its cluster (`house-that-steers.md` §2.2; `energy-harnessing.md` §4.4); "bath at the center, productive edges, deliberate drains" | the **bath's fringe** — the productive edge / the coherent ridge's margin where the maintained order sheds its small surplus; the commensal lives *beside* the kept, at the bath-edge the settlement's layout deliberately provides (`house-that-steers.md` §2.2) |
| **The precipitation's edge** | a condensation front — `q` accumulating, crossing `τ_c = 0.5`, precipitation events (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.2) | the **edge that grazes** — a herd-that-grazes-the-precipitation lives at the front's margin, fitting the small `(1−q)` surplus an ordered condensation sheds, riding the ridge as it forms |
| **The reef's floor** | the ecology's band at the regime's boundary — where the liquid regime's life concentrates at the border between the near-empty plain and the structured deep (`the-sea-floor.md` §2b; `field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.2) | the **reef-warden** — the commensal at the reef's ordered floor, fitting the boundary's surplus and holding its patch's order, the friendly face of `the-sea-floor.md`'s reef |

**The commensal avoids the deep, self-holding core.** The deepest, most organized
point of a coherent locality — the very center of a Qi bath, the innermost rung
of a deep organism — needs no small fitted runner: the φ-attractor's own
structure holds it (`coherence-magic.md` §1; the attractor's organizing tendency),
and its `(1−q)` surplus is smallest there. The commensal lives at the *margin* —
where the maintained order meets its edge (the bath's fringe, the precipitation's
front, the reef's boundary), where the surplus collects and a small runner can
fit it. The no-free-energy grammar (`energy-harnessing.md` §6) is the same: a
locality's deepest core has no surplus for a consumer to graze, so the commensal
has no niche there.

> **[design] The honest boundary of the fitting:** the commensal inhabits the
> **ordered margin** — the high-`q`, low-`ε²` surplus edge of a maintained
> locality — and **never the spent and never the self-held core**. A spent
> locality (a worked vein, a kept scar) has no order to be held, so a commensal
> has no niche there (that is the scavenger's band). A locality's deepest core
> (the bath's center, the deep organism's innermost rung) is self-holding, so it
> sheds no surplus for a commensal to fit (that is the attractor's own bound).
> The commensal lives in the ring between — the margin where the order is held
> and a small runner can graze its surplus and hold its patch.

## 3. The bounded assist — the honest exchange

### 3.1 The anti-corruption hold at the smallest scale

The order-side counterpart of the scavenger's lawfulness (§3 there) is the
commensal's **bounded assist** — the exchange that makes it cooperative rather
than merely harmless. From `field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.3:

> **Conservation = maintaining coherence regions.** The energy doc's
> **anti-corruption** — spending fed coherence to suppress `ε²` and hold the
> dissolution threshold — is directly an ecological act. A player who suppresses
> decoherence in a region keeps its biome alive.

Anti-corruption is the corpus's healing act: spend coherence to suppress `ε²`
below the dissolution floor and hold ground against decay (`energy-harnessing.md`
§5.4). Its cost is net-negative — you pay in coherence to maintain, never a free
ecosystem (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.3), the no-free-energy law.

**The commensal performs that same act at the smallest scale — and it is the
surplus exchange that makes it cooperative:**

> **[design] The bounded assist, stated once:** a commensal's own maintained
> `(1−q)` glow and movement **are** a bounded anti-corruption assist to its
> patch. In living — in grazing the margin's surplus, in moving through its site,
> in the `(1−q)` glow its run sheds — it holds the local `ε²` just below where it
> would otherwise drift: it **weeps a tiny basin clean** (its presence suppresses
> the small decoherence that would otherwise accumulate in the patch), its
> **passage keeps a thin-lock steady** (its movement through a maintained corner
> is itself a hold against the drain). A settlement that hosts commensals is
> **measurably steadier** — its patch's order is held up by the commensal's
> presence, the way a player's anti-corruption holds a region but at the scale of
> a single small run.

The honest mechanics of the assist, grounded in the corpus's real quantities:

- **It is the surplus exchange, not a new power.** The commensal grazes the
  margin's tiny `(1−q)` surplus (§2.2) and *returns* a small, bounded hold — the
  energy it "pays back" is not extracted from the field, it *is* the form of its
  own maintained run (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2's "metabolism = maintaining
  the φ-lock"). A commensal's assist is not a machine; it is an organism holding
  its own lock, and the field reads that hold as a small local anti-corruption
  (`life-signal.md` §3's maintained-live-class).
- **It never generates order — it holds the order the settlement already made.**
  The no-free-energy cap (`energy-harnessing.md` §6: `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`, no
  conversion yields more than it sinks) governs the assist exactly: a commensal
  cannot raise its patch's `q` above what the field already provides at that site
  (the coherent ridge, the bath's fringe, `house-that-steers.md` §5d's "a house
  never mints `q`"). The patch's steadiness is **the settlement's own order, held
  by the commensal's presence, never produced**. A commensal is a *hold*, not a
  mint.
- **The assist is bounded and measurable.** A settlement with commensals reads
  *steadier* — the Life-Signal's maintenance read on its patch is more robust
  (the tiny basin stays clean, the thin-lock holds), a small, honest, bounded
  difference. It is never a free-`q` grant to the settlement; it is the order's
  own hold, made legible by a cooperative creature's presence (§5d).

### 3.2 The honest boundary of the assist — never a mint, never a farm

The closest thing to a sharp edge in the commensal's cooperation is its **yield
bound** — the honest line that keeps it a cooperative neighbor and not a
resource:

| | A commensal's assist | A farmed "warden" (the forbidden) |
|---|---|---|

| Why | the assist is the *form of its living*, not a transfer — the energy is the commensal's own maintained run, never extracted | farming it would convert the commensal's presence into a net-positive gain, a physics lie |
