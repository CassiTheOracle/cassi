## 1. The Witness, stated once

The stranger-object layer, as the corpus has drawn it, is the layer of the
*strange* — the beings the field produces that a settlement must read and answer.
It has six named faces, and **every one is a doer**:

| Face | What it is | It is a… | Its act |
|---|---|---|---|
| The **signature-predator** (Coda) | a **non-willful resonance structure** the local field holds in its `ε²` margins, drawn to a channeling signature ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §1) | **predator** | **hunts** a clean trail |
| The **blight** | a **living-wrong region** — a region's life turned against the field ([`the-blight.md`](./the-blight.md) §1) | **turn** | **turns** a region's organisms |
| The **feral** | a **kept thing that woke** — persistent-Π that outlived its keeper's intent ([`the-feral-instrument.md`](./the-feral-instrument.md) §1) | **waking** | **wakes** on its kept intent |
| The **scavenger** | a **fitted creature** that lives on the spent's residual `ε²` ([`the-scavenger.md`](./the-scavenger.md) §1) | **fitting** | **feeds** on the spent |
| The **commensal** | an **order-side companion** that thrives on the order's surplus ([`the-commensal.md`](./the-commensal.md) §1) | **companion** | **holds** a patch of order |
| The **mirror** | a **reflective stranger** that reads your signature and echoes you ([`the-mirror-creature.md`](./the-mirror-creature.md) §1) | **confusion** | **confuses** your identity |

Every face describes how the field *does something* to you, against you, or
beside you — it hunts your trail, turns against your work, wakes from your
keeping, feeds on your waste, keeps company beside your order, plays you back at
yourself. **None merely watches.** Nothing in the corpus is the field's own
attention — no being whose entire act is *presence*. The Witness is that being.

> **The Witness, stated once.** It is the **field's own eye** — a neutral,
> non-willful being whose presence *is* its meaning. A slow coherence structure
> the field holds at a locality's edge, its **only act is that it is *there***:
> a Witness standing at a place is the field's own attention resting there — a
> presence that says, without a mind, *this place is being watched by the field*.
> It is the stranger-object layer's **seventh face and first *non*-face**, the
> only **neutral**: it does not hunt (no phase-matching), does not turn (no
> band), does not feed (no niche), does not wake (no kept intent). It is the
> field's own held order — the same persistent-Π the feral's kept intent uses,
> held **without intent** — and the meaning it has is the meaning the player
> brings to being watched. **It is not a danger, not a gift, not a sign; it is a
> presence, and what it means is *what you do while it watches*.**

The distinction from all six cousins, drawn exactly:

- **Not a resonance that preys.** The Coda forms *around your signature* in the
  `ε²` margins and accumulates on `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability`
  (`signature-predator.md` §1/§2.2), drawn toward you, its tell the approach.
  The witness needs no signature, accumulates on nothing, approaches nothing —
  **the signature-predator's accumulation never begins**. The Coda is the field
  reordering itself *around the cleanest thing it is shown*; the witness is the
  field *resting its attention on a place*. The one hunts the trail; the other
  simply stands where the field has decided to look.
- **Not a region's life turned.** The blight corrupts *organisms* — a sustained
  wrong-band turns a region's living ecology into a drain (`the-blight.md` §1).
  The witness **has no wrong-band, and no band at all** — the blight's
  corruption threshold never touches it, because there is nothing about the
  witness's held order that admits a band. The blight is a *turn*; the witness
  is the absence of turning — it does not feed on the region, it rests at its
  edge.
- **Not a kept thing that woke.** The feral is a *named, kept* structure whose
  persistent-Π outlived its keeper's intent and **acts on the intent it was
  given** (`the-feral-instrument.md` §1/§2). The witness is the same persistent-Π
  order **held without the keeping and without the will**: the reason-field's
  intent-channel with the intent absent. Where the feral's holding coheres into a
  *keep that acts*, the witness's holding is *order that merely persists* — the
  waking never forms, because there is no intent for the keeping to outlive. The
  feral's will is the kept intent operating; the witness's presence is the held
  order *without* an operating intent — **the persistent-Π held without intent.**
- **Not a fitted creature that feeds.** The scavenger *fits the spent's residual
  `ε²`* and lives on it (`the-scavenger.md` §2.2); the commensal *fits the
  order's surplus* and grazes it (`the-commensal.md` §2.2). The witness has
  **no niche on either side** — the spent's residual `ε²` is *not its food*,
  and the order's surplus is *not its keep*. It converts nothing, holds nothing,
  grazes nothing; it is not an organism-class run that metabolizes a band — it
  is a coherence structure whose only act is to be there.
- **Not a reflective stranger that echoes.** The mirror *reads your signature
  and re-emits it as a distorted other-you* (`the-mirror-creature.md` §2). The
  witness **reflects nothing** — no echo, no read-back, no identity challenge,
  no distorted other-you. The mirror plays you; the witness does not play you
  back. It watches, and watching is not an echo.
- **Not a mind that chose, and not a mind at all.** The decoherence-wielder
  (*chooses* evil, `field-npc-ai.md` §4) and the NPC mind (a persistent Π
  pattern the field holds, `reason-field.md` §3.1) both have an intent-channel
  with a *direction*. The witness's intent-channel is **still** — it holds the
  order, not the direction. It is never a mind, never a god, never an
  omen-giver.

**The through-line of the seventh face:** every face is something the field
*makes at an extreme or beside your work* — the danger layer's house style is "no
monster table, every hazard is the field at an extreme" (`field-hazards.md` §1),
and even the non-hazards (scavenger, commensal) are *readable things the field
does*. The witness is the first face that is **not the field doing anything at
all** — it is the field *being present*. It is not a hazard (no threat row), not
a gift (it provides nothing), not a sign (not the story's omen). It is the
corpus's only being that **is what it does** — a presence, and a presence is the
only honest thing a world that is being watched can be.

## 3. The watching — the load-bearing design

The witness's only act is presence, and the **load-bearing design** is what that
presence *says*:

> **A Witness standing at a place is the field's own attention resting there —
> a presence that says, without a mind, *this place is being watched by the
> field.*** The witness has no mind (`reason-field.md` §3.1 — a mind is a
> persistent Π pattern the field *actively holds for a thinking being*; the
> witness's Π is held still, with no direction, no goal, no read-back), no voice,
> no act. And yet the presence is legible — it is the same persistent-Π the
> field holds anywhere, placed at the edge of a place, and a player who can read
> the intent-channel (`reason-field.md` §2.2) or see the held-order structure on
> the instruments knows, without being told, that the field is *watching this
> place*.

The honest frame, stated three ways — what the witness is **not**, each grounded
in its source doc:

- **It is not a sign.** The story's omens are the field's *real* dynamics read
  as a settlement's myth (`the-story.md` §2: the residue the field holds, the
  language's script — an omen is a named, load-bearing reading of what the field
  is *doing*). The witness is not that. It is not the fate's portent, not the
  reading-ahead's herald, not the scheduled omen on the tide's calendar
  ([`the-reading-ahead.md`](./the-reading-ahead.md) §3). It provides no
  foreknowledge, no warning, no prophecy — it has no relation to what comes next,
  because watching is not prediction. **A player who reads a witness as an omen
  is reading their own hope or fear into it**; the witness itself says nothing
  about the future.
- **It is not a danger.** The danger layer's spine is "no monster table, every
  hazard is the field at an extreme" (`field-hazards.md` §1), and its threats
  have a *readable-before-it-arrives* rule (§5.1) and a *respondable* cost
  (§5.2). **The witness gains no row in the threat table** — it has no threat,
  no response, no counterplay. There is nothing to shelter from, nothing to vent
  against, nothing to anti-correct, nothing to evacuate. It is not a hazard; it
  cannot harm, approach, or accumulate. The danger layer's house style admits it
  as the stranger layer's **first non-face** — present in the layer, absent from
  the threats.
- **It provides nothing.** The corpus's no-free-energy discipline
  (`energy-harnessing.md` §6, `field-hazards.md` §5.3) is strictest here: the
  witness is not a gift, a blessing, a buff, a farm, or a read that *yields*. It
  converts no coherence, sheds no `(1−q)`, writes nothing, grants nothing. Even
  the commensal's *bounded* assist (a hold, never a mint, `the-commensal.md`
