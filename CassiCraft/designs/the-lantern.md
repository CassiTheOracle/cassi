# The Lantern: The Carried Night-Read — the Walkers' Light That Reads the Local Field in the Dark

**Question under design:** how the field is read *at night*, by the light you
choose to carry. The instrument family reads the field continuously by day — the
glass glances, the clock times, the map records, the sky's aurora reads at
scale. **Nothing reads at night.** The aurora is the sky's read — the far field's
legibility in the dark *above* — but a night walker in the dark, glass
pocketed, is blind to the *near* field around them: they cannot see the ridge
they stand on, the thin region ahead, the storm's edge, or the one wrong warm
thing the dark has hidden. This document closes that hole with **the Lantern**:
the carried, always-on personal light that reads the *local* field at night —
the ground-level inverse of the sky's aurora, casting a **field-true glow onto
the ground you walk**.

> **The Lantern, stated once.** The aurora is the sky's night read — the far
> field's legibility in the dark above. The Lantern is its ground-level inverse:
> a held, always-on personal light that reads the *near* field in the dark around
> you. Where the aurora shows the far field's state at sky scale
> (`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` §3.3), the Lantern shows the local field at
> your feet — the same published channels, cast as glow onto the ground you
> walk. **Where the sky reads the far field's drains, the Lantern reads the
> near field's form — and it is why night travel is a different read.**

This is a pure **consumer with a presentation idiom** — the Weatherglass family
rule (`field-instruments.md` §2.1) held exactly: **never a new channel, never new
physics, never a write.** The channels the Lantern reads — `q`, `ε²`, `(1−q)`,
`∇(g·Φ)`, the Coda's legible trail — are all *already published* and *already
presented* as glow by the Weatherglass, as tempo by the Clock, as vibration by
the Mirror, as quiet by the Silence. The Lantern is their **night placement**: the
same base read idiom (`field-instruments.md` §1.2/§2.2) repointed at the ground
the player walks, and gated to *brightness* where the day instruments read *hue*
or *hand-rate*. It is the pocketable sibling of the Weatherglass (which reads by
day) and the inverse of the Mirror (which reads yourself — the Lantern reads the
ground around you).

## 1. The Lantern, stated once

The instrument family reads the field's state and rate all day: the reader
(`coherence-magic.md` §2) aims; the Weatherglass (`field-instruments.md` §1.2)
glances; the Clock (`the-clock.md`) times; the Mirror (`the-mirror.md` §2) reads
the self; the sky (`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`) reads at scale. **At night,
only the aurora remains** — and it reads the *far* field, the discharges at
scale and at range. The near field around a night walker is unread:

> **A night walker in the dark, glass pocketed, is blind to the field.** The
> aurora is the sky's read — the far field's legibility high above. But the
> ground beneath the walker's feet, the ridge they stand on, the thin region
> a step ahead, the edge of a rising front, the one wrong warm thing hiding in
> the noise — none of it is legible in the dark. The day instruments are
> glanceable; at night they are pocketed, and the local field goes unread.

**The Lantern closes that hole.** It is a held, always-on personal light —
physically embedded in the world (a craftable object you carry, like the
Weatherglass, `field-instruments.md` §1.4) — whose brightness is **field-true**:
it reads the *local* published channels at the player's position and casts them
as a glow onto the ground ahead. It is the ground-level inverse of the aurora in
exactly the corpus's sense: the aurora is the far field's legibility in the dark
*above*, resolved as `(1−q)` discharges along the coupling-field lines
(`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` §3.1–§3.3); the Lantern is the near field's
legibility in the dark *around you*, resolved as a glow at your feet.

> **A held light whose glow is field-true** — not a lamp that reveals the world,
> but a read that *is* light. The Lantern's brightness rises and falls, flickers
> and warms, according to the local field it samples together with your held
> practice. **It is the inverse of the Mirror** (which reads you to yourself,
> `the-mirror.md` §2 — inward) and **the pocketable sibling of the Weatherglass**
> (which reads by day, `field-instruments.md` §1 — the state at a glance). The
> Lantern reads the near field, at night, onto the ground you walk.

**The one-line distinction from every sibling instrument:**

| Instrument | It asks | It reads | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| The reader (`coherence-magic.md` §2) | *what is that?* | the published `q`/`ε²`/`∇(g·Φ)` aimed at a target | day |
| The Weatherglass (`field-instruments.md` §1.2) | *what is this, now, constantly?* | the same four channels at your position, glanceable | day |
| The Clock (`the-clock.md` §1) | *how fast is the field running here?* | the local `(1−q)` tempo as a hand | day/night |
| The Mirror (`the-mirror.md` §2) | *what am I to the field?* | your own maintenance, inward | day/night |
| The aurora (`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md` §3.3) | *what is the field doing, far?* | the far channels at sky scale | **night** |
| **The Lantern (this doc)** | ***what is the field doing, right here, in the dark?*** | **the local channels as glow, at your feet** | **night** |

The Lantern is the one instrument that *lives* in the night — the read that
exists specifically because the day instruments are pocketed and the aurora reads
too far. Everything it shows is the same published channels the Weatherglass
shows by day; the *placement* (night, at the ground) is what makes it a new
instrument and not a duplicate.

**The family rule, stated once.** The Lantern obeys it exactly
(`field-instruments.md` §2.1): it is a **consumer of the published channels at
the player's position with the Weatherglass's own glow idiom, re-pointed at the
ground, and shown at night.** It adds no channel, no physics, no write. It is
the piece of the base read idiom (`field-instruments.md` §2.2) that was missing —
the four forms, cast downward, at night (`§2` below).

## 3. Why night travel is different

The design's load-bearing claim, stated once:

> **The same field, read by the light you choose to carry — the Lantern makes
> night a *different read*, not the same read in the dark.**

This is not a night-filter over the day instruments. It is a *placement* that
changes what the same channels mean to a walker:

- **The safe path glows where you'd otherwise walk blind.** A night walker on a
  coherent ridge would, without the Lantern, walk the same ground the dark hides
  — the ridge and the scar read the same in darkness. With the Lantern, the
  high-`q` ground *lightens underfoot*: the safe path is *literally the bright
  line on the ground.* The near field's organization becomes the walker's road,
  not a distant read. (`field-instruments.md` §1.2's lume, cast down at night.)

- **The thin region's dimness warns before the clock's race matters.** The Clock
  reads a thin region's raced tempo (`the-clock.md` §3.1 — the vent backs up, the
  Coda's `τ` counts faster). At night, the Lantern reads the *same* thin `q` as
  **dimness first** — before the walker is walking the margin, before the clock's
  race is a cost. The fade underfoot is the advance warning the tempo would give
  only once you're committed to the thin ground. (`field-hazards.md` §3; the
  deficiency read, §2.)

- **The wrong-warm flicker is the Coda's approach *before* the Mirror's `R`
  climbs.** The Mirror's signature face (`the-mirror.md` §2.2) reads your own
  `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability` — but you consult a Mirror; it is an act,
  not a companion. The Lantern is always on: its wrong-warm lights the *ground
  the Coda's sound-hole hides* the moment you near it, before you think to read
  your own signature, before the accumulation closes. **The Lantern is the
  walker's personal bell** — the Bell's warning reach (`the-bell.md` §4 — the
  Bell-at-night) extended to the lone traveller who carries it, who has no
  bell-pit and no settlement to wake. (`signature-predator.md` §3; `the-silence.md`
  §2.3.)

**The honest boundary rests exactly here.** The channels that make these reads —
`q`, `ε²`, `(1−q)`, the Coda's legible trail — are **engine-real** and *already
published*. The **glow-mapping** — which channel reads as a steady bright, as a
fade, as a flicker, as a wrong-warm — is **this doc's [design]**, probe-calibrated
over the real channel values, and it is the instrument family's presentation
idiom (`field-instruments.md` §2.1). The Lantern never claims the night field is
*a different physics*; it claims the *same* field, read *differently because it
is night and you are carrying a light that tells the truth about it.* That is the
whole point of the design.

discipline (`the-clock.md` §5c, `the-silence.md` §6c, `the-mirror.md` §6c): the
Lantern adds no randomness. **[design]** The glow thresholds over the channel
values are designed constants; the channels they read are deterministic. **The
night is a reproducible, learnable, honest fact about the field at your feet** —
the safety of a path and the danger of a sound-hole are things you can rely on,
and then teach.

### (d) The no-free-energy cap — a lantern informs, never mints

`energy-harnessing.md` §6 (`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`), inherited through the
instrument family (`the-clock.md` §5d, `the-mirror.md` §6d, `the-bell.md` §6d)
and the danger layer's hazard-farming gate (`field-hazards.md` §5.3):

> **The Lantern informs, never mints.** It is a *read* (of the local channels at
> night) cast as light — it converts no coherence, sheds no `(1−q)`, writes
> nothing, and grants the player no power, budget, or recovery they did not earn
> in the field. There is **no light that harvests**: a glowing path is *not* a
> lamp that reaps the `q` it reveals; the Lantern's glow is a presentation of
> the field's own state, not a source, not a collector, not a battery.

Three honest consequences:
1. **No "lantern-to-farm."** Knowing the bright ridge is coherent does not let
   you draw coherence from it; the brightness is a read of existing `q`, bounded
   by what the field already provides, with no write-back that could mint
   (`energy-harnessing.md` §6; `coherence-magic.md` §5.1). A player cannot "light
   a path to extract q."
2. **The Lantern does not raise the field it reads.** A bright carried light
   does not *be* a coherent locality, any more than the Weatherglass raises a
   local `q` (`the-clock.md` §5d). It shows the field's `q`; it never adds to it.
   (The *Worn Field's* EY-shell — `worn-field.md` §2.1 — is the separate designed
   coupling that changes local `q` at the body, gated on Q4; the Lantern is not
   that. Pure read.)
3. **The wrong-warm is a warning, never a reward.** Near a Coda's sound-hole, the
   Lantern goes wrong-warm so you leave; it does not *pay* you for the proximity
   (the Coda sheds nothing a collector can tap — `signature-predator.md` §7e's
   no-free-energy discipline). The Lantern's only output is information and
   timing — the *where safe* and the *when wrong* — costed as information, never
   as energy. That is exactly why it is Phase-1-cheap and why it never corrupts
   the economy.

### (e) Accessibility — the glow is an enhancement of the read, never the only channel

The corpus's hard accessibility rule holds unchanged (`field-music.md` §6;
`field-instruments.md` §1.3):
