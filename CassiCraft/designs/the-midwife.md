### 2.1 The first read — the fresh run's maintenance window

**The landed pieces.** The Life-Signal's discriminating axis is **maintenance**:
a live thing *actively holds* its φ-lock against a drain; a fossil does not; a
wielding vent *raises* `ε²` on purpose; a scar is a fallen floor
([`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §1/§3.1). A fresh run is a new line whose
lock is being held (or not) by a body that just began ([`the-child.md`](./the-child.md)
§1). The classifier reads the four classes from **a maintenance window** — a
short time-series of the published channels at the publish cadence, the axes
real (motion, `(1−q)` cadence, `ε²` gradient, `∇(g·Φ)` alignment), the *decision
rule* designed and Phase-1 probe-calibrated ([`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md)
§3.2/§6a). **The first read the midwife performs is that maintenance read, at the
fresh run's first hour** (the-hour's window, `[t_0, t_0+T_h]` — the midwife's
[design] window over the same channels):

> **[design] The midwife reads the fresh run's first state — is the new line's
> lock strong, thin, or failing at its first hour?** Strong means the fresh
> run's maintenance signature holds: it moves or steers, its `(1−q)` glow pulses
> at the maintainer's cadence, its `ε²` sits flat-low — the body is *actively
> holding* its φ-lock. Thin means the read trends down — the pulse weakens, the
> `ε²` gradient rises slightly, the lock is held but not lively. Failing means
> the read crosses a designed floor — the run is not holding; at its first hour
> it reads toward spent or as a static residue. (Three "states," but one
> continuous maintenance read; the class thresholds are designed over the
> measured channels, exactly as `life-signal.md` §3.2's class boundaries.)

Each state reads a real, published quantity — the `q`/`ε²`/`(1−q)`/`∇(g·Φ)`/
position over the maintenance window (the ≈ 6 MiB publish, on the Weatherglass's
sample-cost profile, [`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md) §1.4 —
*one sample at the fresh run's position*, the same consumer discipline as the
Life-Signal's "an enhancement of the reader, never the only channel,"
[`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §4/§6c). The *window* is the load-bearing
form: a fresh run's strength is a maintained signature, not a single snapshot —
so the first read is a short time-series over the publish cadence, never a
one-glance judgment. **[design]** The first-read is the Life-Signal's maintenance
read pointed at the fresh run, probe-calibrated against the maintenance axis's
own noise-floor probe ([`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §6b).

### 2.3 The hand-off — the fresh run joined to the line

**The landed pieces.** The Family is a shared persistent-Π anchor — a designed

> **[design] The midwife hands the fresh run to the line — the newborn joined to

> line (§2.2), the midwife *presents* the received run to the settlement's anchor

> hand-off is the fresh run's **first hold in the society's book and the
> anchor's**: the child, whose whole field-history is from now on
> ([`the-child.md`](./the-child.md) §1) and who has no joint yet (§2.1 the four
> empty surfaces), is received *into* the anchor's — the joint's persistent-Π held

> ([`the-child.md`](./the-child.md) §4) a *received* truth rather than a latent

**The honest boundary of the hand-off.** The midwife does not *make* the joint —
the co-binding is the Family's own deliberate act ([`the-family.md`](./the-family.md)
§2.1), gated on the persistent-Π frontier; the hand-off *presents* the fresh run
to the anchor and reads the received hold, the way the gatekeeper reads an arrival

mints a joint where the field holds none — a family that stops holding sheds like

is the *receiving* of a real line into a real anchor, never a grant of one.
**[design]** The hand-off's *form* — the presentation act, the first hold's
