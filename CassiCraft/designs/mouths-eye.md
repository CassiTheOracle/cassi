**Question under design:** the gustatory third sense. The corpus has two senses —
sight (the coherence reader, the sky, the instruments) and sound (field music).
Nothing designs *taste* — yet eating is the most intimate field read there is:
**the mouth is inside the medium.** This document designs the Mouth's Eye in two
honestly-separated registers:

1. **Tasting** — a passive read. The mouth samples the local regime's `(ρ, q, ε²)`
   signature as a flavor: where you are, what's wrong, what's forming.
2. **Consuming** — a real act. Ingesting matter interacts with the player's
   coherence budget and the energy economy: the deep-rung reaper at body scale,

   economy — *a meal is fuel, never a mint.*

consumer of the published channels), and it ties the sensory triad to the
metabolic system: the player is a bounded EY injector and ε² vent
(`coherence-magic.md` §1) — **the Mouth's Eye is the sense *on the same channel as
the budget.***

## 1. The sense — tasting the local regime

### 1.1 A taste is a read

The mouth is different from the eye and the ear because **it is inside the
medium.** The reader (`coherence-magic.md` §2, Sense) is a tool you hold and aim;
sound (`field-music.md` §1) is the field you stand in, sampled at your locality;
taste is the field **at the point of your body**, read continuously and
positionally — you do not aim it, you are in it.

The read is a consumer of what the world already publishes. From
`corpus-reconciliation.md` (the ≈ 6 MiB publish: q, ε², ρ, pot, ∇(g·Φ)) and
`async-field-domain.md` §2.1, the sampler reads `(ρ, q, ε²)` at the player's
position every tick. A taste is a **sampled regime signature** at that point —
the same triple `material-regimes.md` §1 defines as a material's *state*:

```
ρ   = EY + EI                       density (how much field)
q   = ρ²/(ρ² + φ⁻² + ε²)            coherence (how φ-locked; attractor ≈ 0.947)
ε²  = (EY − φ·EI)²                  decoherence (the disorder channel)
```

A taste maps that point-in-state into a flavor. The mapping is **[design]** — a
*presentation* of real channels, exactly as `field-music.md` §1 maps the same
channels to pitch/dissonance/amplitude and `custom-blocks.md` §4 maps `(n, q, ε²)`
to color. The physics a taste reports is real; the flavor it wears is the designed
presentation.

**The single rule that keeps it honest:** *same regime, same taste.* `field-magic`
§6(e) — determinism: a region's flavor is a **deterministic read** of its regime.
A `(ρ, q, ε²)` point has exactly one taste, computed from the channels, with no
hidden randomness; the same field state at the mouth reads the same flavor every
time. This is inherited from the field's determinism (`field-archaeology.md` §1.2;
`field-npc-ai.md` §2.3's hard gate) and makes the taste *trustworthy as diagnosis*.

### 1.2 The taste-map — regime → flavor

The taste-map is **[design]** (presentation, not physics): the channels are
grounded, the flavor vocabulary is the aesthetic layer. The register is chosen for
the mouth: where the reader shows *bright/dark* and sound shows *consonant/dissonant
/silent*, taste shows *sweet/flat/bitter/thin*. The mapping below reads each channel
dimension to a gustatory dimension, then composes them into a regime's composite
flavor.

| Channel | Gustatory dimension | What it reports |
|---|---|---|
| **q** (coherence) | **body / presence / sweetness** | q is the organized fraction; a coherent (high-q) region reads *present*, *clean*, *bright*; a low-q region reads *thin*, *empty*. The attractor (`q ≈ 0.947`) is the tonic of taste — a well-locked field tastes *whole* the way it sounds consonant and glows clean. |
| **ε²** (decoherence) | **bitterness / dissonance / sharpness** | ε² is the disorder channel; a scar reads **bitter — dissonant, thin**, the same disorganizing quantity that is dissonance to the ear (`field-music.md` §1) and roughness to the eye (`custom-blocks.md` §4). |
| **ρ** (density) | **weight / richness** | ρ = EY + EI is how much field; a dense region tastes *heavy, rich* (more carrier), a sparse one *thin, insubstantial* — the gustatory twin of sound's amplitude (`field-music.md` §1: ρ → loudness). |
| **∂q/∂t (trend)** | **gathering vs. receding** | the *direction* of q — the state's trajectory, not just its point. A young condensation (q accumulating, held phase) tastes of *gathering* — a rising, held flavor; a receding region tastes of *draining*. |

**Composite flavors of the named regimes** (each a point-in-state from the docs):

| Regime | Field state (grounded) | Taste [design] |
|---|---|---|
| **Coherent ridge** | high q, low ε², the φ-attractor | **clean, bright, sweet/whole** — the field at its most organized. The tonic of taste. |
| **A scar / decoherence well** | high ε², low q (`energy-harnessing.md` §1.3; `field-hazards.md` §2) | **bitter, thin, dissonant** — the ε² flavor. You are tasting your own (or the world's) disorder. |
| **Young condensation** | q accumulating, crossing toward τ_c (`material-regimes.md` §3 ore; `field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.2 condensation front) | **gathering** — a rising, held flavor; coherence still forming, not yet settled. |
| **The desert** | regional q collapse, `q ≈ 1e-3…1e-1` at noise (`field-hazards.md` §3.1) | **flat, flavorless** — the gustatory silence. The desert tastes of *nothing* because it is a vacuum of coherence; the twin of field-music §2.3's silence ("where there is no coherence, there is no sound" — and no taste). |
| **Deep-rung matter** | deep `n`, ε² ≈ 0, stored coherence (`material-regimes.md` §5) | **dense, sweet, cold-matched** — the extreme flavor: maximal order, maximal stored coherence, the richest (and most dangerous-to-eat-wrong) taste. |
| **Fire regime / explosive** | the two-regime flavor on the tongue, §4 | **warm, spreading, controlled** (fire) vs **brittle, near-perfect, primed** (explosive) — a store that tastes "weaponized." |

> **The read is a consumer of the publish; the flavor is the presentation.**
> Tasting does not invent a channel, an ε² meter, or hidden field state — it
> presents the `(ρ, q, ε²)` snapshot the sampler already reads, the way the reader
> and the ambience do. It is Phase-1 because it consumes only what
> `corpus-reconciliation.md` already budgets (the ≈ 6 MiB publish).

## 2. Tasting as navigation and diagnosis

### 2.1 The taste completes the triad's coverage

The three senses divide the presented field by how it is read:

| Sense | How it reads | Instrument | Register |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sight** (reader, sky, instruments) | **aimed** — a tool you hold and point | `coherence-magic.md` §2 Sense; the sky; the Weatherglass / Far Mirror (`field-instruments.md`) | the reader, the aurora, the overlay |
| **Sound** (field music) | **ambient** — the field you stand in, sampled at your locality + key structures | `field-music.md` §1 | the hum, the growl, the silence |
| **Taste** (Mouth's Eye) | **positional** — the field at the point of your body, never aimed | this doc | the flavor |

The reader is a *survey* instrument (wide, aimed, overlapping); sound is a
*peripheral* instrument (hands-free, range-reaching, ambient); taste is the
*micro* instrument — the one that reads **exactly where you are**, without any
tool, continuously, the moment you are in a place. A player never has to glance at
an overlay or listen for a cue to know "this is a scar" — it is already in the
mouth.

**The triad rule (`field-music.md` §6) holds:** taste is an **enhancement, never
the only channel.** Every diagnostic a taste gives (a scar, a desert, a storm, a
residue, a live creature) is also readable on the reader overlay and the sky. A
player who does not have the taste sense on (or cannot use it) loses *nothing* the
docs already give; a player with it on gets the same information more intimately
and positionally. This is a hard accessibility rule, inherited unchanged from
`field-music.md` §6 — taste is the third presentation of one publish, not a
required fourth.

## 3. Consuming — the act

Tasting is passive: it reads the local `(ρ, q, ε²)` and costs nothing (a rung-0
read, like the reader). **Consuming is a real act on the same channel as the
budget** — it ingests matter, and ingesting matter is a field operation with a
budget, a vent, and an economy.

### 3.1 Consuming is the deep-rung reaper at body scale

`energy-harnessing.md` §2.5 defines the **deep-rung reaper** — a precision tool at
a chosen rung that withdraws the field, releasing ordered matter's stored
coherence. The Mouth's Eye extends that machine to the player's body:

> **Consuming deep-rung matter is the deep-rung reaper's act at body scale.**
> Where the reaper is a tool's withdrawal of ordered field, eating is the body's
> withdrawal of the same stored coherence — a deep-rung store `('deep-rung
> matter = stored coherence', energy-harnessing.md §1.5/§3) taken into the player,
> feeding the coherence budget.

This is the **most intimate use of the found-economy** (`custom-blocks.md` §6):
ingesting a found material is its deepest use — more than crafting with it, more
than shipping it, you take its stored coherence *into yourself*. A material's
flavor IS its regime read (§1) — so **eating is, at once, reading the material and
spending it.** The found-economy's crown (archaeology's found-only fossils,
`field-archaeology.md` §4.1) has its most intimate end here: the rarest, most
ordered regime the world keeps is also the one a connoisseur might eat.

### 3.2 A meal is a withdrawal, never a mint

The constraint economy of `energy-harnessing.md` §6 is the hard rule consuming
inherits unchanged:

> **A meal is fuel, never a mint.** No conversion yields more than it sinks;
> `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` (the theory's own ratio) is the amplitude cap. Eating
> withdraws *already-ordered* coherence into the budget — it does not create it.

Concretely, consuming is bounded by the same three legs the energy economy states:
consuming deep-rung matter releases the *stored* coherence (a withdrawal that
de-orders what was eaten — `energy-harnessing.md` §2.5: reaping de-orders the
local field); the `(1−q)` waste applies to the eaten store as it is taken in (a
part of what you eat is shed as vent/glow, exactly the waste law `E_waste =
(1−q)·E_throughput`); and the charge/scar asymmetry holds — charging (ordering) is
slow and density-costly, discharging (eating) is fast and de-orders. You cannot eat
your way to more coherence than the world holds; **the meal only moves stored order
from the field into you, minus the `(1−q)` toll.**

### 3.3 The fire-vs-explosive line on the tongue

`material-regimes.md` §3 names the boundary `coherence-magic.md` §4.3 keys off:
**controlled organizational discharge = fire (fuel); uncontrolled full-cascade
discharge = blast.** This is the same boundary's *most intimate* instance, at the
body:

> **A near-φ-locked store (deep `n`, ε² ≈ 0) is explosive-primed.** `material-regimes.md`
> §3: an explosive is "deep-rung, ε² ≈ 0, near-perfectly-φ-locked matter held far
> from equilibrium against its attractor; perturbing it lets the stored coherence
> discharge as a full-cascade collapse." Consuming one **wrong** — biting into a
> deep, ε²≈0 store without the controlled-release act — is the **overdraw boundary
> on the tongue** (`coherence-magic.md` §4.3: overdraw → full-cascade discharge).
> A **controlled release is a meal** (the stored coherence feeds the budget),
> an **uncontrolled one is a blast from inside** (the φ-lock fails and the
> accumulated coherence relaxes as a full-cascade discharge *within the eater*).

This is the hunter's/forager's sharpest judgment: the same store that is the
richest meal is a full-cascade bomb if consumed wrong. The **controlled-release
act** — how the player *eats* a deep-rung store (the coherence-magic gesture that
withdraws at the store's own rung rather than perturbing it blindly, the same
discipline as `field-archaeology.md` §5.3's safe-reap vs. wrong-tool-rung) — is
the difference between fire and blast on the tongue. The fire-vs-explosive rate
(`material-regimes.md` §5) is not a material flag; it is a **discharge rate on the
same mechanism**, and consuming is where the player *is* the mechanism.

### 3.4 Consuming gates on the Q4 write lane

Like every channeled op and every machine perturbation, **consuming is a consumer
of the Q4 player-return channel** (`async-field-domain.md` §7 Q4; `coherence-magic.md`
§5.1): the meal's "withdraw stored coherence at rung n" and its ε²-vent round-trip
into the domain as **job-dict inputs**, exactly like condense/dissolve/heal and
every machine. It cannot exist before the Q4 schema and cadence are resolved
(`coherence-magic.md` §5.1 Q1/Q2). Tasting (a read) is Phase-1; **consuming (a
write) is gated on Q4 like every energy act** — the same statement `field-music.md`
§6 makes of music-as-field-operation and `player-remains.md` §5(b) makes of the
re-lock cost.

## 4. The metabolic ties — the taste on the budget channel

The Mouth's Eye is the sense **on the same channel as the budget** because the
player's metabolism is `coherence-magic.md` §1's bounded-EY-injector-and-ε²-vent
identity, and eating is the most direct way to feed or unbalance it.

### 4.1 Eating = the budget's recharge channel, and its danger

| The metabolic tie | Mechanism | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| **Recharge** | consuming *ordered* matter feeds the coherence budget B: deep-rung stored coherence → B, the "pull B up" mechanism of `coherence-magic.md` §1.2 made *ingestive*. A meal is stored order taken into you; the budget recovers as the withdrawable coherence arrives. | `energy-harnessing.md` §1.5/§3 (stored coherence); `coherence-magic.md` §1.2 (recovery) |
| **Danger** | consuming *disorder* — a scar, a decoherence well, a wrong deep-rung bite — loads ε² onto the vent. The ε² vent (`coherence-magic.md` §1: the player is an ε² vent; channeling sheds decoherence) backs up; overdraw risk rises. **Eating the wrong thing is the overdraw boundary on the tongue** (§3.3). | `coherence-magic.md` §1.2 (ε² debt, overdraw → §4.3) |
| **Cost** | the `(1−q)` toll on every meal (a part is shed as vent/glow), the charge/scar asymmetry. Eating is a withdrawal that de-orders, never a free refill. | `energy-harnessing.md` §2/§3; §6 no-free-energy |

The mouth is where the economy's constraint is felt most directly: **you are a
bounded heat engine whose fuel is the field's organization, and the same act that
recharges you can overdraw you.** No field system (the player included) is a
coherence mint — `energy-harnessing.md` §6's hard rule is the metabolic wall.

### 4.2 The player as the ε² vent, tasting its own load

Because the player is an ε² vent, the Mouth's Eye reads a **second, self-referential**
signal: the taste of one's own vent load. Standing in a scar while low on budget,
or after overdraw, the taste carries the player's own shed ε² — the mouth tasting
the disorder the body has vented into the medium around it. This is the taste-anchor
of `field-hazards.md` §2.4's vent discipline: **channeling into a scar adds to the
local disorder and the taste darkens with your own vent; you feel the overdraw in
the mouth before the reader flags it** — the gustatory twin of field-music's
"hearing overdraw coming (the rising dissonance of the vent backing up)".

### 4.3 The taste of the tide — the field's flavor seasons with the season

`tide-of-the-attractor.md` is the sense's literal seasoning: the mixing clock
`T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]` (theory-sourced) breathes a region's operating q between
**harvest** (`q` high, `ε²` low, near-lossless) and **thin** (`q` toward the noise
floor, machines glow `(1−q)`-wasteful, the desert-margin creeps). Because the
Mouth's Eye reads the local `(ρ, q, ε²)`, **the same mouth reads richer at harvest
and thinner at trough**:

| Tide phase | The tasted field | The player's meal-read |
|---|---|---|
| **Harvest** | high `q`, low `ε²` — the field organized | flavors are **rich, clean, sweet**; stored coherence is present to be eaten; meals are cheap and near-lossless at the `(1−q)` floor |
| **Thin** | `q` low, `ε²` high — the field running wasted | flavors **thin, wasted**; the `(1−q)` toll on every meal rises (the same meal wastes more); deep-rung stores are scarcer and the desert-margin's flatness creeps |

The tide is quoted as the **season** that literally seasons the field — a connoisseur
reads the phase by the mouth the way a vintner reads a vintage. And the hazard-gate
holds: the tide is a *planning aid*, never a free-energy source (`tide-of-the
-attractor.md` §5d) — **a harvest feast is the field's organized state, not a mint;
a thin-trough fast is the same store at a worse `(1−q)` toll.**

> **Cross-reference `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2.** The tide's *mechanics* (the
> regional-q oscillation / drift, the harvest-vs-thin states, the probes) live in
> that doc. This doc fixes the gustatory form: **the tide is the field's seasoning —
> the same mouth reads the harvest as rich and the trough as thin.** The `(1−q)`
> waste of each meal follows the tide, because `(1−q)` is literally the field's
> state at the mouth.

### 4.4 The diet maps the hazards

Because a taste is a deterministic read, the player's *diet* is a hazard diagnostic
in reverse — what you can eat tells you where you are, and what the field provides
tells you whether it is healthy:

| Food source | What eating it reports | The hazard face |
|---|---|---|
| **Ordered matter (deep-rung ore, a healthy creature, a coherent store)** | the field is organized here; a meal is available and net-positive-after-`(1−q)` | a livable region |
| **A scar's disorder** | bitter — and eating it loads ε², never feeds | a wounded field you should not eat from |
| **The desert's "nothing"** | **flat — there is nothing to eat and no coherence to draw on.** The survival layer's sharpest face: the desert is where you *starve* because the field has no order to taste or take. | the gustatory face of `field-hazards.md` §3 — the hazard a desert *is* is partly "the player cannot live here" |
| **A nearby storm's rising ε²** | a rising bitter edge on the tongue | the readable-before-it-arrives of §2.3 |

**The survival layer: eating the field is how a player lives in it.** The
coherence budget refills where the local field is coherent (`coherence-magic.md`
§1.2: recovery is geographic); eating is the *active* channel of that recovery —
the moment you take the field's order into the budget rather than merely standing in
it. In the desert, **both gates close at once**: the flat taste warns you there is
nothing coherent, and there is nothing to eat. The survival face of the hazard is
that the player's *metabolism* and the local field's *coherence* are the same map.

## 5. Player-facing

### 5.1 The hunter's sense — read before you hunt

`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2/§5.2: a creature is a **moving coherence store** —
a self-supporting, φ-locked body held at a cascade depth, glowing `(1−q)`-waste as
a health-meter. The Mouth's Eye is the **hunter's tasting sense**: because a taste
reads the local `(ρ, q, ε²)` and an organism is exactly that, in motion,
**tasting one is reading it** — its rung-depth (the flavor's richness), its

### 5.3 The connoisseur's economy — regimes as flavors

The found-economy (`custom-blocks.md` §6) distinguishes *finding* (cheap, read-only,
bounded by what the field has made) from *inventing* (expensive, spent coherence,

**tasting culture**: regimes are not just substances to craft or ship — they are
**flavors to know**. A connoisseur reads the world by mouth and catalogues regimes
as a wine list:

- A found regime's flavor is its regime read (§1) — **the connoisseur's map is the

- The rarest flavors — deep-rung, found-only fossils (`field-archaeology.md` §4.1)

The distinction holds: **tasting (read) is free and does not spend a material;
consuming (act) is the deepest use and spends it.** The connoisseur chooses,
per flavor, whether to *know* it (read in place, like archaeology's leave-in-place,
`field-archaeology.md` §5.3 #3) or *take it in* (consume, the most complete and most
destructive knowledge).

`field-npc-ai.md` §2.3. **Grounded.** This is the Phase-1 slice.

spectral character (`field-music.md` §2.2) and the aurora's colors

### (c) Consuming gates on the energy/material economics — a withdrawal, never a mint

Consuming is a **real act on the Q4 write lane** (§3.4) — it cannot exist before

Phase-1; consuming is gated on Q4 and the material lines.**

The triad rule (`field-music.md` §6), inherited unchanged: every diagnostic a taste

## 8. Feasibility verdict

**Phase-1 — feasible, no new physics: tasting as positional diagnosis.** A taste is
a read of the published `(ρ, q, ε²)` snapshot the sampler already reads every tick
(`corpus-reconciliation.md`: ≈ 6 MiB publish). It adds no channel, no new physics,
no Q4 write — **it is a pure consumer**, the gustatory twin of the Phase-1 sense-read
path (`coherence-magic.md` §5.1) and of field-music's Phase-1 ambience
(`field-music.md` §7). The taste-map is a **[design]** presentation over honest
mappings (the same discipline as sound's and color's), and it is deterministic
(same regime, same taste). The Phase-1 deliverable is the read-only slice: the
mouth as a positional diagnostic — where you are, what's wrong, what's forming — on
the exact budget profile of the existing reader.

**Later — consuming, the metabolic channel (gated).** Consuming ingests matter and
interacts with the coherence budget and energy economy — a **real act on the Q4
write lane** (§3.4), gated on the same open plumbing as every channeled op/machine
(`coherence-magic.md` §5.1; `async-field-domain.md` Q4), and bounded by the energy
economy's no-free-energy cap and the material lines' fire-vs-explosive boundary. It
is the deep-rung reaper at body scale (`energy-harnessing.md` §2.5), the found
-economy's most intimate use (`custom-blocks.md` §6), and the overdraw boundary on
the tongue (`coherence-magic.md` §4.3). The diet-metabolic ties (§4) — eating as the
budget's recharge channel and its danger — are designable now in concept; the
*mechanic* is gated on Q4 and the material gates, exactly like energy's machines and
magic's channeling.

**The sensory-triad completion.** The corpus's two senses — sight (the reader, the
sky, the instruments) and sound (field music) — become **three presentations of one
publish**: the same `(ρ, q, ε²)` the reader renders, the sky reads, and the hum
sounds, the Mouth's Eye *tastes*. The triad divides the field by how it is read —
the reader *aims*, sound *surrounds*, taste *positions* — and every register is an
enhancement of the shared publish, never a new or only channel (the triad rule,
`field-music.md` §6). **Tasting is the Phase-1 hand the sensory triad stands on;
consuming is the phase-by-phase act that ties the senses to the metabolic system —
the player is a bounded EY injector and ε² vent, and the Mouth's Eye is the sense
on the same channel as the budget.**

**Binding risks, in order:** (1) **Q4** — consuming, the gated half, is a Q4-op
consumer exactly like every energy act; until the schema/cadence resolve, only
tasting is real. (2) **The taste-map's aesthetic discipline** — keeping every
[design] flavor choice honest against the grounded channel-reads (the same
discipline the corpus practices for sound and color). (3) **The classifier line**
— a storm's rising ε² taste vs. a static scar, the desert's flatness vs. a thin
tide's wastefulness, a live creature's read vs. your own residue's: each must land
on the same distinguishability criterion as the ear and the eye
(`field-music.md` open-Q2; `field-archaeology.md` §7 open-Q3; `tide-of-the
-attractor.md` open-Q3). (4) **The fire-vs-blast threshold at body scale** — the
one [design] cap, applied to the mouth, that can break if a near-φ-locked meal is
consumed wrong. None contradicts the async, dual-world, or regime-collapse
architecture — tasting is the least invasive sense in the corpus because it *adds
no physics*; it only presents the channels the mouth already swims in.
