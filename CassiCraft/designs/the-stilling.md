## 2. The inner hold — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 The maintenance-axis hold, applied to oneself

`life-signal.md` §1/§3 fixes the discriminating axis of a *live* structure:
**maintenance** — "a live thing actively holds its φ-lock against a drain; a fossil
does not" (§1). A living body holds `ε²` low and its `(1−q)` cadence pulses at the
maintainer's rhythm (the read's shape, §3/§3.2); a fossil is static and dark. The
Stilling is that same maintenance axis **made deliberate and turned inward**:

> **The inner hold.** The Stilling is the maintenance-axis hold applied to one's own
> body — the practitioner deliberately holds their `ε²` low and their `q` steady, the
> reader turned inward, for a bounded window. It is the body's *ordinary* maintenance
> (the lock every live body holds against the drain) done **as the whole act**:
> motionless, quiet, at will — not the day's scattered holding while the body acts,
> but the deliberate act of holding, the reader reading its own held read
> (`life-signal.md` §3 — the read's shape: the `(1−q)` cadence, the `ε²` gradient, the
> maintained lock).

This is the anchor of the whole practice: **a Stilling is the live-but-idle body's
act, made voluntary.** Sleep (the shallow cousin) is the live-but-idle state the body
falls into to *refill* ([`sleep.md`](./sleep.md) §1/§2b — the body at rest, the
persistent-Π pattern re-tensioning, the vent settling, the budget refilling). The
Stilling is the *deliberate, waking* instance of that same held state — the body
motionless, `ε²` held low, the lock held steady — taken **without** the refill. It
holds the reservoir steady for a window; it never refills it (§4's no-free-energy,
§2.3 the waste held).

**The honest tier, drawn exactly (after §5a):** the maintenance axis — that a live
body holds `ε²` low and `q` steady and reads as a maintained lock — is
[`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md)'s [design]-over-engine-real layer (the axes read
real channels; the class boundaries are designed/probe-calibrated, `life-signal.md`
§6a). **The maintenance-axis hold itself, applied to oneself and made deliberate at
will — the `ε²`-low/`q`-steady window, its bounded form, its cost — is this doc's
[design]** over that axis. The *channels* the hold reads (`ε²`, `(1−q)`, `q`) are
engine-real (the ≈ 6 MiB publish, cited).

### 2.2 The window — the hold's bounded form

A Stilling is **bounded** — a voluntary hold for a finite window, then released. The
window is the stilling's *form* ([design]), over the maintenance-axis read's own

| **The release** | the hold ends as it began — deliberately, at will; the body returns to ordinary activity | [design] — the release is as deliberate as the hold |

The window is **[design]** — a designed dial over the maintenance-axis read, the same
tier as the vigil's watch-duration ([`the-vigil.md`](./the-vigil.md) §5b, the
hourglass's sand) and sleep's overnight window ([`sleep.md`](./sleep.md) §6b). The
*honest* bound on it is the corpus's own: a Stilling held **too long** is where the
practice's edge lives (§3 — the drift toward the stillness).

### 2.3 The waste held and spent — the voluntary hold is never free

The Stilling holds the body's `(1−q)` **low while it holds** — the deliberate quiet
is a *withdrawal from acting*, not a new op. In the corpus's energy register
([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §2 — the `(1−q)` waste law,
`E_waste = (1−q)·E_throughput`):

> **The voluntary hold is spent, never free.** A body that holds itself still spends
> the same `(1−q)` any maintained lock spends — the deliberate quiet is the field's
> ordinary waste held steady, not a conduit that cancels it. The Stilling does not
> *stop* the body's waste; it *deliberately holds* it low and steady for a window, at
> the same real cost the body always pays to keep its lock. **A Stilling is not a
> resting that banks, and not a tuning that cancels — it is a hold, and a hold costs.**
> The one thing the hold does *not* do is refill: unlike sleep's recovery
> ([`sleep.md`](./sleep.md) §1/§2b — the vent settles, the budget refills), a Stilling
> **refills nothing** (§4's cap) — it holds the reservoir level for a window, and
> ends with the reservoir exactly where it was (or slightly spent), never refilled.

This is the load-bearing honesty of the inner hold: **the Stilling practices what the
corpus already knows a live body does — but at will, deliberately, and at the ordinary
cost.** It is not a free quiet; it is the honest, deliberately-chosen quiet of the
body holding its own lock.

## 3. The drift toward the stillness — the honest edge

The corpus's hazard stillness ([`the-stillness.md`](./the-stillness.md)) is the
**field at absolute rest** — a high-`q` region so perfectly held its channels go flat

the first perturbation is the first event in a place with no event-history, the region
least practiced at closing a wound. **The Stilling's honest edge is that a too-deep
Stilling drifts toward that hazard** — the voluntary quiet, held too long or in the
wrong place, becomes the field's own static:

> **A too-deep Stilling drifts toward the stillness-hazard.** The deliberate hold —
> `ε²` low, `q` steady, the reader inward — is the *same flatness* the stillness reads
> as its resolved end (`the-stillness.md` §2 — the channels at their flat limits). A
> Stilling is a *voluntary* quiet the practitioner holds and releases at will; a
> stillness is a *condition* the field holds. **The line between them is the line
> between *your* quiet and *the field's* quiet — and the practitioner who stills beside
> the stillness's region, or who stills too long and lets the hold deepen past the
> deliberate, drifts across it.** The voluntary hold, held past its window or in the
> field's own resolved region, stops being a hold the body keeps and becomes the field's
> static that keeps the body.

**The honest framing — the drift is a real risk, never a punishment.** The corpus's
stillness is not a passive refuge and its flatness is the sign of how far it has not
been tested ([`the-stillness.md`](./the-stillness.md) §4.3 — "the first perturbation

## 4. The practice, not a power — and the no-free-energy cap

### 4.1 A Stilling is a practice, not a power

The load-bearing honesty of the whole design:

> **A Stilling is a practice, not a power.** It holds your own read steady for a
> window, **nothing more** — no q-gain, no ε²-clear, no invisibility. The deliberate
> hold converts nothing ([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §6 — the
> no-free-energy cap, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`): holding yourself still does not *earn*
> coherence, does not *clear* your ε², does not *stealth* your body. A Stilling is the
> honest act of *holding*, and it yields exactly what a hold yields — a held, steady,
> legible state, at the real cost of holding it.

The three things a Stilling is explicitly **not**:
- **No q-gain.** The deliberate hold does not raise the body's `q` or the local field's
  `q`. It holds the body's `q` *steady* at the value the field supports — nothing is
  manufactured. (The stilling that "charges you up" would be a free-energy mint, closed
  by §4.3 / `energy-harnessing.md` §6.)
- **No ε²-clear.** The hold does not *clear* the body's shed `ε²` faster than the
  field's own heal rate `h`. Unlike sleep (the vent settles during the rest,
  [`sleep.md`](./sleep.md) §1), a Stilling spends its window holding `ε²` *low steady* —
  it does not heal it. Clearing is the field's heal, over time; the Stilling is the
  held-read during that time, never a substitute for it. (A stilling that "heals you"
  faster than the field would be a mint.)
- **No invisibility.** The deliberate quiet does not hide the body. **The honest cost
  of the practice is the quiet itself** (§4.2).

### 4.2 The honest cost — a stilled body reads quieter, and quiet is what the Coda reads

The corpus's trail-read is `signature-predator.md` §2 — the Coda accumulates toward a
clean trail in a field with no order to hide it: `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability`,
where `ρ_signature` is the trail quality (the vent's `ε²` elevation × phase
coherence) and the trail is *legible* in a flat margin ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md)
§1.2 — a coherent ridge drowns the trail; the desert makes it the only order). The
Stilling's honest cost sits exactly there:

> **A stilled body reads quieter — and quiet is what the Coda reads.** The Stilling is
> a deliberately clean, low-`ε²`, quiet presence — the very signature the Coda
> accumulates on in a flat margin. A practiced Stilling is **not a stealth mechanic**:
> the body's quiet is the *same quiet* the hunter reads, the same clean thing the
> vigil's clean signature is (`the-vigil.md` §3 — the clean-signature exposure), the
> same tell the readable-trail slice names (`signature-predator.md` §8). **The Stilling
> does not make you harder to hunt; it makes you the cleanest possible read to the
> thing that hunts clean reads.** Its quiet is legible, honest, and priced in the only
> currency a still body has: its own stillness as exposure.

This is the **practice-not-power** honesty made load-bearing: a player who stills to
*hide* from the Coda finds the stilling is precisely the quiet the Coda reads. **A
Stilling is not a way to be hunted less; it is a deliberate quiet that is *more*
legible to the hunter of quiet** — the exact honest trade the corpus's trail-read
designs (and the exact cost the vigil already pays for keeping a clean signature,
`the-vigil.md` §3). The contrast with the fall, drawn once: the fall is the body
*owned* by the field's gradient ([`the-fall.md`](./the-fall.md) §2 — no `(1−q)` to
soften it); the Stilling is the body *holding* its own field steady — and a held
steady quiet is, to the Coda, the cleanest legible thing there is.

### 4.3 The no-free-energy cap — a Stilling converts nothing

`energy-harnessing.md` §6 inherited unchanged, the same hard rule every practice holds
(the vigil, the working-song, the husbander):

> **A Stilling converts nothing — no quiet that yields, never a mint.** The deliberate
> hold does not create coherence, does not refill the budget, does not clear the vent,
> does not hide the body. It is spent, never free, in exactly the `(1−q)` sense §2.3
> names: the hold costs the waste it holds. **There is no "still to gain," no "still to
> recover faster," no "still to vanish"** — the stilling's entire content is the held
> state, and a held state yields nothing. And the **drift is a real risk, never a
> punishment**: the too-deep stilling's cost (the body held by the field's static, §3)
> is a *consequence of the honest edge*, not a designed penalty — it is the same
> brittleness the stillness's own region holds (the-stillness §4.3), reached by a
> voluntary hold that crossed the deliberate line.
