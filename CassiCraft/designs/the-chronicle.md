## 1. The Chronicle, stated once

The corpus has the oracle's forward read (the-reading-ahead — prophecy as reading
the field's momentum), the society's net numbers (shared-ledger — per-member
`C(M)` off the Q4 op-stream), and the arc (fate-of-a-window — a window's four-arc
trajectory). It has no *record*: the settlement's story as an object, the backward
narrative the ledger's book and the oracle's omens frame.

> **The Chronicle is the settlement's record made narrative.** Not the Board's net
> `C(M)` but *what actually happened*: the stewards who held the core and the
> margin-causers who drained it (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2/§2.2), the storms endured
> vs caused (`weather-not-storm.md` — the provenance told as plot), the dead and
> their fossils (`player-remains.md`, `field-archaeology.md` — the obituary), the
> harvests and thin decades (`tide-of-the-attractor.md`, `fate-of-a-window.md` —
> the arc that was lived). It is the shared-ledger's book *told* — the same record
> the Board sums and the oracle projects, narrated instead of booked.

The Chronicle is what makes a settlement **a place with a past you can walk into**
rather than a set of live channels. Every other meta-layer reads the *present* or
the *near-future* of a field; the Chronicle reads the *lived* — the op-stream's
already-written shape, told backward. The oracle reads the field's becoming; the
Chronicle reads the record's already-was. They are the same read in opposite time
directions (the-reading-ahead's archaeology-twinship, §2.1, mirrored once more:
archaeology reads what the law *kept*, the oracle reads what the law is *building*,
and **the Chronicle reads what the settlement *did***).

### The subject — a settlement, not a window

The Chronicle's subject is the **settlement** — the collective of players and
named structures (`shared-ledger.md` §2, the board of members; `the-name.md` §3.2
the named contribution) read across the Q4 op-stream's `member` field
(`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1). It is not the window's bare `C(W)` trajectory
(`fate-of-a-window.md` §1); it is that trajectory *with a subject and a cast* —
the window's economics told as who held the core, who drained it, who died, who
harvested, who endured a storm. A lone window with one player has a thin chronicle;
a settled window with names and a book has a story. **[design]** The *settlement*
(social) read vs the *window* (structural) read — the Chronicle fixes the former,
fate-of-a-window the latter; both read the same record at two scales.

## 2. The composition — a long coherence score

### 2.1 A Chronicle is a read of the already-written op-stream

The Chronicle obeys the corpus's family rule exactly as the Board does
(`field-instruments.md` §2.1; `shared-ledger.md` §6a):

> **Every instrument in this family is a consumer of the same publish with a
> presentation idiom — never a new channel. The Chronicle is a consumer of the Q4
> op-stream with a narrative idiom; it adds no telemetry, no channel, no physics.**

It reads the **settled record** `{member, op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}`
(`schema-that-settles.md` §2) — the same single-writer stream the Board aggregates
and the oracle projects (`the-reading-ahead.md` §2.2's trend-and-threshold lens) —
and the field's published channels (the ≈ 6 MiB publish: `q` 1 + pot 1 +
`∇(g·Φ)` 3 + ρ 1, `corpus-reconciliation.md` §2), which carry the field-state
context (the bath's `q`, the desert-margin's `ε²`) the narrative frames. The
Chronicle is the *telling* of that already-written record — the same honesty the
Board gets from the single-writer lane (`shared-ledger.md` §6d: no forged
contributions, the ledger is exactly the domain's executed op record) extended to
the story.

### 2.2 A Chronicle is a composed record

Because the world is field-music (§4 of field-music), the Chronicle is not a
report; it is a **score**:

> **A coherence score is a timed sequence of Q4 op-records** (`field-music.md`
> §4.1). The Chronicle composes the settlement's *real, already-written* op-stream
> into a long coherence score that, **re-played**, re-plays the settlement's actual
> operations as a remembered song — the oracle's forward momentum turned backward.
> The oracle reads the field's organized momentum forward (`the-reading-ahead.md`
> §1.1, §2.2); the Chronicle reads the same record's organized *past* backward, and
> composes it as music.

The honest scope of the claim, stated exactly as field-music states its own
(§4.3): **the Chronicle is a *read* of the record, not a new engine act.** Replaying
a chronicle does not *re-execute* the settlement's ops (that would be writing, and
would double-spend their coherence under the no-free-energy cap — §6d). It *plays
them* — the way field-music's `synth_design.md` precedent sonifies the published
channels with a φ-tempered bank (a cascade meter over `q`'s rung-scale
box-difference energies, ~5% CPU, `corpus-reconciliation.md` and the verified
`CassiCosmos/research/meshless/synth_design.md`). A chronicle is the settlement's
op-history *heard*: the sustained healer books as one long `sustain = true` note, a
margin-causer's repeated overdraw as a rising dissonant line, a steward's harvest
as a consonant phrase — because the ops *are* field operations with φ-sounds
(`field-music.md` §3: condense = a chord, heal = a resolution, overdraw = feedback).

**The composition is a pure function of the op-stream.** Determinism (§6c) is what
makes a Chronicle a *true* record and a *stable* replay: same op-stream → same
Chronicle, every time, across every run. The player can re-read the settlement's
sequence exactly as it was, the way they re-read a fixed fossil (`player-remains.md`
§5e).

### 2.3 The presentation idiom — three forms, one read

Following the instrument idiom, the Chronicle has **[design]** three forms, all the
same read of the record:

| Form | What it is | Idiom |
|---|---|---|
| **The room** | a built structure (a chronicle-hall, the settlement's memory) that *renders* the record as a walkable spatial narrative — the named structures, the strata, the settlements' places (`the-name.md` §3) | the family rule's *presentation* form: a consumer with a spatial idiom, never a channel |
| **The song** | the composed coherence score (§2.2) — the settlement's ops heard backward, per-season, per-member | the field-music idiom: the same channels, the audible form |
| **The reading** | a "tell me the settlement's story" surface — the narrative read-back of who did what, when, and what the field did in return | the collective's own book, spoken |

A player with all three surfaces off loses nothing *informational* — every event
the Chronicle names is readable, more laboriously, from the Board (`shared-ledger.md`
§2), the reader, the sky, and the sound (`field-music.md` §6's accessibility rule;
`weather-not-storm.md` §6d). The Chronicle is a layer, never the only channel.

**The record's room (reverse pointer).** The Chronicle's **room** form (line 191 —
"a chronicle-hall, the settlement's memory") is the home of the newest memory-composition:
[`the-memory-palace.md`](./the-memory-palace.md) §2.2 builds its **chronicle room**
on this record — the settlement's story *read aloud* in the palace, a record-shaped

The two docs now cite each other as read-and-answered: this representation is the
palace's chronicle room, and the palace is the record's built, walkable memory.

## 3. The narrative elements — each a designed reading of the real record

The Chronicle is honest because it is the record — it tells only what the op-stream
and the published channels actually hold, with the record's own attribution and the
corpus's misattribution guards. Each narrative element below is a *designed reading*
of a real quantity.

### 3.1 The stewards who held the core, and the margin-causers who drained it

The Board's four member-identities (`shared-ledger.md` §2.2 — steward-of-the-tide,
neutral-middle, margin-causer, dissolved) *are* the Chronicle's cast, told as story
rather than booked as a net. The Chronicle does not render `C(M)` as a number; it
composes each member's op-line across the tide-cycles into a character:

| Member-identity | Board read (`shared-ledger.md` §2.2) | The Chronicle tells |
|---|---|---|
| **Steward-of-the-tide** | strongly positive `C(M)` — holds the high-`q` core up | a sustained, consonant figure — the healer who held the bath through the thin trough, told as a held `sustain = true` line (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.2) |
| **Neutral-middle** | `C(M)` near zero — builds and wounds | a quiet, mid-tone presence — the honest majority, neither upholding nor gutting the commons |
| **Margin-causer** | persistently negative `C(M)` — drains the core toward the desert | a rising-dissonance line — the repeated overdraws that pushed the margin, told with the record's *per-tide net over many cycles* (`shared-ledger.md` §3.3's attrition guard: never a single event) |
| **Dissolved** | the member's line closed against the bath on death | an obituary — see §3.3 |

**The attribution is the record's own.** The member-id is attached at the
world-writer's single, non-forgeable write (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1), so the
Chronicle's "who held the core / who drained it" is exactly the ops the world
executed — the same no-forge strength the Board gets (`shared-ledger.md` §6d). The
**same-member-line rule across death** (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1.1) gives the
Chronicle continuous subjects: a long steward's arc survives their deaths as one
line, so the story of who held the core is told once, not fragmented into
unbookable stubs.

### 3.2 The storms endured vs caused — the provenance, told as plot

The Chronicle narrates the danger layer's honesty (`weather-not-storm.md`): it tells
the storms the settlement *endured* (weather — the field's own self-limiting
extreme, happens *where the field is*, §2.1) separately from the storms it *caused*
(an aimed event — happens *where the player is*, §2.2). The distinction is exactly
the provenance classifier's derived read over the storm's own dynamics — the front
shape (`c_s`-traveling vs margin-resident), the phase structure (disorganized vs
phase-stable), the self-limiting vs persistent character, the tide correlation
(`weather-not-storm.md` §2.3). The Chronicle is where the settlement's *provenance
over time* lives: a harvest-storm that ignites at the field's most organized reads
as the anomaly it is (`weather-not-storm.md` §6e), and the chronicle books it
against the storm's true source rather than framing it as ambient weather.

**The cruelty rule, applied to the story.** A world that punishes must be readable
as punishing, or it is a cruel world (`weather-not-storm.md` §5.3). The Chronicle is
the settlement's own honest account of that: it can tell "this storm was our
making" (the record holds the overdraw's ops) and "this storm was weather" (the
record holds a `c_s`-front with no player source), and it must never blur the two —
a chronicle that blamed a settlement for a storm it merely endured would be the
Board's scar-misattribution (`shared-ledger.md` §3.3) written into history. The
provenance classifier's pre-registered verdict (`weather-not-storm.md` §3, §6a)
gates *which* telling is honest for a given storm; until it returns, the Chronicle
tells the record's raw shape (a front's `ε²`-rise, a margin's creep) without
asserting a source. **[design]** The *narrative frame* (endured vs caused) is the
Chronicle's designed reading; the *classification* is the corpus's shared
provenance read, and the Chronicle never outruns it.

### 3.3 The dead and their fossils — the obituary

`player-remains.md` makes death a field act and the world a place stratified with
its players' remains (§4.1); `field-archaeology.md` reads those remains as residue
(§2, §3.1). The Chronicle is the settlement's **obituary** — the living accounting
of its dead, told from the same record:

| The dead | Record it reads | The Chronicle tells |
|---|---|---|
| **The dissolved member** | the member-line closed against the bath (`shared-ledger.md` §2.2's settled entry; `player-remains.md` §2) | a settled, fading figure — their final op-line told and closed, not fresh blame |
| **Their fossil** | the deep-rung residue where they died (`player-remains.md` §3; `field-archaeology.md` §2.2) | an obituary entry with a *place* — the residual site, its ghost-halo, its stratum (`player-remains.md` §3.1 read as the archaeology instrument's read) |
| **The fossil's provenance** | the same-member-line binding (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1.1) | the obituary names whose it was — "find your own past" (`player-remains.md` §3.3) told as a record |

**The Chronicle is the living stratum — the record before it becomes residue.**
`field-archaeology.md` notes the field keeps only what is structurally stable; the
transient details of a death are washed out (`field-archaeology.md` §1.2 — the
dissolve destroys degrees of freedom). The Chronicle holds, *while it runs*, the
full record of *which* op ended a run and *where* the fossil was laid — the 
thing archaeology will later read as residue. A chronicle is archaeology's living
form: the settlement keeps, in narrative, what the field will eventually keep only
as residue.

### 3.4 The harvests and thin decades — the arc that was lived

`tide-of-the-attractor.md` names the harvest and thin states of the regional `q`
(§2); `fate-of-a-window.md` reads the window's coherence economics forward as one
of four arcs (settle, thrive, decay, hunt). The Chronicle reads the *same* trajectory
backward as the story of what was lived:

| Season/arc | Record it reads | The Chronicle tells |
|---|---|---|
| **Harvest** | `q` high near the attractor (`q ≈ 0.947`), cheap machines, ore precipitating (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2) | a consonant, rich movement — the settlement's productive season, who build and what |
| **Thin** | `q` toward the noise floor (`q ~ 1e-3…1e-1`), bright waste, the desert-margin's creep (§2) | a thin, dissonant, near-silent movement — the settlements' hard season, what the field shed, who held on (`field-music.md` §2.3's desert silence heard) |
| **The arc** | the window-`C(W)` trajectory over tide-cycles (`fate-of-a-window.md` §1) | the arc as lived — a settlement's settle, thrive, decay, or hunt told as its own story, with the cast (§3.1) and the omens (§3.2) folded in |

The tide's honest gift is "information and timing, not energy"
(`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5d); the Chronicle is the settlement's *timing* kept —
the harvests and thin decades read backward, so a settlement that thrived then
decayed can hear *when* it turned and *whose* hold failed. And because the fate is a
deterministic read (`fate-of-a-window.md` §6b — same field state → same trajectory),
the Chronicle is the fate's *verifiable past*: a recorded destiny, unfolding, is
legible as the arc it became.

## 4. What a Chronicle does

### 4.1 A settlement with a past you can walk into

The Chronicle makes the settlement's story *present* — a room, a song, a reading —
rather than a set of live channels:

> **Without the Chronicle, a settlement is its live channels: the Board's numbers,
> the sky's health, the glass's lean, the sound's growl — all present-tense reads.
> With the Chronicle, the settlement *has a past*: a room you stand in that renders
> the strata of what happened, a song that plays the ops that were actually
> performed, a reading that tells who held the core and who drained it. A settlement
> stops being a set of live channels and becomes a place with a history you walk
> into.**

The comparison the corpus already makes is the model: **the oracle reads forward,
the Chronicle reads backward.** `the-reading-ahead.md` §1.1 — determinism gives
momentum, not destiny — and the oracle's trend-and-threshold lens (§2.2) read the
present's organized direction. The Chronicle runs the same read the opposite way:
the present's *organized record*, told as the settlement's lived past. And **the
tutor teaches the player's own past hands** (`resonance-tutor.md` §1: the record
re-presented as a re-enactment against your past self); **the Chronicle teaches the
settlement's** — the collective's past hands, composed and replayable.

### 4.2 The fate tie — the story the next window's strata will hold

`fate-of-a-window.md` §5 makes a decaying window's residue the next window's
archaeology: **the fate is the cosmos's long game** (`fate-of-a-window.md` §5;
`field-archaeology.md` §2 — a sinking window's record is the archaeology-to-come).
The Chronicle is where that arc is *recorded as a story* before it becomes stratum:

> **A window's arc, recorded, is the story the next window's strata will hold.**
> The Chronicle is the lived narrative that `field-archaeology.md` will later read
> as residue — the settlement's story kept in the op-stream's narrative form, the
> same way `the-name.md` notes a named place's strata read as *its* record (§3.3).
> A future archaeologist digging a decayed settlement reads the residue of the arc
> the Chronicle once told — a dead settlement's chronicle is the record its strata
> will hold, made present before the field forgets the transient.

This is the Chronicle's deepest tie: it is the settlement's story as a *made
thing* — a first-class, re-playable record that outlives the transient and hands the
arc's truth to the archaeology-to-come. A chronicle is what a settlement's residue
*will* be, kept in the narrative form while it is still alive to read.

## 6. Honest gates

### (a) The [design]/engine-real line

§5. The op-stream and the channels are engine-real; the narrative reading is this
doc's [design] — a consumer with a presentation idiom, never a new channel, never
new telemetry. The line is never blurred.

### (b) MEDIUM-LATE — gated on the ledger, the fate, and music-composition landed

The **full Chronicle — a live composed re-play of a settlement's op-history** —
sits on three landed pieces:

- **The shared ledger** (`shared-ledger.md`) — the per-member `C(M)` aggregation,
  the member-id (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1), and the Board's read are the
  Chronicle's cast and attribution. Without them the Chronicle aggregates ops, not
  people.
- **The fate** (`fate-of-a-window.md`) — the window-`C(W)` trajectory and the
  four arcs are the Chronicle's structural spine (the arc that was lived). Without
  the fate-read there is no arc, only events.
- **Music-composition** (`field-music.md` §4) — a coherence score as a Q4 op
  sequence is gated on Q4 (settled by `schema-that-settles.md` §2.2's sustain-flag)
  and is the thing the Chronicle composes as a song. Without the composition system
  the Chronicle is a narrative read-back (the reading form), not a score.

**The Phase-1-legible framing, stated plainly:** a settlement's record as a
first-class re-playable object off the Q4 op-stream is **designable now** — before
the live multi-player society, the fate-spine's full arcs, or the composition
system land. The *record-shaped* Chronicle — a one-player settlement's op-stream
rendered as a readable, re-playable narrative object (a small chronicle: "you held
the core through a thin tide; this dissolve scarred the margin") — is a pure
consumer of the Q4 write lane (`coherence-magic.md` §5.1; `schema-that-settles.md`
§2), exactly the pattern the Board's Phase-1-able aggregation slice proves
(`shared-ledger.md` §7b) and the fate's Phase-1 slice inherits (`fate-of-a-window.md`
§7). The *object* — the story as a re-playable thing — is the Phase-1-able core;
the *live composition* is the MEDIUM-LATE heart.

### (c) Determinism — same record, same Chronicle

The field is deterministic (one PDE; `field-archaeology.md` §1.2; `fate-of-a-window.md`
§6b). Therefore:

> **Same op-stream + same field-state records → same Chronicle.** The composition is
> a pure function of the record — the same settlement's ops always compose to the
> same re-playable narrative, across every run. Determinism is what makes a Chronicle
> a *true* record (a player can re-read the settlement's story exactly as it was,
> like a fixed fossil, `player-remains.md` §5e) and a *stable* re-play (the song
> never changes on reload). The one honest caveat: the *frames* (§3) are [design]
> over the record and may be re-selected as the classifiers that gate them land — a
> pre-provenance Chronicle may not "know" a storm's source; the record itself never
> changes.

### (d) The no-free-energy cap — a Chronicle records, never mints

`energy-harnessing.md` §6 (`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`) applies to the Chronicle as a hard
bound:

> **A Chronicle records; it never mints.** Composing the record into a score or a
> story adds no coherence, writes nothing into the field, and moves no energy — the
> same read-only discipline as Sense (`coherence-magic.md` §5.1) and the Weatherglass
> (`field-instruments.md` §5a). And because a Chronicle is a *read*, it cannot become
> a farm: it cannot "cause" the events it tells to harvest their drains (the
> no-free-energy gate of `field-hazards.md` §5.3 closes chronicle-farming the way it
> closes hazard- and tide-farming, `tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5d). **A replayed
> chronicle does not re-execute ops** — that would double-spend their coherence; it
> *plays* them (§2.2). The Chronicle's gift, like the tide's, is information and
> timing, never energy.

### (e) Accessibility — the Chronicle is a layer, never hidden-only

The Chronicle is one rendering layer of the record, and it must never be the *only*
way to read a settlement's history — the same accessibility rule as `field-music.md`
§6 and `fate-of-a-window.md` §6d, applied to the past:

> **The record is readable from the Board, the glass, and the field's raw channels —
> never Chronicle-only.** A player who does not use the Chronicle can still read "who
> held the core" line-by-line from the Board's `C(M)` per member (`shared-ledger.md`
> §2), "a storm was endured or caused" from the provenance classifier's raw axes
> (`weather-not-storm.md` §2.3), "a member died here" from the archaeology scan
> (`field-archaeology.md` §3.1). The Chronicle is the settlement's story *composed*;
> the underlying record stays on the shared, accessible channels. **[design]** The
> narrative reading is an *idiom* (a convenience of scale), never a gated
> information channel.

## 7. Feasibility verdict

**MEDIUM-LATE as a full spine — with a Phase-1-legible framing that designs a
settlement's record as a first-class re-playable object now.**

**Phase-1-legible (design the object now).** The **record-shaped Chronicle** — a
settlement's op-stream rendered as a re-playable narrative object — is a pure
consumer of the Q4 write lane and the field's published channels, exactly the
pattern `field-instruments.md` already proves Phase-1 and the Board's aggregation
slice (`shared-ledger.md` §7b) and the fate's first slice (`fate-of-a-window.md`
§7) reuse. It needs: the Q4 per-op record (`coherence-magic.md` §5.1;
`schema-that-settles.md` §2), the canonical numbers (the ≈ 6 MiB publish, the
≈ 1–6 ms/tick budget, the ≈ 2,000-entity cap, `q ≈ 0.947` — cited, not re-derived),
and the member-id for attribution (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1). It tests the whole
object — the story as a re-playable thing, the recording form, the 
read-back — on a single player before any society or composition exists. This ships
the *record* as a first-class object; the *live composition* rides it.

**MEDIUM-LATE heart (the composition and the society).** The **live composed
Chronicle** — a long coherence score re-playing a settlement's actual ops, with the
four narrative elements (§3) and the fate-spine (§4.2) — gates on the three landed
pieces of Gate (b): the **shared ledger's** per-member book and member-id, the
**fate's** arc-read, and **music-composition's** Q4-gated score system
(`field-music.md` §4). The full multi-player society is the Board's own Phase-2
heart (`shared-ledger.md` §7); the full four-arc spine is the fate's
(`fate-of-a-window.md` §7).

**Binding risks, in order:**

1. **The misattribution guards (§3.2/§3.3, §5).** The Chronicle must not blame a
   settlement (or a member) for a storm, a scar, or a drain they didn't cut — it
   inherits the Board's classifier line verbatim (`shared-ledger.md` §3.3/§6e) and
   the provenance gate's verdict (`weather-not-storm.md` §6a). The story's honesty
   is the record's honesty, and the guards ride.
2. **The [design]/engine-real line (§5/§6a).** Keeping the *events told* strictly
   engine-real and the *narrative reading* [design], so the Chronicle never reads a
   designed frame (which storms are "caused", whose drain is "guilt") as engine
   drama.
3. **The no-free-energy cap (§6d).** A Chronicle must never read (or replay) as
   minting — it records, never writes, never re-executes.
4. **The composition's determinism-under-refinement (§6c).** The record is fixed;
   the *frames* may refine as the classifiers land — a Chronicle composed before the
   provenance verdict may honestly not "know" a storm's source, and that must read
   as the record's own silence, never as wavering.
5. **Accessibility (§6e).** The Chronicle must stay a layer — the record readable
   from the Board/glass/raw channels, never Chronic-only — or it becomes a hidden
   history the design's own honesty forbids.

None contradicts the async, dual-world, or regime-collapse architecture — the
Chronicle is a consumer of the Q4 op-stream and the publish, adding no channel, no
write, and no physics.

> **The honest statement that makes this doc load-bearing: the corpus reads the
> field's present, near-future, and net society — but nothing reads the settlement's
> *story*. And the story was already written, because every field act is a
> single-writer Q4 record (`schema-that-settles.md` §2), the same record the Board
> sums and the oracle projects. The Chronicle is that record *told*: the stewards
> and margin-causers, the storms endured vs caused, the dead and their fossils, the
> harvests and thin decades — the shared-ledger's book made narrative, the oracle's
> forward momentum turned backward, the fate's arc recorded as the story the next
> window's strata will hold. It is the settlement's past as a first-class,
> re-playable object — a room, a song, a reading you walk into — and it is honest
> because it is the record: it never adds events the record doesn't hold and never
> hides the misattribution guards. The record-shaped object is designable now; the
> live composition is MEDIUM-LATE, gated on the ledger, the fate, and the music
> that is the score's form. The Chronicle is what makes a settlement a place with a
> past — read backward, told forward, and re-played as the field's own song.**

## Open questions

1. **Which events the Chronicle tells — the narrative selection.** §3 names the four
   elements, but the *selection rule* (which of the record's events are told, how
   coarsely vs finely — every op, or a reduced season-level reading) is a [design]
   dial over the op-stream. Does the composition reduce the stream (a settlement's
   song is not a raw dump of every record) or replay it near-verbatim? The
   record is fixed; the *telling density* is the design's tuning surface. **[design]**
2. **The continuity of a named chronicle across decay/rebuild.** `the-name.md`
   open-Q4 asks whether a name persists when a named structure falls and a new one
   rises on the site. The Chronicle has the same question at settlement scale: if a
   settlement decays and a new settlement is founded on the same window
   (`field-archaeology.md` §4.3 — a window recedes and re-seeds), does the chronicle
   run on, or does the new settlement open a new chronicle over the old record's
   strata? The design's default leans to the latter (a chronicle is a settlement's
   past, and a new settlement's past begins at its founding); reconciles with the
   same-member-line conservatism at the *window* scale. **[design]**
3. **The sourcing of the "caused" tellings pre-provenance.** §3.2 gates the endured
   vs caused frame on `weather-not-storm.md`'s pre-registered provenance verdict.
   Until it lands, how much should the Chronicle tell a settlement's causal story
   (the record's raw `ε²`-rise and who over-drew) without asserting *whose storm* it
   was — where is the line between "the record's silence" and a useful, guarded
   telling? The Chronicle's honesty claims (it "never asserts a source past the
   classifier") must hold here or the obituary/overdraw readings over-read.
   **[design] / [probe]**
4. **The composition's cost-model.** Composing the record into a score is a read, but
   *rendering* it — the sonification, the spatial room, the read-back — sits in the
   same ~5% CPU / sampled-point budget as field-music's synth precedent (`field-music.md`
   §1, `synth_design.md`). How a long chronicle (many seasons of ops) is rendered
   within that budget — what is coined into the room/song and what is a reduced
   read-out — is a Phase-1 performance question. **[probe]**
5. **The chronicle's relation to the Board's dissolved-entry.** `shared-ledger.md`
   §2.2 books a dissolved member's final `C(M)` as a settled entry. The Chronicle's
   obituary (§3.3) tells that same entry as a story — but where the record is
   *bounded* (a settlement's own chronicle may be readable by other players, since
   the board is public, `shared-ledger.md` §6d), does the obituary stay a
   provenance-neutral read (what the run was, where the fossil is) or extend into
   the "who they were" of `player-remains.md` §3's reading-your-own-past, when the
   subject is a *stranger's* run? The same-member-line continuity (`schema-that-settles.md`
   §2.1.1) makes their fossil a *claimable* record; the chronicle's telling of it
   must not overstep the record's provenance-neutral read. **[design]**
