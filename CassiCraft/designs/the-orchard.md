## 1. The Orchard, stated once

The corpus's three crops already draw the field's standing economy — but each is
*circulated*, *hoarded*, or *replanted*; none is *planted once and left standing*:

- The **farm** is the tide's crop: a shallow-rung regime in a held `q`-band,
  replanted each tide, harvested at tide-high — **replenished** (`farm-that-feeds.md`
  §2 — the fast, forgiving end of the precipitation ladder; §4 the harvest is stored,
  tide-bound coherence).
- The **seed-garden** is the rare-order vault: the window's deepest, un-re-precipitable
  orders, held at deep-rung cost against drift — **collected** (`seed-garden.md` §1/§2
  — the found-economy's deep-time preservation of the *irreplaceable*).
- The **granary** is the harvest store: the ordinary harvest held at tide-high against
  the lean — **stored** (`the-granary.md` §2 — the everyday twin of the rare vault,
  plenty held against winter's thin).

None designs the **multi-year fixed planting** — a grove of rung-trees planted once
that gives *for windows*, standing in the field the way a farm's beds never stand
(replanted each tide) and a garden's seeds never stand (hoarded against drift). The
seed-garden holds *rarity*; the farm grows *renewal*; the granary holds *plenty*.
**The orchard plants *permanence* — the one crop whose yield is deferred across
seasons and whose hold is the settlement's own long patience.**

> **The Orchard, stated once.** It is the **planted stand** — the perennial grove that
> outlasts a season, the farm's slow, fixed cousin: a grove of rung-trees, planted
> once, that gives for windows. The rung-trees are the field's own life at tree scale
> (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.2 — the standing life the field's biome rules
> admit), planted once as runs the field holds (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2) and
> giving their yield across the windows the year's calendar dates

> beds are replanted (the farm is the tide's crop, the orchard the window's), rooted in
> the field's own order (the grove's `q`-rooting is the field's coherence made a
> standing crop). And it is honest in the field's own terms: **the patience is the
> crop** — the yield is `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` over the long horizon, never a mint,
> never a hidden bounty (`energy-harnessing.md` §6); and **the orchard is a standing
> hold, never a standing gift** — it must be kept, or it falls toward the fallow's

**The composition, honestly scoped.** The *rung-tree* is the ecology's landed

## 3. The patience is the crop — the load-bearing design

This is the orchard's load-bearing design, stated once and never blurred:

> **An orchard gives at the field's own pace, never a season's.** A grove planted this

> settlement that plants an orchard commits to the long arc, the way the patient
> field's patience is the field's own tempo (`patient-field.md` §2) — a grove's yield
> is the *waiting* the settlement chooses, held in the standing trees.

Three honest mechanisms make the patience the crop, each grounded in the landed
pieces:

- **The yield is long-horizon, not tide-high.** The farm harvests at the tide's
  high — the crop's stored coherence *peaks at tide-high*, taken at the apex
  (`farm-that-feeds.md` §4/§5). The orchard's rung-trees *do not* peak-and-die at a
  single high; their stored coherence deepens across the window, and the yield is
  taken *over* the long arc, at the field's own rate. **[design]** — the window's
  yield-profile (how much the standing tree gives per window, at what `q`-condition)

  farm's crop-band and the tide's period calibrate on. The *pace* is the field's real

- **No fast crop, ever.** Because the yield is `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` over the long
  horizon (`energy-harnessing.md` §6), the orchard *cannot* give a quick bounty: no
  grove that "yields its store at need" like a hoard, no "harvest a window for free

  by the field's own long-horizon coherence economics, and nothing a keeper does
  lifts that bound. The orchard confronts the player with the field's slow generosity

- **The patience is the commitment.** `the-window-year.md` §2's calendar is the long
  arc's clock, and `patient-field.md` §2's patience is the field's own tempo — the
  orchard is that tempo *made a crop you hold*. A settlement that plants an orchard is

  across the windows *is* the settlement's long patience, committed in the ground. The

**The honest scope.** The *patience* is `patient-field.md` §2's landed tempo (with the
