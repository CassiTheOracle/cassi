# The Shared Ledger: A Window's Player Society, Read Off the Q4 Op-Stream

**Question under design:** how a settled window's *human* players become a
legible society in a world where every field act is already a Q4 record. Every
other society in the corpus is either NPC-only (a village is a Qi-bath commons,
`field-npc-ai.md` §3) or player-divorced (hazards are the field's own
extremes, `field-hazards.md` §1). Nothing yet makes the *players themselves* a
collective subject. This document is the first design that makes the **Q4 schema
itself a gameplay object**: it aggregates every channeled op into a settlement's
**Coherence Board** — the group-instrument that renders, per member-identity,
each player's running net contribution to / drain on the shared Qi bath over a
tide-cycle. The op-stream the world already writes becomes the object of
collective thought; **cooperation and conflict become legible in the field's
own terms.**

The Load-bearing fact, stated once:

> **Because every channeled op is already an immutable Q4 record
> (`{op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}`, `coherence-magic.md` §5.1;
> `async-field-domain.md` §7 Q4), the Board is a *reading of what the field is
> already told* — it is the Weatherglass family rule applied at settlement scale:
> a consumer of the same publish with a presentation idiom, *never a new channel,
> never new telemetry, never a new instrument that asks the field to talk more.*
> The aggregated op-stream is the group's **Shared Ledger** — the single-writer
> lane's own honesty turned into the society's book of record.**

## 1. The Q4 op-stream as collective economics

### 1.1 The load-bearing fact: every op is already a record

`coherence-magic.md` §5.1 fixes the per-op input schema the async field-domain's
**Q4 player-return channel** must carry:

```
{ op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain }
```

batched into the player-return job, cadence following the sampler/world-writer
cadence (`async-field-domain.md` §7 Q4). This is the channel *all* field writes
round-trip through — the player's channeling (`coherence-magic` §5.1), the
harvesting machines (`energy-harnessing.md` §0/§2), the material lab
(`custom-blocks.md` §2), the NPC-intent lane (`field-npc-ai.md` § intro), and
the composition system's coherence scores (`field-music.md` §4.1). **Every field
act in the game lands on the same public, ordered stream.**

That stream is a **book of account before any Board exists.** Each record
carries the op's *field* meaning (`condense` raises ρ; `dissolve` raises ε²;
`heal`/`shield` suppresses ε² and re-locks EY = φ·EI; `steer` injects a
∇(g·Φ) current; `ignite` launches an organized front — `coherence-magic` §2),
its *cost* (the coherence budget B, `coherence-magic` §1.2), and its *disposition*
(whether it feeds the shared high-`q` core or drains it toward the desert
margin). The Shared Ledger is the name we give that stream **once it is summed
per member — the aggregation object is the society.**

### 1.2 What a "contribution" and a "drain" are, in field terms

The corpus already names both directions; the ledger reads them off the ops:

| Op class | Field effect | Ledger disposition | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heal / anti-corruption / a maintained Shield** | suppresses ε², restores/re-holds the φ-lock in a region — *raises* the local `q` the bath's core is | a **contribution** — feeds the shared high-`q` core; a sustained healer is a bath-maintainer | `coherence-magic` §2; `energy-harnessing` §4.4/§5.4 |
| **Condense** at a coherent site | raises ρ where q is already high — *adds organized matter*, which is stored coherence (`energy-harnessing` §3) | a **contribution** — builds the bath's store, if the site is the settlement's core | `coherence-magic` §2; `energy-harnessing` §1.5/§3 |
| **Ignite / steer** within the bath | organized work that the field absorbs — net-neutral *unless* sustained past the vent, then it sheds ε² | **contextual** — a productive op in a healthy core is neutral-to-positive; an ignited front overdrawing is a drain | `coherence-magic` §2/§4.3 |
| **Dissolve / over-reap / sustained overdraw** | raises ε² above the floor (`material-regimes` §2), lowers regional `q` — exactly the desert's §3.2 causes | a **drain** — de-orders the shared core and pushes the locality toward the desert-margin | `field-hazards` §3.2; `coherence-magic` §1.2/§4.3 |
| **Death / re-lock** | a re-lock draws local q and vents ε² (`player-remains` §2.2); a dissolved member's deep core persists as residue/fossil (§3) | a **settled entry** — the member's balance closes against the window's bath on dissolution | `player-remains` §2.2/§3 |

The disposition is not an arbitrary "good/bad" flag the Board invents: it is the
op's own field effect read against the **bath's `q`/`ε²`** (the published `q` 1 +
`ε²` + `∇(g·Φ)` 3 + ρ 1, the ≈ 6 MiB publish, `corpus-reconciliation.md`). A
`heal` into a collapsing margin is *more* of a contribution than a `heal` into an
already-overfull core; a single `dissolve` at a tiny rung in a huge healthy bath
is noise, the same `dissolve` sustained at a deep rung at a thin tide is a
margin-causer's signature.

> **The aggregation object, stated once.** For each member-identity M, per
> tide-cycle `[t_k, t_k+T]`:
>
> ```
> C(M) = Σ ops (net field-contribution of each op at its locality/rung/magnitude)
>        budgeted against the bath's Δq / Δε² over that cycle
> ```
>
> `C(M) > 0` = steward-of-the-tide (holding the high-`q` core up); `C(M) < 0`
> = margin-sourced drain (overdrawing it toward the desert). The **net** is the
> load-bearing word: a player who heals a lot *and* reaps a lot books an
> ambiguous middle — the ledger is honest about players who both build and
> wound.

### 1.3 The honest consequence: the ledger is *already* the economics

Because the stream is single-writer and public, the Shared Ledger is not a
separate economy layered on the game — it **is** the game's economy, read back.
`energy-harnessing.md` §6 calls the economy a **constraint, not a currency**; the
ledger is the *ledger of that constraint*. The no-free-energy cap
(`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`, §6) is the hard rule that makes the ledger *tractable*:

> **Nobody can be a net mint.** The write-back amplitude caps mean no player's
> op-stream can produce more coherence than it consumes. So a member's `C(M)`
> cannot be unboundedly positive — the most any steward can do is *maintain* the
> core (hold `q` up against the `(1−q)` waste and the shared drains), never mint
> it. **The Board shows "X is this tide's steward" as a real, bounded
> achievement, and it can *prove* "Y is draining the bath" because the books
> cannot move otherwise.**

## 2. The Coherence Board

### 2.1 The instrument — a settlement-scale Weatherglass

`field-instruments.md` §2.1 fixes the family rule:

> **Every instrument in this family is a consumer of the same publish with a
> presentation idiom — never a new channel.**

The Board is that rule's **social face** — the Weatherglass idiom (§1.2)
mounted in the settlement and read over the *Q4 op-stream* instead of (only) the
field snapshot. It is, in the family's own table, the **Still Room**'s sibling:
the Still Room renders a room's locality at scale; the Board renders the
settlement's *member-ops* at scale. Both are consumers of existing channels —
the Board's new "channel" is the already-written Q4 record stream, which is not
new telemetry but the society's own bookkeeping.

| Instrument | Idiom | Target | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sense** (reader) | aimed magnetometer overlay | local published `q`/`ε²`/`∇(g·Φ)` | Phase-1 (`coherence-magic.md` §2) |
| **Weatherglass** | glanceable lume/lean | local, at the player | Phase-1 (`field-instruments.md`) |
| **Still Room** | room-scale Weatherglass | the room's locality | later — same idiom, larger scale |
| **Coherence Board** | **settlement-scale Weatherglass over the Q4 op-stream** | per member-identity, per tide-cycle net `C(M)` | this doc — the aggregation slice is Phase-1-able (its live multi-player heart is Phase-2) |
| **any future instrument** | a consumer of the publish with a presentation idiom | whatever locality/range is legible | — |

### 2.2 The read — four member-identities the board renders

The Board presents each member as one of four **legible states**, all derived
from `C(M)` summed over the tide, and all in the Weatherglass's four-form
vocabulary (lume, climbing glow, waste tint, lean):

| Member-identity | Ledger reading (`C(M)` over the tide) | Board form | The field it reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Steward-of-the-tide** | strongly and consistently positive — holds the high-`q` core up against the `(1−q)` waste | a steady, bright lume (the bath's own healthy-state color) | "this member is why the bath is still coherent this tide"; the recognized contribution (`coherence-magic` §1.2; `energy-harnessing` §4.4) |
| **Neutral-middle** | `C(M)` near zero — both builds and wounds, or mostly absent | a calm, mid-tone, no strong lean | the honest majority; a member of the commons neither upholding nor gutting it |
| **Margin-causer** | persistently and deeply negative — drains the core toward the desert-margin | a climbing glow (the `ε²`-dominated form) that rises each tide; a "waste tint" wash | "this member's repeated overdraw is the desert-margin's cause" — attribution with a real consequence (`field-hazards` §3.2 sustained-overdraw cause) |
| **Dissolved** | the member's Π-pattern run ended — the balance settles against the bath on death | a fading, darkening lume (the death-glow of `player-remains` §1.3) then the **fixed record** of their fossil | the ledger's bookkeeping of a dissolved member — their final `C(M)` closes and their residue persists (`player-remains` §3) |

**The display is the Weatherglass idiom at settlement scale** — a mounted wall or
central structure that renders the member-lume set, the bath's overall
`q`/`ε²` trend (a regional lume/lean), and the margin's creep toward the desert
(a second, darker lean). A member is *seen* by the settlement the way a
Weatherglass is seen by its wearer: always on, glanceable, legible before
anything is said.

### 2.3 The honesty: it reads exactly what the world was told

Two loads of honesty, both inherited:

1. **The single-writer Q4 lane means no forged contributions.** `async-field-domain.md`
   §5.2: the world-writer is the *only* mutator; player edits round-trip as
   job-dict inputs, never by the server or a player touching physics state. So
   a contribution cannot be faked into the ledger — there is no "white-heal
   then immediately dissolve" forge that inflates `C(M)`, because the op-stream
   is exactly what the domain executed, ordered and logged. **The Board is as
   honest as the op-stream, and the op-stream is single-writer — a hard,
   structural strength this doc states plainly.** (The one honest caveat is the
   identity attribution itself, §6(c): the stream is unforgeable *as executed*;
   binding a record to a *member* is the designed extension.)

2. **The no-free-energy cap makes "nobody is a net mint" legible.** Because
   `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` (§6), the Board can never show a member *creating*
   coherence. So the positive end of the ledger is always bounded and honest —
   a steward's high `C(M)` is a *maintained* contribution (holding the core up),
   never an invented surplus, exactly as the bath itself "is a draw ... brittle
   if it goes down" (`energy-harnessing` §4.4). The negative end is equally
   legible: a margin-causer's drain shows up in the bath's real `Δq`, not in a
   reputation counter.

## 3. The meta-game: cooperation and conflict in the field's own terms

### 3.1 Cooperation is legible — "X is this tide's steward"

Because the Board renders `C(M)` per tide, a player who **maintains the high-`q`
core** — heals the scars, holds a Still Room / shield through a thin tide, does
anti-corruption `energy-harnessing` §5.4 at the margin — accumulates a
recognized, *readable* positive balance. "X is this tide's steward" is not a
title a player claims or a vote they win; it is the ledger's honest sum of their
ops. This is the **multiplicity theorem made visible**: `field-npc-ai.md` §3.1
gives a village as a superadditive commons (`coherence-commons.md` §7 — the
multiplicative budget `∏ 1/(1−q_i)`, the AM-GM direction that **a commons sharing
coherence evenly is smarter than one concentrated in a few**). The Board renders
that theorem per member: the shared high-`q` core *is* the thing being
cooperatively maintained, and the pool's health — which every member's recovery
rides (`coherence-magic` §1.2 — recovery refills where the local field is
coherent) — is literally everyone's business. **Cooperation has a field
consequence every member can read.**

### 3.2 Honest conflict is legible — "Y's overdraw is the desert-margin's cause"

The desert is the corpus's shared failure (`field-hazards.md` §3): a regional
`q` collapse whose causes are all player-caused choices — sustained overdraw
(§3.2), over-reaping (§5.1/§5.2 of archaeology), BH over-provisioning (§4).
The Board surfaces that attribution **per member**: a player whose op-stream
consistently sheds ε² into the shared core at a thin tide is *readable* as the
margin's driver. This is not a lynch mob mechanic because the Board renders the
field's real direction — a staffed, honest grievance, not a player-color
judgment:

> **You do not ban a player; you stop feeding the bath they drain, and the Board
> shows the consequence.** Governance in this world is played in the field's own
> terms. The settlement's non-coercive lever is the *bath itself*: withdraw the
> maintained high-`q` core's support around a margin-causer (refuse to heal at
> their site, gate the condenser, stop feeding the anti-corruption they sit in)
> and the Board shows the *bath's health curve* dropping and the *margin's creep*
> toward their overdraw. The consequence is the field's, not a ban's — it is
> `energy-harnessing` §4.4's "brittle if it goes down" applied socially.

### 3.3 The ethics — the Board can be wrong

This is the sharpest honesty the ledger owns, and it imports the corpus's
existing classifier line wholesale:

> **The Board's contribution-vs-drain read is a *classifier*, and every classifier
> in this corpus has an honest line.** `field-music.md` open-Q2 and
> `field-archaeology.md` §7 open-Q3 name the same task (distinguish a deliberate
> vent / a live structure from a scar / a residue) and close it two-way
> (`corpus-reconciliation.md` §4) on *one* distinguishability criterion. **Social
> attribution is the same classifier applied to people — and it can misfire.**

Concretely, **misattribution risks** the Board must be designed against:

| Risk | What looks like a drain but isn't | The guard |
|---|---|---|
| **Scar misattribution** | a `dissolve` op (*intended* terrain work) raising ε² reads like overdraw | **intent-signature is [design]** — the ledger must separate *organized* work (condense/reshape that heals behind) from *random* overdraw (the `M ≈ 0` vent); the §6 constraint "the Board must not blame a player for a scar they didn't cut" |
| **Attrition vs. one spike** | a single deep-rung overdraw tip (`coherence-magic` §4.3) vs. a sustained pattern | the *per-tide net over many cycles*, never a single event — a player who overchannels once and heals it is not a margin-causer |
| **Coda poisoning** | the signature-predator's own ε² residue near a clean channeler | the Board and the Coda read the *same* stream; the Board must not attribute the predator's margin to the hunted player (§5) |
| **Field-pocket death** | a member who dies in a scar re-locks with a net-negative × their own death (`player-remains` §2.2) | the dissolved entry must not double-count: a death is a *settled* entry, not fresh blame |

The classifier line, stated once: **the Board may name a *pattern* (a sustained
net drain over many tides); it must never name a *person* off a single reading
or a scar they didn't create.** That is the design's ethical floor, inherited
verbatim from `field-music.md` open-Q2 / `field-archaeology.md` §7 open-Q3.

## 4. The Q4 schema as a design surface

### 4.1 The first design that uses the schema itself as gameplay

Every prior design *consumed* the Q4 op-stream (players channel, machines
pump, NPCs act, scores compose). This doc is the first to make the **schema's
own open questions** the gameplay surface:

**The sustain-flag vs re-emit question becomes socially visible.** `coherence-magic.md`
Q2 / `field-music.md` open-Q3: does a *held* op (a maintained shield, a still
room, a heal-in-place) carry a **sustain flag** (the domain maintains the source
until told to stop) or does the server **re-emit** the op each job? On the Board,
this is *not* plumbing trivia — **it changes how a player's contribution books**:

| Schema choice | A sustained healer books as | A spam-reemitter books as | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| **sustain flag** (domain holds the leak) | **one** high-magnitude, long-sustain record — a continuous contribution | N re-emitted records of the same magnitude — **a noisy book** | the ledger cleanly separates "holding the core up" from "tapping the channel to look active" |
| **per-job re-emit** | N identical re-emitted records | the same N records — **indistinguishable** from a real held op | the ledger *cannot* tell a maintained shield from refresh-spam on magnitude alone |

So the Board is the **first consumer whose design depends on settling Q2** — and
it gives the settlement a *social* reason to prefer the sustain flag: a
society that wants clean books (holding vs. fiddling) mechanically rewards the
`{sustain}` semantics. The aggregator is honest either way (it reads what the
schema writes); the *legibility of sustained cooperation* is what the schema
settle.[^sustain]

[^sustain]: **[design]** The sustain-vs-re-emit resolution is now **settled by
`schema-that-settles.md` §2.2: `sustain` is a flag** — a held op carries
`sustain = true` and the domain maintains the source until the release (the
schema's §2.2 is the owner that §6c open-Q3 deferred to). This doc's
social preference (§4.1) is the settlement's commitment, so the Board
aggregates the **sustain-flag** semantics (a sustained steward books as one
long-sustain record). The "either semantics honestly" hedge is retired; the
Board is built to the settled flag.

### 4.2 What the schema must settle: per-member identity on the records

The ledger's per-member attribution is impossible without an identity on the
op-stream. The canonical schema `{op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}` has
**no member field**. The Board therefore demands a designed extension:

> **[design] Per-member identity on Q4 records.** Each op record carries a
> **member-id** — the persistent-Π-pattern bound (`field-npc-ai.md` §2.1: an
> identity is a persistent Π pattern the field holds) that the world-writer
> attaches at write time, from the player's re-lock (`player-remains` §2). The
> world-writer is already the single mutator; adding the id at *its* write is the
> only non-forgeable point (the extended schema `{member, op, worldPos, rung,
> magnitude, sustain}` — member first, because attribution is the read's
> spine).

This is deliberately framed as **[design]** and not a physics demand: the engine
writes no identity record; the open question is how a *reborn* player's fresh Π
pattern keeps (or legally re-takes) the same member line across deaths — the same
identity question `player-remains.md` §5's conservation-of-"you" open-Q5 names.
The Board is indifferent to the exact rule; it just needs *some* stable member
binding to aggregate against.

### 4.3 What the aggregation reveals back to the schema

Turning the stream into per-member books doesn't just consume the schema — it
*illuminates* its gaps. Three the Board surfaces:

| Schema gap | How the Board reveals it | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| The `sustain` field (Q2) is unsettled | a "sustained healer" vs a "spam-reemitter" cannot be told apart on magnitude alone | the Board makes the *social legibility of sustained cooperation* depend on a plumbing decision — an unexpected weight |
| World-position granularity | two members in the same cell, one healing and one dissolving, book a net that hides both | the Board surfaces the need for per-op locality bounding so contribution and drain don't cancel silently |
| The member field doesn't exist yet | the whole per-member read is impossible until the id lands (§4.2) | the Board is the first design that *requires* the extension rather than merely allowing it |

**All three gaps are now closed by [`schema-that-settles.md`](./schema-that-settles.md):**
the `member` field (§2.1) lands the id, the **cell-ownership locality rule**
(§2.3 — one member books one disposition per cell per tide-window, and a
contribution and a drain by *different* members at one cell do not cancel) closes
the world-position gap, and the `sustain` flag (§2.2) settles the field. This
§4.3 table is the *requirement* those settlements were built from; the schema
doc cites `shared-ledger.md` §4.3 for exactly this list.

whole aggregation: the per-member book, the tide summing, the classifier, and
the display idiom all get tested on one player before the society exists.

### (d) Privacy / trust — as honest as the op-stream, and it is single-writer

### Open questions

1. **The member-id rule across death.** If identity is a persistent Π pattern
   (`field-npc-ai.md` §2.1) and rebirth is a *fresh* lock (`player-remains` §5
   open-Q5), does a reborn player keep the same member line on the ledger — a
   continuous `C(M)` that carries their own past — or open a new line? The Board
   needs a stable binding (§6c); the *identity* is the existing open question,
   and the ledger must be built to average over either choice.
   **DECIDED by [`schema-that-settles.md`](./schema-that-settles.md) §2.1.1: `same member
   line, new lock`** — the persistent-Π re-lock is the same identity (the only
   freshness is the lock, not the self), so the Board aggregates one continuous
   `C(M)` per member across deaths, closed only on the dissolved entry. This
   open-Q and §6c are now the landed settlement, not a fork; the "must average
   over either choice" hedge is retired (the ledger is built to the settled
   rule). The member field on records is the schema's first field (`{member,
   op, worldPos, rung, magnitude, sustain}`), attached at the world-writer's
   write (§2.1).
2. **The classifier's per-member threshold.** Where is the `C(M) < 0` line that
   separates "margin-causer" from "neutral-middle who had a bad tide"? It is a
   [design] dial against the bath's `Δq` and the hazard threshold of
   `field-hazards.md` §6.2 — set so a season trough reads as "weakness," not
   "guilt" (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §5c's thin-trough floor, applied to
   accusation).
3. **Sustain-flag preference.** The Board *prefers* the sustain flag (§4.1) so a
   sustained steward doesn't book as a spammer; but the schema's Q2 is the owner.
   If re-emit wins, the Board must aggregate the noisy book honestly and lose
   the steward/spammer distinction — a social cost, not a blocking one.
   **ADOPTED by [`schema-that-settles.md`](./schema-that-settles.md) §2.2: `sustain` is a
   flag** — a held op carries `sustain = true` and the domain maintains the
   source until the release, so a sustained steward books as **one** high-
   magnitude, long-sustain record (a clean book), never as N re-emits. The
   Board's aggregated social read of §4.1 is the settled semantics; the "if
   re-emit wins" branch is retired. The sustained-hold cost (a standing job-dict
   input) is self-bounding on the vent `v ≤ h` (`coherence-magic.md` §3).
4. **The Coda's attribution.** §5 makes the predator legible *to* the Board, but
   the reverse guard (not reading the Coda's ε² as the clean channeler's drain)
   is the same live-vs-residue line (`field-archaeology` §7 open-Q3). Does the
   Board get enough of the margin's *moving* ε²-resonance shape — or must it
