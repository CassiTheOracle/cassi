# Weather, Not Storm: The Provenance Classifier over the Danger Layer's Extremes

**Question under design:** whether an extreme `ε²` event — the class the corpus
calls a **decoherence storm** ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2) — is
**weather** (the field's own extreme, ordered but self-limiting, following the
law, happening *where the field is*) or **punishment** (something aimed at the
player, happening *where the player is*). The corpus has named both readings
across its danger layer without ever answering which is true, and that
intentional blank is the load-bearing question behind the whole danger layer:
a world whose extreme events are weather can be *read honestly* and answered
with shelter; a world whose extreme events are punishment must be readable as
punishing, or it is a cruel world. This document closes the blank with a
**designed provenance classifier** — a derived read over the storm's own
dynamics — and pre-registers the corpus probe that decides what the storm *is*
before any storm-weather or storm-punishment gameplay follows.

## 1. The question, stated once

> **Is a decoherence storm weather or punishment?**

The danger layer's whole frame rests on the answer. [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md)
§1 states the spine without hedging: hazards are **the field's own behavior at an
extreme — always the field's own dynamics, never player-punishment**, and §6.2's
player-agency gate singles the storm out as "the one hazard that can arrive with
no player-caused trigger — it is the field's own extreme, not a punishment." That
is the honest claim this document exists to *test*, not to assume. The
signature-predator (§1.2's phase-structure, §2) then depends on the *opposite*
capacity: a field structure that *is* aimed at the player's trail — a punishment-
shaped response that happens *where you are*. The corpus will not cohere until
one question is answered: **can the field's own extreme be *read* as weather, and
can an aimed event be *read* as not-weather, from the published channels alone?**

Three capacities separate the two, and each is a designed distinction over real
channels:

- **Self-limiting.** A weather extreme *peaks and passes* — the `−ω₀²(EY−φ·EI)`
  re-lock re-binds the field once the injection ceases ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md)
  §2.2 "stops / passes"). An aimed event *persists while its target persists* —
  it accumulates while the player's clean trail is sustained and decays when the
  trail stops ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §1.3's half-life,
  §4.3). **Weather goes away on its own; an aimed event goes away when you stop
  being its target.**
- **Causally located.** Weather happens *where the field is* — at the crossing
  of a regional-`q` state and a tide phase. An aimed event happens *where the
  player is* — on the channeling trail's phase-structure. The location of the
  extreme relative to the player's hand is not an accident of the two classes;
  it is the *definitional* difference.
- **Readable in the same channel.** Both are `ε²` extremes. The storm is a
  `c_s`-traveling front ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2.1); the Coda
  is a *margin-resident* resonance that accumulates toward a trail
  ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §2). The two must be told
  apart — by a player who must decide to shelter (weather) or to stop channeling
  (punishment) — from the `ε²`/`q`/phase the sampler already reads, *without a
  new channel* (the family rule). That is the provenance classifier this document
  designs.

The commit, stated once, is the load-bearing line of the whole danger layer:

> **The weather-vs-punishment distinction is real (the two classes genuinely
> differ), readable (its axes read the storm's published dynamics), and designed
> (its decision rule is a [design], probe-calibrated classifier). It is not a
> mystery the game hides from the player; it is the world's own honesty, made a
> read.**

## 2. The two classes' signatures

Both classes are `ε²` extremes read over the **published channels** — `q`, `ε²`,
`ρ`, `∇(g·Φ)`, position ([`corpus-reconciliation.md`](./corpus-reconciliation.md)'s
≈ 6 MiB publish) — with **none added**. The signature is a *derived read*:
the classifier takes the same snapshot the sampler already consumes and computes
the **front shape, phase structure, tracking, and tide-phase correlation** from
its time-series. This is the family rule ([`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md)
§2.1) and the read-only-consumer discipline ([`coherence-magic.md`](./coherence-magic.md)
§5.1) applied to the storm itself.

### 2.1 Weather — the field's own extreme

A storm-as-weather is the field's own wave dynamics at an extreme
([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2.1): an injected random-perturbation
region where `ε² = (EY−φ·EI)²` rises out of lock, travelling as a steep `∇ε²`
front at the coherence sound speed `c_s = h₀/dt`. Every axis that follows is a
read of the real front:

| Axis (derived read) | Weather signature | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| **Front shape** | a **moving, `c_s`-traveling `∇ε²`** front — a *rising gradient* over the wave operator's propagation, not a stationary plateau | [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2.1 (`c_s = h₀/dt`); [`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md) §1.3 (weather = the envelope's waves); [`field-music.md`](./field-music.md) §2.2 (the growl = a *rising* ε² front, vs a *static* scar) |
| **Phase structure** | the front's perturbation is **disorganized** — a random-perturbation injection that raises `EY−φ·EI` *without* a coherent phase locking it | [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2.1 (a storm is a "dis-organized seed" — no coherent structure) |
| **Self-limiting** | the front **peaks and passes** — the `−ω₀²(EY−φ·EI)` term re-locks once the injection ceases; the storm's `ε²` rises, crosses the dissolution floor, then falls as the field heals | [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2.2 "stops / passes"; [`volumetric-terrain.md`](./volumetric-terrain.md) (terrain heals) |
| **Tide-phase correlation** | storm **frequency and dissolution rise at thin tide** — the field is already decohered, so an incoming front finds it "ready to dissolve"; storm damage is *seasonal*, tracking the regional-`q` trough | [`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §2 (hazards row); §1.2 (thin = q toward the noise floor) |
| **Regional-`q` state** | a storm is **more likely to ignite and more damaging where regional `q` is already low** — it happens *where the field is* thin | [`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §2; [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §2 |
| **Decoherence-wielder habitat** | a decoherence-wielder **rides the storm as native terrain** — the same `ε²` front is its home, not a threat | [`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §4.1b/§4.3 |

**The weather class's law, stated once:** weather is the field doing what the
field does at its extreme — ordered (a `c_s` front the wave operator generates),
bounded (it peaks and passes), self-limiting (the re-lock re-binds it), and
correlated with the field's own tempo (the tide, the regional `q`). **It happens
*where the field is*.** A player facing it is facing a fact of place and season;
the honest response is to *read it, shelter, and let it pass*.

### 2.2 Punishment — the aimed event

An extreme event that is punishment would not be a second kind of physics; it
would be the **same `ε²` extreme with a different provenance** — a field
structure *aimed at the player's trail*. The signature-predator is the corpus's
definition of the aimed class: a non-willful resonance structure drawn to the
organized-perturbation trail channeling leaves ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md)
§1). Read as a classifier axis, the aimed event is distinguished from weather on
the *same five channels*, each with the opposite signature:

| Axis (derived read) | Punishment (aimed) signature | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| **Front shape** | a **margin-resident, accumulating structure** — not a `c_s`-traveling front but a persistent pattern in the local `ε²` margins that *rises as it accumulates* | [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §2 (a "persistent pattern the local field holds in the margins") |
| **Phase structure** | the perturbation carries a **coherent phase matched to the channeling trail** — not disorganized, but *phase-stable*, `M ≈ 1` against the trail's own rhythm | [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §1.2/§2.2 (`M_stability` — the cleaner the trail, the higher the resonance); [`coherence-technologies.md`](./coherence-technologies.md) concept 3 (the phase-matching `M`) |
| **Self-limiting** | **not self-limiting while the target persists** — it accumulates while the player's hand is clean, and *passes when the trail stops* | [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §2.3 (the precipitating threshold `T_coda`), §4.3 (resonance decays when the trail's half-life runs) |
| **Tide-phase correlation** | the Coda's habitat **peaks at thin tide** — at the `q` trough the `ε²` margins widen and the desert-margin creeps, so exposure peaks with the season — but the *cause* is the trail, not the season | [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §2 (thin tide as the Coda's season) |
| **Tracking** | the structure **tracks the player's channeling trail** — it forms over, and moves toward, the specific phase-locked pocket the player shed | [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §1.3 (the trail is local and indexed to the signature), §2.3 ("now indexed to your signature") |
| **Decoherence-wielder habitat** | a decoherence-wielder **is the willful cousin that aims deliberate venting AT the player** — the aimed-event class's intentional edge, told apart by the `(1−q)` signature and the restless-aurora sky read | [`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §4.1c/§6 (§4 the two are distinguished on every axis) |

**The punishment class's law, stated once:** an aimed event is the field
responding to *the player's trail* — ordered (a phase-stable structure, not a
disorganized front), targeted (it tracks the trail's location), persistent (it
accumulates while the hand is clean, decays when the trail stops). **It happens
*where the player is*.** A player facing it is facing a fact about themselves;
the honest response is to *stop channeling, change your hand, or leave*.

### 2.3 The one channel that separates them cleanly

The classifier needs **one discriminating read** that binds the five axes
together without asking for end-to-end `M`. The maintenance distinction of
[`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §3 supplies it: the *`ε²` gradient's shape
through time*.

| | Weather (a growl) | Punishment (a Coda's approach) |
|---|---|---|
| `ε²` gradient | **moving, rising, `c_s`-traveling** — a front that propagates and passes | **persistent, margin-resident, rising-as-it-accumulates** — a structure that grows *in place* toward a trail |
| Phase structure | **disorganized** (no coherent phase locking the front) | **phase-stable** (a coherent phase matched to the trail) |
| Tracking | none — it happens where the field is | **tracks the trail** — it forms over and moves toward the player's channeling pocket |
| Tide-correlation | the short event the tide's phase *frames* (frequency rises at thin) | the accumulation the tide's phase *exposes* (habitat widens at thin), but caused by the trail, not the season |
| What it answers | a fact of place and season | a fact about the player |

This is the *derived* distinction: **weather is a passing front correlated with
the field's own state; an aimed event is a persistent structure correlated with
the player's own trail.** The classifier reads both correlations off the
time-series of the published channels — the front's propagation vector (does it
move at `c_s` or accumulate in place?), the phase-stability of the `ε²`
elevation (disorganized front vs phase-locked pocket, the `growl vs scar` line of
[`field-music.md`](./field-music.md) §2.2/[`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §3.2),
and whether the structure's position tracks the player's channeling acts
(position deltas vs the trail's location history).

## 3. The provenance probe, pre-registered

The load-bearing measurement behind the whole distinction — and the answer the
whole danger layer's honesty waits on — is
[`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) **open-Q2 (storm provenance)**, restated
here as the storm's provenance probe. (The corpus does not number it among its
engine gaps — the engine-gap list in [`chunk-field-quantization.md`](./chunk-field-quantization.md)
§6 has three items, none about storm provenance; this doc anchors on open-Q2 as
the authoritative question and flags the "engine-gap" framing **[assumption]**):

> **Can the field, on its own live field with terrain-coupling + damping,
> *spontaneously* hold an ordered `c_s` `ε²` structure at all — or does it always
> need an injection?**

The answer decides what storms *are*, and therefore whether the weather class is
a pure-presentation reading or requires a designed source. It is pre-registered
the corpus way — the statistic, the decision tree, the stopping rule, and the
honest verdicts, *including the legitimate negative* — before any storm-weather
or storm-punishment gameplay follows.

### The probe

| Probe step | Action | Deciding output |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Run the **Phase-1.5 living-terrain demo** — the coupled box per [`chunk-field-quantization.md`](./chunk-field-quantization.md) §1.2 (reconciled box, `dt = 0.05`), with normal terrain coupling + RealSim damping ([`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md) §6a's field-coupling caveat) — from a seeded IC with **no injected perturbation source**. | a long temporal + spatial series of the published `ε²`/`q` fields. |
| **P2** | Scan the series for regions where `ε² = (EY−φ·EI)²` rises out of lock **as an ordered `c_s`-traveling front** (a coherent `∇ε²` propagating at `c_s = h₀/dt`) versus mere ambient noise / local `ε²` spikes. **Detect order, not magnitude** — the probe asks whether the field organizes a *moving structured front on its own*, not just whether `ε²` fluctuates. | a per-event classification: **spontaneous ordered front** vs **transient noise** vs **irreducible — no spontaneous order**. |
| **P3** | Against the P2 events, test the **stopping rule** (below): do the detected fronts *peak and pass* (the re-lock re-binds) or *persist*, and does a spontaneous front ever grow to an amplitude that crosses a material dissolution floor ([`material-regimes.md`](./material-regimes.md) §2)? | the storm's self-limiting profile on the spontaneous front. |
| **P4** | Deciding verdict: **A — weather holds** (the field spontaneously organizes and re-binds a `c_s` `ε²` front under coupling + damping) vs **B — weather does not hold** (the field needs an injection to organize any moving `ε²` structure). | the verdict this probe exists to produce. |
| **P5** | **(Tide-correlation check, fold of P4.)** Re-seed the same field over a regional-`q` trough vs a harvest baseline (the thin-vs-harvest states of [`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §1.2) and re-run P2–P4. Does any spontaneous order, if it exists, *correlate with the trough* (weather is seasonal) or appear field-state-independent? | a verdict on whether spontaneous storms, if real, are tide-correlated. |

### The decision tree

```
Run P1–P3. If the stopping rule is met first (P3), stop: the negative is recorded.
Else complete P2 and apply the decision rule.

P4 verdict = "A — weather holds"
   ⟹  storms ARE the field's own extreme. The classifier is a PURE READ
       (§4a): the weather class is real from the channels alone, the storm
       needs no designed source. Proceed to the tide-correlation check (P5).
P4 verdict = "B — weather does not hold"
   ⟹  a spontaneous c_s ε² front never survives coupling + damping. The storm
       is NOT weather in the naive sense. This is a LEGITIMATE FINDING, not a
       blocker — it changes what the storm IS: the field cannot spontaneously
       organize an ε² front, so every ordered front carries an external or
       designed source. The provenance of that source (a planted resonance, a
       wielding hand, a Coda forming) IS the provenance question, and the
       classifier becomes the world's own detector of it (§4b).
```

### The stopping rule

- **Stopping rule (early negative):** if P1–P3 run a pre-registered simulated
  field-time window (an order-of-magnitude multiple of the mixing clock
  `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]` at the box's regional `q`; **[probe]** the exact window is
  set before the run) and **no ordered `c_s` front ever self-organizes** — every
  `ε²` elevation is transient local noise that relocks without propagating as a
  structured front — the probe returns **"B — weather does not hold"** without
  a longer scan. The stopping rule is recorded *before* the run, exactly as the
  tide probe's T1–T4 ([`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §5a)
  and the ecology's silhouette probe ([`field-emergent-ecology.md`](./field-emergent-ecology.md)
  §1.4) pre-register theirs.
- **Determinism check (fold of P3):** re-run the same seed; a spontaneous front,
  if it exists, must re-organize at the same place/time (the field is
  deterministic, [`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §6d's hard gate). A
  front that appears once and never again is noise, not a claim.

### The honest verdicts — what each means for the design

| Verdict | What it says | What the design does |
|---|---|---|
| **A — weather holds** | the field's own extreme can spontaneously organize and re-bind a `c_s` `ε²` front | **storms are field extremes.** The provenance classifier is a **pure read** — it reads the storm's real front and classifies it. The weather class needs no designed source; the desert, the storm's season (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §2), and the Coda's *formation* (an ordered `ε²` margin structure is credible spontaneously — [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §7a) are all licensed by this verdict. |
| **B — weather does not hold** | the field cannot hold a spontaneous `c_s` `ε²` structure under coupling + damping | **the storm is NOT weather in the naive sense.** Every ordered `ε²` front carries a source — and the source's provenance (a **planted resonance** unwinding, a **wielding hand** venting, a **Coda forming** around a trail) IS the provenance question. **The classifier becomes the world's own detector**: whatever ignited the front, the provenance read names it. The honest frame shifts from "the storm is weather" to "storms are always *somebody's* storm — the classifier tells you whose." This is the legitimate negative: it does not block the danger layer, it *reframes* it. |

**Both verdicts are pre-registered and both are acceptable.** The probe is not a
gate that must pass for the design to be honest — it is a measurement that
decides *which honest frame the storm gets*. The design commitments that survive
either verdict are: the provenance classifier reads the storm's real channels
(§2); the player can be wrong about the class (§4); determinism, accessibility,
and the no-free-energy gate (weather can't be farmed, [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md)
§5.3) hold regardless.

## 4. The honest line

### 4a. The axes are real; the rule is [design], probe-calibrated

The classifier's **axes read real storm quantities** — the front's `c_s`-vector,
its phase structure, its persistence, its tracking of the player's trail, its
tide-phase correlation (§2) — all derived reads over the published channels.
The **weather-vs-punishment decision rule** — how long a storm must persist to
be "not self-limiting," how phase-stable the `ε²` elevation must be to be "aimed,"
how close a structure's position must track the trail to be "punishment," and the
relative weights of the five axes — is **[design]**, tuned against the
provenance probe's P4 verdict and the tide-correlation check (P5), exactly as
[`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §3's maintenance-axis thresholds are
probe-calibrated. The line is never blurred:

> **The classifier reads the law; the decision rule is designed and flagged.**

### 4b. Verdict-B makes the classifier the world's own detector

If the provenance probe returns **"weather does not hold"** (§3), the weather
class collapses as a *spontaneous* event, and the classifier's job sharpens: it
no longer separates "field's own extreme" from "aimed at you" — it detects *whose
storm this is*. Each source leaves a matching signature:

| Source | Its provenance signature (read) | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| **The field's own ambient dynamics** | a front with no trail-correlation, no plant-signature, tide/`q`-correlated | [`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §2 (weather is seasonal) |
| **A planted resonance** (a seed unwinding) | a front that re-opens the local most-organized state — `q` rising, `ε²` falling at its origin, a genesis signature | [`resonance-seeds.md`](./resonance-seeds.md) §3.1 (planting re-opens the attractor); a seed is treated as testable, never asserted (§6) |
| **A wielding hand** (a decoherence-wielder) | a front that **tracks** the wielder, shed from a clean phase-matched vent, with the `(1−q)` wasteful glow + restless-aurora sky read | [`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §4.1c (the wielder's tell), §4.3 (storm-ignition as weapon) |
| **A Coda forming** | a margin-resident structure that **tracks the player's channeling trail** and accumulates while the hand is clean | [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §2 (accumulates toward the trail), §3.2 (the tell) |

In verdict-B the classifier is **the world's own detector** — the honest line
that turns "this storm is happening" into "and here is whose storm it is." It is
the signature-predator's whole premise made read-only: before any Coda needs to
be engineered, the field announces whether a given extreme is ambient, planted,
wielded, or aimed — and a player can read that.

### 4c. Misattribution guards — the player can be wrong

The player must be able to mistake the class, because a provenance reader that
always told the truth would be a HUD, not a read. The designed failure modes are
the corpus's own:

- **A storm misread as weather that is actually a Coda's first pass is a real,
  designed failure.** The player shelters ("let it pass") and the field has
  already indexed the structure to their trail ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md)
  §2.3) — the cost is the accumulation they let grow while they waited out a
  "weather event." The world **can lie by omission** (it does not announce which
  class it is; the classifier's confidence is a real read), **but it cannot lie
  by commission** (the classifier never reports a false class — a misread is the
  player's misreading of honest channels, never the field fabricating a signal).
- The guard rails of this honesty are the same **misattribution guards the corpus
  already uses** ([`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §3): a **maintained lock vs
  a deliberate vent** (does the structure pulse to hold a target, or climb to
  shed disorder?) and the audio **growl vs scar** ([`field-music.md`](./field-music.md)
  §2.2) — a rising front vs a static plateau. Where the classifier is genuinely
  ambiguous at the noise floor — a thin-tide storm and a Coda's first accumulation
  both read "rising `ε²` over a low-`q` region" — that ambiguity is *flagged as a
  low-confidence class*, and the player is left with the uncertainty a living
  world honestly presents, never a forced false verdict.

### 4d. Determinism — same field state → same provenance class (hard)

The field is deterministic (one PDE; [`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §6d).
Therefore, exactly as [`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §6d makes its
classifier deterministic:

> **Same field state (and same player trail) → same provenance class.** The
> classifier is a pure function of the published channel time-series over its
> classification window — deterministic, with no hidden randomness. A storm
> reloaded or re-approached yields the identical class; weather and punishment
> read the same way every time.

This is a **hard gate**, not a nice-to-have: **"is this weather or is it about
me?" must be a *true* question about the field** — reproducible, learnable,
honest. It is what lets the player *learn* the provenance read (the skill of
reading the weather is only learnable if the weather reads back the same way), and
it is what keeps the danger layer's honesty from being a dice roll.

## 5. What changes for the player

The two classes are not a theming choice — they are **two different games** at
the moment an `ε²` extreme arrives, and the design commits that both are fully
playable and fully honest. The same channels, two honest framings, designed into
the Weatherglass's form, the sound, and the sky:

### 5.1 If the storm is weather — learn it, shelter, let it pass

A storm read as weather is a fact of **place and season**. The player's loop is
deeply familiar from the corpus's existing danger layer:

| Act | What it is | Channel it reads |
|---|---|---|
| **Read it** | the Weatherglass's climbing `ε²` glow ([`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md) §1.2), the rising growl ([`field-music.md`](./field-music.md) §2.2), the restless aurora sky ([`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md) §3.3) — the storm is **readable before it arrives** ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §5.1) | `ε²` front, sky, sound |
| **Shelter** | a high-`q` refuge — a Qi bath / coherence-barrier regime holds local `q` against the incoming `ε²` so structures resist the dissolution floor while the front passes | `q` (refuge), `ε²` (front) |
| **Let it pass** | the front's self-limiting arc — the `−ω₀²(EY−φ·EI)` re-lock re-binds, terrain heals, scars remain as archaeology | `ε²` (falling), `q` (rising) |

The ethic underneath: **the world is honest.** The storm is not aimed at anybody;
the danger is a fact of place and season, and the player who *learned the weather*
(the tide's phase, the regional `q`, the front's `c_s` cadence — [`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §4's legibility rule) is never caught by it. The storm is a fact of the world, answerable with the world's own tools (read, shelter, let pass) — never with a damage stat and never by blaming the world.

### 5.2 If the storm is punishment — stop channeling, change your hand, leave

An extreme event read as aimed is a fact **about the player**. The response is
the signature-predator's counterplay set ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §4), and it is fundamentally different from sheltering:

| Act | What it is | Channel it reads |
|---|---|---|
| **Stop channeling** | the aimed event's persistence is a function of *your trail* — stop shedding the clean, phase-stable `ε²` pocket and the resonance decays | `ε²` trail, phase-stability, `M`-legibility (§2.2) |
| **Change your hand** | vent messily (M ≈ 0 camouflage), phase-mask (a private φ-offset the structure cannot lock onto), or anti-corrupt the refuge the structure cannot enter | `M_stability`, vent shape |
| **Leave** | the aimed structure tracks *your trail* — remove the player from the region and the trail fades unmaintained; the world responds to your absence | position (trail locale), trail half-life |

The ethic underneath: **the world is responsive.** The danger is a fact about
you, and the honest response is to change what you are doing — never to fight the
field's aimed structure (you cannot damage a field pattern's HP), only to stop
being its target. A player who reads the aimed class correctly is not punished;
they are *told the truth about their own hand* and given the counterplay.

### 5.3 The ethics of a world that can punish

> **A world that punishes must be readable as punishing, or it is a cruel world.**

The provenance classifier is the design that keeps the world honest in **both**
frames:

- **In the weather frame,** the world is honest by being *readable*: every storm
  is announced, passes, and leaves scars — the player can learn it and never be
  victimized by an unreadable fact of place.
- **In the punishment frame,** the world is honest by being *readable-as-punishing*:
  an aimed event's signature — the phase-stable, trail-tracking, persistent
  structure that passes when you stop channeling (§2.2) — is a *readable*
  truth about the player, not a silent judgment dropped from nowhere. The
  player who learns to read the aimed class is never *trapped* by a punishment
  they could not have seen coming — they are always, at minimum, given the
  counterplay (stop, change your hand, leave).

The **cruel-world failure mode** is precisely the one the classifier closes: a
world that punishes *without a readable provenance* — where the player cannot tell
"this is weather, wait it out" from "this is about me, change my hand" — despoils
the danger layer's honesty ([`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §1). The
classifier's whole design is that the player *is* given the read that separates
the two, so the world cannot punish opaquely. **The world may lie by omission
(the classifier's confidence is a real read, never inflated); it cannot lie by
commission (no class is fabricated); and it can never punish without leaving the
readable trace that says so.**

### 5.4 The same channels, two honest framings

| Channel | Weather form (it happens *where the field is*) | Punishment form (it happens *where the player is*) |
|---|---|---|
| **Weatherglass / reader** ([`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md) §1.2) | climbing `ε²` glow that *peaks and falls* as the front passes | climbing `ε²` glow that *keeps climbing and tracks you*, or a growing coherent lume rising in a dead zone | 
| **Sound** ([`field-music.md`](./field-music.md) §2.2/§2.3) | the **growl** — a rising `ε²` front that passes into healing | a **persistent rising** structure, or the desert-silence inversion (the only ordered thing in flat deadness) |
| **Sky color / motion** ([`atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md`](./atmosphere-orbits-auroras.md) §1.3/§3.3) | a weather band that darkens, passes, and clears — the envelope's storm, seasonal | a restless aurora *tracking your motion / over your trail* — the sky reading a structure aimed at you |

Both are the **same published channels** read through the same Weatherglass and
the same sky. The player who learns to read the difference — to feel "the field is
raising itself" versus "the field is reading *me*" — has learned the provenance
read, and that read is the whole point: **the danger layer's honesty is not that
the world never offers a storm, but that the player can always tell what the storm
is.**

### (c) Determinism (hard)

Same field state + trail → same provenance class (§4d). A hard gate — the

### (d) Accessibility — the provenance read is a layer, never the only channel

The provenance read is a **layer of the reader / Weatherglass / sound / sky** —
the same channels, one more classification — **never the only channel**, per

glanceable, honest *framing*; the underlying read stays on the shared, accessible
channels.

### (e) The tide-correlation check — a storm at a thin trough vs a harvest storm must book differently

The tide is the weather's **season, not its cause** ([`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §1: a pacing lens, "it does not inject anything, change any constant, or add a channel"). The provenance read must therefore **book a thin-trough storm and a harvest storm differently**: a storm arriving at a `q` trough is the season's expected weather (the field is already decohered, the front finds it ready to dissolve — [`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §2); a *harvest* storm (regional `q` high, `ε²` low) is the *anomaly* — at the field's most organized, a `c_s` front igniting on its own is either the spontaneous weather the P4-verdict-A licenses, or (verdict-B) **a designed/aimed source's storm**, which the provenance read must flag as such. **A harvest storm cannot be dismissed as "just weather in season"; the classifier must carry the tide phase as an axis** (P5) so the anomaly books as an anomaly, keeping the tide the weather's season and never the weather's hidden cause.

### (f) The no-free-energy discipline

The provenance read of a storm adds no droppable source and no farmable drain —
it classifies an existing field extreme, it does not mint coherence. The §6 cap
([`energy-harnessing.md`](./energy-harnessing.md) §6; [`field-hazards.md`](./field-hazards.md) §5.3) that closes hazard-farming closes **provenance-farming** the same way: you cannot "tag a storm as planted" to harvest it, because the tagging changes nothing about the field's real channels, and a storm's drains are the same net-negative, amplitude-capped taps the corpus already gates. In verdict-B, the aimed class sheds nothing a collector can tap (the Coda's organized part, not a `(1−q)` waste fraction — [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §7e).

## 7. Feasibility verdict

**MEDIUM, in two honest slices.**

**Slice 1 — the provenance classifier (Phase-1, feasible now).** The weather-vs-punishment classifier is a **consumer of published channels** — a derived read of the storm's `ε²` front shape, phase structure, persistence, trail-tracking, and tide-phase, all off the ≈ 6 MiB publish the sampler already consumes ([`corpus-reconciliation.md`](./corpus-reconciliation.md)). No new channel, no new physics, no Q4 write — the same family-rule discipline as the Weatherglass ([`field-instruments.md`](./field-instruments.md) §2.1) and the Life-Signal ([`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §2). This slice is **Phase-1-able** — it is the *reading* of the storm, and it can ship as a pure-presentation layer on the existing danger-layer read: "this front is moving at `c_s`, disorganized, tide-correlated → weather" versus "this structure is phase-stable, persists, tracks your trail → aimed." The Phase-1 slice's honest span is **exactly the signature-predator's Phase-1 slice** ([`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §8): *"you leave a huntable signature"* becomes *"you can read whether the storm is weather or aimed"* — a read-only consequence of the storm's real channels, with zero new physics and no source yet.

**Slice 2 — the load-bearing claim (what storms ARE), gated on the Phase-1.5 provenance probe.** The *classifier* is Phase-1, but **the load-bearing claim — that storms are weather — gates on the Phase-1.5 probe (§3)**. Verdict-A licenses "storms are the field's extreme, weather is real" (and with it the storm's tide-season and the Coda's spontaneous formation). Verdict-B reframes "storms are always *somebody's* storm — the classifier tells you whose" (and forces the Coda's formation or aimed-resonance to be a designed trigger over an existing margin, honestly flagged). The design cannot claim either reading before the probe runs; it pre-registers the decision, then inherits whatever honest frame the field returns.

**Binding risks, in order:** **(a)** the provenance probe (§3) — does the field spontaneously hold an ordered `c_s` `ε²` structure, and does it tide-correlate (P5)? This is the deciding gate, shared with the storm's season, the Coda's formation, and the decoherence-wielder's storm-ignition. **(b)** the [design] decision rule's calibration (§4a) — the axes are real, but the weather-vs-punishment thresholds must be probe-tuned and never presented as engine-real. **(c)** determinism (§4d) — a hard gate, unbreakable. **(d)** the tide-correlation check's anomaly booking (§6e) — a harvest storm must read as the anomaly it is, keeping the tide the season and never the cause. None contradicts the async, dual-world, or regime-collapse architecture — the provenance read is a *consumer* of the publish, the same way the reader, the Weatherglass, sound, and the sky are, reading the storm's extremes the field's determinism already guarantees.

> **The honest statement that makes this doc load-bearing: the danger layer has
> never answered whether an extreme `ε²` event is the field's own weather (learn
> it, shelter, let it pass) or something aimed at the player (stop channeling,
> change your hand, leave) — and the whole layer's honesty hangs on the answer.
> This doc commits that the distinction is real, readable, and designed: a
> provenance classifier over the storm's own published dynamics — weather is
> ordered, bounded, self-limiting, and follows the law where the field is;
> punishment, if it exists, is ordered, targeted, persistent, and aimed where the
> player is. The load-bearing measurement — can the field spontaneously hold an
> ordered `c_s` `ε²` structure at all — is pre-registered the corpus way, and
> both verdicts are honest: either storms are the field's extreme (and the
> classifier is a pure read), or every storm carries a source (and the classifier
> becomes the world's own detector of whose storm it is). Either way, the player
> is never trapped by an unreadable punishment — the world may lie by omission,
> never by commission, and a world that punishes is always readable as punishing,
> which is what keeps it honest in both frames.**

## Open questions

1. **The probe's stopping window (§3).** How long a pre-registered simulated
   field-time window counts as "no spontaneous order" before the early-negative
   verdict-B is credibly recorded — an order-of-magnitude-multiple of the mixing
   clock `T = 2π/[λ(1−q)]` is [probe], not fixed; the exact window is set before
   the run, following [`tide-of-the-attractor.md`](./tide-of-the-attractor.md) §5a's
   T1–T4 precedent. What duration separates "the field relocks faster than it
   organizes" from "the field organizes on too long a scale to be weather"?
2. **The harvest-storm anomaly (§6e).** When a `c_s` front ignites at the field's
   most organized state (harvest tide, high `q`), is that an honest
   spontaneous-weather book (verdict-A's license) or the clearest aimed/designed
   storm (verdict-B's flag)? The probe's verdict and P5's tide-correlation decide
   which; the design must state the book before the run.
3. **The `M`-availability shadow (§2.3).** The phase-stability axis (the aimed
   event's coherent-phase signature) is at its sharpest when the true phase-matching
   `M` is published per-trail — which Phase-1 does not budget (the same open line
   as [`signature-predator.md`](./signature-predator.md) §7 open-Q2, [`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §7 open-Q2). Without per-trail `M`, the classifier's aimed
   reading collapses to *persistence + trail-tracking + phase-stability-of-the-ε²-
   gradient* (a real, coarser read — [`life-signal.md`](./life-signal.md) §3.2's
   cadence-shape line) — sharp enough to separate the classes, but the true `M`
   would deepen the aimed class's signature. This is the honest limitation,
   exposed as an open question, never a blocker.
4. **Weather-as-drift in verdict-B.** If the probe returns verdict-B (no spontaneous
   front), does the storm-as-weather class survive as a *drift*-weather — a
   field-poor / tide-linked state where the *sources* (a wielding hand, a planted
   seed, a Coda) are simply more likely — or does it collapse entirely into
   "every storm is somebody's storm"? The former preserves a weak seasonal
   weather read; the latter is the fully-reframed aimed-first world. The design
   prefers the reframed reading if B returns (§4b), but the seasonal-drift gloss is
   open until P5's tide-correlation is measured.
5. **The planted-resonance storm's frame (§2.2/§3-verdict-B).** A storm whose
   provenance is a planted/held resonance (a seed unwinding, a founding act —
   [`resonance-seeds.md`](./resonance-seeds.md) §2/§3) is neither ambient weather
   nor aimed punishment — it is **engineered provenance**. Does the player read a
   planted storm as "this is the world's own deepest logic re-opening" (an
   *invitation*, not a punishment) or as "someone (including a past you) seeded
   this here"? The provenance read can separate a planted source from an aimed
   one; whether the player *should* read the planted storm as weather-adjacent or
   as deliberate is a designed decision this doc does not settle, matching
   [`resonance-seeds.md`](./resonance-seeds.md) §6's testable-never-asserted stance.
