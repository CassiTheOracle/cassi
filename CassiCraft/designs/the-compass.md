## 1. The Compass of Intent, stated once

The instrument family reads the field's state, rate, self, night, and the one *named* thing — but all six are reads of what the field *is*, at a place or toward a bound anchor: the reader aims at what is *here and named*, the Weatherglass glances what is *here*, the Clock times what is *here*, the Mirror reads *yourself*, the Lantern reads the ground *underfoot*, the Seeker points at *one Π-pattern you named*. None reads **the direction of the field's own organizing** — the nearest place, *unnamed and unasked*, where the field's busyness is actively reaching. `reason-field.md` designed the intent to be published (§2), and `chunk-field-quantization.md` §5 designed the sites that hold it — but no instrument *renders that direction to a player as a pointing*. This document closes that hole:

> **The Compass of Intent is the wearable site-map — an instrument that reads the nearest intent-channel the way the Lantern reads the near field: it shows the direction the field's busyness is reaching, a needle pointing toward the nearest site the field is actively organizing.** It reads the field's own *attention* — where the law is becoming — and makes it legible in the hand. It is the instrument of promise and warning together: the needle where ore is about to precipitate, where a basin is cohering, and where an intent is turning dangerous.

The composition the design leans on is that the intent is *already scheduled to be published*: `reason-field.md` §2.2 makes the intent-phase a small scalar-or-vector added to the meshless-site block of the ≈ 6 MiB publish (~32 KB f32 / 16 KB f16 for 8,192 sites, ≈ half a percent of the canonical publish), read off the site-organized persistent-Π structure the sites already carry. The Compass does **not** invent a ranged "sense of where the field organizes" out of nothing — **it aggregates the already-published intent-phase over the nearest intent-dense sites into a single needle pointing**, the way the Far Mirror aggregates a sparse far-window publish into a chart (`field-instruments.md` §3.2) and the Seeker aggregates a named well into a homing direction (`the-seeker.md` §2). The difference is the subject: the Seeker reads *one bound named anchor*; the Compass reads *the field's own nearest intent* — unnamed, the sites the field is organizing *through*, as a *directional* read.

The honest once-flag: the **intent-channel and the sites are `reason-field.md`'s / `chunk-field-quantization.md`'s [design]-over-engine-real** (with `reason-field.md` §6a's meshless/Π frontline gate and §6c's channel line + speculation flag); **this doc's directional aggregation and the needle's presentation are its own [design] over them**, probe-calibrated (§4). The two stacks are never blurred.

## 2. The directional read — what the needle reads, and how

### 2.1 The nearest intent-dense site is the read's subject

`chunk-field-quantization.md` §5 establishes the moving-Voronoi sites as the world's own load map: 8,192 sites (`ML_N1 = 16`, `2·16³`), Lloyd-relaxed *weighted on the coherence `q`* (`ML_LLOYD_P = 4`), so they cluster where the field is coherent — **the sites are "where the field is most organized"** (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §5; README "the physics is also the load map"). `reason-field.md` §2.1 fuses that with the memory claim: the sites that schedule chunks are **the sites the field is itself organizing through** — each carries its persistent-Π structure, and the intent-phase is that organization's busyness *and direction* made a printed quantity (`reason-field.md` §2.2: "the busyness/direction of the field's own organization, made a legible quantity; what the field 'wants' to do, in the sense that its coherence is already *going* there, is what intent-phase says").

**The Compass reads that direction.** Where the Weatherglass shows the near-field state glanceable and the Lantern casts it onto the ground at night, the Compass *locates the reach* — it resolves the *site-level* intent-channel as "that way, toward the nearest site the field is actively organizing, where its busyness is *dense*." The mechanics are deliberately un-mysterious:

- **The sites are already published** — the domain publishes 8,192 site positions + per-site coherence on each meshless rebuild (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §5; `async-field-domain.md` §2.2). The intent-phase is the ~32 KB addition to that same site block (`reason-field.md` §2.2). The Compass is a **consumer of that published site/intent block** — it reads what the domain already writes, the same strided read the sampler already does to turn 8,192 sites into the active-chunk map (§5's ≈ 50 µs, `chunk-field-quantization.md` §4.2).
- **A nearest-intent aggregation**: the Compass aggregates the intent-phase of the *nearest sites* — over a bounded sampling window around the player's position and the site set — into a **direction** (which way the field's organized busyness is densest/strongest, the direction of its reach) and a **range-band** (how far away that dense intent sits). It reads the density of the field's own attention, not the position of a named thing.
- **[design]** The exact aggregation — how the published intent-phases over the site window become a *pointing* — is this doc's designed surface, probe-calibrated (§4a), exactly as the Seeker's binding-and-pointing and the Weatherglass's lume mapping are their docs' designed surfaces over real channels (`the-seeker.md` §2.1; `field-instruments.md` §1.2). What is engine-real is the direction the needle points at: the intent-phase is the published read of the site-organized persistent-Π structure (reason-field §2.1), and the sites are real, published moving-Voronoi structures (chunk §5).

### 2.2 A consumer of the publish with a presentation idiom — the family rule, held

The Compass obeys the instrument family rule exactly (`field-instruments.md` §2.1):

> **The directional read is a consumer of the published site/intent channels with the needle's presentation idiom — never a new channel.**

- **It reads only the published channels.** The needle aggregates the *intent-phase* (reason-field §2.2) read off the *published sites* (chunk §5) over the Compass's window — the same channels the domain already writes. It adds no `compass`/`nearest-intent` field to the publish, asks the engine to publish nothing new, and is read-only (`coherence-magic.md` §5.1's discipline — it never touches physics state, and per `reason-field.md` §6d the single-writer seam holds: the domain writes intent, the Compass only reads it).
- **It aggregates, never senses.** The Compass does not "feel" the field's goal through an invisible ranged feeler. It aggregates the published intent-phases into a pointing — the same read the sampler already does to build the active-chunk map, *pointed*. This is what keeps it a consumer and not a new reader: the information it presents is already in the publish, resolved as a direction.
- **It reads the field's *own* intent, not a bound target.** Unlike the Seeker's single bound name (`the-seeker.md` §2.2), the Compass is not bound — it points toward the *nearest intent-dense site*, whatever and wherever that is. It is the site-map worn as a needle; it renders the whole live terrain's reaching, not one entry of a catalog. (The map is the catalog's recording instrument, `the-map.md`; the Compass is the *directional* read of the live sites the map would chart.)

### 2.3 Range — the needle's reach is the intent-channel's reach

The honest boundary of how far a compass points:

> **The Compass reads the nearest intent-dense site the field actually holds, at range from the player's position — it never invents a target, and its range is bounded by how far the intent-phase is legible in the published sites' read at the player's position.**

Two consequences:
- **Range is a real, decaying read.** A site's intent-phase is strongest at the site and its pull is legible out to where the site's organized busyness is distinguishable from the surrounding field. **[design]/[probe]** How far a site's intent resolves into a direction at the player's position is a probe-calibrated dial — the same range question as the Seeker's well-pull (`the-seeker.md` §2.3), posed for a *live site*. The Compass is honest about its reach: a nearest-intent too faint at the player's distance reads as "no pointing," not as a wrong one.
- **A needle is read at range, never invented at range.** The Compass does not extrapolate "the field must be reaching toward there" from absence. If the site set holds no dense intent at the player — the locality is idle, the intent-phase flat, the active sites far — the needle reads *nothing*, honestly (§3.2). It never points to fill the gap.

## 3. The promise-and-warning needle — three readings of one published intent

The Compass's needle points toward the nearest intent-dense site. The site's *meaning* — what the field is reaching to do there — is not carried by the direction alone; it is read from the *nature* of the intent-phase at that site, which the corpus has already designed three times. **A reaching site is a reaching site; whether it is ore, forming life, or a predator's resonance is the provenance question** (§3.4).

| Reading | The needle's reach | The intent it reads | Grounded in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The promise — harvest** | a reaching site where `q` is accumulating | the field's busyness cohering **ore about to precipitate** — a vein is where local `q` accumulates above a second threshold (`material-regimes.md` §3), the same coherence-accumulates claim the quantizer's ore channel already specifies (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §2.2) | material-regimes §3; chunk §2.2 |
| **The promise — go meet** | a cohering basin / condensation front | the field's busyness **forming a basin** — a coherent ridge or condensation front the field returns to (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §4.1/§4.2: the biome⇔regime mapping; a condensation front is "`q` accumulating, crossing `τ_c`" — "precipitation events — bodies condensing *out of* the front") | ecology §4; chunk §2.2 |
| **The warning — a too-insistent reach** | an intent turning dangerous | a site whose intent reads as **too-insistent** — the Coda's resonance, the field's *disorder* intent published the same way (`reason-field.md` §3.3), the persistent-Π pattern the local field holds in its ε² margins, accumulating as `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability` (`signature-predator.md` §2.2) | signature-predator §2; reason-field §3.3 |

### 3.1 The two promises

**The harvest promise.** `material-regimes.md` §3: "ore is not a worldgen height range; it precipitates where q accumulates above a second threshold — a vein is a region where the identity tuple's θ_c is exceeded not by ρ but by local `q`." A site whose intent-phase is dense and *cohering* — `q` rising, the field's busyness concentrating — is a site where ore is about to precipitate. The needle pointing there is the instrument of **promise**: the reaching site is a harvest waiting, and the player reads "the field is concentrating coherence here — go hoe it." This is the Compass as the miner's instrument: the live site-map that tells you where the law is about to mint a deposit, before the deposit is there. (The no-free-energy cap, §5d, keeps this honest: the needle *names* the future precipitation'stendency, it does not cause or extract it — the harvesting itself is the player's field act.)

**The life promise.** `field-emergent-ecology.md` §4: a coherent ridge is a biome (high `q`, low `ε²`, deep layered φ-locked organisms); a condensation front is "`q` accumulating, crossing τ_c" — "precipitation events — bodies condensing out of the front." A site whose intent is dense *and cohering into a structure* — not the flat scatter of a living plain but the rising, organizing concentration of a forming basin — is where the field is about to condense something living. The needle pointing there is the instrument of **go meet the forming life**: the player reads "the field is composing an organism/pool over there — go be present for its becoming." This is the Compass as the ecologist's and the farmer's instrument — the site-map told as a beckoning rather than a chart.

### 3.2 The warning — a too-insistent reach

**The warning promise.** `signature-predator.md` §2: the Coda is "a persistent pattern the local field holds in its ε² margins," accumulating toward a trail with `R = ρ_signature · τ · M_stability`; `reason-field.md` §3.3 makes it first-class — **the Coda is the field's disorder-intent, published the same way** as any NPC's coherent intent. To the Compass, a Coda's forming resonance is exactly a site whose intent-phase is *dense and insistent* — but with a *different* character: it is the field's busyness concentrating not on coherence but on a too-insistent, non-willful reach around a signature (`signature-predator.md` §2.1: "a pattern that forms *around your signature*"). When the player has been channeling cleanly in a desert margin (the legible trail, §1.2), the nearest intent-dense site may *be* the Coda's accumulating sound-hole — **the needle pointing there is the instrument of warning:** "the field is reaching too insistently here, and it is reaching toward you."

The needle's warning is grounded in the same published intent as its promises — reason-field §3.3 publishes the field's disorder honestly beside its order, and the Compass reads both the same way. What separates the warning from the promises is the *nature of the reach* (its ε²/phase character, its insistent concentration against a flat margin), which is the provenance question of §3.4.

### 3.3 The honest line — the needle's meaning is [design]; the ambiguity is inherited

>The **direction** the needle points is this doc's [design] aggregation of the *published intent-channel* (reason-field §2); the **meaning** of that direction — whether the dense site it reaches is a precipitating vein, a forming basin, or a Coda's resonance — is this doc's [design] read over the same channel, and it **inherits the corpus's provenance discipline** (`weather-not-storm.md` §4a; `life-signal.md` §6): a reaching site is a reaching site. **The needle shows the reach honestly; whether that reach is ore, life, or predator is a provenance classification over the real intent/ε²/q, weather-not-storm's discipline at site scale.**

The Compass never pretends to tell the player *which* of the three a dense site is from the direction alone — it points toward the nearest intent-dense site, and the player reads the site's nature the way they read any field locale (the reader's `q`/`ε²`/`∇(g·Φ)` samples, the Weatherglass's forms, the desert-tell of `signature-predator.md` §3). Like the Lantern's wrong-warm vs. its flicker (`the-lantern.md` open-Q3 — a rising ε² front vs. a Coda's sound-hole, separated by the provenance probe), the Compass's needle carries the direction; the *classification* of the site it points at remains the provenance question the corpus already owns. **[design]** The needle's *presentation* — how it signals a reach's character (e.g. a steady promise-point vs. a too-insistent pulsing when the classification is legible) — is this doc's designed idiom, probe-calibrated, never a new channel.

### 3.4 The provenance boundary at site scale

`weather-not-storm.md`'s discipline — "weather, not storm" — is a classifier over the danger layer (`weather-not-storm.md` §1), closed for storms by the provenance probe (`field-hazards.md` open-Q2). At **site scale**, the same discipline applies to the Compass's needle: the nearest intent-dense site is a single honest fact of the published intent-channel; *what* the field is reaching to do there (precipitate ore, condense life, or accumulate a resonance) is a provenance classification over the site's real `q`/`ε²`/intent-phase, gated on the same probes that separate weather from storm and a live Coda from a fossil (`signature-predator.md` open-Q2/§2.3's threshold shape; `field-archaeology.md` §7 open-Q3 / `life-signal.md` §3). **A compass that pointed "this dense site is definitely a predator" would be a physics lie; a compass that points "the field is reaching most densely there, and here is the character of that reach" is the honest promise-and-warning needle.** The classification reads inherit the corpus's gates; the *direction* reads the published intent directly.

### (c) Determinism — same field state → same pointing (a hard gate)

The field is deterministic (one PDE; `field-archaeology.md` §1.2; `life-signal.md` §6d), and `reason-field.md` carries it into the intent-channel (§2.2: intent is a pure function of the domain's own organization; §6b: determinism is *strengthened*, not weakened, when intent is domain-held). The Compass inherits it as a **hard gate**:

> **Same field state → same pointing.** The Compass's direction and nearest-dense-site are a deterministic function of (the published site intents × the player's position), mapped through the designed aggregation. A player who stands in the same spot at the same field state twice gets the same needle, every time; a reloaded world reads the same pointing from the same field. There is no seeded-RNG judgment about which site the needle "locks onto" — the nearest intent-dense site either reads as such at the player (and points deterministically) or does not (and points nowhere), reproducibly. **[design]** The aggregation thresholds are designed constants; the intent they read is deterministic.

### (d) The no-free-energy cap — a compass informs, never mints

`energy-harnessing.md` §6's cap (`output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`), inherited through the instrument family (the Seeker §5d, the Lantern §5d, the Clock §5d), applies hard:

> **The Compass informs, never mints.** It is a *read* (of the published intent at the player) cast as a pointing — it converts no coherence, sheds no `(1−q)`, writes nothing, and grants the player no find, no material, no coherence they did not earn by the field's own movement. **There is no reach that generates:** the needle does not *produce* the precipitating ore, the forming basin, or the warning — it points at the intent the field already holds, and the actual harvest, meeting, or avoidance is the field's own act (mining `material-regimes.md` §3; farming `field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.2; the Coda's counterplays `signature-predator.md` §4), not the instrument's. A compass that "pulls" the field's intent toward the player, or that reads as a free locate-and-extract of a precipitating vein, is a physics lie the cap forbids.

Three honest consequences:
1. **No "point-to-precipitate."** Knowing where the field is concentrating coherence does not let you *accelerate* the ore's precipitation or tap it at range; the pointing is a read of the intent, bounded by what the field already does, with no write-back that could mint (`energy-harnessing.md` §6; `coherence-magic.md` §5.1; reason-field §6d keeps the single-writer seam — the Compass never perturbs the intent it reads).
2. **The Compass does not change the field it reads.** A needle does not pull the dense site toward the player, raise a local `q`, or alter the intent — it shows the reach's direction; it never alters it. (A *channeler* or *re-authorer* who acts on the pointing does the field act of `coherence-magic.md`/`material-regimes.md` — that is theirs, not the needle's.)
3. **The needle's honesty is the no-mint bound's form.** Because there is no reach that generates, there is also no *fake* reach to sell: a player cannot use a Compass to present a dense site's promise (or warning) the field does not hold. The no-false-direction of §2.3 and the no-free-energy cap of §5d are the same honest boundary, viewed from the read and the economy.

### (e) Accessibility — the intent read is never hidden-only

The corpus's hard accessibility rule holds (`field-music.md` §6; `field-instruments.md` §1.3; `the-lantern.md` §5e):

> **The Compass's needle is an enhancement of the read — the same intent is derivable from the site-map/reader, never hidden-only.** Everything the Compass points at — the nearest intent-dense site, its direction, its character — is readable, more laboriously, from the **reader overlay** (`coherence-magic.md` §2, sampling `q`/`ε²`/`∇(g·Φ)` at the site) and from the **site-map/week-chart** (the published sites are the active-chunk map the sampler builds, `chunk-field-quantization.md` §5, which a player can render as a chart the way the Far Mirror renders a sparse read, `field-instruments.md` §3.2). A player who never crafts or carries a Compass loses *nothing informational*: they can still learn where the field is dense by walking with the reader, or compute the nearest active site from the site-map. The Compass is the *pointed* form of the same shared publish — the site-map worn as a needle — never a second information source and never a *requirement* for finding the reachable, because the same truth is always on the map/reader.

**[design]** The needle's presentation (how the direction and range are shown) is the idiomatic layer; the sites and intent it presents are the real published ones. The accessibility parity is structural via the shared-publish architecture, not a tuning goal.

2. **The reach's character — the provenance classification (§3.4).** When the needle points at a dense site, can the promise (ore / basin) vs. warning (Coda) be read from the site's real `q`/`ε²`/intent-phase, or does the Compass need the provenance line (weather-not-storm / life-signal / the desert-tell) settled first? The instrument's honest default is to point and let the player read; whether the needle *also* signals the reach's character is the [design] dial, inheriting the shared provenance discipline. **[probe]**
3. **The idle/no-reach presentation (§2.3).** When the locality is idle or the nearest dense intent is out of range, does the needle read "no pointing" (an honest absence), or should the Compass also hand off to the map's site-chart ("the nearest active site is out of range, but the map remembers this way")? The map-complement is designed (the Compass points, the map charts and remembers); the hand-off dial is **[design]**, Phase-1.5.
4. **The promise-vs-warning threshold's noise-floor honesty (§3.4/§4b).** When a dense site sits near the Coda's habitat boundary (a too-insistent reach forming in a thin margin), the Compass must not over-claim a warning from an ordinary dense reach. The same noise-floor/provenance honesty as the Lantern's wrong-warm (`the-lantern.md` open-Q3), the Silence's hunting register, and `life-signal.md` §6; the classification dial is **[design]/[probe]**. An ambiguous reach must read as "unclear," never a false promise or a false scare.
5. **The intent-channel's shape — scalar vs. directional intent-phase (§2.1).** Does the published per-site intent read best as a single scalar (busyness magnitude, weakest claim) or as the small vector/phase (the direction of the organization, `reason-field.md` §2.2's "phase + rate of change") the Compass's *direction* wants? The needle's directional claim is sharpest with the vector; the scalar is the honest fallback if the probe (`reason-field.md` §6a) only confirms busyness, not direction. **[probe]**
