# The Wage: The Paid Share — Labor Priced, the Market's Time-Honored Form

**Question under design:** the corpus's economy has the *exchange* (the Market
books the trade, `the-market.md`), the *unpaid giving* (the Gift refuses the
book, `the-gift.md`), the *debt* (the Loan borrows forward, `the-loan.md`), the
*funding of the commons* (the Commons-Tithe, `the-commons-tithe.md`), the
*border charge* (the Toll, `the-toll.md`), the *return and store* (the Compost,
the Granary), the *extraction* (the Quarry) — but **no wage**: nothing designs
the *paid sharing of labor*. A member who works another's plot, forge, or
harvest is apprenticed (paid in learning, `the-apprenticeship.md`), sung
(shared rhythm, `the-working-song.md`), or tithe-funded (the commons' upkeep,
`the-commons-tithe.md`) — but **never priced**. Labor in the corpus is learned,
shared, and funded; it is never *bought*. Minecraft's work-for-order is yet
another undealt drag-drop: a player "hires" another with a handshake and a
server rule, outside the field, with no book and no trust. This document
designs the Wage: **the paid share — the economic primitive that prices labor
the way the Market prices goods** — a member works another's plot, forge, or
harvest and is **paid in held order**, the way a trade books a held thing. It
is the economy's **labor-flow**, the Market's own time-form (the exchange
whose "thing" is the worker's time), and the **honest alternative to the Gift**
for a member who will not tithe.

> **The Wage is the paid share — the economic primitive that prices labor the
> way the Market prices goods.** A member works another's plot, forge, or
> harvest (`farm-that-feeds.md`'s farmer, `house-that-steers.md`'s builder)
> and is paid in **held order** — the same order a trade transfers, the
> worked-for's own held store, booked on the shared ledger as a Q4 op of the
> settled record's shape. Where the Market books the exchange of *things*
> (`the-market.md` §2.1), the Wage books the exchange of *labor for order*:
> the worker's day is the priced thing, the worked-for's held order is what it
> buys. **The Loan pays now and borrows forward; the Wage pays for work done
> — the present-tensed, priced sharing of labor, the market's honest
> time-form.**

## 1. The Wage, stated once

The corpus's economy is present-tensed and *thing*-tensed. The Market books
the exchange of *things* — "my harvest for your pick," a Q4 op of the settled
record's shape, trustworthy by construction (`the-market.md` §1/§2.1). The
Gift refuses the book — the un-tallied giving (`the-gift.md`). The Loan
defers the exchange forward (`the-loan.md`). The Tithe funds the commons
(`the-commons-tithe.md`), the Toll charges the border (`the-toll.md`). But
the corpus has **never priced a person's labor for another member**. When one
member works another's plot, forge, or harvest, the corpus currently offers
three unpaid frames: the apprentice learns (`the-apprenticeship.md` — paid in
*learning*), the group sings (`the-working-song.md` — the *unpaid rhythmic
shared* labor), the commons funds (`the-commons-tithe.md` — the shared
structures' upkeep). Minecraft's own work-for-order is a drag-drop with a
server rule — a "you owe me" handshake outside the field, no book, no trust,
the same hole the Market closed for things but no doc has closed for *time*.
That is the gap this document closes.

> **The Wage, stated once.** The paid share — the economic primitive that
> prices labor the way the Market prices goods. **A member works another's
> plot, forge, or harvest and is paid in held order** — the worked-for's own
> held store, the same order a trade transfers (`the-market.md` §2.2: the
> yield's magnitude, the alloy's stored coherence, the basin's held `q`),
> booked on the shared ledger as a Q4 op of the settled record's shape
> (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1). **The Wage is the economy's labor-flow**:
> how the market's goods get made by people who are neither apprenticing nor
> gifting — a settlement's work priced honestly, the way its goods are.

**Why "the market's time-form"?** A trade is the exchange of a thing *now*:
two lines re-allocate the same coherence, a transfer never a gain
(`the-market.md` §2.1/§6d). The Loan is that exchange deferred *forward*
(`the-loan.md` §1 — the same two lines, only the timing changes). **The Wage
is the Market's exchange whose "thing" is the worker's time** — the priced
labor-day, the exchange that exists because *making things takes a person's
work*. The market books goods; the wage books the labor that makes them. It
is the exchange's time-form: the same book, the same trust-by-law, the same
no-mint, pointed at the worker's day instead of a held item.

**The honest flag, once.** The pieces a wage composes are all landed — the Q4
op-record and the member-id at the single non-forgeable point
(`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1/§3), the per-member `C(M)` aggregation
(`shared-ledger.md` §1.2), the market's booked exchange and no-false-booking
(`the-market.md` §2.1/§2.3), the farm's bounded Q4 writes (`farm-that-feeds.md`
§3), the house's built work (`house-that-steers.md` §1), and the no-free-energy
cap (`energy-harnessing.md` §6, through `the-market.md` §6d). **The wage's
*composition* — that a labor-day *is* a purchasable, bookable op of the
settled record's shape, that its price is the book's read, that it is the paid
alternative to the gift — is this document's [design] over them.** No new
channel, no new physics, no "wage record" the engine writes beyond the Q4 op
it already writes: a wage is a *booked paid-labor day*, designed, exactly as a
trade is a booked exchange — not a new engine act.

The Wage is the corpus's **labor-economy doc** because it is the one place the
field's honesty is pointed at *the price of a person's work* — the social
primitive Minecraft never trusted, given the field's own book.

## 2. The price of work — the honest mechanics

The Wage prices labor the way the Market prices goods: a member works another's
plot, forge, or harvest and is **paid in held order**. Its *mechanics* compose
three landed pieces — the booked op-record's shape, the per-member `C(M)` read,
and the work's real field-writes — into a recurring, bookable paid-labor line.
The composition — the share's size, the book — is this doc's [design]; the
pieces it rides are landed.

### 2.1 The wage's form — a priced labor-day, booked the way any op is

A wage is **a Q4 op of the settled record's shape** (`schema-that-settles.md`
§2.1), pointed at the exchange of labor for order. Its *form* is this doc's
[design], over the settled record and the per-member `C(M)` read:

| The wage's form ([design]) | What it is | Grounded on |
|---|---|---|
| **`member`** | the **two hands a wage binds** — the worker's and the worked-for's member-ids at the single non-forgeable write | `schema-that-settles.md` §2.1/§3 — the world-writer attaches the id at *its* write; a wage's two lines are attributable because the book is, exactly as a trade's are (`the-market.md` §2.1) |
| **`op`** | the **paid labor** — a designed op over the bookable set; the exchange applied to a worker's day rather than a held thing | `the-market.md` §2.1's exchange-op dial; the op names the field act — a wage is an op of the bookable set |
| **`worldPos`** | **where the work was done** — the worked-for's plot, forge, or harvest cell, at cell granularity | `schema-that-settles.md` §2.3 — the cell-ownership rule; the farm's plot (`farm-that-feeds.md` §2.1), the house's built form (`house-that-steers.md` §1.1) |
| **`rung`** | **the work's rung** — the plot's crop-band depth, the forge's deep-rung discharge, the harvest's stored-coherence depth | the work's provenance books its rung, the way a traded item's does (`the-market.md` §2.2; `farm-that-feeds.md` §2.2) |
| **`magnitude`** | **the paid share** — the worked-for's held order transferred to the worker, the price of the day | the honest mass of order, booked, never made up (`farm-that-feeds.md` §4 — the yield's magnitude; `the-market.md` §2.1) |
| **`sustain`** | the **standing labor-contract's semantics** — a held work-period (a shift, a harvest) books as one standing record, never a re-emit spam | `schema-that-settles.md` §2.2 — a held op books as one compact record, the same way a held phase or a standing tithe does |

**The wage's bookable shape, stated once.** A wage is *not* a new record kind —
it is the same booked op the settlement already writes, pointed at the *exchange
of labor for order*: a worker's paid day on the worker's `member` line, the
worked-for's spent order on theirs, both reading into the same per-member
`C(M)` aggregation (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2). **The wage books the way any op
does** — `the-market.md` §2.1's settled-record honesty applied to a *labor-day*
instead of a one-off exchange. It is the market's time-form: the market books
the exchange of a thing, the wage books the exchange of a worker's time.

### 2.2 The work the wage pays for — the plot, the forge, the harvest, the house

The wage is not an abstraction — it prices **real field work whose writes are
already designed**. Each is grounded in its source doc's real quantity; the
*wage's form* (the paid share's size, its book) is this doc's [design].

| The work | What it is in field terms | The real writes the wage prices | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The plot (farm-that-feeds)** | a shallow rooted coherence store you tend — the bed's `q` held in the crop's band, harvested at tide-high | the farmer's **bounded Q4 writes**: weeding (anti-corruption), husbanding (the bed's Qi-bath), harvesting (the meal/withdraw op) — the work whose yield a wage can pay with | `farm-that-feeds.md` §3 (the acts), §7 (PHASE-1-ABLE); §4 (the harvest is stored coherence) |
| **The forge / a labor op** | a controlled discharge held within the fire-vs-explosive line — a deep-rung material's stored coherence drawn through | the smith's paced discharge writes (`material-regimes.md` §3, via `the-working-song.md` §3b) — the second repetitive field-op a wage can pay for | `the-market.md` §2.2 (a tool's alloy, provenance-booked); `the-tool.md` §2 |
| **The house (house-that-steers)** | coherence architecture — a building that steers the local field; a maintained bath-node | the builder's construct/heal writes — walls concentrate, a room holds a `q`-core, a hearth anchors; a house is a held shape of the field (`house-that-steers.md` §1), maintained or it collapses toward the attractor (§3) | `house-that-steers.md` §1/§2.2/§3 |

**The paid share is the worked-for's held order.** Whatever the work, the wage
pays in **order** — the worked-for's own held store: the stored yield of a
harvest worked for (`farm-that-feeds.md` §4), the stored coherence of an alloy
or a held basin (`house-that-steers.md` §1.1; `energy-harnessing.md` §3). It is
the **same order a trade transfers** (`the-market.md` §2.2) — the wage moves
that order from the worked-for's line to the worker's, as payment for a done
day. **The worker is paid in the worked-for's exchange** — a transfer of the
same coherence, never minted. And the **wage's form — its share's size** — is
this doc's [design]/[probe] dial over the settled record, set so a day's pay is
an honest fraction of the work's done value (never a fixed wage the worked-for
must mint out of nothing — the same two-margins-set discipline as the tithe's
share, `the-commons-tithe.md` §7 open-Q1).

**The wage's cost is real on both sides.** The worker spends a real labor-day
(the farm's writes, the house's holds — real field ops with real vent and
budget, `coherence-magic.md` §1); the worked-for spends real held order (the
paid share is a *withdrawal* from their line, `farm-that-feeds.md` §4's
withdrawal-never-a-mint). The wage is **never free**: no wage that pays without
a done day, no wage that shares without the worked-for's real held order
(§5d). This is the wage's honest price — the same costed-on-both-sides frame
the apprenticeship holds for the pair (§2.2 there) and the market holds for the
trade.

## 3. The three distinctions — the load-bearing design

Labor in the corpus was apprenticed, sung, and funded — **the Wage is the
first time it is *priced*.** The load-bearing design is that the three unpaid
frames are distinct from the paid wage, and the distinction is what gives the
wage its honesty.

### 3.1 The apprenticeship — paid in *learning*; the wage is paid in *order*

`the-apprenticeship.md` §1: the veteran holds a low-magnitude phase window open
and the student channels *inside* it — craft transfers **by resonance, not by
instruction**, and the student is paid **in learning**. That is the
apprenticeship's whole coin.

> **The apprenticeship is the second practice — the pair — and it is paid in
> learning: the student's coin is the feel of their own hand in phase with an
> expert's. The Wage is paid in the worked-for's order — the same held store a
> trade transfers — and it is the *contracted* day, not the tutored pair.**

The distinction, stated once: an apprentice works *beside* a veteran and is
paid in *craft* (the feel, the resonance, the transferring skill —
`the-apprenticeship.md` §3.1); a wage-earner works *for* a member and is paid
in *order* (the worked-for's held store, booked). A member who apprentices
learns; a member who takes a wage is paid. **The wage is the apprenticeship's
opposite coin** — where the apprentice's pay is the craft they keep, the
worker's pay is the order they carry off. The two may overlap in the world (an
apprentice may, on a later tide, be hired) — but the *frame* is distinct: the
apprenticeship transfers a skill, the wage transfers the worked-for's order.

### 3.2 The working-song — the *unpaid rhythmic shared* labor; the wage is the *contracted solo*

`the-working-song.md` §1: a group of workers phase-locked IS a working (`M ≈
1`), and the song is the honest, learnable, deterministic way to hold many
hands on one phase — labor that **is not priced**, that is *shared*, and that
is held only while sung (a song that stops IS a broken phase-lock).

> **The working-song is the *unpaid rhythmic shared* labor — the music a group
> does so its work becomes a coherent act, held only while sung, its gain the
> group's own composed `(1−q)` efficiency, never a price. The Wage is the
> *contracted solo*: one worker, one worked-for, priced in order, booked.**

The distinction, stated once: the working-song prices nobody — its gain is the
group's composed efficiency and its cost is the singers' attention
(`the-working-song.md` §4); the wage prices one worker's day against one
worked-for's order. A working-song is the commons' shared rhythm; a wage is a
**contract** — the two hands, the day, the price, booked. And the honest cost
is distinct too: a song that stops is a broken phase-lock (the group scatters),
a wage not paid is a **false op the field will not book** (§4 — the day was
done, the order was pledged, the book reads the truth). Where the song's honesty
is that it is *unpaid and shared*, the wage's honesty is that it is *priced and
booked*.

### 3.3 The tithe and the toll — which *fund the commons*; the wage *pays a person*

`the-commons-tithe.md` §1: the Tithe is the periodic, bookable, everyone-pays
contribution that **funds the shared order** — the bath's maintenance, the
Observatory's lamps, the School's lessons. `the-toll.md` §1: the Toll **charges
the outside** at the held door, funding the *border's* upkeep. Both point the
book's contribution stream *at structures and boundaries*.

> **The tithe funds the commons; the toll funds the door's upkeep; the Wage
> pays a person — for their work, in the worked-for's held order. The tithe
> and toll are the settlement's *income* toward its shared things; the wage is
> the *spent price of a done day.***

The distinction, stated once: a tithing member contributes a **share of their
own yield** to structures they do not personally keep; a wage-earner receives
the **worked-for's order in exchange for their own labor**. The tithe is
voluntary-but-expected for the whole window (`the-commons-tithe.md` §3); the
wage is **contracted** — it is one member's agreed payment to another for a
specific day. And this distinction is what makes the wage the **honest
alternative to the tithe** (§4.2): a member who will not tithe (who declines
the commons' share) can still be a full economic participant through the wage —
working, being paid, keeping their own line fed by their labor rather than by
the tithe's pooled funding. The wage is the one economic act whose *whole
meaning* is paying a person for work, not funding a structure or charging a
boundary.

### 3.4 The one line, stated once

> **Labor in the corpus was apprenticed (paid in learning), sung (shared,
> unpaid), and tithe-funded (the commons' upkeep). The Wage is the first time
> it is *priced*: the paid share — a member works another's plot, forge, or
> harvest and is paid in held order, booked on the ledger. Where the
> apprenticeship pays the worker in craft, the working-song pays nobody, and
> the tithe pays the commons, the Wage pays a person, for their work, in the
> worked-for's exchange — the economy's labor-flow, the market's time-honored
> form.**

## 4. The ledger's legibility — the load-bearing honesty

The Wage's honesty is the ledger's own, inherited from the market's trust-by-law
and the settled op-record. Two loads, both stated once.

### 4.1 A wage is bookable — a paid day is a line

> **A wage is bookable — a paid day is a line, the same trust-by-law as the
> market.** The wage's book is the settled op-record `{member, op, worldPos,
> rung, magnitude, sustain}` (`schema-that-settles.md` §2.1), written at the
> single non-forgeable point (§3), single-writer, no-false-booking
> (`shared-ledger.md` §6d) — exactly the book a trade writes. A worker's paid
> day books as an op of that shape; a worked-for's spent order books on their
> line; and because the op-stream is unforgeable (the world-writer is the only
> mutator, `async-field-domain.md` §5.2), **a labor-day cannot be lied about**:
> a worker cannot book a day the field did not execute, a worked-for cannot
> claim a paid share they never spent, and the wage's two lines read into the
> per-member `C(M)` exactly as a trade's do (`shared-ledger.md` §1.2).

This is the load-bearing claim, the wage's whole trust: **a wage that was never
paid is a false op the field will not book.** If the worked-for promises a
share for a done day and does not pay, the day is still booked (the world
executed it), the pledge is not — and the book reads the truth of what was
actually exchanged, the way a broken trade's promise is a false claim the field
does not hold (`the-market.md` §2.3; `the-oath.md` §3). **There is nothing to
enforce** — the wage's honesty is that it books, and the book is the field's
own record.

The wage's two lines on the ledger:

|---|---|---|

### 4.2 A wage is the honest alternative to the gift — the unpaid giving made optional

`the-gift.md` §1 is the economy's **generosity** — the deliberate no-trace
transfer, un-booked, no `C(M)` reads giver or receiver. The Gift is real, warm,
and it is *un-paid*: the giving that refuses the tally. But the corpus's
economy, before the Wage, has **no way to be paid** — no way for a member to
earn their place by labor rather than by the tithe's pooled funding or the
gift's unpaid kindness. The Wage closes that:

> **The Wage is the honest alternative to the Gift — the member who will not

> voluntary by the wage: a poor member keeps a place in the economy by
> trade-in-labor — working and paid, never forced and never free.** Where the
> Gift is the un-booked kindness the ledger cannot audit (`the-gift.md`
> §2.1/§2.2), the Wage is the **booked** exchange of labor for order — the
> paid face of the same giving, made legible. A member who refuses both the
> tithe and the gift is not cast out; they are **kept by the one act that
> needs neither**: the priced day.

**The honest register against "never free."** The Gift's honesty is that it is
un-auditable (`the-gift.md` §2.3); the Wage's honesty is the opposite and the
complement — it is *auditable*, booked, and its payment is real. The wage

   a [design] dial against the settled record, inheriting `the-market.md`

   book without escalation? The honest default leans to the book's own read

5. **The wage's place in the census's active class (§4.1/§2.2).** A wage-earning

   window's working population; whether a pure wage-earner (who neither tithes
   nor gifts) reads *fully* active or a distinct class is a [design] dial over
   the census's class aggregation, set so the wage is a *recognized* place, not
