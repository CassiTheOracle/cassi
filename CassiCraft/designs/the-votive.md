# The Votive: The Giving-to-the-Field — an Offering Left at a Place, the Gift's Other Face

**Question under design:** the corpus designs the giving's economic faces — the
Gift is the **member-to-member** un-booked giving, the deliberate no-trace
transfer that refuses the tally (`the-gift.md` §1/§2.1); the Commons-Tithe is
the **commons-funding**, the booked share that pays the shared order
(`the-commons-tithe.md` §1); the Toll is the **border charge**, the held-door
charge on what enters from the between (`the-toll.md` §2/§4). Every giving the
corpus designs is giving *to someone or something that can receive* — a member,
a commons, a settlement's held door. But nothing designs **giving to the field
itself**: an object or held order deliberately left at a place for *no human
recipient*. The Gift gives member-to-member and refuses the book; **this
document designs the offering the field's own giving can make — the Votive, the
giving-to-the-field, the gift's other face.**

The load-bearing composition, stated once:

> **The Votive is the giving-to-the-field — an offering left at a place, the
> gift's other face: an object or held order deliberately left at a place for no
> human recipient.** Where the Gift gives to a member and refuses the book
> (`the-gift.md` §1), the Votive gives to the field itself and **deliberately
> leaves no recipient** — the offering that is a **conversation with the field,
> not with a settlement**: the field does not answer, does not repay, does not
> book. **The load-bearing honesty is the giving's own law: a votive gives what
> it leaves and asks nothing back** — the offering is the field's own order
> returned, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` held hard (`energy-harnessing.md` §6): the
> votive converts nothing, the giving asks nothing back — **never a bribe to the
> field** (the field cannot be bribed: it is indifferent, deterministic, legible,
> and a "votive that bought a favor" would be a false giving the field's
> indifference sheds, `the-dispute.md` §2) and **never a minted favor** (no
> offering that yields — the votive is the one economic act with no return at
> all, the gift's other face: the un-booked giving that refuses even the giver's
> own name, never a hidden bribe and never a hidden investment). **And the votive
> is honest about its silence** — the field does not answer; the giving is a
> conversation with no reply, the settlement's own act of giving with no
> recipient, legible and deterministic, never a hidden rite and never a secret
> petition.

This document is the corpus's **giving-to-the-field doc** — the face of the
giving economy that completes the giving's *direction*: the Gift gives a member
order un-booked, the tithe funds the commons' shared things, the toll charges the
held door, and **the Votive gives what it leaves, to the field, for no one**. It
is the giving that asks nothing back in a world where every other economic act —
the booked contribution, the loan's interest, the toll's upkeep, even the gift's
un-tallied warmth — either books, funds, charges, or *remembers*. The Votive is
the one act the field cannot answer, and its honesty is that it does not expect
one.

## 2. The leaving — the honest mechanics

### 2.1 What a votive is — an object or held order deliberately left at a place

The Votive's act is the **leaving**: not a transfer (the Gift moves order between
members, `the-gift.md` §2), not a funding (the tithe pays a maintained object,
`the-commons-tithe.md` §1), not a charge (the toll prices a crossing, `the-toll.md`
§1). It is an object or held order — the field's own order, in the handed or held
regime the settlement already moves (`the-market.md` §2.1, `schema-that-settles.md`
§2.1) — **deliberately left at a place, for no human recipient.** The distinction
from every sibling giving, stated once:

| The giving | It does | Its recipient | Its relation to the book |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Gift** (`the-gift.md` §1/§2.1) | gives a member's order to another, un-booked | a **member** (a receiver's held state) | the book's deliberate silence — no `C(M)` reads giver or receiver |
| **The Commons-Tithe** (`the-commons-tithe.md` §1) | pays a fixed share to the shared order | the **commons** (a funded maintained thing) | a booked `C(M)` contribution |
| **The Toll** (`the-toll.md` §1) | charges at the held door | the **border** (a door's upkeep) | a booked `C(M)` contribution |
| **The Compost** (`the-compost.md` §1/§2) | re-turns spent order as a feed | a **plot** (a depleted bed) | a booked deposit/draw of spent matter |
| **The Votive (this doc)** | **leaves an offering at a place** | **none — the field, for no human recipient** | **reads nowhere** — a leaving, not a booked or refused contribution |

The Votive is the giving's **direction-completing face**: the Gift is the
un-booked giving *to a member*, the Votive is the un-recipiented giving *to the
field*; the tithe funds a commons, the Votive gives with no commons; the toll
charges a door, the Votive gives at a place with no charge. Every sibling giving
has a recipient that can receive, fund, feed, or charge back; **the Votive
deliberately has none.**

## 3. The conversation with the field — the load-bearing design

The load-bearing design, stated once and never blurred:

> **The Votive is the offering that is a conversation with the field, not with a
> settlement.** The Gift gives member-to-member and refuses the book
> (`the-gift.md` §1); **the Votive gives to the field itself and deliberately
> leaves no recipient** — the field does not answer, does not repay, does not
> book. The Votive is the giving that asks nothing back — **the one economic act
> with no return at all.**

**Why the field cannot be a recipient in any human sense.** The corpus's
determinism is the field's defining legibility: the same state reads the same
outcome, every time (`the-dispute.md` §2; `field-npc-ai.md` §6d). A deterministic
field has no will to exchange, no ledger to credit, no favor to return — it
cannot *acknowledge* a giving the way a member, a commons, or a door can. **The
field does not answer.** That is not an absence the design papers over; it is
the design's whole point:

- **The field does not repay.** A member gifts and the receiver can decline, the
  commons fund and the bath is held, the door tolls and the crossing is kept —
  every sibling giving provokes a held, bookable, *living* counterpart. A votive
  provokes none: the offered order leaves the giver's held state and is left at
  the place, and nothing comes back — not a booked contribution, not a held
  improvement, not a kept door. **The Votive is the giving with no return at
  all.**
- **The field does not book.** Where the gifts, the tithes, and the tolls ride
  the Q4 op-record (booked or deliberately un-booked), a votive's leaving
  **reads nowhere** — it is not a booked contribution (no `C(M)` line), and it is
  not the gift's *refused* contribution line (there is no member and no receiver
  to refuse for). The census's active class reads the members who **booked a
  contribution this tide** (`the-census.md` §2.1); **a votive books no member's
  contribution, so it is invisible to the active class — the giving with no
  recipient reads nowhere, and that is the design's point.** The votive is not a
  member's act on the book; it is the field's own giving, which the roster
  cannot read.
- **The field does not answer — and the leaving is the conversation.** A votive
  is not a petition and not a rite; it is the settlement's own act of giving with
  no recipient, **legible and deterministic** — a conversation with no reply, but
  a conversation *spoken anyway*, onto the field's own order. The leaving speaks
  not to a hidden power but to the place the field already holds legible — the
  welling's abundance, the voice's scale, the named land's anchor — and its
  honesty is that it expects no word back.

**The one-line direction that makes this the gift's other face.** The Gift is the
un-booked giving that *keeps a receiver* — the kindness the book cannot tally but
a member receives (`the-gift.md` §1). The Votive is the un-booked giving that
*refuses even the receiver* — the kindness that not only the book but *no one*
receives, deliberately. **Where the Gift is the un-tallied member-to-member
giving, the Votive is the un-recipiented giving-to-the-field: the gift turned
outward past every recipient, to the field's own order.** The two are the same
giving's two faces — the book's refusal (the Gift) and the recipient's refusal
(the Votive).

## 4. The honest giving — the load-bearing honesty

### 4.1 The giving's own law — a votive gives what it leaves and asks nothing back

The load-bearing honesty of the Votive, stated once and never blurred:

> **A votive gives what it leaves and asks nothing back — the offering is the
> field's own order returned, `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` held hard, never a bribe and
> never a minted favor.**

The no-free-energy cap (`energy-harnessing.md` §6 — `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input`)
inherited unchanged as the giving's hard bound. **The offering is the field's own
order returned to the field: a votive converts nothing.** The held order left is
the **same coherence's leaving from one member's held state to a place** — a
meal's stored yield, an alloy's held order, a carved mark, *moved out of the
giver's hands and left*, never *into* any settlement as new. The sum of the
giver's held coherence after a votive is the same coherence that existed before,
minus what was left, consumed by the leaving itself — the `(1−q)` waste law of
any moving order (`energy-harnessing.md` §2) applied to the leaving's own loss.
**A votive cannot mint a yield, cannot recover the order it leaves, cannot turn
the giving-activity itself into coherence.** The leaving is a *spend*, never a
gain — and it is the one economic act whose honesty is that it spends with no
return at all.

### 4.2 Never a bribe to the field — the field cannot be bribed

The sharpest honesty the Votive owns is the one that answers the obvious
misreading: **a votive that "bought a favor" would be a false giving the field's
indifference sheds.** The field cannot be bribed because it cannot be addressed
as a power:

> **The field is indifferent, deterministic, legible** (`the-dispute.md` §2 — the
> same channels that adjudicate live-vs-residue adjudicate whose claim is true,
> because the field cannot lie the way a witness can; `field-npc-ai.md` §6d — the
> same state reads the same outcome, every time). **A deterministic field has no
> preference a bribe could reach and no discretion a favor could move.** A
> "votive that bought a favor" — an offering left *to influence* the field's
> judgment, weather, or fate — is a contradiction: the field's answer to a given
> state is fixed by the state itself, and no leaving changes it. **The Votive's
> offering moves no judgment because the field has no judgment to move.**

The boundary to hold hard: **a votive is never a price paid to the field for the
field's cooperation.** The toll pays a door with a keeper who *holds* the crossing
(`the-toll.md` §3), the tithe funds a commons that *maintains* the bath
(`the-commons-tithe.md` §1) — each is an honest exchange with a held counterpart
that receives and keeps. **The Votive pays nothing because the field keeps
nothing** — the leaving is not a payment for a favorable read, a protected well,
or a turned-away hazard. A votive that reads as "I left this so the spring stays
full / the Coda stays away" has missed the design: the spring stays full by the
field's own conservation (`the-spring.md` §2.1) and the Coda gathers by the
trail's own legibility (`signature-predator.md` §1/§2), never by the influence of a
gift. **The field's indifference is the votive's guarantee — there is nothing to
bribe.**

### 4.3 Never a minted favor — no offering that yields; never a hidden bribe, never a hidden investment

The second honesty, stated once: **the votive is the one economic act with no
return at all — the un-booked giving that refuses even the giver's own name,
never a hidden bribe and never a hidden investment.**

- **No offering that yields.** A votive is not an *investment* — no leaving
  returns more than it leaves, no votive compounds, no votive earns a `C(M)` or a
  held improvement or a future draw. **The votive converts nothing and expects
  nothing** — it is the one economic act with no return, in a corpus where every
  other act (a booked contribution, a loan's forward order, a toll's upkeep)
  either returns, funds, or is held. The `output ≤ φ⁻¹·input` cap (§5d) is held
  at the leaving, hard.
- **The un-booked giving that refuses even the giver's own name.** The Gift
