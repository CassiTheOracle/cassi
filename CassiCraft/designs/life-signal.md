## 1. The axis: maintenance

The single discriminating dimension, stated once:

> **A live thing *actively holds* its φ-lock against a drain; a fossil does not;
> a wielding vent *raises* `ε²` on purpose; a scar is a fallen floor.**

Every structure and every anomaly in the corpus is a coherence configuration the
local field is either *actively holding* or *has stopped holding* (or is
*intentionally un-holding*). The distinction between living and dead, deliberate
and degenerate, is not in the static `q`/`ε²` snapshot — a fossil's dark core and a
live deep organism's body both read q-locked, `ε²≈0` (`field-archaeology.md` §7
open-Q3) — **it is in whether the configuration *maintains itself* over time**,
which is exactly what a single snapshot cannot show and what a time-series read can.

The corpus already supplies every motion that constitutes maintenance:

- **Motion is steering that is maintained.** The ecology's creatures move by the
  river law (`chunk-field-quantization.md` §2.2; `field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2) —
  a live body changes position over the publish cadence; a fossil does not.
- **Feeding is a maintained lock.** A creature that feeds keeps re-raising `ρ` /
  re-quantizing its body against the drain (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §2.2; the
  `(1−q)` glow when wounded, §5.2) — it is *still doing the work of holding*.
- **The `(1−q)` glow pulses at the maintainer's cadence.** A living thing's glow
  is not constant; it breathes with the maintainer's rhythm — the vent backing up
  and settling, the resonance of `coherence-magic.md` §3.1, the pulse of feeding
  and recovering. A fossil's `ε²≈0` core never glows; a scar's broad high-`ε²` is a
  static plateau with no pulse.
- **Steering alignment (`∇(g·Φ)`)** tells *whether the structure is directing
  itself*. A live body's motion is *against* the field's own gradient only as it
  steers (the sheer act of a body not simply flowing with the river is the tell of
  an active steersman); a fossil's position is a stable point with no steering
  offset; a vent *creates* a gradient it then depends on.

These are the **axes** of the classifier. Each is a *derived* read over already
published quantities — none requires a new channel.

## 2. The derived read — no new channel

The classifier is a **derived read over already-published quantities**
(`corpus-reconciliation.md`: the ≈ 6 MiB publish = `q` 1 + pot 1 + `∇(g·Φ)` 3 + ρ
1; `async-field-domain.md` §2.1). It adds nothing to the publish — it *reads the
same snapshot the sampler already consumes* and computes a maintenance signature
from it. This is exactly the **read-only-consumer discipline** of
`coherence-magic.md` §5.1 (a reader consumes the published snapshot, never physics
state) and the **family rule** of `field-instruments.md` §2.1 (a consumer with a
presentation idiom, never a new channel).

| Published quantity | Derived axis | What it contributes to the classification |
|---|---|---|
| **position** (the entity/body record the sampler already tracks) | **motion** — position deltas over the publish cadence | a live thing moves (or steers to hold position); a fossil never moves |
| **`(1−q)`** (the waste fraction, `energy-harnessing.md` §2) | **lock-maintenance cadence** — the glow's *time-variation* (pulsing at the maintainer's rhythm vs static) | a maintained lock pulses; a fossil is dark (`ε²≈0`); a scar is a flat plateau |
| **`(1−q)` mean level** | the glow's **mean** over a window | live bodies glow while feeding (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.2); a fossil does not; a vent glows rising |
| **`ε²`** (decoherence) | **the `ε²` gradient** — rising (vent) vs flat (fallen floor) | a deliberate vent *raises* `ε²` on purpose; a scar is a static high-`ε²` plateau; a maintained lock holds `ε²` low |
| **`∇(g·Φ)`** (the river gradient) | **steering alignment** — does the structure's motion/signature track or oppose the gradient? | a live body steers (a body not simply flowing with the river is an active steersman); a fossil has none; a vent *creates* the gradient it feeds on |

**The cost.** Each axis is a cheap per-entity / per-structure read — the motion
deltas are a two-sample subtraction; the cadence is a small time-window variance
over the `(1−q)` channel; the `ε²` gradient is a spatial or temporal first
difference; the `∇(g·Φ)` alignment is a dot product. This is the same class of work
as the sampler's per-entity river-law steering (≈ 40 ns/entity,
`corpus-reconciliation.md`), inside the ≈ 1–6 ms/tick server sample budget and the
≈ 2,000-entity cap. The classifier is **a consumer, never a channel** — it reads
what is published, computes a derived maintenance signature, and presents a
class. No new publish, no new physics, no Q4 write.

**No new channel — the load-bearing statement.** The deliberate-vent axis is *not*
the per-entity phase-matching `M` the five peers said Phase-1 does not budget. It
is a **time-variation read of the published `ε²` and `(1−q)` channels** — a rising
`ε²` gradient over a moving/steering body with a clean phase is the vent's
signature, available without publishing `M`. The classifier answers the peers'
"or does it need `M` published per-entity?" with **"no — the time-series of the
channels you already publish carries the maintenance distinction."** Where the true
phase-match `M` would sharpen the read, the classifier exposes that limitation as
an open question (§7), not a blocker.

## 3. The four classes, honestly distinguished

**The fifth class — live-but-idle ([`sleep.md`](./sleep.md) §2.1).** Sleep's design
adds a sub-class at the maintenance axis' *idle extreme*: a body at rest shares the
fossil's *stillness* (no motion) but not its *non-maintenance* (it still holds the

resting cadence, `coherence-magic.md` §3.1's resonance at rest). The classifier
must read a sleeper as a **held run**, not a fossil/scar/vent — the same motion+
cadence+`ε²`-gradient matrix, with motion collapsed to zero but the slow pulse and

other; this class table's fifth row belongs to `sleep.md` §2.1.

### 3.2 The honest classifier line — where the noise floor sits

The **failure the five peers name** is not that the classes are indistinguishable;
it is that *at the noise floor* the static channels collapse together:

- **A live body vs its own fossil on the same line.** A player standing on their
  fossil (`player-remains.md` open-Q3), a deep organism beside a fossil, a Coda
  forming in a desert next to an old fossil (`signature-predator.md` §7 Q4) — all
  read q-locked, `ε²≈0` on the static `q`/`ε²` channels. **The static read cannot
  separate them; the maintenance read can.**
- **The deliberate vent vs the maintained lock.** A feeding creature glows
  `(1−q)` (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.2) and a decoherence-wielder vents `(1−q)`
  (`field-npc-ai.md` §4.1c) — both glow. **The discriminator is the `ε²` gradient's
  *sign* plus the cadence's shape:** the maintainer's glow is *bounded* and
  *pulsing* (it feeds, settles, feeds — the vent backs up and recedes, resonance
  `coherence-magic.md` §3.1), while the wielder's glow *climbs* (the vent is
  sustained and aimed at elevating disorder).
- **The growl vs the scar.** A storm's approaching `ε²` front and a static scar
  both read elevated `ε²` (`field-music.md` open-Q2; `field-hazards.md` §2). **The
  discriminator is whether the `ε²` is a *moving*, *rising front* (a growl — the
  leading edge of a `c_s`-traveling disturbance; `field-hazards.md` §2.1) or a
  *static plateau* (a scar).** The classifier reads the `ε²` gradient through time —
  rising-and-propagating = growl; flat = scar.
- **The recorded phase vs the live phase.** The tutor's re-enactment
  (`resonance-tutor.md` open-Q2) challenges "is the phase I hold now the one I held
  then?" The challenge is not classical — it is whether the recorded-phases
  re-presented by the tutor read as a *live* maintained lock or as a *static
  replay* of a lock. **The maintenance axis resolves it: a re-enacted live hold
  *pulses* (the maintainer is active); a static replay is *flat* (nothing is
  holding it).** The tutor's season-correction (`tide-of-the-attractor.md` §1.2)
  preserves this by re-basing the target phase for the regime the live body
  currently maintains in.

**The classifier line, honestly drawn.** The classifier does **not** claim to
separate the four classes from a single snapshot. It claims to separate them from
**a maintenance window** — a short time-series of the published channels at the
publish cadence. The *axes* (motion, cadence, `ε²` gradient, `∇(g·Φ)` alignment)
read real quantities; the *decision rule* — how long the window, what the cadence
threshold, where the `ε²`-gradient sign flips, how far the `∇(g·Φ)` offset counts
as "steering" — is **[design]** and is Phase-1 probe-calibrated (§6). The line is
the same one the ecology draws where the engine's channels "do not settle" the
organism judgment and a designed threshold does (`field-emergent-ecology.md` §6b);
here the channels *do* settle the maintenance axis, and only the threshold is
designed.

## 4. "Does this anomaly breathe?" — the reader's vitality layer

The Life-Signal is **not a new tool**. It is the **natural successor to the shared
observation instrument the corpus already treats as central**:

- `coherence-magic.md` §2 — the coherence reader **Sense**, the Phase-1 deliverable,
  is the instrument this classifier *extends*.
- `field-archaeology.md` §3.1 — the reader as the scan tool, aimed at the residue
  signature (dark core + ghost halo).
- `field-emergent-ecology.md` §5.1 — the reader as the observation tool (the probe
  colors the live sim by `q`).

The corpus has always had *one* instrument — the coherence reader and its
glanceable/atmospheric descendants (the Weatherglass, sound, the sky) — that reads
the published channels and presents them. **The Life-Signal is a new *layer* of
that read, not a new *tool*.** It answers a question the single-snapshot read
cannot: not just "what is the q/ε² here" but **"is the thing here alive — is it
holding its own lock?"**

> **"Does this anomaly breathe?"** The reader was a magnetometer; the Life-Signal
> makes it a stethoscope. A live structure's read *pulses* — the `(1−q)` glow
> breathes at the maintainer's cadence; a fossil's read is still and dark; a vent's
> read *climbs*; a scar's read is a flat, dead plateau. The vitality layer turns
> the four classes into four *readable breathing states*.

The Weatherglass (`field-instruments.md` §1.2) already has the four glanceable
forms — lume (q), climbing glow (ε²), waste tint (1−q), lean (∇(g·Φ)). The

   as the settlement's answer (you carry the vent-vs-sustained read without `M`), and
