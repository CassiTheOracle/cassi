## 2. The water-soaked earth — the honest mechanics

### (a) The marsh's deeper cousin — the water soaked in, not lying on

The Marsh is the slow sea — the shallow *textured water* that hides, the side the
sea's uniformity breaks into (`the-marsh.md` §1/§2). Its hiding is a **water
that lies on** the land, a shallow sheet the crossing reads and the trap sets.
**The Bog is the marsh's deeper cousin: the water *soaked into* the earth itself** —
not a sheet over the ground but the ground's own give, the land holding the water
it has drunk (`the-rain.md` §2.1 — the landed source; `the-flood.md` §4 — the
surfeit's residue). Where the marsh hides, the bog *softens* — the step sinks not
into water but into earth that has become soft, the land's own give where the
water has soaked it through.

### (b) The sink — the step that gives, its cost legible

The Bog's defining act is the **sink**: a body steps onto the soft ground and the
ground gives, the step going down where it expected to hold. The sink's cost is
the same gradient's give the corpus elsewheres designs — the fall owns the body
in the air (`the-fall.md` §2.1 — the river law, the gradient owns you), and the
bog's sink owns the step in the ground, the same field-law applied downward into
the earth. The sink is **never free and never hidden** — the softening reads on
the same q/ρ the walk moves through (`the-walk.md` §2 — the crossing designed as
a read), and the deeper the give, the more the instruments show before the step.
The sink's cost — a slowed exit, a mired crossing, the extra `(1−q)` of the
pulled step — is the same waste every soft crossing spends, legible and honest.

### (c) The softness's read — the pre-read applied to ground

The Climb's honesty is that you read a wall's height-feasibility before you
commit — a coherent face gives good holds, a scarred or thin face wastes
`(1−q)`, and the read is legible before the holds fail (`the-climb.md` §3.1/§3.2/
§3.3). **The Bog inherits that pre-read discipline and turns it to ground: you
read the softness before you step** — the softening's extent, its depth, its
give all read on the instruments the walk already uses (`the-walk.md` §1.2 — a
read, never a channel). **A bog is legible before commitment** — the step that
would sink is the step whose ground was read soft, and a bog that "hid its give
until you were in it" is a false trap the field's determinism sheds
(`the-dispute.md` §2).

### (d) The bog's water — the flood's residue, the rain's keep

A bog is water-held ground, and its water has a provenance the field's own
circulation gives it: the rain's nourishing fall that the earth keeps
(`the-rain.md` §1/§2.1) and the flood's surfeit that, instead of draining away,
stays soaked in the soft ground (`the-flood.md` §4 — the settlement's acts,
husbanded, not harvested). **The bog is where the water stays** — the earth that
drank the fall and held the rise, its softness the honest residue of the field's
own water-logging. The softness is not a separate water; it is the same q/ρ the
rain and the flood move, held in the ground, shown as give.

### (e) The soft ground in the ground's family — the opposite of the thin and the open

The ground's family is the corpus's own texture: the meadow is the fertile open
(`the-meadow.md` §1 — the grass re-precipitates, kept by being grazed), the
desert is the thin (`the-desert.md` §2 — `q` sits low), the quarry is the cut,
the bedrock is the hard floor. **The bog is the family's soaked member — the
ground where the water stays**, the opposite of the desert's leaving and the
meadow's open fertility, the land the pasture cannot graze (`the-meadow.md` §2c —
not the fallow, not the meadow) because the ground gives instead of holding. Its
softness is one more term of the field's own ground, read like the others.

### (f) The softness's form — this doc's [design] over the landed primitives

The Bog's mechanics are entirely composed from the landed primitives — the
marsh's shallow hide (`the-marsh.md` §2), the flood's residue and the rain's

trap is the water you misread, the bog's give is the earth you stepped on without

landed honesty — the same legible-before-commitment discipline (`the-marsh.md`

### (b) The give is honest to design because the softness is the field's own — never a spawned trap

A bog is honest to design because the ground's give is *the field's own
water-logging*, not a game-device trap: the water is the rain's keep and the

   the ford is the river's natural shallow (`the-ford.md` §1), the causeway the
   raised way over the hiding water (`the-causeway.md` §1), the marsh the shallow
   hide, the bog the soft ground. Is the bog the family's *un-built* member (the
   crossing no way lifts over, the soft ground the walker reads and turns around)
   or does a designed way (a corduroy, a raised plank) belong in the family? The
   honest default is the un-raised read — the bog's give is its honesty, and a
   way over it would be a causeway's design, not the bog's. **[design]**
4. **The sink vs. the fall's skill.** The corpus's movements are skill-buffed
