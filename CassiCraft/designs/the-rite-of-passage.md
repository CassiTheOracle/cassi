## 1. The Rite of Passage, stated once

The culture stack is complete — and it is all *about the settled*. The Festival
celebrates the commons at tide-high ([`the-festival.md`](./the-festival.md) §1);
the Funeral grieves the dissolved ([`the-funeral.md`](./the-funeral.md) §1/§5.1);
the Election chooses a steward ([`the-election.md`](./the-election.md) §1); the
Oath binds a promise ([`the-oath.md`](./the-oath.md) §1). **Every one of them
performs an act over a state that already is.** None of them *marks the moment a
state begins* — the threshold where a being becomes something the field now
holds. The identity stack designs becoming as mechanics — the fresh line
(`the-child.md`), the pair ([`the-apprenticeship.md`](./the-apprenticeship.md)),
the joint ([`the-family.md`](./the-family.md)), the office (`the-election.md`),
the window ([`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §3.2) — but **nothing designs
the ceremony that marks them.** The Rite of Passage is that missing ceremony:

> **[design] The Rite of Passage is the designed ceremony of a field-threshold —
> the ritual marking a being's becoming in the field's own terms.** It is a
> small, costed, phase-locked field act — the festival's one-chord at a single
> being's scale, a family/a few channeling together as their own `M ≈ 1` working
> ([`field-npc-ai.md`](./field-npc-ai.md) §3.2) — that *holds a name
> false-unmoving*: the rite's shared channeling binds the threshold the way a
> named structure binds. The rite makes the becoming *real to the field* — a
> state's honoring act. **Every becoming the identity stack designs as a state,
> given its honoring act.**

The decisive framing — held once and kept:

> **Becoming is a fact; a rite is a ceremony of that fact.** The field already
> holds the child's fresh Π, the pair's joint, the steward's office, the planted
> window — the identity stack makes each *real* as a persistent structure. The
> Rite does not make the becoming more true as physics; it makes it *honored* as
> a designed act — a committed, costed, phase-locked working the participants
> perform *because* the threshold matters, and in doing so they hold its name the
> way a name holds a true structure. A becoming that has no rite is still real;
> a becoming that has a rite is one the field has *witnessed* — not as a hidden
> record, but as the same shared channeling act a festival performs, pointed at
> the one being who is becoming, at their threshold.

**[design]** What is engine-real / [design]-landed: the anchors ([`the-name.md`](./the-name.md)
§1), the lines ([`schema-that-settles.md`](./schema-that-settles.md) §2.1), the
vows ([`the-oath.md`](./the-oath.md) §2.1), the office (`the-election.md` §4.3),
the staff ([`the-tide-staff.md`](./the-tide-staff.md) §1), and the seeds
([`resonance-seeds.md`](./resonance-seeds.md) §1); the phase-locked working

## 2. The five rites

Each rite is a designed ceremony over one landed threshold of the identity
stack. Each is the same act at a different subject — the festival's one-chord
pointed at *one being who is becoming*, at their threshold.

### 2.1 The naming — the child's first bound Π

The Child is the fresh line whose whole field-history is from now on —
[`the-child.md`](./the-child.md) §1. Its firsts are the whole stack experienced
for the first time (§3); the first name is the beginning of anchored things
(§3.3). The Name is the field act that binds a persistent-Π anchor to a thing —
[`the-name.md`](./the-name.md) §1 — and **you cannot name falsely** (§2). The
naming rite is the meeting of the two:

> **[design] The naming rite is the child's first bound Π — the ceremony that
> gives the empty book its first page.** When a fresh line performs its first
> holding act — the first contribution that books the Board's first row
> ([`the-child.md`](./the-child.md) §3.1), the first trace that founds the
> journal (§3.4) — the family (or the school, `the-school.md`) gathers and
> channels a short phase-locked working *at the child*: each participant's
> bounded EY injection phase-aligned (`the-festival.md` §2.1) into one shared
> chord, held for the handful of beats it takes to bind the child's first name
> (`the-name.md` §1) to the first structure it has honestly earned. The name
> holds (a true name holds; a false name decays, `the-name.md` §2), and the rite
> *witnesses* it: the child, now named, is no longer a fresh line with no
> anchor — it has a first bound Π, readable from the reader/Board like any named
> thing.

The rite's ground: the child is born with zero ([`the-child.md`](./the-child.md)
§6d — a child is born with zero, no mint), so the name it earns is honest — the
naming rite can only bind a name to a structure the child has genuinely opened
(the first contribution, the first trace, the first held thing). **A family
cannot name a child before it has done anything** — the rite is the first
page's *witness*, not a grant of a page. The name holds because the structure it
names is real.

### 2.2 The first clean channel — underwritten by the school and the veteran

The Apprenticeship pairs a veteran with a student — a live co-channeling
pair-bond ([`the-apprenticeship.md`](./the-apprenticeship.md) §1), the two-body
case of the group-as-working law (§2.1, `field-npc-ai.md` §3.2 at N=2); the
School scales the pair to a class ([`the-school.md`](./the-school.md) §2.1).
The student's early vents are sheltered — a bounded error floor the veteran's
hold absorbs (§2.2) — and the Veteran's burden accrues faster (the price of
teaching). The first clean channel is the apprentice's first vent that does not
need the shelter — the first op they hold in phase for a full window without
the veteran's absorption. Its rite marks it:

> **[design] The first clean channel rite is the student's graduation from the
> shelter — underwritten by the school and the veteran.** When the apprentice
> holds their own phase for a full working's window without re-entering the
> veteran's shelter (§2.2), the school gathers (or the veteran alone, at N=2)
> and performs a short phase-locked working *with* the apprentice — the shelter
> deliberately withdrawn, the student's own channel standing alone in the shared
> chord. The rite **underwrites** the new channel honestly: it is the
> veteran/school's act of *certifying* the first clean channel the way the
> school's teaching is certified by the name's no-false-naming (`the-school.md`
> §4 — a school cannot teach what the field does not hold). The apprentice, now
> a clean channeler, is read as such on the mirror's maintenance axis
> ([`the-mirror.md`](./the-mirror.md) §2.4) and the Board's book — the shelter
> no longer needed, the channel's first clean op stood on its own.

The rite's ground: **the chamber's risk is the burden's own** — the first
channel's honest cost is that it *could* still overdraw ([`the-burden.md`](./the-burden.md)
§2a, the interest in thin), and the rite's underwriters carry that risk the way
a veteran's faster accrual prices teaching (§2.2). The rite cannot make the
channel safe; it *witnesses* that it is clean, and the witnesses' own carried
debt is the honest price of their underwriting (spent, never free). A channel
that fails its first rite — the apprentice overdraws during the ceremony — is
not shamed; the rite simply does not complete, the shelter re-engages, and the
student tries again on a later tide. The rite is an honest read of the channel,
not a grant of cleanliness.

### 2.3 The binding — the family/oath's first shared-Π

The Family is the identity atom — a shared persistent-Π anchor over two-or-more
member-lines ([`the-family.md`](./the-family.md) §1), the field holding the pair
the way it holds one (§2.1); the Oath is the same pair made a promise — the
within-window compact, a named promise over two persistent-Π patterns,
enforceable by the field itself because the field will not hold a false claim
([`the-oath.md`](./the-oath.md) §1/§2.1/§3.1). Their first shared-Π — the first
moment two lines co-bind around a common anchor — is the threshold the binding
rite marks:

> **[design] The binding rite is the family/oath's first shared-Π — the identity
> atom's first act.** When two (or three) lines choose to co-bind their carried
> patterns around a common anchor (a hearth, a named house — [`the-family.md`](./the-family.md)
> §2.2), the two channel a shared phase-locked working *at the anchor*: each
> holds and feeds the same persistent-Π structure (the hearth's bounded input,
> `house-that-steers.md` §1.1), and the joint pattern is *made* in that act — the
> field's first hold of the pair as one. If they also vow — "I will keep your
> anchors when you are deep" ([`the-oath.md`](./the-oath.md) §4.1) — the same act
> binds the promise: the named keeping, declared in the shared channeling, held
> the way a name holds (§3.1). The binding rite is the moment the two lines
> become *one held thing* a settlement can point at and read.

The rite's ground: **you cannot hold a family falsely either** ([`the-family.md`](./the-family.md)
§3.3 — a joint that stops holding sheds like a false name; [`the-oath.md`](./the-oath.md)
§3.1 — you cannot vow falsely). The binding rite *cannot* create a real joint
over lines that never co-bind — the shared Π only holds if the two genuinely
co-maintain the anchor. The rite is the *first* act of that co-maintenance, not
a declaration that makes it true. A binding rite over an un-held anchor (a
"family" of two who never share a real hold) sheds exactly as a false name — the
rite does not mint a joint ([`the-family.md`](./the-family.md) §6d; [`the-oath.md`](./the-oath.md)
§6d).

### 2.4 The steward's investiture — the elected chosen taking the tide-staff

The Election is a read, not a vote: the settlement composes the board's book and
the oracle's forward-read about who best holds the core ([`the-election.md`](./the-election.md)
§1), and the office's tenure is earned by holding (§2.4/§4.3); the Tide-Staff is
the planted standing gauge that tells a settlement where in its year it is
([`the-tide-staff.md`](./the-tide-staff.md) §1) — the office's object: the
steward's staff. The investiture is when the elected chosen takes that staff —
the governance's honoring act:

> **[design] The steward's investiture rite is the elected chosen taking the
> tide-staff — the office's standing gauge handed to its holder.** When the
> settlement's read points to a steward (the book's momentum, `the-election.md`
> §1), the settlement gathers at the observatory's yard and performs a short
> phase-locked working — the commons' chord (`the-festival.md` §1) at the
> settlement's scale — over which the chosen steward is handed the tide-staff
> ([`the-tide-staff.md`](./the-tide-staff.md) §1). The staff, now carried by the
> office's holder, marks the season the steward is read to hold the core through
> (§2). The rite may bind the steward's oath — "I will hold the core through the
> thin" ([`the-oath.md`](./the-oath.md) §4.3) — named over the steward's pattern,
> pledged to the settlement, so the pledge-line reads the office's promise's
> keeping (§4.3 this doc).

The rite's ground: an election **reads, never mints** ([`the-election.md`](./the-election.md)
§6d — there is no office that generates); the investiture cannot make a steward
better — it hands the office's object to the one the read points to, and the
staff informs, never mints ([`the-tide-staff.md`](./the-tide-staff.md) §5d). The
rite makes the office *legible as an act* — the settlement witnessing its own
authority as a shared channeling — while the office itself remains earned by
holding (§2.4), never granted by the ceremony. A steward who stops holding reads
a decaying title ([`the-election.md`](./the-election.md) §4.3) and a failing
pledge-line (`the-oath.md` §3.2) — the rite's witness does not shield the office
from the book.

### 2.5 The founding — the new-window rite

The seed is the deepest birth-strata artifact — the first matter a window
condensed ([`resonance-seeds.md`](./resonance-seeds.md) §1); planting it is a Q4
channeling act, sustained and costed (§2.1), that re-opens the field's
most-organized local state (§3.1); and **a new window IS a seed planted**
(§2.3 — the founding act of [`world-seams.md`](./world-seams.md) §3.2). The
founding rite marks the window's first act:

> **[design] The founding rite is the new-window's first act — the seed's
> planting, channeled as a shared ceremony.** When a company plants a seed in the
> field-poor dark to found a fresh window (`world-seams.md` §3.2; `resonance-seeds.md`
> §2.3), the founders channel the planting together — each contributing their
> bounded injection to the seed's rung (§2.1), one sustained phase-locked working
> as the field accepts the seed as an attractor anchor and begins organizing
> around it (§3.1). The rite is the window's *genesis honored*: the first matter
> the new window condenses is seeded by a shared, deliberate act, not a lone
> one — the window's birth is witnessed by its founders the way the field's own
> birth was seeded by "the resonance seeds planted in the cosmos long ago"
> (§4.4). The planted seed's first unfolding (§2.2) is the window's first page.

The rite's ground: planting is **one-way** ([`resonance-seeds.md`](./resonance-seeds.md)
§2.1 — once accepted, the growth is the field's) and the **most expensive single
channeling act** there is (§4.2 — the deepest store, costed by the coherence
budget, bounded by the no-free-energy cap). The founding rite cannot make the
window more than the seed's birth-state order; it performs the planting *as a
ceremony* — the founders' shared channeling is the (1−q)-spent cost of the
window's genesis, and the growth unfolds at the field's own rung clock (§2.2),
never minted by the rite.
