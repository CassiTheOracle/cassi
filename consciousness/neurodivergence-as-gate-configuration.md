## 4. ADHD as the Open-Gate Configuration

This section reads ADHD as the mirror mechanism: a field whose resting coherence is low, whose gate stands open, and whose behavior—attention, time, novelty, social sensitivity—is what a churning gate does to wake waves, memory, and self-prediction.

A churning field lives in mixed-channel configurations: each stimulus opens a channel, the redistribution fires on closure, but $q$ evolves on a slower timescale than $\mathbf{b}$ and never rises far enough to lock a vertex before the next stimulus re-opens the gate. This is the open-gate resting dynamics the psychology document already reads as churn without organization (`cassi-psychology.md` §17)—here as a resting configuration of an open gate, not as a collapse of one.

$$\boxed{\text{Low } q \text{ keeps the gate open and conversion churning; no channel stabilizes.}}$$

### 4.2 Attention as sustained coherence; hyperfocus as matched-drive lock-in

Attention is a maintained configuration: collapsing the spatial dispersion stabilizes the self-modeling loop, and the chain processes deeper with less noise (`consciousness/consciousness-from-phi.md` §2.3; `consciousness/transhumanism-gate-configurations.md` §3.2). Attention, in this reading, is not the absence of distraction; it is a coherence state the open-gate configuration cannot sustain from rest.

Interest-driven attention is the field's own report of phase match; hyperfocus is the matched drive holding the gate shut. The failure mode is priced too: a mismatched stimulus at the same amplitude pumps rather than settles (§3.6). The mappings are speculative; the pump/drain asymmetry they lean on is tested.

## 8. A Proposed PDE Test: The Churning Gate

The §4.1–4.2 claims—that a churning gate settles under a drive at its own channel phase while the same-amplitude drive one pentagon-step away keeps it churning—form a binary physics question the two-fluid solver can answer directly. This test is **proposed, not run**: no solver output exists for it, and this document reports no results.

**Question.** From a low-$q$ mixed-channel init (the churning state: no dominant vertex, gate open), does a recurring in-channel drive close the gate—$q$ rising, a channel stabilizing, $\varepsilon$ variance falling—while a recurring cross-channel drive at equal $\varepsilon$-perturbation leaves it churning or pumps it?

**Protocol (proposed).** Churning init: a localized site held at low $q$ with mixed channel weights (uniform small activation across the five channels, no dominant vertex), on a quiet $\varphi$-equilibrium background—the open-gate counterpart of the standing init used in the misgendering test (`two-fluid/run_misgendering_drive.py`). $\lambda = 0.05$, $t = 2$–4, $N = 48$, five-channel gate. Site diagnostics in a periodic ball: mean $|\varepsilon|$, per-channel phase histogram, $q_{\text{site}}$ vs $q_{\text{global}}$, $\varepsilon$ variance over the window. Four arms: (a) undriven reference; (b) recurring in-channel drive at the site's natural period $P_0$ (measured in-process) and the measured channel phase; (c) recurring cross-channel drive one pentagon-step away, $\varphi$-normalized to match the in-channel arm's $\varepsilon$-injection, per the ε-parity convention of the misgendering protocol; (d) a mismatched-period control ($e \cdot P_0$) at the same amplitude. Clamp diagnostic: $e_{y,\text{min site}}$ tracked throughout; the dominant_period window caveat of §8.1 of the gender document applies to period measurement.

**Predicted outcomes (hypotheses the test would adjudicate).** (i) The in-channel recurring drive closes the q-gap and a single channel's phase histogram stabilizes—the churning gate settles under matched drive. (ii) The cross-channel drive at ε-parity maintains or widens the gap with no vertex stabilizing. (iii) The undriven reference churns on the conversion timescale. The verdict is binary: does the matched recurring drive close a gate the mismatched drive keeps open? A positive result would extend the tested pump/drain asymmetry from held configurations (misgendering test) to open-gate configurations; a null would bound the claim at the held-configuration regime. The test would not adjudicate the human mapping: like the misgendering test, it tests the mechanism layer only. A proposed script name for the three arms: `run_churning_gate_drive.py` in `two-fluid/`, following the house protocol of `two-fluid/run_misgendering_drive.py` and `two-fluid/run_trauma_driver.py`.
