# Time, Memory, and Ghosts: The Field as the Medium of Time, Memory, and Persistence

## Status: Speculative—August 2026

## Abstract

The Cassi framework declares the canonical density-conversion target $r_\star=E_Y/E_I=\varphi$ as a model input. Under that input and the stated conversion dynamics, local ratios can relax toward the target and support a conditional account of time, memory, and persistence. This document reads those possible dynamical behaviors as a theory of time, memory, and persistence. The conversion term is dissipative, so the arrow of time is a derived property of that declared model rather than an assumed postulate. Because information lives in patterns of Yang-Yin imbalance, and because such patterns dissolve only through conversion, a record is anything the field declines to dissolve—which makes memory a coherence phenomenon with a quantitative lifetime. A frozen gate can preserve a field configuration long after the structure that wrote it is gone; in the wake-lock language of the trauma formalism, that is the physics of a haunting: persistence without a body. The same mathematics that lets a phase-locked reader couple to wake waves bounds what such reading can see: remote sensing of the present and bounded extrapolation, never reception of unwritten information. Rung-shifting changes which cascade clock a system couples to, while the declared dissipative model can forbid re-visiting any field configuration—so “going back in time” means re-converging to $\varphi$, and the past is reachable only as record. The arithmetic de-resonance result motivates a physical hypothesis about locking and scale survival; it does not itself establish those consequences.

**Epistemic status:** Creative exploration grounded in Cassi formalism. Every mechanism is anchored to a specific equation or documented framework property—the conversion term, the IIR memory, the wake-lock tests, the phase-matching factor, the cascade clock hierarchy—but the synthesis into a phenomenology of memory, hauntings, precognition, and time travel is extrapolation beyond what the framework currently claims. The Derived number-theory result behind de-resonance supplies motivation only; suppression of rational locking, collapse, or scale loss remains a Hypothesized dynamical mapping requiring specified dynamics and observables. Nothing in this document should be cited as a Cassi prediction or derivation.

## 1. The Arrow of Time from the Field

Within the declared conversion model, time's arrow is not an additional postulate—it follows conditionally from the conversion term, because the conversion term is a one-way valve in that model: it drains any imbalance between Yang and Yin and can never, by itself, re-create one.

The two-fluid dynamics contain the conversion pair (from `foundations/cassi-first-principles.md` §1.2–1.3, displayed with the sign convention verified by the PDE tests of 2026-07-31 in `consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.4 and `foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §2.5):

$$\partial_t E_Y \supset -\lambda(1-q)(E_Y - \varphi E_I), \qquad
\partial_t E_I \supset +\lambda(1-q)(E_Y - \varphi E_I)$$

Here $\lambda = 0.1$ is the conversion rate and $(1-q)$ is the gate openness: the further the local field sits from $\varphi$-equilibrium, the harder conversion runs. The pair is antisymmetric—Yang excess converts into Yin, Yin excess into Yang—so the rates are equal and opposite and the total density $\rho = E_Y + E_I$ is conserved by it exactly. What matters for time is what the pair does to the deviation $\varepsilon = E_Y - \varphi E_I$. Writing the attractor potential as $V_{\text{attr}} = \tfrac{\lambda}{2}(E_Y - \varphi E_I)^2$, the pair gives

$$\frac{dV_{\text{attr}}}{dt} = -\lambda^2(1-q)(1+\varphi)\,(E_Y - \varphi E_I)^2 \leq 0$$

$$\boxed{\frac{dV_{\text{attr}}}{dt} \leq 0,\qquad \text{equality only at } E_Y = \varphi E_I}$$

The potential is a Lyapunov function for this declared conversion pair: it decreases along every conversion-active trajectory and is flat only at the fixed point. The ratio $r = E_Y/E_I$ therefore evolves monotonically toward $\varphi$ within the model (`foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §2.3), and that motion has an unambiguous direction. Run the equations backward and the pair changes sign: the time-reversed system is a repeller, not a solution of the same PDE. The dynamics break time-reversal symmetry for the same reason a damped oscillator does—dissipation—but here the dissipation is the core interaction of the theory, with strength set by $\lambda$ and the gate openness.

A second fact makes the arrow structural rather than incidental. The field carries a causal memory filter, the IIR average $\bar{\varepsilon}^2(t) = (1-\tau)\bar{\varepsilon}^2(t-\Delta t) + \tau\,\varepsilon^2(t)$ with $\tau = \varphi^{-1}$ (`foundations/cassi-first-principles.md` §2.4). The IIR uses only past values of $\varepsilon^2$; under time reversal it would have to use future values. The field's state is a functional of its history, so the present contains a compressed trace of the past and none of the future—a second, independent breaking of time-reversal symmetry at the level of the state itself. A de-resonance reading of this arrow is only a **Hypothesized dynamical mapping**: the Derived arithmetic fact that $\varphi$ is poorly approximated by rationals motivates testing whether the declared $\varphi$-target dynamics reduce phase locking or preserve multi-scale structure, but it does not establish either outcome.

**Conditional (Speculative):** below $\varphi$, the stabilized conversion→expansion realization (`cassi-psychology.md` §12.1) makes the monotone relaxation target $r_* \approx 0.9503$, not $\varphi$—"return to $\varphi$" holds only above the watershed, and the WRITE family (organized Yang injection, §2) is the only untested reverse drive at the global-$r$ level.

## 2. Memory as Field Persistence

A record is any pattern of Yang-Yin imbalance that the field declines to dissolve—memory is persistence, and persistence is a matter of coherence. The consciousness mapping presents the same law as wake persistence (`consciousness/consciousness-from-phi.md` §2.2).

Information in the Cassi framework can be represented by a spatial configuration of the raw Yang-Yin density difference $\Pi = E_Y - E_I$, while the canonical conversion dynamics evolve the distinct deviation $\varepsilon = E_Y - \varphi E_I$. These are not equivalent diagnostics: $\Pi$ is a raw imbalance/readout variable, whereas $\varepsilon$ is the deviation that enters the conversion pair, attractor potential, and record-decay estimate below. The $\Pi$-to-$\varepsilon$ relation therefore requires a constitutive or observation map; in this section the quantitative decay rate refers to $\varepsilon$ (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §1.1). The field's default behavior is to erase: wherever $q < 1$, conversion drives $\varepsilon$ back toward the attractor at rate $(1+\varphi)\lambda(1-q) = \varphi^2\lambda(1-q)$, and the pattern decays. A record survives only where the gate is nearly closed—the conversion that processes the field is the same mechanism that dissolves its structure. The decay rate of an imprinted pattern is

$$\lambda_{\text{eff}} = (1+\varphi)\lambda\,(1-q) = \varphi^2\lambda(1-q), \qquad
\tau_{\text{record}} \approx \frac{1}{(1+\varphi)\lambda(1-q)}$$

$$\boxed{\tau_{\text{record}} \approx \frac{1}{(1+\varphi)\lambda\,(1-q)} \;\xrightarrow{\;q \to 1\;}\; \infty}$$

At $q \to 1$ the record's lifetime diverges, and the energy cost of erasing it scales as $(1-q)$ (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §2.3). Within the creative mapping, this supplies the field-theoretic bookkeeping for three proposed Qi-computation operations: WRITE is an organized Yang injection with phase-matching $\mathcal{M} \approx 1$; ERASE is a temporary lowering of $q$ that lets conversion do its natural work; TRANSFER is a **Hypothesized constitutive transport/compact-phase extension**, not a consequence of the current alone. The quantity $J_\Psi = \Psi_0\nabla\Psi_1 - \Psi_1\nabla\Psi_0$ is a diagnostic of local phase structure; using it as a transported imbalance pattern requires a separate constitutive law and boundary conditions (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §2.2). Memory is a stored $\Pi$ pattern, forgetting is ERASE running passively, and fidelity is set by the Qi noise floor $\delta\Pi_{\text{min}} \propto (1-q)^{1/2}$: the closer $q$ is to one, the finer the distinctions the field can hold (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §1.2).

The framework gives the field two memory regimes, matching two familiar kinds of remembering. The IIR average is working memory: with $\tau = \varphi^{-1} \approx 0.618$, a perturbation's trace in the smoothed deviation has a half-life near $0.72$ timesteps (`foundations/cassi-first-principles.md` §2.4, `speculations/qi-computation.md` §5.1). Because the IIR stabilizes $q$, this is also how the field "predicts itself": when a pattern repeats quasi-periodically, the smoothed deviation converges to the pattern's mean, $q$ stabilizes, and conversion stays suppressed—the field locks into configurations that match its own memory (`foundations/cassi-first-principles.md` §2.4). Long-term memory lives in persistent $\Pi$ patterns sitting at local minima of the attractor potential, stabilized by the same conversion-diffusion balance that maintains cosmological bubbles (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §5.2); such patterns persist as long as the ambient $q$ stays above the dissolution threshold. A body is one elaborate example: the human gate chain spans 26 rungs from cellular ($n = 142$) to organism ($n = 168$) with 13 chakra nodes (`consciousness/chakras-as-cascade-bubbles.md`, summarized in `consciousness/cascade-consciousness.md` §4.2), and its records are readable in proportion to their depth—a deep, somatic record is shielded from cognitive readout by cascade suppression, $\varphi^{-1}$ per rung (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §1.1). Memory is field persistence, and field persistence is a coherence budget.

## 3. Ghosts and Hauntings as Wake-Locks

If memory is a field pattern, then a pattern can outlive the structure that wrote it—a ghost is a wake-lock whose driver is environmental rather than biological.

The trauma formalism supplies the mechanism. A perturbation too intense for the field to process in real time can form a standing-pattern wake candidate at the cascade rung where it struck; in the present solver interpretation, persistence requires a recurring driver that re-stimulates the site, and the local gate may remain displaced while that drive continues (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §1.3). The locked state has four measurable signatures at the site: the perturbation amplitude $\varepsilon_{\text{site}}$ held elevated, the local coherence $q_{\text{site}}$ depressed below the field's global value, the field phase displaced into the event's channel, and the spatial dispersion $\sigma_r$ elevated (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §2.2). These are exactly the quantities tracked in the wake-lock test runs of `two-fluid/run_trauma_wake_lock.py`: a pinned perturbation is a cosine pattern with an anti-node at the site, a radiating perturbation has the same peak amplitude as a localized packet, and the verdict is whether the site stays displaced when the driving source is removed.

What the 2026-07-31 PDE tests established matters directly for hauntings. An un-driven standing pattern does not pin the gate: it decays at the same conversion-driven rate as a radiating packet (42% vs 44% retained over $t = 10$ at $\lambda = 0.1$; the $q$-gap closes in both), so a wake is not self-sustaining (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.4, TR7). A wake is a driven structure. The driver test identifies the sustainer: a weak recurring trigger—0.005% of the event peak per step—holds the site at 80% of event intensity with a 4.5× widened $q$-gap and a fully displaced phase, and stopping the trigger releases the site on the conversion timescale ($|\varepsilon|$ falls to 22%, the $q$-gap closes, the phase returns 64% by $t = 20$; `consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.5). In the trauma model the driver is usually the person—avoidance-rehearsal loops, trigger generalization, hypervigilance (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §13, open question 7). But nothing in the physics requires the driver to be alive. At ambient rates the sustaining is phase-blind: the $\varphi$-pulsed and $e$-pulsed envelopes hold identical wakes, so a weak chronic trigger accumulates by mean delivered rate regardless of when it lands (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.5). A building can be such a driver—settling timbers, drafts, footsteps on a fixed schedule, thermal cycles, a creaking stair at the same time each night: weak, periodic re-injections into a cavity that happens to be a standing wave's home. The wake does not care where its re-stimulation comes from.

$$\boxed{\text{Haunting} = \text{frozen wake at a rung} + \text{environmental driver}}$$

The phenomenology follows from the signatures. The cold spot of a haunted room is mapped to the $q$-gap: the site has depressed local $q$, while the canonical scalar depends on density, composition, and residual memory and is not a phase-synchrony measure. Any standing-wave phase relation—including an anti-phase relation between Yang and Yin—belongs to an optional **Hypothesized** phase extension, not to the canonical density solver. Within that phenomenological reading, the site registers as "not fully real," a dissociation-like interpretation of the depressed $q$ (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §2.2, TR4). Under a sustaining driver, the standing pattern's phase displacement persists, and driven Fire or Wood events remain in their event channel through $t=10$ (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.8). Without that driver, the standing wake decays as in §10.4. The haunting replay is therefore driver-maintained, not a self-sustaining wave; it does not learn because the frozen channel does not execute the adiabatic redistribution closure.

Within this solver-level model, an un-driven wake decays on the conversion timescale, so the modeled lock ends when its driver stops. A $\varphi$-phased oscillation at the site at period $\varphi \cdot P_0$ is a solver-level comparison, not a clinical protocol or human prediction: it drains the wake at the held configuration at short times ($t \lesssim 4 \approx 0.2/\lambda$, `consciousness/gender-as-qi-configuration.md` §8.3), dropping $|\varepsilon|$ to 65% retained (vs 91% undriven) and returning the gate to baseline, while a same-amplitude non-$\varphi$ drive at $e \cdot P_0$ pumps the site instead, growing $|\varepsilon|$ to 188% of its initial value (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.4, `two-fluid/run_trauma_drive_compare.py`). The $\varphi$-channel engages only at high rates, with $\varphi$-specificity present at onset (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.7). Applying these solver-level drain and pump results to a place or person remains a Speculative mapping.

Finally, why a ghost is so often barely-there. A wake ten rungs down is 99.2% decoupled from visible interactions—cascade suppression attenuates a signal by $\varphi^{-1}$ per rung, so $\varphi^{-10} \approx 0.008$ (`speculations/qi-bubble-propulsion.md` §3.1, `foundations/cascade-suppression-formula.md`). And the boundary of a coherent wake presents a $\varphi$-detuned interface to ambient organized energy: with the phase-matching factor $\mathcal{M} \approx 0$, energy cannot couple across it—the no-sonic-boom mechanism of `speculations/qi-bubble-propulsion.md` §2.2 applied to a wake instead of a hull. The ghost passes through walls for a mechanical reason: its boundary is phase-detuned to every interaction the living can mount. It is a record with a driver, sitting at a rung we can barely reach—and the ladder has no floor. Below $n = 0$ the microcascade continues to infinite depth, with the Planck scale a smooth $\sigma$-regularized crossover rather than a wall (`foundations/microcascade-mirror.md`); a wake that sinks past Planck is shielded from every rung above, the limiting case of the same retreat.

## 4. Precognition and Remote Viewing

The field is a medium, and information travels through it at finite speed with phase structure—what the mathematics allows a phase-locked reader is remote sensing of the present and bounded extrapolation, never reception of unwritten information.

Start with what propagates. Perturbations of the two-fluid field radiate as wave packets with a distinctive structure: Yang-Yin interference imprints $\varphi$-periodic modulation, $\Delta(\ln k) = \ln\varphi \approx 0.4812$, on the wake of any disturbance—the same log-periodic signature predicted for the matter power spectrum (`predictions/falsifiable-predictions.md` §3, `foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §8.5). A wake wave is coherent, phase-structured, and periodic in logarithmic scale; a receiver whose gate is tuned to the wake's period can phase-lock to it, the way two coherent fields couple in the field-resonance picture of communication (`consciousness/cascade-consciousness.md` §2.1). Phase-locking requires $\mathcal{M} \approx 1$—the receiver's perturbation pattern must match the sender's phase structure (`foundations/quantum-measurement-derivation.md` §3.1). A trained reader is, in this vocabulary, a gate chain tuned to the wake's period.

Locking onto a wake ahead of its full arrival gives three things, each with a bound. First, remote sensing of the present: the wake's leading edge already carries the source's phase content—reading the front is reading the source state at light-speed delay, the defensible kernel of remote viewing. Second, scale-shifted reading: because the wake is log-periodic, the same phase content repeats at scales $\varphi^k$ apart, so a reader tuned to the periodicity can lock onto the wave's structure at a different rung than the one at which it was written. This is seeing the same configuration at another scale, which is why precognitive impressions feel symbolic and displaced—they are the phase content of a real wake, read at the wrong rung. Third, deterministic extrapolation: the field carries a running model of its own past in the IIR filter, and at high $q$ its evolution is regular enough that the model extends forward—the "waveform predicting itself" mechanism of `foundations/cassi-first-principles.md` §2.4. A phase-locked reader inherits that model. The fidelity of extrapolation is bounded by the gate openness, because conversion is the source of both dynamics and noise:

$$\boxed{\Delta t_{\text{lookahead}} \lesssim \frac{1}{(1+\varphi)\lambda\,(1-q)}}$$

The lookahead horizon is the conversion timescale: beyond it, accumulating $(1-q)$ noise swamps the extrapolation. A reader can see further only by raising $q$—and at $q \to 1$ the field is also at its most reversible, its least likely to do anything new to predict. The seer's horizon and the seer's stability are the same knob, and it points both ways.

A fourth channel deserves care: cascade-clock simultaneity. When the global ratio $r(t)$ crosses a cascade step, all associated scales activate simultaneously—horizon synchronization through temporal emergence (`foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §8.5). If the field's own ratio defines a foliation of simultaneous instants, then a reader phase-locked to the global $r(t)$ knows "what time it is" at every scale at once. That is a physically meaningful remote-present, the strongest reading of seeing-at-a-distance the framework supports.

What the mathematics does not allow is any channel carrying unwritten information. The conversion term is causal—$\lambda > 0$ fixed, no term references future states—the IIR filter is causal by construction, and wave propagation is finite-speed. The future is not a region of the field that can be listened to; it is a direction of the field's relaxation, and its content does not exist as a $\Pi$ pattern until conversion has written it. A prediction is an extrapolation of the present, with an explicit horizon. And there is a final subtlety for fiction: a seer who acts on a vision is no longer reading. Acting is WRITE—organized perturbation with $\mathcal{M} \approx 1$ (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §2.2)—and a vision acted upon is a self-perturbation of the field. The moment a precognition becomes an intervention, it stops being a measurement and becomes a cause.

## 5. Time Travel

In this framework there is no empty stage called time for a machine to travel through—there are fields and their ratio, and what a rung-shift changes is which cascade clock a system couples to.

Every cascade rung carries its own clock. The characteristic timescale at rung $n$ is $t_n = \ell_n / c = (\ell_{\text{Pl}}/c)\,\varphi^n$, so adjacent rungs tick in the ratio $\varphi$ (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §4.1). A system's experienced time is the clock of the rung it couples to, and coupling is a matter of the gate: a coherent system's effective rung is where its structure is anchored. Rung-shifting, the propulsion concept of `speculations/qi-bubble-propulsion.md` §1.1, is the modulation of that anchoring through the Qi gate. Apply that to time and you get a clock exchange rather than a journey:

$$\frac{t_{n+\Delta n}}{t_n} = \varphi^{\Delta n}$$

$$\boxed{\text{A rung shift of } \Delta n \text{ multiplies the system's clock period by } \varphi^{\Delta n}}$$

A system that shifts down the ladder ($\Delta n < 0$) couples to a faster clock—more of its own dynamics per ambient second. A system that shifts up runs slow. This is dilation by detuning, not travel: the traveler's clock changes, the world's clock does not, and nothing re-orders the world's events. "Returning earlier than you left" is available only as clock arithmetic—you return to the same worldline having aged differently—which is physics the framework can actually cash.

What the framework cannot cash is a closed loop. Along any conversion-active trajectory of the declared model the attractor potential strictly decreases, $dV_{\text{attr}}/dt = -\lambda^2(1-q)(1+\varphi)(E_Y - \varphi E_I)^2 \leq 0$ with equality only at the fixed point. A closed timelike loop requires the field to revisit a configuration it has already occupied; the potential forbids it within those model assumptions, because it is strictly monotone along the trajectory. There are no periodic orbits of the stated dissipative dynamics for a worldline to thread, and the effective metric inherits its time orientation from the ratio's monotone relaxation toward $\varphi$ (`foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §2.3). The de-resonance principle contributes motivation, not an independent proof: its Derived number-theory result does not universally prohibit locking or collapse; the no-loop interpretation is a Hypothesized consequence of the declared dynamics.

$$\boxed{\text{No closed timelike loops: } V_{\text{attr}}(t_2) < V_{\text{attr}}(t_1) \text{ for every } t_2 > t_1 \text{ along any conversion-active trajectory}}$$

The one respect in which the past is reachable is the one this document has been building toward: the past survives as record. The IIR memory holds a compressed trace; persistent $\Pi$ patterns hold full configurations where $q \to 1$ (§2); frozen wakes hold moments, complete with phase content (§3). Reading the past is phase-locking to those records. And that sets the cost of "going back": to re-enter an earlier configuration is to erase everything the conversion has written since—the interval between then and now is the accumulated imbalance the field has processed, and undoing it is ERASE at full scale, whose cost per bit scales as $(1-q)$ (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §2.3). The traveler is made of record: a coherent structure survives only while its $q$ stays high and conversion stays suppressed—the proton's coherence budget, $P \approx \varphi^{-4506}$ per cycle for a 92-rung structure, is the extreme case (`foundations/proton-coherence-budget.md`, `foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §6.6)—and every act of going back would have to open the gates that keep the traveler intact. The deeper the journey, the more of the traveler must be dissolved to pay for it. Time travel is not forbidden by fiat; it is forbidden by the same budget that makes memory, and therefore identity, possible: the field can hold the past only as structure, and structure can be exchanged for the past only by being spent.

## 6. The Storytelling Toolkit

For fiction, this picture hands over a physics with costs: time is a coherence resource, ghosts are records with drivers, and going back is a form of self-erasure.

Time as coherence. The arrow's stiffness is $(1-q)$, a field variable: where the gate is open, time runs hard and irreversible; where $q \to 1$, processes approach reversibility and a system's time is more its own. A character who has "all the time in the world" has, literally, coherence to spare—the world moves at the ambient conversion rate while the coherent character moves through reversible dynamics. Longevity, the meditative state, and the frozen moment of trauma are the same variable at different values of $q$. The cascade clock adds texture: every rung has its own period, $\varphi$ times its neighbor's, and when the global ratio $r(t)$ crosses a cascade step, all associated scales activate at once (`foundations/cassi-theory-reference.md` §8.5)—instants when the wave crests at every scale simultaneously, moments of universal simultaneity a story can hang on. Step numbers give them names: 80 (electroweak), 95 (QCD), 117 (Bohr), 142 (cellular), 168 (human), 200 (Earth), 292 (Hubble) (`foundations/dimensionful-cascade.md`). A story can count its turning points in rungs.

Ghosts in this fiction are modeled with wake-lock rules. The underlying solver tests cover field-level decay, fixed phase content, phase-matched triggering, and driven persistence; they do not test hauntings. Within the story mapping, a haunting decays on the conversion timescale unless driven; it replays fixed phase content rather than learning; it is triggered by phase-matched stimuli; it registers as cold and unreal because its site's $q$ is depressed; it sits at a rung, deeper and more somatic the older the event (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §§2.2, 4.1, 10.4–10.8). Its boundary is $\varphi$-detuned, so it can be phase-locked to under the selected model (`speculations/qi-bubble-propulsion.md` §2.2). A $\varphi$-phased oscillation at its own period clears the fictional wake, while energy off-$\varphi$ feeds it (`consciousness/trauma-as-frozen-gate.md` §10.4). Each rule supplies a scene: the investigator who finds the period, the house that feeds the wake, the exorcism that is a tuning problem. Persistence requires a driver, making the deepest ghost story about the driver: a haunting is a contract between a frozen moment and whatever keeps re-injecting it, and the driver can be impersonal—a building, a schedule, a tide. The ghost is the record; the house is the ghost's metabolism.

Precognition with a budget. A seer's horizon is $1/[(1+\varphi)\lambda(1-q)]$: further sight requires more coherence, and more coherence means a more stable, more predictable world to see into (§4). Visions are scale-shifted readings of real wakes, symbolic because they are read at the wrong rung; acting on a vision converts it from a reading into a cause, and the story must pay for that conversion. A seer who can see the future cannot change it without ceasing to be the seer of that future—the vision acted upon is a perturbation of the field that wrote it.

The cost of going back. Time travel is clock exchange plus record-keeping. A traveler who shifts rungs returns aged differently, to a world that has converged further toward $\varphi$; the past is reachable only as record, and reading it is phase-locking to someone's wake—with all the consent questions that implies, because TRANSFER moves the pattern into the reader (`speculations/qi-computation.md` §2.2). To actually re-enter an earlier configuration is to erase the interval, and the interval is the traveler. "You can't go home" is here a quantitative statement: home is a configuration the field has left behind, the traveler's coherence is what prevents re-entering it, and every attempt to return spends the very structure that remembers what home was.

Three rules close the toolkit, and they are the document's whole argument:

$$\boxed{\begin{aligned}
& \text{1. The arrow is the conversion: time flows where imbalance drains, at rate } (1+\varphi)\lambda(1-q).\\
&\text{2. Memory is persistence: a record lasts as long as } q \to 1 \text{ keeps conversion at bay.}\\
&\text{3. The past is a record, not a destination: you can return to } \varphi \text{, never to a moment.}
\end{aligned}}$$
